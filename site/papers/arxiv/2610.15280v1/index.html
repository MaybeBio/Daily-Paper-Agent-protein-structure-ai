GeoBridge: Evolving Transferable Decoder Topologies for Geometric Vision

Rui Wang; Haoyu Zhang; Ran Cheng
arXiv 预印本 · 2026-10-11 · 全文(ar5iv) · 原文

该文研究几何视觉中深度估计与高斯重建的可迁移解码器拓扑进化搜索，不涉及蛋白质结构计算，与课题无关。

摘要

Depth estimation and Gaussian reconstruction share a need for multi-scale feature processing, yet use different prediction interfaces and training objectives. Whether a decoder's internal computation can be optimized once and reused across these systems remains a structural design question. Search over primitive configurations alone leaves the organization of transformations fixed. To address this gap, we propose GeoBridge, an evolutionary framework for transferable decoder topologies. First, GeoBridge makes stage-internal topology searchable through residual, sequential, and parallel composition of configurable primitives. Shape-preserving composition retains the stage interfaces of a fixed multi-scale scaffold. Second, a functional-tree encoding supports stage-aware crossover and hierarchical mutation, while a gradient sensitivity-alignment proxy evaluates candidates without training. Multi-objective evolution balances this proxy with computation and parameter cost. Third, selected topologies are instantiated and trained in target systems with task-specific prediction interfaces. A single source search takes approximately 12 GPU hours. GeoBridgeNet-A3 achieves the best depth accuracy among four adapted decoder baselines under a common encoder and training protocol on NYU Depth V2 and KITTI. The same topology reaches 27.11 dB PSNR in Gaussian novel-view synthesis on RealEstate10K, and its refined representations support open-vocabulary segmentation on LERF. These results support decoder topology as a reusable design object across the evaluated geometric vision systems. Code and pretrained weights are available at https://github.com/EMI-Group/GeoBridge-NAS.

摘要翻译

深度估计与高斯重建共享对多尺度特征处理的需求，但使用不同的预测接口和训练目标。解码器的内部计算能否被优化一次并在这些系统间复用，仍是一个结构设计问题。仅对原始配置进行搜索会使变换的组织方式保持固定。为弥补这一空白，我们提出GeoBridge，一个用于可迁移解码器拓扑的进化框架。首先，GeoBridge通过可配置原语的残差、序列和并行组合，使阶段内部拓扑可搜索。形状保持组合保留了固定多尺度脚手架的阶段接口。其次，功能树编码支持阶段感知交叉和层次变异，而梯度敏感性对齐代理无需训练即可评估候选方案。多目标进化在计算和参数成本之间平衡该代理。第三，选定的拓扑被实例化并在具有任务特定预测接口的目标系统中训练。单次源搜索约需12 GPU小时。GeoBridgeNet-A3在NYU Depth V2和KITTI上，在共同编码器和训练协议下，于四个适配解码器基线中达到最佳深度精度。同一拓扑在RealEstate10K上的高斯新视角合成中达到27.11 dB PSNR，其精炼表示支持LERF上的开放词汇分割。这些结果支持解码器拓扑作为所评估几何视觉系统中可复用的设计对象。代码和预训练权重可在https://github.com/EMI-Group/GeoBridge-NAS获取。
