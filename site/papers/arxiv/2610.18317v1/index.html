Accurately Generalizing Deep-Learning Hamiltonian Methods to Plane-Wave Density Functional Theory

Chuntian Wu; Jiaxiang Li; Qiang Xu; Xuecheng Shao; Congwei Xie; Yu Xie; Yanming Ma
arXiv 预印本 · 2026-10-13 · 全文(ar5iv) · 原文

该文用深度学习预测材料结构的DFT哈密顿量，虽涉及电子结构计算，但核心对象是材料而非蛋白质结构，且未涉及蛋白质结构预测、构象采样或对接等本课题核心问题，仅算法框架可能对蛋白体系有间接启发。

摘要

Deep neural networks that predict density functional theory (DFT) Hamiltonians from material structures offer a route to efficient electronic-structure calculations. However, extending localized atomic-orbital (AO) Hamiltonian learning to plane-wave (PW) calculations with hybrid functionals requires a compact AO representation that preserves the effects of nonlocal operators. Here we propose a projectability-guided construction of nonorthogonal AO Hamiltonians from a small, selected set of PW eigenstates and eigenvalues. High-projectability states are retained, and selected higher-energy states complete the AO subspace, avoiding an empirical shift and its arbitrary contribution to the learning targets. Because the construction uses PW eigenstates and eigenvalues, it incorporates nonlocal contributions present in the reference calculation without explicitly reconstructing their AO matrix elements. Benchmarks for MoS2 and sodium phases show accurate reproduction of PW bands with smaller AO bases than real-space reconstruction. The resulting PBE and HSE Hamiltonians can be learned by equivariant neural networks, connecting PW reference calculations with electronic-structure prediction. This approach broadens the range of PW DFT calculations available for AO Hamiltonian learning and enables compact, accurate training targets for developing more widely applicable electronic-structure models.

摘要翻译

预测材料结构对应的密度泛函理论（DFT）哈密顿量的深度神经网络，为高效电子结构计算提供了一条途径。然而，将局域原子轨道（AO）哈密顿量学习扩展到具有杂化泛函的平面波（PW）计算，需要一种紧凑的AO表示，以保留非局域算符的影响。在此，我们提出了一种基于可投影性引导的构造方法，从一小部分选定的PW本征态和本征值中构建非正交AO哈密顿量。高可投影性的态被保留，选定的高能态则补全AO子空间，从而避免了经验位移及其对学习目标的任意贡献。由于该构造使用PW本征态和本征值，它纳入了参考计算中存在的非局域贡献，而无需显式重建其AO矩阵元。对MoS2和钠相的基准测试表明，与实空间重建相比，使用更小的AO基组即可准确再现PW能带。所得的PBE和HSE哈密顿量可通过等变神经网络学习，将PW参考计算与电子结构预测联系起来。该方法拓宽了可用于AO哈密顿量学习的PW DFT计算范围，并为开发更广泛适用的电子结构模型提供了紧凑、准确的训练目标。
