Doppler-compensated Space-time Coding for MIMO PMCW Radar

Gabriel Daniel; Hanqing Wu; Ashish Pandharipande; Geert Leus
arXiv 预印本 · 2026-10-19 · 全文(ar5iv) · 原文

该文研究MIMO PMCW雷达的Doppler补偿空时编码，属于雷达信号处理领域，与蛋白质结构计算完全无关。

摘要

Phase modulated continuous wave (PMCW) radar is an attractive choice for automotive sensing due to its high dynamic range, interference robustness, and support for integrated communication and sensing. In multiple-input multiple-output (MIMO) PMCW radar, code division multiplexing (CDM) separates transmit signals, but non-ideal auto- and cross-correlation properties increase range sidelobes and degrade dynamic range. This paper proposes a Doppler-compensated space-time coding (STC)-based MIMO PMCW radar framework that separates transmit signals before range correlation, eliminating cross-correlation terms under perfect compensation and allowing the same binary sequence to be used across transmit antennas. The Doppler influence on STC decoding is analytically characterized, showing that chip-wise and inter-STC-slot phase distortions increase range sidelobes and generate ghost components in the direction-of-arrival (DOA) spectrum. To mitigate these effects, Doppler is estimated first, followed by joint fast-time and STC-slot compensation before decoding. Residual STC leakage under imperfect Doppler estimation is quantified, and the processing is extended to multiple moving targets through Doppler-bin-dependent compensation. Theoretical analysis and simulation results demonstrate reduced range sidelobes and suppression of DOA ghosts, while maintaining flexibility in code sequence design.

摘要翻译

相位调制连续波（PMCW）雷达因其高动态范围、抗干扰鲁棒性以及对集成通信与感知的支持，成为汽车传感领域颇具吸引力的选择。在多输入多输出（MIMO）PMCW雷达中，码分复用（CDM）用于分离发射信号，但非理想的自动相关和互相关特性会增加距离旁瓣并降低动态范围。本文提出一种基于多普勒补偿的空时编码（STC）MIMO PMCW雷达框架，该框架在距离相关之前分离发射信号，在完美补偿条件下消除互相关项，并允许在所有发射天线上使用相同的二进制序列。对STC解码中的多普勒影响进行了解析表征，表明码片级和STC时隙间相位畸变会增加距离旁瓣，并在到达方向（DOA）频谱中产生虚假分量。为减轻这些影响，首先估计多普勒，然后在解码前进行联合快时间和STC时隙补偿。量化了多普勒估计不完美情况下的残余STC泄漏，并通过多普勒频点依赖补偿将处理扩展到多个运动目标。理论分析和仿真结果表明，该方法在保持码序列设计灵活性的同时，降低了距离旁瓣并抑制了DOA虚假分量。
