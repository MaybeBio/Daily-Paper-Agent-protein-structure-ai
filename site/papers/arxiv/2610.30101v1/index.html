Machine Learning-Enabled Wireless Power Transfer: A Task-Oriented Review of Structural Optimization, State and Parameter Estimation, and Adaptive Control

Denghao Luo; Yiheng Jiang; Jiaqi Zhao; Yuan Gao; Changsong Cai; Weiyi Huang; Yun Zhang
arXiv 预印本 · 2026-10-21 · 全文(ar5iv) · 原文

该文综述机器学习在无线能量传输系统结构优化、状态估计与自适应控制中的应用，对象为电力电子系统，与蛋白质结构计算完全无关。

摘要

Wireless power transfer (WPT) systems must maintain efficient and safe energy delivery under variations in geometry, coupling, load, alignment, and operating conditions. Machine-learning methods are increasingly applied to accelerate structural design, estimate operating parameters and states, and support adaptive control and scheduling. This review develops a task-oriented framework organized around three interconnected domains: structural design and optimization, parameter and state estimation, and control and optimization. Supervised learning, reinforcement learning, and hybrid or physics-informed methods are compared in terms of data requirements, dependence on physical models, information sources, computational requirements, accuracy, online response, application scope, and optimization objectives. Supervised learning provides rapid surrogate modeling, regression, and classification when representative labeled data are available. Reinforcement learning addresses sequential control and scheduling but introduces challenges in reward design, safe exploration, and simulation-to-reality transfer. Hybrid methods integrate physical knowledge with data-driven models to improve data efficiency and physical consistency. The principal barriers to engineering deployment include limited and biased datasets, weak out-of-distribution generalization, insufficient physical consistency, unsafe online adaptation, embedded-resource constraints, and the absence of standardized benchmarks. Future research should prioritize uncertainty-aware and physics-informed learning, safe simulation-to-reality transfer, lightweight edge deployment, distributed coordination, reproducible evaluation, and digital-twin-assisted development.

摘要翻译

无线功率传输（WPT）系统必须在几何结构、耦合、负载、对准及运行条件变化下保持高效、安全的能量传输。机器学习方法日益被应用于加速结构设计、估计运行参数和状态，并支持自适应控制与调度。本综述构建了一个以任务为导向的框架，围绕三个相互关联的领域展开：结构设计与优化、参数与状态估计，以及控制与优化。监督学习、强化学习及混合或物理信息方法在数据需求、对物理模型的依赖、信息源、计算需求、精度、在线响应、应用范围和优化目标方面进行了比较。监督学习在具有代表性标注数据时，提供快速代理建模、回归和分类。强化学习处理序列控制和调度，但在奖励设计、安全探索及仿真到现实迁移方面带来挑战。混合方法将物理知识与数据驱动模型相结合，以提高数据效率和物理一致性。工程部署的主要障碍包括有限且有偏的数据集、分布外泛化能力弱、物理一致性不足、在线自适应不安全、嵌入式资源限制以及缺乏标准化基准。未来研究应优先关注不确定性感知和物理信息学习、安全的仿真到现实迁移、轻量级边缘部署、分布式协调、可复现评估及数字孪生辅助开发。
