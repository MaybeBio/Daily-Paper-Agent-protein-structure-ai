A Metadynamics-Based Protocol for Sampling and Scoring Peptide Nanofibril Conformations.

Yang, Chao-Yu; Miller, Aline F; Saiani, Alberto; Bryce, Richard A
The journal of physical chemistry. B · 2026-10-29 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该工作提出基于元动力学的采样与打分协议，利用超分子扭转集合变量探索肽纳米纤维的构象景观并预测优势形态，属于蛋白质/多肽结构计算中的增强采样与构象生成研究，与课题高度相关。

摘要

The morphology of a cross-beta nanofibril, ranging from ribbon to rod conformations, is commonly determined by the amino acid sequence of its constituent peptides in addition to environmental factors such as pH and salt concentration. The use of all-atom molecular dynamics simulation of a given peptide nanofibril to explore its energetically favorable fibril conformations is a desirable but challenging goal, due to potential trapping in local energy minima. In this work, we explore a metadynamics-based approach to investigate morphologically distinct peptide nanofibrils, introducing collective variables based on supramolecular twist as a sampling coordinate. We artificially construct a search space consistent with integral fibril structures and perform metadynamics simulations to overcome energy barriers and traverse the rugged conformational landscape of the large peptide assemblies in water. Based on this, we compute a measure which we term the resistance score to identify the propensity of distinct fibril morphologies across the conformational space. This computational protocol is then used to investigate the impact of fibril length and peptide sequence on the supramolecular twist of peptide nanofibrils in aqueous solution, comparing these results with the fibril morphologies observed in electron microscopy. The proposed workflow demonstrates an ability to identify a preferred morphology for beta-sheet assemblies, which could aid in the design of new peptide self-assembled architectures.

摘要翻译

cross-beta纳米纤维的形态，从带状到棒状构象，通常由其组成肽的氨基酸序列以及环境因素（如pH和盐浓度）决定。利用全原子分子动力学模拟特定肽纳米纤维以探索其能量上有利的纤维构象，是一个理想但具有挑战性的目标，因为可能陷入局部能量最小值。在本工作中，我们探索了一种基于元动力学的方法来研究形态上不同的肽纳米纤维，引入基于超分子扭转的集体变量作为采样坐标。我们人为构建了一个与整体纤维结构一致的搜索空间，并进行元动力学模拟以克服能量障碍，穿越水中大型肽组装体的粗糙构象景观。基于此，我们计算了一个称为抗性评分（resistance score）的度量，以识别不同纤维形态在构象空间中的倾向性。该计算协议随后用于研究纤维长度和肽序列对水溶液中肽纳米纤维超分子扭转的影响，并将这些结果与电子显微镜观察到的纤维形态进行比较。所提出的工作流程展示了识别β-折叠组装体优选形态的能力，这可能有助于设计新的肽自组装架构。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract describes a conceptually interesting and potentially useful computational protocol for exploring conformational landscapes of peptide nanofibrils, an area where all-atom sampling is genuinely difficult. The central ideas, namely twist-based collective variables and a resistance score for morphology propensity, are plausible and of interest to the peptide self-assembly and biomolecular simulation communities. However, from the supplied material alone, the work is not technically established. Key elements required to judge whether the protocol samples the intended landscape, whether the resistance score is meaningful and well-defined, and whether the electron microscopy comparison is quantitative and unbiased are absent. The claims are therefore currently supported only at the level of stated intent, not demonstrated evidence.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers in peptide and protein self-assembly, amyloid and cross-beta fibril structure, biomolecular simulation method development, enhanced sampling, and soft matter computational chemistry would be interested. Interest would stem from the possibility of a transferable protocol for sampling large supramolecular assemblies and from a proposed scoring scheme that could guide design of peptide architectures. Experimentalists using electron microscopy to characterize fibril morphology may also be interested if the computational predictions are shown to align quantitatively with observed morphologies.
  • Major strengths The problem is well motivated, since all-atom exploration of large peptide assemblies is limited by trapping in local minima. The introduction of supramolecular twist as a collective variable is a reasonable and potentially original direction for this class of systems. Combining enhanced sampling with a morphology-oriented score and comparing against electron microscopy is a sensible overall workflow. The stated aim of aiding design of new self-assembled architectures gives the work clear application relevance.
