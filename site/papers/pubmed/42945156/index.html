7

Structural basis for inhibition of the lipid flippase MurJ by phage lysis protein SglPP7 suggests convergent evolution with other Sgl peptides.

Hosoda, Kaito; Kohga, Hidetaka; Wu, Sixian; Tanaka, Hiroyuki; Shigematsu, Hideki; Miyazaki, Ryoji; Chek, Min Fey; Tsukazaki, Tomoya
PLoS biology · 2026-10-27 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文通过冷冻电镜结构解析和分子动力学模拟揭示噬菌体裂解蛋白SglPP7抑制脂质翻转酶MurJ的机制，虽以膜蛋白复合物为对象，但结构建模与MD模拟方法可迁移至蛋白质结构计算研究。

摘要

Some bacteriophages encode lysis proteins that inhibit essential bacterial processes, and elucidating their mechanisms is valuable for developing antibacterial strategies against drug-resistant pathogens. We determined the cryo-EM structure of the complex between the essential Escherichia coli lipid II flippase MurJ and the phage lysis protein SglPP7. Structural, mutational, and molecular dynamics (MD) simulation analyses revealed that SglPP7 binds along the intramembrane cleft of MurJ and inserts its C-terminal tail into the internal cavity, stabilizing MurJ in an outward-facing conformation. Structural comparison with LysM (SglM) and SglCJ3 showed that these sequence-divergent Sgl proteins stabilize MurJ in the outward-facing conformation through distinct binding modes, consistent with the possibility of convergent evolution toward MurJ inhibition.

摘要翻译

一些噬菌体编码能抑制细菌关键过程的裂解蛋白，阐明其机制对于开发针对耐药病原体的抗菌策略具有重要意义。我们解析了必需大肠杆菌脂质II翻转酶MurJ与噬菌体裂解蛋白SglPP7复合物的冷冻电镜结构。结构、突变及分子动力学（molecular dynamics, MD）模拟分析揭示，SglPP7沿MurJ的膜内裂隙结合，并将其C末端尾部插入内部空腔，稳定MurJ于外向构象。与LysM（SglM）和SglCJ3的结构比较显示，这些序列分化的Sgl蛋白通过不同的结合模式稳定MurJ的外向构象，这与向MurJ抑制的趋同进化可能性一致。

02 一句话总结

本文通过 cryo-EM 解析了大肠杆菌 MurJ 与噬菌体裂解蛋白 SglPP7 的复合物结构（3.2 Å），结合突变分析和 MD 模拟，发现 SglPP7 通过其跨膜螺旋沿 MurJ 膜内裂隙结合并将 C 端尾部插入 C-lobe 内部腔体，将 MurJ 稳定在 outward-facing 构象，且其结合模式与序列无关的 LysM 和 SglCJ3 截然不同，提示趋同进化。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：SglPP7 如何抑制 MurJ 的 lipid II 翻转酶功能？其结合模式与已知的 MurJ 抑制剂 LysM 是否相同？
  • 为什么重要：MurJ 是细菌细胞壁合成的必需蛋白，是潜在抗菌靶点；理解噬菌体来源的 Sgl 蛋白如何抑制 MurJ，可为开发新型抗菌策略提供结构基础。
  • 现有方法为何不足：此前仅有 LysM/MurJ 复合物结构，SglPP7 虽经遗传筛选提示靶向 MurJ，但其结构机制未知；SglPP7 与 LysM 无序列相似性，无法通过同源建模推断结合模式。
  • 精确研究问题：Can SglPP7 inhibit MurJ through a binding mode distinct from that of LysM, and if so, what is the structural basis?

04 背景与发展脉络

  • 阶段一：Sgl 蛋白的发现与功能鉴定。蛋白 E（ΦX174）抑制 MraY，蛋白 A2（Qβ）抑制 MurA，LysM（噬菌体 M）靶向 MurJ。优点：证明 Sgl 蛋白可作为「蛋白抗生素」。局限：结构机制多数未知。
  • 阶段二：MurJ 的结构与转运机制研究。MurJ 为 14 次跨膜蛋白，N-lobe（TM1–6）、C-lobe（TM7–12）、TM13–14，通过 alternating-access 机制转运 lipid II，类似 MATE 转运蛋白。优点：明确了构象循环框架。局限：抑制剂如何锁定特定构象态尚不清楚。
  • 阶段三：MurJ/LysM 复合物 cryo-EM 结构解析。LysM 楔入 MurJ outward-facing 构象，阻止构象变化。优点：首个 MurJ-Sgl 复合物结构。局限：仅一个例子，无法判断是否为通用机制。
  • 阶段四（本文位置）：解析 MurJ/SglPP7 复合物结构，与 LysM 和 SglCJ3 比较，提出趋同进化假说。本文主张：序列分歧的 Sgl 蛋白通过不同结合模式（cavity-occupying / surface-binding / cleft-binding）稳定同一构象态。

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：利用 cryo-EM 解析 MurJ/SglPP7 复合物结构（3.2 Å），结合体内裂解实验、突变分析和 MD 模拟，阐明 SglPP7 的抑制机制，并与 LysM 和 SglCJ3 结构比较。
  2. 核心洞察：SglPP7 通过其跨膜螺旋沿 MurJ 的 TM7 形成广泛接触面，并将 C 端尾部（residues 50–55，关键残基 W53）深深插入 C-lobe 内部腔体（由 TM7、TM8、TM10、TM12 形成），将 MurJ 锁定在 outward-facing 构象。这一「embedded tail」机制与 LysM 的「surface-binding」和 SglCJ3 的「cleft-binding」截然不同，但三者均稳定同一构象态，提示趋同进化。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：序列高度分歧的蛋白可通过完全不同的结合模式靶向同一蛋白的同一功能态；AlphaFold3 在膜蛋白构象态预测上可能存在系统性偏差（偏好 PDB 中占多数的构象态），实验结构仍不可替代；MD 模拟可作为验证实验结构稳定性和构象动力学的补充工具。

07 方法总览

  • 输入：SglPP7 基因序列（codon-optimized for E. coli）、E. coli MurJ 序列、LysM 和 SglCJ3 已知结构。
  • 输出：MurJ/SglPP7 复合物 cryo-EM 结构（3.2 Å）、裂解活性数据、MD 模拟轨迹、结构比较结果。
  • 模块：
  • 体内裂解实验（lysis assay）：评估 SglPP7 及其突变体的毒性。
  • 蛋白表达与纯化：共表达 Met-Ala-EcMurJ2-511-3×FLAG、Met-His10-SglPP7、BsAmj，Ni-NTA 纯化，Superose 6 凝胶过滤。
  • Nanodisc 重构：MSP1D1 + E. coli 磷脂，摩尔比 1.4:1:120。
  • Cryo-EM 数据收集与处理：CRYO ARM 300，K3 检测器，cryoSPARC 处理，3.2 Å 分辨率。
  • 模型构建与精修：AlphaFold3 预测 SglPP7 结构 + MurJ/LysM 复合物中 MurJ outward 模型（PDB 9UKV）docking 入 map，Coot 手动修改，PHENIX real_space_refine。
  • MD 模拟：YASARA dynamics，AMBER14 力场，POPE/POPG 膜，0.9% NaCl，120 ns，3 次独立模拟。
  • 突变分析：N 端截短、C 端截短、W53 突变、alanine scanning。
  • 训练：不适用（无机器学习模型训练）。
  • 工具：cryoSPARC v4.6.2–v5.0.6、UCSF ChimeraX、Coot、PHENIX、PyMOL、YASARA、AlphaFold3。
  • 反馈回路：结构观察 → 突变设计 → 裂解实验验证 → MD 模拟验证构象稳定性 → 结构比较。
  • 假设：SglPP7 与 LysM 结合模式不同；C 端尾部插入对功能至关重要；MD 可捕捉 SglPP7 对 MurJ 构象动力学的稳定作用。
  • 文字流程：基因合成 → 质粒构建 → 共表达纯化 → Nanodisc 重构 → cryo-EM 数据收集 → 数据处理与 3D 重构 → 模型构建与精修 → 结构分析 → 突变体设计与裂解实验 → MD 模拟 → 与 LysM/SglCJ3 结构比较 → 提出工作模型。

09 关键公式符号

  • Cα RMSD：衡量两个结构之间 Cα 原子位置的均方根偏差。用途：比较 MurJ/SglPP7 与 MurJ/LysM 等复合物中 MurJ 构象的相似性。直觉：值越小，构象越相似。来源：Results 节，Fig 4A，S2D Fig，S7 Fig。
  • Cα–Cα distance：两个残基 Cα 原子之间的距离。用途：监测 MD 模拟中特定跨膜螺旋间距离变化（Y266–L247 和 G360–G415）。直觉：距离减小表示螺旋间靠近，距离维持表示结构稳定。来源：Fig 3C–3D。
  • 无其他理解必需的公式。

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围：本文结论限于 E. coli MurJ 与噬菌体 PP7 SglPP7 的复合物，以及与其他 Sgl 蛋白的结构比较。
  • Oracle/真值输入：cryo-EM map 为实验测定，非 oracle；AlphaFold3 预测仅用于初始模型搭建，最终模型经实验 map 精修。
  • 端到端状态：从基因到结构到功能验证的完整链条，但 MD 模拟为 120 ns，可能不足以捕捉慢速构象变化。
  • 算力成本：未提供。
  • 历史数据依赖：结构比较依赖 PDB 中已有的 MurJ/LysM 和 MurJ/SglCJ3 结构。
  • 模型依赖：AlphaFold3 用于 SglPP7 初始模型预测，但复合物界面预测失败。
  • 最难情形：SglPP7 N 端 1–25 区域在 cryo-EM map 中不可见，无法建模；脂质样密度无法确定分子种类。
  • 群体/领域边界：结论限于 E. coli MurJ 和特定噬菌体 Sgl 蛋白，未验证其他细菌或其他 MurJ 同源物。
  • 不确定性：趋同进化结论为「consistent with the possibility」，非确定性结论；MD 模拟仅 3 次独立运行，统计力度有限。
  • 有边界的复述：在 E. coli MurJ 与 SglPP7 的复合物中，SglPP7 通过跨膜螺旋沿 TM7 广泛接触并将 C 端尾部插入 C-lobe 腔体，稳定 MurJ 于 outward-facing 构象；该结合模式与 LysM 和 SglCJ3 不同，提示序列分歧的 Sgl 蛋白可能独立进化出不同策略抑制 MurJ。

Review setup

  • Input scope Full manuscript text, including abstract, introduction, results and discussion, methods, and supporting information references.
  • Assessment boundary Scientific content, technical soundness, structural and functional claims, and alignment with Nature-style criteria. Editorial or formatting issues are noted only where they affect comprehension.
  • Shared manuscript claim summary The authors report the cryo-EM structure of the E. coli lipid II flippase MurJ in complex with the phage lysis protein SglPP7 at 3.2 Å resolution. They propose that SglPP7 inhibits MurJ by binding along the intramembrane cleft and inserting its C-terminal tail into the internal cavity, stabilizing MurJ in an outward-facing conformation. Through structural comparison with LysM and SglC3, they suggest convergent evolution of distinct binding modes toward MurJ inhibition.
  • Visible evidence base Cryo-EM structure determination and refinement statistics (S1 Table, S1 Fig), lysis assays with truncation and point mutants (Figs 1D, 2C, S4, S5, S9), MD simulations (Fig 3), structural comparisons with previously reported complexes (Fig 4, S7, S8), and in vivo crosslinking data referenced from prior work.
  • Missing materials affecting confidence The cryo-EM map quality for the N-terminal region of SglPP7 (residues 1–25) is described as poor, and these residues are not modeled. The lipid-like feature is not identified at the molecular level. The MD simulation trajectories and convergence metrics are not fully described. The alanine-scanning data are presented only as a summary without quantitative details. The structural comparison with SglC3 relies on a previously reported structure, but the coordinate files and superposition details are not provided in the visible text.

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment This manuscript presents a technically sound cryo-EM structure of the MurJ/SglPP7 complex at 3.2 Å resolution, with supporting functional assays and MD simulations. The structural findings are clear and the comparison with LysM and SglC3 is informative. However, several claims regarding the mechanism of inhibition and the evolutionary interpretation are not fully supported by the presented evidence. The functional significance of the embedded tail, the role of the lipid-like feature, and the convergence argument require additional experimental or computational support. The manuscript is of interest to the structural biology and antimicrobial discovery communities, but the case for convergent evolution and the detailed inhibitory mechanism is not fully established from the provided materials.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Structural biologists studying membrane transporters and phage-host interactions, microbiologists investigating peptidoglycan biosynthesis and cell lysis mechanisms, and researchers developing new antibacterial strategies against drug-resistant pathogens. The structure provides a new example of how phage proteins target essential bacterial membrane proteins, which is relevant for protein antibiotic design.
  • Major strengths The cryo-EM structure is determined at a higher resolution than a recently reported comparable structure, using full-length SglPP7 in lipid nanodiscs. The functional assays with truncation and point mutants directly link the structural observations to lytic activity. The MD simulations provide a dynamic view of MurJ stabilization. The structural comparison across three Sgl proteins is a valuable addition to the field.

  • Axis Functional validation
  • Claim pointer The authors claim that the C-terminal embedded tail of SglPP7, particularly W53, is important for stabilizing the interaction with MurJ and that this is essential for inhibition.
  • Evidence pointer Figs 2A–2C, S5; location not provided for specific residue positions in the text.
  • Concern The mutational analysis shows that W53A retains full activity, and only W53K and W53R lose activity. The distinction between a specific interaction and a general steric or electrostatic effect is important for understanding the mechanism and for designing inhibitors.

Quantify lytic activity with a dose-response or time-course assay. Alternatively, perform MD simulations with the W53 mutants to show whether the tail remains inserted and whether MurJ stability is affected.
