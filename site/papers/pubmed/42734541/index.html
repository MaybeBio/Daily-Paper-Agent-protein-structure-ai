Exploring Protein Conformational Ensembles Using Evolutionary Conditional Diffusion.

Cui, Xinyue; Ge, Lingyu; Yang, Xinguang; Li, Xuhui; Hou, Dongliang; Zhou, Xiaogen; Zhang, Guijun
Journal of chemical information and modeling · 2026-09-14 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文提出基于进化条件扩散的DiffEnsemble框架，从静态蛋白结构学习潜在动态表征并生成构象系综，在ATLAS基准上优于现有方法，直接命中蛋白质构象采样与生成这一核心计算问题。

摘要

Protein conformational ensembles encode the dynamic landscapes underlying biological function, regulation, and allostery. Accurately reconstructing such ensembles while balancing the accuracy of conformational distributions and physical plausibility remains a fundamental challenge in structural biology, particularly when dynamic data are scarce. Here, we propose DiffEnsemble, a diffusion-based framework designed for modeling protein conformational ensembles. DiffEnsemble learns latent dynamical representations from static protein structures in the Protein Data Bank and integrates the structural profile derived from the AlphaFold Protein Structure Database as conditional guidance during the diffusion process. Benchmarking on 72 protein targets from the ATLAS molecular dynamics simulation data set demonstrates that DiffEnsemble outperforms existing methods, including BioEmu and AlphaFLOW. Compared with AlphaFLOW, DiffEnsemble achieves improvements of 28.9% and 7.5% in Pearson correlation coefficients for ensemble pairwise root-mean-square deviation and root-mean-square fluctuation, respectively. The results demonstrate that latent dynamical information embedded in static structural data can effectively support the modeling of protein conformational ensembles.

摘要翻译

蛋白质构象系综编码了支撑生物功能、调控和别构效应的动态景观。在平衡构象分布的准确性与物理合理性的同时，精确重建此类系综仍是结构生物学中的一项基本挑战，尤其是在动态数据稀缺的情况下。在此，我们提出DiffEnsemble，一个基于扩散的框架，旨在建模蛋白质构象系综。DiffEnsemble从蛋白质数据银行（Protein Data Bank）中的静态蛋白质结构学习潜在动态表征，并将AlphaFold蛋白质结构数据库（AlphaFold Protein Structure Database）导出的结构谱作为扩散过程中的条件引导进行整合。在来自ATLAS分子动力学模拟数据集的72个蛋白质靶标上的基准测试表明，DiffEnsemble优于现有方法，包括BioEmu和AlphaFLOW。与AlphaFLOW相比，DiffEnsemble在系综成对均方根偏差和均方根涨落的皮尔逊相关系数上分别实现了28.9%和7.5%的提升。结果表明，静态结构数据中嵌入的潜在动态信息能有效支持蛋白质构象系综的建模。

  • 现有方法为何不足：传统 MD 模拟计算成本高且难以充分采样；现有生成式方法（如 BioEmu、AlphaFLOW）或依赖大规模 MD 训练数据，或需要显式物理约束，在动态数据稀缺时性能受限。
  • 精确研究问题：Can latent dynamical information embedded in static protein structures (PDB + AlphaFold) serve as sufficient conditional signal for diffusion models to generate accurate conformational ensembles without explicit MD training data?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：DiffEnsemble 是一个条件扩散模型，以 AlphaFold 结构特征为条件引导，从 PDB 静态结构中学习潜在动力学表示，生成蛋白质构象系综。
  2. 核心洞察：静态蛋白质结构数据库中（PDB）隐含着丰富的进化保守的动力学信息——不同物种/同源蛋白的静态结构差异编码了构象变化的潜在模式；AlphaFold 数据库中的结构特征可作为有效的条件信号，引导扩散过程生成物理上合理的构象系综，而无需显式 MD 数据。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：静态数据（如 PDB 快照）中可能编码了动态信息，通过合适的表示学习方法可以提取并用于动态建模；条件扩散模型可以灵活整合多种结构特征作为引导信号，为「数据稀缺条件下的构象建模」提供新范式。

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围：仅针对 72 个 ATLAS 蛋白靶标的构象系综生成，未覆盖所有蛋白类型或更大规模测试集。
  • oracle/真值输入：使用 AlphaFold 结构特征作为条件引导，但 AlphaFold 本身是预测结构，可能引入预测误差。
  • 端到端状态：方法端到端可运行，但依赖 PDB 预训练和 AlphaFold 特征提取，非完全从序列直接生成。
  • 算力成本：未提供训练和推理的计算成本信息。
  • 历史数据依赖：依赖 PDB 静态结构的多样性和 AlphaFold 数据库的覆盖度。
  • 模型依赖：依赖扩散模型的生成质量和条件编码器的表达能力。
  • 最难情形：未讨论高度动态蛋白、 intrinsically disordered proteins 或多结构域蛋白的挑战。
  • 不确定性：仅报告 Pearson 相关系数，未提供误差条、统计显著性检验或绝对误差指标。

14 学到什么

Agent 提炼的知识候选：

  1. 静态结构中的潜在动力学提取：PDB 中大量静态结构（不同物种、不同配体状态）可能编码进化保守的构象变化模式。可迁移到本课题：在缺乏 MD 数据时，利用 PDB 同源结构集合学习构象变化的潜在表示，作为 MD 模拟或构象生成的先验。
  2. AlphaFold 结构特征作为条件引导：将 AlphaFold 预测的结构特征（而非仅序列）作为扩散模型的条件信号，可提供目标蛋白的结构上下文。可迁移到本课题：在构象生成、分子对接或结构设计中，将 AlphaFold 特征作为条件输入，增强生成结构的目标特异性。
  3. 条件扩散模型用于系综生成：扩散模型天然适合生成多样化的构象集合，通过条件控制可平衡多样性与物理合理性。可迁移到本课题：在构象采样、构象生成任务中，采用条件扩散框架替代传统 MD 或自回归生成。
  4. 评测指标选择：使用系综级 RMSD 和 RMSF 的 Pearson 相关系数评估生成系综与 MD 参考的一致性。可迁移到本课题：在构象生成方法评测中，同时报告分布级和残基级指标，避免单一指标误导。
  5. 无 MD 数据的构象建模范式：本文展示了在无显式 MD 训练数据条件下，通过静态结构 + 进化信息进行构象建模的可行性。可迁移到本课题：对于缺乏实验动力学数据的目标蛋白，可探索类似「静态结构 → 潜在动力学 → 构象生成」的 pipeline。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a potentially interesting application of diffusion models to protein conformational ensemble prediction, with a conceptually appealing idea of leveraging static structure databases to infer dynamical properties. However, the evidence provided in the abstract alone is insufficient to evaluate the technical soundness, statistical rigor, or generalizability of the claims. The reported performance improvements are presented without confidence intervals, statistical tests, or methodological details, and the absence of any description of the model architecture, training procedure, or evaluation protocol prevents independent assessment. The core claim that latent dynamical information in static structures can effectively support ensemble modeling is plausible but not established from the supplied material.

The work addresses a recognized challenge in reconstructing conformational ensembles when experimental dynamic data are scarce, and the proposed approach of using static structure databases as a source of dynamical information could be of practical value if validated.
  • Major strengths The conceptual framing is clear and addresses a relevant problem in structural biology. The use of AlphaFold-derived structural profiles as conditional guidance is a sensible and timely integration of existing resources. The choice of benchmarking against established methods such as BioEmu and AlphaFLOW on the ATLAS dataset provides a reasonable comparative context.

Major Concerns

  • Concern ID R1-M1
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Statistical rigor
  • Claim pointer The claim that DiffEnsemble outperforms BioEmu and AlphaFLOW, with specific improvements of 28.9% and 7.5% in Pearson correlation coefficients for ensemble pairwise root-mean-square deviation and root-mean-square fluctuation, respectively.
  • Evidence pointer Abstract, location not provided
  • Concern The performance improvements are reported as single point estimates without any indication of variance, confidence intervals, or statistical significance testing. It is unclear whether these improvements are consistent across the 72 targets or driven by a subset. No information is provided on how the Pearson correlations were computed, whether they are averaged per target or pooled, or whether the differences are statistically meaningful.
  • Why it matters Without measures of uncertainty or statistical testing, the reported improvements cannot be distinguished from noise. The claim of superiority over existing methods is central to the paper's contribution, and unsupported performance claims undermine the credibility of the entire study.

  • Resolution test Provide confidence intervals or standard errors for the reported metrics, perform appropriate statistical tests (for example paired tests across the 72 targets), and report per-target distributions of the correlation coefficients. If the improvements are not uniformly observed, discuss the conditions under which DiffEnsemble performs better or worse.
