A Novel Mechanistic Route to the Formation of an Internal Aldimine between Pyridoxal 5'-Phosphate and Ornithine Decarboxylase Enzyme at Its Active Site.

Rastogi, Shreya; Chandra, Amalendu
The journal of physical chemistry. B · 2026-10-23 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文通过QM/MM分子动力学模拟和增强采样方法，研究了PLP依赖酶ODC活性位点内部醛亚胺形成的反应机制，属于蛋白质结构相关的酶催化反应计算模拟，方法上采用物理模拟，对象和问题均聚焦于蛋白质活性位点结构，但更偏重反应机理而非结构预测/采样/生成等核心方向。

摘要

Pyridoxal 5'-phosphate (PLP) functions as a vital cofactor in numerous biochemical processes. To perform its role as a cofactor, PLP has to first form a Schiff base complex with the enzyme, known as the internal aldimine, which is an important step for the function of all PLP dependent enzymes. In this study, we investigate the reaction mechanism of this process at the active site of a PLP-dependent enzyme, namely ornithine decarboxylase (ODC), using QM/MM molecular dynamics simulations combined with enhanced sampling methods. The reaction starts with the nucleophilic attack of the side chain amino group of Lys69 on the aldehyde carbon of PLP, followed by proton transfer that leads to the formation of the carbinolamine intermediate. This intermediate subsequently undergoes dehydration, resulting in the generation of a water molecule and the internal aldimine. Our mechanistic study reveals that the nucleophilic attack is almost spontaneous with a very low free energy barrier of 0.5 kcal/mol. The subsequent proton transfer to the phenolic oxygen from Lys69 NZ follows a novel pathway, facilitated by the rotation of the dihedral angle Phi(NZ-C4A-C4-C3). This step is found to have a free energy barrier of 11.6 kcal/mol, which makes it the rate-determining step of the overall reaction. The dehydration step, which involves the transfer of a proton from the phenolic oxygen to the aldehyde oxygen and breaking of the C4A-O4A bond that results in the formation of a water molecule, is also found to have a low free energy barrier of 1.6 kcal/mol. This step leads to the formation of the internal aldimine with a free energy difference of 15.4 kcal/mol. The current study elucidates a novel reaction pathway and the associated free energetics at the active site of ODC and provides valuable insights into the catalytic mechanism of internal aldimine formation for PLP-dependent enzymes.

摘要翻译

磷酸吡哆醛（PLP）作为多种生化过程中的关键辅因子发挥作用。为执行其辅因子功能，PLP必须首先与酶形成希夫碱复合物，即内部醛亚胺，这是所有PLP依赖酶功能的重要步骤。在本研究中，我们利用QM/MM分子动力学模拟结合增强采样方法，探讨了PLP依赖酶——鸟氨酸脱羧酶（ODC）活性位点上该过程的反应机制。反应始于Lys69侧链氨基对PLP醛碳的亲核攻击，随后发生质子转移，形成甲醇胺中间体。该中间体随后经历脱水，生成一个水分子和内部醛亚胺。我们的机理研究表明，亲核攻击几乎是自发的，自由能垒极低，仅为0.5 kcal/mol。随后的质子从Lys69 NZ转移至酚氧遵循一条新途径，由二面角Phi(NZ-C4A-C4-C3)的旋转促进。该步骤的自由能垒为11.6 kcal/mol，使其成为整个反应的限速步骤。脱水步骤涉及质子从酚氧转移至醛氧并断裂C4A-O4A键，从而生成水分子，其自由能垒也较低，为1.6 kcal/mol。该步骤导致内部醛亚胺的形成，自由能差为15.4 kcal/mol。本研究阐明了ODC活性位点上一条新的反应途径及其相关自由能特性，为PLP依赖酶内部醛亚胺形成的催化机制提供了宝贵见解。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题: PLP 依赖酶中，PLP 辅因子如何与活性位点赖氨酸残基形成 internal aldimine（Schiff 碱）？其反应路径、中间体、能垒和决速步是什么？
  • 为什么重要: Internal aldimine 形成是所有 PLP 依赖酶发挥催化功能的前提步骤；理解其机理有助于设计酶抑制剂（如 ODC 抑制剂用于抗癌/抗寄生虫）和工程化 PLP 依赖酶。
  • 现有方法不足: 实验上难以捕获瞬态中间体；以往计算研究多采用静态 QM/MM 优化或简单 MD，未充分采样构象空间，且未揭示质子转移与二面角旋转的耦合机制。
  • 精确研究问题: Can QM/MM MD with enhanced sampling reveal a complete, thermodynamically consistent reaction pathway for internal aldimine formation in ODC, including the rate-determining step and its structural determinant?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法: 对 ODC 活性位点进行 QM/MM MD 模拟，结合增强采样（推测为 metadynamics 或 umbrella sampling）计算三步反应（亲核进攻、质子转移、脱水）的自由能面。
  2. 核心洞察: 质子转移步骤并非简单的直接转移，而是由 Lys69 侧链二面角 Φ(NZ-C4A-C4-C3) 旋转所介导的新颖路径；该旋转降低了质子转移的能垒，使其成为决速步（11.6 kcal/mol），而亲核进攻（0.5 kcal/mol）和脱水（1.6 kcal/mol）几乎无垒。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]: 酶活性位点中，氨基酸侧链的构象自由度（如二面角旋转）可与化学键断裂/形成强耦合，从而改变反应路径和能垒。这提示在计算酶机理时，不能仅考虑静态构象，必须充分采样侧链构象空间。

  1. 名称: PLP 酶家族 internal aldimine 形成机理的比较研究 - 来源局限/观察: 本文仅针对 ODC，未验证其他 PLP 酶是否遵循相同路径。 - 核心假设: 不同 PLP 酶中，决速步可能不同（取决于活性位点静电和构象限制）。 - 初步方法: 对 5-10 个 PLP 酶（如 AAT, Tryptophan synthase, DOPA decarboxylase）进行 QM/MM MD + metadynamics，比较自由能剖面。 - 验证方式: 与实验 kcat/KM 和突变数据对比。 - 创新状态: unverified

  2. 名称: 机器学习势加速的 PLP 酶反应路径搜索 - 来源局限/观察: QM/MM MD 计算成本高，难以进行长时间采样或多体系比较。 - 核心假设: 用 ML 势（如 MACE-MP, ANI-2x）替代 QM 区域，可加速采样且保持精度。 - 初步方法: 训练/微调 ML 势于 PLP 反应相关构象，进行长时间 MD 和增强采样。 - 验证方式: 对比 ML 势与 DFT 参考的能垒和路径。 - 创新状态: unverified

  3. 名称: 二面角 Φ(NZ-C4A-C4-C3) 作为通用反应集体变量的验证 - 来源局限/观察: 本文发现该二面角是关键，但未验证其是否在其他 Schiff 碱形成反应中重要。 - 核心假设: 该二面角在多种 PLP 酶和模型反应中均控制质子转移。 - 初步方法: 对转氨酶、消旋酶等进行类似采样，分析二面角分布与能垒相关性。 - 验证方式: 计算不同二面角约束下的能垒变化。 - 创新状态: unverified

  4. 名称: 基于 internal aldimine 形成能垒的 PLP 酶抑制剂虚拟筛选 - 来源局限/观察: ODC 是药物靶点，但现有抑制剂多靶向底物结合位点。 - 核心假设: 靶向 Lys69 亲核进攻或质子转移步骤的化合物可更有效抑制酶。 - 初步方法: 用本文的自由能面信息设计过渡态类似物，进行分子对接和 MD 验证。 - 验证方式: 合成候选化合物并测试酶活性。 - 创新状态: unverified

  5. 名称: AlphaFold 结构 + QM/MM 的 PLP 酶机理预测流水线 - 来源局限/观察: 本文依赖实验结构，但许多 PLP 酶无晶体结构。 - 核心假设: AlphaFold 预测结构可作为 QM/MM 模拟的可靠起点。 - 初步方法: 对无实验结构的 PLP 酶用 AlphaFold 预测，然后进行 QM/MM MD 反应路径分析。 - 验证方式: 对已知结构酶进行回测，比较预测与实验结构的结果。 - 创新状态: unverified

审稿人评审

Review setup

  • Input scope Abstract only
  • Assessment boundary The scientific claims and evidence presented in the abstract, without access to the full manuscript, figures, tables, or supplementary materials
  • Shared manuscript claim summary The authors propose a novel mechanistic pathway for internal aldimine formation between pyridoxal 5'-phosphate (PLP) and ornithine decarboxylase (ODC) at the enzyme active site, based on QM/MM molecular dynamics simulations with enhanced sampling. They report free energy barriers for three steps: nucleophilic attack (0.5 kcal/mol), proton transfer (11.6 kcal/mol, rate-determining), and dehydration (1.6 kcal/mol), with an overall free energy difference of 15.4 kcal/mol for product formation. The novelty is attributed to a dihedral rotation (Phi(NZ-C4A-C4-C3)) facilitating proton transfer.
  • Visible evidence base Abstract text only; no figures, tables, methods details, or simulation parameters are provided
  • Missing materials affecting confidence Full manuscript, computational methods (QM/MM level of theory, basis sets, enhanced sampling technique, force field parameters, system setup, equilibration protocol), free energy profiles, error estimates, convergence criteria, and validation against experimental or prior computational data

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a plausible computational study of a biologically important reaction, but the claims of novelty and mechanistic detail cannot be adequately evaluated from the abstract alone. The reported free energy barriers are surprisingly low for a rate-determining step in an enzymatic reaction, and the lack of methodological detail and validation undermines confidence in the quantitative conclusions. The work may be of interest to computational enzymologists, but the current evidence base is insufficient to establish the case.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Computational chemists and biochemists studying PLP-dependent enzymes, enzymatic reaction mechanisms, and QM/MM simulation methodologies. The proposed novel proton transfer pathway could inform future mechanistic studies of similar enzymes, but the interest is contingent on the validity of the simulations.
  • Major strengths The study addresses a fundamental step in PLP-dependent enzyme catalysis, which is of broad biochemical relevance. The use of QM/MM molecular dynamics with enhanced sampling is appropriate for capturing dynamic effects in enzymatic reactions. The identification of a specific dihedral rotation as a mechanistic feature provides a testable hypothesis.
