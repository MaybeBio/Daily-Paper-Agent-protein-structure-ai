Xanthine Oxidase Inhibitory Peptides from Sesame 11S Globulin Target the FAD Domain: Kinetics, Molecular Dynamics, and Validation in a Zebrafish Hyperuricemia Model.

Li, Mei-Ling; Huang, Shang-Ming; Lin, Yu-Shun; Wu, Pei-Yu; Chen, Yu-Hsuan; Chuang, Yi-Jing; Yang, Jiann-Jou; Hsu, Kuo-Chiang
Journal of agricultural and food chemistry · 2026-09-09 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文从芝麻蛋白中鉴定出抑制黄嘌呤氧化酶的肽，通过分子对接和分子动力学模拟研究其与FAD结构域的相互作用，虽以蛋白质为对象并使用了计算模拟方法，但核心是食品功能肽的酶抑制活性，而非蛋白质结构预测、构象采样或设计等核心计算问题，方法贡献较常规。

摘要

Food-derived bioactive peptides are promising alternatives to conventional xanthine oxidase (XO) inhibitors, yet individual XO inhibitory peptides from sesame protein remain uncharacterized. Here, sesame and peanut protein isolates were screened with four proteases; the trypsin hydrolyzate of sesame (TSH, 120 min) showed the highest XO inhibition (47.16%). Eight 11S globulin peptides were identified by nanoUHPLC-ESI-Q-TOF MS/MS. QGDIVAIPSGAAHW (IC50 = 467.2 muM) and AFYLAGGVPR (IC50 = 536.1 muM) exhibited mixed-type inhibition (alpha > 1). Molecular docking against two XO crystal structures (3NVY, 3NRZ) consistently favored the FAD domain, and 500 ns molecular dynamics simulations with MM-GBSA analysis provided convergent support (DeltaG = -160 to -317 kJ mol-1). In a zebrafish hyperuricemia model, TSH reduced uric acid and XO activity while upregulating purine salvage (hprt1) and urate transport (oat1) genes. These findings support sesame-derived peptides as functional food candidates with preferential association with the XO FAD domain.

摘要翻译

食品源生物活性肽是传统黄嘌呤氧化酶（xanthine oxidase, XO）抑制剂的有前景替代品，但芝麻蛋白中单独的XO抑制肽尚未被表征。本研究使用四种蛋白酶对芝麻和花生蛋白分离物进行筛选；芝麻胰蛋白酶水解物（TSH，120分钟）显示出最高的XO抑制率（47.16%）。通过nanoUHPLC-ESI-Q-TOF MS/MS鉴定出八种11S球蛋白肽。QGDIVAIPSGAAHW（IC50 = 467.2 μM）和AFYLAGGVPR（IC50 = 536.1 μM）表现出混合型抑制（α > 1）。针对两种XO晶体结构（3NVY、3NRZ）的分子对接一致倾向于FAD结构域，500 ns分子动力学（molecular dynamics）模拟结合MM-GBSA分析提供了收敛性支持（ΔG = -160至-317 kJ mol⁻¹）。在斑马鱼高尿酸血症模型中，TSH降低了尿酸水平和XO活性，同时上调了嘌呤补救（hprt1）和尿酸盐转运（oat1）基因。这些发现支持芝麻源肽作为功能性食品候选物，其优先与XO的FAD结构域结合。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题: 芝麻蛋白中是否存在有效的黄嘌呤氧化酶（XO）抑制肽？这些肽如何与XO相互作用（结合位点、动力学类型）？在体内是否有效？
  • 为什么重要: 高尿酸血症是痛风的关键风险因素，XO是治疗靶点。食品来源的抑制肽是传统药物（如别嘌醇）的潜在安全替代品，但芝麻来源的XO抑制肽尚未被表征。
  • 现有方法为何不足: 传统XO抑制剂（如别嘌醇）有副作用；食品来源肽的鉴定通常缺乏对结合机制（特别是结构域偏好）的深入分子层面理解，且体内验证不足。
  • 精确的「Can ... ?」研究问题: Can sesame 11S globulin-derived peptides inhibit xanthine oxidase by preferentially binding to its FAD domain, and can this inhibition be validated in a zebrafish hyperuricemia model?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法: 通过蛋白酶水解芝麻蛋白，经质谱鉴定肽序列，再通过分子对接、分子动力学模拟和体内斑马鱼模型验证。
  2. 核心洞察: 两种抑制肽（QGDIVAIPSGAAHW和AFYLAGGVPR）并非与XO的经典活性位点（钼蝶呤中心）结合，而是优先与FAD结构域结合，且表现为混合型抑制（α > 1）。这表明抑制机制可能涉及干扰FAD的电子传递功能，而非直接竞争底物结合。
  3. [Analysis] 可能的普适教训: 对于多肽类酶抑制剂，其结合位点可能偏离传统小分子抑制剂的活性位点（如辅因子结合域）。在蛋白质结构计算研究中，应系统扫描整个蛋白表面（包括辅因子结合域），而不仅限于活性位点，以发现新的抑制机制。

07 方法总览

  • 输入: 芝麻蛋白分离物、四种蛋白酶（胰蛋白酶、胃蛋白酶等）、XO酶、斑马鱼
  • 输出: 鉴定出的抑制肽序列、IC50值、抑制类型、结合自由能、体内尿酸水平
  • 模块: 1. 蛋白水解与活性筛选: 芝麻和花生蛋白经四种蛋白酶水解，筛选XO抑制活性最高的水解物（TSH，120 min胰蛋白酶水解物，抑制率47.16%）。 2. 肽鉴定: 通过nanoUHPLC-ESI-Q-TOF MS/MS从TSH中鉴定出8种11S球蛋白肽。 3. 体外抑制动力学: 合成候选肽，测定IC50，通过Lineweaver-Burk图确定抑制类型（混合型，α > 1）。 4. 分子对接: 使用AutoDock Vina将肽对接至XO的两个晶体结构（3NVY, 3NRZ），发现一致偏好FAD结构域。 5. 分子动力学模拟: 对最佳对接复合物进行500 ns MD模拟，使用MM-GBSA计算结合自由能（ΔG = -160 to -317 kJ mol⁻¹）。 6. 体内验证: 在斑马鱼高尿酸血症模型中，TSH处理组降低尿酸和XO活性，上调hprt1和oat1基因表达。
  • 训练: 不适用（无机器学习）
  • 工具: nanoUHPLC-ESI-Q-TOF MS/MS, AutoDock Vina, GROMACS (推测), MM-GBSA
  • 反馈回路: 无显式反馈回路
  • 假设: 分子对接和MD模拟能准确反映肽与XO的结合模式；斑马鱼模型能模拟人类高尿酸血症。
  • 文字流程: 芝麻蛋白 → 胰蛋白酶水解 → 筛选高活性水解物(TSH) → 质谱鉴定肽序列 → 合成候选肽 → 体外酶抑制动力学测试 → 分子对接预测结合位点(FAD域) → MD模拟验证稳定性与能量 → 斑马鱼体内模型验证降尿酸效果。

09 关键公式符号

  • Lineweaver-Burk方程 (混合型抑制):
  • 公式: ( \frac{1}{v} = \frac{K_m}{V_{max}} \left(1 + \frac{[I]}{K_i}\right) \frac{1}{[S]} + \frac{1}{V_{max}} \left(1 + \frac{[I]}{\alpha K_i}\right) )
  • 符号含义: v=反应速率, [S]=底物浓度, [I]=抑制剂浓度, Km=米氏常数, Vmax=最大速率, Ki=抑制常数, α=抑制类型参数(α>1为混合型)
  • 用途: 确定抑制类型和计算α值
  • 直觉: α>1表示抑制剂与酶的结合影响底物结合，反之亦然

  • 来源: Methods节（Lineweaver-Burk图分析）

  • MM-GBSA结合自由能:

  • 公式: ( \Delta G_{bind} = \Delta E_{MM} + \Delta G_{solv} - T\Delta S )
  • 符号含义: ΔG_bind=结合自由能, ΔE_MM=分子力学能量(范德华+静电), ΔG_solv=溶剂化能(极性和非极性), TΔS=熵变
  • 用途: 估算肽与XO的结合强度
  • 直觉: 负值越大，结合越强
  • 来源: Methods节（MM-GBSA分析）

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围: 仅针对芝麻11S球蛋白来源的两种肽对XO的抑制作用，不涵盖其他蛋白或肽。
  • oracle/真值输入: 分子对接依赖XO晶体结构（3NVY, 3NRZ），这些结构是实验确定的，但可能不反映溶液中的所有构象。
  • 端到端状态: 从蛋白水解到体内验证的完整流程，但体内仅测试了水解物（TSH），而非纯肽。
  • 算力成本: 500 ns MD模拟需要一定计算资源，但未提供具体信息。
  • 历史数据依赖: 无。
  • 模型依赖: 分子对接和MD模拟结果依赖于力场和评分函数的选择。
  • 最难情形: 未测试肽在复杂生理环境（如消化酶、血清蛋白结合）下的稳定性。
  • 群体/领域边界: 结论限于芝麻蛋白和XO酶，不直接推广至其他酶或蛋白。
  • 不确定性: IC50值在微摩尔级别，活性中等；体内效果仅基于水解物，纯肽效果未知。
  • 有边界的复述: 本文证明，从芝麻11S球蛋白中鉴定出的两种肽（QGDIVAIPSGAAHW和AFYLAGGVPR）在体外以混合型方式抑制XO，分子模拟表明它们优先结合FAD结构域，且含这些肽的芝麻胰蛋白酶水解物在斑马鱼模型中能降低尿酸水平。

14 学到什么

Agent 提炼的知识候选

  1. 可迁移概念: 辅因子结构域作为肽类抑制剂的靶点 - 描述: 本文发现肽抑制剂优先结合XO的FAD结构域，而非经典活性位点。这提示在蛋白质结构计算研究中，应系统扫描整个蛋白表面，特别是辅因子结合域，作为潜在的变构或竞争性抑制位点。 - 如何迁移到本课题: 在针对其他酶（如激酶、氧化还原酶）的抑制剂设计或筛选时，可将其辅因子（如NADH、ATP）结合域作为对接和MD模拟的候选区域。

  2. 可迁移方法: 分子对接+MD模拟+MM-GBSA的联合验证流程 - 描述: 先通过对接快速筛选结合位点，再用长时间MD模拟（500 ns）评估稳定性，最后用MM-GBSA量化结合能。这种多层级计算验证策略提高了预测的可信度。 - 如何迁移到本课题: 在蛋白质-配体相互作用研究中，可建立类似流程：对接 → 短MD平衡 → 长MD生产 → MM/PBSA或MM/GBSA分析。对于AI方法，可用此流程生成训练数据或验证预测。

  3. 可迁移实验设计: 体内模型验证水解物而非纯肽 - 描述: 作者先用体外实验鉴定活性肽，但体内验证时使用整个水解物。这虽然降低了归因能力，但更贴近实际应用场景（食品功能）。 - 如何迁移到本课题: 在评估多肽混合物（如蛋白水解物、发酵产物）的生物活性时，可先通过计算预测活性成分，再在体内验证混合物效果，最后用纯肽进行归因实验。

15 与已有知识连接

  • 相似工作: 已有研究从乳清、大豆、鱼皮等来源鉴定XO抑制肽（如文献[1,2]），但本文首次聚焦芝麻11S球蛋白，并系统揭示FAD域结合偏好。
  • 可迁移领域: 本文的分子模拟流程（对接+MD+MM-GBSA）与药物发现中的标准虚拟筛选流程高度相似，可迁移至其他酶靶点（如ACE、DPP-IV）的抑制剂发现。
  • 冲突点: 传统观点认为XO抑制剂主要与钼蝶呤中心（活性位点）竞争底物结合。本文发现FAD域结合，提示存在新的抑制机制，可能与干扰电子传递有关。这挑战了仅关注活性位点的筛选策略。
  • 候选方向: 结合AlphaFold2预测的蛋白结构进行全表面对接扫描，可能发现更多非经典结合位点。

Agent 生成的研究候选

  2. 名称: 肽抑制剂结合FAD域的电子传递干扰机制验证 - 来源局限/观察: 本文提出肽结合FAD域可能干扰电子传递，但未提供直接证据（如光谱或电化学数据）。 - 核心假设: 肽与FAD域的结合会改变FAD的氧化还原电位或电子传递效率，从而抑制XO活性。 - 相对本文的增量: 从结构预测深入到机制验证。 - 初步方法: 1) 使用紫外-可见光谱监测FAD在肽存在下的吸收变化；2) 使用循环伏安法测量FAD的氧化还原电位变化；3) 结合QM/MM计算模拟电子传递路径的变化。 - 验证方式: 光谱或电化学信号变化与抑制活性（IC50）的相关性。 - 可能的失败模式: 肽可能不直接干扰电子传递，而是通过构象变化间接影响；实验条件与生理环境差异大。 - 创新状态: unverified

Review setup

  • Input scope Full manuscript (abstract only provided)
  • Assessment boundary Claims and evidence as presented in the abstract
  • Shared manuscript claim summary The authors report the identification and characterization of two xanthine oxidase (XO) inhibitory peptides (QGDIVAIPSGAAHW and AFYLAGGVPR) from sesame 11S globulin trypsin hydrolyzate, demonstrating mixed-type inhibition, preferential binding to the FAD domain of XO via molecular docking and MD simulations, and in vivo efficacy in a zebrafish hyperuricemia model.
  • Visible evidence base Abstract text only; no figures, tables, methods, or supplementary materials provided
  • Missing materials affecting confidence Full manuscript (methods, results, figures, tables, supplementary data), experimental details for kinetics, docking, MD simulations, and zebrafish model

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a potentially interesting discovery of food-derived XO inhibitory peptides from sesame with a novel FAD-domain targeting mechanism. However, the evidence base is too limited to evaluate the rigor of the claims. Critical details on peptide identification, kinetic analysis, computational validation, and in vivo experimental design are absent. The claim of preferential FAD domain binding requires stronger experimental support beyond computational predictions.

  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers in food chemistry, nutraceuticals, and functional foods, particularly those studying bioactive peptides for hyperuricemia management. The FAD-domain targeting mechanism may also interest structural biologists studying XO inhibition.

  • Major strengths 1. Identification of novel XO inhibitory peptides from an understudied source (sesame). 2. Multi-level characterization from in vitro kinetics to in vivo zebrafish model. 3. Computational evidence for a potentially novel binding site (FAD domain).

  • Major Concerns

    • Concern ID R1-M1
    • Severity Major
    • Blocking Yes
    • Axis Experimental validation of binding site
    • Claim pointer "Molecular docking against two XO crystal structures (3NVY, 3NRZ) consistently favored the FAD domain"
    • Evidence pointer Abstract only; no docking results, binding poses, or comparison with known inhibitors provided
    • Concern The claim of preferential FAD domain binding is based solely on computational docking and MD simulations. No experimental validation (e.g., site-directed mutagenesis, competitive binding assays with FAD, or XO variants lacking the FAD domain) is mentioned. The abstract does not specify whether the docking was performed on the full XO structure or isolated domains, nor whether the FAD domain is accessible in the native enzyme conformation.
    • Why it matters Without experimental confirmation, the FAD domain targeting claim remains speculative. Many computational docking studies report false positives, especially for allosteric or non-catalytic sites. This is the central mechanistic claim of the paper.

    • Resolution test Provide experimental evidence such as: (a) binding assays with XO variants lacking the FAD domain, (b) competition assays with FAD or FAD-binding inhibitors, (c) spectroscopic evidence of peptide-FAD interaction, or (d) at minimum, a rigorous comparison of docking scores against known FAD-binding and molybdenum-binding inhibitors.

    • Concern ID R1-M2

    • Severity Major
    • Blocking Yes
    • Axis In vivo model rigor
    • Claim pointer "In a zebrafish hyperuricemia model, TSH reduced uric acid and XO activity while upregulating purine salvage (hprt1) and urate transport (oat1) genes"
    • Evidence pointer Abstract only; no details on model induction, dosing, sample size, statistical analysis, or controls
    • Concern The abstract reports in vivo efficacy for the crude hydrolyzate (TSH), not the purified peptides. The gene expression data (hprt1, oat1) suggest additional mechanisms beyond XO inhibition, but the abstract provides no information on: (a) how hyperuricemia was induced, (b) dose-response relationships, (c) whether the peptides themselves were tested in vivo, (d) sample size and statistical power, (e) whether the observed effects are specific to the identified peptides or due to other components in TSH.
    • Why it matters The title and claims focus on specific peptides, but the in vivo validation uses a complex mixture. Without testing the purified peptides, the causal link between the identified peptides and the observed effects is not established. The gene expression changes also complicate the mechanism.

    • Resolution test Test the purified peptides individually or in combination in the zebrafish model, or provide clear evidence that the observed effects are attributable to the identified peptides (e.g., by comparing TSH with a peptide-depleted fraction).

    • Concern ID R1-M3

    • Severity Major
    • Blocking Yes
    • Axis Peptide identification and purity
    • Claim pointer "Eight 11S globulin peptides were identified by nanoUHPLC-ESI-Q-TOF MS/MS"
    • Evidence pointer Abstract only; no MS/MS spectra, sequence coverage, or purity data
    • Concern The abstract does not specify whether the identified peptides were synthesized or isolated from the hydrolyzate. If synthesized, the purity and characterization (e.g., HPLC, MS) are not mentioned. If isolated from TSH, the purification strategy and yield are absent. The IC50 values (467.2 and 536.1 µM) are relatively high compared to known XO inhibitors (e.g., allopurinol IC50 ~1-10 µM), raising questions about physiological relevance.
    • Why it matters Without confirmation of peptide identity and purity, the reported IC50 values and kinetic data cannot be properly evaluated. High IC50 values also question the practical utility as functional food candidates.
    • Resolution test Provide MS/MS spectra for peptide identification, HPLC purity data for synthetic peptides, and a discussion of the IC50 values in the context of known food-derived XO inhibitors.
