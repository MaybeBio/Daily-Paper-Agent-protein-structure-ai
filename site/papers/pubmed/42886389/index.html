Computer-aided development of novel thiazolidinone-thiazole derivatives: multidimensional structure-activity relationships revealed by quantum chemical analysis, molecular docking and biological investigation.

Iqbal, Tayyiaba; Khan, Shoaib; Khan, Asrar; Sajjad, Hassnain; Almalki, Masi A; Abid, Souhir; Abid, Majdi; Ali, Syed Kashif; Bakridin, Zaripov; Baltabay, Eshmuratov; Gomha, Sobhi M; Zaki, Magdi E A
Journal of computer-aided molecular design · 2026-10-16 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该研究通过分子对接和DFT计算辅助设计噻唑烷酮-噻唑衍生物作为α-淀粉酶/α-葡萄糖苷酶抑制剂，涉及蛋白质-配体对接但核心是药物化学而非蛋白质结构计算建模，方法可部分借鉴。

摘要

Diabetes mellitus is a major global health challenge, highlighting the need for new inhibitors of the carbohydrate-hydrolyzing enzymes alpha-amylase and alpha-glucosidase. In this study, a series of thiazole-based thiazolidinone derivatives (1-10) was designed, synthesized, and structurally characterized using spectroscopic techniques. Among the synthesized compounds, 8 showed the strongest inhibitory activity against alpha-amylase and alpha-glucosidase, with IC(50) values of 5.50 +/- 0.40 and 5.90 +/- 0.10 microM, respectively, and compared with 10.80 +/- 0.50 and 11.10 +/- 0.30 microM for acarbose. Molecular docking suggested that compound 8 can adopt a favorable binding orientation within the active sites of both enzymes through hydrogen-bonding and hydrophobic contacts. Enzyme kinetic studies further characterized its mode of inhibition. DFT calculations were performed to examine the electronic properties, frontier molecular orbitals, and molecular electrostatic potential of selected derivatives. ADMET analysis was also conducted to provide preliminary insight into their predicted pharmacokinetic and toxicity-related properties. Overall, the experimental and computational findings identify compound 8 as a promising lead structure for further investigation as an alpha-amylase and alpha-glucosidase inhibitor.

摘要翻译

糖尿病是全球性的重大健康挑战，凸显了对碳水化合物水解酶α-淀粉酶和α-葡萄糖苷酶新型抑制剂的需求。本研究设计、合成了一系列基于噻唑的噻唑烷酮衍生物（1-10），并利用光谱技术对其结构进行了表征。在合成的化合物中，化合物8对α-淀粉酶和α-葡萄糖苷酶表现出最强的抑制活性，其IC(50)值分别为5.50 ± 0.40和5.90 ± 0.10微摩尔，而阿卡波糖的对应值为10.80 ± 0.50和11.10 ± 0.30微摩尔。分子对接表明，化合物8可通过氢键和疏水接触在两种酶的活性位点内采用有利的结合取向。酶动力学研究进一步表征了其抑制模式。进行了DFT计算以考察所选衍生物的电子性质、前沿分子轨道和分子静电势。还进行了ADMET分析，以初步了解其预测的药代动力学和毒性相关性质。总体而言，实验和计算研究结果将化合物8确定为一种有前景的先导结构，值得进一步研究其作为α-淀粉酶和α-葡萄糖苷酶抑制剂的潜力。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：能否通过计算机辅助设计（对接 + DFT）与实验验证相结合，发现新型 alpha-amylase 和 alpha-glucosidase 抑制剂，并阐明其构效关系？
  • 为什么重要：糖尿病是全球性健康挑战，抑制碳水化合物水解酶（alpha-amylase、alpha-glucosidase）是控制餐后高血糖的有效策略；现有药物（如 acarbose）存在副作用和效力不足的问题，需要新骨架抑制剂。
  • 现有方法为何不足：传统抑制剂发现依赖高通量筛选，成本高且缺乏机制理解；纯计算预测（如仅对接）缺乏电子性质层面的解释，且预测结果常缺乏实验验证。
  • 精确研究问题：Can thiazolidinone-thiazole derivatives with optimized electronic properties (via DFT) and favorable docking poses achieve superior enzyme inhibition compared to acarbose?
