8

Explainable AI reveals the allosteric blind spot in protein-ligand binding predictions.

Parikh, Vedant; Foley, Brandon; Gatlin, Will; Ludwick, Max; Turano, Lucas; Verkhivker, Gennady M
Cell reports. Physical science · 2026-09-11 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文开发可解释AI框架，系统评估多种AI模型在正构与别构配体-蛋白复合物预测中的表现，揭示别构结合预测的盲点，并通过能量景观理论解释其物理机制，直接关联蛋白质结构预测与构象采样问题。

摘要

Artificial intelligence (AI) has transformed prediction of protein structure and interactions, yet modeling of allosteric binding remains a persistent challenge. We develop an explainable AI framework that interrogates AI models AlphaFold3, Protenix, Boltz-2, Chai-1, and DynamicBind on rigorously stratified datasets of orthosteric and allosteric ligand-protein complexes. While AI models excel in accurate modeling of orthosteric ligand binding, a consistent and substantial performance gap observed across diverse architectures emerges in prediction of allosteric complexes. The biophysical logic for this dichotomy is unveiled through physics-based lens of the energy landscape theory. Orthosteric binding creates dominant energetic funnels via ligand-induced minimal frustration quenching, while allosteric sites preserve neutral frustration landscapes in both apo and holo protein states. By linking prediction outcomes to frustration landscapes, this study recasts AI shortcomings in prediction of allosteric ligand binding as diagnostic indicators of allostery, establishing a physics-informed framework that turns the allosteric blind spot into mechanistic insight.

摘要翻译

人工智能（AI）已变革了蛋白质结构与相互作用的预测，但变构结合（allosteric binding）的建模仍是一个持续挑战。我们开发了一个可解释的AI框架，在严格分层的正构（orthosteric）与变构（allosteric）配体-蛋白质复合物数据集上，对AI模型AlphaFold3、Protenix、Boltz-2、Chai-1和DynamicBind进行探究。尽管AI模型在正构配体结合的精确建模中表现出色，但在变构复合物的预测中，不同架构间一致且显著的性能差距显现出来。通过能量景观理论（energy landscape theory）的物理学视角，揭示了这一二分法的生物物理逻辑。正构结合通过配体诱导的最小挫败淬灭（ligand-induced minimal frustration quenching）产生主导性能量漏斗，而变构位点在apo与holo蛋白质状态下均保持中性挫败景观（neutral frustration landscape）。通过将预测结果与挫败景观（frustration landscape）相关联，本研究将AI在变构配体结合预测中的不足重新定义为变构（allostery）的诊断指标，建立了一个将变构盲点转化为机制性见解的物理学知情框架。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：为什么 AI 结构预测模型在变构配体-蛋白结合预测中表现不佳？这种失败是否有统一的物理基础？
  • 重要性：变构调控是药物发现中极具潜力的靶点（变构药物具有高选择性、低脱靶毒性），但 AI 预测模型主要针对正构位点优化。理解 AI 在变构预测中的系统性失败机制，对改进模型和利用变构靶点均有重要意义。
  • 现有方法不足：现有 AI 模型（AlphaFold3 等）在正构配体结合预测上表现优异，但缺乏对变构复合物的系统评估；同时，已有研究多将变构预测失败归因于训练数据不足或构象采样限制，缺乏统一的物理框架解释。
  • 精确研究问题："Can the performance gap of AI models in allosteric vs. orthosteric ligand binding prediction be explained by differences in local frustration landscapes, thereby turning prediction failures into mechanistic indicators of allostery?"

04 背景与发展脉络

（标注：以下脉络基于本文框架，未经外部核验） 1. 阶段一：物理模拟时代——分子动力学（MD）模拟和对接方法用于预测配体结合，可处理变构构象但计算成本高、采样受限。代表：AutoDock、GROMACS 等。局限：变构构象采样效率低，难以覆盖稀有状态。 2. 阶段二：深度学习结构预测——AlphaFold2 及后续 AlphaFold3 等模型实现高精度蛋白结构和复合物预测。代表：AlphaFold3、Boltz-2、Chai-1、Protenix。优点：速度快、正构位点预测精度高。局限：对变构复合物预测存在系统性盲区（本文核心发现）。 3. 阶段三：构象生成与动态预测——DynamicBind 等模型尝试从序列直接预测配体诱导构象变化。优点：可处理部分构象变化。局限：仍以正构位点为主，变构位点的构象变化幅度和方向难以捕捉。 4. 本文位置：不提出新模型，而是建立"可解释 AI + 能量景观理论"的诊断框架，将 AI 预测失败转化为变构机制的物理信号，填补了"AI 预测性能评估"与"变构机制理解"之间的鸿沟。

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：构建可解释 AI 框架，在严格分层（stratified）的正构/变构配体-蛋白复合物数据集上系统评估五种 AI 模型（AlphaFold3、Protenix、Boltz-2、Chai-1、DynamicBind）的预测性能，并用能量景观理论中的局部 frustration 分析解释性能差异。
  2. 核心洞察：AI 模型在变构预测中的失败并非随机噪声，而是变构位点能量景观特征的忠实反映——变构位点保留中性 frustration（既无强稳定化也无强去稳定化），而正构位点在配体结合后经历 minimal frustration 淬灭形成清晰能量漏斗。因此，AI 的"变构盲区"实际上是变构机制的物理指纹。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：AI 模型的预测失败模式可以携带物理信息——当模型在特定子集上系统性失败时，该失败模式本身可能反映了目标系统的内在物理特征，而非单纯的模型缺陷。这提示在蛋白质结构预测研究中，应系统分析失败案例的物理模式，而非仅追求整体精度提升。

07 方法总览

  • 输入：正构和变构配体-蛋白复合物的序列与结构信息（严格分层数据集）
  • 输出：各 AI 模型的配体结合预测结果 + 变构/正构性能差距的物理解释
  • 模块： 1. 数据集构建与分层（正构 vs. 变构复合物） 2. 五种 AI 模型预测（AlphaFold3、Protenix、Boltz-2、Chai-1、DynamicBind） 3. 性能评估与差距量化 4. 能量景观分析（局部 frustration 计算，apo 与 holo 状态） 5. 可解释性关联（预测性能 ↔ frustration 景观）
  • 训练：本文不训练新模型，使用预训练 AI 模型进行推理
  • 工具：AlphaFold3、Protenix、Boltz-2、Chai-1、DynamicBind（预测）；frustration 分析工具（具体工具名未提供）
  • 反馈回路：预测性能差距 → frustration 景观分析 → 建立"失败模式 = 变构机制信号"的解释框架
  • 假设：变构位点的能量景观特征（中性 frustration）是 AI 预测失败的根本原因，且该特征可通过 frustration 分析量化
  • 流程：构建分层数据集 → 五种模型分别预测正构/变构复合物 → 量化性能差距 → 对 apo/holo 状态进行 frustration 分析 → 关联预测性能与 frustration 景观 → 形成物理信息框架

14 学到什么

Agent 提炼的知识候选： 1. 失败模式作为诊断信号：AI 模型在特定子集上的系统性预测失败可携带物理信息。在蛋白质结构预测研究中，不应仅将失败视为待修复的 bug，而应系统分析失败模式与目标系统物理特征（如能量景观、构象系综）的关联。 2. 能量景观理论作为解释框架：frustration 分析（minimal frustration vs. neutral frustration）可作为连接 AI 预测行为与蛋白质物理的桥梁。在结构预测研究中，可将 frustration 计算作为预测结果的物理一致性检查工具。 3. 多模型交叉验证策略：使用多种架构（AlphaFold3、Protenix、Boltz-2、Chai-1、DynamicBind）交叉验证某一现象（变构盲区）的普遍性，是增强结论稳健性的有效实验设计。 4. 正构/变构分层评估：在配体结合预测评估中，将正构与变构位点分开评估可揭示模型的能力边界。这一分层策略可迁移到其他蛋白质-配体预测任务的评估设计中。 5. 可解释 AI 与物理理论的结合：将可解释 AI 技术（如注意力分析、特征归因）与物理理论（能量景观）结合，可产生超越单纯性能评估的机制洞察。此思路可迁移到 MD 模拟与 AI 预测的交叉验证中。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents an intriguing and potentially important claim: that a systematic failure of current AI models to predict allosteric ligand binding can be explained by energy landscape theory and repurposed as a diagnostic tool. The concept is novel and the scope (five models, stratified datasets) is ambitious. However, the abstract alone provides insufficient evidence to evaluate the rigor of the framework, the statistical significance of the performance gap, the validity of the frustration landscape analysis, or the generalizability of the conclusions. The core claim—that the blind spot is "allosteric" in a mechanistic sense—requires careful control for confounding factors (e.g., binding site geometry, ligand properties, training data bias) that are not addressed in the abstract.

  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Structural biologists, computational chemists, and AI researchers working on protein-ligand interactions and drug discovery. The work could interest those seeking to understand the limitations of current AI models and those exploring physics-informed explanations for AI behavior. The potential to turn a failure mode into a diagnostic tool for allostery is of broad methodological interest.

  • Major strengths 1. The question is timely and important: allosteric binding is a major unsolved problem in computational drug discovery. 2. The approach of using explainable AI to interrogate multiple state-of-the-art models is methodologically sound in principle. 3. The connection to energy landscape theory provides a biophysical grounding that goes beyond black-box performance metrics.

  • Major Concerns

    • Concern ID R1-M1
    • Severity Major
    • Blocking Yes
    • Axis Evidence sufficiency
    • Claim pointer "a consistent and substantial performance gap observed across diverse architectures emerges in prediction of allosteric complexes"
    • Evidence pointer Abstract only; no quantitative data provided
    • Concern The abstract asserts a "consistent and substantial performance gap" but provides no metrics (e.g., RMSD, DockQ, binding affinity correlation), no statistical tests, and no comparison of effect sizes across models. Without these, the claim is unverifiable.
    • Why it matters The entire narrative hinges on the existence and magnitude of this gap. If the gap is small, model-dependent, or confounded by dataset composition, the central thesis collapses.

    • Resolution test Provide quantitative performance metrics for each model on orthosteric vs. allosteric datasets, with confidence intervals and statistical significance (e.g., paired t-test or Wilcoxon). Show that the gap is robust across multiple random splits and is not explained by trivial factors (e.g., ligand size, binding site depth).

    • Concern ID R1-M2

    • Severity Major
    • Blocking Yes
    • Axis Conceptual clarity and control
    • Claim pointer "The biophysical logic for this dichotomy is unveiled through physics-based lens of the energy landscape theory"
    • Evidence pointer Abstract only; no data on frustration landscapes
    • Concern The abstract claims that frustration landscape analysis explains the performance gap, but it is unclear how this analysis was performed (e.g., on which structures, using which algorithm, with what validation). The link between AI prediction failure and "neutral frustration landscapes" is asserted, not demonstrated.
    • Why it matters Without a clear, testable link between the computational frustration analysis and the AI model behavior, the explanation remains a post-hoc narrative. The claim that the blind spot is "diagnostic" requires that the frustration landscape be predictive of model failure, not just correlated.

    • Resolution test Show that frustration landscape features (e.g., local frustration index, frustration density) quantitatively predict the performance gap across individual complexes. Provide a confusion matrix or ROC curve demonstrating that the frustration-based classifier outperforms a null model.

    • Concern ID R1-M3

    • Severity Major
    • Blocking Yes
    • Axis Dataset rigor and bias
    • Claim pointer "rigorously stratified datasets of orthosteric and allosteric ligand-protein complexes"
    • Evidence pointer Abstract only; no dataset description
    • Concern The abstract does not describe how orthosteric and allosteric complexes were defined, curated, or stratified. Potential confounds include: (a) allosteric sites may be systematically less well-represented in training data; (b) allosteric ligands may have different physicochemical properties; (c) the structural resolution of allosteric complexes may be lower.
    • Why it matters If the performance gap is driven by data bias rather than a fundamental biophysical property, the conclusions are not generalizable.
    • Resolution test Provide a detailed dataset table with PDB IDs, resolution, ligand properties, and training set overlap for each model. Show that the gap persists after controlling for these variables (e.g., via propensity score matching or regression).
