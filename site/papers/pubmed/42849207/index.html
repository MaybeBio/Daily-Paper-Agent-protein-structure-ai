8

Gaussian accelerated molecular dynamics simulations reveal inhibitor-dependent loop dynamics in aminopeptidase N.

Yu, Shuang; Wang, Jinhong; Zhao, Yi; Zhuo, Xinlan; Li, Xiang; Liang, Guizhao
Journal of molecular graphics & modelling · 2026-10-08 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文用高斯加速分子动力学模拟研究氨肽酶N在不同抑制剂作用下的构象动态差异，属于蛋白质构象采样与抑制剂结合机制的计算研究，与课题高度相关。

摘要

Aminopeptidase N (APN) is a critical exopeptidase that plays key roles in physiological processes such as tumor metastasis, immune regulation, and viral infections. However, the conformational dynamics associated with different APN inhibitors remain insufficiently understood, particularly whether inhibitors with varying potency induce similar or distinct dynamic behaviors. Here, we applied Gaussian accelerated molecular dynamics (GaMD) simulations to investigate the conformational effects of three representative APN inhibitors, Bestatin, CHR-79888, and Kelatorphan, revealing inhibitor-dependent differences in APN conformational dynamics. The results showed that, in the CHR-79888- and Kelatorphan-bound systems, the flexible loop (891)YGGGSFSF(898) preferentially sampled distinct conformations and formed more frequent direct contacts with the ligands. These systems also exhibited tighter interdomain coupling around the active site and shorter, more distributed residue communication pathways, consistent with a more restricted and coordinated conformational ensemble. In contrast, the Bestatin-bound system exhibited fewer direct contacts between the ligand and the flexible loop, together with larger conformational fluctuations and less coordinated interdomain communication. Overall, our study reveals the inhibitor-dependent unique loop dynamics of APN, providing new theoretical insights for the design of efficient APN inhibitors.

摘要翻译

氨肽酶N（Aminopeptidase N, APN）是一种关键的肽链外切酶，在肿瘤转移、免疫调节和病毒感染等生理过程中发挥重要作用。然而，与不同APN抑制剂相关的构象动态仍未被充分理解，特别是具有不同效力的抑制剂是否诱导相似或不同的动态行为。在此，我们应用高斯加速分子动力学（Gaussian accelerated molecular dynamics, GaMD）模拟来研究三种代表性APN抑制剂——贝他汀（Bestatin）、CHR-79888和凯拉托芬（Kelatorphan）——的构象效应，揭示了APN构象动态中依赖抑制剂的差异。结果表明，在CHR-79888和凯拉托芬结合的系统中，柔性环(891)YGGGSFSF(898)优先采样不同的构象，并与配体形成更频繁的直接接触。这些系统还表现出活性位点周围更紧密的结构域间耦合，以及更短、更分散的残基通讯路径，这与更受限且协调的构象集合一致。相比之下，贝他汀结合的系统中，配体与柔性环之间的直接接触较少，同时伴随更大的构象波动和较不协调的结构域间通讯。总体而言，我们的研究揭示了APN中依赖抑制剂的独特环动态，为高效APN抑制剂的设计提供了新的理论见解。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：不同效力的 APN 抑制剂是否诱导 APN 产生相似或不同的构象动力学行为？特别是活性位点附近柔性 loop 的构象变化是否具有抑制剂依赖性？
  • 为什么重要：APN 是肿瘤转移、免疫调节和病毒感染中的关键外肽酶，抑制剂设计需要理解结合诱导的构象变化；loop 动力学可能影响抑制剂亲和力和选择性。
  • 现有方法为何不足：常规 MD 模拟时间尺度有限，难以充分采样 loop 的稀有构象转变；且以往研究多关注静态结合模式，缺乏对不同抑制剂诱导的动态差异的系统比较。
  • 精确研究问题：Can GaMD simulations reveal inhibitor-dependent conformational dynamics of the APN flexible loop (891)YGGGSFSF(898) that correlate with inhibitor potency?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：对三种 APN-抑制剂复合物分别进行 GaMD 模拟，分析 loop 构象采样、配体-残基接触频率、域间耦合强度和残基通信路径。
  2. 核心洞察：抑制剂的化学结构差异（而非仅效力差异）导致 APN 活性位点 loop 的构象偏好和动态耦合模式不同；CHR-79888 和 Kelatorphan 诱导更紧致、更协调的构象系综，而 Bestatin 诱导更松散、波动更大的系综。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：配体结合不仅改变蛋白的静态结构，更重塑其动态系综；增强采样 MD 结合网络分析可揭示配体特异性的构象耦合模式，这一思路可迁移至其他酶-抑制剂体系或蛋白-蛋白相互作用研究。

  • 算力成本：未提供；GaMD 通常比常规 MD 计算开销略高，但具体成本未知。

  • 最难情形：loop 构象差异是否在更长模拟或不同力场下保持稳定未知。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a plausible and potentially interesting computational study on inhibitor-dependent conformational dynamics of APN. However, the claims are broad and mechanistic, and the abstract alone provides insufficient evidence to evaluate the technical rigor, statistical robustness, or biological significance of the findings. The central conclusion that different inhibitors induce distinct loop dynamics and interdomain communication patterns requires substantial supporting data that are not visible in the supplied material. The work may be of interest to computational enzymologists and drug designers, but the case is not established from the abstract alone.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Computational biophysicists and medicinal chemists studying enzyme-inhibitor interactions, particularly those focused on aminopeptidases and conformational dynamics in drug design. The findings could inform structure-based design of APN inhibitors, a target relevant to cancer and viral infections. Researchers using enhanced sampling methods such as GaMD may also find the methodological application relevant.
  • Major strengths The study addresses a meaningful question about whether inhibitors of different potency induce similar or distinct conformational dynamics, which is relevant to understanding inhibitor mechanisms beyond static binding affinity. The use of GaMD is appropriate for exploring conformational ensembles that may be inaccessible to conventional MD. The focus on a specific flexible loop and interdomain communication provides a mechanistically oriented framework.
