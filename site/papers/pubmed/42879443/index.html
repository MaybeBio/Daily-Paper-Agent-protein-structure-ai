From prompts to genomes: A constraint-first framework for AI-driven sequence design across life and viruses.

Wicaksono, Adhityo; Parikesit, Arli Aditya
PLoS computational biology · 2026-10-15 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文提出面向细菌、古菌、真核生物和病毒的‘提示词到基因组’AI设计框架，聚焦长DNA序列生成与约束验证，虽涉及序列设计但对象非蛋白质结构，且不涉及结构预测、构象采样或MD等蛋白质结构计算问题，仅部分算法思路可迁移。

摘要

A constraint-first framework for future 'prompt-to-genome' AI across Bacteria, Archaea, Eukaryota, and viruses is proposed. Genome-scale models can now generate long DNA sequences, and experimentally viable genome-scale designs have begun to appear, but sequence generation alone does not establish biological feasibility. Constraints should not require designs to remain within natural sequence space; rather, they should define testable boundaries for function, inheritance, lifecycle coherence and safe use, including new-to-nature designs. We propose a two-part system. First, a Constraint Compiler translates natural-language prompts into machine-checkable specifications defining scope, context, objectives, assumptions and governance requirements. Second, a modular Validator Stack (C1-C6) evaluates candidate designs for sequence integrity, gene and regulatory plausibility, genome completeness, comparative coherence, systems-level feasibility, and safety and governance. Extreme prompts serve as stress tests to expose hidden assumptions, conflicting objectives, and refusal points, complemented by adversarial testing and risk assessment. A phased roadmap and community benchmarks are proposed to measure specification accuracy, biological feasibility, robustness, and governance performance. The goal is to move prompt-to-genome systems from impressive generation toward reliable, auditable, and accountable biological design.

摘要翻译

本文提出了一种面向未来“提示词到基因组”人工智能的约束优先框架，适用于细菌、古菌、真核生物及病毒。基因组规模模型现已能够生成较长的DNA序列，且实验上可行的基因组规模设计已开始出现，但仅凭序列生成并不能确立生物学可行性。约束不应要求设计局限于自然序列空间；相反，它们应定义功能、遗传、生命周期连贯性及安全使用的可测试边界，包括新自然设计。我们提出一个由两部分组成的系统。首先，约束编译器（Constraint Compiler）将自然语言提示词转换为机器可检查的规范，明确范围、上下文、目标、假设及治理要求。其次，模块化验证栈（Validator Stack，C1-C6）评估候选设计的序列完整性、基因及调控合理性、基因组完整性、比较连贯性、系统级可行性以及安全与治理。极端提示词作为压力测试，以揭示隐藏假设、冲突目标及拒绝点，并辅以对抗性测试和风险评估。我们提出了分阶段路线图和社区基准，以衡量规范准确性、生物学可行性、鲁棒性及治理性能。目标是将提示词到基因组系统从令人印象深刻的生成能力推进至可靠、可审计且负责任的生物设计。
