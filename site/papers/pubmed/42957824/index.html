Macrocycle-Integrated MOF Nanoarchitectures: Cooperative Active Sites from Pillararenes, Calixarenes, and Crown Ethers.

Mohan, Brij; Sharma, Harish Kumar; Najafov, Bakhtiyar; Garazade, Ismayil M; Seyidova, Khayala; Gupta, Rakesh Kumar; Virender, Virender; da Silva, M Fatima C Guedes; Pombeiro, Armando J L; Kim, Sang Sub
Topics in current chemistry (Cham) · 2026-10-30 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文综述大环分子与MOF复合材料的协同活性位点，聚焦吸附、传感和分离，不涉及蛋白质结构计算或AI/物理模拟方法，与本课题无关。

摘要

Metal-organic frameworks (MOFs) offer crystalline porosity, modular structures, and tunable chemical environments, but they often lack the molecular recognition needed for highly selective adsorption, transport, and catalysis. Integrating macrocycles such as pillararenes, calixarenes, and crown ethers provides a route to macrocycle@MOF nanoarchitectures in which supramolecular cavities cooperate with framework pores, metal nodes, and linker functionalities. Calixarene-based MOFs create adaptive pockets for iodine capture, NO(2) sensing, and molecular separation; pillararene systems provide rigid, electron-rich channels for sieving, controlled release, and supramolecular docking; and crown-ether-functionalized MOFs enable selective ion transport, lithium sieving, and Sr(2+)/Cs(+) remediation. Their function arises from coupled host-guest recognition, pore confinement, electrostatic complementarity, and active-site organization. Key challenges include pore blocking, synthetic complexity, structural disorder, incomplete active-site validation, and device integration. This review highlights design principles for converting macrocycle-integrated MOFs from proof-of-concept materials into practical recognition platforms for advanced catalysis, separations, sensing, remediation, energy technologies, and biomedical applications.

摘要翻译

金属有机框架（MOFs）提供晶体孔隙率、模块化结构和可调化学环境，但往往缺乏高选择性吸附、传输和催化所需的分子识别能力。将大环化合物（如柱芳烃、杯芳烃和冠醚）整合其中，为构建大环@MOF纳米结构提供了一条途径，在此类结构中，超分子空腔与框架孔道、金属节点和连接体官能团协同作用。基于杯芳烃的MOFs形成适应性口袋，用于碘捕获、NO(2)传感和分子分离；柱芳烃体系提供刚性、富电子的通道，用于筛分、控释和超分子对接；而冠醚功能化的MOFs则实现选择性离子传输、锂筛分以及Sr(2+)/Cs(+)修复。其功能源于主客体识别、孔道限域、静电互补和活性位点组织的耦合作用。关键挑战包括孔道堵塞、合成复杂性、结构无序、活性位点验证不完整以及器件集成。本综述重点阐述了将大环整合MOFs从概念验证材料转化为先进催化、分离、传感、修复、能源技术和生物医学应用的实用识别平台的设计原则。
