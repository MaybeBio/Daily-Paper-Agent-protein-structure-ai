SprintX: mitigating data scarcity in antibody design via synthetic structural prior knowledge dataset.

Heo, Ryong; Lee, Dahyeon; Jung, Jaeho; Kim, Byung Ju; Park, Chihyun
mAbs · 2026-10-29 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文构建了AlphaFold3衍生的合成抗体-抗原复合物结构数据集SprintX，以缓解数据稀缺问题，并验证其在下游抗体生成、亲和力预测和界面预测任务中的预训练增益，属于蛋白质结构计算建模与AI方法结合的核心贡献。

摘要

Structure-based artificial intelligence (AI)-driven antibody design is increasingly important for therapeutic development, yet progress has been hindered by the scarcity of high-quality antibody-antigen complex structures for model training. To alleviate this bottleneck, we introduce SprintX (Structural Prior for Antibody-Antigen CompleXes), a large-scale AlphaFold3-derived synthetic dataset designed to provide structural prior knowledge for antibody-antigen modeling. Unlike conventional docking-based synthesis pipelines, SprintX uses AlphaFold3 to generate antigen-conditioned candidate complex-structure predictions from sampled antibody sequences paired with selected antigens, without imposing predefined binding-site or pose constraints. Candidate structures were ranked to select one representative structure per antibody-antigen pair before terminal trimming and multistage quality control. To improve reliability and learning stability, we applied a multi-stage quality-control pipeline integrating global confidence metrics, stereochemical validation, and interface-level contact analysis. SprintX recapitulated key geometric characteristics and physicochemical regularities of experimentally resolved antibody-antigen complexes while filtering out structurally unstable or implausible synthetic outputs. Across downstream tasks including antibody generation, computational binding-affinity prediction, and binding-interface prediction, models pretrained with SprintX showed meaningful improvements in structural plausibility and competitive performance on computational binding-affinity and binding-interface prediction benchmarks. These results support SprintX not as a substitute for experimentally resolved complexes, but as a complementary source of structural prior knowledge for structure-aware antibody modeling. Finally, we release SprintX as an openly accessible resource to facilitate data-efficient learning for AI-driven antibody design.

摘要翻译

基于结构的AI驱动抗体设计在治疗开发中日益重要，然而其进展一直受到高质量抗体-抗原复合物结构稀缺的制约，这限制了模型训练。为缓解这一瓶颈，我们引入了SprintX（抗体-抗原复合物的结构先验），这是一个大规模、源自AlphaFold3的合成数据集，旨在为抗体-抗原建模提供结构先验知识。与传统的基于对接的合成流程不同，SprintX利用AlphaFold3从采样的抗体序列与选定抗原配对中生成抗原条件化的候选复合物结构预测，而不施加预定义的结合位点或姿态约束。候选结构经过排序，为每个抗体-抗原对选择一个代表性结构，随后进行末端修剪和多阶段质量控制。为提高可靠性和学习稳定性，我们应用了一个多阶段质量控制流程，整合了全局置信度指标、立体化学验证和界面级接触分析。SprintX重现了实验解析的抗体-抗原复合物的关键几何特征和物理化学规律，同时过滤掉结构不稳定或不合理的合成输出。在下游任务中，包括抗体生成、计算结合亲和力预测和结合界面预测，使用SprintX预训练的模型在结构合理性方面显示出显著改进，并在计算结合亲和力和结合界面预测基准上表现出有竞争力的性能。这些结果支持SprintX并非替代实验解析的复合物，而是作为结构感知抗体建模的结构先验知识的补充来源。最后，我们将SprintX作为开放可访问的资源发布，以促进AI驱动抗体设计中的数据高效学习。

06 核心思想

1) 表面方法：用 AlphaFold3 对「采样抗体序列 + 选定抗原」生成抗原条件下的候选复合物结构预测，不设结合位点/pose 约束；对候选结构排序，每个抗体-抗原对选一个代表结构；末端修剪；多阶段质量控制（全局置信度指标、立体化学验证、界面接触分析）；发布为 SprintX 数据集。 2) 核心洞察：结构预测模型本身可作为「结构先验生成器」，在无位点/pose 约束下产生候选复合物，再通过质量控制筛选出接近实验结构几何与理化规律的结构，从而把预测能力转化为可复用的训练数据。 3) 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：当实验结构稀缺时，「生成 + 严格筛选」可能比「约束式合成」更能保留多样性；质量控制管线（置信度 + 立体化学 + 界面接触）是可迁移的数据可信度分层手段。

The scientific case is therefore not established from the supplied material, and a full assessment requires access to the manuscript's methods and results.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers in computational antibody design, structural biology, and AI-driven drug discovery would be interested. The dataset could serve as a training resource for models in antibody generation, affinity prediction, and interface prediction, and the quality-control pipeline may be of methodological interest to those developing synthetic structural datasets.
  • Major strengths The proposed approach addresses a clear and important problem, namely data scarcity in antibody-antigen complex structures. The use of AlphaFold3 without predefined binding-site constraints is a reasonable and potentially more general strategy than docking-based synthesis. The emphasis on multi-stage quality control and the stated intention to release the dataset openly are commendable and align with community needs.
