Rational design of hydrophobic deep eutectic solvents tunes the catalytic pocket of lipase for the sustainable synthesis of structural lipids.

Han, Yiran; Chen, Zhiheng; Fang, Hao; Hu, Yi; Nian, Binbin
Food research international (Ottawa, Ont.) · 2026-11-01 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文通过MD模拟研究疏水深共熔溶剂调控脂肪酶催化口袋构象以促进结构脂合成，虽以酶为对象且涉及蛋白构象计算，但核心是溶剂工程应用，方法创新有限。

摘要

The global epidemic of metabolic diseases has accelerated the demand for functional structured lipids, particularly medium- and long-chain triacylglycerols (MLCTs), which have attracted significant attention due to their dual advantages of rapid energy supply and provision of essential fatty acids. However, the enzymatic synthesis of MLCTs has long been constrained by the solvent-enzyme compatibility paradox, wherein solvents capable of effectively dissolving hydrophobic substrates tend to strip the essential water layer from the enzyme, leading to catalytic failure. Here, we report a strategy with notable improvement potential that overcomes this bottleneck by employing a rationally designed hydrophobic deep eutectic solvent (DESs) system, cyclohexanone and lauric acid at a 2:1 ratio. Systematic screening of 44 solvent systems revealed that this specific DESs enables Candida antarctica Lipase B (Novozym 435) to achieve an outstanding yield of 88.17% +/- 1.42% under optimized conditions (55 degrees C, 24 h), significantly outperforming conventional organic solvents. Molecular dynamics (MD) simulations further unveiled its unique microscopic mechanism: the hydrophobic DES not only acts as a reaction medium but also actively modulates the enzyme's catalytic pocket. By stabilizing the open conformation of the lipase lid region (alpha5 and alpha10 helices) and maintaining an optimal pocket volume (V(index) = 1.11), this solvent system significantly enhances the substrate binding free energy to -122.25 kJ/mol, thereby promoting proper substrate anchoring. Moreover, the system demonstrates excellent industrial robustness, retaining over 90% of enzyme activity after five reuse cycles. This study establishes an alternative pathway distinct from mainstream frameworks of solvent-guided enzymatic reaction engineering, where rational design of non-biological media is used to regulate the machinery of biological macromolecules, providing a new theoretical foundation for green biomanufacturing.

摘要翻译

全球代谢性疾病的流行加速了对功能性结构脂质的需求，特别是中长链甘油三酯（MLCTs），因其兼具快速能量供应和提供必需脂肪酸的双重优势而受到广泛关注。然而，MLCTs的酶法合成长期受制于溶剂-酶相容性悖论，即能有效溶解疏水性底物的溶剂往往剥离酶表面的必需水层，导致催化失效。在此，我们报告了一种具有显著改进潜力的策略，通过采用合理设计的疏水性低共熔溶剂（DESs）体系，即环己酮与月桂酸以2:1比例组合，克服了这一瓶颈。对44种溶剂体系的系统筛选揭示，该特定DESs能使南极假丝酵母脂肪酶B（Novozym 435）在优化条件下（55°C，24小时）实现88.17% ± 1.42%的卓越产率，显著优于传统有机溶剂。分子动力学（MD）模拟进一步揭示了其独特的微观机制：疏水性DES不仅作为反应介质，还主动调节酶的催化口袋。通过稳定脂肪酶盖子区域（α5和α10螺旋）的开放构象并维持最佳口袋体积（V(index) = 1.11），该溶剂体系显著增强了底物结合自由能至-122.25 kJ/mol，从而促进底物的正确锚定。此外，该体系展现出优异的工业稳健性，在五次循环使用后仍保留超过90%的酶活性。本研究建立了一条区别于主流溶剂引导酶反应工程框架的替代路径，其中通过合理设计非生物介质来调控生物大分子的机制，为绿色生物制造提供了新的理论基础。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：如何解决酶催化合成 MLCT 中「溶剂-酶相容性悖论」——即能溶解疏水底物的溶剂往往会剥夺酶必需的水化层导致失活？
  • 为什么重要：MLCT 具有快速供能与提供必需脂肪酸的双重优势，是代谢疾病背景下功能性结构脂质的重要目标；其酶法合成长期受限于溶剂选择。
  • 现有方法为何不足：传统有机溶剂虽能溶解疏水底物但破坏酶水化层；已有 DES 研究多聚焦亲水性 DES 或仅关注产率提升，缺乏对 DES 如何从分子层面调控酶构象的系统理解。
  • 精确研究问题：Can a rationally designed hydrophobic DES simultaneously dissolve hydrophobic substrates and preserve/activate lipase's catalytic conformation, thereby achieving high-yield MLCT synthesis?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：系统筛选 44 种溶剂体系，确定环己酮:月桂酸=2:1 的疏水 DES 为最优介质；在 55°C、24h 条件下实现 88.17% 产率；通过 MD 模拟解析机制。
  2. 核心洞察：DES 不仅是反应介质，更是酶构象的主动调节器——通过稳定 lid 区域（α5/α10 螺旋）的开放构象并维持口袋体积（V_index=1.11），增强底物结合自由能（-122.25 kJ/mol），从而促进底物正确锚定。
  3. [Analysis] 可能的普适教训：溶剂工程可从「被动相容」转向「主动调控」——即通过理性设计非生物介质的物理化学性质来靶向调控酶的功能构象，这一思路可迁移至其他酶-溶剂体系乃至蛋白质-材料界面设计。

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围：仅针对 CALB 催化 MLCT 合成；未涵盖其他脂肪酶或结构脂质类型。
  • oracle/真值输入：MD 模拟的初始结构、力场参数、溶剂模型未提供，无法独立验证模拟可靠性。
  • 端到端状态：实验与模拟结合，但模拟仅用于解释而非预测；未建立从 DES 性质到产率的定量预测模型。
  • 算力成本：未提供 MD 模拟的规模（体系大小、模拟时长、采样方法）。
  • 历史数据依赖：筛选的 44 种溶剂选择标准未说明，可能存在选择偏差。
  • 模型依赖：MD 结果依赖力场选择与采样充分性，未提供收敛性证据。
  • 最难情形：未测试底物种类变化、酶固定化载体影响、工业放大条件。
  • 群体/领域边界：结论限于 CALB + 环己酮/月桂酸 DES 体系；推广至其他 DES-酶组合需谨慎。
  • 不确定性：产率误差 ±1.42% 已报告；MD 能量值的统计误差未报告。
  • 有边界的复述：在环己酮:月桂酸=2:1 的 DES 中，55°C/24h 条件下，CALB 可达到 88.17% 的 MLCT 产率，且 MD 模拟提示该 DES 通过稳定 lid 开放构象和优化口袋体积增强底物结合；该结论仅适用于所测试的酶-溶剂-底物组合。

Review setup

  • Assessment boundary Claims and evidence as presented in the abstract; no access to full manuscript, figures, tables, or supplementary materials
  • Shared manuscript claim summary The authors report that a rationally designed hydrophobic deep eutectic solvent (DES) composed of cyclohexanone and lauric acid at a 2:1 ratio enables Candida antarctica Lipase B (Novozym 435) to achieve 88.17% ± 1.42% yield of medium- and long-chain triacylglycerols (MLCTs) under optimized conditions (55 °C, 24 h), outperforming conventional organic solvents. Molecular dynamics simulations are used to propose a mechanism whereby the DES stabilizes the open conformation of the lipase lid region (alpha5 and alpha10 helices), maintains an optimal pocket volume (V_index = 1.11), and enhances substrate binding free energy to −122.25 kJ/mol.   • Missing materials affecting confidence Full methods, experimental replicates and error analysis, MD simulation setup and validation, enzyme activity assay details, product characterization data, comparison data for all 44 solvent systems, and reuse cycle experimental details

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a potentially interesting approach to enzymatic synthesis of structured lipids using a hydrophobic DES, with a claimed mechanistic insight from MD simulations. The reported yield and reusability are encouraging. However, the abstract alone does not provide sufficient detail to assess the robustness of the experimental design, the validity of the simulation methodology, or the statistical significance of the reported improvements. The mechanistic claims, while plausible, require substantially more evidence than can be evaluated from the abstract. The work may be of interest to researchers in enzymatic catalysis, green chemistry, and lipid biotechnology, but the case as presented is not fully established.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers in enzymatic catalysis and biocatalysis, particularly those working on lipase-mediated reactions in non-conventional media; scientists in green chemistry and sustainable manufacturing; food scientists and lipid technologists focused on structured lipid production; computational chemists interested in enzyme-solvent interactions and molecular dynamics simulations of biocatalysts.
  • Major strengths The study addresses a well-recognized bottleneck in enzymatic synthesis of MLCTs, namely the solvent-enzyme compatibility paradox. The systematic screening of 44 solvent systems provides a broad empirical basis for solvent selection. The combination of experimental yield data with MD simulation offers a dual approach that could provide mechanistic insight beyond simple empirical optimization. The reported reusability data suggest potential industrial relevance.
