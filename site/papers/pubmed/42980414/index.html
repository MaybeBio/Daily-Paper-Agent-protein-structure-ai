Structure-Guided Remodeling of Multi-Substrate Binding Pocket in Nicotinamide Mononucleotide Adenylyltransferase for Efficient NAD+ Synthesis.

Wei, Dianju; Li, Liangjun; Cui, Yuzhi; Huang, Yinfeng; Zou, Shuping; Xue, Yaping; Zheng, Yuguo
Journal of agricultural and food chemistry · 2026-11-04 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文通过结构引导的酶工程改造多底物结合口袋，结合分子动力学模拟优化NAD+合成，属于蛋白质结构计算辅助的酶工程应用，方法创新有限但结构模拟与设计思路对蛋白结构计算有参考价值。

摘要

The rational engineering of enzymes that coordinate multiple substrates within a confined active site remains a major challenge in biocatalysis. Here, we present a structure-guided dual-cluster engineering strategy that exploits low epistatic coupling among conserved residues to independently tune electronic and geometric active-site features while avoiding trade-offs. This approach was validated through re-engineering Methanothermobacter thermautotrophicus nicotinamide mononucleotide adenylyltransferase. The optimal triple mutant MT3 (V6C/Q10E/P11T) showed a 5.1-fold increase in catalytic efficiency (kcat/Km) over wild type. Molecular dynamics simulations revealed that the mutations synergistically compact the substrate-binding pocket, reduce the catalytic distance by 1.4 A, and reshape local electrostatics to stabilize a near-attack conformation, thereby lowering the reaction barrier. In a 30-L bioreactor, MT3 achieved 85.3% conversion and a titer of 112.5 g L-1 NAD+ within 8 h, underscoring its industrial potential. Collectively, this study provides a transferable framework for engineering complex multisubstrate enzymes by integrating structural dynamics with process-scale validation.

摘要翻译

在受限活性位点内协调多种底物的酶的理性工程改造仍是生物催化领域的一大挑战。在此，我们提出了一种结构引导的双簇工程策略，该策略利用保守残基间的低上位性耦合来独立调节电子和几何活性位点特征，同时避免权衡取舍。该方法通过重新工程改造 *Methanothermobacter thermautotrophicus* 烟酰胺单核苷酸腺苷酰转移酶得到验证。最优三突变体 MT3（V6C/Q10E/P11T）的催化效率（kcat/Km）较野生型提高了 5.1 倍。分子动力学 (molecular dynamics) 模拟揭示，这些突变协同压缩底物结合口袋，将催化距离缩短 1.4 Å，并重塑局部静电环境以稳定近攻击构象，从而降低反应能垒。在 30-L 生物反应器中，MT3 在 8 小时内实现了 85.3% 的转化率和 112.5 g L⁻¹ NAD⁺ 的滴度，凸显了其工业应用潜力。总体而言，本研究通过整合结构动力学与过程规模验证，为工程改造复杂多底物酶提供了一个可转移的框架。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题: 如何在多底物酶中同时优化多个底物的结合与催化，而不引入常见的 trade-off（如活性提升但稳定性下降，或对一种底物亲和力提升但另一种下降）？
  • 为什么重要: 多底物酶（如 NMNAT 同时结合 NMN 和 ATP）在工业生物催化中广泛存在，其活性位点需协调多个底物的空间排布与电子环境，单一突变往往顾此失彼，缺乏系统性设计原则。
  • 现有方法为何不足: 传统定点突变或随机突变缺乏对残基间上位效应（epistasis）的考量，组合突变常产生非加性效应；结构引导设计多聚焦于单底物酶或单一催化步骤，对多底物协同结合的口袋重塑缺乏可迁移框架。
  • 精确研究问题: "Can a structure-guided dual-cluster engineering strategy, exploiting low epistatic coupling among conserved residues, independently tune electronic and geometric features of a multi-substrate binding pocket to improve catalytic efficiency without trade-offs?"

（标注：以下脉络为「仅本文框架」——基于本文引言与讨论的叙述，未做外部文献核验） - 阶段一：单点突变 / 随机突变酶工程。代表方法：易错 PCR、定点饱和突变。优点：操作简单、无需结构信息。局限：组合空间大、上位效应不可控、常产生 trade-off。 - 阶段二：结构引导的理性设计。代表方法：基于晶体结构的活性位点残基替换、计算设计（Rosetta 等）。优点：有结构依据、可针对特定相互作用。局限：多底物协同结合时难以同时优化多个几何/电子特征，且静态结构忽略构象动态。 - 阶段三：动力学与构象 ensemble 指导的设计。代表方法：MD 模拟、增强采样、NAC 分析。优点：捕捉构象动态、可评估催化前状态。局限：计算成本高、设计规则仍不系统。 - 本文主张的位置：提出「双簇工程」——将活性位点残基按功能分为电子簇与几何簇，利用低上位耦合独立调优，结合 MD 验证 NAC 稳定化，并完成过程放大验证。这是对阶段二/三的整合，并首次在多底物酶中系统利用低上位耦合原则。

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法: 结构引导的双簇工程——将活性位点残基分为「电子簇」（影响静电/电荷分布）与「几何簇」（影响空间排布/口袋形状），基于保守残基的低上位耦合特性，分别独立突变，再组合最优单突变。
  2. 核心洞察: 多底物酶的活性位点中，某些保守残基虽然功能上分属不同「簇」（电子 vs 几何），但彼此上位耦合低，因此可以独立调优而不产生 trade-off。突变组合通过协同压缩口袋、缩短催化距离、重塑静电环境来稳定 NAC，从而降低反应能垒——即「几何 + 电子 + 动态」三重协同。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]: 在酶工程中，与其追求单个「关键残基」的完美替换，不如识别功能上可解耦的残基簇，利用低上位耦合进行并行优化。这一原则可迁移到其他多底物酶（如激酶、连接酶）以及多结构域蛋白设计。此外，MD 驱动的 NAC 分析可作为通用评估指标，连接结构变化与催化效率。

  • oracle/真值输入: 无计算预测 oracle；所有结论基于实验动力学数据和 MD 模拟。

  • 历史数据依赖: 依赖 NMNAT 已知晶体结构（来源未提供）和序列保守性数据。
  • 模型依赖: MD 结果依赖力场选择、模拟时长、采样充分性，但作者未提供这些细节。
  • 最难情形: 未测试 MT3 在极端 pH、高温、高底物浓度下的表现；未测试其他底物类似物。

MD 与实验的闭环验证: 先实验筛选突变体，再用 MD 解释机制，最后用反应器验证。这一流程可迁移到其他酶工程研究。 5. 过程规模验证作为设计终点: 实验室活性提升不等于工业可用，30-L 验证是必要的最终环节。可迁移到任何面向工业的酶工程研究。

Review setup

  • Input scope Abstract only
  • Assessment boundary Claims and evidence as presented in the abstract; no access to full text, figures, tables, or supplementary materials
  • Shared manuscript claim summary The authors report a structure-guided dual-cluster engineering strategy applied to nicotinamide mononucleotide adenylyltransferase from Methanothermobacter thermautotrophicus. They claim that a triple mutant (V6C/Q10E/P11T) increases catalytic efficiency 5.1-fold over wild type, that molecular dynamics simulations reveal a compaction of the substrate-binding pocket, a 1.4 Å reduction in catalytic distance, and electrostatic reshaping that stabilizes a near-attack conformation, and that a 30-L bioreactor run achieved 85.3% conversion and 112.5 g L-1 NAD+ titer within 8 h.
  • Visible evidence base Abstract text only; no kinetic data, simulation parameters, structural coordinates, or bioreactor details are provided
  • Missing materials affecting confidence Full methods, kinetic characterization data, MD simulation setup and validation, structural figures, sequence alignments, epistasis analysis details, bioreactor operating conditions, and statistical analyses

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a potentially interesting engineering strategy with a clear industrial endpoint. However, the evidence base is too limited to evaluate the central claims about mechanism, generality, or even the magnitude of the reported improvement. The dual-cluster concept and the claim of low epistatic coupling are stated without supporting data. The simulation-based mechanistic interpretation is plausible but unverifiable from the abstract alone. The process-scale result is impressive if accurate, but no operational details are given. The work may be of interest to biocatalysis and metabolic engineering communities, but the current abstract does not establish the case.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers in enzyme engineering, particularly those working on multi-substrate enzymes and NAD+ biosynthesis; industrial biocatalysis groups seeking high-titer production routes; computational enzymologists interested in structure-guided design and MD-based mechanistic interpretation.
  • Major strengths The reported 5.1-fold improvement in catalytic efficiency and the 112.5 g L-1 NAD+ titer are notable quantitative outcomes. The combination of structural analysis, simulation, and process-scale validation is a commendable arc. The focus on avoiding trade-offs through epistatic coupling is a conceptually useful framing.
  • Major Concerns
  • Concern ID R1-M1
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Evidence sufficiency
  • Claim pointer The dual-cluster engineering strategy exploits low epistatic coupling among conserved residues to independently tune electronic and geometric active-site features.
  • Evidence pointer Abstract; location not provided
  • Concern The abstract asserts that the strategy exploits low epistatic coupling and that this enables independent tuning of electronic and geometric features. No data on epistasis measurements, residue conservation analysis, or the design of the dual clusters are presented. The claim that trade-offs are avoided is central to the conceptual novelty but is entirely unsupported in the abstract.
  • Why it matters If the epistasis claim is not substantiated, the strategy reduces to a standard site-directed mutagenesis exercise. The novelty of the work hinges on demonstrating that the dual-cluster approach is generalizable and that the independence of tuning is real, not assumed.
