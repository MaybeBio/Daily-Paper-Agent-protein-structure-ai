Structures of pUG-fold RNA bound to DNMT1 reveal a mechanism for RNA-mediated epigenetic regulation.

Song, Jessica J; Cech, Thomas R; Kasinath, Vignesh
bioRxiv : the preprint server for biology · 2026-10-25 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该研究通过冷冻电镜和元动力学模拟解析DNMT1与pUG折叠RNA的复合物结构，揭示RNA介导的表观遗传调控机制，但核心对象是RNA-蛋白复合物且以实验结构解析为主，与蛋白质结构计算建模关系较弱。

摘要

Many chromatin-associated proteins have been found to bind RNA as a means of epigenetic regulation. Specifically, DNA methyltransferase 1 (DNMT1), which maintains cytosine methylation at CpG dinucleotides, is inhibited by RNA at transcribed DNA loci in cells. However, the mechanisms by which RNA binds DNMT1 and inhibits its activity remain unknown. Here, we determine a series of cryogenic electron microscopy (cryo-EM) structures of human DNMT1 bound to pUG-fold RNA, a non-canonical G-quadruplex previously observed to inhibit activity, revealing two distinct RNA-binding modes. The pUG-fold RNA binds the surface of DNMT1 in its autoinhibited conformation across a positively charged surface between the methyltransferase domain and the CXXC domain, and it binds directly in the active site of an open DNMT1 conformation. RNA binding is sterically incompatible with substrate DNA engagement in both states. Our 2.5 A structure captures the intricate network of hydrogen bonds and electrostatic interactions between amino acids in the methyltransferase domain and the tetrad layers of pUG- fold RNA. Metadynamics molecular dynamics simulations provide an orthogonal view of the conformational landscape of DNMT1, revealing the two distinct RNA-binding modes. Furthermore, our analysis of published DNMT1 RIP-seq and eCLIP-seq data confirms that DNMT1-interacting RNAs in cells exhibit a strong propensity to form non-canonical G- quadruplex RNA structures. Collectively, our study provides the first structural basis for pUG- fold RNA recognition by a protein and illustrates how cryo-EM and AI-based methods for protein and RNA structure prediction synergize to inform the mechanism of RNA-mediated regulation of DNMT1.

摘要翻译

许多染色质相关蛋白已被发现能够结合RNA，以此作为表观遗传调控的一种手段。具体而言，DNA甲基转移酶1（DNMT1）负责维持CpG二核苷酸处的胞嘧啶甲基化，而在细胞中，转录的DNA位点处的RNA会抑制该酶。然而，RNA如何结合DNMT1并抑制其活性的机制仍不清楚。在此，我们解析了一系列人源DNMT1与pUG-fold RNA（一种此前被观察到具有抑制活性的非经典G-四链体）结合的冷冻电镜（cryo-EM）结构，揭示了两种不同的RNA结合模式。pUG-fold RNA以DNMT1的自抑制构象结合于甲基转移酶结构域与CXXC结构域之间带正电荷表面的DNMT1表面，并且直接结合于开放构象DNMT1的活性位点。在两种状态下，RNA结合在空间上与底物DNA的结合不相容。我们的2.5 Å结构捕获了甲基转移酶结构域中的氨基酸与pUG-fold RNA的四联体层之间复杂的氢键和静电相互作用网络。元动力学分子动力学（metadynamics molecular dynamics）模拟提供了DNMT1构象景观的正交视角，揭示了两种不同的RNA结合模式。此外，我们对已发表的DNMT1 RIP-seq和eCLIP-seq数据的分析证实，细胞中与DNMT1相互作用的RNA表现出强烈的形成非经典G-四链体RNA结构的倾向。总之，我们的研究首次提供了蛋白质识别pUG-fold RNA的结构基础，并展示了冷冻电镜与基于人工智能的蛋白质和RNA结构预测方法如何协同作用，以揭示RNA介导的DNMT1调控机制。
