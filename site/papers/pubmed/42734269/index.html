Molecular Dynamics Simulations Suggest Folded Chain Ends Compatible with the Cellulose-II Fibril Structure.

Kong, Linghan; Eichhorn, Stephen J; Bryce, Richard A
Biomacromolecules · 2026-09-14 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文用分子动力学模拟研究纤维素-II晶体中链折叠末端的结构可行性，对象为多糖而非蛋白质，但MD方法可迁移至蛋白质构象采样，与课题关系较弱。

摘要

The structural feasibility of chain folding within an accepted cellulose-II structure is investigated in this study. We incorporate 3-residue and 5-residue folded glucosyl turns into a cellulose-II fibril crystal model to assess if the dominant structure can be retained. Ten candidate turn models were generated by metadynamics simulations and ranked by semiempirical quantum mechanical energies. Across >30 independent 300 ns molecular dynamics trajectories, the crystalline core retains its unit cell parameters, X-ray diffraction pattern, hydrogen bonding network, and canonical 4C1 ring conformation. This suggests that, should such folds be present, they need not compromise bulk crystalline integrity. No significant fibril twisting was observed in either the unfolded or chain-fold models. At the chain-folding ends of the fibril, high-energy boat and skew-boat conformations drive hydroxymethyl rotamer redistribution and noncanonical hydrogen bonding contacts. The greater structural flexibility of the 5-residue folded turns allows the chain-fold ends to access a broader conformational space, yet the structural perturbation remains localized within the same 3-residue region as the 3-residue folded turns. The 3-residue turn is therefore considered the more plausible chain-fold structure for cellulose-II, should such a structure actually exist in reality.

摘要翻译

本研究探讨了在公认的纤维素-II结构中链折叠的结构可行性。我们将3残基和5残基折叠的葡萄糖基转角纳入纤维素-II原纤维晶体模型，以评估主导结构是否能够得以保留。通过元动力学模拟生成了十个候选转角模型，并依据半经验量子力学能量进行排序。在超过30条独立的300 ns分子动力学轨迹中，晶体核心保持了其晶胞参数、X射线衍射图谱、氢键网络以及经典的4C1环构象。这表明，若此类折叠确实存在，它们未必会损害块体晶体的完整性。在未折叠模型和链折叠模型中均未观察到显著的原纤维扭转。在原纤维的链折叠末端，高能量的船式和扭船式构象驱动羟甲基旋转异构体重新分布及非经典氢键接触。5残基折叠转角具有更大的结构灵活性，使得链折叠末端能够进入更广泛的构象空间，但结构扰动仍局限于与3残基折叠转角相同的3残基区域内。因此，若此类结构在现实中确实存在，3残基转角被认为是纤维素-II更合理的链折叠结构。
