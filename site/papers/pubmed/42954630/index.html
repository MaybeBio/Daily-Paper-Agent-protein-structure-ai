Multiscale Modeling of Lytic Polysaccharide Monooxygenases in Oxidized and Reduced Redox States.

Szleper-Shendy, Katarzyna; Escorcia, Andres M; Kumar, Ravi; Gora, Artur; Stein, Matthias
The journal of physical chemistry. B · 2026-10-29 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文用MD、QM/MM和EPR计算多尺度模拟LPMO铜氧化还原态的结构响应，虽对象为酶催化机制而非典型蛋白结构预测/设计，但聚焦蛋白活性位点构象动态与计算建模，方法可迁移至蛋白结构计算研究。

摘要

Lytic polysaccharide monooxygenases (LPMOs) are copper-dependent oxidative enzymes whose catalytic activity depends on redox cycling of a single copper ion in the active site, coordinated by the conserved histidine brace motif. Although the static coordination geometry of the active site has been characterized crystallographically, the dynamic response of the histidine brace to Cu(I)/Cu(II) cycling and the role of second-sphere residues in modulating this response remain poorly understood at the atomistic level. Here, we address this gap using a multiscale computational approach combining molecular dynamics (MD) simulations, QM/MM optimization, and EPR parameter calculations, applied to two representative LPMOs: NcAA9C (AA9, axial tyrosine) and BaAA10A (AA10, axial phenylalanine). The structural response to the change in oxidation state is seen almost entirely in the exogenous ligands rather than in the histidine brace itself, with the oxidized form showing markedly higher and longer-lived water coordination. In BaAA10A, the equatorial water is anchored by a second-sphere glutamate with no counterpart in NcAA9C. QM/MM electrostatic perturbation analysis identifies Asp76 (NcAA9C) and Glu68 (BaAA10A) as the dominant stabilizing residues of the catalytic core, acting through water-mediated hydrogen-bond bridges that are more pronounced in the Cu(II) state. Computed EPR parameters respond to the displacement of the copper site from a planar geometry, and the equatorial water position was shown to have the dominant effect, differentiating the two LPMOs.

摘要翻译

裂解性多糖单加氧酶（LPMOs）是依赖铜的氧化酶，其催化活性依赖于活性位点中单个铜离子的氧化还原循环，该铜离子由保守的组氨酸支架基序配位。尽管活性位点的静态配位几何结构已通过晶体学表征，但组氨酸支架对Cu(I)/Cu(II)循环的动态响应以及第二配位层残基在调节该响应中的作用，在原子水平上仍知之甚少。在此，我们通过结合分子动力学（MD）模拟、QM/MM优化和EPR参数计算的多尺度计算方法，应用于两种代表性LPMOs：NcAA9C（AA9，轴向酪氨酸）和BaAA10A（AA10，轴向苯丙氨酸），来解决这一空白。对氧化态变化的结构响应几乎完全体现在外源配体上，而非组氨酸支架本身，其中氧化态形式表现出显著更高且更持久的水配位。在BaAA10A中，赤道水由第二配位层谷氨酸锚定，而NcAA9C中无对应残基。QM/MM静电扰动分析将Asp76（NcAA9C）和Glu68（BaAA10A）识别为催化核心的主要稳定残基，通过水介导的氢键桥发挥作用，这些氢键桥在Cu(II)态中更为显著。计算的EPR参数响应于铜位点偏离平面几何的位移，且赤道水位置被证明具有主导效应，从而区分了这两种LPMOs。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract describes a coherent and potentially useful computational comparison of two LPMO scaffolds in two redox states, and the framing around second-sphere control of the copper site is timely. However, the supplied material is an abstract only, and several of the central claims are stated at a level of specificity (dominant residues, dominant EPR determinant, redox-dependent water lifetimes) that cannot be assessed without methods, convergence evidence, and numerical results. The work is interesting in principle but currently unverifiable from the provided evidence.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Computational biochemists and biophysicists working on copper enzymes and metalloprotein active sites; enzymologists studying LPMO mechanism and redox cycling; researchers using QM/MM and EPR parameter prediction as a combined tool; and the broader polysaccharide-degradation and biomass-conversion community, for whom second-sphere effects on LPMO reactivity are mechanistically relevant.
  • Major strengths The comparison is well chosen, pairing an AA9 member with an axial tyrosine against an AA10 member with an axial phenylalanine, which provides a natural contrast for testing second-sphere contributions. Combining MD, QM/MM, and EPR parameter calculations is an appropriate multiscale strategy for a paramagnetic copper site where geometry and electronic structure are coupled. The stated focus on exogenous ligand dynamics rather than the histidine brace itself is a specific and testable mechanistic proposition rather than a generic structural description.
