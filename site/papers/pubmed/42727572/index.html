Computational investigation of ternary Mg-Co-B compounds as cathode materials for Mg-ion batteries.

Paitandi, Somnath; Upadhyay, Shivansh; Bhattacharyya, Kalishankar
Physical chemistry chemical physics : PCCP · 2026-09-11 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文研究Mg-Co-B三元硼化物作为镁离子电池正极材料，核心对象为无机晶体材料而非蛋白质，虽用结构预测和机器学习势，但问题与方法均不涉及蛋白质结构计算，与课题无关。

摘要

The sluggish development of Mg-ion batteries is largely attributed to the scarcity of cathode materials that concurrently offer high specific capacity, suitable intercalation voltages, and low Mg-ion migration barriers. In this work, a structure prediction workflow combining an evolutionary algorithm with convex hull analysis was employed to generate and screen Mg-Co-B ternary boride materials, from which three thermodynamically stable compounds MgCoB, MgCo(3)B(2), and Mg(3)Co(3)B(2) were identified. The dynamical, thermodynamic, and ground-state stability of all three compounds was thoroughly confirmed through phonon dispersion calculations, Gibbs free energy analysis, and convex hull formation energies, respectively. To efficiently screen the large number of possible symmetry inequivalent Mg-extraction configurations, a machine learning interatomic potential was employed for structural exploration, followed by DFT calculations for accurate energetic evaluation, thereby substantially reducing the computational cost. Subsequent DFT-based electrochemical analysis reveals that Mg(3)Co(3)B(2) is electrochemically unsuitable due to anomalously large negative intercalation voltages, while MgCo(3)B(2) is limited by a prohibitively high Mg-ion migration barrier of 0.75 eV. In contrast, MgCoB emerges as the most promising material, exhibiting a theoretical specific capacity of 356.2 mAh g(-1), a gravimetric energy density of 278 Wh kg(-1), and a substantially reduced Mg-ion migration barrier of 0.35 eV, depicting it as a highly competitive cathode material for next-generation Mg-ion batteries.

摘要翻译

镁离子电池发展缓慢，主要归因于缺乏同时具备高比容量、合适插层电压和低镁离子迁移势垒的正极材料。本研究采用结合进化算法与凸包分析的结构预测工作流程，生成并筛选了Mg-Co-B三元硼化物材料，从中鉴定出三种热力学稳定的化合物：MgCoB、MgCo(3)B(2)和Mg(3)Co(3)B(2)。通过声子色散计算、吉布斯自由能分析和凸包形成能，分别全面确认了这三种化合物的动力学、热力学和基态稳定性。为高效筛选大量可能的不等价镁脱出构型，采用机器学习原子间势进行结构探索，随后通过DFT计算进行精确能量评估，从而大幅降低计算成本。后续基于DFT的电化学分析表明，Mg(3)Co(3)B(2)因插层电压异常负而不适合电化学应用，而MgCo(3)B(2)则受限于0.75 eV的过高镁离子迁移势垒。相比之下，MgCoB成为最具前景的材料，其理论比容量为356.2 mAh g(-1)，重量能量密度为278 Wh kg(-1)，且镁离子迁移势垒显著降低至0.35 eV，使其成为下一代镁离子电池中极具竞争力的正极材料。
