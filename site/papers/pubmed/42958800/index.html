8

Supramolecular assembly and membrane anchoring alter variant-induced SARS-CoV-2 Spike conformational dynamics.

Gramm, Amanda J; Braet, Sean M; Srinivasu, Bindu Y; Venkatakrishnan, Varun; Bass, Elijah J; Kearns, Fiona L; Amaro, Rommie E; Stahelin, Robert V; Wales, Thomas E; Anand, Ganesh S
Biophysical journal · 2026-10-30 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文结合HDXMS与加权系综分子动力学模拟，研究膜锚定和病毒样颗粒组装如何改变SARS-CoV-2 Spike蛋白变体的构象动态，核心聚焦蛋白质结构计算问题，方法涉及MD模拟，对象为蛋白结构，但偏重实验与模拟结合，方法创新性中等。

摘要

Viral surface glycoproteins function within crowded, membrane-anchored supramolecular assemblies, yet their dynamics are often inferred from soluble, engineered ectodomains. For SARS-CoV-2 Spike (S), this distinction is especially important because variant mutations evolved in the context of intact virions, not engineered trimers. Here, we combined cyclic ion mobility mass spectrometry (CIM-MS) for hydrogen deuterium exchange mass spectrometry (HDXMS) with computational weighted ensemble molecular dynamic (WE-MD) simulations to compare the conformational effects of SARS-CoV-2 mutations in two S protein constructs: soluble recombinant 2P S and full-length S displayed in virus-like particles (VLPs). Our results reveal that membrane anchoring and virion-like assembly substantially dampen global dynamic changes produced by variant mutations. In VLP S, D614G induced decreased dynamics across interprotomer contacts, whereas Omicron BA.1 mutations systematically reversed this decrease in dynamics in the S1 domain while preserving decreased dynamics near the fusion machinery. These effects are more spatially constrained than those observed in recombinant 2P S. Comparison of the 2 constructs further reveals that 2P substitutions alter native interprotomer communication. Recombinant S shows a predominantly fast-exchanging population in the receptor binding domain (RBD) hinge region, consistent with disruption of hydrogen-bonding and salt bridge networks that couple RBD motion to the trimer core. Thus, recombinant S captures important structural features of S without recapitulating the native conformational ensemble presented on virions. Together, these results show that the supramolecular context of S reshapes the conformational dynamics of variant evolution with direct implications for epitope presentation, antibody recognition, and interpretation of recombinant S structural studies.

摘要翻译

病毒表面糖蛋白在拥挤的、膜锚定的超分子组装体中发挥功能，然而其动力学往往是从可溶性、工程化的胞外域推断而来。对于SARS-CoV-2刺突蛋白（Spike, S），这一区别尤为重要，因为变异突变是在完整病毒粒子的背景下进化而来的，而非工程化三聚体。在此，我们将用于氢氘交换质谱（hydrogen deuterium exchange mass spectrometry, HDXMS）的环状离子迁移质谱（cyclic ion mobility mass spectrometry, CIM-MS）与计算加权系综分子动力学（weighted ensemble molecular dynamics, WE-MD）模拟相结合，比较SARS-CoV-2突变在两种S蛋白构建体中的构象效应：可溶性重组2P S和展示在病毒样颗粒（virus-like particles, VLPs）中的全长S。我们的结果揭示，膜锚定和病毒粒子样组装大幅削弱了变异突变所产生的全局动力学变化。在VLP S中，D614G诱导原体间接触处的动力学降低，而Omicron BA.1突变系统性地逆转了S1结构域中这种动力学降低，同时保留了融合机器附近的动力学降低。这些效应在空间上比在重组2P S中观察到的更为受限。两种构建体的比较进一步揭示，2P取代改变了天然的原体间通讯。重组S在受体结合域（receptor binding domain, RBD）铰链区显示出以快速交换为主的群体，这与将RBD运动偶联至三聚体核心的氢键和盐桥网络的破坏一致。因此，重组S捕获了S的重要结构特征，但未能重现病毒粒子上呈现的天然构象系综。总之，这些结果表明，S的超分子背景重塑了变异进化的构象动力学，对表位呈递、抗体识别以及重组S结构研究的解读具有直接影响。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a potentially important finding that the supramolecular context of SARS-CoV-2 Spike alters the conformational dynamics associated with variant mutations, with implications for interpreting recombinant protein studies. The combination of experimental HDXMS with computational WE-MD is appropriate and potentially powerful. However, the abstract alone does not provide sufficient methodological detail, quantitative results, or statistical validation to assess the robustness of the claims. The central conclusion that membrane anchoring "reshapes" variant-induced dynamics is plausible but requires substantially more evidence than is visible here.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Structural biologists studying viral glycoproteins, biophysicists using HDXMS or enhanced sampling simulations, SARS-CoV-2 researchers investigating variant evolution and antibody recognition, and developers of recombinant protein reagents for vaccine and therapeutic development would find these results relevant. The work challenges the common practice of using soluble ectodomains as proxies for native viral surface proteins.
