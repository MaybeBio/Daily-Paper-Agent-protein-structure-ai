The AML-Associated A338V Mutation Rewires GPR183 Signaling through C-Terminal Ensemble Redistribution.

Andersson, Louise; Wesolowski, Patryk A; Jahrstorfer, Laura; Rosa, Alexander De; Heger, Tomas; Neuman, Vilmos; Sieradzan, Adam K; Wales, David J; Kozielewicz, Pawel
Journal of the American Chemical Society · 2026-10-23 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该研究结合分子动力学模拟与实验，解析AML相关GPR183 A338V突变通过重塑C端无序区构象ensemble选择性调控G蛋白与arrestin信号，属于蛋白质构象计算与功能关联研究，对GPCR构象采样方法有参考价值。

摘要

G protein-coupled receptors (GPCRs) rely on dynamic conformational ensembles to coordinate G protein activation and recruitment of regulatory transducers such as GPCR kinases and beta-arrestins. The oxysterol receptor GPR183 has been implicated in hematological malignancies, including acute myeloid leukemia (AML), where a rare A338V substitution occurs within its intrinsically disordered C-terminal tail. Here, we combined bioluminescence/fluorescence resonance energy transfer, bioluminescence- and fluorescence-based assays as well as migration experiments, with molecular dynamics simulations and allosteric communication networks to define the structural and functional consequences of this mutation. The A338V variant preserved agonist potency and largely maintained or even enhanced agonist-induced Gi protein activation, likely by inducing different receptor core conformations. On the other hand, it consistently impaired GRK2 and beta-arrestin2 recruitment across multiple experimental configurations. Atomistic simulations supported these observations and further suggested that A338V reshapes the conformational ensemble of the C-terminal tail, altering its disorder, compaction, and membrane interactions. In particular, the mutation redistributes lipid interactions of phosphorylation-prone serine and threonine residues within the tail, suggesting disrupted spatial presentation of regulatory motifs required for productive GRK2 and beta-arrestin coupling. Importantly, overexpression of GPR183 A338V in differentiated HL-60 cells led to a significant increase in migration of these cells toward the chemoattractant. Together, these results identify the intrinsically disordered C-terminal tail of GPR183 as a determinant of selective transducer coupling and show how a single naturally occurring mutation within a GPCR intrinsically disordered region can alter its conformational ensemble, selectively modulate intracellular protein coupling, and affect receptor-mediated cellular behavior.

摘要翻译

G蛋白偶联受体（GPCRs）依赖动态构象集合来协调G蛋白激活及调控性转导因子（如GPCR激酶和β-arrestins）的招募。氧固醇受体GPR183已被涉及血液恶性肿瘤，包括急性髓系白血病（AML），其中在其 intrinsically disordered C末端尾部发生罕见的A338V替换。在此，我们结合生物发光/荧光共振能量转移、基于生物发光和荧光的检测以及迁移实验，与分子动力学模拟和变构通信网络相结合，以确定该突变的结构和功能后果。A338V变体保留了激动剂效力，并在很大程度上维持甚至增强了激动剂诱导的Gi蛋白激活，可能通过诱导不同的受体核心构象实现。另一方面，在多种实验配置中，它一致地损害了GRK2和β-arrestin2的招募。原子级模拟支持这些观察结果，并进一步表明A338V重塑了C末端尾部的构象集合，改变其无序性、压缩性和膜相互作用。特别是，该突变重新分配了尾部内易磷酸化的丝氨酸和苏氨酸残基的脂质相互作用，提示破坏了对有效GRK2和β-arrestin偶联所需的调控基序的空间呈现。重要的是，在分化的HL-60细胞中过表达GPR183 A338V导致这些细胞向趋化因子的迁移显著增加。总之，这些结果将GPR183的 intrinsically disordered C末端尾部确定为选择性转导因子偶联的决定因素，并展示了GPCR intrinsically disordered区域内单个自然发生的突变如何改变其构象集合、选择性调节细胞内蛋白偶联，并影响受体介导的细胞行为。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：GPR183 的 A338V 突变（位于 C 端 intrinsically disordered region）如何从分子层面改变受体信号输出？
  • 为什么重要：GPR183 与 AML 等血液肿瘤相关，A338V 是罕见自然突变；理解其机制有助于揭示 IDR 在 GPCR 信号选择性中的作用，并可能指导靶向治疗。
  • 现有方法不足：传统 GPCR 研究聚焦于跨膜核心（7TM domain）的构象变化，对 C 端 IDR 的功能关注不足；静态结构（如 cryo-EM）难以捕捉 IDR 的动态 ensemble 特性。
  • 精确研究问题：Can a single disease-associated mutation in a GPCR intrinsically disordered C-terminal tail selectively rewire transducer coupling by redistributing the conformational ensemble of the tail?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：结合 BRET/FRET 实验（检测 Gi 激活、GRK2/β-arrestin2 招募）、MD 模拟（分析 C 端 tail 构象 ensemble）和细胞迁移实验，系统比较 A338V 与野生型 GPR183 的功能差异。
  2. 核心洞察：A338V 突变并非简单地「增强或减弱」信号，而是通过改变 C 端 IDR 的 ensemble 分布（disorder、compaction、膜相互作用），选择性地重新分配 transducer 的招募——Gi 通路保留/增强，而 GRK2/β-arrestin 通路受损。这提示 IDR 的 ensemble 特性是 GPCR 信号偏向的分子基础。
  3. [Analysis] 可能的普适教训：对于任何含 IDR 的蛋白质，单点突变的功能影响不能仅从静态结构或单一构象推断，而应视为 ensemble 的重新分布。在蛋白质结构预测/设计中，需考虑 IDR 的动态 ensemble 而非单一折叠态。

  1. 候选名称：IDR 突变功能预测的 ensemble 评分模型 - 来源局限/观察：本文未提供定量预测模型，MD 模拟成本高。 - 核心假设：IDR 的 ensemble 特征（如 disorder score、compaction、膜接触频率）可作为突变功能影响的预测因子。 - 初步方法：对已知 GPCR IDR 突变进行 MD 模拟，提取 ensemble 特征，训练分类器预测功能偏向（Gi vs arrestin）。 - 验证方式：用 BRET 实验验证预测结果。 - 创新状态：unverified

  2. 候选名称：膜相互作用介导的 IDR 磷酸化可及性调控 - 来源局限/观察：本文发现 A338V 改变 Ser/Thr 的膜接触，但未直接检测磷酸化状态。 - 核心假设：IDR 的膜结合动态决定 GRK2 磷酸化效率，进而调控 arrestin 招募。 - 初步方法：使用增强采样 MD（如 metadynamics）模拟 tail 与膜的结合自由能面，结合体外磷酸化实验。 - 验证方式：比较 WT 与突变体的磷酸化速率。 - 创新状态：unverified

  3. 候选名称：GPCR IDR 突变数据库与 ensemble 分析平台 - 来源局限/观察：本文仅研究一个突变，缺乏系统性。 - 核心假设：多个疾病相关 IDR 突变可能共享 ensemble redistribution 机制。 - 初步方法：收集 GPCR IDR 突变，统一进行 MD 模拟和特征提取，建立公开数据库。 - 验证方式：与已知功能数据交叉验证。 - 创新状态：unverified

  4. 候选名称：IDR 序列设计以调控 GPCR 信号偏向 - 来源局限/观察：A338V 显示 IDR 序列可微调信号输出。 - 核心假设：通过设计 IDR 序列（如改变 Ser/Thr 密度或疏水性），可定向增强或减弱 arrestin 招募。 - 初步方法：基于本文的 ensemble 分析，设计一系列 IDR 变体，用 BRET 筛选。 - 验证方式：功能实验 + MD 验证。 - 创新状态：unverified

  5. 候选名称：AlphaFold 与 MD 结合的 IDR ensemble 预测流程 - 来源局限/观察：本文未说明初始结构来源，AlphaFold 对 IDR 预测不可靠。 - 核心假设：使用 AlphaFold 预测核心结构 + 增强采样 MD 采样 IDR，可得到可靠 ensemble。 - 初步方法：开发自动化流程，测试不同力场和采样方法。 - 验证方式：与 NMR 或 HDX 数据对比。 - 创新状态：unverified

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a potentially interesting finding that a single disease-associated mutation in a GPCR intrinsically disordered region can selectively modulate transducer coupling and cellular behavior. The combination of experimental and computational approaches is appropriate for the question. However, the abstract alone does not provide sufficient detail to evaluate the rigor of the methods, the robustness of the statistical analyses, or the strength of the causal links between the observed conformational changes and functional outcomes. The claim that the mutation "rewires" signaling through "C-terminal ensemble redistribution" is plausible but not fully established from the provided evidence.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers in GPCR biology, particularly those studying biased agonism, intrinsically disordered regions in signaling proteins, and the molecular basis of disease-associated mutations. The work may also interest investigators focused on AML biology and oxysterol receptor signaling, as well as computational biologists studying allosteric communication in membrane proteins.
  • Major strengths The study addresses a timely and important question about the functional role of intrinsically disordered regions in GPCR signaling. The use of multiple orthogonal experimental assays (BRET, FRET, bioluminescence, fluorescence, migration) combined with molecular dynamics simulations and allosteric network analysis is commendable. The finding that a single mutation can selectively impair GRK2 and beta-arrestin2 recruitment while preserving or enhancing Gi activation suggests a potential mechanism for biased signaling.
  • Major Concerns
  • Concern ID R1-M1
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Technical soundness
  • Claim pointer The abstract claims that A338V "consistently impaired GRK2 and beta-arrestin2 recruitment across multiple experimental configurations" and that this is due to "disrupted spatial presentation of regulatory motifs."
  • Evidence pointer Abstract text; specific figures and statistical details not provided
  • Concern The abstract does not report effect sizes, statistical significance, or the number of independent replicates for the BRET/FRET recruitment assays. It is unclear whether the impairment is modest or substantial, and whether the "multiple experimental configurations" are truly independent or variations of the same assay.
  • Why it matters Without quantitative details, the reader cannot assess whether the observed impairment is biologically meaningful or within experimental noise. The causal link between altered lipid interactions of phosphorylation-prone residues and impaired recruitment is inferred from simulations but not directly tested experimentally.
  • Resolution test Provide effect sizes with confidence intervals, exact p-values, and replicate numbers for all recruitment assays. Include a direct experimental test of the proposed mechanism, such as mutation of the phosphorylation-prone residues or manipulation of membrane lipid composition.

  • Claim pointer The abstract claims that "atomistic simulations supported these observations and further suggested that A338V reshapes the conformational ensemble of the C-terminal tail, altering its disorder, compaction, and membrane interactions."
  • Evidence pointer Abstract text; simulation details not provided
  • Concern No information is given on simulation length, force field, number of replicas, convergence criteria, or how the "allosteric communication networks" were constructed. The abstract does not state whether the simulations were validated against experimental data or whether the observed conformational differences are statistically robust across independent simulations.
  • Why it matters Molecular dynamics simulations of intrinsically disordered regions are notoriously sensitive to force field choice and sampling length. Without these details, the computational claims cannot be evaluated, and the "support" provided by simulations for the experimental observations remains anecdotal.
  • Resolution test Provide full simulation parameters, convergence metrics, and a statistical comparison of conformational ensembles between wild-type and mutant. Ideally, include experimental validation of the predicted changes in tail dynamics or membrane interactions.
