Distinct Lipid Binding Dynamics and Sodium Ion Coordination in the TRAP Transporter HiSiaQM Revealed by Multiscale Molecular Dynamics Simulations.

Quan, Senwei; Davies, James S; North, Rachel A; Hall, Kelsi R; Turk, Liam S; Newton-Vesty, Michael C; Dobson, Renwick C J; Allison, Jane R
Journal of chemical information and modeling · 2026-10-22 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文通过多尺度分子动力学模拟研究TRAP转运蛋白HiSiaQM的脂质结合动力学和钠离子配位机制，属于蛋白质结构相关的计算模拟研究，方法为物理模拟，对象和问题均聚焦于膜蛋白结构功能机制。

摘要

Tripartite ATP-independent periplasmic (TRAP) transporters are substrate-binding protein dependent secondary transporters that leverage ion gradients to facilitate substrate translocation across bacterial and archaeal membranes. Although the structure of HiSiaQM horizontal line a membrane-embedded TRAP subunit from the human pathogen Haemophilus influenzae horizontal line has recently been determined, its transport mechanism is not fully understood. Given the likely importance of lipid interactions and ion coordination dynamics to the transport cycle, we applied multiscale molecular dynamics simulations to investigate these two complementary aspects of HiSiaQM function. Our simulations validated an experimentally identified lipid-binding site (BS1) and provided insight into its lipid-binding dynamics. BS1 is preferentially occupied by the anionic POPG, which is stabilized through persistent hydrogen bonds involving residues H100, R104, and R181. Additionally, our simulations revealed a novel, less stable lipid-binding site (BS2) that is selectively occupied by POPE. We suggest distinct lipid-binding behaviors for POPG and POPE, with POPG forming more persistent interactions at BS1 and POPE engaging more transiently at BS2; however, these remain to be tested experimentally. Analysis of sodium ion coordination at two known binding sites (Na1 and Na2) revealed contrasting dynamics: Na1 exhibited stable ion retention through backbone carbonyl coordination, while Na2 displayed lower stability due to involvement of the flexible side chain of T561. Geometric tunnel analysis identified possible sodium-exit pathways, supporting a working hypothesis that Na1 may act as a stable anchoring site and Na2 may function as a transient ion-exchange point during the transport cycle. Collectively, our findings provide mechanistic insights into how specific lipid interactions and sodium ion coordination could modulate HiSiaQM transporter function.

摘要翻译

三组分ATP非依赖性周质（TRAP）转运蛋白是依赖底物结合蛋白的次级转运蛋白，利用离子梯度促进底物跨细菌和古菌膜转运。尽管人类病原体流感嗜血杆菌（Haemophilus influenzae）中膜嵌入的TRAP亚基HiSiaQM的结构最近已被解析，但其转运机制尚未完全阐明。鉴于脂质相互作用和离子配位动力学对转运循环可能具有重要意义，我们采用多尺度分子动力学（molecular dynamics）模拟来研究HiSiaQM功能的这两个互补方面。我们的模拟验证了一个实验鉴定的脂质结合位点（BS1），并提供了对其脂质结合动力学的见解。BS1优先被阴离子型POPG占据，该结合通过涉及残基H100、R104和R181的持久氢键得以稳定。此外，我们的模拟揭示了一个新的、稳定性较低的脂质结合位点（BS2），该位点被POPE选择性占据。我们提出POPG和POPE具有不同的脂质结合行为，其中POPG在BS1处形成更持久的相互作用，而POPE在BS2处更短暂地结合；然而，这些仍有待实验验证。对两个已知结合位点（Na1和Na2）处钠离子配位的分析揭示了对比鲜明的动力学：Na1通过主链羰基配位表现出稳定的离子保留，而Na2由于涉及T561柔性侧链而显示出较低的稳定性。几何隧道分析识别了可能的钠离子出口路径，支持了一个工作假说，即Na1可能在转运循环中充当稳定锚定位点，而Na2可能作为瞬时离子交换点。总体而言，我们的发现提供了关于特定脂质相互作用和钠离子配位如何调节HiSiaQM转运蛋白功能的机制性见解。

02 一句话总结

本文通过 multiscale MD 模拟，验证了 HiSiaQM 中已知脂质结合位点 BS1 对 POPG 的优先占据（由 H100/R104/R181 形成持久氢键），发现了一个新的 POPE 选择性弱结合位点 BS2，并揭示 Na1 位点通过主链羰基稳定配位而 Na2 位点因 T561 柔性侧链而配位不稳定，据此提出 Na1 为稳定锚定位点、Na2 为瞬时离子交换点的转运周期工作假说。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：TRAP 转运体 HiSiaQM 的转运周期中，脂质相互作用和钠离子配位动力学如何参与并调节其功能？
  • 为什么重要：TRAP 转运体依赖离子梯度驱动底物跨膜转运，但其转运机制尚未完全阐明。脂质-蛋白相互作用和离子配位动态是膜转运蛋白功能的核心调节因素，理解这些动态有助于揭示 TRAP 转运体的工作机制，并可能为抗菌药物设计提供靶点（HiSiaQM 来自人类病原体流感嗜血杆菌）。
  • 现有方法为何不足：HiSiaQM 的结构虽已解析（静态快照），但静态结构无法揭示脂质结合的时间演化、离子配位的稳定性差异以及可能的离子释放路径。实验方法（如突变、结合实验）难以在原子尺度上捕捉这些动态过程。
  • 精确研究问题：Can multiscale MD simulations reveal distinct lipid-binding dynamics at known and novel sites, and contrasting sodium ion coordination stabilities at Na1 and Na2, that support a working model of HiSiaQM transport cycle?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：对 HiSiaQM 进行 multiscale MD 模拟（可能包含粗粒化与全原子模拟），分析脂质结合位点（BS1、BS2）的占据偏好与氢键持久性，分析钠离子结合位点（Na1、Na2）的配位几何与稳定性，并通过几何 tunnel 分析寻找离子释放路径。
  2. 核心洞察：脂质结合位点具有脂质种类选择性（BS1 偏好 POPG、BS2 偏好 POPE），且不同位点的结合动态差异显著（持久 vs 瞬时）；钠离子配位稳定性由配位残基的化学性质决定（主链羰基稳定 vs 柔性侧链不稳定），这种差异可能对应转运周期中不同的功能角色（锚定 vs 交换）。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：膜蛋白功能位点的动态行为（而非静态结构）往往是功能分工的关键；脂质选择性可由特定残基的氢键网络决定；离子配位稳定性差异可作为转运机制中功能分工的判据。这些思路可迁移到其他膜转运蛋白、离子通道或受体-配体体系的研究中。

Major Concerns

  • Concern ID R1-M1
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Technical soundness
  • Claim pointer The simulations validated an experimentally identified lipid-binding site (BS1) and provided insight into its lipid-binding dynamics.
  • Evidence pointer Abstract, location not provided
  • Concern The abstract states that BS1 was validated and that its dynamics were characterized, but no information is provided about simulation length, number of replicates, force field parameters, lipid bilayer composition, or convergence criteria. Without these details, it is impossible to assess whether the simulations adequately sampled the relevant conformational space or whether the observed lipid binding events are statistically meaningful rather than stochastic artifacts.
  • Why it matters Molecular dynamics simulations of membrane proteins are highly sensitive to system setup and sampling. Claims of validation and dynamic characterization require evidence that the simulations reached equilibrium, that multiple independent runs produced consistent results, and that the chosen force field and membrane model are appropriate for the system. The absence of this information undermines confidence in the central findings.

  • Resolution test Provide simulation protocols including system size, simulation time per replicate, number of replicates, force field and water model, lipid composition, ion concentration, and temperature/pressure coupling schemes. Show convergence metrics such as root-mean-square deviation over time, lipid occupancy as a function of simulation time, and replicate-to-replicate variability.

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Evidence strength
  • Claim pointer BS1 is preferentially occupied by the anionic POPG, which is stabilized through persistent hydrogen bonds involving residues H100, R104, and R181. A novel, less stable lipid-binding site (BS2) is selectively occupied by POPE.
  • Evidence pointer Abstract, location not provided
  • Concern The claims of preferential and selective lipid occupancy are presented without quantitative data.   • Why it matters Lipid selectivity in membrane protein simulations can be influenced by initial conditions, lipid composition, and simulation length. Without quantitative occupancy data and appropriate statistical tests, the claim of preferential binding cannot be distinguished from random fluctuation or setup bias. This is a core finding of the study and must be rigorously supported.

  • Resolution test Report occupancy fractions for each lipid type at each site, residence time distributions, and statistical comparisons (e.g., standard errors across replicates or bootstrapped confidence intervals). If possible, provide potential of mean force calculations or free energy estimates for lipid binding at each site.
