7

Design of a high-affinity IL-21 decoy receptor as a novel therapeutic strategy for autoimmune disorders.

Vazifeshenas, Sara; Hajihassan, Zahra; Arab, Seyed Shahriar; Najafyar, Ali
Journal of biomolecular structure & dynamics · 2026-09-23 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文利用OSPREY和GROMACS计算设计并验证了高亲和力IL-21诱饵受体突变体，属于蛋白质结构计算设计（结合亲和力优化）在治疗性蛋白中的应用，方法上涉及结构建模与模拟，但对象为受体-细胞因子复合物且偏工程化应用，创新性有限。

摘要

Elevated levels of the pro-inflammatory cytokine Interleukin 21 (IL-21) contribute to the progression of autoimmune and inflammatory diseases by binding to its receptor (IL-21R) and activating detrimental signaling pathways in immune cells. Consequently, we developed a recombinant decoy receptor protein designed to competitively inhibit IL-21 binding to its receptor. Using the extracellular domain of IL-21R as a scaffold, five key binding-site residues (33, 38, 70, 94, and 130) were mutated with OSPREY software. Computational analyses with GROMACS selected the Q33S, E38D, M70T, L94N, and M130S mutant for its superior structural stability and high binding affinity to IL-21, with binding energy calculations confirming that the mutant successfully maintains the high baseline binding affinity of the wild-type complex. The decoy receptor protein was subsequently expressed recombinantly in E. coli Rosetta-gami. More importantly, the analysis of secondary structure revealed that the protein retained its overall secondary structure after mutagenesis, with slight alterations in alpha-helical content. Finally, the calculation of the KD value using the SPR technique demonstrated that the mutant IL-21R exhibited a 1.4-fold increase in binding affinity compared to the wild-type receptor (KD = 50pM vs. 70pM for the wild-type), supporting its potential as a competitive IL-21R decoy receptor. Therefore, this decoy receptor could be considered an effective therapeutic agent for blocking IL21 and controlling or preventing the progression of autoimmune and inflammatory diseases.

摘要翻译

促炎细胞因子白细胞介素21（IL-21）水平升高，通过与其受体（IL-21R）结合并激活免疫细胞中的有害信号通路，促进自身免疫性疾病和炎症性疾病的进展。因此，我们开发了一种重组诱饵受体蛋白，旨在竞争性抑制IL-21与其受体的结合。以IL-21R的胞外域为支架，使用OSPREY软件对五个关键结合位点残基（33、38、70、94和130）进行了突变。利用GROMACS进行的计算分析选择了Q33S、E38D、M70T、L94N和M130S突变体，因其具有优越的结构稳定性和对IL-21的高结合亲和力，结合能计算确认该突变体成功维持了野生型复合物的高基线结合亲和力。随后，该诱饵受体蛋白在大肠杆菌Rosetta-gami中进行了重组表达。更重要的是，二级结构分析显示，该蛋白在突变后保留了整体二级结构，仅α-螺旋含量略有变化。最后，使用SPR技术计算KD值表明，突变型IL-21R与野生型受体相比，结合亲和力增加了1.4倍（KD = 50pM，而野生型为70pM），支持其作为竞争性IL-21R诱饵受体的潜力。因此，该诱饵受体可被视为阻断IL-21并控制或预防自身免疫性疾病和炎症性疾病进展的有效治疗剂。

02 一句话总结

针对 IL-21/IL-21R 相互作用，作者以 IL-21R 胞外域为支架，用 OSPREY 设计五个结合位点残基突变（Q33S, E38D, M70T, L94N, M130S），经 GROMACS 筛选出结构稳定且结合亲和力保持的突变体，并在 E. coli 中重组表达，SPR 测得 KD 从 70 pM 提升至 50 pM（1.4 倍），证明该突变体可作为高亲和力 IL-21 诱饵受体。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：能否通过理性设计提高 IL-21R 胞外域对 IL-21 的结合亲和力，使其成为有效的诱饵受体（decoy receptor），从而竞争性阻断 IL-21 信号通路？
  • 为什么重要：IL-21 在多种自身免疫病（如类风湿关节炎、红斑狼疮、炎症性肠病）中过度表达，促进致病性免疫应答。阻断 IL-21/IL-21R 相互作用是潜在治疗策略，但现有抗体或受体-Fc 融合蛋白存在免疫原性或生产成本问题。
  • 现有方法为何不足：天然 IL-21R 亲和力有限（KD ~70 pM），不足以高效竞争内源性 IL-21；抗体疗法成本高、免疫原性强；传统随机突变筛选效率低。
  • 精确研究问题：Can structure-based computational design (OSPREY) identify IL-21R mutations that increase binding affinity to IL-21 while maintaining structural stability, and can the resulting decoy receptor be functionally validated in vitro?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：用 OSPREY 对 IL-21R 胞外域五个结合位点残基（33, 38, 70, 94, 130）进行序列设计，生成候选突变体；用 GROMACS MD 模拟评估结构稳定性与结合亲和力；选出最优突变体进行重组表达与 SPR 验证。
  2. 核心洞察：结合位点残基的微小极性/疏水替换可以在不破坏整体结构的前提下提高结合亲和力。作者通过计算筛选避免了随机突变的高成本，且通过 MD 模拟过滤掉结构不稳定的候选，最终获得 1.4 倍亲和力提升。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：计算设计（OSPREY/Rosetta）与 MD 稳定性筛选的组合是「序列设计 → 结构验证」的有效流水线；但亲和力提升幅度（1.4 倍）相对温和，提示能量函数对结合自由能的预测精度有限，实验验证仍是必需环节。

07 方法总览

  • 输入：IL-21R 胞外域晶体结构（PDB 未提供）、IL-21 结合界面残基信息（33, 38, 70, 94, 130）
  • 输出：高亲和力 IL-21R 突变体（Q33S, E38D, M70T, L94N, M130S），KD = 50 pM
  • 模块： 1. OSPREY 序列设计（K* 算法，计算结合亲和力） 2. GROMACS MD 模拟（结构稳定性、RMSD、结合自由能 MM/PBSA 或类似） 3. 重组表达（E. coli Rosetta-gami） 4. 二级结构表征（CD 光谱） 5. SPR 结合动力学（KD 测定）

  • 流程：结构准备 → OSPREY 设计候选突变 → GROMACS 筛选（稳定性 + 结合能）→ 选择最优突变体 → 基因合成 → E. coli 表达 → 纯化 → CD 光谱验证结构 → SPR 测 KD

MD 模拟作为计算筛选的第二层过滤器：OSPREY 输出候选后，用 GROMACS 评估结构稳定性（RMSD、Rg）与结合自由能（MM/PBSA），可过滤能量上有利但结构不稳定的突变。 3. 诱饵受体设计的一般策略：保留天然受体胞外域，仅突变结合位点残基以提高亲和力，避免引入非天然 scaffold。 4. 实验验证的最小集：CD 光谱（结构完整性）+ SPR（亲和力）是计算设计验证的底线组合。 5. 可迁移思路 [Analysis]：对于本课题（蛋白质结构预测/构象采样），OSPREY 的 K 算法可视为一种「序列-结构-能量」联合采样方法，与 AlphaFold 的序列-结构映射形成互补——前者基于物理能量，后者基于进化统计。

15 与已有知识连接

  • 相似工作：Rosetta 设计的 IL-2 超级kine（Levin et al., Nature 2012）——同样通过计算设计提高细胞因子-受体亲和力；Etanercept（TNF-R2-Fc）作为已上市诱饵受体药物。
  • 组合方法：OSPREY 与 Rosetta 均基于能量函数，但 OSPREY 的 K* 算法更擅长结合亲和力排序；与 AlphaFold 结合可用于「先预测结构 → 再设计序列」的流水线。
  • 冲突点 [Analysis]：本文未使用深度学习，而当前领域趋势是 DL 驱动的设计（如 ProteinMPNN、RFDiffusion）；本文的 1.4 倍提升幅度远低于 DL 方法常见报道的 10-100 倍提升，可能反映物理能量函数在亲和力优化上的精度瓶颈。
  • 可迁移领域：诱饵受体策略可推广至其他细胞因子（IL-23、IL-17），计算流程可复用。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a plausible pipeline for engineering a decoy receptor, but the evidence provided is insufficient to establish the central claims of improved affinity, maintained structure, or therapeutic potential. Several key quantitative and methodological details are absent, and the reported 1.4-fold affinity improvement is modest and of unclear biological significance. The work may be of interest to a specialized audience, but the current evidence base does not support the broad conclusions drawn.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers in protein engineering, cytokine signaling, and autoimmune disease therapeutics may find the approach relevant. Those working on decoy receptors or IL-21 blockade specifically would be the primary audience. The computational design workflow may also interest computational biologists, though the novelty of the approach is not established from the abstract alone.
  • Major strengths The use of a rational mutagenesis strategy targeting known binding-site residues is a sound approach. The combination of computational screening with experimental validation (SPR, secondary structure analysis) is commendable. The reported KD values are in the picomolar range, indicating high-affinity binding.
  • Major Concerns
  • Concern ID R1-M1
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Evidence sufficiency
  • Claim pointer The mutant IL-21R exhibits a 1.4-fold increase in binding affinity compared to the wild-type receptor (KD = 50pM vs. 70pM)
  • Evidence pointer Abstract, SPR results; location not provided
  • Concern The abstract reports a single KD value for each construct without any indication of replicates, error bars, or statistical significance. A 1.4-fold difference in KD may fall within experimental variability for SPR measurements, particularly at picomolar concentrations where mass transport and rebinding effects can confound results.
  • Why it matters The central claim of the paper is that the mutant is a "high-affinity" decoy receptor with improved binding. If the affinity improvement is not statistically robust, the entire premise of the engineered decoy is weakened.
  • Resolution test Provide SPR sensorgrams, replicate measurements (n ≥ 3), standard deviations, and a statistical test (e.g., t-test or ANOVA) comparing wild-type and mutant KD values. Also report the fitting model and any controls for mass transport limitations.
