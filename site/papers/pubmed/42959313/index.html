Ultrasound-assisted free radical grafting of sesamol onto whey protein isolate: Structural modifications, functional enhancements, and antioxidant properties.

Feng, Xiandan; Lv, Mengting; Tian, Mu; Li, Sining; Tang, Shanhu
Food chemistry: X · 2026-10-30 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该研究用超声辅助自由基法将芝麻酚接枝到乳清蛋白上，虽涉及蛋白结构修饰和分子对接，但核心是食品功能与抗氧化性质，结构计算仅作为辅助验证，与蛋白质结构计算课题关联较弱。

摘要

This study fabricated conjugates of whey protein isolate (WPI) and sesamol using an ultrasound-assisted free radical method. This approach significantly reduced the reaction time from 24 h to 90 min relative to the conventional free radical method, while achieving a comparable sesamol binding equivalent (4.65 mg/g). Structural analyses (amino acid side-chains, FTIR, XRD, surface hydrophobicity) revealed chemical modifications, as supported by reduced free thiol/amino groups and altered protein conformation under ultrasound. Molecular docking suggested potential hydrogen bonds and hydrophobic interactions between sesamol and WPI. Particle size and zeta-potential measurements revealed formation of larger, stable aggregates. Furthermore, WPI-sesamol conjugates exhibited superior functional properties, including protein solubility and emulsifying capacity, as well as enhanced antioxidant activity (DPPH* and ABTS(+)* scavenging), with ultrasound-assisted treatment yielding the most pronounced improvements. The ultrasound-assisted method establishes an efficient strategy for producing WPI-sesamol conjugates with superior functionality and antioxidant capacity, highlighting their potential as novel food ingredients.

摘要翻译

本研究采用超声辅助自由基法制备了乳清分离蛋白（WPI）与芝麻酚的缀合物。相较于传统自由基法，该方法将反应时间从24 h显著缩短至90 min，同时实现了相当的芝麻酚结合当量（4.65 mg/g）。结构分析（氨基酸侧链、FTIR、XRD、表面疏水性）揭示了化学修饰，游离巯基/氨基的减少以及超声下蛋白质构象的改变均支持这一结论。分子对接表明芝麻酚与WPI之间可能存在氢键和疏水相互作用。粒径和zeta电位测量揭示了较大且稳定的聚集体的形成。此外，WPI-芝麻酚缀合物表现出优异的功能特性，包括蛋白质溶解性和乳化能力，以及增强的抗氧化活性（DPPH*和ABTS(+)*清除），其中超声辅助处理产生了最显著的改善。超声辅助方法为生产具有优异功能性和抗氧化能力的WPI-芝麻酚缀合物建立了一种高效策略，凸显了其作为新型食品配料的潜力。
