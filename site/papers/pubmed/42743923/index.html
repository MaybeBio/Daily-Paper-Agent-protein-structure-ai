Sampling protein language models for functional protein design.

Darmawan, Jeremie Theddy; Gal, Yarin; Notin, Pascal
Cell systems · 2026-09-15 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文系统比较并提出了针对蛋白质设计的语言模型采样策略，通过多突变同时采样捕捉上位效应，并在多个蛋白家族及从头设计任务中验证，核心聚焦蛋白质序列设计这一结构计算问题，方法上使用蛋白语言模型，对象、问题、方法三者齐备。

摘要

Protein language models have emerged as powerful tools for learning rich protein representations, improving tasks like structure prediction, mutation effect estimation, and homology detection. Their ability to model complex sequence distributions also holds promise for designing novel, functional proteins with applications in therapeutics, materials, and sustainability. Given the vastness of sequence space, efficient exploration is essential. However, most existing design approaches rely on single-mutant sampling strategies borrowed from natural language processing, which fail to capture epistatic interactions critical for function. Here, we develop an in silico framework to systematically compare sampling methods and introduce several approaches tailored for protein design. We show that sampling multiple mutations simultaneously substantially outperforms single-mutant approaches by better capturing epistatic effects. We evaluate these strategies across three protein families spanning eukaryotic, prokaryotic, and viral origins, examine key hyperparameters, and validate our findings on a de novo binder design task against a major therapeutic target.

摘要翻译

蛋白质语言模型已成为学习丰富蛋白质表征的强大工具，改善了结构预测、突变效应估计和同源检测等任务。它们对复杂序列分布建模的能力也为设计具有治疗、材料和可持续性应用的新型功能性蛋白质提供了前景。鉴于序列空间的广阔性，高效探索至关重要。然而，大多数现有设计方法依赖于从自然语言处理中借鉴的单突变采样策略，这些策略未能捕捉对功能至关重要的上位效应相互作用。在此，我们开发了一个计算框架，系统比较采样方法，并引入了几种针对蛋白质设计定制的策略。我们表明，同时采样多个突变通过更好地捕捉上位效应，显著优于单突变方法。我们在跨越真核、原核和病毒来源的三个蛋白质家族中评估这些策略，检查关键超参数，并针对一个主要治疗靶标的从头设计结合蛋白任务验证我们的发现。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：如何从蛋白质语言模型中高效采样以生成功能性的新序列？现有采样策略（如beam search、top-k采样）多借鉴NLP，但忽略了蛋白质特有的上位效应（即多个位点突变间的非加性相互作用）。
  • 为什么重要：蛋白质序列空间极其庞大，高效探索是设计新型功能蛋白（治疗性抗体、酶、材料蛋白等）的关键瓶颈；上位效应是决定蛋白功能的核心因素，忽略它将导致设计序列活性低下。
  • 现有方法不足：NLP采样策略（如单token逐步生成）假设序列位置独立，无法建模协同突变；而蛋白质功能往往依赖多个位点的协同变化。
  • 精确研究问题：Can multi-mutant joint sampling from protein language models outperform single-mutant sequential sampling in generating functional protein variants by better capturing epistatic interactions?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：开发一个in silico框架，系统比较多种pLM采样策略（单突变逐步、多突变联合、引导采样等），并在三个蛋白家族及de novo binder任务上评估。
  2. 核心洞察：蛋白质功能依赖上位效应，而多突变联合采样天然能捕获这种协同性——因为模型在生成时同时考虑多个位点的联合概率，而非逐步独立决策。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：对于任何序列生成任务，若目标函数（如功能）存在非加性相互作用，则生成策略必须显式建模联合分布，而非分解为独立步骤。这一原则可迁移至RNA设计、小分子生成等领域。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a plausible and potentially valuable contribution to the field of protein design, addressing a recognized limitation of current sampling strategies. The central claim, that multi-mutant sampling better captures epistatic effects, is biologically reasonable and aligns with known constraints on protein fitness landscapes. However, the abstract provides no quantitative evidence, no methodological specificity, and no statistical support. The validation on a de novo binder design task is mentioned but not described in any detail. As presented, the case is not established from the supplied material.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Computational protein designers, machine learning researchers working on generative models for biological sequences, and experimentalists seeking improved design pipelines would be interested. The work addresses a practical bottleneck in applying protein language models to functional design, which has direct relevance for therapeutic and industrial applications.
  • Major strengths The problem is well framed and important. The comparison of sampling strategies across multiple protein families is a sensible design. The inclusion of a de novo binder design task as a downstream validation is a strong choice for demonstrating practical utility.
  • Major Concerns The abstract lacks all quantitative results, making the central claim unverifiable.
