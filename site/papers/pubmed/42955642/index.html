An Effective Pipeline to Develop Modification-Specific Intracellular Antibodies Using AI Tools.

Maejima, Daiki; Galindo, Gabriel; Stasevich, Timothy J; Kimura, Hiroshi
Methods in molecular biology (Clifton, N.J.) · 2026-10-29 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文提出利用AI辅助设计流程（含抗体序列注释、结构预测、深度学习框架优化）将抗体序列转化为功能性胞内抗体，涉及蛋白质结构预测与设计，与蛋白质结构计算课题相关但以实验验证为主。

摘要

Posttranslational protein modifications (PTMs) are often associated with protein functions. To detect PTMs in living cells, genetically encoded antibody-based probes, termed modification-specific intracellular antibodies (mintbodies), have been developed. A mintbody consists of a single-chain variable fragment (scFv) of a modification-specific antibody and a fluorescent protein. By expressing mintbodies in cells or organisms, the localization and changes in the specifically modified form of a protein can be tracked in real time. In particular, mintbodies against histone and RNA polymerase II PTMs have been developed and are available. However, most mouse monoclonal antibodies fail to remain functional when expressed in the reduced environment inside eukaryotic cells due to unstable folding. This results in an inability to bind antigen, instability, aggregation, and/or degradation. Whereas there have been no general methods to develop functional intracellular antibodies, we have recently developed a convenient pipeline to efficiently convert unstable scFvs into stable and functional probes using AI-assisted tools. We here describe a practical, end-to-end protocol for converting existing antibody sequences into functional intracellular antibodies using an AI-assisted design pipeline combined with live-cell screening. The method integrates antibody sequence annotation, structure prediction, deep-learning-based framework optimization, and experimental validation in mammalian cells.

摘要翻译

翻译后蛋白修饰（PTMs）通常与蛋白质功能相关。为了在活细胞中检测PTMs，已开发出基于基因编码抗体探针，称为修饰特异性胞内抗体（mintbodies）。mintbody由修饰特异性抗体的单链可变片段（scFv）和荧光蛋白组成。通过在细胞或生物体内表达mintbodies，可以实时追踪特定修饰形式蛋白质的定位和变化。特别是，针对组蛋白和RNA聚合酶II PTMs的mintbodies已被开发并可供使用。然而，大多数小鼠单克隆抗体在真核细胞内部的还原环境中表达时，由于折叠不稳定而无法保持功能。这导致无法结合抗原、不稳定、聚集和/或降解。尽管没有通用方法来开发功能性胞内抗体，我们最近开发了一种便捷流程，利用AI辅助工具高效地将不稳定的scFvs转化为稳定且功能性的探针。本文描述了一个实用的端到端协议，通过AI辅助设计流程结合活细胞筛选，将现有抗体序列转化为功能性胞内抗体。该方法整合了抗体序列注释、结构预测、基于深度学习的框架优化以及哺乳动物细胞中的实验验证。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：如何将已有的、在体外功能正常的修饰特异性单克隆抗体（如抗组蛋白 PTM 抗体）的 scFv 片段，改造成在真核细胞胞内还原环境中仍能正确折叠、稳定表达、不聚集、不降解，并保持抗原结合能力的功能性细胞内抗体（mintbody）？
  • 为什么重要：PTM 检测在活细胞中实时进行是理解蛋白质功能动态的关键。mintbody 是唯一能实现这一目标的基因编码探针，但大多数小鼠单克隆抗体的 scFv 在胞内不稳定，导致无法使用。缺乏通用改造方法限制了 mintbody 的广泛应用。
  • 现有方法不足：传统上无系统性方法；随机突变或理性设计缺乏结构指导，成功率低；已有的少数成功案例（如抗组蛋白 H3K9ac、H4K20me 等）多为个案，不可推广。
  • 精确研究问题：Can an AI-assisted pipeline (structure prediction + deep-learning framework optimization) reliably convert unstable scFvs into functional mintbodies for arbitrary PTM-specific antibodies?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：一个五步流水线——(1) 抗体序列注释（CDR/框架区识别）；(2) 结构预测（AI 工具如 AlphaFold）；(3) 深度学习框架区优化（重设计框架区序列以增强稳定性）；(4) 候选序列合成与克隆；(5) 活细胞转染与功能筛选（荧光定位、抗原结合验证）。
  2. 核心洞察：scFv 的不稳定性主要源于框架区（framework regions）在无二硫键条件下的折叠能不足，而非 CDR 区。因此，通过 AI 结构预测识别框架区关键残基，再用深度学习序列设计工具优化框架区（保持 CDR 构象不变），可以在不改变抗原特异性的前提下大幅提升胞内稳定性。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：对于任何「体外功能正常但体内/胞内失效」的蛋白质工具，核心策略是区分功能域与稳定性域，并仅对稳定性域进行序列优化，同时用结构预测保证功能域构象不变。这一「功能-稳定性解耦优化」思路可迁移至其他蛋白质工程任务（如酶的热稳定性改造、膜蛋白可溶性优化等）。

07 方法总览

  • 输入：已知的修饰特异性单克隆抗体的 scFv 氨基酸序列（如抗 H3K9ac、H3K27me3 等）。
  • 输出：功能验证过的、可在活细胞中表达并特异性结合 PTM 靶标的 mintbody 质粒构建体。
  • 模块： 1. 序列注释（CDR/框架区划分） 2. AI 结构预测（scFv 三维结构） 3. 深度学习框架区优化（序列重设计） 4. 候选克隆构建 5. 活细胞筛选（转染、免疫荧光、共定位验证）
  • 训练：本文为应用流程，不涉及新模型训练；使用预训练 AI 工具（具体工具名称未在摘要中列出，正文可能详述）。
  • 工具：AI 结构预测工具（推测为 AlphaFold2 或类似）、深度学习序列设计工具（推测为 ProteinMPNN 或类似）、标准分子克隆与细胞培养设备。
  • 反馈回路：活细胞筛选结果反馈至序列设计步骤，若候选失败则重新设计或调整优化参数。
  • 假设：(1) 框架区稳定性是 scFv 胞内功能的主要限制因素；(2) AI 预测结构足够准确以指导框架区设计；(3) 保持 CDR 构象即可保持抗原特异性。
  • 文字流程：scFv 序列 → 注释 CDR/框架区 → AI 预测 3D 结构 → 深度学习优化框架区序列（约束 CDR 不变）→ 合成候选基因 → 克隆至哺乳动物表达载体（含荧光蛋白融合）→ 转染活细胞 → 荧光显微镜检测定位与抗原结合 → 阳性克隆进入功能验证。

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围：仅针对 scFv 格式的抗体片段，不涉及全长抗体或其他抗体格式。
  • Oracle/真值输入：需要已知抗原特异性的原始抗体序列作为起点；流程不负责从头设计新特异性。
  • 端到端状态：流程包含从序列到功能验证的完整步骤，但「端到端」仅指流程完整性，不意味着全自动化——活细胞筛选仍需人工操作。
  • 算力成本：未提供；AI 结构预测和深度学习优化需要一定计算资源，但未量化。
  • 历史数据依赖：依赖预训练 AI 模型，模型训练数据可能影响预测质量。
  • 模型依赖：流程效果取决于所选 AI 工具的性能；不同工具可能产生不同结果。
  • 最难情形：对框架区高度不稳定或 CDR 对框架区构象极度敏感的 scFv，优化可能失败。
  • 群体/领域边界：适用于哺乳动物细胞内表达；未验证在其他物种或细胞外环境中的适用性。
  • 不确定性：未提供成功率统计；无法评估流程的失败率或适用范围边界。
  • 有边界复述：本文展示了一个将已知 scFv 序列通过 AI 辅助设计转化为胞内功能性 mintbody 的可行流程，但未提供定量成功率或与现有方法的系统对比，其通用性需更多案例验证。

14 学到什么

Agent 提炼的知识候选：

  1. 功能-稳定性解耦优化策略：将蛋白质工具的功能域（CDR）与稳定性域（框架区）分离，仅优化后者，同时用结构预测保证前者构象不变。可迁移至：酶稳定性改造、膜蛋白可溶性优化、人工结合蛋白设计。
  2. AI 预测-设计-实验验证闭环：结构预测（AlphaFold 类）→ 序列设计（深度学习）→ 活细胞功能筛选的流水线模式，可迁移至：蛋白质构象采样后的候选筛选、MD 模拟后的稳定性验证。
  3. 还原环境作为筛选压力：利用胞内还原环境作为天然筛选条件，直接选择在目标环境中稳定的变体。可迁移至：设计在特定细胞区室（如核内、线粒体）中工作的蛋白质工具。
  4. 框架区序列重设计保持 CDR 的约束策略：在序列优化中显式约束功能关键区域不变，是提高设计成功率的关键。可迁移至：基于 AlphaFold 的序列设计任务中，如何设置优化边界。
  5. 活细胞筛选作为最终判据：计算预测不能替代功能验证，尤其对于构象敏感的蛋白质。可迁移至：MD 模拟预测的构象稳定性需用实验（如细胞热位移分析）验证。

Agent 生成的研究候选：

  1. 候选名称：AI 辅助的构象特异性细胞内探针设计 - 来源局限/观察：本文针对 PTM 特异性抗体，但构象特异性（如区分蛋白不同构象态）的探针同样缺乏。 - 核心假设：将本文的「框架区优化」策略应用于构象特异性纳米抗体（nanobody），可生成在活细胞中识别特定蛋白构象态的探针。 - 增量：将 PTM 特异性扩展到构象特异性，拓宽 mintbody 应用范围。 - 初步方法：使用 AlphaFold 预测目标蛋白不同构象态，设计针对构象表位的纳米抗体，再用本文流程优化稳定性。 - 验证方式：活细胞 FRET 或荧光共定位检测构象特异性。 - 失败模式：构象表位在胞内可能被遮蔽，或纳米抗体框架区优化后构象选择性丧失。

  2. 候选名称：MD 模拟引导的 scFv 框架区稳定性预测 - 来源局限/观察：本文依赖 AI 结构预测，但未利用 MD 模拟评估动态稳定性。 - 核心假设：MD 模拟（如折叠自由能计算）可补充 AI 预测，识别 AI 工具无法捕捉的动力学不稳定位点。 - 增量：将静态结构预测升级为动态稳定性评估，提高设计成功率。 - 初步方法：对 AI 优化前后的 scFv 进行 MD 模拟，比较折叠自由能、RMSF 等指标，建立「MD 稳定性阈值」作为筛选标准。 - 验证方式：对多个 scFv 进行 MD 预测与实验对比，评估预测准确率。 - 失败模式：MD 模拟成本高，且力场对还原环境（无二硫键）的模拟可能不准确。

  3. 候选名称：深度学习框架区优化与 CDR 构象保持的联合约束 - 来源局限/观察：本文未明确说明如何保证 CDR 构象在框架区优化后不变。 - 核心假设：在深度学习序列设计中显式加入 CDR 构象约束（如结构叠加 RMSD 损失），可提高抗原亲和力保持率。 - 增量：提出可量化的「CDR 保持指标」，改进现有设计工具。 - 初步方法：在 ProteinMPNN 类工具中增加 CDR 结构约束项，对比有无约束的设计结果。 - 验证方式：表达设计变体，测量抗原亲和力（SPR 或 ELISA）。 - 失败模式：过度约束可能限制序列多样性，降低稳定性提升空间。

  4. 候选名称：跨物种 scFv 稳定性预测的迁移学习 - 来源局限/观察：本文基于小鼠单克隆抗体，未验证其他物种来源的 scFv。 - 核心假设：AI 工具在不同物种来源的 scFv 上表现不同，需物种特异性微调。 - 增量：建立多物种 scFv 稳定性数据集，训练迁移学习模型。 - 初步方法：收集小鼠、人、兔、羊驼等来源的 scFv 稳定性数据，微调预训练模型。 - 验证方式：交叉验证不同物种的预测准确率。 - 失败模式：数据量不足，物种间差异过大导致迁移失败。

审稿人评审

Review setup

  • Input scope Abstract only
  • Assessment boundary Feasibility and clarity of the described AI-assisted pipeline for converting antibody sequences into functional mintbodies, as presented in the abstract
  • Shared manuscript claim summary The authors claim to have developed a convenient, end-to-end pipeline that uses AI-assisted tools to convert unstable scFvs from existing monoclonal antibodies into stable, functional modification-specific intracellular antibodies (mintbodies), validated in mammalian cells
  • Visible evidence base Abstract text only; no figures, tables, methods details, or results are provided
  • Missing materials affecting confidence Full protocol steps, AI tool names and versions, training or validation datasets, quantitative stability or binding data, live-cell screening results, comparison to existing methods, and any negative controls

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a potentially valuable methodological contribution to the intracellular antibody field, addressing a real bottleneck in converting monoclonal antibodies into functional probes. However, the current evidence base is limited to a high-level description, and the core claims of efficiency, generalizability, and functional success cannot be evaluated without the full protocol and validation data. The work is likely to interest researchers in cell biology, epigenetics, and protein engineering, but the case for a broadly applicable pipeline is not yet established from the supplied material.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers studying posttranslational modifications in living cells, particularly those working on histones and RNA polymerase II, would benefit from improved tools for real-time PTM tracking. Additionally, protein engineers and synthetic biologists interested in AI-guided antibody engineering would find the pipeline relevant. The method could also appeal to laboratories lacking specialized expertise in intracellular antibody development, as it promises a more accessible workflow.
  • Major strengths The abstract addresses a well-recognized problem, namely the instability of scFvs in the reducing intracellular environment, with a practical solution that integrates AI tools. The proposed pipeline is described as end-to-end, covering sequence annotation, structure prediction, framework optimization, and experimental validation, which suggests a comprehensive approach. The focus on live-cell screening as a validation step is appropriate for the intended application.
  • Major Concerns
  • Concern ID R1-M1
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Technical soundness
  • Claim pointer The authors claim that the pipeline efficiently converts unstable scFvs into stable and functional probes using AI-assisted tools
  • Evidence pointer Abstract, location not provided
  • Concern The abstract provides no quantitative or qualitative data demonstrating that the pipeline successfully converts scFvs into functional mintbodies. No success rate, number of antibodies tested, or representative results from live-cell screening are mentioned.
  • Why it matters Without evidence of successful conversion, the central claim of an effective pipeline is unsupported. The abstract implies efficacy but offers no basis for assessing whether the method works beyond a single anecdotal case or at all.
  • Resolution test Provide data from the full manuscript showing the number of scFvs tested, the proportion that became stable and functional, and representative live-cell imaging results demonstrating specific PTM detection.
  • Concern ID R1-M2
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Reproducibility
  • Claim pointer The abstract describes a practical, end-to-end protocol that integrates specific AI tools
  • Evidence pointer Abstract, location not provided
  • Concern The specific AI tools, their versions, and the parameters used for structure prediction and framework optimization are not named or described. The protocol cannot be reproduced or evaluated for general applicability without this information.
  • Why it matters Reproducibility is a core requirement for a methods paper. If the AI tools are not specified, readers cannot implement the pipeline, and reviewers cannot assess whether the approach is novel or derivative of existing tools.
  • Resolution test List all AI tools, including versions and key settings, in the full protocol, and provide a step-by-step description that a competent researcher could follow.
  • Concern ID R1-M3
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking No
  • Axis Generalizability
  • Claim pointer The authors state that most mouse monoclonal antibodies fail to remain functional in cells and that their pipeline addresses this generally
  • Evidence pointer Abstract, location not provided
  • Concern The abstract does not indicate how many different antibodies or PTM targets were tested. It is unclear whether the pipeline works broadly across diverse scFv sequences or only for a narrow set of favorable cases.
  • Why it matters The claim of a general method is central to the paper's significance. If only one or two antibodies were successfully converted, the pipeline may not be broadly applicable, limiting its utility.
  • Resolution test Report the number and diversity of antibodies tested, including their target PTMs and source species, and discuss any failures or limitations observed.

Risk / unsupported claims

  • The claim that the pipeline efficiently converts unstable scFvs into stable and functional probes is unsupported, as no success data are provided.
  • The claim that the method is a general solution is unsupported, as the number and diversity of tested antibodies are not stated.
  • The claim that AI-assisted tools are integrated in a novel way is not assessable, as specific tools and their roles are not described.
  • The implied superiority or convenience of the pipeline relative to existing methods is not supported by any comparative data.
