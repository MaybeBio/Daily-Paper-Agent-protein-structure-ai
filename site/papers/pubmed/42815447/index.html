7

What deep learning can and cannot (yet) do for enzyme design.

Ahr, Judith A; de Waal, Moor M; Cotet, Tudor-Stefan; Howell, Daniel C; Bunzel, H Adrian
Current opinion in chemical biology · 2026-09-30 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文综述了深度学习在基于结构的酶设计中的进展与局限，聚焦扩散模型生成支架和序列设计，虽为综述且方法创新有限，但紧密围绕蛋白质结构计算设计问题，对课题有参考价值。

摘要

Deep learning has substantially advanced structure-based enzyme design, yet reliably creating highly efficient enzymes remains out of reach. One emerging enzyme design strategy combines diffusion-based scaffold generation around active-site models with sequence design. Such structure-based approaches have expanded the accessible reaction space and enabled the emergence of enzymes with diverse catalytic functions and complex catalytic motifs. Despite notable catalytic efficiencies (k(cat)/K(M)), turnover rates (k(cat)) often remain limited, suggesting that current approaches typically yield tight substrate binders rather than enzymes that selectively stabilize the chemical transition state. Computational design of electrostatic catalysis, multistate behavior, and conformational dynamics could potentially enhance transition-state stabilization and improve catalytic activity. More reliable activity oracles, integration of evolutionary information, protein language models, and lab-in-the-loop optimization could further enhance design performance. Ultimately, advancing structure-based enzyme design will require directly targeting the physicochemical drivers of catalysis to move beyond substrate binding toward the reliable design of efficient catalysts.

摘要翻译

深度学习已大幅推进了基于结构的酶设计，但可靠地创造高效酶仍难以实现。一种新兴的酶设计策略将基于扩散的支架生成与活性位点模型及序列设计相结合。此类基于结构的方法扩展了可及的反应空间，并促成了具有多样催化功能和复杂催化基序的酶的出现。尽管催化效率（k(cat)/K(M)）显著，但周转率（k(cat)）往往仍受限，这表明当前方法通常产生紧密底物结合体，而非选择性稳定化学过渡态的酶。静电催化、多态行为和构象动力学的计算设计可能增强过渡态稳定化并提升催化活性。更可靠的活性预测器、进化信息的整合、蛋白质语言模型及实验室闭环优化可进一步提升设计性能。最终，推进基于结构的酶设计需直接针对催化的物理化学驱动因素，以超越底物结合，迈向高效催化剂的可靠设计。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：深度学习在基于结构的酶设计中究竟能做什么、不能做什么？为什么当前方法难以可靠地设计出高效酶？
  • 为什么重要：酶设计是蛋白质工程的终极目标之一，高效催化对工业、医药和合成生物学有重大价值；深度学习虽已大幅推进结构设计，但催化效率仍远低于天然酶。
  • 现有方法为何不足：现有结构导向方法（扩散模型 + 序列设计）主要优化底物结合（K_M），而非过渡态稳定（k_cat），导致 turnover rate 受限；对静电催化、多态行为、构象动力学的计算设计尚未成熟。
  • 精确研究问题：Can current deep learning-based structure-guided enzyme design methods reliably produce enzymes that selectively stabilize the chemical transition state, rather than merely tight substrate binders?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：基于结构的酶设计流程 = 扩散模型生成 scaffold（围绕活性位点模型）+ 序列设计；辅以进化信息、蛋白质语言模型、lab-in-the-loop 优化。
  2. 核心洞察：当前深度学习方法在结构生成和序列设计上已成功，但其优化目标（结构稳定性、底物结合）与催化本质（过渡态稳定）之间存在系统性错位；高效催化的关键在于选择性稳定化学过渡态，而这需要直接靶向静电、多态和动力学因素。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：在 AI 驱动的生物分子设计中，「可生成 ≠ 有功能」——生成模型擅长满足几何/序列约束，但功能（尤其催化）依赖超越静态结构的物理化学性质；因此，设计流程必须显式建模功能相关的物理量（如过渡态、静电势、构象系综），而非仅依赖结构代理指标。

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围：仅覆盖基于结构的酶设计（structure-based enzyme design），不涉及非结构导向方法（如定向进化）的全面比较。
  • oracle/真值输入：综述依赖文献中已报道的酶设计案例，其活性数据来自各原始研究的实验测量，但本文未提供统一评测协议。
  • 端到端状态：当前方法尚未实现端到端的高效酶设计——结构生成和序列设计可计算完成，但高效催化仍不可靠。
  • 算力成本：未提供。
  • 历史数据依赖：扩散模型和序列设计模型依赖已有蛋白质结构/序列数据，但本文未讨论数据偏差。
  • 模型依赖：结论基于对现有深度学习方法的汇总观察，不针对特定模型。
  • 最难情形：高效催化（高 k(cat)）是当前最难实现的目标，尤其涉及静电催化和构象动力学的体系。
  • 群体/领域边界：结论适用于基于结构的酶设计领域，不直接推广至其他蛋白质设计任务（如结合蛋白设计）。
  • 不确定性：作者对「静电/多态/动力学设计可提升活性」的论断为前瞻性主张，缺乏直接实验证据。
  • 有边界的复述：在基于结构的深度学习酶设计框架下，现有方法能有效生成结构多样的候选酶并实现可观的 k(cat)/K_M，但 k(cat) 普遍受限，表明这些方法主要优化底物结合而非过渡态稳定；因此，要可靠设计高效酶，需将催化的物理化学驱动因素（静电、多态、动力学）显式纳入计算设计流程。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a coherent and timely perspective on the current state and limitations of deep learning in enzyme design. The central thesis, that current structure-based approaches often produce tight substrate binders rather than efficient catalysts due to insufficient transition-state stabilization, is plausible and aligns with trends in the field. However, the abstract provides no empirical evidence, case examples, or quantitative support for this claim, and the proposed solutions are listed without critical evaluation of their feasibility or expected impact. As a perspective piece, the abstract is readable and logically structured, but its scientific depth is limited by the absence of supporting data and the lack of engagement with counterarguments or alternative frameworks.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Computational biologists, protein engineers, and enzymologists working on enzyme design and directed evolution would find this perspective relevant. Researchers developing generative models for protein design, particularly those using diffusion models or language models, would also be interested in the critique of current limitations and the proposed future directions. The abstract could inform research prioritization in both academic and industrial settings focused on biocatalysis.
  • Major strengths The abstract clearly identifies a specific and important gap in current enzyme design, namely the distinction between substrate binding and transition-state stabilization. It offers a focused set of proposed improvements that are grounded in established physicochemical principles. The writing is concise and accessible to a broad scientific audience.
  • Major Concerns The central claim that current methods yield tight substrate binders rather than transition-state stabilizers is presented without supporting evidence, such as comparative kinetic data or structural analyses. The proposed solutions, including electrostatic catalysis design and multistate modeling, are not critically assessed for their current maturity or practical challenges. The abstract does not acknowledge potential counterexamples where deep learning has achieved high turnover rates, nor does it discuss the role of directed evolution in complementing computational design.

Without such evidence, the central thesis remains an assertion rather than an established observation. Additionally, the proposed improvements are not linked to any preliminary results or feasibility assessments, leaving their potential impact unsubstantiated.
