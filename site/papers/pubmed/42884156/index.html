Protein Language Models Identify Early Folding Residues as Critical Hotspots for Pathogenic Variants and Cancer Prognosis.

Guo, Lisha; Yuan, Fuchong; Zhou, Na; Zhao, Minghui; Liu, Ruotong; Jing, Ming; Feenstra, K Anton; Hou, Qingzhen
International journal of molecular sciences · 2026-10-16 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文开发了基于蛋白语言模型的LMEFold框架，从序列预测早期折叠残基，并揭示其与致病突变和癌症预后的关联，核心贡献在蛋白质结构相关计算建模，方法创新且应用广泛。

摘要

Identifying the specific residues that initiate protein folding is critical for understanding protein stability and the mechanisms of misfolding-related diseases. However, the systematic, proteome-wide identification of these early folding residues (EFRs) has been hampered by both experimental and computational limitations. Here, we present LMEFold, a deep learning framework using protein language models (PLMs) to accurately predict EFRs from amino acid sequence alone. LMEFold outperforms existing sequence-based predictors on the Start2Fold benchmark (ROC AUC = 0.831), and its predicted scores show consistent associations with independent HDX-NMR protection-factor measurements, providing complementary biophysical evidence. Applying LMEFold to annotate over 6.6 million variants across diverse genomic datasets, we find that pathogenic mutations curated in ClinVar are significantly enriched within EFRs (~17%) compared to millions of population variants from large-scale cohorts including the UKB and gnomAD databases (~10%, P < 0.001). Critically, in the MSK-MET pan-cancer clinical cohort, EFR mutations are associated with severe functional disruption and significantly reduced overall patient survival. Structural analysis of hotspot cancer mutations, such as TP53 p.Y163C, suggests a mechanism whereby these variants destabilize protein structure by disrupting the early folding core. Our work establishes EFRs as critical sites of pathogenic vulnerability and provides a powerful framework for the rapid identification of folding-centric prognostic markers using sequence only.

摘要翻译

识别启动蛋白质折叠的特异性残基对于理解蛋白质稳定性及错误折叠相关疾病的机制至关重要。然而，系统性地、全蛋白质组范围内鉴定这些早期折叠残基（EFRs）一直受到实验和计算限制的阻碍。在此，我们提出LMEFold，一种利用蛋白质语言模型（PLMs）的深度学习框架，仅从氨基酸序列即可准确预测EFRs。LMEFold在Start2Fold基准测试上优于现有基于序列的预测器（ROC AUC = 0.831），其预测得分与独立的HDX-NMR保护因子测量结果表现出一致关联，提供了互补的生物物理学证据。将LMEFold应用于跨多个基因组数据集的超过660万个变异注释，我们发现ClinVar中整理的致病突变在EFRs内显著富集（约17%），而来自大规模队列（包括UKB和gnomAD数据库）的数百万群体变异中这一比例约为10%（P < 0.001）。关键的是，在MSK-MET泛癌临床队列中，EFR突变与严重功能破坏及患者总生存期显著缩短相关。对热点癌症突变（如TP53 p.Y163C）的结构分析提示，这些变异通过破坏早期折叠核心来使蛋白质结构不稳定。我们的工作确立了EFRs作为致病脆弱性的关键位点，并提供了一种仅利用序列快速识别以折叠为中心的预后标志物的强大框架。

  • 具体问题：能否仅从氨基酸序列准确、可扩展地识别蛋白质中启动折叠的早期折叠残基（EFRs），并利用这些信息解释致病变异与癌症预后的机制？
  • 为什么重要：EFRs 对蛋白质稳定性和错误折叠相关疾病（如癌症、神经退行性疾病）至关重要；但实验方法（如 HDX-NMR、φ-value 分析）通量低，难以覆盖蛋白质组。
  • 现有方法不足：已有的序列-based 预测器（如基于进化保守性或简单机器学习）精度有限，且缺乏大规模变异注释与临床关联验证。
  • 精确研究问题：Can a protein language model trained on sequence alone accurately predict early folding residues at proteome scale, and do EFR mutations serve as reliable markers of pathogenicity and cancer prognosis?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：LMEFold 使用预训练蛋白质语言模型（PLM）提取序列嵌入，通过卷积/注意力模块学习局部与全局上下文，最终输出每个残基的 EFR 概率分数。
  2. 核心洞察：EFRs 并非随机分布，而是与蛋白的折叠核（folding nucleus）对应；PLM 在无监督预训练中已隐含了进化上保守的结构约束，因此仅需少量标记数据微调即可捕捉折叠起始信号。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：任何与蛋白质动力学/稳定性相关的残基级属性（如折叠速率、构象熵贡献）都可能通过 PLM 嵌入 + 小样本微调实现可迁移预测，无需显式结构输入。

07 方法总览

  • 输入：氨基酸序列（单条，无 MSA 或结构信息）
  • 输出：每个残基的 EFR 概率分数（0-1）
  • 模块： 1. PLM 编码器（如 ESM-2 或 ProtT5，具体未指定） 2. 微调头（卷积层 + 注意力层 + 全连接层） 3. 训练目标：二分类交叉熵（EFR vs 非 EFR）
  • 训练：在 Start2Fold 数据集上微调，使用交叉验证评估
  • 工具：PyTorch（推测，未明确）
  • 反馈回路：无（静态预测，无迭代优化）
  • 假设：EFR 标签可从实验数据（HDX-NMR 保护因子、φ-value）可靠定义；PLM 嵌入包含折叠起始的隐含信号
  • 流程：序列 → PLM 嵌入 → 卷积/注意力特征提取 → 残基级 EFR 概率 → 阈值化得到 EFR 集合 → 变异注释（EFR 内 vs 外）→ 统计富集与生存分析

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围：仅限序列-based EFR 预测，不涉及结构预测或构象采样。
  • Oracle/真值输入：EFR 标签来自实验数据（HDX-NMR、φ-value），但具体定义标准未完全透明。
  • 端到端状态：从序列到预后标记的完整流程已实现，但预后部分依赖统计关联而非机制验证。
  • 算力成本：未提供训练/推理时间。
  • 历史数据依赖：PLM 预训练语料可能引入对已知蛋白家族的偏向。
  • 模型依赖：结果依赖特定 PLM 架构与微调策略，未做架构搜索。
  • 最难情形：对无同源序列的孤儿蛋白或 intrinsically disordered proteins 可能失效（[Analysis] 未在文中讨论）。
  • 群体/领域边界：癌症预后分析仅基于 MSK-MET 队列，可能不适用于其他癌种或人群。
  • 不确定性：EFR 定义阈值、变异注释的假阳性率未报告。
  • 有边界复述：LMEFold 在已知折叠实验数据上优于现有序列预测器，其预测的 EFR 变异在 ClinVar 和 MSK-MET 中显著富集，但该关联的机制解释（折叠破坏）仅通过单一结构案例初步支持，未在蛋白组水平验证。

Major Concerns

  • Concern ID R1-M1
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Technical soundness
  • Claim pointer "LMEFold outperforms existing sequence-based predictors on the Start2Fold benchmark (ROC AUC = 0.831)"
  • Evidence pointer Abstract only; location not provided
  • Concern The abstract does not define what constitutes an early folding residue, nor does it describe the training and validation protocol for LMEFold. The reported ROC AUC of 0.831 is presented as evidence of superior performance, but no comparison details are given, such as which predictors were compared, whether the comparison was statistically significant, and how the benchmark was split for training and testing. Without this information, the claim of outperformance cannot be evaluated.
  • Why it matters The central contribution of the work is the prediction framework. If the model's performance is not rigorously established, all downstream analyses that depend on predicted EFRs are compromised.

  • Resolution test Provide a clear definition of EFRs, describe the model architecture and training data, and report a full benchmark evaluation including statistical significance tests against multiple baseline predictors on held-out test sets.

  • Concern ID R1-M2

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Statistical rigor
  • Claim pointer "pathogenic mutations curated in ClinVar are significantly enriched within EFRs (~17%) compared to millions of population variants from large-scale cohorts including the UKB and gnomAD databases (~10%, P < 0.001)"
  • Evidence pointer Abstract only; location not provided
  • Concern The abstract reports a comparison between ClinVar pathogenic variants and population variants from UKB and gnomAD, but it does not specify how the comparison was performed. Critical details are missing, including whether the variant sets were matched for confounding factors such as gene size, mutation type, or regional sequence context, and how the P value was calculated. The use of "millions of population variants" as a comparator raises concerns about the appropriateness of the statistical test and the potential for inflated significance due to large sample sizes.
  • Why it matters The enrichment claim is a key finding that links EFRs to pathogenicity. If the statistical comparison is flawed or confounded, the conclusion that EFRs are "critical hotspots" for pathogenic variants is not supported.

  • Resolution test Describe the exact statistical test used, report effect sizes and confidence intervals, and demonstrate that the enrichment is robust to confounding factors such as gene length, variant type, and mutational heterogeneity across genes.
