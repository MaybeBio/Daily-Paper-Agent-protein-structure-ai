Sequence Scanning Improves Accuracy for Antibody-Antigen and Molecular Glue Ternary Complex Cofolding.

Linker, Stephanie M; Ploetz, Tobias; Evers, Andreas; Musil, Djordje; Kaiser, Jesko; Schindler, Christina
ChemMedChem · 2026-10-30 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文提出序列扫描工作流，通过系统约束增强采样多样性，提升AlphaFold3类共折叠模型对抗体-抗原和分子胶三元复合物结构预测的准确性，属于蛋白质结构预测方法改进，与课题高度相关。

摘要

Accurately modeling biomolecular interactions is crucial for understanding biological function and can accelerate drug discovery efforts. Deep-learning-based cofolding methods such as AlphaFold3 and Boltz-2 can successfully predict many different biomolecular complexes. However, these models show lower success rates in predicting antibody-antigen and molecular glue (MG) ternary complexes, two cases of high relevance to pharmaceutical research. Here, we present sequence scanning, a new workflow, evaluated on Boltz and Protenix, that improves prediction accuracy in these domains by applying systematic constraints to enhance overall sampling diversity. For both use cases, we assess the performance of the method on large, diverse benchmark sets. We find improved accuracy of the sequence scanning workflow in predicting antibody-antigen interface contacts and in pose prediction quality for MGs compared to previously published results. We also discuss remaining challenges and potential improvements to the method.

摘要翻译

精确建模生物分子相互作用对于理解生物学功能至关重要，并能加速药物发现工作。基于深度学习的共折叠方法，如AlphaFold3和Boltz-2，能够成功预测许多不同的生物分子复合物。然而，这些模型在预测抗体-抗原和分子胶（MG）三元复合物方面成功率较低，这两种情况与药物研究高度相关。在此，我们提出序列扫描（sequence scanning），一种新的工作流程，在Boltz和Protenix上进行了评估，通过应用系统性约束来增强整体采样多样性，从而提高这些领域的预测准确性。对于这两种用例，我们在大型、多样化的基准集上评估了该方法的性能。我们发现，与先前发表的结果相比，序列扫描工作流程在预测抗体-抗原界面接触和MG的姿势预测质量方面提高了准确性。我们还讨论了剩余的挑战及对该方法的潜在改进。
