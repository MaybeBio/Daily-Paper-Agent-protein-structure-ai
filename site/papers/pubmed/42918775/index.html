Calcium induced N-terminal gating and pore collapse in connexin-46/50 gap junctions.

Flores, Jonathan A; O'Neill, Susan E; Jarodsky, Joshua M; Haddad, Bassam G; Reichow, Steve L
Nature communications · 2026-10-23 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文通过冷冻电镜和分子动力学模拟揭示钙离子诱导的缝隙连接蛋白Cx46/50门控机制，核心对象为蛋白质结构，问题涉及构象变化与孔道关闭，方法包含MD模拟，但结构预测/生成等计算建模创新较弱，故评7分。

摘要

Gap junctions facilitate electrical and metabolic coupling essential for tissue function. Under ischemic conditions (e.g., heart attack or stroke), elevated intracellular calcium (Ca(2)(+)) uncouples these communication pathways to limit the spread of cytotoxic signals, yet the structural basis of this protective response remains unclear. Here, using single-particle cryo-EM, we define the Ca(2)(+)-dependent gating mechanism of native connexin-46/50 (Cx46/50) gap junction channels. Ca(2+) binding promotes an ensemble of N-terminal (NT) domain conformations spanning destabilized and fully gated states. These conformational changes are coupled to coordinated subunit rearrangements that asymmetrically collapse the pore and enable steric occlusion by the NT domains. Comparison with recently resolved low-pH gating structures reveals that distinct physiological stimuli converge on a common NT-centered gating mechanism involving coupled NT displacement and TM2 hinge motion. Long-timescale molecular dynamics simulations identify multiple Ca(2+) interaction sites along the permeation pathway and suggest that transient inter-subunit Ca(2+) coordination can promote NT destabilization and displacement. Together, these findings support a model in which distributed Ca(2+) interactions reshape the energetic landscape of the channel to favor NT-mediated pore closure, unifying previous models of gap junction regulation and providing a mechanistic basis for Ca(2+)-dependent uncoupling during cellular stress.

摘要翻译

间隙连接促进对组织功能至关重要的电学和代谢耦联。在缺血条件下（如心脏病发作或中风），升高的细胞内钙离子（Ca(2)(+)）解耦联这些通讯通路以限制细胞毒性信号的扩散，然而这种保护性反应的结构基础仍不清楚。在此，我们利用单颗粒冷冻电镜（cryo-EM）解析了天然连接蛋白-46/50（Cx46/50）间隙连接通道的Ca(2+)依赖性门控机制。Ca(2+)结合促进N端（NT）结构域构象的集合，涵盖从去稳定化到完全门控的状态。这些构象变化与协调的亚基重排相耦联，这些重排不对称地塌缩孔道并使NT结构域能够实现空间阻塞。与最近解析的低pH门控结构比较揭示，不同的生理刺激汇聚于一个共同的以NT为中心的门控机制，涉及耦联的NT位移和TM2铰链运动。长时间尺度分子动力学（molecular dynamics）模拟识别出沿通透路径的多个Ca(2+)相互作用位点，并提示亚基间的瞬时Ca(2+)配位可促进NT去稳定化和位移。综合来看，这些发现支持一个模型，即分布式的Ca(2+)相互作用重塑通道的能量景观以利于NT介导的孔道闭合，统一了先前间隙连接调控模型，并为细胞应激期间Ca(2+)依赖性解耦联提供了机制基础。

02 一句话总结

本文通过单颗粒 cryo-EM 解析了 Cx46/50 缝隙连接通道在 Ca²⁺ 结合下的多种构象状态，结合长时程 MD 模拟，揭示了 Ca²⁺ 通过多个亚基间结合位点 destabilize N-terminal 结构域并诱导其位移，进而不对称塌陷孔道、实现空间位阻封闭的门控机制，并发现该机制与低 pH 门控共享 NT 位移 + TM2 铰链运动的共同路径。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：Ca²⁺ 升高如何从结构层面导致缝隙连接通道关闭（门控）？即 Ca²⁺ 结合后，connexin 通道的哪些结构域发生何种构象变化，最终实现孔道封闭？
  • 为什么重要：缝隙连接在缺血条件下（心梗、中风）的 Ca²⁺ 依赖性解偶联是保护性细胞反应，防止毒性信号扩散。理解其结构基础对开发心脏/神经保护策略、以及 connexin 相关疾病（如白内障、耳聋、皮肤病）的机制研究具有重要意义。
  • 现有方法为何不足：此前对 connexin 门控的结构理解主要来自低 pH 门控结构（如 Cx46/50 的酸敏感构象），而 Ca²⁺ 门控的结构中间态、NT 结构域的动态行为、以及 Ca²⁺ 结合位点的精确定位均不清楚。常规晶体学难以捕获动态构象 ensemble，而仅靠 MD 缺乏实验约束。
  • 精确研究问题：Can Ca²⁺ binding to Cx46/50 gap junction channels induce a structurally definable ensemble of NT domain conformations that, coupled with subunit rearrangements, leads to asymmetric pore collapse and steric occlusion?

06 核心思想

1) 表面方法： - 用单颗粒 cryo-EM 解析 Cx46/50 通道在 Ca²⁺ 存在下的结构，通过 3D 分类获得多个构象状态。 - 用长时程 MD 模拟（µs 级）探索 Ca²⁺ 结合位点与 NT 动态。 - 将 Ca²⁺ 门控结构与已发表的低 pH 门控结构进行结构比对。

2) 核心洞察： - Ca²⁺ 并非通过单一「锁死」构象实现门控，而是通过多个亚基间结合位点重塑 NT 的构象能量景观（energy landscape），使 NT 从稳定状态进入 destabilized 和 fully gated 状态的 ensemble。 - 门控的关键不是 NT 单独运动，而是 NT 位移与 TM2 铰链运动的耦合，导致亚基不对称重排、孔道不对称塌陷。 - 不同刺激（Ca²⁺、低 pH）汇聚于同一 NT-centered 机制，提示 connexin 门控的进化保守性。

3) 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]： - 配体门控通道的「关闭」可能不是单一构象，而是一个构象 ensemble 的偏向性转变——这提示在结构解析中应重视 3D 分类与构象异质性分析。 - 多个弱结合位点的协同效应可能比单个强结合位点更能解释配体诱导的构象重排——对 MD 模拟中配体位点搜索策略有启示。 - 不同刺激共享下游结构机制的现象，提示在研究其他通道时，应主动比较不同门控条件的结构。

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围：本文仅针对 Cx46/50 这一特定 connexin 亚型，在体外（纯化蛋白）条件下研究 Ca²⁺ 门控。不能直接推广到所有 connexin。
  • oracle/真值输入：cryo-EM 结构为实验数据，但 3D 分类得到的构象状态存在主观分类标准；MD 模拟依赖力场精度。
  • 端到端状态：从结构到功能的推断（NT 位移导致孔道封闭）是间接的——本文未做功能验证（如电生理突变实验）。
  • 算力成本：MD 模拟为长时程（具体时长未提供），计算成本较高，但未提供具体资源信息。
  • 历史数据依赖：低 pH 结构来自已发表工作，比对质量依赖两者的分辨率匹配。
  • 模型依赖：MD 结论依赖力场选择与采样策略。
  • 最难情形：NT 的高度动态性导致 cryo-EM 中该区域分辨率有限，模型构建存在不确定性。
  • 群体/领域边界：结论限于 Cx46/50 的 Ca²⁺ 门控，且未覆盖其他二价阳离子（如 Mg²⁺）或其他门控刺激（如电压）。

有边界的复述：在 Cx46/50 缝隙连接通道中，Ca²⁺ 结合可诱导 NT 结构域进入 destabilized 和 fully gated 的构象状态，伴随不对称孔道塌陷；MD 模拟支持 Ca²⁺ 通过多个亚基间位点发挥作用；该机制与低 pH 门控共享 NT 位移 + TM2 铰链运动的特征。此结论限于该亚型、该刺激条件、该实验体系。

Major Concerns

  • Concern ID R1-M1
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Technical soundness
  • Claim pointer The abstract claims that cryo-EM defines the calcium-dependent gating mechanism of native Cx46/50 channels, including an ensemble of N-terminal domain conformations spanning destabilized and fully gated states.
  • Evidence pointer Abstract text; no figures or methods provided
  • Concern The claim of defining an ensemble of conformations from single-particle cryo-EM requires substantial validation. The abstract does not report resolution values, the number of particles, classification strategies, or how the ensemble was distinguished from conformational heterogeneity that might arise from sample preparation or grid artifacts. The distinction between "destabilized" and "fully gated" states is not operationally defined.
  • Why it matters The central conclusion of the paper rests on the interpretation of conformational ensembles. Without clear evidence that the observed states are biologically meaningful and not artifacts of the cryo-EM workflow, the mechanistic model cannot be accepted. The field has seen multiple instances where cryo-EM heterogeneity was over-interpreted.

  • Resolution test The full manuscript must provide resolution estimates (gold-standard FSC), particle numbers per class, and a clear description of how the conformational states were defined and validated. Ideally, the authors would show that the ensemble is reproducible across independent datasets and that the distribution of states is calcium-dependent.

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Technical soundness
  • Claim pointer The abstract claims that comparison with low-pH gating structures reveals a common N-terminal-centered gating mechanism involving coupled NT displacement and TM2 hinge motion.
  • Evidence pointer Abstract text; no structural comparison data provided
  • Concern The claim of a common mechanism across different stimuli requires rigorous structural comparison. The abstract does not specify which low-pH structures were used, how the comparison was performed (e.g., superposition metrics, RMSD calculations), or whether the observed similarities are statistically significant beyond what would be expected for related channel structures. The term "common mechanism" implies a level of quantitative similarity that cannot be assessed from the abstract.
  • Why it matters The unifying model is a major selling point of the paper. If the comparison is superficial or based on limited structural features, the claim of convergence on a common mechanism would be overstated. This could mislead the field into assuming a universal gating model without sufficient evidence.

  • Resolution test The full manuscript must provide quantitative structural comparisons, including specific residues involved in NT displacement and TM2 hinge motion, with clear metrics for similarity. Ideally, the authors would show that the conformational changes are not merely qualitatively similar but quantitatively comparable across stimuli.

  • Severity Major

  • Claim pointer The abstract claims that long-timescale molecular dynamics simulations identify multiple calcium interaction sites along the permeation pathway and suggest that transient inter-subunit calcium coordination can promote NT destabilization and displacement.

  • Resolution test The full manuscript must provide simulation details including force field, water model, ion parameters, simulation length, number of replicates, and convergence metrics. The calcium binding sites should be validated against the cryo-EM density if possible, and the proposed mechanism should be supported by multiple independent simulations.
