Comparative Hydrophobicity Analysis of Human beta-Tubulin and Bacterial FtsZ Using the Fuzzy Oil Drop Model: Potential Implications for Chaperone-Assisted Folding.

Roterman, Irena; Stapor, Katarzyna; Dulak, Dawid; Konieczny, Leszek
Computational and structural biotechnology journal · 2026-10-31 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文用模糊油滴模型比较人β-微管蛋白与细菌FtsZ的疏水分布，探讨伴侣蛋白辅助折叠差异，虽涉及蛋白结构但方法为简单模型分析，缺乏AI或物理模拟计算贡献。

摘要

The issue of protein folding in vivo remains an unsolved problem despite important progress achieved by artificial intelligence (AlphaFold). Experimental studies have shown that there are several mechanisms involved in this process. The main distinction concerns the involvement of chaperone proteins in this process as opposed to spontaneous folding. Evolutionary analysis shows that tubulin in eukaryotic organisms requires engagement of the ATP-dependent ring-shaped chaperonin TRiC/CCT, while the homologous protein in prokaryotic organisms obtains its functional structure without the support from other proteins. This study-limited to 2 examples: one human tubulin beta chain and one structure of homologous bacterial FtsZ protein-presents hypothetical interpretation of these differences based on the external conditions for folding process. The differences of the distribution of hydrophobicity in the compared proteins are taken as the criteria for comparison. The fuzzy oil drop model (FOD-M) assumes the folding of prokaryotic proteins as a spontaneous process aided only by the presence of the water environment that directs the folding toward the generation of a centrally located hydrophobic core with a polar surface. Eukaryotic protein-tubulin-exhibits a distribution of hydrophobicity that lacks this type of order as it is interpreted based on the FOD-M assumptions. The difference seems to be the result of the involvement of different external force fields in the folding process. These 2 proteins are an example that suggest the need to include the active influence of the local environment. The hypothesis for in silico methods of folding process simulation is assumed to use a multiple-object optimization technique that takes into account the optimization of the energy state of protein E as a function dependent on 2 functions: E (INT) (internal force field) and E (EXT) (external force field).

摘要翻译

尽管人工智能 (AlphaFold) 取得了重要进展，但体内蛋白质折叠问题仍未解决。实验研究表明，这一过程涉及多种机制。主要区别在于分子伴侣蛋白 (chaperone proteins) 是否参与该过程，而非自发折叠。进化分析表明，真核生物中的微管蛋白 (tubulin) 需要ATP依赖性环形伴侣蛋白 (chaperonin) TRiC/CCT的参与，而原核生物中的同源蛋白无需其他蛋白的支持即可获得其功能结构。本研究仅限于2个例子：一条人类微管蛋白β链和一个同源细菌FtsZ蛋白的结构，基于折叠过程的外部条件对这些差异提出假设性解释。以所比较蛋白中疏水性分布 (distribution of hydrophobicity) 的差异作为比较标准。模糊油滴模型 (fuzzy oil drop model, FOD-M) 假设原核蛋白的折叠是一个自发过程，仅由水环境的存在辅助，水环境引导折叠朝向生成中心定位的疏水核心和极性表面。真核蛋白——微管蛋白——表现出缺乏此类有序性的疏水性分布，这是基于FOD-M假设所解释的。这种差异似乎是折叠过程中涉及不同外力场 (external force fields) 的结果。这2种蛋白是一个例子，表明需要纳入局部环境的主动影响。折叠过程模拟的计算机方法 (in silico methods) 假说被假定为使用多目标优化技术 (multiple-object optimization technique)，该技术考虑将蛋白质能量状态E的优化作为依赖于2个函数的函数：E (INT)（内力场）和E (EXT)（外力场）。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：为何进化上同源的 tubulin（真核）需要 ATP 依赖的环状 chaperonin TRiC/CCT 辅助折叠，而细菌 FtsZ 可自发折叠？能否用疏水性分布差异来刻画这一区别？
  • 为什么重要：蛋白质体内折叠机制尚未解决，即使 AlphaFold 取得进展；理解 chaperone 依赖 vs 自发折叠的物理判据，对折叠模拟与结构预测的势函数设计有直接意义。
  • 现有方法为何不足：AI 方法（如 AlphaFold）主要解决结构预测，未直接解释体内折叠过程与外部环境（chaperone、力场）的作用；本文认为需纳入局部环境的主动影响。
  • 精确研究问题（Can ... ?）：Can the hydrophobicity distribution, as characterized by the fuzzy oil drop model (FOD-M), distinguish chaperone-dependent (human β-tubulin) from spontaneous (bacterial FtsZ) folding, and does this suggest a multi-objective optimization framework E = f(E(INT), E(EXT)) for in silico folding simulation?
