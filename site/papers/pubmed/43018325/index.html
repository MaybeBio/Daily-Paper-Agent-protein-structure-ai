Terahertz-Driven Conformational Remodeling of Filamentous Actin: Insights into Intense Terahertz-Field-Mediated Biopolymer Responses.

The journal of physical chemistry letters · 2026-11-12 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该研究利用太赫兹脉冲调控肌动蛋白丝构象，结合光谱实验与机器学习分析，虽涉及蛋白质构象变化但缺乏结构计算建模或AI/物理模拟方法，对蛋白质结构计算课题启发有限。

摘要

We demonstrate the precise, noninvasive control of filamentous actin (F-actin) conformations using high-intensity terahertz (THz) pulses via a dominantly nonthermal mechanism. By combining femtosecond time-resolved mid-infrared spectroscopy, Fourier-transform infrared (FTIR) spectroscopy, super-resolution confocal imaging, and a data-driven machine learning pipeline (CNN-UMAP-K-means), we show that 0.4 THz pulses selectively weaken intermolecular beta-sheet structures (1615 cm-1) while enhancing alpha-helical content (1650 cm-1) in F-actin, driving a dynamical shift toward monomer-like (G-actin) vibrational energy relaxation (VER) with up to 95% spectral similarity. In A549 cells, THz irradiation systematically reduces F-actin aggregation, as evidenced by fluorescence imaging. The spectral and dynamical changes induced by THz differ distinctly from thermal denaturation, with no characteristic peak shift observed under heating, supporting a dominantly nonthermal mechanism; our observations are consistent with coupling between intense terahertz fields and collective low-frequency intermonomer vibrational motions within F-actin filaments. The machine learning framework enables unbiased clustering and quantitative similarity analysis to identify optimal irradiation parameters (70 muJ, 10 min) that maximize selective F-actin remodeling without perturbing G-actin integrity. This work establishes intense THz as a powerful photonic tool for manipulating protein assemblies at molecular and cellular levels, advancing our fundamental understanding of THz-biopolymer interactions for biophotonic applications.

摘要翻译

我们展示了利用高强度太赫兹（THz）脉冲，通过主要非热机制，对丝状肌动蛋白（F-actin）构象进行精确、非侵入性控制。结合飞秒时间分辨中红外光谱、傅里叶变换红外（FTIR）光谱、超分辨共聚焦成像以及数据驱动的机器学习流程（CNN-UMAP-K-means），我们表明0.4 THz脉冲选择性削弱F-actin中的分子间β-折叠结构（1615 cm⁻¹），同时增强α-螺旋含量（1650 cm⁻¹），推动其向类单体（G-actin）振动能量弛豫（VER）的动态转变，光谱相似度高达95%。在A549细胞中，THz照射系统性减少F-actin聚集，荧光成像证实了这一点。THz诱导的光谱和动态变化与热变性明显不同，加热下未观察到特征峰位移，支持主要非热机制；我们的观察与强太赫兹场与F-actin丝内集体低频单体间振动模式之间的耦合一致。机器学习框架实现了无偏聚类和定量相似性分析，以确定最佳照射参数（70 μJ，10分钟），从而在不干扰G-actin完整性的情况下最大化选择性F-actin重塑。这项工作确立了强THz作为在分子和细胞水平操纵蛋白质组装的有力光子工具，推进了我们对THz-生物聚合物相互作用的基础理解，为生物光子应用奠定基础。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：高强度太赫兹脉冲能否以非热方式精确、可逆地重塑丝状肌动蛋白（F-actin）的构象？其分子机制是什么？
  • 为什么重要：太赫兹波段对应蛋白质集体低频振动模式（如氢键网络、亚基间运动），若能非热调控蛋白组装体构象，将为生物光子学、非侵入性细胞工程和蛋白纤维相关疾病（如细胞骨架病变）提供新工具。
  • 现有方法不足：传统热变性或化学变性缺乏空间/构象选择性；已有 THz 研究多关注水合作用或热效应，缺乏对蛋白二级结构特异性转变的系统定量分析，且缺少无偏的数据驱动方法来识别最优照射参数。
  • 精确研究问题：Can intense 0.4 THz pulses selectively remodel F-actin secondary structure via a nonthermal mechanism, driving it toward G-actin-like vibrational dynamics without thermal denaturation?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：用 0.4 THz 强脉冲照射 F-actin 溶液及 A549 细胞，结合飞秒时间分辨中红外光谱、FTIR、超分辨成像和 CNN-UMAP-K-means 机器学习流程，分析二级结构变化并优化参数。
  2. 核心洞察：THz 场与 F-actin 亚基间集体低频振动模式耦合，选择性削弱分子间 β-sheet（维持纤维聚合的界面）而增强 α-helix，使 F-actin 向 G-actin 单体态偏移——这是一种非热、非破坏性的「构象重塑」而非变性。
  3. [Analysis] 可能的普适教训：对于蛋白质组装体，低频集体模式（THz 范围）可作为「构象开关」的靶点；机器学习聚类可有效从高维光谱数据中提取无偏的构象状态标签，该思路可迁移至 MD 模拟轨迹分析或构象采样中的状态识别。

07 方法总览

  • 输入：F-actin 溶液（体外）或 A549 细胞（体内）；0.4 THz 脉冲（强度可调，最优 70 μJ，10 min）。
  • 输出：二级结构变化（β-sheet 减少、α-helix 增加）、F-actin 聚集程度（细胞成像）、G-actin 相似度（95%）。
  • 模块： 1. THz 照射系统（0.4 THz，强度/时间可控） 2. 飞秒时间分辨中红外光谱（探测 VER 动力学） 3. FTIR（静态二级结构定量） 4. 超分辨荧光成像（细胞中 F-actin 形态） 5. CNN-UMAP-K-means 机器学习流程（无偏聚类与相似性分析）
  • 训练：CNN 用于光谱特征提取，UMAP 降维，K-means 聚类；训练数据来自不同照射条件下的光谱集合（具体规模未提供）。

  • 假设：THz 场与 F-actin 亚基间低频振动模式共振耦合；非热机制占主导。
  • 流程：制备 F-actin → 不同 THz 参数照射 → 采集时间分辨中红外光谱与 FTIR → 机器学习聚类识别构象状态 → 计算与 G-actin 光谱相似度 → 细胞实验验证 → 确定最优参数（70 μJ, 10 min）。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents an intriguing and potentially significant claim regarding nonthermal terahertz control of protein conformation. However, the evidence base provided is insufficient to evaluate the technical soundness of the central claims. The abstract reports multiple advanced techniques and a striking quantitative result (95% spectral similarity) without presenting the underlying data or analysis details. The mechanistic interpretation, while plausible, is not substantiated by the visible evidence. The work could be of broad interest if the claims hold, but the current abstract alone does not establish the case.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers in terahertz biophotonics, protein biophysics, and nonthermal electromagnetic field effects on biomolecules would find these results relevant. The potential for noninvasive manipulation of cytoskeletal proteins could interest cell biologists and those developing photonic tools for biomedical applications. The machine learning approach to parameter optimization may appeal to researchers applying data-driven methods in biophysics.
  • Major strengths The combination of multiple orthogonal techniques (time-resolved mid-IR spectroscopy, FTIR, super-resolution imaging, machine learning) is a comprehensive approach. The explicit comparison to thermal denaturation is a thoughtful control design. The claim of nonthermal mechanism, if supported, would represent a meaningful advance in understanding THz-biopolymer interactions.
