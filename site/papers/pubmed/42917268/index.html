Modeling the Impact of Active Site Mutations on the Quenching Fate and Cyclization Depth of a Rare Oxidosqualene Cyclase.

Liu, Chenxu; Liang, Shun; Zheng, Ying; Wang, Feng; Jiao, Jiaqi; Li, Bing; Liang, JinCai; Liu, Zhongqiu; Duan, Lixin; Wu, Ruibo; Zhang, Fan
Journal of chemical theory and computation · 2026-10-23 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文通过QM/MM模拟与定点突变研究氧化角鲨烯环化酶活性位点突变对环化深度和淬灭命运的影响，属于蛋白质结构相关的计算模拟与机制研究，方法上采用多尺度QM/MM，与课题高度相关。

摘要

Oxidosqualene cyclases (OSCs) convert linear 2,3-oxidosqualene into diverse polycyclic triterpenoids. The precise control over highly reactive carbocation intermediates, particularly the ultimate quenching mechanism (deprotonation vs hydroxylation), remains a fundamental challenge in mechanism-driven enzyme reshaping. Here, we investigate AaOSC-20433, a rare OSC from Artemisia argyi exhibiting absolute specificity for pure deprotonation to produce dammara-20,24-dien-3beta-ol. This starkly contrasts with the homologous dammarenediol synthase from Panax ginseng (PgDDS), which favors hydroxylation to yield dammarenediol II. Integrating multiscale QM/MM simulations and site-directed mutagenesis, we modeled the impact of crucial active site mutations on the reaction cascade. Our simulations revealed that S411 acts as a general base governing the terminal deprotonation. More importantly, we demonstrate that mechanism-guided active site substitutions can dramatically alter the chemical trajectory. Relaxing steric constraints (F728A) induces catalytic promiscuity to yield tricyclic and tetracyclic products. Furthermore, the L259Y mutation significantly alters the quenching network. It unlocks the hydroxylation pathway to form dammarenediol II and acts as an engineered base extending cyclization depth to yield the pentacyclic lupeol. Notably, combining these functional loci (L259Y+F728S) reveals notable synergistic epistasis between the steric cavity and the proton-transfer network, driving competitive hydration events to diversify the hydroxylated product profile. This study provides a comprehensive mechanistic framework for understanding how OSC active site mutations govern product specificity, offering a robust theoretical basis for the tailored, mechanism-driven functional reprogramming of triterpene synthases in synthetic biology.

摘要翻译

氧化角鲨烯环化酶（OSCs）将线性2,3-氧化角鲨烯转化为多种多环三萜类化合物。对高反应性碳正离子中间体的精确控制，特别是最终淬灭机制（去质子化与羟基化），仍是机制驱动酶重塑中的基本挑战。在此，我们研究了AaOSC-20433，一种来自艾蒿（Artemisia argyi）的稀有OSC，其表现出对纯去质子化的绝对特异性，生成dammara-20,24-dien-3beta-ol。这与来自人参（Panax ginseng）的同源达玛烯二醇合酶（PgDDS）形成鲜明对比，后者倾向于羟基化以生成达玛烯二醇II。通过整合多尺度QM/MM模拟和定点突变，我们模拟了关键活性位点突变对反应级联的影响。我们的模拟揭示S411作为通用碱，控制末端去质子化。更重要的是，我们证明机制引导的活性位点替换能显著改变化学轨迹。放松空间约束（F728A）诱导催化混杂性，生成三环和四环产物。此外，L259Y突变显著改变淬灭网络，解锁羟基化途径以形成达玛烯二醇II，并作为工程化碱延伸环化深度，生成五环羽扇豆醇。值得注意的是，结合这些功能位点（L259Y+F728S）揭示了空间腔与质子转移网络之间显著的协同上位效应，驱动竞争性水合事件以多样化羟基化产物谱。本研究提供了理解OSC活性位点突变如何调控产物特异性的全面机制框架，为合成生物学中三萜合酶的定制化、机制驱动功能重编程提供了坚实的理论基础。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题: OSC 活性位点突变如何精确控制高反应性碳正离子中间体的淬灭命运（去质子化 vs 羟基化）以及环化深度？
  • 为什么重要: OSC 催化产生结构多样的三萜类化合物，是药物和天然产物的重要来源。理解淬灭机制的控制规则是实现机制驱动的酶功能重塑（即理性设计产物特异性）的前提。
  • 现有方法为何不足: 尽管已有大量 OSC 结构和功能研究，但缺乏对淬灭命运（去质子化 vs 羟基化）的原子级机制理解，尤其是突变如何重塑淬灭网络和环化深度的定量模型。
  • 精确研究问题: Can active site mutations in AaOSC-20433 predictably switch the quenching fate (deprotonation vs hydroxylation) and extend cyclization depth (from tetracyclic to pentacyclic products)?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法: 对 AaOSC-20433 及其突变体（F728A, L259Y, L259Y+F728S）进行多尺度 QM/MM 模拟，结合定点突变实验验证产物谱变化。
  2. 核心洞察: 淬灭命运（去质子化 vs 羟基化）由活性位点中「淬灭网络」的竞争平衡决定——S411 是去质子化的通用碱，而 L259Y 通过引入新的碱基功能解锁羟基化途径；环化深度则由立体约束（如 F728 的空间位阻）和质子转移网络的协同控制。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]: 酶活性位点的功能重塑不能仅考虑单一残基的化学性质，而需将「空间约束」与「质子转移网络」视为耦合系统——这类似于蛋白质工程中「活性位点残基的远距离协同」概念，可迁移到其他萜类合酶甚至一般酶的设计中。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a compelling and mechanistically rich hypothesis about how active site mutations in a rare OSC can reprogram product specificity. The central claims are plausible and potentially significant for the terpene biosynthesis community. However, the abstract alone provides insufficient evidence to evaluate the rigor of the simulations, the validity of the mutagenesis results, or the robustness of the epistasis conclusion. The case is intriguing but not established from the supplied material.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Enzymologists and computational chemists studying terpene cyclases and carbocation chemistry would find the mechanistic insights valuable. Synthetic biologists and metabolic engineers interested in triterpene diversification would also be interested in the demonstrated potential for mechanism-guided enzyme reprogramming. The work could inform future engineering of OSC variants for producing high-value triterpenoids.
  • Major strengths The study addresses a fundamental question in OSC catalysis, namely the control of quenching fate and cyclization depth. The contrast between AaOSC-20433 and PgDDS provides a natural comparative framework. The identification of specific residues (S411, F728, L259) with distinct functional roles offers a clear mechanistic narrative. The reported synergistic epistasis between steric and proton-transfer elements is conceptually novel and could have broad implications.
