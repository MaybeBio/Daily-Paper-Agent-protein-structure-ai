Structural and Mechanistic Insights Into GSDME Pore Formation and Functional Regulation.

Mo, Fei; Chen, Xiaoling; Zhang, Jiyun; Li, Danyang; Xing, Feiyue; Guo, Feng-Biao; Zhang, Zhengyu; Dong, Changjiang
FASEB journal : official publication of the Federation of American Societies for Experimental Biology · 2026-10-08 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文通过冷冻电镜结构和分子动力学模拟揭示GSDME成孔机制，虽对象为膜蛋白孔而非典型可溶性蛋白结构，但结构解析与MD模拟方法对蛋白质结构计算研究有参考价值。

摘要

Gasdermin E (GSDME) is a unique member of the gasdermin family linked to deafness, cancer, and neurodegenerative diseases, and mediates chemotherapy-induced pyroptosis; however, its pore-forming mechanism remains poorly understood. Here, we present cryo-EM structures of GSDME pores with oligomeric stoichiometries ranging from 23 to 29 protomers. Using mutagenesis, liposome leakage assays, and analysis of cancer-associated mutations, we identified key residues responsible for membrane binding, shallow membrane insertion, and oligomerization through three distinct structural interfaces. Molecular dynamics simulations reveal dynamic lipid recruitment and specificity: Anionic TMCL2 is selectively recruited by basic residues to facilitate shallow membrane penetration, while zwitterionic DPhPC solvates polar protein regions. Within the assembled 25-mer pore, specific lipids act as structural stabilizers to reinforce the intermolecular hydrogen bond network. Our findings support a stepwise, time-dependent mechanism underlying GSDME pore assembly. This study provides critical mechanistic insights into GSDME function and may guide the development of targeted therapeutics for cancers and neurodegenerative diseases.

摘要翻译

Gasdermin E (GSDME) 是gasdermin家族中一个独特的成员，与耳聋、癌症和神经退行性疾病相关，并介导化疗诱导的细胞焦亡；然而，其成孔机制仍知之甚少。在此，我们展示了GSDME孔的冷冻电镜结构，其寡聚化学计量范围从23到29个原体。通过诱变、脂质体泄漏实验以及癌症相关突变分析，我们鉴定了负责膜结合、浅层膜插入以及通过三个不同结构界面进行寡聚化的关键残基。分子动力学 (molecular dynamics) 模拟揭示了动态脂质募集和特异性：阴离子型TMCL2被碱性残基选择性募集以促进浅层膜穿透，而两性离子型DPhPC则溶剂化极性蛋白区域。在组装的25聚体孔中，特定脂质作为结构稳定剂，增强分子间氢键网络。我们的发现支持GSDME孔组装遵循逐步、时间依赖的机制。本研究为GSDME功能提供了关键的机制性见解，并可能指导针对癌症和神经退行性疾病的靶向治疗开发。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题: GSDME 如何通过寡聚化形成跨膜孔道？哪些残基和脂质参与膜结合、插入和寡聚化？孔道组装的动态过程如何被调控？
  • 为什么重要: GSDME 是 gasdermin 家族中与耳聋、癌症、神经退行性疾病相关的独特成员，介导化疗诱导的焦亡。理解其孔道形成机制对开发靶向治疗策略至关重要。
  • 现有方法为何不足: 此前对 gasdermin 家族（如 GSDMD）的孔道结构已有研究，但 GSDME 的孔道结构、寡聚态范围、脂质选择性及组装动态机制尚不清楚；缺乏高分辨率结构及与脂质互作的原子级细节。
  • 精确研究问题: "Can we define the structural interfaces and lipid-recruitment rules that govern GSDME pore assembly, and does a stepwise, time-dependent mechanism explain the observed oligomeric heterogeneity?"
