Physics-Inspired Multi-Body Descriptors Enable Interpretable Density Prediction of Energetic Ionic Salts.

Wang, Xiaoyan; He, Xiaokai; Zhang, Yining; Li, Guojin; Chen, Chao; Liu, Yingzhe
Journal of chemical information and modeling · 2026-10-22 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文用物理启发的多体描述符预测含能离子盐晶体密度，虽涉及晶体结构特征但非蛋白质结构计算，且方法为传统描述符与机器学习回归，与蛋白质结构AI/模拟课题关联微弱。

摘要

Machine learning has introduced a transformative paradigm for the rapid prediction of crystal properties, circumventing the need for explicit knowledge of crystal packing structures. However, when applied to high-density organic systems such as energetic ionic salts, conventional general-purpose molecular descriptors often exhibit limited prediction accuracy, generalization capability, and extrapolation capability. To address this challenge, we propose a physics-inspired multi-body descriptor (PIMBD). By decoupling the modeling of cations and anions and employing the co-evolutionary optimization of multi-body function hyperparameters, PIMBD effectively captures spatial arrangement features of atoms governed by strong Coulombic interactions. We established a benchmark dataset comprising more than 1200 energetic ionic crystals and conducted a systematic comparison of 6 representative descriptor frameworks and 29 machine learning algorithms. The results show that PIMBD achieves high predictive accuracy, with a mean absolute error (MAE) of 0.034 g/cm3. Meanwhile, it achieves an MAE of 0.0282 g/cm3 on an independent validation set composed of pentazolate-based ionic salts, shows pronounced advantages in the high-density region, and outperforms other descriptors in terms of generalization robustness and extrapolation capability. Furthermore, atomic-scale interpretability analyses were performed to elucidate the critical structural factors governing crystal density, leading to the distillation of three physically meaningful design principles. This work provides an efficient and interpretable descriptive methodology specifically tailored for ionic salts and may potentially accelerate the rational structure design of high-energy-density materials.

摘要翻译

机器学习为晶体性质的快速预测引入了变革性范式，绕过了对晶体堆积结构显式知识的需求。然而，当应用于高密度有机体系（如含能离子盐）时，常规通用分子描述符往往表现出有限的预测精度、泛化能力和外推能力。为应对这一挑战，我们提出了一种物理启发的多体描述符（PIMBD）。通过解耦阳离子和阴离子的建模，并采用多体函数超参数的协同进化优化，PIMBD有效捕捉了由强库仑相互作用控制的原子空间排列特征。我们构建了一个包含1200多种含能离子晶体的基准数据集，并对6种代表性描述符框架和29种机器学习算法进行了系统比较。结果表明，PIMBD实现了高预测精度，平均绝对误差（MAE）为0.034 g/cm³。同时，在由五唑基离子盐组成的独立验证集上，其MAE为0.0282 g/cm³，在高密度区域表现出显著优势，并在泛化鲁棒性和外推能力方面优于其他描述符。此外，进行了原子尺度可解释性分析，以阐明控制晶体密度的关键结构因素，从而提炼出三条具有物理意义的设计原则。这项工作为离子盐提供了一种高效且可解释的描述方法，并可能加速高能量密度材料的合理结构设计。
