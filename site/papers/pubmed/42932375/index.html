Hydrophobic remodeling of the SARS-CoV-2 RBD large loop attenuates ACE2 engagement and viral fitness.

Wang, Aojie; Guo, Huimin; Yang, Haonan; Chen, Haojin; Imojara, Anna; Ju, Bin; Zhang, Zheng; Yan, Renhong
PLoS pathogens · 2026-10-26 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

结合冷冻电镜、分子动力学模拟和功能实验，研究SARS-CoV-2 RBD大环疏水重塑如何影响ACE2结合与病毒适应性，属于蛋白质结构-功能计算模拟研究。

摘要

The SARS-CoV-2 Omicron subvariant JN.4 harbors a unique A475V substitution within the receptor binding domain (RBD) but exhibits limited expansion compared to its parallel descendant JN.1. Here, we investigate how hydrophobic remodeling within RBD large loop (residues 475-490) modulates receptor engagement and spike function. Cryo-electron microscopy reveals that A475V induces local remodeling of the receptor-binding motif (RBM) and alters the RBD-ACE2 interaction geometry. Local refinement revealed that the bulky valine substitution disrupts the local interaction network surrounding residue 475, resulting in a 3.5-fold reduction in ACE2-binding affinity compared with BA.2.86. In contrast to the L455S substitution in JN.1, which primarily alters the receptor-binding interface, A475V within the flexible large loop influences both local loop conformation and global spike conformational properties. Systematic mutagenesis identifies A475 and F486 as functionally constrained hydrophobic residues that preserve the structural framework of ACE2 engagement despite extensive sequence variation within the surrounding loop. Together with molecular dynamics simulations and functional assays, our findings establish the RBD 475-490 large loop as a structurally plastic yet functionally constrained element that balances receptor recognition, conformational stability, and antigenic diversification. These results provide a mechanistic framework for understanding region-specific evolutionary constraints that shape SARS-CoV-2 RBD adaptation.

摘要翻译

SARS-CoV-2 Omicron亚变体JN.4在受体结合域（RBD）内携带独特的A475V替换，但与其平行后代JN.1相比，其扩张有限。在此，我们研究了RBD大环（残基475-490）内的疏水重塑如何调节受体结合和刺突蛋白功能。冷冻电子显微镜揭示，A475V诱导受体结合基序（RBM）的局部重塑，并改变RBD-ACE2相互作用几何结构。局部细化显示，庞大的缬氨酸替换破坏了残基475周围的局部相互作用网络，导致与BA.2.86相比，ACE2结合亲和力降低3.5倍。与JN.1中主要改变受体结合界面的L455S替换相反，柔性大环内的A475V同时影响局部环构象和整体刺突蛋白构象特性。系统性突变分析确定A475和F486为功能受限的疏水残基，尽管周围环内存在广泛序列变异，它们仍保持ACE2结合的结构框架。结合分子动力学模拟和功能实验，我们的发现确立了RBD 475-490大环作为一个结构可塑但功能受限的元件，平衡受体识别、构象稳定性和抗原多样化。这些结果为理解塑造SARS-CoV-2 RBD适应的区域特异性进化约束提供了机制框架。

02 一句话总结

针对 SARS-CoV-2 JN.4 的 RBD A475V 突变，作者用 Cryo-EM、BLI 结合动力学、假病毒感染/融合实验与 MD 模拟，发现该疏水替换通过重塑 475–490 large loop 的局部构象与 RBD–ACE2 界面几何，使 ACE2 结合亲和力较 BA.2.86 降低约 3.5 倍并削弱病毒功能，从而提出该 loop 是「结构可塑但功能受约束」的元件。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：RBD 475–490 large loop 内的疏水替换（以 JN.4 的 A475V 为代表）如何改变 RBD–ACE2 结合几何、结合亲和力、spike 构象稳定性与病毒功能（感染性、膜融合、抗体逃逸）？
  • 为什么重要：该 loop 是反复出现的突变热点，直接接触 ACE2 并重叠多个中和抗体表位；理解其突变后果有助于解释为何 JN.4 与 JN.1 同为 BA.2.86 后代却流行程度差异巨大，并揭示 RBD 进化的区域特异性约束。
  • 现有方法为何不足：既往 RBD-ACE2 界面研究主要聚焦带电/极性替换对氢键与盐桥的重连（如 N501Y、Q493R、E484K 等），对柔性 loop 中疏水替换如何通过构象重塑影响受体结合与功能稳定性理解不足；对「刚性 middle region」与「柔性 large loop」的差异化机制缺乏系统对比。
  • 精确研究问题（Can ... ?）：Can a nominally conservative hydrophobic substitution (A475V) within the flexible RBD 475–490 large loop remodel the RBD–ACE2 interface geometry and attenuate ACE2 engagement and viral fitness, in a manner mechanistically distinct from the rigid middle-region substitution L455S?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：以 JN.4（BA.2.86 后代，携带 A475V）为对象，整合 Cryo-EM 结构解析（全局 2.90 Å、局部 3.73 Å）、BLI 结合动力学（9 个单突变 + 1 个双突变）、假病毒感染性与细胞-细胞融合实验、热稳定性（TSA）、MD 模拟（300 ns，MM/GBSA）与系统发育/序列比对，解析 A475V 的结构与功能后果。
  2. 核心洞察：RBD-ACE2 界面呈「二分架构」——anchor region（Arg498/Thr500/Tyr501）为不变「铆钉」，large loop（475–490）高度可塑；A475V 虽为化学保守的疏水替换，却通过空间位阻破坏局部互作网络、改变 loop 几何（475–486 距离 3.8 Å→8.5 Å）、可能扰动 Cys480-Cys488 二硫键几何，进而改变界面几何与多价结合，导致亲和力下降约 3.5 倍；A475 与 F486 是维持受体结合框架的功能约束疏水锚。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：在柔性、暴露且高变的蛋白界面区域，化学保守的疏水替换可通过构象重塑产生非局部、非线性的功能后果；「结构可塑性」与「功能约束」可共存，关键锚定残基在序列高度变异背景下仍受约束。这一思路可迁移到其他蛋白-配体界面（如抗体-抗原、酶-底物）的突变效应预测与设计约束建模。

09 关键公式符号

  • KD（平衡解离常数）：KD = koff/kon，用于量化 RBD–ACE2 结合亲和力；文中以 1:1 全局拟合模型分析 BLI 数据。来源：Methods「Measurement of Omicron subvariants and WT strain RBD binding to human ACE2-PD by biolayer interferometry」。
  • MM/GBSA 结合自由能：ΔG_bind ≈ ΔE_MM + ΔG_solv（GB 模型，OBC，盐浓度 150 mM），用于比较 A475V 与 V475A 的结合自由能；取轨迹最后 100 ns 快照。来源：Methods「Molecular Dynamics (MD)」。
  • Deflection angle（偏转角）：ACE2 质心与 RBD 质心之间的偏转角，用于量化 RBD-T–ACE2 相对三聚体中心的倾斜；JN.4 74.9°、BA.2.86 70.0°、JN.1 76.4°。来源：Results 图 1E。
  • RMSD：局部精修模型与 JN.1 (8ZBQ)、BA.2.86 (8WYH) 的 RMSD 分别为 1.1 Å 与 1.058 Å。来源：Results「Region-specific remodeling mechanisms」。
  • 距离指标：residue 475–Pro486 距离 3.8 Å (BA.2.86) → 8.5 Å (JN.4)；Ser494–His34 距离 ~7.5 Å (JN.4) vs ~4.9 Å (JN.1, Tyr453–His34)。来源：Results 图 2D–E。

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围：本文结论限于 SARS-CoV-2 RBD 475–490 large loop 的疏水替换（以 A475V 为核心）在 BA.2.86/JN.1/JN.4 背景下的结构-功能后果。
  • oracle/真值输入：不适用；结构为实验测定，非 oracle 输入。
  • 端到端状态：从序列到结构、结合、功能、模拟的整合分析，但非端到端预测模型；MD 为辅助验证。
  • 算力成本：未提供具体算力成本；MD 为 300 ns/系统。
  • 历史数据依赖：系统发育与序列比对依赖 GISAID/NCBI 已公开序列；结构对比依赖已发表 PDB。
  • 模型依赖：MD 结果依赖 CHARMM36 力场、TIP3P 水模型、MM/GBSA 近似；TSA 依赖多 sigmoid 拟合模型。
  • 最难情形：柔性 loop 的构象异质性、多价结合的空间位阻、以及突变在不同遗传背景下的上位效应。
  • 群体/领域边界：结论基于 Omicron 亚系与部分 sarbecovirus 序列；不直接适用于其他病毒或非 RBD 区域。
  • 不确定性：A475V 对流行适应度的净效应受免疫背景、传播环境等多因素影响；结构观察与功能后果之间的因果链部分依赖作者解释。
  • 有边界的复述：在本文实验与模拟条件下，A475V 通过重塑 475–490 large loop 的局部构象与界面几何，降低 ACE2 结合亲和力（~3.5×）并削弱感染性与融合功能，而 A475/F486 表现为功能约束疏水锚；该结论不宣称可推广到所有 RBD 突变或所有病毒背景。

Review setup

  • Input scope Full manuscript text including abstract, introduction, results, discussion, materials and methods, and supporting information references.
  • Assessment boundary Scientific content, methodological rigor, internal consistency, and support of claims by presented evidence. Editorial or stylistic preferences are noted only where they affect comprehension.
  • Shared manuscript claim summary The authors propose that the A475V substitution in the SARS-CoV-2 RBD large loop (residues 475–490) attenuates ACE2 binding and viral fitness through hydrophobic remodeling and conformational reorganization, in contrast to the L455S substitution in JN.1 which primarily affects local receptor contacts. They further propose that the large loop is a structurally plastic yet functionally constrained element balancing receptor recognition, conformational stability, and antigenic diversification.
  • Visible evidence base Cryo-EM structures of JN.4 spike in complex with ACE2 (global 2.90 Å, local 3.73 Å), biolayer interferometry binding measurements, pseudovirus infectivity assays, cell-cell fusion assays, thermal shift assays, molecular dynamics simulations, and phylogenetic analyses.
  • Missing materials affecting confidence Raw cryo-EM maps and validation reports (half-map FSC curves, model vs map Q-scores) are not provided. Statistical details for binding measurements (number of replicates, error bars) are not fully specified. MD simulation convergence criteria and replicate information are not provided. Pseudovirus infectivity data are presented without numerical values or statistical comparisons in the text. Supporting figures (S1–S7) and tables (S1–S2) are referenced but not available for review.

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment This manuscript addresses a relevant question in SARS-CoV-2 evolution: how a hydrophobic substitution in a flexible RBD loop affects receptor engagement and viral fitness. The combination of structural, biophysical, and functional approaches is appropriate and potentially informative. However, the central claim that A475V attenuates viral fitness through a specific structural mechanism is only partially supported by the presented evidence. The structural analysis is descriptive and lacks quantitative validation of the proposed conformational differences. The functional data are presented without sufficient statistical detail, and the connection between reduced ACE2 affinity and viral fitness is inferred rather than directly demonstrated. The manuscript would benefit from clearer presentation of quantitative results and a more rigorous treatment of the structural comparisons.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers studying SARS-CoV-2 evolution, spike protein structure-function relationships, and viral entry mechanisms. The work may also interest those investigating how structural flexibility in protein interfaces constrains or permits sequence diversification under immune pressure.
  • Major strengths The study combines multiple complementary approaches (cryo-EM, BLI, pseudovirus assays, MD simulations) to address a focused question. The comparison between JN.1 and JN.4 provides a natural experiment for dissecting region-specific effects of mutations. The identification of A475 and F486 as functionally constrained hydrophobic residues within a variable loop is a potentially useful conceptual contribution.

  • Concern ID R1-M1
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Evidence sufficiency for structural claims
  • Claim pointer The authors claim that A475V induces "local remodeling of the receptor-binding motif" and "alters the RBD–ACE2 interaction geometry," and that this results in a "substantial reorganization of loop geometry" based on a distance change from 3.8 Å to 8.5 Å between residues 475 and 486.
  • Evidence pointer Results section "Region-specific remodeling mechanisms at the RBD-ACE2 interfaces," Fig 2
  • Concern The structural interpretation relies on a single cryo-EM structure of JN.4 at 3.73 Å local resolution. The claim of "substantial reorganization" is based on a single distance measurement between two residues, without statistical comparison across multiple structures or states. The RMSD values reported (1.1 Å and 1.058 Å against JN.1 and BA.2.86, respectively) suggest overall structural conservation, yet the authors emphasize local differences without providing quantitative measures of local conformational change (e.g., per-residue RMSF, dihedral angle changes, or interaction energy calculations). The proposed mechanism of steric hindrance from the bulkier valine side chain is plausible but not directly demonstrated.
  • Why it matters The central mechanistic claim of the paper rests on the structural interpretation. If the conformational differences are within the noise of the cryo-EM maps at this resolution, the proposed mechanism is not established.
  • Resolution test Provide quantitative comparison of local conformational differences (e.g., per-residue RMSD or RMSF between structures), map-to-model validation metrics (Q-scores) for the loop region, and ideally multiple independent reconstructions or focused classification to demonstrate that the observed loop conformation is stable and not an artifact of local resolution limitations.
