Early Pathway Selection Precedes Conformational Locking in Protein Self-Assembly.

Peng, Ruoxuan; Zhou, Lele; Bai, Runnan; Yang, Yunhao; Zhou, Yi; Zhang, Hao; Wang, Xinyan; Ren, Jing; Li, Jian; Liu, Yifan; Ling, Shengjie
ACS nano · 2026-11-10 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文以再生丝素蛋白为模型，结合电场扰动、多尺度表征和粗粒化模拟研究蛋白质自组装早期路径选择，虽非典型蛋白结构计算，但粗粒化模拟和组装机制对蛋白构象采样有借鉴意义。

摘要

When early intermediates begin to bias subsequent pathways during protein self-assembly remains difficult to resolve experimentally because early-stage processes are transient and poorly accessible. Here, using regenerated silk fibroin as a model system, we experimentally capture and analyze an early nucleation window in which assembly pathways begin to diverge before conformational locking occurs. By combining controlled electric-field perturbation with multiscale structural characterization and coarse-grained simulations, we identify reproducible nanoscale intermediates that mediate pathway selection. Within this window, electric fields bias early assembly kinetics in a nonmonotonic manner, modulating the formation, elongation, and competition of intermediates rather than directly enforcing structure or orientation. Time-resolved measurements show that subsequent growth proceeds primarily through elongation and fusion of existing intermediates, while beta-sheet formation and crystallization emerge only at later stages and serve to stabilize pathways that were biased at earlier stages. Together, we locate the emergence of detectable pathway biasing within the nucleation stage and provide a framework for understanding and guiding protein self-assembly under nonequilibrium conditions.

摘要翻译

在蛋白质自组装过程中，早期中间体何时开始偏向后续路径，这一问题的实验解析仍具挑战性，因为早期阶段过程短暂且难以触及。在此，我们以再生丝素蛋白作为模型系统，实验捕获并分析了一个早期成核窗口，在该窗口中，组装路径在构象锁定发生前开始分化。通过结合受控电场扰动、多尺度结构表征和粗粒化模拟，我们识别出可重复的纳米尺度中间体，这些中间体介导路径选择。在此窗口内，电场以非单调方式偏向早期组装动力学，调节中间体的形成、延伸和竞争，而非直接强制结构或取向。时间分辨测量显示，后续生长主要通过现有中间体的延伸和融合进行，而β-折叠形成和结晶仅在后期出现，并用于稳定早期阶段已被偏向的路径。综合来看，我们将可检测的路径偏向定位在成核阶段内，并为理解和指导非平衡条件下的蛋白质自组装提供了一个框架。

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：用再生丝素蛋白作为模型系统，施加受控电场扰动，结合多尺度结构表征（时间分辨）与粗粒化模拟，捕获成核早期窗口中的纳米级中间体。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a conceptually interesting and potentially significant claim about the temporal ordering of pathway selection relative to conformational locking in protein self-assembly. The use of electric-field perturbation as a kinetic probe is creative. However, the abstract alone provides insufficient detail to evaluate the technical soundness of the experimental and computational approaches, the robustness of the evidence for the central claims, or the generalizability of the findings beyond the specific silk fibroin system. The core claim that pathway biasing precedes conformational locking is plausible but not established from the supplied material.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers in protein self-assembly, biomaterials, and nonequilibrium soft matter would be interested. The work addresses a fundamental question about when assembly pathways become committed, which has implications for designing self-assembling materials and for understanding pathological protein aggregation. The methodological combination of electric-field perturbation with structural characterization may also interest experimentalists seeking new kinetic probes.
  • Major strengths The research question is well-posed and addresses a genuine gap in understanding early-stage assembly dynamics. The use of electric-field perturbation as a noninvasive kinetic modulator is an innovative approach. The claim that beta-sheet formation and crystallization are late-stage stabilizers rather than early drivers is a testable and potentially important distinction. The multiscale approach combining experiments and simulations is appropriate for the question.
