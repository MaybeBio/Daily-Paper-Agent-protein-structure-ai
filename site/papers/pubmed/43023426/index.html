Leveraging amino acid embeddings for accurate translation efficiency prediction.

Terai, Goro; Asai, Kiyoshi
Bioinformatics (Oxford, England) · 2026-11-13 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文利用蛋白语言模型氨基酸嵌入预测翻译效率，虽涉及蛋白序列嵌入，但核心是mRNA翻译效率预测，非蛋白质结构计算问题，方法可借鉴性有限。

摘要

MOTIVATION: Accurate prediction of translation efficiency from mRNA sequences is an important step toward mRNA sequence design. In recent years, the widespread adoption of high-resolution ribosome profiling techniques, such as Ribo-seq, has led to rapid accumulation of large-scale datasets that capture the relationship between mRNA sequences and translation efficiency. This progress has facilitated the development of machine-learning-based models for translation efficiency prediction. However, most existing approaches rely primarily on mRNA-derived features, such as codon usage frequency and 5' untranslated regions (5'UTRs), and do not directly utilize the amino-acid-level information. RESULTS: In this study, we show that incorporating distributed representations (embeddings) of amino acid sequences translated from mRNA as model features can improve translation efficiency prediction. By combining fixed amino acid embeddings derived from a pretrained protein language model with mRNA-derived features, the proposed model integrates complementary protein-level and nucleotide-level information for more accurate prediction of translation efficiency. AVAILABILITY AND IMPLEMENTATION: Codes are available at GitHub (https://github.com/gterai/AA_emb) and Zenodo (https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23968476).

摘要翻译

动机：从mRNA序列准确预测翻译效率是迈向mRNA序列设计的重要一步。近年来，高分辨率核糖体图谱技术（如Ribo-seq）的广泛采用，导致捕捉mRNA序列与翻译效率关系的大规模数据集迅速积累。这一进展促进了基于机器学习的翻译效率预测模型的发展。然而，大多数现有方法主要依赖mRNA衍生的特征，如密码子使用频率和5'非翻译区（5'UTRs），并未直接利用氨基酸水平的信息。结果：在本研究中，我们表明，将mRNA翻译出的氨基酸序列的分布式表示（嵌入）作为模型特征，可以改善翻译效率预测。通过将预训练蛋白质语言模型衍生的固定氨基酸嵌入与mRNA衍生特征相结合，所提出的模型整合了互补的蛋白质水平和核苷酸水平信息，以实现更准确的翻译效率预测。可用性和实现：代码可在GitHub（https://github.com/gterai/AA_emb）和Zenodo（https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23968476）获取。
