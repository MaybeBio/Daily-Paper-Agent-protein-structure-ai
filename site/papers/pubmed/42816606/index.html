Function-preserving watermarking of AI-generated proteins.

该文提出SynthIDBio方法，在AI生成的蛋白质序列和结构中嵌入保持功能的数字水印，涉及蛋白质结构预测与设计，并利用微调AlphaFold3模型，属于蛋白质结构计算与AI方法创新。

摘要

Generative artificial intelligence (AI) models are revolutionizing biology, with tools such as AlphaFold 3 and protein design models accelerating breakthroughs in protein structure prediction and the creation of new functional proteins(1). Tracking and establishing the provenance of AI-generated protein sequences and structures is becoming increasingly important to tackle a range of emerging challenges, including biosecurity and concerns about information veracity(2-4). Here we introduce SynthIDBio, a family of methods for watermarking protein sequences and structures to establish the provenance of those generated with AI. SynthIDBio-sequence actively embeds a watermark into protein sequences while preserving function. We demonstrate this by creating watermarked, functional designed protein binders with binding affinity comparable with non-watermarked counterparts and near-perfect watermark detection accuracy. Furthermore, SynthIDBio-structure, a fine-tuned AlphaFold3 model, embeds an imperceptible watermark into biomolecular structures. Our work is a proof-of-concept that function-preserving biological watermarking is feasible, introducing a potential tool for provenance in the rapidly expanding era of AI-driven biological engineering.

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：如何在不损害蛋白质功能（如结合亲和力）的前提下，为 AI 生成的蛋白质序列和结构嵌入可检测的水印，以建立来源追踪（provenance）？
  • 为什么重要：AI 生成蛋白质工具（如 AlphaFold 3、蛋白设计模型）加速了功能蛋白的创造，但随之而来的是生物安全（biosecurity）与信息真实性（veracity）问题，需要可靠的手段追踪 AI 生成内容的来源。
  • 现有方法不足：现有水印方法多针对文本或图像，不适用于生物序列；直接对序列加扰动可能破坏功能；结构层面的水印尚未有系统方案。
  • 精确研究问题：Can we embed a watermark into AI-generated protein sequences and structures while preserving their biological function (e.g., binding affinity)?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法： - SynthIDBio-sequence：在序列生成过程中主动嵌入水印（如特定密码子/氨基酸模式），同时优化功能保持。 - SynthIDBio-structure：微调 AlphaFold3 模型，使其在预测结构时嵌入不可感知的几何水印。
  2. 核心洞察： - 功能保持的关键在于「水印嵌入与功能优化联合进行」，而非事后扰动。 - 结构水印利用生成模型的隐空间，通过微调使水印成为模型输出的固有属性。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]： - 任何「功能保持的隐式标记」问题，都可考虑「在生成过程中嵌入」而非「事后修改」。 - 微调预训练生成模型（如 AlphaFold3）可同时实现功能保持与标记嵌入，这一思路可迁移至其他生物分子生成任务。
