UFold-X: an enhanced Dual & Dynamic U-Mamba model for long-range RNA secondary structure prediction.

Fu, Laiyi; Li, Jiachun; Wang, Ruiqi; Sun, Hequan; Wu, Danyang
Nucleic acids research · 2026-09-13 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文提出UFold-X，一种结合卷积与Mamba的深度学习模型用于长链RNA二级结构预测，虽对象为RNA而非蛋白质，但其长程依赖建模和结构预测方法可迁移至蛋白质结构计算，具有借鉴价值。

摘要

RNA secondary structure is essential for understanding the functions of non-coding RNAs, ribosomal RNAs, and viral genomes. However, accurate prediction of long RNA structures remains challenging due to complex long-range interactions and the limited availability of long-RNA training data. We present UFold-X, a dual-branch deep learning framework that combines a convolutional encoder for local structure modeling with a Mamba-based Visual State Space Module for capturing long-range dependencies. A dynamic gating mechanism adaptively integrates the two branches according to sequence length. UFold-X was evaluated on multiple benchmark datasets containing RNAs up to 5000 nucleotides. To rigorously assess generalization, we introduced a cross-clan benchmark for long RNAs. Under this stringent setting, UFold-X achieved performance comparable to state-of-the-art classical approaches while achieving the best performance among deep learning-based methods. Additional cross-family and within-family evaluations further demonstrated robust transferability and competitive predictive performance. UFold-X also maintained excellent computational efficiency, requiring only 0.08 s per sequence on average. To assess biological consistency, we developed a SHAPE-based reactivity prediction variant (UFold-X-R) and an integrated metric, the Hybrid Reactivity-Pairing Score (HRPS). UFold-X-R showed strong agreement with experimental icSHAPE data and achieved the highest HRPS among all evaluated methods. A user-friendly web server is available at https://ufold-x.ai4bread.com.

摘要翻译

RNA二级结构对于理解非编码RNA、核糖体RNA和病毒基因组的功能至关重要。然而，由于复杂的长程相互作用以及长RNA训练数据的有限性，准确预测长RNA结构仍然具有挑战性。我们提出了UFold-X，一种双分支深度学习框架，结合了用于局部结构建模的卷积编码器和基于Mamba的视觉状态空间模块，以捕捉长程依赖性。动态门控机制根据序列长度自适应地整合两个分支。UFold-X在包含长达5000个核苷酸的RNA的多个基准数据集上进行了评估。为了严格评估泛化能力，我们引入了针对长RNA的跨家族基准。在这种严格设置下，UFold-X达到了与最先进的经典方法相当的性能，同时在基于深度学习的方法中取得了最佳表现。额外的跨家族和家族内评估进一步证明了其稳健的可迁移性和竞争性的预测性能。UFold-X还保持了出色的计算效率，平均每条序列仅需0.08秒。为了评估生物学一致性，我们开发了基于SHAPE的反应性预测变体（UFold-X-R）以及一个综合指标，即混合反应性配对评分（HRPS）。UFold-X-R与实验icSHAPE数据表现出强烈的一致性，并在所有评估方法中取得了最高的HRPS。用户友好的网络服务器可在https://ufold-x.ai4bread.com访问。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：如何准确预测长 RNA 序列（>600 nt，甚至长达 5000 nt）的二级结构，特别是长程碱基配对相互作用？
  • 为什么重要：长 RNA（如 lncRNA、rRNA、病毒基因组）在基因调控中发挥关键作用，但其结构预测因序列长、结构复杂而极具挑战。现有方法在长序列上性能显著下降。
  • 现有方法不足：
  • 传统热力学方法（RNAfold、Mfold）时间复杂度为 O(L³)，无法扩展到长序列。
  • 现有深度学习方法（如 UFold、e2efold）主要基于卷积网络，感受野有限，难以捕捉长程依赖。
  • Transformer 类方法虽能建模长程依赖，但注意力机制 O(N²) 复杂度在长序列上计算代价过高。
  • 精确的「Can ... ?」研究问题：Can a dual-branch architecture combining convolutional local feature extraction with Mamba-based global state space modeling, adaptively fused by a length-aware gating mechanism, achieve accurate and efficient RNA secondary structure prediction for sequences up to 5000 nucleotides?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：构建一个双分支 U-Net 编码器-解码器架构。一个分支使用全卷积层提取局部结构特征；另一个分支使用基于 Mamba 的 Visual State Space Module (VSSM) 捕捉长程依赖。两个分支通过动态门控机制融合，门控权重由输入序列长度决定。输入为 17 通道的伪图像张量（16 通道 Kronecker 编码 + 1 通道配对势能），输出为碱基配对概率矩阵。

  2. 核心洞察： - RNA 结构形成具有层次性：局部茎环结构（短程配对）与全局拓扑（长程配对）需要不同类型的特征提取器。 - 卷积擅长局部模式识别，但感受野有限；Mamba/SSM 能以线性复杂度建模长程依赖，且无需 O(N²) 的注意力矩阵。 - 不同长度的 RNA 对局部与全局特征的依赖程度不同：短序列以局部结构为主，长序列需要更多全局上下文。因此，门控权重应随序列长度动态调整。

  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]： - 对于生物大分子结构预测，混合架构（局部+全局）优于单一架构，关键在于如何自适应地平衡两者。 - 状态空间模型（SSM/Mamba）是 Transformer 的高效替代方案，尤其适用于长序列建模任务，可迁移至蛋白质长序列结构预测。 - 任务相关的先验知识（如 RNA 配对规则、长度依赖）可以通过输入编码和门控机制注入网络，而非仅依赖网络自动学习。

07 方法总览

  • 输入：RNA 序列，转换为 17 通道伪图像张量 I ∈ R^(17×L×L)。前 16 通道为 Kronecker 积编码（每种碱基对组合一个通道），第 17 通道为基于三种规范配对规则（A-U, G-C, G-U）的连续配对势能分数。
  • 输出：碱基配对概率矩阵 Y ∈ [0,1]^(L×L)，经后处理（对称化、约束优化）得到最终二级结构。
  • 模块： 1. 双分支编码器：卷积分支（局部特征）+ VSSM 分支（全局特征） 2. 动态门控融合：根据序列长度计算门控权重 α, β，融合两分支特征 3. 解码器：U-Net 对称结构，上采样恢复分辨率 4. 后处理：基于物理约束（无假结、无尖锐环）的优化
  • 训练：二元交叉熵损失，SGD 优化，ReLU 激活，输出层 sigmoid
  • 工具：未提供具体深度学习框架信息
  • 流程：序列 → Kronecker 编码 → 双分支编码 → 门控融合 → 解码 → 概率矩阵 → 后处理 → 结构

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围：本文聚焦于 RNA 二级结构预测，输入仅为序列信息，不涉及三级结构、配体结合或动力学。
  • oracle/真值输入：训练标签来自 RNAstralign 等数据库中的已知结构（实验或计算获得）；UFold-X-R 使用 RMDB 实验反应性数据训练。
  • 端到端状态：UFold-X 是端到端训练的深度网络，但后处理步骤（对称化、约束优化）非可微，属于混合 pipeline。
  • 算力成本：未提供具体训练算力信息；推理效率高（0.08 s/序列）。
  • 历史数据依赖：依赖 RNAstralign 等数据库的结构注释质量；跨家族/跨分支评估显示性能下降，说明对训练数据分布有依赖。
  • 模型依赖：性能依赖于 VSSM 和门控机制的设计；UFold-X-R 的伪反应性预测依赖短序列训练数据。
  • 最难情形：跨分支（cross-clan）评估中，所有方法 F1 均低于 0.4，说明远程同源结构预测仍是开放挑战。
  • 群体/领域边界：结论适用于 600-5000 nt 的 RNA 序列；对 <600 nt 短序列，UFold-X 与 UFold 性能相当（Supplementary Fig. S8）。
  • 有边界的复述：UFold-X 在长 RNA（600-5000 nt）二级结构预测中，在标准基准和跨数据集/跨家族评估下，性能优于现有深度学习方法，与经典热力学方法在严格跨分支条件下相当，同时保持高效推理。

14 学到什么

Agent 提炼的知识候选：

  1. 双分支架构设计：将局部特征提取器（卷积）与全局依赖建模器（SSM/Mamba）并行组合，通过门控机制自适应融合。此思路可直接迁移至蛋白质结构预测：卷积分支捕捉局部二级结构（α-螺旋、β-折叠），SSM 分支建模长程残基接触。

  2. 长度感知的动态门控：根据输入序列长度动态调整局部/全局特征权重，而非使用固定融合策略。对于蛋白质设计，可类比为根据蛋白质长度调整局部序列模式与全局折叠约束的平衡。

  3. Kronecker 积输入编码：将序列对关系显式编码为多通道张量，而非依赖网络隐式学习。此方法可迁移至蛋白质残基对编码，或分子对接中的原子对特征。

  4. SSM 替代注意力机制：Mamba 以 O(N) 复杂度实现长程依赖建模，相比 Transformer 的 O(N²) 在长序列上具有显著效率优势。对于蛋白质序列（通常 100-1000+ 残基），SSM 可能是比注意力更高效的骨干网络选择。

  5. 分层评估协议：本文设计了从 within-family → cross-dataset → cross-family → cross-clan 的递进式评估体系，严格程度递增。这种评估设计可迁移至蛋白质结构预测，用于区分「记忆训练分布」与「真正泛化」。

  6. 生物学一致性指标（HRPS）：将结构预测精度与实验反应性数据结合，构建综合评估指标。对于蛋白质结构预测，可类比为结合实验突变数据（如深度突变扫描）与结构精度，评估预测结构的生物学有效性。

  7. 数据增强策略：通过随机突变（5%-10% 碱基）生成增强数据，在保持结构相关性的同时增加序列多样性。此策略可迁移至蛋白质结构预测中的数据增强，或蛋白质设计中的序列多样化。

  8. 跨分支评估的启示：在严格同源控制下，深度学习方法与经典物理方法性能相当，说明纯数据驱动方法在远程同源场景仍有瓶颈。对于蛋白质结构预测，这提示将物理约束（能量函数、分子动力学）与深度学习结合可能是突破方向。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The manuscript addresses a relevant and timely problem in RNA bioinformatics: accurate secondary structure prediction for long sequences. The proposed architecture is a reasonable extension of prior work (UFold) with a clear motivation for incorporating long-range modeling via Mamba-based modules. The evaluation strategy, particularly the introduction of cross-clan benchmarks, is thoughtful and addresses a known limitation in the field. However, the manuscript as presented has several significant issues. The most critical is the lack of a clear, quantitative comparison of the proposed dynamic gating mechanism against simpler alternatives (e.g., fixed weighting, learned scalar weighting) and against the individual branches alone. The claim of "state-of-the-art among deep learning methods" is made but the comparison set is limited and the significance of improvements is not always established. The HRPS metric is introduced but its validation and the interpretation of its components require more rigorous justification. The biological consistency analysis relies on a model (UFold-X-R) trained on short sequences and applied to long ones, and the validity of this transfer is not thoroughly discussed. The computational efficiency claim is based on a single benchmark and lacks comparison with other efficient methods. Overall, the work has potential but the current evidence does not fully establish the superiority or novelty of the proposed approach over existing methods, especially given the missing figures and tables.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Computational biologists and bioinformaticians working on RNA structure prediction, particularly those interested in deep learning approaches for long non-coding RNAs, viral genomes, and ribosomal RNAs. The work may also be of interest to researchers developing Mamba-based architectures for biological sequence analysis, as it demonstrates an application of this architecture to a structured prediction problem. The web server and open-source code could be useful for practitioners needing a fast and accessible prediction tool.
  • Major strengths 1. The problem addressed is important and the focus on long-range interactions is well-motivated. 2. The dual-branch architecture with dynamic gating is a sensible and potentially effective design. 3. The introduction of cross-clan evaluation is a significant methodological contribution, addressing a critical gap in the field's evaluation practices. 4. The development of a reactivity prediction variant and the HRPS metric attempts to bridge structural prediction with biochemical interpretability. 5. The reported computational efficiency is a practical advantage.
  • Major Concerns
  • R1-M1
    • Severity Major
    • Blocking Yes
    • Axis Technical soundness
    • Claim pointer The claim that the dynamic gating mechanism (Approach I) is superior to Approach II and to static fusion methods is not sufficiently supported.
    • Evidence pointer Section "Dynamic gating mechanism for range-aware feature fusion", Results section (referenced but not fully provided), Supplementary Fig. S3.
    • Concern The manuscript states that "Empirical results show that Approach I generally yields better performance on test sets" but provides no quantitative data in the text. The comparison is relegated to a supplementary figure. More importantly, there is no ablation study comparing the full model against (a) the convolutional branch alone, (b) the VSSM branch alone, and (c) a version with fixed (non-dynamic) fusion weights.     • Why it matters The dynamic gating mechanism is a core novelty of the proposed architecture. If its contribution is not rigorously demonstrated, the added complexity of the model cannot be justified. The claim of a "dynamic" and "adaptive" mechanism requires evidence that it outperforms simpler, static alternatives.
    • Resolution test Provide a full ablation study in the main text or a clearly referenced supplementary table, showing F1 scores (and other metrics) for: (1) UFold-X with Approach I gating, (2) UFold-X with Approach II gating, (3) UFold-X with fixed weights (e.g., α=β=0.5), (4) convolutional branch only, (5) VSSM branch only.   • R1-M3
    • Severity Major
    • Blocking Yes
    • Axis Scientific importance
    • Claim pointer The claim that the HRPS metric provides a "biologically meaningful" and "quantitatively balanced" evaluation that is superior to using F1 score alone.

The metric's behavior is not analyzed: how sensitive is the ranking of methods to the choice of weights? Is the Spearman correlation term measuring something that is not already captured by F1? The validation of UFold-X-R is limited to a short-sequence dataset, and its application to long sequences is based on the assumption that the learned reactivity patterns generalize, which is not directly tested. The claim that HRPS is "biologically meaningful" requires a demonstration that it correlates with some independent biological readout or that it provides information beyond what F1 provides.
    • Why it matters Introducing a new metric is a significant contribution only if it is well-justified, validated, and shown to provide unique and useful information. If the metric is arbitrary, its use in the paper's main conclusions weakens the overall argument.
    • Resolution test Provide a sensitivity analysis of HRPS to its weights. Show that the ranking of methods is robust to reasonable variations in the weights. Validate UFold-X-R on a held-out long RNA dataset with experimental reactivity data, if available. Justify the choice of weights based on a principled argument or empirical optimization.
