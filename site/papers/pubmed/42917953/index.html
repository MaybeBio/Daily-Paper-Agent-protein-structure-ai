Mapping the N/OFQ(1-13)-NH2 Address Domain to Identify G Protein-Biased NOP Receptor Agonists.

Meneguzzo, Giulio; Morrone, Erika; Scarpa, Daniele; Rizzo, Alessandra; Marzola, Erika; Ciancetta, Antonella; Illuminati, Davide; Albanese, Valentina; De Ventura, Tiziano; Preti, Delia; Calo, Girolamo; Malfacini, Davide; Pacifico, Salvatore; Guerrini, Remo
Journal of medicinal chemistry · 2026-10-23 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文通过合成NOP受体肽类似物并结合分子动力学分析其G蛋白偏向性，虽涉及蛋白受体结构计算，但核心是肽药物设计而非蛋白质结构预测/构象生成等本课题核心问题。

摘要

The nociceptin/orphanin FQ peptide receptor (NOP) couples to G proteins and beta-arrestins, but how the peptide address domain influences transducer preference remains unclear. We synthesized ninety N/OFQ(1-13)-NH2 analogues with acylated lysine-based or related non-natural residues at positions 5-13 and evaluated NOP-G protein and NOP-beta-arrestin 2 interactions in BRET assays. Substitutions at positions 5-7 most affected signaling. Position 7 was the main hotspot for G protein bias, while bulky benzoyl- and cyclohexanoyl-lysine substitutions at position 5 also produced significant bias. Position 8 was poorly tolerant to modification, whereas positions 9-13 generally retained agonist activity and balanced signaling. No reproducible beta-arrestin 2-biased profile was identified. Molecular dynamics and allosteric network analyses of [Lys(CyCO)5]N/OFQ(1-13)-NH2 suggested that engagement of a hydrophobic pocket at the TM3-TM5/ECL2 interface may contribute to its profile. These findings provide a position-dependent map for designing NOP peptide agonists with altered transducer preference.

摘要翻译

痛敏肽/orphanin FQ肽受体（NOP）与G蛋白和β-arrestin偶联，但肽的地址域如何影响转导子偏好仍不清楚。我们合成了九十种在5-13位含有酰化赖氨酸基或相关非天然残基的N/OFQ(1-13)-NH2类似物，并在BRET实验中评估了NOP-G蛋白和NOP-β-arrestin 2的相互作用。5-7位的取代对信号传导影响最大。第7位是G蛋白偏向的主要热点，而第5位的大体积苯甲酰基和环己酰基赖氨酸取代也产生了显著偏向。第8位对修饰耐受性差，而9-13位通常保留激动剂活性和平衡信号传导。未发现可重复的β-arrestin 2偏向特征。对[Lys(CyCO)5]N/OFQ(1-13)-NH2的分子动力学和变构网络分析表明，TM3-TM5/ECL2界面疏水口袋的参与可能有助于其特征。这些发现为设计具有改变转导子偏好的NOP肽激动剂提供了位置依赖性图谱。
