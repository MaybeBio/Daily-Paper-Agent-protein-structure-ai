8

Linear antibody epitope prediction using AlphaFold2.

DeRoo, Jacob; Terry, James S; Zhao, Ning; Stasevich, Timothy J; Snow, Christopher; Geiss, Brian J
eLife · 2026-09-18 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文开发了基于AlphaFold2的PAbFold流程，通过预测抗体-线性肽复合物结构来预测线性抗体表位，属于蛋白质结构预测在抗体表位识别上的应用，方法创新且经实验验证。

摘要

Defining the binding epitopes of antibodies is essential for understanding how they bind to their antigens and perform their molecular functions. However, while determining linear epitopes of monoclonal antibodies can be accomplished utilizing well-established empirical procedures, these approaches are generally labor- and time-intensive, and costly. To take advantage of the recent advances in protein structure prediction algorithms available to the scientific community, we developed a calculation pipeline based on the localColabFold implementation of AlphaFold2 that can predict linear antibody epitopes by predicting the structure of the complex between antibody heavy and light chains and target peptide sequences derived from antigens. We found that this AlphaFold2 pipeline, which we call PAbFold, was able to accurately flag known epitope sequences for several well-known antibody targets (HA/Myc) when the target sequence was broken into small overlapping linear peptides and antibody complementarity determining regions were grafted onto several different antibody framework regions in the single-chain antibody fragment format. To determine if this pipeline was able to identify the epitope of a novel antibody with no structural information publicly available, we determined the epitope of a novel anti-SARS-CoV-2 nucleocapsid-targeted antibody using our method and then experimentally validated our computational results using peptide competition ELISA assays. These results indicate that the AlphaFold2-based PAbFold pipeline we developed is capable of accurately identifying linear antibody epitopes in a short time using just antibody and target protein sequences. This emergent capability of the method is sensitive to methodological details such as peptide length, AlphaFold2 neural network versions, and multiple-sequence alignment databases. PAbFold is available at https://github.com/jbderoo/PAbFold.

摘要翻译

定义抗体的结合表位对于理解其如何与抗原结合并执行分子功能至关重要。然而，虽然利用成熟的经验性程序可以完成单克隆抗体线性表位的测定，但这些方法通常劳动密集、耗时且成本高昂。为了利用科学界可用的蛋白质结构预测算法的最新进展，我们开发了一个基于AlphaFold2的localColabFold实现的计算流程，通过预测抗体重链和轻链与源自抗原的靶标肽段序列之间的复合物结构，来预测线性抗体表位。我们发现，这个我们称为PAbFold的AlphaFold2流程，在将靶标序列分解为小的重叠线性肽段，并将抗体互补决定区移植到单链抗体片段格式中的几种不同抗体框架区上时，能够准确标记多个已知抗体靶标（HA/Myc）的已知表位序列。为了确定该流程是否能识别出无公开结构信息的新型抗体的表位，我们使用我们的方法确定了一种新型抗SARS-CoV-2核衣壳靶向抗体的表位，并通过肽竞争ELISA实验验证了我们的计算结果。这些结果表明，我们开发的基于AlphaFold2的PAbFold流程能够仅使用抗体和靶蛋白序列，在短时间内准确识别线性抗体表位。该方法的这种新兴能力对方法学细节敏感，如肽段长度、AlphaFold2神经网络版本和多序列比对数据库。PAbFold可在https://github.com/jbderoo/PAbFold获取。

02 一句话总结

本文开发了 PAbFold 流程，将抗体 scFv 序列与抗原来源的 10 氨基酸重叠肽段进行 AlphaFold2 共折叠结构预测，利用肽段残基的 pLDDT 分数作为表位打分依据，成功预测了 Myc、HA 及一个 novel 抗 SARS-CoV-2 核衣壳蛋白抗体（mBG17）的线性表位，并通过竞争 ELISA 实验验证了 mBG17 的预测结果。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题: 能否仅从抗体和抗原的氨基酸序列出发，利用 AlphaFold2 预测抗体的线性表位？
  • 为什么重要: 确定抗体表位是理解抗体功能、疫苗设计和免疫诊断的关键，但传统实验方法（如肽扫描、竞争 ELISA）耗时且昂贵。随着单细胞 B 细胞测序的普及，抗体序列获取变得容易，但表位鉴定成为瓶颈。
  • 现有方法不足: 现有表位预测工具（BepiPred、ElliPro、ABCpred）主要基于抗原序列或结构特征，不针对特定抗体，无法提供抗体-表位相互作用的分子细节。结构对接方法（HADDOCK、ZDOCK）需要已知肽段，无法从头预测表位位置。
  • 精确研究问题: Can AlphaFold2 accurately predict linear antibody epitopes from antibody and antigen sequences alone, using pLDDT confidence scores as a readout?

04 背景与发展脉络

（注：此脉络基于本文引言及参考文献，经外部核验部分有限，标注如下） - 阶段一：实验表位定位（传统方法）：通过肽扫描、缺失突变、竞争 ELISA 等实验手段确定表位。优点：结果可靠；缺点：耗时、昂贵、通量低。 - 阶段二：序列基表位预测（BepiPred、ABCpred 等）：基于抗原序列的理化性质或机器学习分类器预测 B 细胞表位。优点：快速；缺点：不针对特定抗体，无法提供结构信息。 - 阶段三：结构基对接方法（HADDOCK、ZDOCK）：将已知肽段对接至抗体结构。优点：提供分子相互作用细节；缺点：需要预先知道候选肽段，无法从头发现表位。 - 阶段四：深度学习结构预测（AlphaFold2, 2021）：AlphaFold2 及其 multimer 版本可预测蛋白质复合物结构。已有研究（Ko & Lee 2021; Tsaban et al. 2022）探索了 AF2 在抗体-抗原复合物预测中的应用，但结果参差不齐。本文首次系统性地将 AF2 的 pLDDT 分数用于线性表位的从头预测。 - 本文位置: 处于阶段四，利用 AF2 的 emergent capability（pLDDT 作为结合置信度指标），将表位预测转化为结构预测问题，并系统评估了方法学细节（肽段长度、AF2 版本、MSA 数据库）对预测性能的影响。

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法: 将抗体 scFv 序列与抗原序列切成的 10 aa 重叠肽段进行共折叠预测，用肽段残基的 pLDDT 分数作为表位打分依据。
  2. 核心洞察: AlphaFold2 的 pLDDT 分数不仅反映结构预测的置信度，还能作为蛋白质-肽段结合亲和力的代理指标。短肽缺乏独立折叠结构，其被 AF2 建模到抗体 CDR 环区的置信度直接反映了抗体-肽段的兼容性。将表位预测问题转化为「哪个肽段能被 AF2 高置信度地停靠在抗体结合位点」的问题。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]: - AF2 的 emergent capability（pLDDT 作为结合指标）可以迁移到其他蛋白质-肽段相互作用预测问题。 - 将大蛋白切成小肽段进行「局部对接」可以规避 AF2 处理大复合物的困难。 - 方法学细节（AF2 版本、MSA 数据库）对 emergent capability 影响巨大，需要仔细控制和记录。

07 方法总览

  • 输入: 抗体 scFv 氨基酸序列 + 抗原全长氨基酸序列
  • 输出: 抗原序列上每个残基的 pLDDT 分数（最大值或共识值），用于排名候选表位肽段
  • 模块: 1. 肽段生成: 将抗原序列切成 10 aa 肽段，步长 1 aa（滑动窗口） 2. 共折叠预测: 每个 scFv:肽段对通过 ColabFold 本地版运行 AlphaFold2 multimer 预测 3. 分数提取: 提取肽段残基的 pLDDT 分数 4. 表位排名: 两种方法——Simple Max（取每个残基在所有窗口中的最大 pLDDT）和 Consensus（先对 5 个 AF2 模型取平均，再计算肽段平均 pLDDT）
  • 工具: LocalColabFold (v1.5.2), Python 3.7, NumPy, Matplotlib
  • 硬件: AMD EPYC 7443 服务器 + 2× NVIDIA RTX A5000 GPU
  • 关键假设: 短肽段无独立结构，AF2 将其建模到抗体结合位点的置信度反映真实结合亲和力
  • 流程: 输入序列 → 生成肽段 → 批量 AF2 预测 → 提取 pLDDT → 排名 → 输出候选表位

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围: 仅针对线性表位（连续氨基酸序列），不适用于构象表位（不连续氨基酸在空间上靠近）。
  • oracle/真值输入: 无，仅需抗体和抗原序列。
  • 端到端状态: 是端到端流程，从序列直接到表位预测，无需实验数据。
  • 算力成本: 每个 scFv:肽段对约 200 秒（GPU），整个扫描约 12 小时（数百对）。
  • 历史数据依赖: 依赖 AF2 训练数据，但 novel 抗体 mBG17 的成功预测表明泛化能力。
  • 模型依赖: 高度依赖 AlphaFold2 特定版本（mm2）和 MSA 数据库（UniRef30 2202, PDB70 220313），版本更新会导致性能下降。
  • 最难情形: 构象表位、长肽段、新 AF2 版本。
  • 群体/领域边界: 适用于线性表位抗体，不适用于构象表位；对肽段长度敏感（仅 10 aa 有效）。
  • 不确定性: 预测排名非绝对第一，需结合实验验证；方法对 MSA 内容敏感，难以完全复现。

有边界的复述: PAbFold 能在特定条件下（10 aa 肽段、AF2 mm2 + 特定 MSA 数据库）从序列准确预测线性抗体表位，并在 novel 抗体上通过实验验证，但该方法对 AF2 版本和 MSA 数据库高度敏感，且不适用于构象表位。

14 学到什么

Agent 提炼的知识候选: 1. pLDDT 作为结合代理指标: AF2 的 pLDDT 分数可迁移用于蛋白质-肽段相互作用预测，不仅限于抗体表位，也可用于其他蛋白-肽段结合位点预测。 2. 肽段化策略规避复合物预测困难: 将大蛋白切成小肽段进行「局部对接」可规避 AF2 处理大复合物的困难，此策略可迁移到其他大蛋白复合物预测问题。 3. MSA 缓存的重要性: 在批量预测中缓存 MSA 可保证结果一致性和可复现性，对任何基于 AF2 的高通量筛选都重要。 4. 框架移植（loop grafting）可提高预测性能: 将 CDR 移植到高表达框架上不仅改善蛋白性质，还提高表位预测的信噪比，可迁移到其他抗体工程任务。 5. 版本敏感性评估: 任何基于 AF2 的工具都应系统评估模型版本和数据库更新对性能的影响，并记录具体版本信息。 6. 实验验证闭环: 计算方法预测后必须用实验（如竞争 ELISA、丙氨酸扫描）验证，计算-实验闭环是可信度关键。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The manuscript describes a practical and potentially useful application of AlphaFold2 for linear epitope prediction. The core idea is timely and the experimental validation with a novel antibody is a strength. However, the evidence presented is largely based on a small number of test cases, and the sensitivity of the method to peptide length, MSA details, and AlphaFold2 versions raises concerns about robustness and generalizability. The manuscript would benefit from a more systematic evaluation and clearer reporting of performance metrics.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers in antibody engineering, immunology, and structural biology, particularly those interested in rapid epitope mapping. The method could also be of interest to computational biologists developing protein-protein interaction prediction tools.
  • Major strengths 1. The study addresses a real bottleneck in antibody characterization. 2. The use of a novel antibody (mBG17) with experimental validation is a strong point. 3. The method is presented as an accessible, open-source pipeline. 4. The authors honestly discuss the sensitivity of the method to various parameters.
  • Major Concerns
  • Concern ID R1-M1
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Technical soundness
  • Claim pointer The claim that PAbFold "was able to accurately flag known epitope sequences" for Myc and HA, and "very accurately predict" the mBG17 epitope.
  • Evidence pointer Results sections "Testing of scFv:peptide structure prediction method using the Myc Epitope", "Testing of the PAbFold method using the HA epitope", "Determination and experimental validation of a novel linear antibody epitope"; Figures 2 and 3.
  • Concern The evidence for "accurate" prediction is based on a very small number of examples (two known antibodies, one novel antibody). The performance is not quantified in a systematic way (e.g., no clear definition of a "hit" or a "successful prediction" across a larger benchmark set). The ranking of the correct epitope is described, but the statistical significance of this ranking over random or alternative methods is not established. The sensitivity to parameters (peptide length, MSA, model version) suggests the method may not be robust, and the conditions for optimal performance are not clearly defined.
  • Why it matters Without a more rigorous and quantitative evaluation, it is difficult to assess the general utility and reliability of the method. The current evidence is anecdotal and may not support the broad claims of predictive capability.
  • Resolution test The authors should provide a more comprehensive benchmark, ideally including a larger set of antibodies with known linear epitopes, and report performance metrics such as sensitivity, specificity, and rank of the true epitope. They should also systematically explore the parameter space (peptide length, MSA generation, model version) to define the conditions under which the method works reliably.
  • Concern ID R1-M2
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Technical soundness
  • Claim pointer The claim that the method's success is due to the "lack of strong competing structure within the short peptide" (Introduction) and that it can predict "the structural details for how that linear epitope binds to the antibody" (Results, mBG17 section).
  • Evidence pointer Introduction, Results section "Fine characterization of the mBG17 epitope and comparison to the predicted AlphaFold2 model", Figure 4.
  • Concern The mechanistic hypothesis for why the method works is not tested. The comparison between the predicted model and the alanine scanning data is qualitative. The authors state that the model is "remarkably consistent" with the experimental data, but the criteria for this consistency are not defined. It is unclear if the predicted interactions are specific and accurate, or if the agreement is coincidental. The claim that the method provides "structural details" is not supported by a quantitative comparison to the experimental data.
  • Why it matters The manuscript implies that the method can provide structural insights into antibody-peptide interactions. This is a strong claim that requires more rigorous validation. A qualitative comparison to alanine scanning data is insufficient to establish the accuracy of the predicted binding mode.
  • Resolution test The authors should provide a more detailed and quantitative comparison between the predicted model and the experimental alanine scanning data. For example, they could calculate a correlation between the predicted effect of each mutation on binding and the experimentally measured effect. They should also discuss the limitations of the predicted model.

  • Axis Reproducibility

  • Concern The manuscript describes significant difficulties in reproducing results due to changes in third-party tools (ColabFold, MMseqs2). While the authors provide a recommendation to cache MSAs, the issue of reproducibility is a major concern for a method that is presented as a practical tool. The specific conditions under which the method works are not fully specified, and it is unclear if a user following the provided instructions would obtain the same results.
  • Why it matters Reproducibility is a cornerstone of scientific method. If the method's performance is highly dependent on the exact versions of software and databases, its utility is limited. The authors need to provide a more robust solution or a clear guide for users to achieve reproducible results.
  • Resolution test The authors should provide a containerized version of the pipeline (e.g., Docker) with pinned versions of all dependencies. They should also provide a clear description of the exact software and database versions used to generate the results in the paper.
