Keep AI-augmented science contestable.

Trattner, Christoph
Science (New York, N.Y.) · 2026-10-01 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文讨论AI在科学中的可争议性，以AlphaFold为例但聚焦于科学伦理与治理，未涉及蛋白质结构计算建模方法本身。

摘要

Artificial intelligence (AI) is moving upstream in science. Systems such as AlphaFold have already transformed protein-structure prediction, while newer agentic systems can search literatures, generate hypotheses, write and execute code, interpret results, and automate much of the path from idea to paper. Recent debates have asked how such systems may change scientific progress, surprise, and human understanding. Yet they also change something more fundamental: who-or what-participates in deciding which questions are asked, which evidence is surfaced, and which alternatives survive. As machine intelligence becomes part of science's epistemic infrastructure, steps must be taken to ensure that researchers who did not build, own, or operate the system can still contest how ideas and evidence became a claim.

摘要翻译

人工智能（AI）正朝着科学的上游迈进。诸如AlphaFold之类的系统已经变革了蛋白质结构预测，而更新的智能体系统能够检索文献、生成假设、编写并执行代码、解释结果，并自动化从想法到论文的许多路径。近期的讨论关注这些系统可能如何改变科学进展、意外发现和人类理解。然而，它们还改变了更为根本的东西：谁——或什么——参与决定哪些问题被提出、哪些证据被呈现、哪些替代方案得以存续。随着机器智能成为科学认识论基础设施的一部分，必须采取措施，确保未构建、拥有或操作该系统的研究人员仍能对想法和证据如何转化为主张提出质疑。
