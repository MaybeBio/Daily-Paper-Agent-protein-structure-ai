Non-muscle proteins alleviate 118 degrees C sterilization-induced gel quality deterioration by non-covalently modulating myofibrillar protein conformation and aggregation.

Wu, Wenmin; Chen, Fan; Gao, Pei; Yu, Dawei; Jiang, Qixing; Liao, E; Xia, Wenshui
Food chemistry · 2026-09-21 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文研究非肌肉蛋白对高温灭菌鱼糜凝胶品质的改善，虽涉及分子对接和动力学模拟，但核心对象是食品凝胶体系而非蛋白质结构计算，方法仅作为辅助验证，与蛋白质结构计算课题关联较弱。

摘要

The improvement effects of non-muscle proteins (soy protein isolate (SPI), egg white protein (EWP) and whey protein concentrate (WPC)) on the quality deterioration of high-temperature (118 degrees C) sterilized surimi gels were systematically compared. Supplementation with 4% SPI, 4% EWP, or 8% WPC effectively alleviated the heat-induced quality deterioration of gels, among which 4% SPI exhibiting the best improvement effect, increasing gel strength by 49.15% and water holding capacity by 7.6% compared with the control group. Multi-spectral analysis showed SPI improved myofibrillar protein (MP) solubility, promoted protein unfolding, and strengthened molecular cross-linking. Rheology indicated increased storage modulus (G'), while microstructural images confirmed denser gel network. SPI also inhibited excessive aggregation of MP at 118 degrees C. Molecular docking and dynamics simulations confirmed stable MP-SPI complexes through non-covalent interactions. These findings demonstrate innovative application of SPI in sterilized surimi gels, providing new insights for the development of ready-to-eat surimi products.

摘要翻译

系统比较了非肌肉蛋白（大豆分离蛋白（SPI）、蛋清蛋白（EWP）和浓缩乳清蛋白（WPC））对高温（118 摄氏度）灭菌鱼糜凝胶品质劣化的改善效果。添加4% SPI、4% EWP或8% WPC能有效缓解热诱导的凝胶品质劣化，其中4% SPI表现出最佳改善效果，与对照组相比，凝胶强度提高49.15%，持水性提高7.6%。多光谱分析显示，SPI改善了肌原纤维蛋白（MP）的溶解性，促进了蛋白质展开，并增强了分子交联。流变学结果表明储能模量（G'）增加，而微观结构图像证实了更致密的凝胶网络。SPI还抑制了MP在118 摄氏度下的过度聚集。分子对接和动力学模拟证实，MP-SPI复合物通过非共价相互作用稳定形成。这些发现展示了SPI在灭菌鱼糜凝胶中的创新应用，为即食鱼糜产品的开发提供了新见解。
