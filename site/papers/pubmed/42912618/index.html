8

Crowder Environments Control Interfacial Dynamics and Topological Stability in a Knotted Protein.

Thakuria, Sanjib; Paul, Sandip
Biomacromolecules · 2026-10-23 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文通过分子动力学模拟研究拥挤剂PEG和葡聚糖对打结蛋白AOTCase界面动力学和拓扑稳定性的影响，核心对象为蛋白质结构，问题涉及构象动力学与稳定性，方法采用MD模拟，与课题高度相关。

摘要

Knotted proteins are unique biomolecules where backbone entanglement imposes intrinsic constraints on structure and dynamics. The interaction of such a topologically restrained protein with molecular crowders remains unexplored. Using molecular dynamics simulations, we investigate the effects of polyethylene glycol (PEG) and dextran on the structure, interactions, and topological dynamics of the trefoil-knotted enzyme AOTCase. Persistent, heterogeneous accumulation of dextran near the protein surface is observed, whereas PEG exhibits a transient interaction pattern. The distinct interfacial behaviors propagate into the protein's dynamical organization: dextran induces fragmentation of communication pathways, while PEG largely preserves network coherence. At elevated temperatures, dextran retards global denaturation compared to PEG or pure water, with the knotted core exhibiting reduced fluctuations. Our findings reveal that the effects of molecular crowding on knotted proteins arise from both excluded-volume effects and the chemical nature of crowder-protein interactions. This interplay can influence both local topological dynamics and global stability.

摘要翻译

打结蛋白质是一类独特的生物分子，其骨架缠结对结构和动力学施加了内在约束。这种拓扑受限蛋白质与分子拥挤剂之间的相互作用尚未被探索。通过分子动力学模拟，我们研究了聚乙二醇（PEG）和葡聚糖对三叶结酶AOTCase的结构、相互作用和拓扑动力学的影响。观察到葡聚糖在蛋白质表面附近持续、异质的积累，而PEG则表现出瞬时的相互作用模式。不同的界面行为传播到蛋白质的动力学组织中：葡聚糖诱导通信通路碎片化，而PEG在很大程度上保持网络连贯性。在升高温度下，与PEG或纯水相比，葡聚糖延缓了全局变性，且打结核心表现出减少的波动。我们的发现揭示，分子拥挤对打结蛋白质的影响源于排除体积效应和拥挤剂-蛋白质相互作用的化学性质。这种相互作用可以影响局部拓扑动力学和全局稳定性。

02 一句话总结

本文通过 MD 模拟发现，PEG 与 dextran 两种拥挤剂对三叶结蛋白 AOTCase 的界面相互作用模式不同（dextran 持续异质富集、PEG 瞬时互作），进而导致通信路径碎片化（dextran）或网络连贯性保持（PEG），并在高温下对去折叠速率和打结核心波动产生差异化调控。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：分子拥挤剂（PEG vs dextran）如何影响打结蛋白 AOTCase 的界面动力学、内部通信网络和拓扑稳定性？
  • 为什么重要：打结蛋白的骨架缠结本身对结构-动力学关系施加约束；细胞内拥挤环境是生理常态，但拥挤剂对拓扑受限蛋白的影响此前未被探索。理解这一交互对蛋白质折叠、稳定性及功能调控的生理相关性至关重要。
  • 现有方法为何不足：以往拥挤研究多针对未打结蛋白，且常将拥挤效应简化为纯排除体积（excluded volume）效应，忽略拥挤剂化学性质与蛋白表面的特异性相互作用。对拓扑受限体系，这种简化可能失效。
  • 精确研究问题：Can the chemical identity of a crowder (PEG vs dextran) differentially modulate the interfacial dynamics, internal communication network, and thermal stability of a knotted protein (AOTCase) beyond mere excluded-volume effects?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：对三叶结蛋白 AOTCase 进行 MD 模拟，分别置于 PEG 和 dextran 拥挤环境中，分析界面接触模式、内部通信网络（如动态相关性或路径分析）及高温去折叠行为。
  2. 核心洞察：拥挤剂对打结蛋白的影响不能简化为排除体积效应——dextran 的持续异质富集与 PEG 的瞬时互作模式导致截然不同的内部动力学组织（通信路径碎片化 vs 网络连贯），并进一步调控热稳定性与打结核心的波动。化学互作（而非仅体积排斥）是决定性因素之一。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：在蛋白质-环境相互作用研究中，需将环境分子视为具有化学身份的参与者，而非惰性背景。对 MD 模拟设计而言，选择拥挤剂时需考虑其与蛋白表面的特异性互作模式，这可能对构象采样和自由能计算产生定性影响。

  • Overall assessment The abstract addresses a potentially interesting and underexplored question, namely how molecular crowders affect the dynamics and stability of knotted proteins. The central claim that crowder chemical identity, not just excluded volume, modulates topological stability is plausible and could be of interest to the biophysics and protein folding communities. However, the abstract provides no quantitative evidence, no methodological detail, and no statistical context. The conclusions are stated as definitive but cannot be evaluated from the supplied material. The work may have merit, but the case is not established from the abstract alone.

The comparison of two chemically distinct crowders, PEG and dextran, is a sensible design for separating generic excluded-volume effects from specific chemical interactions. The focus on both interfacial dynamics and global stability provides a potentially integrated view.
