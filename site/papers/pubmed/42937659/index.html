Recognition Loop Dynamics Differentiates m6A-mRNA Reader Domains of YTHDF Proteins.

Li, Yaozong; Bedi, Rajiv Kumar; Ojeda-May, Pedro; Caflisch, Amedeo
Journal of chemical theory and computation · 2026-10-27 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文结合晶体学、ITC和分子动力学模拟，研究YTHDF蛋白识别m6A-mRNA的构象动态与二聚化差异，属于蛋白质结构相关的计算模拟研究，但方法创新性一般。

摘要

The three human YTH-domain family proteins (YTHDF1, YTHDF2, and YTHDF3) are highly homologous and recognize the N6-methyladenosine (m6A) modification of mRNA with essentially identical binding modes and affinities. This homogeneity challenges the well-established view that different YTHDF proteins play distinct cellular functions (the specificity view), with an emerging argument that these proteins may instead play redundant roles. To study this apparent discrepancy, we investigated the mechanism of binding of m6A-containing RNA using mutagenesis, X-ray crystallography, isothermal titration calorimetry (ITC), and molecular dynamics simulations. Specifically, we compared different properties of YTHDF proteins, including their binding capacities to m6A-RNA, conformational changes, and homodimerization propensity. Our results show that the YTHDF proteins share an identical binding mode of m6A-RNA, which requires rare protonation of m6A for binding. Additionally, the three proteins share a similar open-closed switch for their recognition loop regulating the m6A binding. On the other hand, ITC data and crystal structures provide consistent evidence that the YTHDF proteins differ significantly in the flexibility of the recognition loop and its conformational preferences. Only YTHDF1 can homodimerize, which prevents binding to m6A-RNA. Furthermore, we integrate the available biophysical and biochemical evidence to define both the shared and distinct features of YTHDF1-3 in m6A-RNA recognition and propose a hybrid model that reconciles the redundancy-specificity debate. Finally, we discuss the limitations of the work and propose experiments for validating the presented model.

摘要翻译

三个人类YTH结构域家族蛋白（YTHDF1、YTHDF2和YTHDF3）高度同源，并以基本相同的结合模式和亲和力识别mRNA的N6-甲基腺苷（m6A）修饰。这种同质性挑战了不同YTHDF蛋白发挥不同细胞功能的既定观点（特异性观点），而一种新兴论点则认为这些蛋白可能发挥冗余作用。为研究这一明显矛盾，我们通过诱变、X射线晶体学、等温滴定量热法（ITC）和分子动力学模拟，研究了含m6A RNA的结合机制。具体而言，我们比较了YTHDF蛋白的不同特性，包括其与m6A-RNA的结合能力、构象变化及同源二聚化倾向。我们的结果表明，YTHDF蛋白共享m6A-RNA的相同结合模式，该模式需要m6A的罕见质子化才能结合。此外，这三种蛋白对其调控m6A结合的识别环具有相似的开闭开关。另一方面，ITC数据和晶体结构提供了一致证据，表明YTHDF蛋白在识别环的灵活性及其构象偏好方面存在显著差异。仅YTHDF1能发生同源二聚化，这阻止了其与m6A-RNA的结合。进一步地，我们整合现有的生物物理和生化证据，以定义YTHDF1-3在m6A-RNA识别中的共享和独特特征，并提出一个混合模型，以调和冗余-特异性之争。最后，我们讨论了本工作的局限性，并提出了验证所提出模型的实验方案。

02 一句话总结

通过结合诱变、X 射线晶体学、等温滴定量热法 (ITC) 和分子动力学 (MD) 模拟，比较 YTHDF1/2/3 对 m6A-RNA 的结合模式、构象变化和同源二聚化倾向，发现三者共享相同的 m6A 结合模式和 recognition loop 开-关切换机制，但在 recognition loop 的柔性、构象偏好及同源二聚化能力上存在显著差异，据此提出一个调和冗余性与特异性的混合模型。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：高度同源的 YTHDF1/2/3 蛋白以几乎相同的结合模式和亲和力识别 m6A-mRNA，为何却被认为具有不同的细胞功能？这种功能差异的结构和动力学基础是什么？
  • 为什么重要：m6A 是 mRNA 上最丰富的修饰之一，YTHDF 蛋白是其主要阅读器。理解它们如何区分功能，对 RNA 表观转录组调控机制至关重要。
  • 现有方法为何不足：仅靠静态晶体结构或结合亲和力数据无法解释同源蛋白的功能分化，因为它们在结合模式和亲和力上几乎无差异。
  • 精确研究问题：Can the functional specificity of YTHDF1/2/3 be explained by differences in recognition loop dynamics and homodimerization propensity rather than by differences in m6A binding mode or affinity?

04 背景与发展脉络

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：结合诱变、X 射线晶体学、ITC 和 MD 模拟，系统比较 YTHDF1/2/3 的 m6A-RNA 结合模式、构象变化和同源二聚化倾向。
  2. 核心洞察：YTHDF 蛋白的功能分化不源于 m6A 结合模式或亲和力的差异，而源于 recognition loop 的动力学差异（柔性和构象偏好）以及同源二聚化能力的有无。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：对于高度同源的蛋白质家族，功能分化可能由局部构象动力学（而非静态结构或结合亲和力）决定；MD 模拟是捕捉这类差异的关键工具。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The manuscript addresses a genuinely interesting and timely question, namely how highly homologous m6A reader proteins can combine near-identical binding chemistry with reported functional differentiation. The proposed hybrid model is conceptually attractive and the multi-method strategy is appropriate in principle. However, from the supplied material the central mechanistic claims are not established. The abstract asserts a protonation-dependent binding mode, a shared open-closed switch, protein-specific recognition-loop flexibility, and a homodimerization-dependent block of RNA binding, but no quantitative, structural, or statistical support is visible. The work is potentially publishable if the technical concerns below are resolved with explicit data.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers in RNA epigenetics and m6A biology, structural and computational biophysicists studying protein-RNA recognition, and groups working on YTH domain function and reader-protein specificity. The results would also interest those modeling protonation-coupled or conformational-selection binding mechanisms, and scientists trying to reconcile redundancy versus specificity in paralogous protein families.
  • Major strengths The question is well motivated and targets a real tension in the literature. The combination of experimental biophysics with molecular dynamics is a sensible way to connect structure, energetics, and dynamics. The explicit proposal of a hybrid model, together with a stated discussion of limitations and validation experiments, is a constructive framing. Comparing binding capacity, conformational change, and homodimerization within one study is a coherent design.
