Structure and spectroscopy of propane and propene in the solid state from neutron powder diffraction and inelastic neutron scattering.

Fortes, A Dominic; Howard, Christopher M; Parker, Stewart F
IUCrJ · 2026-09-11 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文利用中子衍射和力场预测方法研究丙烷/丙烯的固态结构与光谱，对象为小分子晶体而非蛋白质，不涉及蛋白质结构计算问题，与本课题无关。

摘要

Using high-resolution neutron powder diffraction data, we have refined the previously determined monoclinic structure of the stable beta-phase of perdeuterated propane at 10 K and characterized the anisotropic thermal expansion from 10 to 80 K. Using the same methods, we have determined the structure of the stable orthorhombic beta-phase of perdeuterated propene for the first time and similarly characterized its thermal expansion between 10 and 85 K. Combining these results with published thermodynamic data, we estimate the magnitude of the volume isotope effect (VIE) in propane and propene to provide parameterizations of the density of each substance in the natural protiated form from limiting low temperature up to their respective melting points. We have been able to establish, using force-field-based structure prediction methods, that the metastable alpha-phase of propene forms a body-centred tetragonal crystal with a nearly identical packing arrangement to the stable beta-phase. This represents an original use of structure prediction methods to obtain results retrospectively from a 28-year-old powder diffraction study with data of low quality, rather than the more usual application of pro-active materials discovery, suggesting promising computational - and perhaps AI or machine learning driven - routes to extracting value from archived historical data. Inelastic neutron scattering spectroscopy, combined with density functional theory calculations, have yielded vibrational mode assignments for beta-propane, and for both alpha- and beta-propene, in protiated and deuterated isotopologues. This work provides astronomers with the data required to interpret accurately the infrared spectra of interstellar ice grains and cloud condensates in gas giant planet atmospheres, and provides planetary scientists with information on the temperature-dependent densities necessary to model accurately the behaviour of hydrocarbon sediment grains in various extraterrestrial fluid environments, such as Titan's ethane rivers and seas.

摘要翻译

利用高分辨率中子粉末衍射数据，我们精修了全氘代丙烷在10 K下稳定β相先前确定的单斜结构，并表征了其在10至80 K范围内的各向异性热膨胀。采用相同方法，我们首次确定了全氘代丙烯稳定β相的正交结构，并类似地表征了其在10至85 K之间的热膨胀。将这些结果与已发表的热力学数据相结合，我们估算了丙烷和丙烯中体积同位素效应 (VIE) 的大小，从而提供了每种物质在天然质子化形式下从极限低温至各自熔点的密度参数化。利用基于力场的结构预测方法，我们得以确定丙烯的亚稳态α相形成体心四方晶体，其堆积排列与稳定β相几乎相同。这是结构预测方法的一种创新应用，用于从28年前的低质量粉末衍射研究中回顾性地获取结果，而非更常见的主动材料发现应用，这表明了从存档历史数据中提取价值的计算驱动（或许还有AI或机器学习驱动）途径具有前景。非弹性中子散射光谱学结合密度泛函理论计算，得出了β-丙烷以及α-和β-丙烯在质子化和氘化同位素体中的振动模式归属。这项工作为天文学家提供了准确解释星际冰粒和气态巨行星大气中云凝聚物红外光谱所需的数据，并为行星科学家提供了温度依赖性密度的信息，这些信息对于准确模拟各种地外流体环境（如土卫六的乙烷河流和海洋）中烃类沉积颗粒的行为至关重要。
