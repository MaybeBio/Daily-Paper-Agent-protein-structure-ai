Actions of Pharmaceutical Lactones and Lactams on the Enzymes with Lactonase Activity: In Silico Prediction and In Vitro Study.

Efremenko, Elena; Domnin, Maksim; Sarapina, Anastasia; Zagidulina, Aigul
International journal of molecular sciences · 2026-10-27 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文通过分子对接和体外实验研究药物内酯/内酰胺对具有内酯酶活性的酶（如金属β-内酰胺酶）的抑制，属于环境毒理与酶学应用，虽涉及对接但非蛋白质结构计算建模的核心问题，与蛋白质结构预测/设计/构象采样等课题关系较远。

摘要

Pharmaceutical pollutants that contain lactone or lactam rings in their structure are frequently detected in wastewater. Enzymes with lactonase activity, synthesized by the microorganisms in aerobic activated sludge, are expected to be able to participate in the hydrolysis of these pharmaceutical pollutants, but the actual activity of many lactonases towards such compounds remains poorly characterized. In this work, a systematic molecular docking study of 12 enzymes and 39 pharmaceutical pollutants (beta-lactam antibiotics, macrolides, coumarins, statins) was undertaken. The binding affinity, active-site occupancy, total enzyme surface occupancy and geometric characteristics of the molecules (the distance from the catalytic nucleophile to the carbonyl carbon of the lactone ring, and the attack angle formed between the nucleophilic atom of the enzyme and the C-N/C-O bond of the substrate) were evaluated in silico for a set of hydrolases displaying lactonase activity (AidC, AaL, hexahistidine-tagged organophosphate hydrolase (His(6)-OPH), etc.) and metallo-beta-lactamases (MbetaLs) (NDM-1, MIM-1, SAM-1). Lactamases exhibited favorable intermolecular interaction geometry for the majority of beta-lactams. No enzyme was found that satisfied the geometric criteria corresponding to the biodegradation of macrolides. With coumarins, only Paraoxonase PON2 provided an optimal geometry of biocatalysis; however, this enzyme is absent from activated sludge but may be of interest for in vivo applications. For several statins widely used in practice, combinations with enzymes having virtually ideal geometry for biocatalysis were identified: AidC/rosuvastatin lactone, AaL/lovastatin, His(6)-OPH/mevastatin, and PON2/simvastatin. For the first time, several pharmaceutical lactones were identified in silico and confirmed in vitro as inhibitors of MbetaL and His(6)-OPH. Competitive inhibition of NDM-1 by lactone-containing signaling molecules of Gram-negative bacteria present in activated sludge was revealed for the first time both in silico and in vitro. The obtained results' may be taken into account when implementing monitoring of biological stages at water treatment stations and improve the efficiency of water purification. The revealed characteristics of MbetaL inhibition by macrolides may be taken into account as one of possible explanations for the effective application of beta-lactams in combinations with macrolides known from multiple paratactical investigations.

摘要翻译

结构中含有内酯环或内酰胺环的药物污染物在废水中频繁检出。好氧活性污泥中的微生物合成的具有内酯酶活性的酶，预期能够参与这些药物污染物的水解，但许多内酯酶对此类化合物的实际活性仍鲜有表征。本研究对12种酶和39种药物污染物（β-内酰胺类抗生素、大环内酯类、香豆素类、他汀类）进行了系统的分子对接研究。通过计算机模拟，评估了一组具有内酯酶活性的水解酶（AidC、AaL、六组氨酸标签有机磷酸酯水解酶（His(6)-OPH）等）和金属-β-内酰胺酶（MbetaLs）（NDM-1、MIM-1、SAM-1）的结合亲和力、活性位点占有率、总酶表面占有率及分子的几何特征（从催化亲核试剂到内酯环羰基碳的距离，以及酶亲核原子与底物C-N/C-O键之间形成的攻击角）。内酰胺酶对大多数β-内酰胺类化合物表现出有利的分子间相互作用几何构型。未发现任何酶满足大环内酯类生物降解对应的几何标准。对于香豆素类，仅对氧磷酶PON2提供了最佳的生物催化几何构型；然而，该酶在活性污泥中不存在，但可能对体内应用具有意义。对于实践中广泛使用的几种他汀类药物，识别出具有几乎理想生物催化几何构型的酶组合：AidC/瑞舒伐他汀内酯、AaL/洛伐他汀、His(6)-OPH/美伐他汀和PON2/辛伐他汀。首次通过计算机模拟识别出几种药物内酯，并在体外证实其为MbetaL和His(6)-OPH的抑制剂。首次在计算机模拟和体外实验中揭示，活性污泥中革兰氏阴性菌产生的含内酯信号分子对NDM-1的竞争性抑制。所得结果可在水处理站生物阶段监测实施时予以考虑，并提高水净化效率。所揭示的大环内酯类对MbetaL抑制的特征，可作为多项实践研究中已知的β-内酰胺类与大环内酯类联合应用有效性的可能解释之一。
