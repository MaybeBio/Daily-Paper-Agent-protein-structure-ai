Bioinformatics-driven discovery of bioactive natural peptides from cyanobacterial genomes.

Tabarzad, Maryam; Heidari, Mohadeseh; Norouzi, Bita
Bioresource technology · 2026-10-13 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该综述聚焦蓝藻基因组中天然活性肽的生物信息学挖掘，虽涉及肽类结构预测和基因组挖掘工具，但核心是天然产物发现而非蛋白质结构计算建模，与课题关联较弱。

摘要

Cyanobacteria, as photosynthetic microorganisms, are emerging as a significant source of bioactive peptides with tremendous therapeutic potential against cancer, infections, and inflammatory diseases. Recent studies have highlighted that cyanobacteria produce a wide variety of non-ribosomal peptides (NRPs), polyketides (PKs), hybrids of NRPs and PKs, as well as ribosomally synthesized and post-translationally modified peptides (RiPPs). They are produced through modular biosynthetic pathways that involve NRPS, PKS, and hybrid systems, along with extensive enzymatic modifications, resulting in large families of related compounds. The current review highlights advanced bioinformatics techniques that improve our capacity to discover and characterize natural biomaterials from cyanobacterial genomes, focusing on both ribosomally and non-ribosomally synthesized peptides. Advanced genome mining tools, such as antiSMASH and PRISM, alongside specialized databases like the Cyanobacterial Metabolomics Database (CyanoMetDB), are revolutionizing our understanding of biosynthetic gene clusters and biochemical scaffold prediction. Furthermore, integrating genomics with transcriptomics, proteomics, and metabolomics is crucial for linking orphan gene clusters to their corresponding products and prioritizing underexplored pathways for validation. While significant advancements have been made, challenges remain in accurate structure prediction and functional annotation. Nevertheless, the application of bioinformatics and artificial intelligence is transforming cyanobacterial natural product discovery, unveiling a largely untapped reservoir of peptide diversity with strong implications for drug discovery and biotechnology. Realizing this potential could result in innovative solutions to critical medical and environmental challenges.

摘要翻译

蓝藻（Cyanobacteria）作为光合微生物，正逐渐成为具有巨大治疗潜力的生物活性肽的重要来源，尤其在抗癌、抗感染和抗炎性疾病方面。近期研究强调，蓝藻产生多种非核糖体肽（NRPs）、聚酮类化合物（PKs）、NRPs与PKs的杂合体，以及核糖体合成并经翻译后修饰的肽（RiPPs）。这些化合物通过模块化生物合成途径产生，涉及NRPS、PKS和杂合系统，并伴随广泛的酶促修饰，从而形成相关化合物的大型家族。本综述重点介绍了先进的生物信息学技术，这些技术提升了我们从蓝藻基因组中发现和表征天然生物材料的能力，聚焦于核糖体和非核糖体合成的肽。先进的基因组挖掘工具，如antiSMASH和PRISM，以及专门的数据库如蓝藻代谢组学数据库（CyanoMetDB），正在革新我们对生物合成基因簇和生化支架预测的理解。此外，将基因组学与转录组学、蛋白质组学和代谢组学整合，对于将孤儿基因簇与其相应产物关联，并优先验证未充分探索的途径至关重要。尽管已取得显著进展，但在精确结构预测和功能注释方面仍存在挑战。然而，生物信息学和人工智能的应用正在转变蓝藻天然产物的发现过程，揭示了一个尚未充分开发的肽多样性库，对药物发现和生物技术具有深远意义。实现这一潜力可能为关键医疗和环境挑战提供创新解决方案。
