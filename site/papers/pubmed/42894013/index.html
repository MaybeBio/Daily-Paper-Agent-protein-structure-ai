Deep learning coupled with scalable domain-specific structural validation expands RNA virus discovery from metatranscriptomes.

Luo, Gaoyang; Zang, Zelin; Yuan, Ling; Zhou, Jingbo; Dong, Ao; Huang, Yufei; Li, Stan Z; Ju, Feng
Nature communications · 2026-10-19 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文用蛋白语言模型和结构预测从宏转录组中发现RNA病毒，虽以病毒为对象，但其结构域验证和滑动窗口预测策略可迁移至蛋白质结构计算，故给6分。

摘要

The discovery of RNA viruses from metatranscriptomic data remains challenging due to extreme sequence divergence and length heterogeneity, ranging from massive polyproteins to short assembly artifacts. We present Rider, a two-stage framework that synergizes deep representation learning with scalable domain-specific structural validation. In the first stage, Rider leverages a compact pretrained protein language model to broadly detect highly divergent viral signals. To overcome the inherent input length limitations of language models and accurately capture catalytic core domains, the second stage employs an overlapping sliding-window structure prediction strategy. These structures are then aligned against our curated database of non-redundant RdRp (RNA-dependent RNA polymerase) domains. This domain-centric design detects RdRp domains embedded within long polyproteins and truncated fragments. Across >10,000 metatranscriptomes spanning diverse ecosystems, Rider achieves sensitivity comparable to LucaProt, recalling >99% of previously cataloged RNA viruses. Rider identifies thousands of deeply divergent sequences and clades that elude existing methods. In a human inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) cohort, Rider agrees with other methods in detecting RNA viruses while extending detection to divergent lineages, providing a scalable pipeline for global RNA virome exploration.

摘要翻译

从宏转录组数据中发现RNA病毒仍然具有挑战性，原因在于极端的序列分歧和长度异质性，范围从巨大的多聚蛋白到短的组装伪影。我们提出了Rider，一个两阶段框架，将深度表示学习与可扩展的结构域特异性结构验证协同结合。在第一阶段，Rider利用一个紧凑的预训练蛋白质语言模型广泛检测高度分化的病毒信号。为克服语言模型固有的输入长度限制并准确捕获催化核心结构域，第二阶段采用重叠滑动窗口结构预测策略。这些结构随后与我们的非冗余RdRp（RNA依赖性RNA聚合酶）结构域精选数据库进行比对。这种以结构域为中心的设计能够检测嵌入长多聚蛋白和截短片段中的RdRp结构域。在跨越多样生态系统的超过10,000个宏转录组中，Rider实现了与LucaProt相当的灵敏度，召回超过99%的先前编目的RNA病毒。Rider识别出数千个深度分化的序列和进化枝，这些是现有方法无法检测到的。在人类炎症性肠病（IBD）队列中，Rider在检测RNA病毒方面与其他方法一致，同时将检测扩展到分化谱系，为全球RNA病毒组探索提供了可扩展的流程。

02 一句话总结

Rider通过「ESM2嵌入+Transformer分类器」的序列级初筛和「滑动窗口ESMFold结构预测+Foldseek结构比对」的结构级验证两阶段框架，在超过10,000个宏转录组中实现了对高度分化RNA病毒RdRp的高灵敏度、低假阳性发现，并识别出数千条现有方法遗漏的深度分化序列。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题: 如何从大规模宏转录组数据中高效、准确地发现高度分化的RNA病毒（尤其是RdRp序列），同时处理序列极端分化、长度异质性（从短片段到>4000 aa的多聚蛋白）和大量非病毒序列干扰？
  • 为什么重要: RNA病毒在生态系统和人类健康中具有重要作用，但极端序列分化使传统同源比对方法（BLAST、HMMER）和基序检测方法失效；现有深度学习方法（如LucaProt）受限于输入长度（≤4000 aa）和缺乏结构验证，无法全面覆盖真实宏转录组中的病毒多样性。
  • 现有方法不足:
  • 基于序列同源性的方法（BLAST、HMMER）对高度分化序列灵敏度低，易产生假阴性
  • 基于基序的方法（如LucaProt的RdRp基序检测）对短片段和缺失基序的序列不敏感
  • 现有深度学习方法缺乏显式结构验证，是"黑盒"预测
  • 计算成本高，难以扩展到百万级ORF的宏转录组数据
  • 精确的"Can ... ?"问题: Can a two-stage framework combining protein language model-based sequence screening with domain-specific structural validation achieve both high sensitivity and low false positive rate in discovering highly divergent RNA viruses from large-scale metatranscriptomic data?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法: 两阶段框架。第一阶段用冻结的ESM2-35M提取序列嵌入，经轻量Transformer分类器进行病毒/非病毒二分类；第二阶段对初筛阳性且与已知序列相似性<90%的候选，用滑动窗口ESMFold预测3D结构，再与RDSDB（RdRp结构数据库）进行Foldseek结构比对验证。

  2. 核心洞察: - 序列级初筛 + 结构级精验的分工：语言模型嵌入能捕获隐式进化信息，适合大规模粗筛；而结构比对能识别序列相似性极低但结构保守的真阳性，同时过滤序列相似但结构不匹配的假阳性。两者互补，兼顾灵敏度与特异性。 - 域级（domain-level）而非全长级验证：RdRp催化核心域是保守的，即使全长序列分化极大，核心域结构仍高度保守。将验证粒度从全长蛋白缩小到结构域，既降低计算成本，又提高对多聚蛋白和片段的适用性。 - 滑动窗口解决长度异质性：对>1024 aa的长序列，用500 aa步长的重叠窗口切分，每个窗口独立预测和比对，避免截断丢失关键域。

  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]: - 在"大海捞针"式的大规模筛选任务中，级联式（cascade）设计——用廉价方法粗筛、昂贵方法精验——是平衡计算成本与精度的通用策略。 - 结构域而非全长作为比对单元，可显著提高对多结构域蛋白和片段化序列的鲁棒性，这一思想可迁移到分子对接、构象采样等任务中。 - 语言模型嵌入的"隐式结构知识"可作为显式结构验证的预筛器，两者不是替代关系而是互补关系。

07 方法总览

  • 输入: 宏转录组组装后的ORF蛋白序列（长度100-2000 aa，经MMSeqs2在90%相似性去冗余）
  • 输出: 经结构验证的RNA病毒RdRp候选序列列表，附带结构比对分数（Prob-score、TM-score）
  • 模块: 1. 序列嵌入模块: 冻结的ESM2-35M，输出480维/残基嵌入，取[CLS] token（首token）作为序列级表示 2. 分类模块: 2层Transformer编码器（8头注意力，FFN隐层960维，dropout 0.3）+ 线性分类层 → 二分类概率 3. 候选筛选模块: 概率阈值分三档（Rider-st ≥0.99、Rider-nr ≥0.90、Rider-se ≥0.50）；与已知RdRp序列相似性<90%的候选进入结构验证 4. 结构预测模块: ESMFold v1，滑动窗口（窗口1000 aa，步长500 aa）处理>1024 aa序列 5. 结构比对模块: Foldseek，将预测结构与RDSDB比对，计算Prob-score（贝叶斯后验概率）和TM-score 6. 数据库模块: RDSDB（217,762个病毒蛋白结构，来源NCBI、Neri et al.、Zayed et al.、Edgar et al.）和RDSDB30（30%相似性去冗余后9,735个代表性RdRp结构域）
  • 训练: 加权交叉熵损失（处理类别不平衡），AdamW优化器（lr=1e-4），batch size=256，早停策略（patience=5），每400步评估
  • 流程: ORF序列 → ESM2嵌入 → Transformer分类 → 概率阈值筛选 → 相似性过滤 → ESMFold结构预测（滑动窗口） → Foldseek结构比对 → 输出验证结果

09 关键公式符号

1. Prob-score（Foldseek结构比对后验概率）

$$P(\text{TP} | \text{score}) = \frac{p(\text{score}|\text{TP}) \cdot p(\text{TP})}{p(\text{score}|\text{TP}) \cdot p(\text{TP}) + p(\text{score}|\text{FP}) \cdot p(\text{FP})}$$

  • $P(\text{TP} | \text{score})$: 给定比对分数下为真阳性的后验概率
  • $p(\text{score}|\text{TP})$: 真阳性比对的分数似然
  • $p(\text{score}|\text{FP})$: 假阳性比对的分数似然
  • $p(\text{TP})$, $p(\text{FP})$: 先验概率
  • 用途: 判断预测结构是否与已知RdRp结构域真正同源

2. Ave_Probscore（Top-K聚合）

$$\text{Ave_Probscore} = \frac{\sum \text{Top}_K \text{Probscore}}{K}$$

  • $K$: 默认10，取每个查询结构在RDSDB中前K个最佳比对的Prob-score均值
  • 用途: 综合多个结构比对结果，减少单次比对偶然性

3. DNA mapping rate（正交验证指标）

$$\text{DNA mapping rate} = \frac{\text{number of positions with coverage} \ge 1}{\text{total contig length}}$$

  • 用途: 验证RNA病毒候选是否具有基因组DNA支持，排除组装假象

4. RPKM（表达量归一化）

$$\text{RPKM} = \frac{10^9 \times C}{N \times L}$$

  • $C$: 映射到含Rider命中ORF的contig的reads数
  • $N$: 实验中总映射reads数
  • $L$: contig长度（bp）
  • 用途: 评估病毒转录丰度

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围: 本文结论仅限于从宏转录组ORF中识别RdRp（RNA病毒标志基因），不涉及完整病毒基因组重建、病毒-宿主关联或表型预测。
  • 真值/基准: 训练和测试依赖已知RdRp注释（NCBI、RefSeq、Neri et al.、Edgar et al.），这些注释本身可能不完整或有误；"novelty"定义基于与LucaProt超组的序列相似性阈值，是操作化定义而非生物学本质。
  • 端到端状态: Rider是"发现工具"而非"验证工具"。结构验证（ESMFold+Foldseek）提供的是计算层面的支持，未经湿实验验证；Prob-score>50的阈值是经验设定的，不同数据集可能需要调整。
  • 算力成本: 两阶段设计显著降低了计算成本，但结构验证阶段仍需对每个候选运行ESMFold，在超大规模数据集上仍是瓶颈。作者未提供端到端总计算成本的详细报告。
  • 性能边界: 在1:100和1:400极端不平衡条件下表现良好，但假阳性率仍非零（96/100,000）；对"暗物质"（<30%相似性）序列，结构验证成功率有限（2294/13,407未验证）。
  • 有边界的复述: Rider在宏转录组RdRp发现任务中，通过两阶段"序列初筛+结构验证"设计，在保持高灵敏度的同时将假阳性率控制在较低水平，并能识别出部分现有方法遗漏的深度分化序列；但其性能受限于当前结构预测工具对极端分化序列的建模能力，且所有结论均为计算预测，需实验验证。

14 学到什么

Agent 提炼的知识候选:

  1. 两阶段级联设计（序列粗筛→结构精验）：这是本文最核心的可迁移方法论。在蛋白质结构预测、分子对接等任务中，面对海量候选（如构象采样中的海量decoy、虚拟筛选中的百万级分子），可借鉴"廉价方法粗筛+昂贵方法精验"的级联策略，在保持精度的同时大幅降低计算成本。具体到本课题，可在MD模拟前用AlphaFold/ESMFold快速过滤不稳定构象，或用语言模型嵌入预筛后再做对接。

  2. 结构域级（domain-level）而非全长级验证：本文通过将验证粒度从全长蛋白缩小到RdRp核心催化域，成功处理了多聚蛋白和片段化序列。这一思想可迁移到：a) 分子对接中，聚焦结合口袋而非全蛋白；b) 构象采样中，聚焦功能域而非全长；c) 蛋白质设计中，验证核心折叠而非全长。"找关键域"比"看全长"更鲁棒、更高效。

  3. 滑动窗口策略处理长度异质性：对超出模型输入限制的长序列（>1024 aa），用重叠窗口分段处理，每个窗口独立预测和比对。这一策略可直接迁移到：a) AlphaFold/ESMFold对超长蛋白（如多聚蛋白、重复蛋白）的结构预测；b) 分子动力学模拟中的区域化处理；c) 任何基于Transformer的蛋白质模型对长序列的处理。

  4. 语言模型嵌入作为"隐式结构预筛器"：本文证明冻结的ESM2嵌入+轻量分类器即可高效区分病毒/非病毒，说明PLM嵌入蕴含丰富的结构/功能信息。这提示本课题：在需要显式结构预测（如AlphaFold）之前，可先用PLM嵌入进行快速预筛或聚类，大幅减少需要昂贵结构计算的对象数量。

  5. Prob-score作为结构验证的置信度指标：Foldseek的Prob-score（贝叶斯后验概率）为结构比对提供了概率化解释，优于单一相似性分数。这一思想可迁移到：a) 分子对接打分函数的概率化校准；b) 构象采样的置信度评估；c) AlphaFold pLDDT的校准使用。

  6. 多正交验证策略：本文使用DNA映射率作为RNA病毒候选的独立验证，结合结构比对和序列相似性，形成多证据链。这提示本课题：任何计算预测都应寻求独立于模型本身的验证信号（如实验数据、独立物理量、跨方法一致性），而非仅依赖模型内部置信度。

  7. "暗物质"分析框架：本文对未通过结构验证的序列进行系统分析（pLDDT分布、novelty分层），区分"真novel但结构可折叠"与"结构预测失败"两种情况。这种对"模型失败模式"的系统分析框架，可迁移到本课题中评估AlphaFold等工具在哪些序列空间失效。

15 与已有知识连接

  • LucaProt (Hou et al., 2024)：本文的直接前作和主要对比对象。LucaProt是首个将蛋白质语言模型（ESM2）与接触图预测结合用于RNA病毒RdRp发现的深度学习方法，但受限于输入长度（≤4000 aa）和缺乏显式3D验证。Rider在LucaProt基础上引入结构域级验证和滑动窗口策略，突破了长度限制。连接点：本课题中AlphaFold等结构预测工具同样面临输入长度限制，Rider的滑动窗口策略可借鉴。

  • ESM2 (Lin et al., 2023)：本文使用的骨干模型。ESM2是Meta训练的蛋白质语言模型，在掩码语言建模任务上预训练，隐式学习了序列-结构-功能关系。连接点：本课题中，ESM2嵌入可作为蛋白质表示学习的通用特征，用于构象聚类、功能预测、序列设计等下游任务。

  • ESMFold (Lin et al., 2023)：本文使用的结构预测工具。ESMFold是单序列快速结构预测器，无需MSA，适合大规模应用。连接点：本课题中，ESMFold可作为AlphaFold的轻量替代，用于需要高通量结构预测的场景（如虚拟筛选、构象采样）。

  • Foldseek (van Kempen et al., 2023)：本文使用的结构比对工具。Foldseek通过将结构编码为3Di字母表实现快速结构搜索，比DALI/TM-align快数个数量级。连接点：本课题中，Foldseek可用于大规模结构聚类、构象空间分析、远程同源检测。

  • RdRp系统发育 (Wolf et al., 2020; Neri et al., 2022)：本文的生物学基础。RdRp是RNA病毒最通用的标志基因，其系统发育框架（如Orthornavirae分类）为病毒发现提供了参考框架。连接点：本课题中，RdRp结构域可作为蛋白质结构预测和设计的测试体系。

  • 宏转录组病毒发现 (Edgar et al., 2022; Zayed et al., 2022)：本文的应用场景。大规模宏转录组测序产生了海量未注释ORF，是病毒发现的富矿。连接点：本课题中，宏基因组/宏转录组数据可作为蛋白质结构预测的大规模应用场景。

  • pLDDT与结构置信度 (Jumper et al., 2021)：本文使用pLDDT评估预测结构质量。AlphaFold2引入的pLDDT已成为结构预测置信度的标准指标。连接点：本课题中，pLDDT可用于过滤低质量预测、指导构象采样、评估模型泛化能力。

16 研究想法

Agent 生成的研究候选:

  1. 名称: 基于结构域级滑动窗口的AlphaFold长序列预测增强 - 来源局限/观察: AlphaFold2/3对>2000 aa的多域蛋白预测质量下降，且显存消耗巨大。Rider的滑动窗口策略（窗口1000 aa、步长500 aa）在结构域级验证中有效，但尚未系统应用于结构预测本身。 - 核心假设: 对长蛋白进行重叠窗口切分，分别预测各窗口结构，再基于域间相互作用和几何约束拼接，可获得优于全长预测的质量，同时降低显存需求。 - 初步方法: 在Rider的滑动窗口基础上，引入域间接触图预测和几何拼接算法（如基于TM-score的刚体叠加），在CASP15/16长链目标上系统评估。 - 验证方式: 与AlphaFold2/3全长预测对比TM-score、LDDT、域间相对方位误差；消融窗口大小和重叠率。 - 可能的失败模式: 域间相对方位预测不准导致拼接失败；窗口边界处二级结构断裂。

  2. 名称: PLM嵌入引导的构象采样加速 - 来源局限/观察: Rider证明冻结的ESM2嵌入+轻量分类器可高效区分蛋白类别，说明PLM嵌入蕴含丰富的结构信息。在构象采样（如MD、MCMC）中，大量采样时间浪费在低能垒区域。 - 核心假设: PLM嵌入的梯度或隐空间距离可作为构象采样的偏置势，引导采样器快速跨越能垒、探索功能相关构象。 - 初步方法: 将ESM2嵌入的隐空间距离作为collective variable，结合增强采样方法（如metadynamics、REMD），在模型蛋白（如激酶、GPCR）上测试采样效率和构象覆盖度。 - 验证方式: 与无偏MD对比构象空间探索速度、自由能面收敛时间、已知功能构象的命中率。 - 可能的失败模式: PLM嵌入对构象变化不敏感（主要编码序列信息而非构象信息）；偏置势引入人为自由能面畸变。

  3. 名称: 结构域级Prob-score校准的分子对接虚拟筛选 - 来源局限/观察: Rider使用Foldseek Prob-score（贝叶斯后验概率）作为结构验证指标，在远程同源检测中优于序列方法。分子对接的评分函数常产生大量假阳性，缺乏概率化校准。 - 核心假设: 将对接pose的结构相似性（对已知复合物结构库的Foldseek比对）转化为概率化评分，可提高虚拟筛选的富集因子。 - 初步方法: 构建蛋白-配体复合物结构库，对每个对接pose计算其与库中已知复合物的Foldseek Prob-score，作为评分函数的补充特征，训练排序模型。 - 验证方式: 在DUD-E、LIT-PCBA等标准虚拟筛选基准上对比富集因子、ROC-AUC。 - 可能的失败模式: 结构库覆盖度不足导致Prob-score不可靠；计算开销过大。

  4. 名称: 基于结构域嵌入的RNA病毒RdRp功能分类 - 来源局限/观察: Rider识别出大量深度分化RdRp，但仅用结构相似性验证，未进行功能注释。RdRp的催化域结构保守但底物特异性、复制策略可能不同。 - 核心假设: RdRp催化域的三维结构嵌入（如从ESM2或结构比对中提取）可区分不同复制策略（如正链/负链/双链RNA病毒）和宿主范围。 - 初步方法: 对Rider验证的11,113个RdRp结构域提取结构嵌入，结合已知RdRp功能注释训练分类器，预测未知序列的功能类别。 - 验证方式: 交叉验证分类准确率；对预测的新类别进行系统发育和结构基序验证。 - 可能的失败模式: RdRp结构域过于保守，无法区分功能亚型；功能注释本身不完整。

  5. 名称: 结构验证引导的蛋白质设计迭代优化 - 来源局限/观察: Rider的两阶段框架（序列筛选→结构验证）本质上是"生成-验证"循环。在蛋白质设计中，类似循环可系统化：设计序列→预测结构→结构验证→反馈优化。 - 核心假设: 将Foldseek结构比对（对目标结构库）作为设计序列的反馈信号，可引导设计算法（如扩散模型、遗传算法）生成结构更接近目标的序列。 - 初步方法: 在RFDiffusion或ProteinMPNN生成序列后，用ESMFold预测结构，再与目标结构进行Foldseek比对，将Prob-score作为奖励信号进行强化学习或进化优化。 - 验证方式: 在从头设计蛋白基准（如CASP15设计赛道）上对比成功率、结构相似性。 - 可能的失败模式: 结构预测误差在迭代中被放大；奖励信号稀疏导致优化困难。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment This manuscript presents a technically sophisticated and potentially valuable framework for RNA virus discovery. The two-stage design, combining a frozen protein language model with structural validation, is a logical and innovative response to known limitations in sequence-based viral detection. The benchmarking against multiple baselines and the application to real-world datasets are commendable. However, several methodological details and validation aspects require clarification and strengthening before the claims can be fully accepted. The reliance on structural validation as a gold standard, the handling of false positives, and the interpretation of the IBD cohort results are areas of particular concern.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Virologists, metagenomics and metatranscriptomics researchers, computational biologists developing tools for pathogen discovery, and researchers studying the human microbiome, particularly in the context of inflammatory bowel disease. The framework's scalability and sensitivity for divergent viruses would be of broad interest to the global virosphere discovery community.
  • Major strengths
  • The two-stage architecture is a well-reasoned and practical solution to the dual challenges of sequence divergence and length heterogeneity.
  • The use of a frozen, compact ESM2 model with a lightweight trainable head is computationally efficient and scalable, a significant practical advantage.
  • The construction of a domain-specific structural database (RDSDB) and the use of structural alignment for validation is a rigorous approach that goes beyond sequence-based homology.
  • The extensive benchmarking against multiple baseline methods and on diverse datasets, including short fragments and long polyproteins, is thorough.
  • The application to a clinical cohort (IBD) demonstrates real-world utility and provides a compelling case study for the framework's ability to uncover previously missed viral diversity.

However, the claim of "effectively eliminating false positives" is too strong. The analysis of the 2,294 unverified sequences (Figure 4g) shows that structural validation fails for a significant portion of highly divergent candidates, which the authors attribute to limitations of ESMFold. This means the method's final output is still dependent on the quality of the initial structure prediction. The manuscript does not provide a clear strategy for how to handle these unverified but potentially real viral sequences, other than acknowledging they are a "challenge". The paper needs to more clearly define the operational meaning of a "false positive" in this context. A sequence that fails structural validation is not necessarily a false positive; it could be a true positive that ESMFold cannot fold. The authors should provide a more nuanced discussion of this limitation and its impact on the final set of reported viruses.
  • Why it matters The core value proposition of the two-stage framework is the combination of sensitive screening with specific validation. If the validation step is known to have a high failure rate for the most divergent sequences (the very ones of most interest), then the framework's ability to "expand RNA virus discovery" is potentially limited. The paper must be transparent about this trade-off and provide guidance on how users should interpret the unverified set.

  • Resolution test The authors should provide a quantitative analysis of the unverified sequences. For example, how many of the 2,294 unverified sequences are found by other tools (LucaProt, PalmScan)? If a significant number are co-detected, this would suggest they are likely true positives that the structural validation step is incorrectly rejecting. The authors should also discuss potential improvements to the structural validation step, such as using multiple structure prediction models or relaxing the threshold for highly divergent sequences.

  • Concern ID R1-M2

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Technical soundness / Benchmarking
  • Claim pointer The claim that Rider achieves "sensitivity comparable to LucaProt, recalling >99% of previously cataloged RNA viruses" and outperforms it on short and fragmented sequences.
  • Evidence pointer Results section "Rider: a deep representation model for diverse RNA virus discovery", Figure 2, Supplementary Datasets 3, 4, 6.
  • Concern The benchmarking methodology, while extensive, has a potential critical flaw. The authors state that Rider's training data includes sequences from NCBI and other sources. It is unclear if the test sets derived from LucaProt's study (e.g., the 161,979 sequences) were rigorously excluded from Rider's training data. The authors mention "Pre-training aware test sets" for the ESM2 backbone, but it is not clear if the same rigor was applied to the fine-tuning data for the Transformer classifier. If any of the test sequences or their close homologs were seen during training, the reported recall rates would be inflated. The paper must explicitly state the exact overlap between the training data and all test sets, and ideally, perform a time-based split where the training data is strictly older than the test data.
  • Why it matters This is a fundamental issue of data leakage in machine learning. If not addressed, the reported performance metrics are not trustworthy, and the claimed superiority over other tools could be an artifact of training on the test data.

  • Resolution test The authors must provide a detailed analysis of sequence identity between the training set and each test set. They should also retrain the model on a dataset that excludes all sequences with >30% identity to the test sets and re-report the performance metrics. This would provide a true measure of generalization.

  • Concern ID R1-M3

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking No
  • Axis Scientific importance / Biological interpretation
  • Claim pointer The claim that the IBD cohort analysis reveals that "the depletion of the gut RNA virome remains a consistent descriptive pattern of Crohn's disease" and that the apparent "viral bloom" in UC is due to underestimation of fragmented viruses in healthy controls.
  • Evidence pointer Results section "Extending to human gut virome: insights from an IBD cohort", Figure 5b–d.
  • Concern The biological interpretation of the IBD data is a significant leap from the computational results. The analysis is based on a small cohort (n=77 total) and the statistical analysis is performed at the contig level, not the participant level, as the authors acknowledge. The conclusion that the "viral bloom" in UC is an artifact of methodology is a strong claim that has clinical implications. The authors need to be more cautious. The difference in detected viral abundance between UC and healthy controls when using a >100 aa threshold could be due to many factors, including differences in sequencing depth, sample processing, or true biological differences in the viral community structure that are not captured by this analysis. The paper should frame this as a hypothesis-generating observation, not a definitive re-interpretation of previous findings.
  • Why it matters Over-interpreting results from a small, exploratory computational analysis can mislead the field and have implications for how future studies are designed. The authors should temper their conclusions and clearly state the limitations of the cohort and the statistical approach.

  • Resolution test The authors should rephrase the conclusions to be more tentative. They should also consider performing a participant-level analysis, perhaps by aggregating contig-level data per participant, to see if the trends hold. A discussion of potential confounders and the need for validation in larger, independent cohorts is essential.

  • Concern ID R1-M4

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking No
  • Axis Technical soundness / Reproducibility
  • Claim pointer The claim that the framework is "scalable" and can be applied to "global RNA virome exploration."
  • Evidence pointer Results section "Computational efficiency and scalability across synthetic and real-world datasets", Methods section "Runtime benchmarking and computational efficiency profiling".
  • Concern While the speed of Stage 1 is impressive, the overall scalability of the pipeline is not fully demonstrated. The "stress test" for the structural validation module (Stage 2) used a maximum sequence length of 1200 amino acids. However, the paper also discusses the challenge of "massive polyproteins" exceeding 4000 amino acids. The sliding-window strategy is designed to handle this, but the computational cost of running ESMFold on multiple windows for thousands of long polyproteins is not benchmarked. The end-to-end runtime profiling on real-world data is useful, but it is not clear how many of the sequences in those datasets were long polyproteins. The paper should provide a more detailed analysis of the computational bottleneck of Stage 2 and its scalability to truly massive datasets.
  • Why it matters The claim of scalability is central to the paper's potential impact. If the structural validation step is prohibitively expensive for large-scale studies, the practical utility of the framework is diminished.

  • Resolution test The authors should provide a benchmark of the end-to-end pipeline on a dataset with a significant number of long polyproteins, showing the time taken for the structural validation step. They should also discuss potential strategies for optimizing this step, such as parallelization or using faster structure prediction models.
