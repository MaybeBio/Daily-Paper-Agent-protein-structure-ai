AI-Assisted Molecular Structure Prediction from Tandem Mass Spectra: Reliability and Validation.

Yu, Jun Sang; Jeong, Sung Hue; Kim, Yejin; Lee, Dong Keun; Yoo, Hye Hyun
International journal of molecular sciences · 2026-11-14 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文综述AI辅助串联质谱预测小分子结构，聚焦代谢物等非蛋白对象，不涉及蛋白质结构计算建模，仅边缘相关。

摘要

Artificial intelligence (AI) has broadened the interpretation of tandem mass spectra (MS/MS) beyond experimental library matching, enabling molecular fingerprint prediction, candidate ranking, and generation of previously unlisted structures. Yet these outputs do not all support the same claim about chemical identity. This review briefly summarizes database-restricted retrieval, database-free generation, and scaffold-guided hybrid methods, and then asks what additional evidence is needed to turn a plausible structure into a defensible annotation. We examine failure modes involving incomplete candidate spaces, erroneous precursor assignments, structurally related isomers, leakage between training and test data, and misinterpreted confidence scores. The review contributes two practical elements: a candidate-discrimination workflow, in which alternative structures are retained and targeted measurements such as multienergy fragmentation, retention behavior, ion mobility, and authentic standards are selected according to the unresolved structural question; and an evidence-to-claim checklist that links each question to the most informative measurement and to the strongest supportable identification confidence level. Both synthesize established analytical principles for small-molecule electrospray LC-HRMS/MS rather than constituting a validated scoring algorithm. By making the basis of each annotation transparent, they are intended to improve the practical value of AI-assisted MS/MS in metabolomics, pharmaceutical analysis, environmental screening, and forensic toxicology.

摘要翻译

人工智能（AI）已拓宽了串联质谱（MS/MS）谱图解读的范畴，超越了实验库匹配，实现了分子指纹预测、候选排序及生成先前未列出的结构。然而，这些输出并非都支持关于化学同一性的相同主张。本综述简要总结了数据库限制检索、无数据库生成及骨架引导的混合方法，随后探讨了将合理结构转化为可辩护注释所需补充的证据。我们考察了涉及候选空间不完整、前体离子分配错误、结构相关异构体、训练与测试数据间泄漏以及置信度分数误读的失败模式。本综述贡献了两个实用要素：一是候选区分工作流程，其中保留替代结构，并根据未解决的结构问题选择靶向测量，如多能量碎裂、保留行为、离子迁移率和真实标准品；二是证据到主张的检查清单，将每个问题与最具信息量的测量及最强可支持的鉴定置信水平相关联。两者综合了针对小分子电喷雾液相色谱-高分辨质谱/质谱（LC-HRMS/MS）的既定分析原理，而非构成经过验证的评分算法。通过使每项注释的基础透明化，旨在提升AI辅助MS/MS在代谢组学、药物分析、环境筛查和法医毒理学中的实用价值。
