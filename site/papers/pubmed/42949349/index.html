Structure-Guided Engineering of Cu/Zn Superoxide Dismutase for Enhanced Catalytic Efficiency and Thermostability.

Al-Adeeb, Abdulqader; Muhammad Aqeel, Sahibzada; Al-Jaberi, Hend Sadeq; Gu, Qiuya; Al-Ansi, Waleed; Al-Maqtari, Qais Ali; Liu, Wen-Long; Yu, Xiaobin
Journal of agricultural and food chemistry · 2026-10-28 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文通过计算辅助的结构引导工程改造Cu/Zn超氧化物歧化酶，结合分子对接提升催化效率和热稳定性，虽涉及蛋白结构计算但方法贡献较浅，属于工程应用。

摘要

Cu/Zn superoxide dismutase (Cu/Zn-SOD) is a key antioxidant enzyme that catalyzes superoxide radical dismutation. Here, an integrated computational-assisted, structure-guided engineering strategy was applied to improve Cu/Zn-SOD catalytic performance. Gln22 and Glu134 were identified as promising engineering sites, and saturation mutagenesis yielded Q22I and E134 M as the most beneficial single substitutions, with specific activities 1.55- and 2.39-fold higher than wild type (WT), respectively. Combining both substitutions generated M2 (Q22I/E134M), which showed the highest specific activity of 7121.0 U mg-1, corresponding to a 3.43-fold increase over WT. The optimal temperature of Q22I and M2 increased from 40 to 50 degrees C, and M2 also exhibited enhanced thermostability. Kinetic analysis showed that M2 had a 41.18% lower Km and a 2.95-fold higher Vmax than WT. Molecular docking suggested complementary effects of Q22I and E134 M on structural stability and active-site optimization, supporting improved catalytic performance. These findings provide a basis for engineering efficient, thermostable SODs.

摘要翻译

Cu/Zn 超氧化物歧化酶 (Cu/Zn-SOD) 是一种关键的抗氧化酶，催化超氧自由基的歧化反应。本研究应用了一种集成的计算辅助、结构导向的工程策略来提高 Cu/Zn-SOD 的催化性能。Gln22 和 Glu134 被鉴定为有前景的工程位点，饱和突变产生了 Q22I 和 E134M 作为最有益的单点替换，其比活性分别比野生型 (WT) 高 1.55 倍和 2.39 倍。将两个替换组合产生了 M2 (Q22I/E134M)，其显示出最高的比活性 7121.0 U mg-1，相当于比 WT 提高了 3.43 倍。Q22I 和 M2 的最适温度从 40 摄氏度提高到 50 摄氏度，且 M2 还表现出增强的热稳定性。动力学分析表明，M2 的 Km 比 WT 低 41.18%，Vmax 比 WT 高 2.95 倍。分子对接 (molecular docking) 表明 Q22I 和 E134M 在结构稳定性和活性位点优化方面具有互补效应，支持了催化性能的提高。这些发现为工程化高效、热稳定的 SOD 提供了基础。
