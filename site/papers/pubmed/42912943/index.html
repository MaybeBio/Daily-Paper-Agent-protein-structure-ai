Binding Free Energy Landscape of Different SARS-CoV-2 Nsp1 Variants Interacting with NXF1-RRM: Insights from Molecular Dynamics Simulations and Markov State Models.

Nguyen, Hung Van; Pham, Tri Minh; Li, Mai Suan
Journal of chemical information and modeling · 2026-10-23 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文用分子动力学模拟和马尔可夫状态模型计算SARS-CoV-2 Nsp1突变体与NXF1-RRM结合的自由能景观，属于蛋白质-蛋白质结合自由能计算与构象采样研究，方法创新且与课题高度相关。

摘要

The interaction of the SARS-CoV-2 Nsp1 protein with the host NXF1-RRM domain plays an important role in host gene expression, which influences virus replication and host immune surveillance. Thus, understanding the key factors that determine their binding affinity, such as mutations, is of great interest. In this work, we investigated the effects of three Nsp1 mutants horizontal line D33K/E36K/E37K/E41K, L27D/V28D, and R124E/K125E horizontal line on its binding to the NXF1-RRM domain using structural and energetic analysis. To quantify the affinity changes, we developed a computational framework for calculating the free energy landscape of protein-protein binding based on the Markov state model (MSM). Our approach is based on fluctuations of the radius of gyration and the root mean square deviation (RMSD) from a reference state. We found that the D33K/E36K/E37K/E41K mutant exerts the strongest destabilizing effect, resulting in greater structural deviation, reduced compactness, and a significant loss of intermolecular hydrogen bonds. In contrast, the L27D/V28D and R124E/K125E mutants retain structural and dynamical properties closer to those of wild-type Nsp1 (WT), indicating that these mutations have a more limited impact on the stability of the Nsp1-NXF1-RRM complex. These results are in good agreement with the previous experimental work. We also used the molecular mechanics Poisson-Boltzmann surface area (MM-PBSA) method to estimate the binding free energy of these complexes, yielding results consistent with the MSM analysis and experimental data. However, the binding free energy values obtained from the MSM method are more realistic compared to the MM-PBSA method, which tends to overestimate binding free energy values or produce unphysically negative results. Therefore, the MSM approach provides a useful framework for characterizing mutation-induced changes in the structural stability and binding energetics of Nsp1-NXF1-RRM complexes.

摘要翻译

SARS-CoV-2 Nsp1蛋白与宿主NXF1-RRM结构域的相互作用在宿主基因表达中发挥重要作用，这影响病毒复制和宿主免疫监视。因此，理解决定其结合亲和力的关键因素（如突变）具有重要意义。在本工作中，我们利用结构和能量分析研究了三种Nsp1突变体——D33K/E36K/E37K/E41K、L27D/V28D和R124E/K125E——对其与NXF1-RRM结构域结合的影响。为量化亲和力变化，我们开发了一种基于马尔可夫状态模型（Markov state model, MSM）的计算框架，用于计算蛋白质-蛋白质结合的自由能景观。我们的方法基于回转半径的波动以及相对于参考状态的均方根偏差（root mean square deviation, RMSD）。我们发现，D33K/E36K/E37K/E41K突变体产生最强的去稳定效应，导致更大的结构偏差、降低的紧凑性以及分子间氢键的显著损失。相比之下，L27D/V28D和R124E/K125E突变体保留了更接近野生型Nsp1（WT）的结构和动力学特性，表明这些突变对Nsp1-NXF1-RRM复合物稳定性的影响较为有限。这些结果与先前的实验工作高度一致。我们还使用分子力学泊松-玻尔兹曼表面积（molecular mechanics Poisson-Boltzmann surface area, MM-PBSA）方法估算这些复合物的结合自由能，所得结果与MSM分析和实验数据一致。然而，与MM-PBSA方法相比，MSM方法获得的结合自由能值更为真实，因为MM-PBSA方法倾向于高估结合自由能值或产生非物理性的负结果。因此，MSM方法为表征突变诱导的Nsp1-NXF1-RRM复合物结构稳定性和结合能量学变化提供了一个有用的框架。

  • 具体问题: 不同 Nsp1 突变（D33K/E36K/E37K/E41K, L27D/V28D, R124E/K125E）如何影响其与 NXF1-RRM 的结合自由能和结构稳定性？
  • 为什么重要: Nsp1-NXF1-RRM 相互作用调控宿主基因表达，影响病毒复制和免疫逃逸；理解突变效应有助于揭示病毒适应机制和设计干预策略。
  • 现有方法不足: MM-PBSA 等传统方法常高估结合自由能或产生非物理的负值，缺乏对构象动态和自由能景观的显式描述。
  • 精确研究问题: "Can an MSM-based free energy landscape approach, using radius of gyration and RMSD as collective variables, provide more realistic and experimentally consistent binding affinity estimates for Nsp1-NXF1-RRM mutants than MM-PBSA?"

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法: 对 WT 和三个 Nsp1 突变体分别进行 MD 模拟，提取轨迹后以回转半径 (Rg) 和 RMSD 为反应坐标构建 MSM，计算自由能景观；同时用 MM-PBSA 估算结合自由能，并与实验数据对比。
  2. 核心洞察: 突变对结合稳定性的影响可以通过自由能景观的拓扑变化（如盆地深度、转移概率）来捕捉，而 Rg 和 RMSD 作为简单但有效的集体变量足以区分不同突变体的构象状态；MSM 提供的自由能值比 MM-PBSA 更接近实验。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]: 对于蛋白-蛋白结合突变效应评估，选择与功能相关的几何反应坐标（如 Rg, RMSD）构建 MSM，可能比依赖单一能量函数（如 MM-PBSA）更稳健，尤其当突变主要影响构象动态而非局部相互作用时。

14 学到什么

Agent 提炼的知识候选: 1. MSM 用于结合自由能景观: 将 MD 轨迹投影到几何反应坐标（Rg, RMSD）并构建 MSM，可提供比单点能量计算更丰富的构象-能量关系。可迁移到其他蛋白-蛋白/蛋白-配体体系，尤其当突变影响整体构象动态时。 2. 反应坐标选择策略: 简单几何变量（Rg, RMSD）虽粗糙，但计算成本低且可解释性强；可作为复杂反应坐标（如 TICA）的基线对比。 3. MM-PBSA 的局限警示: 在突变效应评估中，MM-PBSA 可能产生非物理结果，需结合其他方法交叉验证。 4. 实验-计算对比框架: 用实验数据作为定性基准，评估计算方法的相对排序能力，而非绝对精度。 5. 突变效应多维分析: 结合自由能、结构偏差、紧凑性、氢键数等多指标，可提供更全面的突变影响图景。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract describes a potentially useful computational framework for assessing mutation effects on protein-protein binding, and the biological question regarding Nsp1-NXF1-RRM interactions is relevant to SARS-CoV-2 host modulation. However, the abstract alone provides insufficient detail to evaluate the validity of the MSM-based free energy approach, the statistical robustness of the results, or the strength of the claimed agreement with experiment. The central methodological novelty, namely the use of radius of gyration and RMSD fluctuations as MSM collective variables for binding free energy estimation, is not adequately justified or benchmarked within the visible material. The claim that MSM yields "more realistic" values than MM-PBSA requires quantitative support that is not presented.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Computational biophysicists and structural biologists studying protein-protein interactions, particularly those developing or applying enhanced sampling and Markov state model methodologies. Researchers working on SARS-CoV-2 host-pathogen interactions and viral immune evasion mechanisms would also find the biological context relevant. The methodological comparison between MSM and MM-PBSA may interest practitioners seeking more physically grounded free energy estimates for mutational scanning.
  • Major strengths The study addresses a biologically important interaction with clear translational relevance. The use of multiple complementary approaches (structural analysis, energetic analysis, MSM, MM-PBSA) is methodologically sound in principle. The comparison with prior experimental work provides a benchmark for the computational predictions. The focus on mutation-induced changes rather than absolute binding affinities is a sensible framing that may reduce systematic error concerns.

  • Axis Methodological validity
  • Claim pointer The authors claim that their MSM-based framework, using fluctuations of radius of gyration and RMSD from a reference state, can calculate the free energy landscape of protein-protein binding and yield "more realistic" binding free energy values than MM-PBSA.
  • Evidence pointer Abstract, methods description (location not provided)
  • Concern The abstract does not explain how a one-dimensional or low-dimensional projection onto radius of gyration and RMSD captures the relevant binding free energy surface. Protein-protein binding involves complex conformational rearrangements, desolvation, and entropic contributions that are unlikely to be fully described by these two collective variables. No validation against known binding free energies, no comparison with more rigorous methods (e.g., umbrella sampling, metadynamics, or free energy perturbation), and no convergence assessment are presented.
  • Why it matters If the MSM collective variables do not adequately resolve the binding/unbinding transition states and metastable states, the resulting free energy estimates could be systematically biased. The claim of superiority over MM-PBSA rests entirely on this methodological foundation, which is not established from the abstract.
  • Resolution test Provide a detailed description of the MSM construction, including state space discretization, lag time selection, implied timescale plots, Chapman-Kolmogorov tests, and validation of the collective variables against known binding pathways. Show convergence of free energy estimates with simulation length and replicate runs. Benchmark the method on a system with experimentally known binding free energy before applying it to the Nsp1 mutants.
