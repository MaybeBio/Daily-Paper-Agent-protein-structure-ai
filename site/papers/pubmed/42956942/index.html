Stochastic and quantum perspectives on teleost immunity: Linking immunogenetics, structure, and biophysics.

Abo-Al-Ela, Haitham G
Progress in biophysics and molecular biology · 2026-10-29 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该综述探讨硬骨鱼免疫受体多样性与量子生物学假说，提出用结构预测和MD等计算手段验证，但核心对象非蛋白质结构计算问题，方法贡献较弱。

摘要

Teleost fishes possess highly diverse, lineage-specific pattern-recognition receptor (PRR) repertoires shaped by teleost-specific whole-genome duplication (3R-WGD), subsequent duplication, differential retention, functional divergence, and gene loss. Toll-like, RIG-I-like, and NOD-like receptor families show lineage-specific expansion and, in some cases, adaptive sequence evolution, particularly in regions implicated in ligand recognition. Separately, hydrogen tunneling is supported in specific enzyme reactions, while radical-pair chemistry provides a bounded mechanistic precedent rather than evidence for teleost immune function. This review examines whether teleost PRR diversification creates concrete, testable opportunities for investigating analogous biophysical mechanisms, without assuming that receptor diversity or environmental sensitivity entails non-classical effects. A five-level evidence hierarchy is applied: direct teleost evidence, evidence from other systems, indirect teleost evidence, testable hypothesis, and unsupported or contradicted claim. No direct evidence was identified for a functionally consequential quantum-biological mechanism in teleost immune recognition or signaling. However, increasing genomic, structural, transcriptomic, single-cell, and functional-genomic resolution provides a tractable framework for testing such possibilities. Sequence and structural divergence among PRR paralogs can be evaluated using structure prediction, molecular dynamics, and, where chemically justified, quantum-chemical or QM/MM methods, with uncertainty assessed across stages and experimental validation through ligand binding, signaling, and environmental-response phenotypes. The NADPH-to-flavin hydride-transfer step in an immune effector oxidase is proposed as a chemically defined candidate, not an established teleost tunneling mechanism. Quantum mechanisms therefore remain hypotheses rather than established features of teleost immunity. A falsifiable research roadmap is proposed linking receptor evolution to structure-function relationships, signaling diversity, redox processes, and candidate quantum-sensitive molecular events.

摘要翻译

硬骨鱼类拥有高度多样化的、谱系特异性的模式识别受体（pattern-recognition receptor, PRR）库，其形成受硬骨鱼特异性全基因组复制（teleost-specific whole-genome duplication, 3R-WGD）、随后的复制、差异保留、功能分化和基因丢失所塑造。Toll样、RIG-I样和NOD样受体家族表现出谱系特异性扩增，并在某些情况下出现适应性序列进化，尤其是在涉及配体识别的区域。此外，氢隧穿在特定酶反应中得到支持，而自由基对化学提供了有界的机制先例，而非硬骨鱼免疫功能证据。本综述考察硬骨鱼PRR多样化是否创造了具体且可检验的机会，以研究类似的生物物理机制，而不假设受体多样性或环境敏感性必然涉及非经典效应。采用五级证据层级：硬骨鱼直接证据、其他系统证据、硬骨鱼间接证据、可检验假设，以及无支持或矛盾的主张。未发现直接证据支持硬骨鱼免疫识别或信号传导中存在功能上重要的量子生物学机制。然而，日益增加的基因组、结构、转录组、单细胞和功能基因组分辨率提供了检验此类可能性的可行框架。PRR旁系同源物之间的序列和结构差异可通过结构预测、分子动力学（molecular dynamics）以及，在化学上合理的情况下，量子化学或QM/MM方法进行评估，并在各阶段评估不确定性，并通过配体结合、信号传导和环境响应表型进行实验验证。免疫效应氧化酶中的NADPH-to-flavin氢化物转移步骤被提议为化学上明确的候选机制，而非已确立的硬骨鱼隧穿机制。因此，量子机制仍是假设，而非硬骨鱼免疫的既定特征。提出了一条可证伪的研究路线图，将受体进化与结构-功能关系、信号多样性、氧化还原过程及候选量子敏感分子事件联系起来。
