Tran, Jenny U; Martinez, Thyandra A; Jurich, Christopher; Josling, Margaret R; Zhang, Julian M; Ayres-Galhardo, Pedro H; Meiler, Jens; Yang, Zhongyue J; MacGurn, Jason A; Brown, Breann L
Science advances · 2026-10-16 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文结合X射线晶体学和分子动力学模拟研究酵母ALAS底物结合构象变化，虽以酶结构为核心但偏重实验表征与功能调控，MD模拟为辅助，方法创新有限，与蛋白质结构计算课题部分相关。

摘要

Heme is an essential prosthetic group involved in multiple biological processes in nearly all organisms. Heme biosynthesis is initiated by the pyridoxal 5'-phosphate-dependent enzyme aminolevulinic acid synthase (ALAS). Mutations in ALAS cause multiple hematological diseases. Despite sharing approximately 70% sequence similarity, ALAS homologs exhibit distinct expression and regulatory mechanisms. However, the molecular basis for their functional divergence remains poorly understood. Here, we used x-ray crystallography and molecular dynamics simulations to elucidate the structure and dynamics of Saccharomyces cerevisiae ALAS (scALAS) upon substrate binding. We identified critical conformational changes that occur during catalysis, regulating enzyme activity and protein-protein interactions specific to eukaryotic heme synthesis. Using evolution-guided engineering, we generated scALAS variants with altered catalytic activity and cellular metabolic profiles. Our findings reveal molecular and structural mechanisms underlying heme synthesis regulation and establish a framework for developing targeted therapeutics to treat heme-deficiency disorders and inform a rational design of next-generation therapeutic tools.

摘要翻译

血红素是一种必需的辅基，参与几乎所有生物体中的多种生物学过程。血红素生物合成由依赖吡哆醛5'-磷酸的酶氨基乙酰丙酸合酶（ALAS）启动。ALAS的突变导致多种血液疾病。尽管ALAS同源物共享约70%的序列相似性，但它们表现出不同的表达和调控机制。然而，其功能分化的分子基础仍知之甚少。在此，我们使用X射线晶体学和分子动力学模拟来阐明酿酒酵母ALAS（scALAS）在底物结合时的结构和动态。我们鉴定了催化过程中发生的关键构象变化，这些变化调节酶活性和真核血红素合成特有的蛋白质-蛋白质相互作用。通过进化引导的工程改造，我们生成了具有改变催化活性和细胞代谢谱的scALAS变体。我们的发现揭示了血红素合成调控的分子和结构机制，并为开发针对血红素缺乏疾病的靶向治疗建立了框架，同时为下一代治疗工具的设计提供了理性依据。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：ALAS 同源蛋白虽共享约 70% 序列相似性，却表现出不同的表达和调控机制，其功能分化的分子基础尚不清楚。具体而言，底物结合如何诱导 scALAS 的构象变化，这些变化如何调控催化活性和蛋白质-蛋白质相互作用？
  • 为什么重要：血红素是几乎所有生物体中必需的辅基，参与多种生物学过程。ALAS 是血红素生物合成的限速酶，其突变导致多种血液疾病。理解 ALAS 的构象动力学机制对开发靶向疗法至关重要。
  • 现有方法为何不足：此前缺乏底物结合态的 ALAS 高分辨率结构，静态结构无法揭示催化启动过程中的动态构象变化；序列相似性高但功能差异大的现象无法从单一静态结构解释。
  • 精确研究问题：Can substrate binding induce specific conformational changes in scALAS that regulate catalytic activity and protein-protein interactions, and can these changes be rationally engineered to modulate enzyme function?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：X 射线晶体学解析 scALAS 底物结合态结构 + MD 模拟分析构象动力学 + 进化引导工程（evolution-guided engineering）生成功能变异体。
  2. 核心洞察：底物结合不是简单的「锁钥」或「诱导契合」，而是触发一系列特定的构象重排，这些重排不仅影响催化活性，还调控蛋白质-蛋白质相互作用，从而将酶活性与细胞代谢网络耦合。进化引导工程表明，这些构象变化位点可以被理性设计以调节功能。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：对于酶学研究，静态结构 + 单一功能实验不足以解释同源蛋白的功能分化；结合底物结合态结构、MD 模拟和进化/序列分析，可以系统性地定位功能关键残基和构象开关，这一策略可迁移到其他 PLP 依赖酶或构象变化驱动的酶系统。

Review setup

  • Input scope Abstract only
  • Assessment boundary Claims and conclusions as presented in the abstract, without access to full text, figures, tables, or supplementary materials
  • Shared manuscript claim summary The authors report x-ray crystallography and molecular dynamics simulations of Saccharomyces cerevisiae aminolevulinic acid synthase (scALAS) upon substrate binding, identifying conformational changes that regulate enzyme activity and protein-protein interactions specific to eukaryotic heme synthesis. They further report evolution-guided engineering of scALAS variants with altered catalytic activity and cellular metabolic profiles, and propose a framework for therapeutic development.
  • Visible evidence base Abstract text only; no structural coordinates, statistics, methodological details, or experimental data are available
  • Missing materials affecting confidence Full manuscript, all figures and tables, crystallographic data and refinement statistics, simulation parameters and convergence criteria, variant construction and assay details, cellular metabolic profiling methods, and any statistical analyses

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a potentially interesting structural and functional study of a medically relevant enzyme, with a clear gap in understanding the molecular basis for functional divergence among ALAS homologs. The proposed combination of structural biology, molecular dynamics, and evolution-guided engineering is logical and could yield valuable insights. However, the abstract provides insufficient detail to evaluate the technical soundness of the crystallographic and simulation work, the robustness of the functional claims, or the strength of the therapeutic framework. Several claims appear to extend beyond what the described evidence can support, particularly regarding regulatory mechanisms and therapeutic applicability.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Structural biologists studying pyridoxal 5'-phosphate-dependent enzymes, biochemists and geneticists investigating heme biosynthesis and its regulation, clinicians and translational researchers focused on hematological disorders such as sideroblastic anemia and porphyrias, and computational biologists interested in enzyme dynamics and evolution-guided protein engineering.
  • Major strengths The study addresses a clinically relevant enzyme with known disease associations, filling a gap in understanding functional divergence among ALAS homologs. The combination of experimental and computational approaches is appropriate for probing conformational dynamics. The evolution-guided engineering strategy offers a potentially novel route to modulate enzyme activity with implications for metabolic engineering and therapeutics.

Major Concerns

  • Concern ID R1-M1
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Technical soundness
  • Claim pointer The authors claim that x-ray crystallography and molecular dynamics simulations elucidate the structure and dynamics of scALAS upon substrate binding, and that critical conformational changes occur during catalysis.
  • Evidence pointer Abstract only; no structural or simulation details provided
  • Concern The abstract provides no information on the resolution of the crystal structures, the number of structures determined, the ligands or substrate analogs used, the simulation length, force field parameters, or convergence criteria. Without these details, the reliability of the structural and dynamic conclusions cannot be evaluated.
  • Why it matters Structural and simulation claims are central to the paper's contribution. If the crystallographic data are low resolution or the simulations are insufficiently sampled, the identified conformational changes may be artifacts rather than biologically relevant states.

  • Resolution test Provide crystallographic statistics including resolution, R-factors, and validation metrics, and describe simulation protocols with evidence of convergence and reproducibility.

  • Concern ID R1-M2

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Support for claims
  • Claim pointer The authors claim that the identified conformational changes regulate enzyme activity and protein-protein interactions specific to eukaryotic heme synthesis.
  • Evidence pointer Abstract only; no functional or interaction data described
  • Concern The abstract does not describe any experiments that directly test the functional consequences of the observed conformational changes, such as mutagenesis coupled with activity assays or binding studies. The claim that these changes regulate activity and protein-protein interactions is therefore unsupported by the evidence presented.
  • Why it matters The regulatory claim is a key part of the paper's significance. Without direct functional validation, the conformational changes remain descriptive and their biological relevance is speculative.

  • Resolution test Include mutagenesis or biochemical assays that link specific conformational states to changes in enzyme activity or interaction partners, with appropriate controls and statistical analysis.

  • Concern ID R1-M3

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Support for claims
  • Claim pointer The authors claim that evolution-guided engineering generated scALAS variants with altered catalytic activity and cellular metabolic profiles.
  • Evidence pointer Abstract only; no variant details or assay results provided
  • Concern The abstract states that variants were generated and characterized but provides no information on the engineering strategy, the number of variants, the magnitude or direction of activity changes, or the metabolic phenotypes observed. The claim of altered catalytic activity and metabolic profiles is therefore not verifiable.
  • Why it matters The engineered variants are presented as a key outcome with potential therapeutic relevance. Without quantitative data, the significance and reproducibility of this result cannot be assessed.

  • Resolution test Provide detailed descriptions of the engineering approach, the specific mutations introduced, and quantitative data on enzyme kinetics and metabolic profiling with appropriate statistical comparisons.

  • Concern ID R1-M4

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking No
  • Axis Scope of claims
  • Claim pointer The authors claim that their findings establish a framework for developing targeted therapeutics to treat heme-deficiency disorders and inform rational design of next-generation therapeutic tools.
  • Evidence pointer Abstract only; no therapeutic or translational data presented
  • Concern The abstract presents no evidence that the structural or functional findings have been applied to any disease model or therapeutic context. The claim of establishing a therapeutic framework is speculative and extends beyond the described scope of the study.
  • Why it matters Overstating translational implications can mislead readers and detract from the actual contributions of the work. The therapeutic claim should be framed as a potential implication rather than an established outcome.
  • Resolution test Reframe the claim as a future direction or provide preliminary data in a disease-relevant model to support the therapeutic potential.
