Protein engineering in Brazil: a biophysical perspective.

de Lima, Mariana Zuliani Theodoro; Umbehaun, Gustavo Foltran; Tomazini, Atilio Jr
Biophysical reviews · 2026-11-12 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

系统综述巴西蛋白质工程研究，涵盖结构生物学、计算建模和AI辅助设计，但以领域概览为主，缺乏具体方法创新。

摘要

Protein engineering has become an increasingly interdisciplinary field, integrating molecular biology, structural biology, biophysics, computational modeling, and artificial intelligence to modify protein structure, stability, dynamics, molecular recognition, and catalytic activity. This systematic review examines the historical development and scientific contributions of protein engineering research associated with Brazilian institutions between 1988 and June 2026. A total of 792 peer-reviewed articles were identified in PubMed through eight thematic search strategies covering protein and enzyme engineering, directed evolution, rational and computational design, recombinant protein expression, therapeutic proteins, structural biology, and artificial intelligence-assisted engineering. The reviewed literature reveals a transition from early residue-level mutagenesis and conventional biochemical assays to increasingly integrated experimental and computational approaches. Advances in X-ray crystallography, nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR), circular dichroism (CD), mass spectrometry, small-angle X-ray scattering (SAXS), calorimetry, molecular dynamics, and structural bioinformatics enabled the quantitative investigation of protein folding, stability, conformational landscapes, molecular interactions, and catalytic mechanisms. More recently, machine learning, protein language models, AlphaFold-derived structures, and generative approaches have expanded the capacity to predict mutations and design proteins, peptides, antibodies, and enzymes with predefined properties. Overall, protein engineering in Brazil has evolved into a mature and diverse field with applications in health, infectious diseases, agriculture, biotechnology, bioenergy, biomaterials, environmental remediation, and industrial biocatalysis. The findings indicate that biophysics has moved beyond a predominantly descriptive role and has become central to the design and validation of engineered proteins. Future progress will depend on the convergence of experimental biophysics, multiscale simulations, artificial intelligence, high-throughput validation, and stronger national and international research networks.

摘要翻译

蛋白质工程已日益成为一个跨学科领域，整合了分子生物学、结构生物学、生物物理学、计算建模和人工智能，以修饰蛋白质结构、稳定性、动力学、分子识别和催化活性。本系统综述考察了1988年至2026年6月期间与巴西机构相关的蛋白质工程研究的历史发展及科学贡献。通过八项主题检索策略，在PubMed中识别出共792篇同行评审文章，涵盖蛋白质和酶工程、定向进化、理性及计算设计、重组蛋白表达、治疗性蛋白质、结构生物学和人工智能辅助工程。所综述的文献揭示了一个从早期残基水平突变和常规生化分析向日益整合的实验与计算方法相结合的转变。X射线晶体学、核磁共振（NMR）、圆二色光谱（CD）、质谱、小角X射线散射（SAXS）、量热法、分子动力学和结构生物信息学的进展，使得对蛋白质折叠、稳定性、构象景观、分子相互作用和催化机制的定量研究成为可能。近期，机器学习、蛋白质语言模型、AlphaFold衍生结构及生成方法进一步扩展了预测突变和设计具有预定特性的蛋白质、肽、抗体和酶的能力。总体而言，巴西的蛋白质工程已发展成为一个成熟且多样化的领域，其应用涵盖健康、传染病、农业、生物技术、生物能源、生物材料、环境修复和工业生物催化。研究结果表明，生物物理学已超越其主要的描述性角色，成为工程化蛋白质设计和验证的核心。未来进展将依赖于实验生物物理学、多尺度模拟、人工智能、高通量验证以及更强国家和国际研究网络的融合。

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法: 对 792 篇 PubMed 文献进行系统检索与主题分类，按五个生物物理维度（稳定性、折叠、动力学、分子识别、催化效率）组织综述，并绘制 1988–2026 方法学演进时间线。
  2. 核心洞察: 生物物理在蛋白质工程中的角色发生了根本转变——从"事后表征工具"（解释已构建突变体的性质）变为"事前设计驱动力"（定义目标性质并指导序列选择）。这一转变由三个技术浪潮推动：(1) 结构生物学提供原子级静态图像；(2) MD/SAXS/HDX-MS 增加时间与系综维度；(3) AI 生成模型（AlphaFold、ProteinMPNN、ESM-IF1、PLM）实现序列空间的高效压缩与逆向设计。作者强调，最有效的工作流程是"混合式"的——计算压缩搜索空间、实验提供最终验证，二者形成迭代闭环。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]: 本文隐含一个可迁移的方法论原则：领域成熟度可由"预测-验证闭环"的紧密程度衡量。巴西蛋白质工程的演进本质上是这个闭环从"宽开（经验筛选）"到"收紧（计算优先+实验验证）"的过程。对课题研究者而言，这意味着任何单一工具（无论是 MD 还是 AlphaFold）的价值都取决于其在闭环中的位置——不是替代实验，而是压缩实验空间。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment This manuscript provides a broad narrative synthesis of protein engineering research in Brazil, organized around five biophysical properties. The historical framing and the emphasis on the growing centrality of biophysics are potentially useful contributions. However, the review's evidentiary basis is not transparently presented. The search strategy is described only at a high level, and the quantitative claims (e.g., 792 articles, 57% addressing stability) are not traceable to reproducible methods. The manuscript would benefit from a more rigorous systematic methodology, including explicit search strings, screening criteria, and a transparent data extraction framework. The narrative is readable but at times asserts trends without direct citation support in the visible text.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers in protein engineering, structural biology, and biophysics, particularly those interested in the scientific landscape of Latin America. The review may also be of interest to science policy analysts and funding agencies seeking an overview of capacity and trends in Brazilian protein science. The historical narrative and the emphasis on hybrid experimental-computational workflows could serve as a useful entry point for newcomers to the field.
  • Major strengths
  • The manuscript addresses a genuinely under-documented topic: the historical and thematic development of protein engineering in Brazil.
  • The organization around five biophysical properties (folding, stability, dynamics, molecular recognition, catalytic efficiency) provides a coherent conceptual scaffold.
  • The authors correctly emphasize the interdependence of protein properties and the need for orthogonal validation, which is a scientifically sound perspective.
  • The inclusion of recent AI-based methods (AlphaFold, ProteinMPNN, language models) reflects an up-to-date understanding of the field.
