Metal-ion coordination drives active-site remodeling for backsplicing in circularly permuted group II introns.

Zhang, Jie; Yao, Yuqi; Ma, Jinbiao
Nucleic acids research · 2026-10-29 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文通过冷冻电镜研究II型内含子剪接中的金属离子配位与活性位点重塑，对象为RNA而非蛋白质，且不涉及蛋白质结构计算建模或AI/物理模拟方法，与本课题关系较弱。

摘要

Circularly permuted (CP) group II introns are self-splicing ribozymes that undergo a sequential branching and backsplicing reaction to yield branched introns and circular exons (circRNAs). While the initial branching step is structurally characterized, the mechanistic principles that govern the subsequent large-scale active-site remodeling and stringent metal-ion specificity required for backsplicing remain poorly understood. Here, we present a series of high-resolution cryo-EM structures of the CP group II intron from Gluconacetobacter diazotrophicus PAL5 (Gdi-1), capturing the ribozyme at discrete stages along the branching -to- backsplicing pathway under distinct ionic conditions. Comparative analyses under K+/Mg2+, K+/Ca2+, and Cs+/Mg2+ conditions demonstrate that productive backsplicing relies on ion-specific coordination networks that govern 3' splice site (3'SS) docking and catalytic center formation, revealing the precise metal-ion-coordinated conformational rearrangements and dynamic splice-site exchange mechanisms. Crucially, we identify a previously unrecognized, highly selective monovalent cation-binding site, K3, that structurally couples the gamma-gamma' interaction to reassembly of the heteronuclear four-metal core, thereby promoting productive second-step active-site formation. Together, these findings establish a structural basis for metal-ion-dependent active-site remodeling during group II intron catalysis and provide mechanistic insights into metal-ion selectivity in circRNA biogenesis.

摘要翻译

环状排列（CP）II型内含子是一类自剪接核酶，通过依次进行分支反应和反剪接反应，生成分支内含子和环状外显子（circRNA）。尽管初始分支步骤在结构上已被表征，但调控后续大规模活性位点重塑及反剪接所需严格金属离子特异性的机制原理仍知之甚少。在此，我们呈现了一系列来自固氮葡糖酸醋杆菌PAL5（Gdi-1）的CP II型内含子的高分辨率冷冻电镜结构，捕捉了核酶在分支至反剪接途径中不同离子条件下离散阶段的构象。在K+/Mg2+、K+/Ca2+和Cs+/Mg2+条件下的比较分析表明，高效反剪接依赖于离子特异性配位网络，该网络调控3'剪接位点（3'SS）对接和催化中心形成，揭示了精确的金属离子配位构象重排及动态剪接位点交换机制。关键的是，我们识别出一个此前未被认识的高选择性单价阳离子结合位点K3，它在结构上将γ-γ'相互作用与异核四金属核心的重组装偶联，从而促进高效第二步活性位点的形成。综合而言，这些发现为II型内含子催化过程中金属离子依赖性活性位点重塑建立了结构基础，并为circRNA生物发生中的金属离子选择性提供了机制性见解。
