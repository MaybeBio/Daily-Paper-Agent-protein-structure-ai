A dual-function XCL1-CD40L mRNA adjuvant potentiates cancer vaccines by coordinating DC recruitment and maturation.

Wu, Chunjie; Shao, Wanxuan; Liu, Yadi; Pan, Shuyuan; Wang, Wei; Deng, Li; Zhang, Suyue; Cui, Zhimin; Wang, Yujiao; He, Peng; Yang, Yong; Zhao, Yiheng
Frontiers in immunology · 2026-10-29 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该研究开发XCL1-CD40L双功能mRNA疫苗佐剂并验证其抗肿瘤免疫效果，仅用AlphaFold 3辅助验证融合蛋白结构完整性，属于免疫治疗/实验研究，不涉及蛋白质结构计算建模的方法学贡献。

摘要

BACKGROUND: Despite the significant promise of mRNA tumor vaccines in treating solid tumors, their clinical response rate is constrained by the low effectiveness of antigen presentation resulting from the immune-suppressive tumor microenvironment (TME). Conventional type 1 dendritic cells (cDC1) are pivotal in antigen cross-presentation; yet, they frequently exhibit insufficient immune-activating communication within solid malignancies. METHODS: This investigation comprehensively examined the functional attributes of cDC1 by TCGA cohorts and single-cell transcriptomic data. Guided by these bioinformatic insights, we engineered and synthesized a dual-functional mRNA adjuvant encoding the mXCL1-mCD40L fusion protein. We assessed the biological functions and anti-tumor efficacy of this adjuvant in combination with Glypican-3 (GPC3) and HPV-associated mRNA vaccines via protein expression, structure prediction, in vivo antigen uptake and presentation assays, antigen-presenting cell activation assays, antigen-specific T cell activation tests, and therapeutic studies in tumor-bearing mouse models. RESULTS: A high cDC1 signature score was associated with improved overall survival across LIHC and SKCM TCGA cohorts. Compared to normal sample, cDC1s in HCC and melanoma retained their antigen-presentation machinery but received reduced activating ligand inputs (XCL1-XCR1 signaling in HCC and CD40-CD40L signaling in melanoma). AlphaFold 3 showed that the mXCL1-mCD40L fusion protein can maintain structural integrity without spatial steric hindrance. The fusion protein was confirmed to be secreted and to retain both XCL1-dependent chemotactic activity and CD40-dependent dendritic-cell activation. mXCL1-mCD40L mRNA-LNP activated macrophages and dendritic cells in vitro, upregulating immune-related gene expression as confirmed by qPCR. In vivo, the dual-functional adjuvant significantly enhanced the anti-tumor effect of the GPC3 vaccine, outperformed co-delivery of separate mXCL1 and mCD40L mRNA-LNP, and induced stronger specific IFN-gamma producing T cells. Meanwhile, this adjuvant also boosted the anti-tumor efficacy of HPV-associated mRNA vaccines. Mechanistically, this adjuvant increased tumor-antigen acquisition and cross-presentation by CD103(+) DCs and promoted DC maturation in draining lymph nodes, accompanied by an increased proportion of peripheral CD8(+) T cells. CONCLUSION: By enhancing the recruitment and maturation of cDC1s, the dual-function mXCL1-mCD40L mRNA adjuvant boosts the efficacy of GPC3 and HPV-associated mRNA vaccines, supporting its potential as an antigen-flexible adjuvant platform against solid tumors.

摘要翻译

背景：尽管mRNA肿瘤疫苗在治疗实体瘤方面具有重大前景，但其临床应答率受限于免疫抑制性肿瘤微环境（TME）导致的抗原呈递效率低下。传统1型树突状细胞（cDC1）在抗原交叉呈递中起关键作用；然而，它们在实体恶性肿瘤中常表现出免疫激活通讯不足。方法：本研究通过TCGA队列和单细胞转录组数据全面分析了cDC1的功能特征。在这些生物信息学发现的指导下，我们设计并合成了编码mXCL1-mCD40L融合蛋白的双功能mRNA佐剂。我们通过蛋白表达、结构预测、体内抗原摄取与呈递实验、抗原呈递细胞激活实验、抗原特异性T细胞激活实验以及荷瘤小鼠模型中的治疗研究，评估了该佐剂联合Glypican-3（GPC3）和HPV相关mRNA疫苗的生物学功能与抗肿瘤疗效。结果：高cDC1特征评分与LIHC和SKCM TCGA队列中改善的总生存期相关。与正常样本相比，HCC和黑色素瘤中的cDC1保留了其抗原呈递机制，但接收到的激活配体输入减少（HCC中XCL1-XCR1信号传导和黑色素瘤中CD40-CD40L信号传导）。AlphaFold 3显示mXCL1-mCD40L融合蛋白能够保持结构完整性且无空间位阻。该融合蛋白被证实可分泌并保留XCL1依赖性趋化活性和CD40依赖性树突状细胞激活。mXCL1-mCD40L mRNA-LNP在体外激活巨噬细胞和树突状细胞，经qPCR证实上调免疫相关基因表达。在体内，该双功能佐剂显著增强了GPC3疫苗的抗肿瘤效果，优于分别递送mXCL1和mCD40L mRNA-LNP的联合方案，并诱导了更强的特异性产生IFN-γ的T细胞。同时，该佐剂还增强了HPV相关mRNA疫苗的抗肿瘤疗效。在机制上，该佐剂增加了CD103(+) DCs对肿瘤抗原的获取和交叉呈递，并促进了引流淋巴结中DC的成熟，同时伴有外周CD8(+) T细胞比例的增加。结论：通过增强cDC1的募集和成熟，双功能mXCL1-mCD40L mRNA佐剂提升了GPC3和HPV相关mRNA疫苗的疗效，支持其作为针对实体瘤的抗原灵活佐剂平台的潜力。
