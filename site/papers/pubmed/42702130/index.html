Discovery of Emodin as a novel competitive KPC-2 inhibitor through virtual screening: Synergistic efficacy with Meropenem against Carbapenem-resistant Klebsiella pneumoniae infection.

Wang, Yan; Geng, Jiamin; Li, Bingyan; Deng, Xuming; Wang, Jiaxi; Guo, Na
Bioorganic chemistry · 2026-09-06 · 仅摘要 · 原文

通过虚拟筛选和分子动力学模拟发现天然化合物大黄素作为KPC-2蛋白竞争性抑制剂，并验证其与美罗培南的协同抗菌效果，涉及蛋白质结构对接与MD模拟，但核心是药物发现而非蛋白质结构计算方法创新。

摘要

Carbapenem-resistant Klebsiella pneumoniae produces the carbapenemase KPC-2, which rapidly confers resistance and poses a significant threat to public health worldwide. Natural compounds have broad application potential, novel structures, and are not easily recognized by existing bacterial resistance mechanisms. Therefore, through virtual screening based on the KPC-2 protein structure and in vitro antimicrobial assessment, the natural anthraquinone compound emodin was identified as a KPC-2 inhibitor (IC(50) = 24.4 muM) and a synergist of meropenem. Molecular dynamics simulation and interaction analyses indicated that emodin competitively occupied the active site of KPC-2 at Trp105 and Thr237, resulting in reduced alpha-helices and increased beta-sheets, thereby inhibiting KPC-2 activity. The combination of emodin and MEM exhibited a synergistic effect. Furthermore, emodin modulates succinate dehydrogenase (SDH) activity and its transcriptional expression, resulting in metabolic perturbation that renders bacteria susceptible to meropenem, thereby potentiating the synergistic antibacterial activity of the combination. This disrupted the metabolic homeostasis of the bacteria, thereby compromising the integrity of bacterial cells and biofilms. No resistance development was observed over 30 generations of passage. In a mouse model of pneumonia, the combination therapy significantly reduced MEM usage, decreased the pulmonary bacterial load by 1.43 log CFU/g of model group, attenuated lung inflammation, and restored normal lung histology. These findings suggest that emodin acts as a promising candidate of KPC-2 inhibitor, which is capable of reinstating carbapenem efficacy against carbapenem-resistant Gram-negative pathogens in vitro and in vivo.

摘要翻译

耐碳青霉烯肺炎克雷伯菌（Carbapenem-resistant Klebsiella pneumoniae）产生碳青霉烯酶KPC-2，该酶可快速赋予耐药性，对全球公共卫生构成重大威胁。天然化合物具有广泛的应用潜力、新颖的结构，且不易被现有细菌耐药机制识别。因此，通过基于KPC-2蛋白结构的虚拟筛选（virtual screening）及体外抗菌评估，天然蒽醌化合物大黄素（emodin）被鉴定为KPC-2抑制剂（IC(50) = 24.4 μM）及美罗培南（meropenem）的增效剂。分子动力学模拟（molecular dynamics simulation）及相互作用分析表明，大黄素竞争性占据KPC-2活性位点Trp105和Thr237，导致α-螺旋减少、β-折叠增加，从而抑制KPC-2活性。大黄素与MEM联用表现出协同效应。此外，大黄素调节琥珀酸脱氢酶（succinate dehydrogenase, SDH）活性及其转录表达，引起代谢紊乱，使细菌对美罗培南敏感，从而增强联合用药的协同抗菌活性。这破坏了细菌的代谢稳态，进而损害细菌细胞及生物被膜的完整性。在30代传代过程中未观察到耐药性产生。在肺炎小鼠模型中，联合治疗显著减少MEM用量，使模型组肺部细菌载量降低1.43 log CFU/g，减轻肺部炎症，并恢复肺组织正常组织学结构。这些发现表明，大黄素作为KPC-2抑制剂的有前景候选物，能够在体外和体内恢复碳青霉烯类药物对耐碳青霉烯革兰阴性病原菌的疗效。

02 一句话总结

通过基于 KPC-2 蛋白结构的虚拟筛选，发现天然蒽醌化合物大黄素（emodin）作为竞争性 KPC-2 抑制剂（IC₅₀ = 24.4 μM），与美罗培南联用在体外和肺炎小鼠模型中协同恢复碳青霉烯类抗菌活性，机制涉及竞争性占据活性位点（Trp105/Thr237）及调节琥珀酸脱氢酶（SDH）代谢扰动。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题: 碳青霉烯耐药肺炎克雷伯菌（CRKP）通过产 KPC-2 碳青霉烯酶水解碳青霉烯类抗生素，导致治疗失败。能否通过结构虚拟筛选发现天然化合物作为 KPC-2 抑制剂，恢复美罗培南的抗菌活性？
  • 为什么重要: KPC-2 是 CRKP 耐药的主要机制之一，现有 KPC-2 抑制剂（如 avibactam）存在耐药突变和成本问题，天然化合物具有结构多样性和低耐药倾向。
  • 现有方法不足: 传统抑制剂开发周期长、易被现有耐药机制识别；天然产物筛选缺乏系统性结构导向方法。
  • 精确研究问题（Can ... ?）: Can a natural anthraquinone compound identified by KPC-2 structure-based virtual screening act as a competitive inhibitor and synergize with meropenem against CRKP in vitro and in vivo?

04 背景与发展脉络

（标注：仅本文框架，未经外部系统核验） 1. 阶段一：KPC-2 抑制剂开发（如 avibactam、vaborbactam）——优点：有效恢复碳青霉烯活性；局限：耐药突变出现、成本高。 2. 阶段二：天然产物抗菌筛选——优点：结构新颖、不易被耐药机制识别；局限：缺乏靶向性，随机筛选效率低。 3. 阶段三：结构虚拟筛选 + MD 模拟（本文）——优点：靶向明确、可解释结合机制；局限：需实验验证，体内效果需动物模型确认。 - 本文主张的位置: 将虚拟筛选与代谢扰动机制结合，提出天然化合物 emodin 作为 KPC-2 竞争性抑制剂，并揭示其双重机制（酶抑制 + 代谢调节）。

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法: 基于 KPC-2 蛋白结构的虚拟筛选 → 体外酶活抑制测定 → MD 模拟结合模式分析 → 联合药敏实验 → 代谢酶（SDH）活性与表达检测 → 耐药传代实验 → 小鼠肺炎模型验证。
  2. 核心洞察: Emodin 不仅竞争性占据 KPC-2 活性位点（Trp105/Thr237），还通过调节 SDH 活性及转录表达引起代谢扰动，使细菌对美罗培南敏感化，从而协同增强抗菌效果。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]: 酶抑制剂的抗菌协同作用可能不仅限于靶酶本身，代谢调节可作为辅助策略；虚拟筛选结合 MD 可提供可解释的结合机制，但需实验验证代谢层面的额外效应。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a potentially interesting dual-mechanism hypothesis for a natural product as a KPC-2 inhibitor and meropenem synergist. However, the evidence as summarized is insufficient to establish the mechanistic claims or the translational significance. The competitive inhibition mechanism is inferred from molecular dynamics without supporting kinetic or structural data. The SDH-mediated metabolic perturbation claim is presented without quantitative evidence linking it to the observed synergy. The in vivo efficacy is reported with a single log reduction value and no statistical context. The abstract is not currently sufficient to support the conclusions drawn.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers in antimicrobial resistance, particularly those focused on carbapenemase inhibitors and combination therapy; medicinal chemists interested in natural product scaffolds for enzyme inhibition; and investigators studying metabolic modulation as an antibacterial strategy. The potential to restore carbapenem activity against KPC-producing pathogens is of broad clinical relevance.
  • Major strengths The study addresses a clinically urgent problem with a clear translational goal. The dual-mechanism hypothesis is intellectually interesting and, if rigorously supported, would represent a novel contribution. The inclusion of in vivo data, even if limited in the abstract, suggests an attempt to move beyond in vitro characterization. The resistance development assessment over 30 generations is a valuable addition.
