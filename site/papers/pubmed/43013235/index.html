A Thermostable GH29A alpha-L-Fucosidase from Thermotogaceae with Bifunctional Hydrolytic and Transfucosylation Activities: Computational Mechanistic Insights and Efficient Enzymatic Synthesis of 2'-Fucosyllactose.

Hamouda, Hamed I; Chen, Changzheng; El-Sayed, Mohamed H; Nassar, Hussein N; El-Gendy, Nour Sh; Hashem, Amr H; Liu, Qishun; Qu, Jialin; Li, Tang; Yin, Heng
World journal of microbiology & biotechnology · 2026-11-11 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文研究GH29A岩藻糖苷酶的结构-功能关系，通过分子动力学模拟揭示活性位点环的底物依赖性柔性如何调控水解与转糖基活性，属于蛋白质结构相关的计算模拟研究，但核心贡献偏向酶学表征而非方法创新。

摘要

How GH29 alpha-L-fucosidases balance hydrolysis against transfucosylation at the structural level remains poorly defined, limiting rational engineering of these enzymes for glycoside synthesis. We report Thermo-Fuc, a thermostable GH29A alpha-L-fucosidase from a Thermotogaceae bacterium with an unusually alkaline pH optimum (9.0), a temperature optimum of 50 ℃, and a half-life beyond 6 h. The enzyme hydrolyzes diverse fucosylated substrates, favoring branched Lewis antigens (Leᵃ, Leˣ) over linear HMO backbones such as 2'-FL and 3-FL (K(m) = 1.015 mM, kcat = 0.00232 s(-)(1) for pNP-alpha-L-fucopyranoside), and under optimized conditions converts substrate to 2'-fucosyllactose at a 72.1% (mol/mol) transfucosylation ratio with high regioselectivity, confirmed by LC-MS and (1)H-NMR, among the highest yields reported for a wild-type GH29A enzyme. Sequence and structural analyses identified a conserved Asp219-Glu261 catalytic dyad, while molecular dynamics simulations revealed substrate-dependent flexibility across five active-site loops. The most catalytically productive complex had the lowest PC1 variance (17.4%) of all those tested, suggesting its activity depends on a broad, distributed range of active-site motions rather than a single dominant conformational switch. Together, these findings offer a testable, flexibility-based model of catalytic selectivity in GH29 fucosidases and mark Thermo-Fuc as a promising biocatalyst for enzymatic HMO synthesis.

摘要翻译

GH29 α-L-岩藻糖苷酶如何在结构水平上平衡水解与转糖基作用仍不清楚，这限制了这些酶在糖苷合成中的理性工程改造。我们报道了Thermo-Fuc，一种来自Thermotogaceae细菌的热稳定GH29A α-L-岩藻糖苷酶，具有异常碱性的pH最适值（9.0）、50 ℃的温度最适值以及超过6小时的半衰期。该酶水解多种岩藻糖基化底物，优先选择支链Lewis抗原（Leᵃ、Leˣ）而非线性HMO骨架如2'-FL和3-FL（对pNP-α-L-岩藻吡喃糖苷的K(m) = 1.015 mM，kcat = 0.00232 s(-)(1)），并在优化条件下以72.1%（mol/mol）的转糖基化比例将底物转化为2'-岩藻糖基乳糖，具有高区域选择性，经LC-MS和(1)H-NMR确认，这是野生型GH29A酶报道的最高产率之一。序列和结构分析鉴定了一个保守的Asp219-Glu261催化二元体，而分子动力学 (molecular dynamics) 模拟揭示了五个活性位点环的底物依赖性灵活性。最具催化效率的复合物在所有测试中具有最低的PC1方差（17.4%），表明其活性依赖于广泛分布的活性位点运动范围，而非单一主导构象开关。综合这些发现，我们提出了一个可测试的、基于灵活性的GH29岩藻糖苷酶催化选择性模型，并将Thermo-Fuc标记为酶促HMO合成的有前景生物催化剂。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：GH29 α-L-岩藻糖苷酶如何在结构层面平衡水解与转糖苷（transfucosylation）活性？这一平衡机制长期不明确，限制了理性工程改造这些酶用于糖苷合成。
  • 为什么重要：2'-FL 是重要的母乳寡糖（HMO），具有益生元和免疫调节功能，酶法合成是绿色替代化学合成的关键路径；理解水解/转糖苷选择性机制可指导高效生物催化剂的开发。
  • 现有方法为何不足：已有研究多聚焦于定点突变或定向进化，缺乏对活性位点构象动力学的系统理解；GH29 家族酶的选择性决定因素尚未被结构-动力学层面解释。
  • 精确研究问题：Can the substrate-dependent flexibility of active-site loops in a GH29A fucosidase explain its hydrolytic vs. transfucosylation selectivity, and can this knowledge enable efficient enzymatic synthesis of 2'-FL?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：从 Thermotogaceae 细菌中克隆表达一个 GH29A α-L-岩藻糖苷酶（Thermo-Fuc），进行生化表征（pH/温度最适、动力学、底物谱），结合序列/结构分析鉴定催化残基，用 MD 模拟研究不同底物结合态下活性位点 loop 的柔性，最后在优化条件下进行 2'-FL 的酶法合成。
  2. 核心洞察：催化活性与 loop 柔性的关系不是「越灵活越好」，而是「分布式柔性」——最有效的催化复合物具有最低的 PC1 方差（17.4%），表明其活性依赖于广泛的、分布式的活性位点运动，而非单一主导构象开关。这暗示选择性可能由多个 loop 的协同运动编码，而非单个残基或 loop 决定。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：在酶工程中，关注单一「关键残基」可能不如关注「构象动态网络」有效；MD 模拟的 PC 分析可作为筛选高活性突变体的预筛工具。这一思路可迁移到其他糖苷水解酶或转移酶的设计中。

09 关键公式符号

  • Michaelis-Menten 方程：v = V_max [S] / (K_m + [S])
  • v：反应速率；V_max：最大速率；[S]：底物浓度；K_m：米氏常数
  • 用途：量化 Thermo-Fuc 对 pNP-α-L-fucopyranoside 的亲和力与催化效率
  • 直觉：K_m 反映底物结合亲和力，kcat 反映催化转换速率
  • 来源：摘要中给出 K_m = 1.015 mM, kcat = 0.00232 s⁻¹
  • 转糖苷产率：产率(%) = (2'-FL 摩尔数 / 初始供体摩尔数) × 100
  • 用途：评估合成效率
  • 直觉：72.1% 表示供体底物大部分转化为目标产物
  • 来源：摘要「72.1% (mol/mol) transfucosylation ratio」
  • 主成分分析（PCA）方差：PC1 variance (%) = (λ₁ / Σλᵢ) × 100
  • λ₁：第一主成分特征值；Σλᵢ：所有主成分特征值之和
  • 用途：量化 MD 轨迹中活性位点 loop 构象变化的集中程度
  • 直觉：低 PC1 方差（17.4%）表示构象变化分散在多个运动模式中，而非单一主导模式
  • 来源：摘要「lowest PC1 variance (17.4%)」

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a potentially interesting biocatalyst with a notable transfucosylation yield and a novel mechanistic hypothesis linking active-site loop flexibility to catalytic selectivity. However, the evidence base visible in the abstract is insufficient to evaluate the rigor of the kinetic, structural, and simulation analyses. The mechanistic model, while testable, is supported only by a single variance metric from MD simulations, and the relationship between PC1 variance and catalytic productivity is not established from the provided material. The claim of "among the highest yields reported" requires systematic comparison that is not visible. The work may interest the glycoscience and biocatalysis communities, but the case is not fully established from the abstract alone.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers in enzymatic synthesis of human milk oligosaccharides, glycoside hydrolase engineering, and biocatalysis for industrial applications would be interested. The thermostability, alkaline pH optimum, and high transfucosylation yield are practically relevant for process development. Computational enzymologists studying structure-function relationships in GH families may also find the flexibility-based selectivity model of interest.
  • Major strengths The reported transfucosylation yield of 72.1% for a wild-type enzyme is notable and practically significant. The combination of biochemical characterization, structural analysis, and MD simulations represents a multi-pronged approach. The identification of a conserved catalytic dyad and the proposal of a testable mechanistic model add conceptual value beyond a purely descriptive enzyme report.
