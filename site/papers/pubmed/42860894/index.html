Sequential high hydrostatic pressure and polyphenol oxidase treatments modulate the structure, digestion behavior, and antibody-binding immunoreactivity of amandin.

Gao, Weiman; Zhang, Jingyue; Ren, Lining; Jiang, Shijing; Ma, Xinyu; Ru, Guangjiao; Lin, Zihan; Zhang, Yuhang; Sun, Aidong
Journal of the science of food and agriculture · 2026-10-12 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文研究高静水压和多酚氧化酶处理对杏仁过敏原amandin结构、消化及抗体结合免疫反应性的影响，虽涉及蛋白结构表征和分子对接，但核心是食品加工与免疫反应性，非蛋白质结构计算建模方法创新，与课题关联较弱。

摘要

BACKGROUND: Amandin (Pru du 6), a major almond allergen, exhibits immunochemical recognition that is influenced by protein conformation and the accessibility of antibody-recognized regions. This study investigated whether processing sequence governs the effects of high hydrostatic pressure (HHP) and polyphenol oxidase (PPO) on the structural, digestive, and commercial antibody-based immunoreactivity of amandin. RESULTS: HHP and PPO conditions were optimized before individual and sequential treatments were compared. Among the tested pressures, 400 MPa produced the lowest commercial antibody-based relative immunoreactivity and was selected for the sequential experiments. HHP pretreatment followed by PPO treatment produced the greatest reduction in antibody-binding immunoreactivity and the most pronounced accumulation of high-molecular-weight material. Spectroscopic and physicochemical analyses showed altered secondary structure, fluorescence behavior, surface hydrophobicity, and sulfhydryl accessibility. Molecular docking supported a potential non-covalent PPO-amandin interaction but was not interpreted as evidence of aggregate formation. HHP-PPO treatment also generated a more interconnected aggregate morphology, increased radical-scavenging activity, and maintained comparatively low antibody-binding immunoreactivity during simulated gastrointestinal digestion despite a lower degree of hydrolysis. CONCLUSION: Processing sequence was a key determinant of amandin structural remodeling and antibody-binding immunoreactivity. The observed structural changes are consistent with HHP pretreatment influencing subsequent PPO-mediated association, although the exact intermolecular linkages and antibody-recognized regions were not determined. Because the enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay used commercial anti-almond-protein antibodies rather than patient immunoglobulin E, further validation with allergic sera and biological models is required before conclusions regarding allergenicity can be made. 摘要翻译

背景：杏仁蛋白（Amandin，Pru du 6）是杏仁的主要过敏原，其免疫化学识别受蛋白质构象及抗体识别区域可及性的影响。本研究探讨了加工顺序是否决定高静水压（HHP）和多酚氧化酶（PPO）对杏仁蛋白结构、消化特性及基于商业抗体的免疫反应性的影响。结果：在比较单独处理和顺序处理之前，先对HHP和PPO条件进行了优化。在测试的压力中，400 MPa产生了最低的基于商业抗体的相对免疫反应性，因此被选用于顺序实验。HHP预处理后接PPO处理产生了最大的抗体结合免疫反应性降低，并最显著地积累了高分子量物质。光谱学和物理化学分析显示，二级结构、荧光行为、表面疏水性和巯基可及性均发生改变。分子对接支持PPO与杏仁蛋白之间可能存在非共价相互作用，但未被解释为聚集体形成的证据。HHP-PPO处理还生成了更互联的聚集体形态，增加了自由基清除活性，并在模拟胃肠消化过程中保持了相对较低的抗体结合免疫反应性，尽管水解程度较低。结论：加工顺序是杏仁蛋白结构重塑和抗体结合免疫反应性的关键决定因素。观察到的结构变化与HHP预处理影响后续PPO介导的关联一致，但确切的分子间连接和抗体识别区域未被确定。由于酶联免疫吸附测定使用的是商业抗杏仁蛋白抗体而非患者免疫球蛋白E，在得出关于过敏原性的结论之前，需使用过敏血清和生物模型进行进一步验证。(c) 2026 化学工业学会。
