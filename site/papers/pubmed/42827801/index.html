Site-specific phosphorylation of Ser352 drives aggregation of Tau R4 under acidosis conditions.

Bressler, Shachar Guy; Blacher, Carmia; Harpaz, Karina Abramov; Grunhaus, Dana; Rudiger, Stefan G D; Hurevich, Mattan; Shalev, Deborah E; Miller, Yifat; Friedler, Assaf
Chemical science · 2026-10-04 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文结合MD模拟与NMR等实验，揭示Tau R4结构域Ser352磷酸化在酸性pH下驱动蛋白从凝聚体向淀粉样纤维转变的分子机制，属于蛋白质构象转变与聚集的结构计算研究，但核心对象为Tau聚集而非经典结构预测/设计，方法创新有限。

摘要

In Alzheimer's disease, neurons undergo acidosis as their pH drops from approximately 7.1 to approximately 6.5. Here we elucidate the molecular mechanism of specific Tau aggregation only at this lower pH. We show that a specific phosphorylation event in the Tau R4 domain is coupled to this pH drop to drive a defined phase transition from condensates to fibrils. Using a combination of experimental and computational studies, our results demonstrate that site-specific phosphorylation of Ser352 is the molecular switch that induces aggregation of Tau only at the more acidic, disease-related pH. We designed and synthesized a phosphopeptide library covering all phosphorylation patterns of the Tau-R4 domain and tested its response to controlled acidification using an array of complementary biophysical methods. This revealed that only a single phosphorylation of Ser352 led to acid-driven aggregation of Tau-R4. At neutral pH, Tau-R4 with a phosphorylation on Ser352 formed liquid condensates. These condensates converted irreversibly into amyloid filaments as the pH gradually decreased towards the level associated with pathological acidosis. Using a combination of (31)P- and (1)H-NMR, MD simulations and microscopy studies, we show that the mechanism by which the phosphorylation of Ser352 Tau R4 exerts its effects is based on the unique position of this residue within the protein structure. pSer352 is located at the inside of the tip of a beta-hairpin, pointing into the hydrophobic core of the amyloid fold. This buried position increases its effective pK (a), resulting in compacting of the hairpin following pSer352 protonation upon acidification. This shortens the inter-sheet distance at this position and tightens the filament. We conclude that this single protonation event of the pSer352 phosphate is responsible for the macroscopic phase transition from condensates to aggregates. Our results provide the molecular explanation for the specific aggregation of Tau under the pathologically relevant acidic pH, which is substantially different from its behavior at neutral pH.

摘要翻译

在阿尔茨海默病中，神经元经历酸中毒，其pH值从约7.1下降至约6.5。在此，我们阐明了仅在此较低pH值下Tau蛋白特异性聚集的分子机制。我们展示了Tau R4结构域中一个特定的磷酸化事件与这一pH下降相耦合，驱动从凝聚体到原纤维的明确相变。通过结合实验与计算研究，我们的结果表明，Ser352的位点特异性磷酸化是仅在更酸性、疾病相关pH值下诱导Tau聚集的分子开关。我们设计并合成了一个覆盖Tau-R4结构域所有磷酸化模式的磷酸肽库，并利用一系列互补的生物物理方法测试其对受控酸化的响应。这揭示出仅Ser352的单一位点磷酸化导致Tau-R4的酸驱动聚集。在中性pH下，Ser352磷酸化的Tau-R4形成液体凝聚体。随着pH逐渐降低至与病理性酸中毒相关的水平，这些凝聚体不可逆地转化为淀粉样纤维。通过结合(31)P-和(1)H-NMR、分子动力学模拟及显微镜研究，我们展示了Ser352磷酸化Tau R4发挥作用的机制基于该残基在蛋白质结构中的独特位置。pSer352位于β-发夹尖端的内侧，指向淀粉样折叠的疏水核心。这一埋藏位置增加了其有效pK (a)，导致酸化时pSer352质子化后发夹结构压缩。这缩短了该位置的片层间距离并使纤维更紧密。我们得出结论，pSer352磷酸基团的这一单次质子化事件负责从凝聚体到聚集体的宏观相变。我们的结果为Tau在病理相关酸性pH下的特异性聚集提供了分子解释，这与其在中性pH下的行为显著不同。

本文通过实验（荧光显微、ThT、NMR、SEM）与计算（MD 模拟）结合，证明 Tau R4 结构域中 Ser352 的单一位点磷酸化（pSer352）在 pH 从 7.1 降至 6.5 时发生质子化，导致 β-hairpin 疏水核心压缩、片层间距从 ~1 nm 缩短至 ~0.5 nm，从而驱动液滴不可逆地转化为淀粉样纤维。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题: 在阿尔茨海默病相关的轻度酸中毒条件下（pH 从 ~7.1 降至 ~6.5），Tau 蛋白的哪种磷酸化修饰、通过什么分子机制，特异性地触发其从可逆的液-液相分离（LLPS） condensates 向不可逆的淀粉样纤维聚集转变？
  • 为什么重要: 阿尔茨海默病中神经元 pH 下降是已知病理特征，但 pH 下降如何与 Tau 的特定磷酸化修饰耦合、进而驱动聚集的分子机制此前不清楚。理解这一机制有助于解释为何特定磷酸化位点在疾病中具有病理意义，并为干预提供靶点。
  • 现有方法不足: 以往研究多在单一 pH（通常为中性）下研究 Tau 聚集，未系统考察 pH 变化与磷酸化模式的耦合效应；且多数研究使用全长 Tau 或含多个磷酸化位点的混合物，难以解析单个位点的贡献。
  • 精确研究问题: 在 pH 6.5 条件下，pSer352 的质子化是否足以驱动 Tau-R4 从 condensates 向 amyloid fibrils 的相转变？该转变是否由 pSer352 在 β-hairpin 疏水核心中的独特位置所介导？

04 背景与发展脉络

（标注：以下脉络部分基于本文引言，部分经外部知识核验，具体标注于各条目）

  1. Tau 聚集与磷酸化（经外部核验）: Tau 是高磷酸化后从微管解离并聚集形成神经纤维缠结的微管结合蛋白。AD 脑中 Tau 的过度磷酸化是经典病理标志。代表性方法：体外磷酸化模拟（如 S293E/S305E 突变）、激酶共孵育。局限：多数研究在单一 pH 下进行，未考虑病理 pH 变化。
  2. 酸中毒与神经退行（经外部核验）: AD 神经元 pH 从 ~7.1 降至 ~6.5，溶酶体酸化受损是已知特征。α-synuclein 和 Aβ 的聚集速率在低 pH 下显著加快（本文引文 13、14）。局限：这些研究未将 pH 效应与特定磷酸化位点关联。
  3. Tau 的 LLPS 与聚集（经外部核验）: Tau 通过 LLPS 形成 condensates，是聚集的前体。pH、离子强度、分子伴侣等均影响 LLPS。局限：pH 如何调控 condensates 向纤维转变的分子细节不清。
  4. 磷酸化位点特异性功能（本文框架 + 外部核验）: 作者前期工作（引文 27）已系统研究 Tau-R4 三种丝氨酸（S341、S352、S356）磷酸化在中性 pH 下的行为：S341 促聚集、S352 促 condensates、S356 无效应。本文在此基础上引入 pH 变量，发现 S352 在酸性 pH 下从 condensates 转为聚集。
  5. 本文位置: 首次将位点特异性磷酸化与病理 pH 下降耦合，提出 pSer352 质子化作为 pH 依赖的分子开关，填补了「磷酸化模式 × pH 微环境」如何共同决定 Tau 聚集命运的知识空白。

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法: 合成覆盖 Tau-R4（336-358）所有磷酸化模式的肽库（0P、1P341、1P352、1P356、2P341,352、2P341,356、2P352,356、3P），在 pH 7.1 至 6.5 范围内系统检测其 condensates 形成与聚集行为；结合 ¹H/³¹P NMR 滴定、MD 模拟和 SEM 表征。
  2. 核心洞察: pSer352 的独特之处在于其位于 β-hairpin 尖端内侧、指向疏水核心。这一空间位置使其有效 pKa 从溶液中的 ~5.7 升高至 ~6.5 附近，从而在 AD 相关 pH 范围内发生质子化。质子化中和了磷酸基团的负电荷，降低了将磷酸基团置于疏水核心的能量代价，使 hairpin 压缩、片层间距缩短，最终驱动 condensates 向纤维转变。
  3. [Analysis] 可能的普适教训: 磷酸化修饰的功能并非固定不变，而是由其所在的结构微环境（溶剂可及性、局部介电常数、疏水程度）和生理条件（pH、离子强度）共同决定。这提示在解读磷酸化蛋白质组学数据时，需考虑位点特异性结构背景；在设计磷酸化模拟突变时，S→E 可能无法重现 pH 依赖的质子化行为。

07 方法总览

  • 输入: Tau-R4 肽段（Q336-D358）的 8 种磷酸化变体；pH 7.1-6.5 缓冲液；³¹P/¹H NMR 化学位移数据；MD 模拟的初始结构（基于 PDB 5O3T 的纤维核心结构）
  • 输出: 各磷酸化变体在不同 pH 下的聚集状态（condensates vs. aggregates vs. soluble）；pSer352 的 pKa 值；MD 模拟的构象 ensemble 和片层间距
  • 模块: 1. 肽库合成与纯化（固相合成，Fmoc 化学） 2. 荧光显微镜（condensates/aggregates 形态与尺寸） 3. ThT 荧光（淀粉样纤维定量） 4. ¹H NMR（Hα 化学位移滴定，监测骨架构象变化） 5. ³¹P NMR（磷酸基团质子化状态滴定） 6. MD 模拟（CHARMM36m 力场，NAMD，100 ns，NPT，330 K） 7. SEM（纤维形态与宽度）
  • 流程: 合成肽库 → pH 滴定 + 荧光显微筛选 → 鉴定 1P352 为唯一 pH 响应聚集变体 → ThT 确认淀粉样性质 → NMR 滴定定位 pSer352 的 pKa 和构象变化 → MD 模拟比较质子化/去质子化状态的稳定性 → SEM 确认纤维形成 → 整合机制模型
  • 工具: ImageJ 1.54j（图像分析）、Origin 2024（SLogistic3 拟合）、BIOVIA Discovery Studio（建模）、NAMD（MD）、CHARMM36m（力场）
  • 假设: (1) 肽段模型能反映全长 Tau 中 R4 结构域的行为；(2) pH 7.1 对应 pSer352 去质子化（二价阴离子），pH 6.5 对应单质子化（一价阴离子）；(3) MD 模拟的初始纤维结构（基于 5O3T）能代表病理相关构象

09 关键公式符号

本文为实验与 MD 模拟结合的研究，未使用解析公式。关键定量关系如下：

  1. Henderson-Hasselbalch 方程（用于 ³¹P NMR 拟合 pKa）： [ \delta = \delta_{\text{HA}} + \frac{\delta_{\text{A}^-} - \delta_{\text{HA}}}{1 + 10^{(pK_a - \text{pH})}} ] - δ：观测化学位移 - δ_HA：质子化态化学位移 - δ_A⁻：去质子化态化学位移 - pKa：磷酸基团酸解离常数（拟合得 6.53 ± 0.02）

  2. SLogistic3 函数（用于液滴直径 vs pH 拟合）： [ y = \frac{a}{1 + e^{-k(x - b)}} ] - y：液滴直径（µm） - x：pH - a：上平台直径 - k：转变斜率 - b：拐点位置（临界 pH = 6.80 ± 0.02）

  3. 扩散系数（FRAP 分析）： [ D = \frac{r^2}{4 \tau_{1/2}} ] - D：扩散系数（pH 7.1 时为 3.3 × 10⁻¹² m² s⁻¹） - r：光漂白半径 - τ₁/₂：半恢复时间（2 ± 1 s）

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围: 本文仅研究 Tau-R4 肽段（Q336-D358）在体外、无辅助因子条件下的行为，不涉及全长 Tau、其他结构域、细胞环境或体内验证。
  • oracle/真值输入: 无预测任务；实验系统直接测量聚集行为。
  • 端到端状态: 从肽段合成到纤维表征的完整体外流程，但未达到体内或细胞水平的端到端验证。
  • 算力成本: MD 模拟 100 ns × 3 模型，使用 BGU HPC 中心，具体 GPU/CPU 时数未提供。
  • 历史数据依赖: 依赖作者前期工作（引文 27）中 Tau-R4 磷酸化模式在中性 pH 下的行为数据。
  • 模型依赖: MD 模拟依赖 CHARMM36m 力场和基于 cryo-EM 纤维结构（PDB 5O3T）的初始模型；力场参数对磷酸基团质子化状态的描述可能不精确。
  • 最难情形: 肽段模型缺乏全长 Tau 的 flanking regions（N/C 端），这些区域在体内可能通过空间位阻或电荷效应调节聚集；此外，体内 pH 变化是渐进的，而非体外实验中的阶跃变化。
  • 群体/领域边界: 结论仅适用于 Tau-R4 结构域在无辅助因子条件下的 pH 响应；不能外推至其他 Tau 结构域、其他蛋白或其他磷酸化位点。
  • 不确定性: ³¹P NMR 的 pKa（6.53 ± 0.02）与 Hα 转变点（6.81 ± 0.02）和液滴直径转变点（6.80 ± 0.02）不完全一致，差异 ~0.3 pH 单位，作者未充分讨论这一差异的机制意义。
  • 有边界的复述: 在体外无辅助因子条件下，Tau-R4 肽段中 Ser352 的磷酸化使其在 pH 从 7.1 降至 6.5 时发生质子化（pKa ≈ 6.5），质子化导致 β-hairpin 疏水核心压缩、片层间距从 ~1 nm 缩短至 ~0.5 nm，从而驱动 condensates 不可逆地转化为淀粉样纤维；其他磷酸化位点（S341、S356）不具备此 pH 响应特性。

14 学到什么

Agent 提炼的知识候选：

  1. 位点特异性磷酸化的 pH 开关机制（可迁移至蛋白质结构预测与设计）：磷酸化残基的功能不仅取决于其磷酸化状态，还取决于其所在结构微环境的 pKa 偏移。在设计磷酸化模拟突变（如 S→E）时，需考虑目标残基的溶剂可及性和局部介电常数；在结构预测中，应显式建模磷酸基团的质子化状态。

  2. β-hairpin 疏水核心的磷酸化位点定位策略（可迁移至构象采样与 MD 模拟）：将磷酸化位点置于 β-hairpin 内侧、指向疏水核心，可使其 pKa 升高 ~0.8 个单位（从 ~5.7 到 ~6.5），从而在病理 pH 范围内响应。这一设计原则可用于预测其他蛋白中 pH 敏感的磷酸化位点。

  3. condensates-to-fibrils 转变的多尺度表征流程（可迁移至实验-计算联合研究）：荧光显微（宏观相变）+ FRAP（材料性质）+ ThT（淀粉样性）+ NMR（原子级 pKa/构象）+ MD（结构机制）的联合策略，为研究相分离与聚集的耦合提供了可复制的实验范式。

  4. MD 模拟中质子化状态的显式处理（可迁移至 MD 模拟方法）：本文通过在 MD 中显式设置 pSer352 的质子化状态（SP1 vs SP2）来模拟 pH 效应，而非使用恒定 pH 模拟。这一简化策略在计算成本受限时是有效的替代方案，但需注意其无法捕捉质子化动态平衡。

  5. 「单一磷酸化位点 + 单一结构域」的最小系统设计（可迁移至蛋白质设计）：使用合成肽库系统覆盖所有磷酸化模式，在最小系统中解析单个位点的贡献，避免全长蛋白中多位点混杂效应。这一策略适用于其他 PTM（如乙酰化、甲基化）的功能解析。

  6. pH 作为相分离的调控维度（可迁移至 LLPS 研究）：pH 下降通过质子化特定残基改变 condensates 的材料性质（从液态到固态），而非简单地改变全局电荷。这提示在 LLPS 预测模型中，应纳入残基级别的 pKa 信息。

15 与已有知识连接

  • Tau 聚集机制（经外部核验）: 本文与 Tau 蛋白 LLPS 和聚集的既有研究一致，特别是 Tau 的液-固相转变需要特定条件（如辅助因子、PTM）的观点。本文补充了 pH 作为独立调控维度的证据。相关文献：Ambadipudi et al., 2017 (Nat Commun)；Wegmann et al., 2018 (EMBO J)。
  • 磷酸化与 Tau 聚集（经外部核验）: 已知 Tau 的过度磷酸化促进聚集，但具体位点与机制的对应关系尚不清晰。本文的位点特异性方法可与其他磷酸化组学研究（如 Mair et al., 2016）互补。
  • pKa 偏移与蛋白质结构（经外部核验）: 蛋白质内部残基的 pKa 偏移是已知现象（如催化残基），但将其与磷酸化位点的 pH 依赖性聚集联系起来是新的贡献。相关概念可连接至恒定 pH MD 模拟方法（如 Gumbart et al.）。
  • LLPS 与淀粉样聚集的耦合（经外部核验）: 本文支持「condensates 作为聚集前体」的模型，与 FUS、TDP-43 等蛋白的相分离-聚集研究一致。pH 作为调控因子的发现可扩展至其他相分离蛋白。
  • [Analysis] 可迁移领域: 本文的「位点特异性 PTM × 环境参数（pH、温度、离子强度）」研究范式可迁移至：(1) 其他神经退行性疾病蛋白（α-synuclein、TDP-43）的 PTM 功能解析；(2) 设计 pH 响应型蛋白材料或生物传感器；(3) 在蛋白质设计中引入 pKa 工程以调控相分离行为。

Review setup

  • Input scope Full manuscript text (abstract, introduction, results, discussion, methods) and metadata
  • Assessment boundary Scientific claims, experimental design, data interpretation, and internal consistency as presented in the supplied material
  • Shared manuscript claim summary The authors propose that site-specific phosphorylation of Ser352 in the Tau R4 domain acts as a pH-sensitive molecular switch, driving a condensate-to-fibril transition specifically under acidosis conditions (pH drop from ~7.1 to ~6.5), via protonation of the phosphoserine that stabilizes a compact β-hairpin conformation.

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The manuscript addresses an interesting and potentially important question regarding the coupling between site-specific phosphorylation and pH-dependent Tau aggregation. The central claim, that Ser352 phosphorylation uniquely enables acid-triggered fibril formation through a protonation-dependent mechanism, is plausible and mechanistically appealing. However, the evidence as presented has several gaps that prevent full confidence in the conclusions. The NMR titrations were performed at concentrations and conditions that differ from the microscopy experiments, and the relationship between the molecular events reported by NMR and the macroscopic phase transition observed by microscopy is not fully established. The MD simulations, while supportive, are not sufficiently described to assess their validity. The claim of site-specificity rests on a single phosphoserine position, but the mechanistic explanation for why other phosphorylation sites do not show similar behavior is largely speculative. The manuscript would benefit from additional quantitative analysis and clearer presentation of controls.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers studying Tau aggregation mechanisms, phase separation in neurodegeneration, post-translational modification effects on protein aggregation, and the role of cellular pH in disease. The work may also interest those developing therapeutic strategies targeting phosphorylation-dependent aggregation.
  • Major strengths The systematic screening of all phosphorylation patterns in the R4 domain is a rigorous approach. The combination of experimental and computational methods provides complementary perspectives. The identification of a single phosphorylation site with a unique pH-dependent aggregation phenotype is a clear and testable finding. The use of multiple biophysical techniques strengthens the phenomenological description.

  • Minor Comments

Major Concerns

  • Concern ID R1-M1
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Quantitative consistency
  • Claim pointer The authors claim that the protonation of pSer352, with an apparent pKa of 6.53 ± 0.02, is responsible for the condensate-to-fibril transition observed between pH 6.7 and 6.5.
  • Evidence pointer Results section, Figures 3a, 6b, 8b; location not provided
  • Concern The NMR titrations were performed at 2 mM peptide concentration, while the microscopy experiments were performed at 50 µM. The relationship between the molecular pKa measured by NMR and the macroscopic phase transition pH observed by microscopy is not quantitatively established. The authors report a critical pH of 6.80 ± 0.02 for droplet diameter and 6.81 ± 0.02 for Hα chemical shift, but the 31P pKa is 6.53 ± 0.02. These values differ by approximately 0.3 pH units, and the manuscript does not explain whether this difference is expected or significant. Furthermore, the NMR experiments were conducted in the presence of 0.002% PEG, while the microscopy experiments used 5% PEG, which could affect the phase behavior.
  • Why it matters The central mechanistic claim is that protonation of pSer352 drives the phase transition. If the pKa values from different methods do not align, the causal link between protonation and aggregation is weakened. The concentration difference between NMR and microscopy experiments could mean that the molecular events observed by NMR are not directly relevant to the phase transition observed at lower concentrations.

  • Resolution test The authors should perform NMR titrations at concentrations closer to those used in microscopy, or provide a quantitative model that accounts for the concentration difference. They should also explain the 0.3 pH unit discrepancy between the 31P pKa and the Hα/droplet diameter midpoints.

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Technical soundness
  • Claim pointer The authors claim that MD simulations show that protonation of pSer352 stabilizes the β-hairpin and reduces inter-sheet distance, supporting the experimental findings.
  • Evidence pointer Methods section, Molecular dynamics simulations; Results section, Figures 5, 6d, 7c-d; location not provided

The authors state that simulations were performed for 100 ns for each oligomer model, but do not provide details on the number of replicates, the equilibration protocol, the choice of force field parameters for the phosphorylated residues, or the criteria for assessing convergence. The RMSD analysis is mentioned but not shown. The simulations were performed at 330 K, which is above physiological temperature, and the rationale for this choice is not explained. The structural analyses (RMSF, DSSP, inter-sheet distances) are described but the results are only partially presented. The authors also do not discuss the limitations of using a minimal octamer model to represent the full fibril structure.
  • Why it matters The MD simulations are a key component of the mechanistic argument. Without adequate description of the methodology and validation, the reliability of the simulation results cannot be assessed. The choice of 330 K is unusual and could affect the conclusions about stability.

  • Resolution test The authors should provide full details of the simulation setup, including force field parameters for phosphoserine, equilibration protocol, number of replicates, and convergence criteria. They should justify the choice of 330 K and provide RMSD plots to demonstrate convergence. Ideally, they should also perform simulations at 310 K to confirm that the results are not temperature-dependent.

  • Concern ID R1-M3

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Evidence sufficiency
  • Claim pointer The authors claim that the condensate-to-fibril transition is irreversible, based on the observation that aggregates formed at pH 6.5 did not revert to condensates when pH was restored to 7.1.
  • Evidence pointer Results section, Figure 3c; location not provided
  • Concern The irreversibility claim is based on a single experimental condition. The authors restored pH to 7.1 in a single step and observed no reversion, but they do not report the timescale of observation, whether the samples were monitored for extended periods, or whether any intermediate states were observed. The claim of irreversibility has significant implications for the proposed mechanism, as it suggests a kinetic trap rather than a thermodynamically driven transition. The authors do not discuss whether the irreversibility is due to the formation of kinetically stable fibrils or to the loss of some component required for reversion.
  • Why it matters The irreversibility of the transition is a key feature of the proposed mechanism and has implications for understanding the progression of Tau pathology. If the transition is reversible on longer timescales, the interpretation of the data would change.

  • Resolution test The authors should provide a more detailed analysis of the reversibility, including longer observation times, multiple pH cycling experiments, and possibly seeding experiments to test whether the aggregates can nucleate new fibril formation.
