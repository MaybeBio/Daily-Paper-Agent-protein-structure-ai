Planning for an AlphaFold for Drug Safety Assessment: Benchmarking and Data Infrastructure as Prerequisites.

Patel, Chirag J
NEJM AI · 2026-10-26 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文讨论药物安全评估中NAMs的数据基础设施和基准测试，类比AlphaFold但非蛋白质结构计算研究，仅间接相关。

摘要

AbstractThe transition from animal testing to new approach methodologies (NAMs) is a scientific and ethical imperative championed by regulators and industry. In this issue, Tieghi and colleagues outline a federal road map to accelerate this shift; however, we argue that the operationalization of NAMs is currently hindered by a lack of benchmarking infrastructure. Drawing parallels to the "AlphaFold moment" in protein structure prediction, we posit that success requires community-driven data standards and neutral evaluations that define structural biology. Currently, predictive toxicology is constrained by undefined phenotypic end points, fragmented data, and reliance on animal models with limited human concordance. To replicate the prediction success of AlphaFold, the field must build a data infrastructure for toxicology - establishing open, standardized repositories and recurring, blinded challenges. Key differences between AlphaFold and NAMs, however, are the risk functions and tasks - one is built for predictive discovery, the other for harm mitigation, which requires integration with regulatory decision-making frameworks. Nevertheless, establishing data infrastructure is the prerequisite for transforming NAMs from niche research tools into trusted regulatory instruments capable of reliably predicting human harm.

摘要翻译

摘要从动物试验向新方法方法论（NAMs）的转变，是监管机构和行业所倡导的科学与伦理必然要求。在本期中，Tieghi及其同事概述了加速这一转变的联邦路线图；然而，我们认为，NAMs的实际操作目前受限于缺乏基准测试基础设施。借鉴蛋白质结构预测中的“AlphaFold时刻”，我们主张，成功需要社区驱动的数据标准和中性评估，这些正是定义结构生物学的要素。当前，预测毒理学受制于未定义的 phenotypes 终点、碎片化数据以及对人类一致性有限的动物模型的依赖。要复制AlphaFold的预测成功，该领域必须构建毒理学数据基础设施——建立开放、标准化的存储库和定期、盲法挑战。然而，AlphaFold与NAMs之间的关键差异在于风险功能和任务——前者旨在预测性发现，后者则用于危害缓解，这需要与监管决策框架整合。尽管如此，建立数据基础设施是将NAMs从边缘研究工具转变为能够可靠预测人类危害的可信监管工具的前提条件。
