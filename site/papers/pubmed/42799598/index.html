Aspects of plant intelligence (ii).

Trewavas, Anthony
Plant signaling & behavior · 2026-09-27 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文讨论植物智能的哲学与生物学定义，仅提及钙信号通过蛋白结构转导，未涉及蛋白质结构计算建模或AI/物理模拟方法，与本课题关系微弱。

摘要

Plant intelligence was earlier described as adaptively variable behavior during the lifetime of the individual plant (Trewavas 2003). This general definition for animals and plants (biological intelligence) is now justified from generic terms and emphasis on adaptive behavior. Plant intelligence can also be defined from dictionary definitions of intelligence. Artificial intelligence has been indicated as adaptive behavior. Prominent psychological opinion now indicates that human intelligence is adaptive: IQ does not measure human intelligence. The uniformity of intelligence definitions negates views that plant intelligence is controversial. It is suggested that the early stages of transduction through calcium are interpreted through complex protein structures attached to mobile lipid rafts. Cell signaling acting as a cognitive process is investigated and evidence supports a relationship. Plants are biologically purposeful; they express goal-directed behavior throughout their life cycle and act as biologically purposeful agents in the wilderness. The demise of the Modern Synthesis of Evolution is indicated and could be replaced by one that correctly emphasizes biological purpose. Two sensory mechanisms are described for gravitropism as an illustration of the fail-safe mechanisms needed for all forms of behavior because true wilderness is often environmentally uncertain. Definitions of learning and memory are suggested and five kinds of plant biological memories are described.

摘要翻译

植物智能早前被描述为个体植物在其生命周期中表现出的适应性可变行为（Trewavas 2003）。这一对动物和植物的通用定义（生物智能）现基于通用术语及对适应性行为的强调而得到合理阐述。植物智能也可依据词典中对智能的定义来界定。人工智能已被指为适应性行为。当前重要的心理学观点表明，人类智能是适应性的：智商并不衡量人类智能。智能定义的统一性否定了植物智能具有争议性的观点。有人提出，通过钙进行的信号转导早期阶段是由附着于流动脂筏上的复杂蛋白质结构所介导的。细胞信号传导作为认知过程被研究，证据支持二者之间存在关联。植物具有生物目的性；它们在整个生命周期中表现出目标导向行为，并在荒野中作为具有生物目的性的主体行动。现代综合进化论的衰落已被指出，可能被一个正确强调生物目的性的理论所取代。本文描述了向地性的两种感觉机制，作为所有行为所需故障安全机制的例证，因为真正的荒野环境往往具有不确定性。文中提出了学习与记忆的定义，并描述了五种植物生物记忆类型。
