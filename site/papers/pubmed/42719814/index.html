Benchmarking antibody modeling tools across structure prediction, docking, and paratope-epitope interface analysis.

Yu, Zeyuan; Wu, Jilei; Ning, Ziyao; Qiao, Chunxia; Wang, Jing; Li, Xinying; Liu, Chenghua; Chen, Guojiang; Feng, Jiannan; Yu, Jijun
Bioinformatics advances · 2026-09-10 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文系统比较了多种抗体结构预测、对接及界面分析工具的性能，属于蛋白质结构计算领域的工程化评估，方法创新性有限但问题和方法轴均相关。

摘要

MOTIVATION: Computational antibody engineering requires reliable prediction of antibody variable-fragment structures, antigen-antibody complexes, and binding interfaces. However, publicly available tools for these tasks have rarely been compared across the complete workflow under a controlled and statistically grounded design. RESULTS: We evaluated ImmuneBuilder, IgFold, AlphaFold3, GRAMM, and dyMEAN on 50 non-redundant humanized antibody-antigen complexes using multiple retained predictions and paired statistical testing. All three antibody structure predictors were accurate, with AlphaFold3 performing best overall and for the third complementarity-determining region of the heavy chain. AlphaFold3 also substantially outperformed GRAMM and dyMEAN in complex prediction, producing medium- or high-quality binding interfaces for 46% of the complexes, although overall interface accuracy remained limited. When docking was reliable, AlphaFold3 accurately recovered epitope and paratope residues, salt bridges, and non-bonded contacts, but reproduced hydrogen bonds and fine-grained contact strengths less consistently. These findings provide practical guidance for selecting tools across antibody-modeling workflows and identify persistent limitations in fine-grained interface prediction. AVAILABILITY AND IMPLEMENTATION: Data, structural predictions, evaluation results, and analysis code are available from Zenodo under record 20710876.

摘要翻译

动机：计算抗体工程需要对抗体可变片段结构、抗原-抗体复合物及结合界面进行可靠预测。然而，针对这些任务的公开可用工具，在受控且具有统计依据的设计下，很少被跨完整工作流程进行比较。结果：我们使用多种保留预测和配对统计检验，在50个非冗余人源化抗体-抗原复合物上评估了ImmuneBuilder、IgFold、AlphaFold3、GRAMM和dyMEAN。所有三种抗体结构预测器均表现准确，其中AlphaFold3整体性能最佳，并在重链第三互补决定区表现最优。AlphaFold3在复合物预测方面也显著优于GRAMM和dyMEAN，为46%的复合物生成了中等或高质量的结合界面，尽管整体界面准确性仍有限。当对接可靠时，AlphaFold3能准确恢复表位和互补位残基、盐桥及非键接触，但在氢键和细粒度接触强度的再现上一致性较差。这些发现为跨抗体建模工作流程选择工具提供了实用指导，并识别了细粒度界面预测中的持续局限性。可用性和实现：数据、结构预测、评估结果及分析代码可从Zenodo记录20710876获取。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题: 在抗体建模的完整流程（Fv结构预测、抗体-抗原复合物对接、互补位-表位界面分析）中，当前公开可用的计算工具（AI和传统方法）在受控、统计严谨的设计下，性能如何比较？
  • 为什么重要: 抗体工程依赖于可靠的结构预测和界面分析，但不同工具（尤其是AI方法）缺乏直接的、端到端的比较，导致工具选择缺乏依据。
  • 现有方法为何不足: 现有研究通常只评估单一任务（如结构预测或对接），或使用不同的数据集和指标，缺乏在统一、受控框架下对完整工作流的比较。此外，对接精度对下游界面分析的影响未被系统量化。
  • 精确的「Can ... ?」研究问题: 在受控的基准测试下，AlphaFold3能否在抗体Fv结构预测、抗体-抗原复合物对接和界面恢复上，显著优于抗体专用工具（IgFold, ABB2）和传统/几何驱动对接方法（GRAMM, dyMEAN）？

04 背景与发展脉络

  • 脉络: 该文框架，经外部核验（引用文献）。
  • 阶段1: 传统实验方法: X射线晶体学、冷冻电镜。优点：准确。局限：耗时、昂贵，不适合大规模筛选。
  • 阶段2: 通用AI结构预测: AlphaFold2 (Jumper et al. 2021) 带来范式转变，但抗体建模，特别是CDR环构象，仍有局限 (Fernández-Quintero et al. 2023, Chen et al. 2024)。
  • 阶段3: 抗体专用AI工具: ABodyBuilder2/ImmuneBuilder (Abanades et al. 2023), IgFold (Ruffolo et al. 2023)。优点：整合抗体先验知识，速度快。局限：训练数据规模可能小于通用模型。
  • 阶段4: 通用AI复合物预测: AlphaFold3 (Abramson et al. 2024)。优点：扩散架构，可联合建模多种生物分子。局限：在抗体-抗原这类挑战性复合物上的性能需系统评估。
  • 阶段5: 传统与AI对接方法: GRAMM (Singh et al. 2024) 基于刚体对接和形状互补；dyMEAN (Kong et al. 2023) 使用等变图网络和表位引导。局限：刚体对接无法处理柔性，dyMEAN依赖表位信息质量。
  • 本文主张的位置: 本文填补了上述方法在统一、统计严谨的基准测试下的比较空白，并首次将对接精度与下游界面恢复质量关联起来。

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法: 在50个非冗余抗体-抗原复合物上，对5种工具（IgFold, ABB2, AF3, GRAMM, dyMEAN）进行Fv结构预测、复合物对接和界面恢复的端到端基准测试，使用多次预测和配对统计检验。
  2. 核心洞察: 对接精度是界面恢复质量的“门控”因素。只有当AF3产生正确对接姿态时，其界面恢复（表位/互补位残基、盐桥）才高度准确。然而，即使对接成功，精细的氢键网络和接触强度仍难以准确预测。这表明当前AI方法擅长捕捉几何互补性，但尚未完全掌握抗体-抗原识别的完整能量景观。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]: 对于蛋白质-蛋白质相互作用预测，评估应分层进行：先评估整体复合物结构（如DockQ），再评估界面残基恢复，最后评估原子级相互作用细节。一个方法可能在高层级任务上表现良好，但在低层级任务上存在显著局限。这为开发更精细的评估指标和设计针对性改进策略（如界面感知训练、后处理精炼）提供了方向。

07 方法总览

  • 输入: 50个抗体-抗原复合物的序列（Fv预测）或序列+结构（对接）。对于dyMEAN，额外需要抗原表位信息（来自HDOCK）。
  • 输出: 预测的Fv结构（PDB文件）和/或抗体-抗原复合物结构（PDB文件）。
  • 模块:
    1. 数据集构建: 从PDB筛选50个非冗余人源化Fab复合物（分辨率<3.0Å，发布日期晚于AF3截止日）。
    2. Fv结构预测: 使用IgFold, ABB2, AF3分别预测Fv结构。
    3. 复合物对接: 使用AF3, GRAMM, dyMEAN分别预测复合物结构。AF3输入序列，GRAMM输入结构，dyMEAN输入序列+结构+表位。
    4. 结构评估: 使用TM-score, lDDT, RMSD, DockQ评估预测结构。
    5. 界面恢复分析: 仅对AF3预测的复合物，使用PDBsum提取界面接触，计算表位/互补位残基、接触对、氢键、盐桥、非键接触的精确率、召回率、F1分数，以及接触强度的Spearman相关性。
  • 训练: 所有工具均为预训练模型，本文不涉及训练。
  • 工具: PyMOL, Structure Assessment Online Service, PDBsum, AbYsis。
  • 反馈回路: 无。这是一个单向的评估流程。
  • 假设: 实验解析的PDB结构是“金标准”真值；评估指标（如DockQ）能有效反映结构质量；多次预测的平均值能代表工具性能。

文字流程: 1. 从PDB获取50个抗体-抗原复合物的实验结构。 2. 提取Fv区域序列，分别输入IgFold、ABB2和AF3，每个工具生成5个预测Fv结构。 3. 提取抗原和抗体Fv的序列和结构，分别输入AF3、GRAMM和dyMEAN，每个工具生成5个预测复合物结构。 4. 将预测结构与实验结构比对，计算TM-score, lDDT, RMSD, DockQ。 5. 对AF3预测的复合物，使用PDBsum分析界面，计算界面恢复指标。 6. 使用配对Wilcoxon符号秩检验和Bootstrap进行统计分析。

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围: 该基准测试严格限定于人源化抗体-抗原复合物的Fv结构预测、对接和界面分析。结论不能直接推广到其他物种的抗体、纳米抗体、T细胞受体或其他类型的蛋白质复合物。
  • Oracle/真值输入: 实验解析的PDB结构被视为“金标准”，但实验结构本身也存在不确定性（如B因子、构象集合）。dyMEAN使用了来自HDOCK的oracle表位信息，这使其性能评估不完全独立。
  • 端到端状态: 这是一个评估研究，不是端到端的抗体设计流程。它评估了现有工具的性能，但未提出新的设计方法。
  • 算力成本: AF3使用公共服务器，成本未量化。本地工具（ABB2, IgFold, dyMEAN）在RTX 3080上运行，但具体时间未提供。
  • 历史数据依赖: 所有工具都依赖其训练数据。AF3的优势可能部分源于其更大、更近期的训练集。基准测试特意选用了AF3截止日期后的结构，以减轻数据泄露。
  • 模型依赖: 结论严格依赖于所测试的特定版本的工具（如AF3 web server版本，dyMEAN特定代码版本）。
  • 最难情形: CDR-H3环预测和界面氢键恢复被识别为最困难的任务。
  • 群体/领域边界: 结论适用于计算抗体工程领域，为工具选择提供指导，但不能直接用于临床决策或替代实验验证。
  • 不确定性: 所有性能指标都附有95%置信区间，工具间差异通过统计检验确认。但50个复合物的样本量相对较小，可能无法完全代表抗体-抗原相互作用的多样性。

有边界的复述: 在50个人源化抗体-抗原复合物的受控基准测试中，AlphaFold3在Fv结构预测、复合物对接和界面恢复上均优于其他测试工具，但对接成功率（46%）和精细界面细节（特别是氢键）的预测精度仍是主要瓶颈。

AF3在Fv预测上的优势可能部分源于其更大的训练集和更近的截止日期，而非架构上的根本优势。 抗体专用工具（IgFold, ABB2）的训练数据可能更小或更旧。AF3可能“见过”更多类似的CDR-H3构象。 这会影响对“哪种方法更好”的归因。如果训练数据是主要因素，那么更新抗体专用工具的训练集可能就能缩小差距。 使用相同训练集（或控制训练数据规模和时间）重新训练或微调抗体专用工具，再与AF3比较。 文中提到AF3有“substantially larger and more diverse training corpus” (Discussion 4.1)。
界面恢复分析仅针对AF3，未评估其他方法在成功对接时的界面恢复能力。 如果dyMEAN或GRAMM偶尔产生正确对接，它们的界面恢复能力可能不同。 无法判断AF3的界面恢复优势是源于其更好的对接，还是其模型本身能更好地捕捉界面特征。 对dyMEAN或GRAMM产生的少数成功对接案例（如dyMEAN的1个中等质量案例）进行同样的界面恢复分析。 作者将分析限制在AF3，理由是“AF3 is the only method to dock a substantial fraction of complexes correctly” (Results 3.3)。
使用PDBsum提取的接触作为“金标准”可能引入偏差。 PDBsum的接触定义（如氢键几何标准）可能与AF3模型内部的表示不一致。 评估指标本身可能对某些方法有利或不利。 使用多种接触定义工具（如Arpeggio, Proximity）进行交叉验证，或使用更连续的界面评分（如Rosetta能量）。 Methods 2.6节提到使用PDBsum。
基准测试的50个复合物均为“人源化”抗体，限制了结论的泛化性。 小鼠抗体、骆驼科纳米抗体或完全人源抗体的结构特征和预测难度可能不同。 结论可能不适用于其他类型的抗体，限制了其在实际应用中的指导价值。 构建包含不同物种和抗体类型的更大、更多样化的基准测试集。 数据集描述为“humanized antibody–antigen Fab complexes” (Methods 2.1)。

14 学到什么

Agent 提炼的知识候选:

  1. 分层评估框架: 对于蛋白质-蛋白质相互作用预测，应采用分层评估策略：先评估整体复合物结构（DockQ），再评估界面残基（F1），最后评估原子级相互作用（氢键、盐桥）。这能更精细地揭示方法的优势和局限。

    • 迁移到本课题: 在评估任何蛋白质复合物预测方法（如设计蛋白-配体、酶-底物）时，都应采用此分层框架，避免仅依赖单一指标（如RMSD）得出片面结论。

  2. 对接精度是界面分析的“门控”因素: 界面恢复的质量高度依赖于对接姿态的正确性。一个错误的对接姿态会导致界面分析完全失效。

    • 迁移到本课题: 在进行基于结构的蛋白质设计（如设计新的结合蛋白）时，必须首先确保对接或复合物预测的可靠性。如果对接不可靠，基于预测界面的任何设计（如突变扫描、亲和力优化）都可能无效。

  3. AI方法擅长几何互补性，但弱于精细化学细节: AF3能准确预测整体结合姿态和强静电相互作用（盐桥），但难以准确预测方向性更强的氢键和精细的接触强度。

    • 迁移到本课题: 在利用AI预测结构进行下游分析时，应区分“粗粒度”和“细粒度”信息。对于识别结合位点或热点残基，AI预测是可靠的；但对于需要精确原子坐标的物理模拟（如自由能微扰、分子动力学模拟），应谨慎使用，并考虑结合物理精炼或实验验证。

  4. 统计严谨的基准测试设计: 使用多次预测（5次）、配对统计检验（Wilcoxon）和Bootstrap置信区间，能有效控制随机性和提高结论的可靠性。

    • 迁移到本课题: 在比较不同方法（如不同的构象采样算法、不同的序列设计模型）时，应采用类似的统计设计，避免仅凭单次运行或简单平均值下结论。

16 研究想法

Agent 生成的研究候选:

  1. 名称: 界面感知的抗体结构预测后处理精炼

    • 来源局限/观察: AF3预测的界面氢键和接触强度不准确（recall 0.64, ρ=0.57）。
    • 核心假设: 通过一个轻量级的、专门针对抗体-抗原界面进行训练的图神经网络（GNN）或能量最小化步骤，可以显著改善AF3预测复合物中的氢键网络和侧链堆积。
    • 相对本文的增量: 本文仅评估了问题，本候选提出一个解决方案。它不改变AF3的对接，而是对输出进行后处理。
    • 初步方法: 1) 收集AF3预测的、对接成功的复合物。2) 使用PDBsum或Rosetta提取界面特征（残基类型、距离、角度）。3) 训练一个GNN，以AF3的界面结构为输入，预测一个“精炼”的侧链构象或氢键网络，目标是最小化与实验结构的差异。4) 使用Rosetta的侧链包装和能量最小化作为替代或基线方法。
    • 验证方式: 在本文的23个成功对接案例上，比较精炼前后界面氢键的召回率和接触强度的Spearman相关性。在独立的抗体-抗原测试集上验证泛化性。
    • 可能的失败模式: 1) 精炼过程可能破坏AF3原本正确的整体骨架。2) 训练数据不足，无法学习到氢键网络的复杂模式。3) 后处理带来的改进有限，无法达到实验精度。
    • 创新状态: unverified

  2. 名称: 基于扩散模型的柔性对接，显式建模CDR环构象变化

    • 来源局限/观察: 刚体对接（GRAMM）和几何驱动方法（dyMEAN）在处理抗体-抗原界面柔性时失败。AF3虽好，但仍有54%的复合物对接失败。
    • 核心假设: 在扩散模型的去噪过程中，显式地允许抗体CDR环（特别是CDR-H3）的构象变化，可以显著提高抗体-抗原对接的成功率，特别是对于需要诱导契合的复合物。
    • 相对本文的增量: 本文指出柔性是挑战，本候选提出一个具体的、可实现的AI架构来应对这一挑战。
    • 初步方法: 1) 基于AF3的扩散架构。2) 在输入表示中，为CDR环残基添加额外的噪声或可学习的“柔性”嵌入。3) 修改去噪网络，使其在预测坐标更新时，对CDR环区域应用更大的学习率或独立的更新步长。4) 训练时，使用包含多种CDR环构象的抗体-抗原复合物数据（如来自MD模拟或不同晶体构象）。
    • 验证方式: 在本文的50个复合物基准上，比较新方法与AF3的对接成功率（DockQ ≥ 0.49）和CDR-H3 RMSD。在需要诱导契合的已知案例上（如存在显著构象变化的复合物）进行重点测试。
    • 可能的失败模式: 1) 显式建模柔性可能导致训练不稳定或模式坍塌。2) 模型可能过度柔化CDR环，导致预测结构不物理。3) 计算成本显著高于AF3。
    • 创新状态: unverified

  3. 名称: 结合序列和结构互补性的抗体-抗原对接评分函数

    • 来源局限/观察: AF3的对接成功与否高度依赖于其内部评分，但仍有大量失败案例。DeepSCFold的成功表明序列互补性信息（如共进化）有价值。
    • 核心假设: 将AF3的几何评分与从序列衍生的结构互补性评分（如DeepSCFold的paired MSA特征）结合，可以更可靠地识别正确的对接姿态，从而提高整体对接成功率。
    • 相对本文的增量: 本文比较了现有方法，本候选提出一个集成方法，旨在直接提升AF3的性能。
    • 初步方法: 1) 对每个靶标，使用AF3生成多个候选对接姿态（如5个）。2) 对每个候选姿态，计算其AF3内部置信度（如pLDDT, pAE）。3) 使用一个独立的模型（如预训练的DeepSCFold或一个轻量级MLP）计算该姿态的序列互补性得分。4) 训练一个融合模型（如逻辑回归或简单加权和），以实验DockQ为标签，学习如何最佳组合这两个得分来重排候选姿态。
    • 验证方式: 在本文的50个复合物上，比较使用重排后的最佳姿态与AF3默认最佳姿态的对接成功率。在DeepSCFold的测试集上验证泛化性。
    • 可能的失败模式: 1) 两个评分来源高度相关，融合后无显著提升。2) 序列互补性模型在未见过的抗体-抗原对上泛化能力差。3) 重排过程可能将正确的姿态降级。
    • 创新状态: unverified

Review setup

  • Input scope Full manuscript (including main text, figures, methods, and supplementary information)
  • Assessment boundary Evaluation of computational benchmarking methodology, statistical rigor, dataset construction, and conclusions drawn from the presented evidence
  • Shared manuscript claim summary The authors benchmark five computational tools (ImmuneBuilder/ABB2, IgFold, AlphaFold3, GRAMM, dyMEAN) for antibody Fv structure prediction, antibody-antigen complex docking, and paratope-epitope interface analysis on a non-redundant set of 50 humanized antibody-antigen complexes, finding that AlphaFold3 outperforms the other methods across all tasks but still shows limited accuracy for fine-grained interface features.
  • Visible evidence base Main text (Sections 1–5), Methods (Section 2), Results (Section 3), Discussion (Section 4), Figures 1–6, Supplementary Tables S1–S4, Supplementary Data 1–2
  • Missing materials affecting confidence The actual supplementary material files (Supplementary Data 1–2, Tables S1–S4) are not provided; only their descriptions are visible. The Zenodo repository (record 20710876) is referenced but not accessible for verification. Figure contents are described but the figures themselves are not visible.

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment This manuscript presents a timely and practically useful benchmarking study of computational antibody modeling tools. The experimental design is generally sound, with careful attention to dataset construction, statistical testing, and multi-level evaluation. The finding that AlphaFold3 outperforms antibody-specific tools even for CDR-H3 prediction is noteworthy and challenges prevailing assumptions in the field. However, several methodological concerns reduce confidence in the conclusions: the dataset size (n=50) is modest for the number of comparisons performed, the exclusion of recently published methods (particularly DeepSCFold) limits the study's currency, and the interface recovery analysis is conditional on docking success in a way that may introduce selection bias. The manuscript would benefit from clearer justification of the dataset size, discussion of statistical power, and inclusion of additional methods or a clear rationale for their exclusion.

  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Computational antibody engineers, structural biologists working on therapeutic antibody design, and developers of protein structure prediction methods. The study provides practical guidance for tool selection across the antibody modeling workflow and identifies specific limitations (CDR-H3 accuracy, fine-grained interface prediction) that define clear targets for method development.

  • Major strengths 1. Controlled, statistically grounded comparison using paired statistical testing with multiple-testing correction, which is more rigorous than typical benchmarks in this field. 2. Multi-level evaluation spanning Fv structure, complex docking, and interface recovery, providing a complete workflow assessment rather than isolated metric comparisons. 3. Careful dataset construction with temporal separation from training data and sequence redundancy analysis, reducing the risk of training-test leakage. 4. Practical guidance for tool selection with clear identification of trade-offs (AF3 accuracy vs. ABB2/IgFold reproducibility and speed).

  • Major Concerns

Concern ID R1-M1 Severity Major Blocking No Axis Statistical rigor / dataset adequacy Claim pointer The study evaluates five tools on 50 complexes and reports statistically significant differences between methods. Evidence pointer Section 2.1, Section 2.5, Section 3.1–3.2 Concern The dataset of 50 complexes is modest for the number of comparisons performed (multiple tools, multiple metrics, multiple stratification levels). The authors use paired Wilcoxon tests with Benjamini-Hochberg correction, but do not report effect sizes or confidence intervals for the differences between methods beyond the bootstrap CIs for individual methods. For the CDR-H3 comparison (Section 3.1), the adjusted P-values are reported as 0.026 and 0.017, which are only marginally significant after correction. With n=50, the statistical power to detect moderate effect sizes is limited, and the study may be underpowered for some of the subgroup analyses (e.g., interface recovery stratified by DockQ threshold, where n=23 for the reliable docking subset). Why it matters Without adequate statistical power, the reported "significant" differences may be unreliable, and the absence of significant differences between ABB2 and IgFold (adjusted P=0.75) could reflect insufficient power rather than true equivalence. The study's conclusions about tool rankings depend on these statistical comparisons. Resolution test Provide a power analysis or justification that n=50 is sufficient for the planned comparisons. Report effect sizes (e.g., Cohen's d or rank-biserial correlation) alongside P-values for all pairwise comparisons. For the CDR-H3 comparison, consider whether the marginal significance (adjusted P=0.026, 0.017) supports the strong claim that "AF3 achieved the lowest CDR-H3 RMSD, significantly lower than both ABB2 and IgFold."

Concern ID R1-M2 Severity Major Blocking No Axis Methodological completeness / currency Claim pointer The study evaluates "current publicly available computational tools" and provides "practical guidance for tool selection in antibody-modeling workflows" (Section 5). Evidence pointer Section 2.4, Section 4.2, Section 5 Concern The study explicitly mentions DeepSCFold (Hou et al. 2025) in the Introduction and Discussion as a recently developed method that "was reported to achieve competitive interface DockQ relative to AF3 and a 12.4% improvement in prediction success rate at DockQ > 0.23," yet DeepSCFold is not included in the benchmark. The authors state this is because it was "not included in the predefined experimental framework" (Section 4.2). Given that the manuscript is being submitted in 2026 and DeepSCFold was published in 2025, its exclusion is a significant gap that undermines the claim of providing comprehensive practical guidance. The study also does not include other recent methods such as ABlooper, DeepAb, or ESMFold-based antibody predictors. Why it matters The practical value of the benchmark for tool selection is substantially reduced if a method that reportedly outperforms AF3 on the same task is not evaluated. Readers cannot determine whether AF3 remains the best choice or whether DeepSCFold (or other recent methods) would be preferable. Resolution test Either include DeepSCFold and other recent methods in the benchmark, or provide a clear, justified rationale for their exclusion (e.g., unavailability of public code, inability to run at scale, or fundamental incompatibility with the evaluation framework). If exclusion is justified, temper the claims about providing comprehensive guidance.

Concern ID R1-M3 Severity Major Blocking No Axis Selection bias / conditional analysis Claim pointer "When docking was reliable, AF3 accurately recovered epitope and paratope residues, salt bridges, and non-bonded contacts" (Abstract, Section 3.3). Evidence pointer Section 3.3, Figure 6 Concern The interface recovery analysis is restricted to the 23/50 complexes where AF3 achieved DockQ ≥ 0.49 (medium or high quality). This conditional analysis introduces potential selection bias: the 23 "successful" complexes may systematically differ from the 27 "failed" complexes in ways that affect interface recovery metrics. For example, complexes with simpler interfaces, less conformational flexibility, or more canonical binding modes may be both easier to dock and easier to analyze for interface features. The authors report that "when docking failed (DockQ < 0.23), the predicted and experimental contacts barely overlapped (mean residue-pair F1 = 0.04)," but this does not address whether the 23 successful cases are representative of antibody-antigen complexes generally. Why it matters The study's key practical conclusion—that AF3 is "well suited to identifying epitope and paratope regions in correctly docked complexes"—is based on a non-random subset of complexes. If the successful subset is biased toward easier cases, the reported interface recovery metrics may overestimate AF3's general performance. Resolution test Compare the characteristics of the 23 successful and 27 failed complexes (e.g., interface size, number of CDR contacts, antigen type, conformational change magnitude) to assess whether selection bias is present. Report interface recovery metrics for all 50 complexes (as is partially done in Figure 6A) and discuss how the conditional analysis affects generalizability.

Concern ID R1-M4 Severity Major Blocking No Axis Reproducibility / methodology transparency Claim pointer "Data, structural predictions, evaluation results, and analysis code are available from Zenodo under record 20710876" (Availability and implementation). Evidence pointer Data availability statement, Section 2.4.6 Concern The Zenodo repository is referenced but not accessible for verification. More critically, several methodological details that affect reproducibility are unclear: (1) For AF3, the authors used the "official AlphaFold3 web server" but do not specify which version or whether the server's model weights were updated during the study period. (2) For GRAMM, the "public GRAMM web server" was used in "free docking mode" but the specific version and parameter settings are not reported beyond this description. (3) For dyMEAN, "antigen epitope information used for dyMEAN input was obtained from HDOCK-based docking results" (Section 2.4.5), but the HDOCK parameters and how epitope information was extracted are not described. (4) The "five repeated local prediction runs" for ABB2 and IgFold used "different random seeds where seed control was available" (Section 2.4.6), but it is unclear which tools support seed control and what seeds were used. Why it matters Without precise methodological details, other researchers cannot reproduce the results or apply the same evaluation framework to new methods. The use of web servers (AF3, GRAMM) that may have changed between the study period and any attempted replication is particularly problematic. Resolution test Provide version numbers and specific parameters for all web servers and software tools. Describe the HDOCK procedure and epitope extraction method in sufficient detail for replication. Report whether seed control was available for each tool and what seeds were used. If possible, provide a frozen software environment (e.g., Docker container) for locally deployed tools.

Concern ID R1-m1 Severity Minor Axis Presentation / clarity Affected element Section 2.2.4 (DockQ description) Evidence pointer Section 2.2.4 Issue The description of DockQ computation for Fv prediction states "DockQ was computed for the heavy–light variable-domain interface," but the standard DockQ metric is designed for protein-protein complexes, not intra-complex domain interfaces. Concern ID R1-m4 Severity Minor Axis Interpretation / nuance Affected element Section 4.1 (Discussion) Evidence pointer Section 4.1 Issue The authors state that "AF3's strong performance on CDR-H3 is noteworthy because this hypervariable loop has traditionally been regarded as the regime in which antibody-specific models...would be expected to hold an advantage." However, they do not discuss the possibility that AF3's training data may include antibody structures that overlap with the test set, despite the temporal separation. The cutoff date (19 December 2023) ensures test structures were deposited after AF3's training cutoff, but AF3 may have been trained on other antibody structures that are structurally similar to the test set. Required correction Acknowledge the limitation that temporal separation does not guarantee structural non-redundancy with training data, and discuss whether any additional measures were taken to assess potential training-test similarity (e.g., structural clustering against known AF3 training structures).

Concern ID R1-m5 Severity Minor Axis Completeness Affected element Section 2.5 (Statistical analysis) Evidence pointer Section 2.5 Issue The authors use bootstrap resampling with 10,000 resamples to estimate 95% confidence intervals for group-level performance. However, they do not specify the bootstrap method (e.g., percentile, BCa, or basic bootstrap) or whether they accounted for the paired nature of the data in the bootstrap procedure. Required correction Specify the bootstrap method used and confirm that the paired structure of the data was preserved during resampling.
