Spatially Partitioned Engineering of an Alginate Lyase Overcomes the Stability-Activity Trade-Off via Distal Cap Loop Rigidification and Network Remodeling.

Zhao, Mengxuan; Zhao, Mengqi; Zhang, Peiyi; Fan, Liqiang; Li, Xu; Deng, Chen; Zhao, Liming
Journal of agricultural and food chemistry · 2026-10-28 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文通过空间分区半理性策略结合GRAPE方法改造海藻酸裂解酶，利用分子动力学模拟和残基相互作用网络分析揭示远端Cap Loop刚性化与网络重塑如何克服稳定性-活性权衡，虽对象为多糖裂解酶而非蛋白质结构核心，但其基于MD和网络分析的结构-功能计算策略可迁移至蛋白质工程。

摘要

The stability-activity trade-off limits engineering of alginate lyases for alginate oligosaccharide production. Here, a spatially partitioned semirational strategy coupled with the greedy accumulated strategy for protein engineering (GRAPE) was applied to an alginate lyase from Vibrio sp. The optimized variant M13 achieved a 4.6-fold increase in catalytic efficiency (kcat/Km) and an 11.4-fold extension of half-life at 50 degrees C. Molecular dynamics simulations indicated that distal Cap Loop rigidification suppresses thermal unfolding while Gate Loop flexibility is retained for catalysis. A67K reversion increased Cap Loop flexibility and shifted the optimal temperature from 40 to 30 degrees C, supporting its thermostability contribution. Residue interaction network analysis identified a Val272-Tyr260-Arg73 communication pathway associated with active site preorganization into a near-attack conformation. V272A reversion reduced kcat/Km by 45.7%, supporting its catalytic contribution. These findings show that spatial partitioning can concurrently improve thermostability and catalytic efficiency, providing a design principle for polysaccharide lyase engineering.

摘要翻译

稳定性-活性权衡限制了海藻酸裂解酶在海藻酸寡糖生产中的工程改造。本研究将一种空间分区半理性策略与蛋白质工程中的贪婪累积策略（GRAPE）相结合，应用于来自弧菌属（Vibrio sp.）的海藻酸裂解酶。优化后的变体M13实现了催化效率（kcat/Km）提高4.6倍，并在50°C下半衰期延长11.4倍。分子动力学（molecular dynamics）模拟表明，远端Cap Loop的刚性化抑制了热解折叠，而Gate Loop的柔性得以保留以维持催化功能。A67K回复突变增加了Cap Loop的柔性，并将最适温度从40°C降至30°C，支持了其对热稳定性的贡献。残基相互作用网络分析识别出一条Val272-Tyr260-Arg73通讯通路，该通路与活性位点预组织为近攻击构象相关。V272A回复突变使kcat/Km降低45.7%，支持了其催化贡献。这些发现表明，空间分区可同时提高热稳定性和催化效率，为多糖裂解酶工程提供了设计原则。

06 核心思想

1) 表面方法：对 Vibrio sp. 来源 alginate lyase 施加空间分区的半理性突变设计，并用 GRAPE 累积有利突变，得到 M13。 2) 核心洞察：稳定性与活性可由空间上分离的结构区域分别承担——远端 Cap Loop 刚性化抑制热去折叠，而 Gate Loop 保持柔性以维持催化；同时残基相互作用网络（V272-Y260-R73）重塑使活性位点预组织为 near-attack conformation。 3) 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：对「远端、非活性位点」区域做刚性化，可能在不牺牲活性位点柔性的前提下提升稳定性；「空间分区」可作为缓解 stability-activity trade-off 的可迁移设计原则。
