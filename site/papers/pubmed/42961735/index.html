Fine-Tuning a Protein Language Model for Epitope Library Generation and Sequence Filtering.

Flechas Manrique, Natalia; Martinez Diez, Alberto; Lopez-Martinez, Elena; Andrea, Luc; Cortajarena, Aitziber L; Espinosa-Portales, Llorenc; Manteca, Aitor
Methods in molecular biology (Clifton, N.J.) · 2026-10-30 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文微调蛋白语言模型生成线性表位肽库并用Transformer分类器过滤，虽聚焦肽段序列而非完整蛋白结构，但生成与筛选框架可迁移至蛋白质序列设计，与结构计算课题部分相关。

摘要

Linear peptide epitopes play a key role in vaccines, immunotherapies, and diagnostics, yet discovering them remains difficult because peptide sequence space grows exponentially and experimentally confirmed epitopes are relatively few. This chapter presents a computational framework for the generation of synthetic linear epitope libraries that reproduce key statistical and sequence-level properties of naturally occurring epitopes. A large language model pre-trained on protein sequences and subsequently fine-tuned on curated epitope datasets is employed to generate biologically plausible peptide candidates beyond those observed experimentally. To further enrich functional relevance of the generated sequences, they are filtered using transformer-based classification models trained on viral and bacterial epitope datasets. These models are applied as discriminative tools to distinguish likely epitopes from non-epitopes rather than to infer pathogen origin. The resulting libraries are designed to be compatible with high-throughput experimental screening platforms, such as droplet microfluidics, enabling efficient integration of generative artificial intelligence with experimental immunology workflows.

摘要翻译

线性肽表位在疫苗、免疫疗法和诊断中扮演关键角色，然而由于肽序列空间呈指数增长且实验确认的表位相对稀少，发现它们仍然困难。本章介绍了一个计算框架，用于生成合成线性表位文库，该文库再现天然表位的关键统计和序列级特性。采用一个在蛋白质序列上预训练并随后在精选表位数据集上微调的大型语言模型，以生成超出实验观察范围的生物学上合理的肽候选物。为进一步增强生成序列的功能相关性，使用基于Transformer的分类模型（在病毒和细菌表位数据集上训练）对其进行过滤。这些模型被用作判别工具，以区分可能的表位与非表位，而非推断病原体来源。所得文库设计为与高通量实验筛选平台（如液滴微流控）兼容，从而实现生成式人工智能与实验免疫学工作流程的高效整合。
