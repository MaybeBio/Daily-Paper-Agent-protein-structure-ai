Unveiling Novel Metalloprotease Inhibitors Targeting Botulinum Neurotoxin Through Structure-Based Virtual Screening of Drug Libraries and Molecular Dynamics Simulations.

Sulaimon, Ridwan; Selvaraj, Gurudeeban; Chan, Nora W C; Hu, Anguang; Peslherbe, Gilles H
International journal of molecular sciences · 2026-09-26 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文通过结构虚拟筛选和分子动力学模拟，从FDA药物库中识别肉毒杆菌神经毒素金属蛋白酶抑制剂，属于蛋白质结构相关的对接与MD计算研究，但对象为毒素蛋白而非典型结构建模方法创新。

摘要

Botulinum neurotoxin (BoNT) is one of the most lethal biological substances to humans, which inhibits acetylcholine release by the presynaptic nerve in neuromuscular junctions. Of the existing BoNT serotypes, BoNT serotype A (BoNT/A) is particularly potent, making it the primary focus in neurotoxin research. Its catalytic domain exhibits similar structural features and zinc-dependent activity as thermolysin, a key bacterial enzyme, which provides a foundation for designing antibacterial agents targeting related protease mechanisms. Repurposing of preapproved drugs or existing medications has recently proven an effective strategy to accelerate drug discovery. Accordingly, we employed drug-likeness screening, quantitative estimation of drug-likeness (QED), and molecular docking to screen about 9000 ligands from the FDA-preapproved drug library using the known crystal structures of the toxin's light chain, and we identified potential inhibitors with the highest binding affinity. We further refined our selection using molecular dynamics simulations to investigate the stability of the receptor-ligand complexes. The binding mode analysis and binding free energies of the receptor-ligand complexes provide crucial information about the mechanism of action of our top-ranked potential inhibitors. Notably, 16 ligands exhibit a binding affinity greater (in magnitude) than that of the hydroxamate inhibitors that are co-crystallized in the X-ray structure. Most of these ligands contain fluorine, carboxylic and phosphate moieties as key functional groups that enhance their interactions with key residues of the BoNT active site. Our results suggest that dinoprost and 15 other clinically investigated ligands may serve as candidate scaffolds for further evaluation as potential BoNT/A LC inhibitors, pending experimental validation.

摘要翻译

肉毒杆菌神经毒素（BoNT）是人类已知最致命的生物物质之一，它通过抑制神经肌肉接头处突触前神经释放乙酰胆碱而发挥作用。在现有的BoNT血清型中，BoNT血清型A（BoNT/A）尤其高效，使其成为神经毒素研究的重点。其催化结构域展现出与关键细菌酶热溶素（thermolysin）相似的结构特征和锌依赖性活性，这为设计针对相关蛋白酶机制的抗菌剂提供了基础。近期，重新利用已批准药物或现有药物已被证明是加速药物发现的有效策略。因此，我们采用药物相似性筛选、药物相似性定量估计（QED）和分子对接（molecular docking），利用毒素轻链的已知晶体结构，从FDA预批准药物库中筛选了约9000个配体，并识别出具有最高结合亲和力的潜在抑制剂。我们进一步通过分子动力学（molecular dynamics）模拟来研究受体-配体复合物的稳定性，以优化选择。受体-配体复合物的结合模式分析和结合自由能为我们排名靠前的潜在抑制剂的作用机制提供了关键信息。值得注意的是，有16个配体的结合亲和力（绝对值）大于X射线结构中共结晶的羟肟酸抑制剂。这些配体大多含有氟、羧酸和磷酸基团作为关键官能团，增强了它们与BoNT活性位点关键残基的相互作用。我们的结果表明，地诺前列素（dinoprost）及其他15种临床研究中的配体可能作为候选骨架，用于进一步评估其作为潜在BoNT/A LC抑制剂的潜力，有待实验验证。

该文通过药物相似性筛选、QED 评估和分子对接，从约 9000 个 FDA 预批准药物中筛选出 16 个结合亲和力优于共晶羟肟酸抑制剂的候选配体，并用 MD 模拟验证了受体-配体复合物的稳定性，提出 dinoprost 等可作为 BoNT/A 轻链抑制剂的候选骨架，但尚未经实验验证。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：如何从现有 FDA 预批准药物库中快速识别 BoNT/A 轻链（LC）金属蛋白酶的新型抑制剂？
  • 为什么重要：BoNT/A 是已知最致命的生物毒素之一，目前缺乏有效的小分子解毒剂；药物重定位可大幅缩短开发周期和成本。
  • 现有方法为何不足：传统 de novo 药物设计耗时且成本高；已有抑制剂（如羟肟酸类）虽有效但选择性/成药性不足；实验筛选通量低。
  • 精确研究问题：Can structure-based virtual screening of FDA-approved drug libraries, combined with MD simulations, identify stable and high-affinity BoNT/A LC inhibitors that outperform co-crystallized hydroxamate inhibitors in silico?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：多级计算筛选流程——drug-likeness 过滤 → QED 评分 → 分子对接 → MD 模拟稳定性验证 → 结合自由能分析。

14 学到什么

Agent 提炼的知识候选： 1. 多级筛选策略：drug-likeness → QED → docking → MD 的级联流程可有效降低假阳性率，可迁移至其他金属蛋白酶靶点（如 MMP、ADAM 等）的抑制剂发现。 2. Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a plausible computational workflow for identifying potential BoNT/A light chain inhibitors through drug repurposing. The methodological sequence of drug-likeness screening, QED, docking, and molecular dynamics is standard and appropriate for the stated goal. However, the abstract lacks quantitative results, detailed methodological transparency, and any form of experimental validation, which severely limits the ability to assess the robustness and significance of the claims. The central claim that 16 ligands outperform co-crystallized hydroxamate inhibitors is presented without supporting numerical data or statistical context. The conclusion that these compounds "may serve as candidate scaffolds" is appropriately cautious, but the evidence base is insufficient to establish the case from the supplied material.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers in toxinology, drug repurposing, and computational drug discovery would find the approach relevant. Those working on botulinum neurotoxin countermeasures, particularly medical countermeasure development for biodefense, would be interested in potential new inhibitor scaffolds. Computational chemists focused on metalloprotease inhibition and structure-based virtual screening may also find the methodological framework of interest, though the lack of experimental validation limits broader appeal.
  • Major strengths The study addresses a clinically and biodefense-relevant target with a practical drug repurposing strategy. The use of FDA-preapproved drugs as a screening library is a pragmatic approach that could accelerate translation if validated. The combination of multiple computational filters, including docking and molecular dynamics, represents a reasonable tiered screening strategy. The identification of specific functional groups, such as fluorine, carboxylic, and phosphate moieties, that enhance active site interactions provides chemically actionable information.
