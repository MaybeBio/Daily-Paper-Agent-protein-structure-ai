Chiral nanoengineering and biosensors: from construction to selective recognition, and emerging detection applications.

Liu, Menglong; Li, Peizhi; Yang, En; Simao, Janine Magalhaes Guedes; Guo, Fengxian; Zhu, Jian; Peng, Chifang; Ma, Wei
Materials horizons · 2026-09-16 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该综述聚焦手性纳米材料的构建及其在生物传感器中的应用，虽涉及三维结构和分子识别，但核心对象为纳米材料而非蛋白质结构，且未涉及蛋白质结构计算建模或AI/物理模拟方法，与课题关联微弱。

摘要

Chiral nanomaterials have become an important platform for constructing high-sensitivity biosensors due to their unique three-dimensional (3D) structure, tunable optical activity, and excellent molecular recognition ability. Through chiral ligand modification, template induction, intrinsic structure design, and other strategies, their chiral optical response and biological interface recognition performance can be significantly enhanced, providing efficient signal conversion, high stability, and good biocompatibility in complex environments. This review systematically summarizes the construction strategies of chiral nanomaterials and clarifies their optical responses such as circular dichroism (CD), plasmon resonance, fluorescence (FL), Raman, and electrochemical and mass transfer separation mechanisms. The application of these materials in the detection of biomarkers, such as drug residues, metabolites, amino acids, and ions, is further discussed, and a design strategy for multi-modal integration and intelligent response sensors is introduced. Finally, in view of the challenges regarding chiral stability, synthesis uniformity, biocompatibility and equipment integration, the development directions of cross-scale theoretical simulation, green synthesis and intelligent response design are proposed to promote chiral nanobiosensors from basic research to clinical and environmental practical applications.

摘要翻译

手性纳米材料因其独特的三维（3D）结构、可调光学活性及优异的分子识别能力，已成为构建高灵敏度生物传感器的重要平台。通过手性配体修饰、模板诱导、内在结构设计等策略，可显著增强其手性光学响应和生物界面识别性能，在复杂环境中提供高效信号转换、高稳定性和良好生物相容性。本综述系统总结了手性纳米材料的构建策略，并阐明了其圆二色（CD）、等离子体共振、荧光（FL）、拉曼等光学响应及电化学和传质分离机制。进一步讨论了这些材料在药物残留、代谢物、氨基酸和离子等生物标志物检测中的应用，并介绍了多模态集成和智能响应传感器的设计策略。最后，针对手性稳定性、合成均匀性、生物相容性和设备集成方面的挑战，提出了跨尺度理论模拟、绿色合成和智能响应设计的发展方向，以推动手性纳米生物传感器从基础研究走向临床和环境实际应用。
