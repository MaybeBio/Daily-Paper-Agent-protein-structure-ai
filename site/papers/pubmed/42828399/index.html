Rational Immunogenicity Modulation of Virus-Like Particles by VLPIM.

Wang, Chufan; Chen, Yeyun; Wang, Ming-Wei; Ren, Lei; Zhou, Qingtong
Computational and structural biotechnology journal · 2026-10-04 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文提出VLPIM计算框架，整合AI蛋白设计、结构预测和免疫原性预测工具，对病毒样颗粒进行理性免疫调节，虽对象为VLP多聚体而非单体蛋白，但其结构-序列优化和虚拟筛选流程可迁移至蛋白质结构计算设计，具有方法学启发。

摘要

Virus-like particles (VLPs) are versatile platforms for drug delivery and vaccine development, but precisely customizing their immune profiles for specific clinical applications remains challenging. Here, we present a computational framework that integrates complementary artificial-intelligence-driven tools for protein design, structure prediction, and antigenicity assessment to enable rational immunogenicity modulation of VLPs while preserving their structural integrity. Leveraging the central role of CD4(+) T cells in adaptive immunity, this framework first combines class II major histocompatibility complex (MHC-II)-restricted epitope prediction with structure-guided sequence optimization to generate a focused mutation library. Subsequently, its virtual screening workflow combines immunogenicity predictions and structural modeling to pinpoint variants likely to attenuate or enhance CD4(+) T cell activation while preserving VLP integrity. This framework enables a broader and more efficient exploration of the mutation space to identify VLP structural variants with tailored immunogenicity profiles; hence, we term it the VLPIM (Virus-Like Particle Immunogenicity Modulation) platform. Using hepatitis B core VLPs as proof of concept, VLPIM generated a mutation library of 120 variants, computationally filtered down to 4 candidates, and ultimately identified 2 variants that retained native virus-like morphology, as confirmed by transmission electron microscopy. In vitro immunogenicity assays confirmed that epitope substitutions could either suppress or enhance CD4(+) T cell activation. This generalizable pipeline permits precise immunogenicity modulation of VLPs and other multimers, accelerating their development as targeted drug carriers and next-generation vaccine platforms. VLPIM is available as both a locally executable tool on GitHub (https://github.com/RuijinHospitalVNAR/VLPIM) and an online web server (https://ruijinhospitalvnar.github.io/Toolboxes/VLPIM_Web_services/).

摘要翻译

病毒样颗粒（VLPs）是多功能平台，用于药物递送和疫苗开发，但精确定制其免疫特征以适用于特定临床应用仍具挑战性。在此，我们提出一个计算框架，整合了互补的人工智能驱动工具，用于蛋白质设计、结构预测和抗原性评估，以实现对VLPs的合理免疫原性调节，同时保持其结构完整性。利用CD4(+) T细胞在适应性免疫中的核心作用，该框架首先将II类主要组织相容性复合体（MHC-II）限制性表位预测与结构引导的序列优化相结合，生成聚焦突变文库。随后，其虚拟筛选流程结合免疫原性预测和结构建模，以识别可能减弱或增强CD4(+) T细胞激活同时保持VLP完整性的变体。该框架能够更广泛且高效地探索突变空间，以识别具有定制免疫原性特征的VLP结构变体；因此，我们将其命名为VLPIM（病毒样颗粒免疫原性调节）平台。使用乙型肝炎核心VLPs作为概念验证，VLPIM生成了包含120个变体的突变文库，经计算筛选至4个候选，最终鉴定出2个保留天然病毒样形态的变体，并通过透射电子显微镜确认。体外免疫原性测定证实，表位替换可抑制或增强CD4(+) T细胞激活。这一通用流程允许对VLPs及其他多聚体进行精确免疫原性调节，加速其作为靶向药物载体和下一代疫苗平台的开发。VLPIM可作为本地可执行工具在GitHub（https://github.com/RuijinHospitalVNAR/VLPIM）和在线网络服务器（https://ruijinhospitalvnar.github.io/Toolboxes/VLPIM_Web_services/）上获取。

02 一句话总结

开发了一个名为 VLPIM 的计算平台，通过 NetMHCIIpan 识别 CD4⁺ T 细胞表位、ProteinMPNN 生成突变库、AlphaFold3 预测多聚体结构、Rosetta 评估界面稳定性，实现了对乙肝核心蛋白（HBc）VLP 免疫原性的双向调控（增强或减弱），并湿实验验证了 4 个突变体中 2 个成功组装为完整 VLP。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：如何在保持 VLP 多聚体组装完整性的前提下，精确调控其免疫原性（增强或减弱）？
  • 为什么重要：VLP 是疫苗开发和药物递送的重要平台，但其固有免疫原性可能干扰特定应用——疫苗需要增强免疫原性，而药物递送需要降低免疫原性以避免清除。现有方法缺乏系统性的计算框架。
  • 现有方法不足：(1) 传统去免疫化方法主要针对单体蛋白（如抗体人源化），不适用于多聚体 VLP；(2) 非人源蛋白缺乏对应的人源序列空间，无法用序列相似性引导；(3) 现有方法多为单向调控（仅降低免疫原性），缺乏双向调控能力；(4) 未显式考虑多聚体组装界面的结构约束。
  • 精确研究问题：Can a computational pipeline integrating epitope prediction, inverse folding, and multimer-aware structure prediction enable rational bidirectional modulation of VLP immunogenicity while preserving capsid assembly?

06 核心思想

1) 表面方法

一个四步流水线：IEDB/NetMHCIIpan 表位识别 → ProteinMPNN 逆折叠生成突变库 → NetMHCIIpan 再评估免疫原性变化 → AlphaFold3 多聚体结构预测 + Rosetta 界面能量评估筛选候选。

2) 核心洞察

多聚体感知的免疫原性工程：VLP 的免疫原性调控不能仅在单体水平评估，必须同时考虑 (a) 表位序列改变对 MHC-II 结合的影响和 (b) 突变对多亚基组装界面的影响。作者将这两个维度整合为一个可计算的筛选流程，并引入双向设计模式（增强/减弱）。

3) 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]

  • 工具集成 > 单一模型：VLPIM 没有开发新模型，而是将现有最优工具（NetMHCIIpan、ProteinMPNN、AF3、Rosetta）按逻辑串联，这提示在蛋白质工程中，流程设计与模型选择同等重要。
  • 结构约束是免疫工程的隐形瓶颈：传统去免疫化关注序列层面，但多聚体系统中结构约束可能比免疫原性本身更严格——PMD-05 和 PMI-03 的失败即源于组装破坏而非免疫原性预测错误。
  • 双向设计验证了可调性：同一框架既能增强也能减弱免疫原性，说明免疫原性不是固定属性，而是可连续调节的设计变量。

07 方法总览

输入：VLP 蛋白序列（FASTA）、VLP 结构（PDB/CIF，可选）、设计模式（reduce/enhance）、NetMHCIIpan 等参数

输出：候选突变序列列表、免疫原性评分（Sim score）、AF3 预测结构、Rosetta 界面能量指标、排序后的推荐候选

模块： 1. 表位识别：IEDB 查询 + NetMHCIIpan 预测，识别 MHC-II 限制性 CD4⁺ T 细胞表位 2. 突变库生成：ProteinMPNN 逆折叠，固定非表位区域，对表位区域采样突变 3. 免疫原性再评估：NetMHCIIpan 预测突变后肽段结合亲和力，计算 Sim score 4. 结构筛选：AF3 预测多聚体结构 → RMSD 对齐 → Rosetta 界面分析（dG/dSASA、Packstat、BUNS）

流程：

序列 → 表位识别 → ProteinMPNN 突变库 → NetMHCIIpan 评分 → Top 10 → AF3 多聚体预测 → RMSD + Rosetta 筛选 → Top 3-4 → 湿实验验证

训练：无新模型训练，全部使用预训练工具

假设： - NetMHCIIpan 预测的 MHC-II 结合亲和力可作为免疫原性的代理指标 - AF3 预测的多聚体结构可用于评估组装可行性 - Rosetta 界面能量指标可反映突变对组装稳定性的影响

09 关键公式符号

Sim score（Eq. 1）：

$$S_{im} = \begin{cases} \sum_i \frac{100 - x_i - x_{min}}{x_{max} - x_{min}} \times 100 & \text{if mode = reduce} \ \sum_i \frac{x_i - x_{min}}{x_{max} - x_{min}} \times 100 & \text{if mode = enhance} \end{cases}$$

  • $S_{im}$：免疫原性评分（Sim score），越低表示免疫原性调控效果越好
  • $i$：HLA 等位基因索引（推荐 8 个常见等位基因：HLA-DRB101:01, 04:01, 07:01, 08:01, 13:01, 15:01 等）
  • $x_i$：候选突变序列在 HLA 等位基因 $i$ 下的 IC50 结合亲和力排名
  • $x_{min}, x_{max}$：所有候选序列中 IC50 排名的最小值和最大值

直觉：将每个等位基因下的 IC50 排名归一化到 0-100，reduce 模式反转（排名高=弱结合=得分高），enhance 模式直接求和（排名低=强结合=得分高）。最终得分越低，表示调控效果越好。

其他指标： - RMSD：Cα 原子均方根偏差，通过 Kabsch 算法 + 最多 5 轮离群值剔除（>2.0 Å）计算 - pLDDT：AlphaFold3 预测的局部距离差异测试分数 - pTM/ipTM：模板建模分数/链间预测 TM 分数 - dG/dSASA：结合自由能变化/溶剂可及表面积 - Packstat：界面堆积质量 - BUNS：埋藏不饱和氢键数

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围：本文仅验证了 HBc VLP 一个系统，且突变位点集中在两个表位区域。VLPIM 的通用性（"scaffold-agnostic"）是作者主张，但未在其它 VLP 系统上验证。
  • oracle 输入：NetMHCIIpan 预测的 IC50 是免疫原性的代理指标，实际免疫应答受更多因素影响（作者在 Discussion 中承认）。AF3 对多聚体的预测精度有限（作者引用文献 [31] 承认）。
  • 端到端状态：计算 → 湿实验闭环已跑通，但湿实验规模小（4 个突变体、6 个供者），统计功效有限。
  • 算力成本：未提供具体算力需求。AF3 预测多聚体结构计算成本较高，但作者未量化。
  • 历史数据依赖：依赖 IEDB 实验数据和 NetMHCIIpan 训练数据，对新 MHC-II 等位基因或非人源 MHC 的泛化能力未知。
  • 模型依赖：结果高度依赖 NetMHCIIpan 和 AF3 的预测精度。若这些模型更新，VLPIM 流程可能需要重新校准。
  • 最难情形：作者未讨论对低稳定性 VLP 或复杂多聚体（如二十面体）的适用性。HBc VLP 是相对简单的 T=4 二十面体。
  • 群体/领域边界：PBMC 来自 6 名健康供者，未覆盖不同 HLA 背景、疾病状态或年龄群体。未进行 HLA 分型，无法分析等位基因特异性效应。
  • 不确定性：IL-2 分泌的定量结果存在较大供者间变异（如 PMI-01 增强倍数 2.0-5.3 倍），作者未报告置信区间或统计显著性检验。

有边界的复述：VLPIM 在 HBc VLP 系统上，通过整合 NetMHCIIpan、ProteinMPNN、AF3 和 Rosetta，成功设计出 4 个候选突变体中的 2 个（PMD-02 和 PMI-01），分别实现免疫原性降低和增强，且保持 VLP 组装能力。该流程的通用性、预测精度上限和临床相关性仍需更大规模验证。

14 学到什么

标题：Agent 提炼的知识候选

可迁移概念

  1. 多聚体感知的序列设计：在 VLP 等寡聚蛋白中，序列设计必须同时考虑单体折叠和多亚基组装。VLPIM 将 AF3 多聚体预测 + Rosetta 界面分析作为结构筛选层，这一思路可直接迁移到其他多聚体蛋白设计任务（如设计稳定的蛋白纳米颗粒、病毒衣壳修饰）。
  2. 双向免疫调控框架：同一平台支持免疫原性增强和减弱，通过 Sim score 的 mode 参数切换。这种"可调旋钮"设计理念可迁移到其他免疫相关蛋白工程（如细胞因子、抗体 Fc 工程）。
  3. 计算-实验闭环验证：计算筛选 → 湿实验验证 → 反馈优化。虽然本文未做迭代优化，但流程设计为闭环，可迁移到其他蛋白设计项目。

可迁移方法

  1. Sim score 归一化策略：将多等位基因预测结果通过 min-max 归一化聚合为单一分数，支持模式切换。这种"多维度评分聚合"方法可迁移到其他需要综合多个预测指标的筛选任务。
  2. ProteinMPNN 位点掩码策略：固定非表位区域、仅对表位区域采样，在保持整体结构的同时探索局部序列空间。可迁移到其他"局部序列优化"任务（如酶活性位点改造、抗原表位工程）。
  3. AF3 + Rosetta 组合筛选：AF3 提供结构预测，Rosetta 提供界面能量指标（dG/dSASA、Packstat、BUNS），两者互补。这种"预测 + 能量评估"的组合策略可迁移到其他多聚体设计任务。

可迁移实验设计

  1. 配对供者设计：6 个供者、每供者 4 个技术重复的 PBMC 实验设计，适合小样本免疫学评估。
  2. 多维度验证：形态（TEM）+ 稳定性（DSF/DSC）+ 功能（IL-2）三层次验证，全面评估突变体质量。

对课题方向的启示

  • 结构预测工具的边界：AF3 对多聚体的预测精度有限（pTM/ipTM 仅 ~0.3-0.4），提示在类似任务中需要谨慎使用多聚体预测结果，或结合实验约束（如交联质谱、SAXS）。
  • 免疫原性预测的局限：NetMHCIIpan 预测结合亲和力 ≠ 实际免疫原性，需要实验验证。这提示在计算免疫工程中，预测工具应作为筛选器而非最终裁决者。
  • 序列-结构-功能三角验证：VLPIM 的成功在于将序列变化（ProteinMPNN）、结构验证（AF3/Rosetta）和功能验证（IL-2）三者串联，这种三角验证策略是计算蛋白设计的通用范式。

审稿人评审

Review setup

  • Input scope Full manuscript text including abstract, introduction, methods, results, discussion, conclusion, and data availability statements
  • Assessment boundary Scientific validity, technical soundness, methodological rigor, and claims substantiation based solely on the provided manuscript content
  • Shared manuscript claim summary The authors present VLPIM, a computational pipeline integrating NetMHCIIpan, ProteinMPNN, AlphaFold3, and Rosetta to modulate CD4+ T cell epitope immunogenicity in virus-like particles while preserving structural integrity, with experimental validation on hepatitis B core VLPs demonstrating both immunogenicity reduction and enhancement
  • Visible evidence base In silico predictions (NetMHCIIpan IC50 values, AF3 structural models, Rosetta interface metrics), SDS-PAGE, TEM, DSF, DSC, and IL-2 ELISA from human PBMC assays
  • Missing materials affecting confidence Supplementary figures and tables referenced but not provided (Fig. S1, S2, Table S1), raw numerical data for IC50 predictions, statistical analysis details, HLA typing data, negative controls for immunogenicity assays, and quantitative TEM particle size distributions

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The manuscript describes a potentially useful computational workflow for modulating VLP immunogenicity, with a logical integration of established tools. The core concept of bidirectional immunogenicity modulation in multimeric assemblies addresses a genuine gap. However, the experimental validation is limited in scope and depth, with only two successfully assembled mutants characterized immunologically. The claim of "precise" immunogenicity modulation is not fully supported by the data presented, particularly given the small number of validated variants and the absence of mechanistic experiments demonstrating MHC-II-restricted presentation. The manuscript would benefit from more comprehensive validation, including quantitative dose-response analyses, additional structural characterization, and clearer evidence linking predicted epitope changes to observed T cell responses.

  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Vaccine developers and researchers in protein engineering would find this workflow relevant, as VLP immunogenicity tuning is a practical challenge in vaccine design and drug delivery. Computational biologists working on immunogenicity prediction and deimmunization of therapeutic proteins may also be interested in the integrated pipeline. The web server and standalone tool availability increases potential utility for applied researchers without deep computational expertise.

  • Major strengths 1. Addresses a relevant problem: immunogenicity modulation of multimeric assemblies, which is underexplored compared to monomeric proteins 2. Integrates multiple state-of-the-art computational tools into a coherent, accessible pipeline 3. Bidirectional design capability (both reduction and enhancement) is a useful innovation 4. Experimental validation, while limited, does demonstrate proof of concept with two successfully assembled mutants 5. The multimer-aware structural filtering strategy is a thoughtful consideration often missing in deimmunization approaches

  • Major Concerns

  • Concern ID R1-M1

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Evidence sufficiency
  • Claim pointer "VLPIM permits precise immunogenicity modulation of VLPs and other multimers"
  • Evidence pointer Results section, Fig. 6K
  • Concern The claim of "precise" modulation is based on IL-2 secretion data from only 6 donors, with no dose-response curves, no time-course analysis, and no assessment of whether observed differences are statistically significant. The IL-2 data show substantial donor variability, with PMD-02 showing lower secretion than WT in some donors but the manuscript does not report statistical testing. Furthermore, only 2 of 4 designed mutants successfully assembled into VLPs, and only these 2 were tested immunologically. This limited dataset does not support the strong claim of precise modulation.
  • Why it matters The central conclusion of the paper rests on the demonstration that the computational predictions translate into predictable immunological outcomes. Without robust statistical analysis and a larger validation set, the precision of the approach cannot be established. The 50% success rate for assembly competence, while acknowledged, also tempers the claim of reliable structural preservation.

  • Resolution test Provide statistical analysis of IL-2 data (e.g., mixed-effects models accounting for donor variability), include dose-response experiments, and either increase the number of validated mutants or temper the language to reflect proof-of-concept rather than established precision.

  • Concern ID R1-M2

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Mechanistic evidence
  • Claim pointer "epitope substitutions could either suppress or enhance CD4+ T cell activation"
  • Evidence pointer Results section, Fig. 6K
  • Concern The manuscript attributes observed IL-2 differences to altered CD4+ T cell epitope recognition, but no direct evidence for MHC-II-restricted presentation is provided. IL-2 secretion from PBMCs could reflect multiple mechanisms, including differential uptake, processing, or innate immune activation. The discussion acknowledges this limitation but does not resolve it. The claim that reduced structural stability of PMD-02 would facilitate degradation and therefore the reduced IL-2 is due to epitope changes is speculative and not directly tested.
  • Why it matters The mechanistic interpretation is central to the claim that the approach modulates immunogenicity through epitope-specific effects. Without blocking antibodies against MHC-II, CD4-depletion experiments, or antigen-presentation assays, alternative explanations cannot be excluded. This weakens the scientific foundation for the proposed mechanism.

  • Resolution test Include MHC-II blocking experiments, CD4+ T cell depletion or enrichment assays, or use antigen-presenting cells with defined HLA types to demonstrate epitope-specific effects. Alternatively, present data showing that the mutated epitopes are no longer recognized by epitope-specific T cell lines or clones.

  • Concern ID R1-M3

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking No
  • Axis Structural validation
  • Claim pointer "VLPIM generated VLP variants that preserved the native virus-like morphology"
  • Evidence pointer Results section, Fig. 6A to D
  • Concern TEM images are shown for the assembled mutants, but quantitative analysis of particle size distribution is limited. PMD-02 shows a broader size distribution around 50 nm compared to WT at approximately 28 nm, which is a substantial difference. The manuscript does not discuss whether this size difference affects the validity of comparing these particles as "native-like" or whether the larger size reflects aberrant assembly. No cryo-EM or other high-resolution structural validation is provided.
  • Why it matters The claim of preserved native morphology is important for the utility of the approach. A 22 nm difference in diameter is significant and may affect immunogenicity through altered antigen display density or particle geometry. Without more detailed structural characterization, the relationship between the computational structural predictions and actual assembled particles remains unclear.

  • Resolution test Provide quantitative particle size analysis with statistical comparison to WT, and ideally higher-resolution structural data (cryo-EM or negative-stain EM with 2D class averaging) to confirm icosahedral symmetry and T-number preservation.

  • Concern ID R1-M4

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking No
  • Axis Generalizability
  • Claim pointer "VLPIM permits precise immunogenicity modulation of VLPs and other multimers"
  • Evidence pointer Discussion section
  • Concern The claim of generalizability to "other multimers" is not supported by any data. The workflow was applied to a single VLP system (HBc) with a limited set of mutations. The scaffold-agnostic claim is plausible given the tool integration, but no evidence is presented for application to other systems. The discussion acknowledges this but the abstract and conclusion state it as a capability.
  • Why it matters Overstating generalizability without evidence could mislead potential users. The performance of the pipeline on other scaffolds with different symmetries, sizes, or assembly mechanisms is unknown. The claim should be framed as a design principle rather than an established capability.

  • Resolution test Either provide additional examples of application to other VLP systems or multimeric proteins, or revise the language to indicate that the framework is designed to be scaffold-agnostic but has only been validated on HBc VLPs to date.
