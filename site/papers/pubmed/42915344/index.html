Conformational Dynamics and Ligand Binding Kinetics of New Delhi Metallo-beta-Lactamase-1 (NDM-1): A Combined Conventional Molecular Dynamics and Ligand Gaussian Accelerated Molecular Dynamics (LiGaMD) Simulation Study.

Shamshad, Sharoon; Ahmed, Ayaz; Moin, Syed Tarique
The journal of physical chemistry. B · 2026-10-23 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文结合常规MD和LiGaMD模拟研究NDM-1与抑制剂复合物的构象动态和结合动力学，属于蛋白质结构相关的计算模拟研究，方法创新且直接服务于抑制剂设计。

摘要

New Delhi metallo-beta-lactamase-1 (NDM-1) is an enzyme responsible for antimicrobial resistance against all major classes of antibiotics, posing a serious health threat to worldwide healthcare systems. Understanding its structural and dynamical properties due to ligand binding will be helpful in pursuing the development of effective inhibitors. For these purposes, conventional molecular dynamics simulations have been successfully applied to evaluate the structural and dynamical properties of free NDM-1 and its three distinct complexes with SFC, SPC, and CAP inhibitors in terms of root mean square deviation (RMSD), root mean square fluctuation (RMSF), MM-PBSA-based free energy, protein-ligand interaction fingerprints, and ligand network analysis. Comparative analyses have also been performed to characterize changes in the conformational landscape of NDM-1 due to inhibitor binding, as a distant loop L7, was observed to be perturbed by the Zn-binding residue His-165, thus suggesting a potential allosteric site. Furthermore, LiGaMD simulations have been applied to these three complexes in the form of a replica that enabled sampling of rare conformational states and provided insights into ligand binding and unbinding kinetics. Based on LiGaMD simulations, the loop L7 was also highlighted as a transiently accessible binding region; however, further investigation was essentially required to establish this site as a potential binding site. Among these complexes, the SFC/NDM-1 complex was found to be the most optimal one, as deduced based on the most favorable balance of binding thermodynamics and kinetics, suggesting its further evaluation to establish it as a lead scaffold. These findings from the study, therefore, provide a detailed understanding of the mechanistic and kinetic features of NDM-1 and may be implicated in the rational design of novel inhibitors targeting the clinically significant NDM-1 enzyme responsible for broad antibiotic resistance.

摘要翻译

新德里金属-β-内酰胺酶-1（NDM-1）是一种导致对所有主要类别抗生素产生抗药性的酶，对全球医疗系统构成严重健康威胁。理解配体结合引起的其结构和动力学特性，将有助于推动有效抑制剂的开发。为此，常规分子动力学（molecular dynamics）模拟已成功应用于评估游离NDM-1及其与SFC、SPC和CAP抑制剂形成的三种不同复合物的结构和动力学特性，包括均方根偏差（RMSD）、均方根波动（RMSF）、基于MM-PBSA的自由能、蛋白质-配体相互作用指纹图谱以及配体网络分析。还进行了比较分析，以表征抑制剂结合引起的NDM-1构象景观变化，观察到远端环L7受到锌结合残基His-165的扰动，从而提示一个潜在别构位点。此外，LiGaMD模拟以复制品形式应用于这三种复合物，能够采样稀有构象状态，并提供配体结合和解离动力学的见解。基于LiGaMD模拟，环L7也被强调为瞬时可及的结合区域；然而，需要进一步研究以确定该位点作为潜在结合位点。在这些复合物中，SFC/NDM-1复合物被发现是最优的，这是基于结合热力学和动力学最有利的平衡推断得出的，建议进一步评估以将其确立为先导骨架。因此，本研究的发现提供了对NDM-1机制和动力学特征的详细理解，并可能应用于针对临床上重要的NDM-1酶（负责广泛抗生素耐药性）的新型抑制剂的合理设计。

02 一句话总结

本文通过常规 MD 模拟表征 NDM-1 自由态与三种抑制剂复合物的结构动力学差异，并利用 LiGaMD 增强采样揭示配体结合/解离路径，发现远端 loop L7 受 Zn 结合残基 His-165 扰动而成为潜在变构/瞬时结合位点，其中 SFC/NDM-1 复合物在热力学与动力学上表现最优。

  • 具体问题：NDM-1 酶与不同抑制剂（SFC、SPC、CAP）结合后，其构象动力学、结合自由能、结合/解离动力学特征如何变化？是否存在可靶向的变构位点？
  • 为什么重要：NDM-1 可水解几乎所有 β-内酰胺类抗生素，是临床抗菌耐药的关键靶点。理解其配体结合机制有助于理性设计抑制剂。
  • 现有方法为何不足：常规 MD 模拟受限于采样时间尺度，难以捕捉配体结合/解离的稀有事件；且单一热力学评价（如 MM-PBSA）不足以全面反映结合机制。
  • 精确研究问题：Can LiGaMD-enhanced sampling reveal transient binding sites and kinetic distinctions among three NDM-1 inhibitor complexes that conventional MD cannot resolve?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：对 NDM-1 自由态与三种抑制剂复合物分别进行常规 MD 模拟，随后对复合物进行 LiGaMD 增强采样，结合 RMSD、RMSF、MM-PBSA、相互作用指纹与配体网络分析。
  2. 核心洞察：远端 loop L7 虽远离活性位点，但通过 Zn 结合残基 His-165 的扰动与活性位点耦合，构成潜在的变构调控位点；LiGaMD 可揭示该位点的瞬时可及性。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：在酶-配体体系中，远端残基/loop 的构象变化可能通过金属结合位点或氢键网络传递至活性位点；增强采样方法应作为常规 MD 的补充而非替代，以同时获取热力学与动力学信息。

Major Concerns

  • Concern ID R1-M1
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Evidence sufficiency
  • Claim pointer The authors claim that LiGaMD simulations provided insights into ligand binding and unbinding kinetics and that the SFC/NDM-1 complex was found to be the most optimal based on the most favorable balance of binding thermodynamics and kinetics.
  • Evidence pointer Abstract text; location not provided
  • Concern No quantitative kinetic or thermodynamic values are reported. The abstract does not provide binding free energies, residence times, association or dissociation rate constants, or any comparison metrics that would support the ranking of the SFC complex as optimal.
  • Why it matters The central conclusion of the study, namely the identification of the most promising inhibitor scaffold, rests on a comparative ranking that cannot be evaluated without numerical data. Without these values, the claim is unverifiable and the practical utility of the study for inhibitor design is unclear.

  • Resolution test Provide quantitative values for binding free energies (e.g., MM-PBSA or LiGaMD-derived) and kinetic parameters (e.g., k_on, k_off, residence times) for all three complexes, with appropriate error estimates and statistical significance tests.

  • Concern ID R1-M2

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Methodological transparency
  • Claim pointer The authors state that LiGaMD simulations were applied in the form of a replica that enabled sampling of rare conformational states.
  • Evidence pointer Abstract text; location not provided
  • Concern The abstract does not specify the number of replicas, simulation lengths, convergence criteria, or how the replica approach was implemented. It is unclear whether the simulations reached equilibrium and whether the sampling of rare states was statistically robust.
  • Why it matters LiGaMD is a specialized enhanced sampling method whose results are highly sensitive to simulation setup and convergence. Without these details, the reliability of the kinetic and conformational findings cannot be assessed, and the claim of sampling rare states remains unsupported.

  • Resolution test Describe the LiGaMD simulation protocol in detail, including the number of replicas, simulation time per replica, boost parameters, and convergence metrics (e.g., time series of relevant observables, replicate reproducibility). Provide evidence that the sampled states are converged and reproducible.

  • Concern ID R1-M3

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Claim support
  • Claim pointer The authors claim that a distant loop L7 was perturbed by the Zn-binding residue His-165, suggesting a potential allosteric site, and that LiGaMD highlighted L7 as a transiently accessible binding region.
  • Evidence pointer Abstract text; location not provided
  • Concern The abstract provides no structural or dynamical data, such as RMSF values, correlation maps, or free energy landscapes, to support the claim of allosteric communication between His-165 and loop L7. The assertion that L7 is a transiently accessible binding region is not backed by any binding site analysis or ligand occupancy data.
  • Why it matters The proposed allosteric site is a key novel finding of the study. If not supported by robust evidence, the claim could mislead future drug design efforts. The distinction between a transiently accessible region and a bona fide binding site requires rigorous validation.
  • Resolution test Provide quantitative evidence of allosteric communication, such as dynamic cross-correlation matrices, principal component analysis, or perturbation response scanning. For the L7 binding site claim, show ligand occupancy maps, binding free energy profiles, or kinetic evidence of ligand binding at that site from LiGaMD simulations.
