When does a funnel become a trap? A Gomboc-inspired model of protein folding landscapes.

Shityakov, Sergey; Skorb, Ekaterina V; Nosonovsky, Michael
RSC advances · 2026-11-05 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文提出一个受Gomboc启发的蛋白质折叠景观计算模型，通过可调粗糙度参数模拟折叠动力学，并与Rosetta和AlphaFold3基准对比，虽非直接的结构预测或采样方法，但为蛋白质折叠景观质量提供了可迁移的启发式框架。

摘要

The protein folding problem remains a fundamental challenge in biophysics. While energy landscape theory resolves Levinthal's paradox through funnel-shaped landscapes, the quantitative effects of landscape ruggedness on folding kinetics are not fully understood. We introduce a computational model inspired by Gomboc, a convex 3D shape with one stable equilibrium and one unstable equilibrium, as an analogy for protein folding landscapes. Using a tunable ruggedness parameter, we simulate "folding" across multiple ruggedness conditions (r = 0 and r = 10(-8) to 10(-3)), spanning five orders of magnitude in the positive-r regime. At minimal ruggedness (10(-8)), the model achieves 83% success in reaching native-like states. Increasing the ruggedness to 10(-7)-10(-5) reduces the success rate to 25-39%, reflecting landscape trapping as an analog for kinetic trapping in physical folding. Benchmarking against the TPR1 domain via Rosetta and AlphaFold3 reveals native-like coverages of 77% and 90%, respectively, showing qualitative correspondence with the Gomboc model's success rates at low ruggedness. These findings suggest that the Gomboc-inspired model captures essential features of folding landscape quality and may serve as a useful heuristic for comparing foldability across different systems, bridging mathematical topology with protein biophysics.

摘要翻译

蛋白质折叠问题仍然是生物物理学中的一个基本挑战。虽然能量景观理论通过漏斗形景观解决了莱文塔尔悖论，但景观粗糙度对折叠动力学的定量影响尚未完全理解。我们引入了一个受Gomboc启发的计算模型，Gomboc是一种具有一个稳定平衡点和一个不稳定平衡点的凸三维形状，作为蛋白质折叠景观的类比。通过使用可调的粗糙度参数，我们在多种粗糙度条件下（r = 0 和 r = 10(-8) 到 10(-3)）模拟“折叠”，覆盖了正r区域中五个数量级的范围。在最小粗糙度（10(-8)）下，该模型达到83%的成功率以接近天然态。将粗糙度增加到10(-7)-10(-5)会将成功率降低到25-39%，反映了景观捕获作为物理折叠中动力学捕获的类比。通过与TPR1结构域进行基准测试，使用Rosetta和AlphaFold3分别显示出77%和90%的天然态覆盖率，与Gomboc模型在低粗糙度下的成功率具有定性对应关系。这些发现表明，受Gomboc启发的模型捕捉了折叠景观质量的基本特征，并可能作为比较不同系统可折叠性的有用启发式方法，连接数学拓扑与蛋白质生物物理学。

02 一句话总结

本文提出一个受 Gömböc（单稳定平衡凸体）启发的二维闭合链计算模型，通过可调粗糙度参数 r 同时调控随机能量噪声和非天然长程相互作用的强度，发现当 r 从 10⁻⁸ 增至 10⁻⁷–10⁻⁵ 时，折叠成功率从 83% 骤降至 25–39%，并定性对照 TPR1 结构域的 Rosetta 与 AlphaFold3 预测结果，表明该模型可捕捉折叠景观质量的核心特征。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：蛋白质折叠能量景观的粗糙度（ruggedness）如何定量影响折叠动力学结果？在什么条件下，原本高效的折叠漏斗会转变为陷阱主导的动力学陷阱？
  • 为什么重要：能量景观理论用漏斗形貌解决了 Levinthal 悖论，但景观粗糙度对折叠效率的定量影响尚未被系统理解。理解这一关系对蛋白质设计（避免误折叠）、疾病建模（错误折叠相关疾病）和折叠预测方法评估均有直接意义。
  • 现有方法不足：传统 Gō-like 模型只编码天然接触，缺乏对非天然相互作用的显式建模；全原子模拟计算成本高，难以系统扫描粗糙度参数空间；实验方法难以直接测量景观粗糙度。
  • 精确研究问题：Can a Gömböc-inspired 2D chain model with a tunable ruggedness parameter quantitatively capture the transition from funnel-dominated efficient folding to trap-dominated misfolding, and qualitatively correspond to the performance difference between AlphaFold3 and Rosetta on a minimally frustrated protein domain?

06 核心思想

1) 表面方法： - 构建二维闭合链（N=48 点）表示蛋白质构象，目标天然态为 Gömböc 启发的非对称凸形（通过 Fourier 合成 + 多项式剪切生成） - 能量函数含 7 项：天然吸引（Enat）、约束（Econf）、排斥（Erep）、局部几何（Elocal）、键长（Ebond）、随机粗糙度（Erug）、非天然长程 frustration（Efrust） - 用可调参数 r 同时缩放 Erug 和 Efrust，系统扫描 r = 0 到 10⁻³ - 两阶段优化：共轭梯度（2000 步）+ BFGS（6000 步），100 次独立实验 × 7 条件

2) 核心洞察： - Gömböc 的数学性质（单稳定平衡点）为蛋白质"单天然态"提供了几何类比：无论从哪个方向扰动，系统最终回到唯一稳定态 - 将"景观粗糙度"分解为两个可调分量：随机能量噪声（Erug）和非天然长程相互作用（Efrust），二者由同一参数 r 控制，从而在保持 frustration 来源平衡的同时系统扫描粗糙度 - 折叠成功率对 r 呈非线性响应：存在一个临界粗糙度阈值（约 10⁻⁷），超过后折叠效率急剧恶化——这一定性行为与真实蛋白质中"最小 frustration 原则"的预测一致

3) 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]： - 将几何拓扑性质（Gömböc 的单稳定平衡）映射为能量景观的全局最小值唯一性，是一种跨域类比建模的有效策略——但需注意二维模型与三维蛋白质的定量差异 - 用单一参数同时控制多个能量项的做法，牺牲了独立调节的自由度，但换来了可解释性和参数扫描的可操作性——在粗粒化模型中这是一种务实的权衡 - 将模型成功率与 AlphaFold3/Rosetta 的预测质量进行定性对照，为"计算模型验证"提供了一种低成本替代方案——但需警惕不同指标（成功率 vs 覆盖率）之间的可比性问题

07 方法总览

输入：目标天然态构象（Gömböc 启发的二维闭合链，N=48 点，由 Fourier 系数和剪切参数定义）；可调粗糙度参数 r（7 个条件：0, 10⁻⁸, 10⁻⁷, 10⁻⁶, 10⁻⁵, 10⁻⁴, 10⁻³）

输出：折叠轨迹分类（6 类：perfect ≤0.12, success ≤0.20, good ≤0.30, near-native ≤0.40, misfolded ≤0.55, poor >0.55 RMSD）；能量降 ΔE = E_init − E_final；成功率统计

模块： 1. 天然态生成：Fourier 合成 r(θ) = C0 + C1 sinθ + C2 sin(k1θ + φ) + C3 cos(k2θ)，系数 C0=1.0, C1=0.20, C2=0.12, C3=0.08, k1=2.8, k2=4.5, φ=0.5；随后施加 x ← x + 0.12y² − 0.04y³ 剪切 2. 能量函数：7 项求和（见 06 节），其中 Erug 为随机噪声项（N(0,1) 高斯扰动，c=0.01, k=20），Efrust 为非天然长程对惩罚（α=15，链上间隔 ≥5 的残基对） 3. 优化器：两阶段混合策略——Stage I 共轭梯度（2000 步，容差 10⁻³）；Stage II BFGS（6000 步，容差 10⁻⁶）；终止条件为梯度范数 < 10⁻⁶ 或达到 10000 步上限 4. 轨迹分类：按最终 RMSD 阈值分为 6 类

流程：生成天然态 → 随机初始化 100 条链 → 对每个 r 条件运行两阶段优化 → 记录最终 RMSD 和能量降 → 统计成功率 → 与 Rosetta/AF3 结果定性对照

工具：Python 3.9.12, NumPy 1.21.5, SciPy 1.7.3, Matplotlib；Rosetta 2020.03（ab initio relax，2500 decoys，24 核）；AlphaFold3 服务器；PyMOL（结构比对）

计算成本：全部 Gömböc 模型模拟约 4 CPU-小时（标准工作站）

09 关键公式符号

1) 天然态生成： r(θ) = C0 + C1 sinθ + C2 sin(k1θ + φ) + C3 cos(k2θ) - r(θ)：极坐标半径，θ ∈ [0, 2π) - C0 = 1.0, C1 = 0.20, C2 = 0.12, C3 = 0.08：Fourier 系数 - k1 = 2.8, k2 = 4.5：频率参数（非整数，确保非周期不对称） - φ = 0.5：相位偏移

2) 坐标剪切： x ← x + 0.12y² − 0.04y³, y ← y - 二次项 0.12y²：对称横向变形 - 三次项 −0.04y³：打破对称性，确保唯一稳定平衡

3) 能量函数： E = Enat + Econf + Erep + Elocal + Ebond + Erug + Efrust - Enat：天然吸引（RMSD 二次势） - Econf：约束能（防止膨胀） - Erep：排斥能（避免自交） - Elocal：局部几何能（保持 ~114.6° 键角） - Ebond：键长约束 - Erug：随机粗糙度项，含 N(0,1) 高斯噪声，缩放因子 c = 0.01，空间频率 k = 20 - Efrust：非天然长程惩罚，α = 15，作用于链上间隔 ≥5 的残基对

4) 折叠轨迹分类阈值： - perfect: RMSD ≤ 0.12 - success: RMSD ≤ 0.20 - good: RMSD ≤ 0.30 - near-native: RMSD ≤ 0.40 - misfolded: RMSD ≤ 0.55 - poor: RMSD > 0.55

5) 能量降： ΔE = E_init − E_final

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围：本文仅研究二维闭合链模型的折叠成功率与粗糙度参数的关系，不涉及真实蛋白质的折叠速率预测、三维构象采样或序列设计。
  • oracle/真值输入：模型需要预先知道天然态构象（Gömböc 启发的目标形状），属于 Gō-like 模型范畴——即"给定目标，评估景观可达性"，而非从序列预测结构。

14 学到什么

Agent 提炼的知识候选：

  1. 可调粗糙度参数的设计策略：用单一参数 r 同时控制随机噪声（Erug）和非天然长程相互作用（Efrust），避免了多参数调优的复杂性，同时保持了 frustration 来源的平衡。→ 可迁移到蛋白质构象采样或生成模型中，作为控制"景观粗糙度"的旋钮。

  2. Gömböc 几何类比：将"单稳定平衡"的拓扑性质映射为能量景观的全局最小唯一性，为设计具有明确单天然态的粗粒化模型提供了几何直觉。→ 可用于设计合成蛋白或评估设计蛋白的折叠鲁棒性。

  3. 成功率-粗糙度临界行为：模型显示存在一个狭窄的临界 r 区间（10⁻⁸→10⁻⁷），超过后折叠效率急剧恶化。→ 对蛋白质设计有启示：设计目标应保持在"低 frustration"区间，避免临界点附近的脆弱性。

  4. 基线概率校正：作者识别出高 r 条件下成功率"回升"是随机基线假象，而非真实折叠能力恢复。→ 在评估任何折叠/生成模型的成功率时，应始终与随机基线对比。

  5. Rosetta vs AlphaFold3 的互补验证：用物理模拟（Rosetta）和深度学习方法（AF3）同时验证 TPR1 的 minimally frustrated 特性，为评估新模型提供了双轨验证范式。→ 可迁移到结构预测或设计方法的评估中。

  6. 二维链模型的低成本探索：用 4 CPU-小时完成 700 次折叠实验，展示了粗粒化模型在参数空间扫描中的效率优势。→ 在需要大规模探索折叠条件（如突变扫描、环境条件扫描）时，二维/粗粒化模型是可行的预筛选工具。

Review setup

  • Input scope Full manuscript text (abstract, introduction, computational methods, results and discussion, conclusion) for the article "When does a funnel become a trap? A Gomboc-inspired model of protein folding landscapes" by Shityakov, Skorb, and Nosonovsky.

  • Visible evidence base Full text including equations, simulation parameters, results tables/figures referenced (Table 1, Figs 1–4, Fig S1), and benchmark comparisons against Rosetta and AlphaFold3 for the TPR1 domain (PDB 1ELW).

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The manuscript presents an interesting conceptual analogy between the Gömböc shape and protein folding landscapes, and the computational model is clearly described. However, the central claim of qualitative correspondence between model success rates and Rosetta/AF3 performance rests on a comparison of non-equivalent metrics across different systems, and the manuscript does not establish that the Gömböc-inspired model captures features beyond what standard Gō-like models already provide. The sharp transition in success rate with increasing r is presented as a key finding, but the mechanistic interpretation is underdeveloped and the apparent recovery at high r is dismissed as an artifact without rigorous demonstration. The benchmark comparison is suggestive but not quantitatively grounded. The manuscript would benefit from a clearer articulation of what specific, testable prediction the model makes that distinguishes it from existing approaches.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers in protein folding theory, energy landscape analysis, and computational biophysics may find the Gömböc analogy conceptually appealing. The model's simplicity and computational efficiency could interest those seeking coarse-grained or conceptual frameworks for teaching or for generating hypotheses about frustration effects. The bridging of topology and folding may also appeal to mathematicians working on shape optimization or dynamical systems with applications in biophysics.
  • Major strengths The manuscript is clearly written and the Gömböc analogy is pedagogically attractive. The computational model is simple, transparent, and reproducible in principle. The systematic sweep of the ruggedness parameter across five orders of magnitude is a reasonable approach. The use of two independent structure prediction methods (Rosetta and AF3) as benchmarks for the TPR1 domain is appropriate, and the reported energy landscape features for TPR1 are consistent with its known behavior as a well-folded domain.

  • Axis Claim validity
  • Claim pointer "These findings suggest that the Gömböc-inspired model captures essential features of folding landscape quality and may serve as a useful heuristic for comparing foldability across different systems."
  • Evidence pointer Results and discussion, "Gömböc-inspired model reveals frustration-dependent folding behavior"; "Rosetta ab initio simulations"; "AF3 validates the TPR1 domain"

The model success rate at r = 0 is 93%, which is claimed to correspond to AF3's 90% coverage, yet the model at r = 0 has no frustration at all, while AF3 operates on a real protein with real sequence-specific interactions. The comparison is therefore not a validation of the model but an exercise in pattern matching.

Without a defined mapping between model parameters and physical quantities, or between model success rates and prediction confidence metrics, the claim is unfalsifiable and cannot be extended to other systems.

  • Claim pointer "At minimal ruggedness (10−8), the model achieves 83% success in reaching native-like states" and "At zero ruggedness (r = 0.0), the landscape acts as a near-perfect funnel, yielding an average energy drop of 129.5 ± 21.7 arbitrary units, convergence in 21 optimization steps, and a 93% success rate."

If the transition is an optimizer artifact, the claim of landscape trapping must be revised.

  • Evidence pointer Section text
  • Issue The manuscript states that "High-confidence AF3 predictions (pLDdt ≥ 90) with a uniform PAE plot are characteristic of well-folded, minimally frustrated systems." This is an interpretive claim about what AF3 confidence metrics indicate about frustration. AF3 confidence reflects prediction certainty, not the physical frustration of the folding landscape. A protein with high frustration could still be predicted with high confidence if the training set contains similar folds. The claim conflates prediction confidence with biophysical properties.
  • Required correction Soften the claim or provide a citation supporting the link between AF3 confidence metrics and physical frustration. Alternatively, reframe the AF3 results as evidence of structural predictability rather than minimal frustration.

  • Evidence pointer Section text
  • Issue The manuscript states that the model is "pattern-reproducible but not bit-reproducible" and that "reruns give different individual trajectories but the same ensemble statistics." This is a useful clarification, but the manuscript does not specify the random seed policy or whether the reported results are from a single run or averaged over multiple runs with different seeds.
  • Required correction Specify the random seed policy and report whether the results are averaged over multiple independent runs.
  • Technical failings that need to be addressed before the case is established R1-M1 (claim of qualitative correspondence without a principled link between model and benchmark metrics) and R1-M2 (incomplete reporting of simulation diagnostics to distinguish landscape trapping from optimizer failure) are the primary technical issues. The incomplete equations for Erug and Efrust (R1-m2) also need correction for reproducibility.
  • Assessment against Nature-style criteria Originality: The Gömböc analogy is novel as a conceptual framing, but the computational model itself does not demonstrate new physics or methodology beyond standard structure-based models with tunable frustration. Scientific importance: The question of how landscape ruggedness affects folding is important, but the manuscript does not establish that the toy model provides insights beyond what is already known from Gō-like models and energy landscape theory. Interdisciplinary readership: The manuscript bridges topology and biophysics in a way that could appeal to a broad audience, but the lack of quantitative rigor in the central comparison limits its impact. Technical soundness: The model is simple and clearly described, but the incomplete reporting of equations and diagnostics, and the conflation of prediction confidence with physical frustration, undermine technical rigor. Readability for nonspecialists: The manuscript is generally well-written and accessible, with the Gömböc analogy providing an intuitive entry point. However, the abrupt transition from model results to benchmark comparisons without a clear logical bridge may confuse nonspecialist readers.
  • Recommendation posture Supportive if technical concerns are resolved. The conceptual framing is appealing and the model is simple enough to be useful as a pedagogical tool or a starting point for more rigorous work. However, the central claim of correspondence with Rosetta/AF3 performance must be either quantitatively grounded or explicitly reframed as a conceptual analogy. The simulation diagnostics must be reported to distinguish physical trapping from optimizer artifacts. If these issues are addressed, the manuscript could make a modest but useful contribution to the conceptual toolkit of protein folding research.
