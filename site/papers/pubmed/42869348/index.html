Complexation of soybean beta-conglycinin and glycinin with phytic acid: Molecular insights and effects on structural and digestive properties.

Zhang, Yujiao; Fei, Chengxin; Zhao, Ruojie; Dai, Oujun; Deng, Zhixiong; Fan, Bei; Wang, Fengzhong; Huang, Yatao
International journal of biological macromolecules · 2026-10-13 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该研究结合分子对接和MD模拟，探究植酸与大豆7S/11S蛋白的结合机制及其对结构和消化性的影响，虽以食品蛋白为对象，但结构计算建模方法可迁移至蛋白质体系。

摘要

This study investigated the complexation of phytic acid (PA) with soybean beta-conglycinin (7S) and glycinin (11S) and its effects on protein structure and digestibility. Complex formation was most pronounced near pH 3.0, accompanied by decreased solubility and enhanced aggregation. PA binding reduced beta-sheet content from 36.0% to 31.4% in 7S and from 37.3% to 35.1% in 11S, indicating conformational rearrangement. During simulated gastric digestion, hydrolysis decreased from 10.59% to 7.42% for 7S and from 9.93% to 6.92% for 11S, whereas hydrolysis increased during subsequent intestinal digestion. Molecular docking and molecular dynamics simulations provided theoretical support for interpreting the experimental observations, suggesting contributions from electrostatic interactions and hydrogen bonding, with more extensive contacts predicted for PA-11S. Isothermal titration calorimetry confirmed a higher affinity of PA for 11S, with dissociation constants of 12.3 muM for 11S and 32.5 muM for 7S. At pH 3.0 and 37 degrees C, binding was enthalpically and entropically favorable for 7S but enthalpy-driven for 11S. These findings provide molecular insights into PA-soy protein interactions and their implications for protein hydrolysis during simulated gastrointestinal digestion.

摘要翻译

本研究探讨了植酸（phytic acid, PA）与大豆β-伴大豆球蛋白（7S）及大豆球蛋白（11S）的复合形成及其对蛋白质结构和消化性的影响。复合形成在pH 3.0附近最为显著，伴随溶解度降低和聚集增强。PA结合使7S中β-折叠含量从36.0%降至31.4%，11S中从37.3%降至35.1%，表明构象重排。在模拟胃消化过程中，7S的水解度从10.59%降至7.42%，11S从9.93%降至6.92%，而在后续肠消化中水解度增加。分子对接和分子动力学（molecular dynamics）模拟为解释实验观察提供了理论支持，提示静电相互作用和氢键的贡献，并预测PA-11S具有更广泛的接触。等温滴定量热法（isothermal titration calorimetry）确认PA对11S具有更高亲和力，解离常数分别为11S的12.3 μM和7S的32.5 μM。在pH 3.0和37°C条件下，7S的结合在焓和熵上均有利，而11S的结合由焓驱动。这些发现为PA-大豆蛋白相互作用及其在模拟胃肠消化过程中对蛋白质水解的影响提供了分子层面的见解。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：植酸（PA）如何与大豆 β-conglycinin（7S）和 glycinin（11S）在分子水平上相互作用？这种相互作用如何影响蛋白结构（二级结构、聚集）和模拟胃肠消化中的水解行为？
  • 为什么重要：大豆蛋白是重要植物蛋白来源，植酸是豆类中常见抗营养因子，二者在食品加工（尤其酸性条件）中易形成复合物，影响蛋白溶解度、结构及消化吸收。理解互作机制有助于调控食品加工条件以改善营养。
  • 现有方法为何不足：以往研究多关注 PA-蛋白复合物的宏观现象（如溶解度下降），缺乏分子层面的结合位点、驱动力（静电 vs 疏水 vs 氢键）及对消化酶可及性的定量解释。计算模拟（对接/MD）与热力学实验（ITC）结合可弥补这一空缺。
  • 精确研究问题：Can molecular docking and MD simulations, combined with ITC and CD, quantitatively explain the pH-dependent complexation of PA with 7S/11S and its differential effects on gastric vs intestinal digestibility?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：实验上使用 ITC 测结合热力学、CD 测二级结构、模拟胃肠消化测水解率；计算上使用分子对接预测结合构象、MD 模拟验证稳定性与相互作用。
  2. 核心洞察：PA 与大豆蛋白的互作是 pH 依赖的静电驱动过程，结合位点与接触面积差异（11S > 7S）解释了亲和力差异；PA 结合通过改变二级结构（β-sheet 减少）和表面可及性，差异化地影响胃/肠消化阶段。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：对于蛋白质-配体互作研究，单一实验或单一模拟均不足以建立机制解释；「热力学实验（ITC）+ 构象探针（CD）+ 功能实验（消化）+ 原子级模拟（对接/MD）」的组合可形成可检验的闭环。此思路可迁移至蛋白质-药物、蛋白质-多酚等体系。

Kd 差异与接触面积关联：11S 更大接触面积对应更高亲和力，提示在预测蛋白-配体亲和力时，可结合对接接触面积作为特征。 4. 二级结构变化作为功能指标：β-sheet 含量变化可作为配体结合引起构象重排的敏感探针，适用于 CD 实验设计。 5. 消化阶段差异：同一修饰（PA 结合）对胃/肠消化影响相反，提示在评估蛋白质消化性时需分阶段考察，不可单一指标定论。 6. MD 模拟的辅助定位：本文中 MD 仅用于解释实验，未独立预测。可借鉴为「模拟-实验互证」而非「模拟驱动发现」的范式。

Major Concerns

  • Concern ID R1-M1
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Statistical robustness
  • Claim pointer The abstract states that PA binding reduced beta-sheet content from 36.0% to 31.4% in 7S and from 37.3% to 35.1% in 11S, and that hydrolysis decreased from 10.59% to 7.42% for 7S and from 9.93% to 6.92% for 11S during gastric digestion.
  • Evidence pointer Abstract text; location not provided
  • Concern The abstract reports specific numerical changes in secondary structure and digestibility but provides no indication of variability, replication, or statistical significance. The magnitude of the beta-sheet changes (4.6 and 2.2 percentage points) and gastric hydrolysis reductions (about 3 percentage points) may be within experimental noise, especially for circular dichroism-based secondary structure estimation.
  • Why it matters Without statistical measures, the reader cannot determine whether the observed effects are real or artifacts. This is critical because the central claim of the study, that PA binding alters protein structure and digestibility, depends on these quantitative differences being robust.

  • Resolution test Provide mean values with standard deviations or confidence intervals, number of independent replicates, and appropriate statistical tests (e.g., t-test or ANOVA) for each reported comparison. If the differences are not statistically significant, the claims should be revised.

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Integration of computational and experimental evidence
  • Claim pointer The abstract states that molecular docking and molecular dynamics simulations provided theoretical support for interpreting the experimental observations, suggesting contributions from electrostatic interactions and hydrogen bonding, with more extensive contacts predicted for PA-11S.
