Interpretable Protein-Nucleic Acid Binding Site Prediction via Domain-Guided Multimodal Learning.

Xi, Wenyu; Wang, Ruheng; Ye, Xiucai; Sakurai, Tetsuya; Wei, Leyi
ACS synthetic biology · 2026-10-23 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文提出域引导的多模态深度学习框架BiMTBind，用于蛋白质-核酸结合位点预测，融合蛋白语言模型、结构描述符和结构域先验，属于蛋白质结构相关计算任务，方法有创新但对象侧重结合位点而非核心结构建模。

摘要

Accurate identification of protein-nucleic acid binding sites is essential for elucidating molecular mechanisms and for enabling structure-based drug discovery. Existing computational approaches struggle to jointly model sequence, structure, and domain knowledge, which limits both predictive accuracy and interpretability. We present BiMTBind, a domain-guided multimodal deep learning framework that integrates bidirectional state-space modeling with Transformer-based attention for residue-level binding site prediction. The model explicitly incorporates protein-domain annotations as biological priors to guide attention toward functionally relevant regions. Multi-source featurescontextual embeddings from protein language models and handcrafted structural descriptorsare fused via a learnable gating mechanism that adaptively weights each modality. This design enables the model to capture both global dependencies and local context. Extensive evaluations across diverse DNA and RNA benchmarks show that BiMTBind consistently outperforms state-of-the-art sequence- and structure-based methods on multiple metrics, including F1, MCC, and AUC. Case studies further show that domain-guided attention improves interpretability by aligning predicted binding residues with annotated functional domains. These results highlight the effectiveness of combining biologically informed priors with deep multimodal representation learning, offering a scalable and generalizable approach for protein-nucleic acid binding site prediction.

摘要翻译

蛋白质-核酸结合位点的准确识别对于阐明分子机制和实现基于结构的药物发现至关重要。现有计算方法难以联合建模序列、结构和领域知识，这限制了预测准确性和可解释性。我们提出了BiMTBind，一种领域引导的多模态深度学习框架，将双向状态空间建模与基于Transformer的注意力机制相结合，用于残基水平结合位点预测。该模型明确将蛋白质结构域注释作为生物学先验纳入，以引导注意力聚焦于功能相关区域。多源特征——来自蛋白质语言模型的上下文嵌入和手工设计的结构描述符——通过可学习的门控机制融合，该机制自适应地加权每种模态。这种设计使模型能够捕捉全局依赖关系和局部上下文。在多种DNA和RNA基准上的广泛评估表明，BiMTBind在多个指标（包括F1、MCC和AUC）上持续优于最先进的基于序列和结构的方法。案例研究进一步表明，领域引导的注意力通过将预测的结合残基与注释的功能结构域对齐，提高了可解释性。这些结果突显了将生物学信息先验与深度多模态表示学习相结合的有效性，为蛋白质-核酸结合位点预测提供了一种可扩展且可泛化的方法。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：如何准确且可解释地预测蛋白质序列上哪些残基参与核酸（DNA/RNA）结合？
  • 为什么重要：蛋白质-核酸相互作用是基因调控、复制、转录等核心生物学过程的基础；结合位点注释对结构生物学、药物发现和蛋白质工程（如设计核酸结合蛋白）至关重要。
  • 现有方法不足：现有计算方法通常单独使用序列（如 PSSM、PLM 嵌入）或结构（如几何特征），难以联合建模序列、结构与领域知识；且多数模型为黑箱，缺乏可解释性，无法将预测结果与已知功能域关联。
  • 精确研究问题：Can a domain-guided multimodal deep learning framework that jointly models sequence embeddings, structural descriptors, and protein-domain annotations achieve higher accuracy and better interpretability than existing sequence- and structure-based baselines for residue-level protein-nucleic acid binding site prediction?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：BiMTBind 是一个多模态深度学习框架，包含： - 双向状态空间模型（Mamba）编码序列上下文； - Transformer 注意力层捕捉全局依赖； - 蛋白结构域注释作为先验，引导注意力权重； - 多源特征（PLM 嵌入 + 手工结构描述符）通过可学习门控融合。
  2. 核心洞察：结构域注释不仅是特征，更是注意力引导信号——将生物学先验直接注入注意力计算，使模型在预测结合位点时优先关注已知功能区域，从而同时提升精度与可解释性。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：在蛋白质功能预测任务中，将离散的生物学知识（如结构域、活性位点注释） 作为注意力偏置或门控信号，比仅作为额外特征更有效——因为先验直接约束了模型的关注空间，而非仅增加输入维度。此思路可迁移到其他残基级预测任务（如活性位点、界面预测）。

07 方法总览

  • 输入：蛋白质氨基酸序列（一级结构）；可选：预测或实验结构（用于提取结构描述符）；蛋白结构域注释（如 Pfam/InterPro）。
  • 输出：每个残基的结合概率（二分类：结合/非结合），以及注意力权重（用于可解释性）。
  • 模块： 1. 序列编码器：双向状态空间模型（Mamba）捕捉长程依赖； 2. 注意力层：Transformer 自注意力，受结构域先验引导； 3. 特征提取：PLM 嵌入（如 ESM-2） + 手工结构描述符（如 RSA、SS、PSSM 等）； 4. 门控融合：可学习门控向量自适应加权各模态特征； 5. 分类头：MLP 输出残基级概率。
  • 训练：监督学习，交叉熵损失；基准数据集上训练与评估（具体数据集名称未提供）。
  • 工具：未提供具体框架（推测 PyTorch 等，但未声明）。
  • 假设：结构域注释可提供有效的注意力引导；多模态特征互补；门控融合优于简单拼接。
  • 流程：序列 → Mamba 编码 → 与结构域先验结合 → Transformer 注意力 → 与结构描述符门控融合 → MLP 分类 → 残基级结合概率 + 注意力权重。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a plausible and timely approach to a well-recognized problem in computational biology. The combination of state-space models, Transformer attention, and domain priors is conceptually interesting and could offer genuine advances in both accuracy and interpretability. However, the abstract provides no quantitative evidence, no benchmark specifications, and no architectural details sufficient to evaluate the claims. The core assertions of consistent outperformance and improved interpretability are currently unsupported by the supplied material. The work may have merit, but the case is not established from the abstract alone.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Computational biologists and bioinformaticians working on protein-nucleic acid interactions, particularly those developing deep learning methods for binding site prediction. Researchers interested in interpretable AI for biological sequence analysis and in integrating domain knowledge with modern sequence models would also find the approach relevant. The potential for application in structure-based drug discovery and functional annotation of proteins broadens the appeal to structural biologists and pharmaceutical researchers.
  • Major strengths The conceptual integration of bidirectional state-space modeling with Transformer attention is a modern and technically sound direction. The explicit use of protein-domain annotations as biological priors is a thoughtful approach to improving interpretability, which is often lacking in deep learning models for this task. The proposed learnable gating mechanism for multimodal feature fusion is a reasonable design choice that could address the challenge of combining heterogeneous feature types. The focus on both DNA and RNA benchmarks suggests an intention to demonstrate generalizability.
