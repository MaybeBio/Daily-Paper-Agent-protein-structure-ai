6

An Engineered beta-Aminopeptidase With Better Product Selectivity for L-Carnosine Production.

Guan, Bi-Han; Yin, Wu-Tong; Zhao, Chen; Pan, Jiang; Xu, Jian-He
Chembiochem : a European journal of chemical biology · 2026-09-14 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文通过结构引导突变和分子动力学模拟工程化β-氨基肽酶以提高L-肌肽合成选择性，虽以酶工程为主，但涉及蛋白质结构计算和MD模拟，对蛋白结构-功能关系研究有参考价值。

摘要

Efficient enzymaticsynthesis of L-carnosine (L-Car) is often limited by competing hydrolysis and side reactions that reduce substrate utilization and product yield. In this study, a beta-aminopeptidase (CbAP) from Caulobacter sp. was systematically engineered to enhance catalytic selectivity toward L-Car using beta-alaninamide and L-histidine as substrates. Analysis of beta-alaninamide-derived byproducts revealed two major competing pathways, namely beta-alaninamide hydrolysis and self-ligation into short oligopeptides. To evaluate the balance between L-Car synthesis and competing reactions, a composite apparent selectivity index (R(S)) was introduced. Structure-guided mutagenesis targeting residues within 4-6 A of the active site identified several improved variants. Iterative combination of beneficial mutations yielded the optimal mutant CbAP(Q138FF96Y) (CbAP(M2)), exhibiting an approximately 2.3-fold increase in R(S) relative to Q138F and an approximately 3.1-fold increase relative to the wild-type enzyme, with substantial suppression of undesired side reactions. Molecular dynamics simulations indicated that these improvements arise from reduced substrate binding stability and disrupted hydrogen-bonding interactions with beta-alaninamide and L-Car. Under optimized 10 mL-scale conditions, CbAP(M2) produced 37.3 mM L-Car within 1 h, corresponding to a 74.6% yield based on beta-alanine methyl ester, with an STY of 8.44 g L(-) (1) h(-1).

摘要翻译

L-肌肽（L-Car）的高效酶法合成常受竞争性水解和副反应限制，这些反应降低了底物利用率和产物产率。在本研究中，来自柄杆菌属（Caulobacter sp.）的β-氨基肽酶（CbAP）被系统改造，以增强其以β-丙氨酰胺和L-组氨酸为底物时对L-Car的催化选择性。对β-丙氨酰胺衍生副产物的分析揭示了两个主要竞争途径，即β-丙氨酰胺水解和自连接形成短寡肽。为评估L-Car合成与竞争反应之间的平衡，引入了复合表观选择性指数（R(S)）。针对活性位点4-6 Å范围内残基的结构引导突变鉴定出若干改进变体。有益突变的迭代组合产生了最优突变体CbAP(Q138FF96Y)（CbAP(M2)），其R(S)相对于Q138F提高了约2.3倍，相对于野生型酶提高了约3.1倍，并显著抑制了不良副反应。分子动力学 (molecular dynamics) 模拟表明，这些改进源于底物结合稳定性降低以及与β-丙氨酰胺和L-Car的氢键相互作用被破坏。在优化的10 mL规模条件下，CbAP(M2)在1小时内产生37.3 mM L-Car，基于β-丙氨酸甲酯的产率为74.6%，时空产率（STY）为8.44 g L⁻¹ h⁻¹。

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：结构引导的定点突变（活性位点 4–6 Å 内残基）+ 迭代组合有益突变 + MD 模拟验证。
  2. 核心洞察：通过降低底物（β-alaninamide）和产物（L-Car）在活性位点的结合稳定性，破坏与底物的氢键相互作用，从而抑制副反应而不显著牺牲合成活性，实现选择性提升。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：酶工程中，选择性优化不一定需要增强底物结合，反而可通过削弱非生产性结合模式来减少副反应；MD 模拟可提供突变效应的动态机制解释，指导后续设计。
