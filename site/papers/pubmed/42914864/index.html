Melatonin regulates protein structures of wheat under shading for cookie crack ratio and digestibility modifications.

Yang, Hongkun; Wei, Ting; Huang, Xiulan; Fan, Gaoqiong
Food chemistry · 2026-10-23 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文研究褪黑素对遮阴小麦蛋白结构及饼干品质的影响，虽涉及蛋白结构（二级结构）和机器学习预测，但核心是农业食品应用，非蛋白质结构计算建模，方法贡献较弱。

摘要

The crack ratio and nutritional value of cookies depend on wheat protein structures, yet how melatonin modifies these attributes under shading remains unclear. This study evaluated the effects of melatonin (200 mumol L(-1)) on protein structure and cookie-making quality in shaded wheat. Under shading, melatonin increased glutenin, gliadin, total protein, and small polymeric protein content, and raised beta-sheet and alpha-helix proportions by up to 14.4% and 13.2%, respectively, strengthening non-covalent interactions into a more cross-linked gluten network. Melatonin also elevated sulfur-containing and aromatic amino acids, improving nutritional value and digestibility. A SegFormer-based machine learning model showed melatonin reduced the cookie crack ratio by 18.8-30.7% and improved homogeneity. Protein content, gamma-gliadin, and small polymeric proteins governed the crack ratio, whereas digestibility was primarily associated with small polymeric proteins. Collectively, melatonin regulates crack ratio and digestibility by modulating amino acid profiles and protein structures, offering a promising approach to enhance cookie-making quality.

摘要翻译

饼干的开裂率和营养价值取决于小麦蛋白结构，然而褪黑素在遮荫条件下如何修饰这些属性仍不清楚。本研究评估了褪黑素（200 μmol L⁻¹）对遮荫小麦蛋白结构和饼干制作品质的影响。在遮荫条件下，褪黑素提高了谷蛋白、醇溶蛋白、总蛋白和小分子聚合蛋白含量，并将β-折叠和α-螺旋比例分别提升至多14.4%和13.2%，增强了非共价相互作用，形成更交联的谷蛋白网络。褪黑素还提高了含硫氨基酸和芳香族氨基酸含量，改善了营养价值和消化性。基于SegFormer的机器学习模型显示，褪黑素将饼干开裂率降低了18.8-30.7%，并改善了均匀性。蛋白含量、γ-醇溶蛋白和小分子聚合蛋白主导了开裂率，而消化性主要与小分子聚合蛋白相关。总体而言，褪黑素通过调节氨基酸谱和蛋白结构来调控开裂率和消化性，为提升饼干制作品质提供了一种有前景的方法。
