Structural and biochemical basis for the formation of alpha-(1-->3)-linked mannose residue in O-glycans of Saccharomyces cerevisiae.

Hira, Daisuke; Kadooka, Chihiro; Oka, Takuji
The Journal of biological chemistry · 2026-10-08 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文解析酵母O-聚糖α-1,3-甘露糖转移酶Mnt2p的晶体结构，并结合对接与分子动力学模拟阐明其底物特异性和连接选择性，虽以酶结构为核心，但涉及蛋白结构解析与计算模拟，对蛋白质结构计算研究有参考价值。

摘要

The biosynthesis of O-mannose-type glycans in the budding yeast Saccharomyces cerevisiae requires the sequential action of multiple mannosyltransferases. However, the molecular mechanism governing alpha-(1-->3)-linkage formation remains poorly understood. Mnt2p, a member of glycosyltransferase family 71 (GT71), catalyzes the transfer of the fourth and fifth mannose residues of O-glycans via alpha-(1-->3)-linkages. Here, we report the crystal structure of Mnt2p at 2.40 A resolution, representing the first experimentally determined structure of a GT71 family enzyme. The structure revealed an N-terminal domain adopting a canonical GT-A fold harboring a DXD motif that coordinates a Mn(2+) ion and a novel C-terminal domain unique to the GT71 family. The active site is formed at the interface between these two domains, and GDP-mannose binds to a cleft adjacent to the catalytic metal ion. Using recombinant Mnt2p expressed in Escherichia coli, we biochemically demonstrated its alpha-(1-->3)-mannosyltransferase activity and determined its substrate specificity toward alpha-mannobiose acceptors. Docking and molecular dynamics simulations revealed that the C-terminal domain forms the acceptor substrate-binding site, providing the structural basis for alpha-(1-->3)-linkage selectivity, distinct from the linkage specificity of alpha-(1-->2)-mannosyltransferases. These findings establish the structural foundation for understanding the differentiation of alpha-(1-->2) and alpha-(1-->3) mannosyl linkages in yeast O-glycan biosynthesis.

摘要翻译

出芽酵母酿酒酵母中O-甘露糖型聚糖的生物合成需要多种甘露糖基转移酶的依次作用。然而，调控α-(1→3)-键形成的分子机制仍知之甚少。Mnt2p，作为糖基转移酶家族71（GT71）的成员，通过α-(1→3)-键催化O-聚糖第四和第五个甘露糖残基的转移。在此，我们报告了分辨率为2.40 Å的Mnt2p晶体结构，这是GT71家族酶的首个实验确定结构。该结构揭示了一个N端结构域，采用典型的GT-A折叠，包含一个协调Mn(2+)离子的DXD基序，以及一个GT71家族特有的新型C端结构域。活性位点形成于这两个结构域之间的界面，GDP-甘露糖结合在催化金属离子附近的裂隙中。利用在大肠杆菌中表达的重组Mnt2p，我们通过生化实验证明了其α-(1→3)-甘露糖基转移酶活性，并确定了其对α-甘露二糖受体的底物特异性。对接和分子动力学模拟揭示，C端结构域形成受体底物结合位点，为α-(1→3)-键选择性提供了结构基础，这与α-(1→2)-甘露糖基转移酶的键特异性不同。这些发现为理解酵母O-聚糖生物合成中α-(1→2)和α-(1→3)甘露糖基键的分化建立了结构基础。

  • 精确研究问题：Can the crystal structure of Mnt2p, combined with MD simulations, explain the alpha-(1-->3) linkage selectivity distinct from alpha-(1-->2)-mannosyltransferases?

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a potentially significant advance in understanding yeast O-glycan biosynthesis by providing the first structural view of a GT71 family enzyme. The claim of a novel C-terminal domain and its proposed role in acceptor specificity is intriguing. However, the abstract alone provides insufficient evidence to evaluate the rigor of the structural determination, the biochemical validation, or the computational modeling. Several key claims, particularly regarding the molecular basis of linkage selectivity, are presented without supporting quantitative data. The work is likely of interest to glycobiology and glycosyltransferase communities, but the case as presented is not fully established from the supplied material.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers studying glycosyltransferase structure-function relationships, yeast cell wall and glycoprotein biosynthesis, and the molecular evolution of linkage specificity in GT families. The first structure of a GT71 enzyme would also be of interest to structural biologists working on GT-A fold enzymes and to those investigating how acceptor substrate geometry dictates glycosidic linkage formation.
  • Major strengths The abstract reports the first experimentally determined structure of a GT71 family enzyme, filling a clear gap in structural coverage. The identification of a novel C-terminal domain unique to GT71 is a concrete and testable finding. The combination of structural, biochemical, and computational approaches is appropriate for addressing the question of linkage specificity.
