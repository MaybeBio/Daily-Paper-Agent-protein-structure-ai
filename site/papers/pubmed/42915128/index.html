Engineering a Paraburkholderia pallida Tannase for Improved Catalytic Efficiency, Thermal Stability, and Green Tea Clarification.

Gao, Fuquan; He, Keman; Lin, Tingting; Sun, Haorong; Hu, Xiaoyu; Chen, Yaoyu; Li, Qiyu; You, Xinyu; Wang, Xiaxia; Chen, Si; Yu, Xiaomin
Journal of agricultural and food chemistry · 2026-10-23 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文通过计算热点预测和分子动力学模拟对单宁酶进行工程改造，提升催化效率和热稳定性，虽以酶为对象，但结构建模与MD方法可迁移至蛋白质结构计算研究。

摘要

Tannases can reduce haze and astringency in tea beverages, but their application is often limited by poor thermal stability and catalytic efficiency. A tannase from Paraburkholderia pallida (Tanpp) was engineered using active-site mutagenesis and computational hotspot prediction. The double mutant F384T/N307A showed the best performance, with a 2.7-fold increase in catalytic efficiency and an 11-fold longer half-life at 50 degrees C than wild-type Tanpp. Structural modeling and molecular dynamics simulations suggested that N307A widened the substrate-entry region, whereas F384T compacted the catalytic pocket. The double mutant remained more stable at 323.15 K. In green tea clarification assays, F384T/N307A produced the lowest turbidity and promoted conversion of galloylated catechins into non-galloylated products and gallic acid. It retained superior performance across the tested enzyme dosages and treatment times. These findings show that coordinated active-site remodeling can improve both catalytic performance and thermal durability, supporting F384T/N307A as a promising tannase for tea clarification.

摘要翻译

单宁酶可减少茶饮料中的浑浊和涩味，但其应用常受限于较差的热稳定性和催化效率。通过活性位点突变和计算热点预测，对来自Paraburkholderia pallida的单宁酶（Tanpp）进行了工程改造。双突变体F384T/N307A表现出最佳性能，其催化效率较野生型Tanpp提高了2.7倍，在50°C下的半衰期延长了11倍。结构建模和分子动力学模拟表明，N307A拓宽了底物进入区域，而F384T使催化口袋更加紧凑。双突变体在323.15 K下仍保持更高的稳定性。在绿茶澄清实验中，F384T/N307A产生了最低的浊度，并促进了没食子酰化儿茶素转化为非没食子酰化产物和没食子酸。在测试的酶剂量和处理时间范围内，它均保持了优越的性能。这些发现表明，协调的活性位点重塑可同时提高催化性能和热耐久性，支持F384T/N307A作为茶澄清应用中具有前景的单宁酶。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：Tannase 在茶饮料澄清中应用受限于热稳定性差和催化效率低，如何通过理性设计同时提升这两项性能？
  • 为什么重要：Tannase 可降解茶饮料中导致浑浊和涩味的 galloylated catechins，但野生型酶在加工温度下不稳定、效率不足，限制工业应用。
  • 现有方法为何不足：传统随机突变或单一活性位点突变往往只改善单一性能，且缺乏结构层面的机制解释；计算预测与实验验证结合不足。
  • 精确研究问题：Can coordinated active-site remodeling (via computational hotspot prediction and targeted mutagenesis) simultaneously improve both catalytic efficiency and thermal stability of Tanpp, and maintain performance in green tea clarification?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：对 Tanpp 进行活性位点饱和突变 + 计算热点预测，筛选出双突变 F384T/N307A，并通过 MD 模拟和绿茶澄清实验验证。
