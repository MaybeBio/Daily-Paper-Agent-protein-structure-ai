Machine-learning in optimization of CRISPR technology.

Liyanage, Risi; Jin, Lei; Chen, Shi-Jie
Machine learning. Health · 2026-09-23 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

综述机器学习在CRISPR技术优化中的应用，涉及Cas蛋白工程和gRNA设计，但核心对象是基因编辑系统而非蛋白质结构计算，方法上无结构建模或物理模拟贡献。

摘要

The clustered regularly interspaced short palindromic repeats (CRISPR)-Cas system has become an indispensable tool in modern gene-engineering applications over recent years. Despite its rapid adoption, further development and broader applicability are hindered by several inherent limitations. This review surveys a range of machine-learning based approaches that aim to address these challenges. In particular, we focus on the optimization of key components of the CRISPR system, including protospacer adjacent motif recognition, Cas-protein-engineering, guide RNA sequence design and extension of the approaches to alternative editing modalities. Applied machine-learning methodologies, their underlying rationale, and comparisons with experimental observations are critically discussed. Special emphasis is placed on the role of machine-learning frameworks in advancing biophysical research, where complex, high-dimensional data increasingly demand integrative computational approaches. Finally, we outline current limitations, draw overarching conclusions, and propose perspectives for future developments and applications in CRISPR-based technologies.

摘要翻译

成簇规律间隔短回文重复序列（CRISPR）-Cas系统近年来已成为现代基因工程应用中不可或缺的工具。尽管其被迅速采纳，但进一步的发展及更广泛的应用仍受到若干固有局限性的阻碍。本综述考察了一系列旨在应对这些挑战的基于机器学习的方法。特别地，我们聚焦于CRISPR系统关键组件的优化，包括前间隔序列邻近基序识别、Cas蛋白工程、引导RNA序列设计，以及将这些方法扩展到替代编辑模式。本文批判性地讨论了所应用的机器学习方法、其基本原理，以及与实验观察的比较。特别强调了机器学习框架在推进生物物理研究中的作用，其中复杂、高维数据日益需要综合性的计算方法。最后，我们概述了当前局限性，得出总体结论，并对CRISPR基础技术的未来发展和应用提出了展望。
