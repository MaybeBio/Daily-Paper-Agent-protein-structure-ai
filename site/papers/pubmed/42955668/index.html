LSTM-Based Methods for RNA-RNA Interaction Prediction.

Ramachandran, Akhilesh; Varadarajan, Swetha
Methods in molecular biology (Clifton, N.J.) · 2026-10-29 · 仅摘要 · 原文

综述基于LSTM的RNA-RNA相互作用预测方法，研究对象为RNA而非蛋白质，且不涉及蛋白质结构计算建模，与课题关联弱。

摘要

RNA-RNA interactions (RRIs) are central to various biological processes, including post-transcriptional regulation, gene silencing, and viral replication. While traditional computational methods for RRI prediction rely on sequence complementarity and thermodynamic models, deep learning approaches, particularly those leveraging long short-term memory (LSTM) networks, have shown great promise in modeling the complexity of RNA behavior. This chapter reviews recent advances in LSTM-based methods for RRI prediction, discusses the key datasets and evaluation metrics, and outlines future directions in this emerging field.

摘要翻译

RNA-RNA 相互作用 (RNA-RNA interactions, RRIs) 是多种生物过程的核心，包括转录后调控、基因沉默和病毒复制。虽然用于 RRI 预测的传统计算方法依赖于序列互补性和热力学模型，但深度学习方法，特别是那些利用长短期记忆 (long short-term memory, LSTM) 网络的方法，在建模 RNA 行为的复杂性方面已展现出巨大潜力。本章综述了基于 LSTM 的 RRI 预测方法的最新进展，讨论了关键数据集和评估指标，并概述了这一新兴领域的未来方向。
