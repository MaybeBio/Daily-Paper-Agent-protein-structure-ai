Transformer Models in Vibrational Spectroscopy: A Review.

Jafari, Jamile Mohammad; Bocklitz, Thomas
Molecules (Basel, Switzerland) · 2026-10-27 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该综述聚焦Transformer模型在振动光谱分析中的应用，虽涉及光谱到结构预测，但核心是光谱分析而非蛋白质结构计算，且未针对蛋白质体系，方法可迁移性有限。

摘要

Transformer models have attracted increasing interest in vibrational spectroscopy because of their ability to capture long-range dependencies in spectral data. These models have been applied to analytical tasks including spectral preprocessing, classification, quantitative analysis, multimodal data fusion, and spectra-to-structure prediction. Their flexible architectures support applications in Raman, infrared (IR), near-infrared (NIR), and surface-enhanced Raman spectroscopy (SERS), with examples across chemistry, biology, and materials science. Transformer-based models also offer opportunities to integrate chemical, physical, and biological information in spectral analysis and support interdisciplinary research involving chemometrics, deep learning, and experimental spectroscopy. In this review, we elucidate the fundamental principles of Transformer models and their wide-ranging applications in vibrational spectroscopy, spanning from baseline correction and deconvolution to molecular identification, disease diagnosis, food authentication, and quantitative property prediction. Finally, as Transformer models may not be suitable for every analytical task or consistently outperform simpler approaches, we discuss current challenges and future trends in Transformer models in vibrational spectroscopy and related analytical sciences.

摘要翻译

Transformer模型因其能够捕捉光谱数据中的长程依赖性，在振动光谱学领域引起了越来越多的关注。这些模型已应用于多种分析任务，包括光谱预处理、分类、定量分析、多模态数据融合以及光谱到结构的预测。其灵活的架构支持在拉曼光谱、红外光谱（IR）、近红外光谱（NIR）和表面增强拉曼光谱（SERS）中的应用，并在化学、生物学和材料科学中均有实例。基于Transformer的模型还提供了在光谱分析中整合化学、物理和生物信息的机会，并支持涉及化学计量学、深度学习和实验光谱学的跨学科研究。在本综述中，我们阐明了Transformer模型的基本原理及其在振动光谱学中的广泛应用，涵盖从基线校正和解卷积到分子识别、疾病诊断、食品真实性鉴定以及定量性质预测等方面。最后，由于Transformer模型可能并非适用于所有分析任务，也不一定总能优于更简单的方法，我们讨论了Transformer模型在振动光谱学及相关分析科学中当前的挑战和未来趋势。
