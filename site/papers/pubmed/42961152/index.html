TRAILS-MD: Mapping complex conformational landscapes and transition pathways via lightweight lineage-aware adaptive sampling.

Maity, Dibyendu; Majumdar, Rupak; Chakrabarty, Suman
The Journal of chemical physics · 2026-10-30 · 仅摘要 · 原文

提出轻量级自适应采样框架TRAILS-MD，通过轨迹谱系保留和机器学习集体变量，高效映射蛋白质构象景观与转变路径，直接服务于蛋白质构象采样与MD模拟。

摘要

Molecular dynamics (MD) simulations are fundamentally limited by the timescale gap between rapid atomic fluctuations and slow, activated conformational transitions. While adaptive sampling accelerates the exploration of metastable states, conventional implementations often obscure the dynamical connectivity between discovered basins, confusing broad configurational coverage with the sampling of physically viable transition pathways. Here, we present a lightweight, engine-agnostic adaptive sampling framework (TRAILS-MD) that iteratively launches short trajectory ensembles across either fixed or on-the-fly machine-learned (ML) collective-variable (CV) spaces. A defining feature of our approach is explicit trajectory lineage preservation, which allows for the rigorous reconstruction of connected pathways from highly parallelized, disjointed exploration stages. We demonstrate the efficacy of this framework across a hierarchy of molecular challenges: overcoming low-dimensional barriers in peptide isomerization, navigating entropic bottlenecks in protein folding (Trp-cage and WW domain), mapping transient exit tunnels during ligand unbinding (HSP90), and exploring large conformational changes in adenylate kinase. Finally, we show how lineage-aware exploratory ensembles can seamlessly seed unbiased production trajectories for the construction of Markov state models (MSMs). By bridging the gap between deep-learning-driven coordinate discovery and strict parent-child trajectory tracking, this framework democratizes advanced data-driven sampling for routine computational biophysics workflows.

摘要翻译

分子动力学（molecular dynamics, MD）模拟从根本上受限于快速原子波动与缓慢、激活构象转变之间的时间尺度差距。尽管自适应采样加速了对亚稳态的探索，但传统实现往往模糊了所发现盆地之间的动力学连接，将广泛的构象覆盖与物理可行转变路径的采样混为一谈。在此，我们提出一个轻量级、引擎无关的自适应采样框架（TRAILS-MD），该框架在固定或实时机器学习（machine-learned, ML）集体变量（collective-variable, CV）空间中迭代启动短轨迹集合。我们方法的一个显著特征是显式轨迹谱系保留，这使得能够从高度并行化、不连续的探索阶段中严格重建连接路径。我们通过一系列分子挑战展示了该框架的有效性：克服肽异构化中的低维势垒、导航蛋白质折叠（Trp-cage和WW结构域）中的熵瓶颈、绘制配体解离（HSP90）过程中的瞬时出口通道，以及探索腺苷酸激酶中的大尺度构象变化。最后，我们展示了谱系感知的探索性集合如何无缝地播种无偏生产轨迹，以构建马尔可夫状态模型（Markov state models, MSMs）。通过弥合深度学习驱动的坐标发现与严格父子轨迹追踪之间的鸿沟，该框架为常规计算生物物理学工作流程普及了先进的数据驱动采样。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：如何在不牺牲计算效率的前提下，从高度并行化、碎片化的自适应采样轨迹中重建物理上连通的构象转变路径？
  • 为什么重要：MD 模拟受限于时间尺度鸿沟，自适应采样虽能加速亚稳态探索，但若丢失轨迹间的动力学连接，则无法区分「构象覆盖」与「真实转变路径」，影响对功能相关构象转变机制的理解（如配体结合/解离、酶催化构象变化）。
  • 现有方法不足：传统自适应采样（如加权集成、MSM 导向采样）通常只关注状态覆盖或统计收敛，忽略轨迹间的谱系关系；而严格路径采样方法（如 TPS、milestoning）计算成本高、实现复杂，难以用于常规工作流。
  • 精确研究问题：Can a lightweight, lineage-aware adaptive sampling framework reconstruct connected transition pathways from parallelized short trajectory ensembles without sacrificing sampling efficiency?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：一个迭代式自适应采样框架，在固定或 ML 学习的 CV 空间中发射短轨迹集合，并显式记录每条轨迹的父-子谱系关系。
  2. 核心洞察：轨迹谱系信息是连接碎片化探索片段的「胶水」——通过保留「谁从谁出发」的关系，可以在事后严格重建跨越多个采样轮次的连通路径，而无需额外计算。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：在并行化、分布式计算日益普及的背景下，任何采样方法都应默认记录数据的「生成历史」元数据，这不仅是可重复性的要求，更是从碎片化计算中恢复全局动力学信息的低成本途径。

07 方法总览

  • 输入：分子结构（PDB 或初始构象）、力场参数、CV 定义（固定或 ML 生成）、采样预算（总轨迹数/总 MD 步数）
  • 输出：连通构象路径、亚稳态集合、可转移给 MSM 构建的轨迹数据
  • 核心模块： 1. CV 空间定义（固定 CV 或 on-the-fly ML 学习） 2. 自适应选点（基于当前构象分布选择新一轮起始点） 3. 短轨迹发射（并行 MD 模拟） 4. 谱系记录（父-子关系元数据） 5. 路径重建（基于谱系树连接亚稳态） 6. MSM 对接（可选，用谱系轨迹作为 unbiased 数据）
  • 训练：不涉及传统 ML 训练（除非使用 on-the-fly ML-CV，则涉及 CV 模型的在线更新）
  • 工具：引擎无关（可适配 GROMACS, OpenMM, AMBER 等）
  • 假设：短轨迹集合的谱系关系足以重建真实动力学路径；CV 空间能分辨关键中间态
  • 流程：初始构象 → 定义 CV → 发射第一批短轨迹 → 分析构象分布 → 选择新起始点 → 发射下一批（记录父子关系）→ 迭代直至收敛 → 基于谱系树重建路径 → 可选：用谱系轨迹构建 MSM

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围：仅覆盖摘要中提及的四个测试系统，未涉及膜蛋白、 intrinsically disordered proteins 等更复杂场景。
  • oracle/真值输入：CV 空间的选择（固定或 ML 学习）是方法的关键输入，其质量直接影响结果；摘要未说明 CV 选择的自动化程度。
  • 端到端状态：方法框架完整，但代码未公开（摘要未提及），可复现性待验证。
  • 算力成本：声称「lightweight」，但未提供与现有方法的定量对比。
  • 历史数据依赖：谱系追踪需要记录所有轨迹的元数据，存储开销未讨论。
  • 模型依赖：引擎无关是设计目标，但实际实现中是否所有引擎都支持所需元数据记录未说明。
  • 最难情形：大尺度构象变化（腺苷酸激酶）是测试中最复杂场景，但未与专门针对 domain motion 的方法对比。
  • 群体/领域边界：结论限于「常规计算生物物理工作流」场景，未声称适用于超大规模并行或特殊系综。
  • 不确定性：未提供误差估计或多次重复实验的变异性分析。

有边界的复述：TRAILS-MD 在四个测试系统中展示了通过谱系追踪从并行短轨迹重建连通路径的可行性，且与 MSM 工作流兼容；但其相对现有方法的定量优势、代码可用性、以及在大规模/复杂系统中的表现尚未在摘要中证实。

14 学到什么

Agent 提炼的知识候选：

  1. 谱系追踪作为通用元数据模式：在自适应采样中记录「谁从谁出发」的关系，是低成本恢复动力学连通性的关键。可迁移到本课题的任意增强采样工作流（如结合 AlphaFold 初始构象的 MD 采样），作为标准实践。

  2. 覆盖度与连通性的解耦：本文明确区分「构象覆盖」与「路径连通」两个目标，提示本课题在评估采样方法时应同时报告两个指标，而非仅看亚稳态发现数。

  3. 轻量级设计哲学：引擎无关、不依赖特定 CV 形式的设计，使方法易于集成到现有工作流。对本课题的启示：新方法应优先考虑与主流 MD 引擎和 ML 工具的兼容性。

  4. ML-CV 与采样的在线集成：on-the-fly ML-CV 的提法暗示了「学习-采样」交替进行的范式，可迁移到本课题中与深度学习结构预测/构象生成方法的结合。

  5. MSM 的无缝对接：将谱系轨迹直接用于 MSM 构建，避免了传统方法中「先采样后处理」的断裂。对本课题：任何采样方法都应考虑输出格式与下游动力学建模工具的兼容性。

  6. 层次化测试策略：从简单（肽）到复杂（酶）的测试系统设计，是验证采样方法通用性的有效路径，可借鉴到本课题的方法评估中。

15 与已有知识连接

  • 相似方法：加权集成模拟（WE）也通过轨迹复制/剪枝保持路径系综，但 WE 需要预定义反应坐标；TRAILS-MD 的谱系追踪可视为 WE 思想的轻量简化，但更强调事后重建而非在线控制。
  • 组合方向：与 AlphaFold 生成的构象集合结合，用 TRAILS-MD 探索预测结构间的转变路径，是自然延伸。
  • 冲突/差异：与 metadynamics 等偏置采样不同，TRAILS-MD 不施加偏置力，而是通过选点策略加速探索，这保留了 unbiased 轨迹用于 MSM 的优势。
  • 可迁移领域：配体结合/解离路径映射（如本文 HSP90 案例）与分子对接后的构象精修直接相关；酶构象变化（腺苷酸激酶）与催化机制研究相关。
  • 候选方向：将谱系追踪思想应用于粗粒化 MD 或增强采样与 ML 势函数（如 NNP）的结合中，可能进一步降低成本。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a conceptually appealing framework that addresses a genuine limitation in adaptive sampling, namely the loss of dynamical connectivity information during parallelized exploration. The lineage preservation idea is sensible and potentially useful. However, the abstract provides no quantitative evidence, no comparison against existing methods, and no methodological detail sufficient to assess technical soundness. The claims of broad applicability across four distinct molecular systems are stated without supporting data. The manuscript cannot be evaluated for scientific rigor from the supplied material.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Computational biophysicists and cheminformaticians working on enhanced sampling, Markov state model construction, and rare event simulation would be the primary audience. Researchers developing machine-learned collective variables and those applying adaptive sampling to drug discovery or protein dynamics would also find the framework relevant if the claims are substantiated.
  • Major strengths The conceptual framing is clear and addresses a real gap in the field. The engine-agnostic design and the explicit focus on lineage preservation as a means to reconstruct pathways from disjointed exploration stages is a sensible and potentially impactful idea. The stated goal of bridging ML-driven coordinate discovery with rigorous trajectory tracking is timely.

Major Concerns

  • Concern ID R1-M1
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Evidence sufficiency
  • Claim pointer The authors claim demonstration of the framework across peptide isomerization, Trp-cage and WW domain folding, HSP90 ligand unbinding, and adenylate kinase conformational change.
  • Evidence pointer Abstract only; no figures, tables, or quantitative results provided
  • Concern The abstract asserts successful application to four distinct molecular systems but provides no quantitative outcomes, no comparison to conventional adaptive sampling or other enhanced sampling methods, and no metrics of success such as convergence times, pathway fluxes, or free energy profiles.
  • Why it matters Without quantitative evidence, the reader cannot assess whether the framework actually works, how well it performs relative to existing approaches, or whether the claimed demonstrations are meaningful. The core scientific contribution of the paper rests on these results.

  • Resolution test The full manuscript must provide quantitative results for each system, including convergence metrics, comparison against appropriate baselines, and statistical uncertainty estimates.

  • Concern ID R1-M2

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Technical soundness
  • Claim pointer The authors claim that lineage preservation allows rigorous reconstruction of connected pathways from highly parallelized, disjointed exploration stages.
  • Evidence pointer Abstract only; methodological details not provided
  • Concern The abstract does not describe how lineage information is stored, propagated, or used to reconstruct pathways. It is unclear how trajectories launched from different states are connected, how the framework handles branching and merging of lineages, and how the reconstruction avoids artifacts from the adaptive selection process.
  • Why it matters The central methodological claim of the paper is the lineage-aware pathway reconstruction. Without a clear description of the algorithm, its computational overhead, and its limitations, the technical validity of the approach cannot be evaluated.

  • Resolution test The full manuscript must provide a detailed algorithmic description, pseudocode or formal specification, complexity analysis, and validation of the reconstruction procedure on systems with known pathways.

  • Concern ID R1-M3

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Claim substantiation
  • Claim pointer The authors claim that lineage-aware exploratory ensembles can seamlessly seed unbiased production trajectories for Markov state model construction.
  • Evidence pointer Abstract only; no MSM validation results provided
  • Concern The abstract states that the framework can seed MSM construction but provides no evidence that the resulting MSMs are statistically valid, that the seeding improves MSM quality relative to standard approaches, or that the lineage information is correctly propagated into the MSM framework.
  • Why it matters The MSM application is presented as a key outcome of the framework. Without validation of the MSMs, including implied timescale tests, Chapman-Kolmogorov checks, and comparison to reference simulations, the claim of seamless integration is unsupported.
  • Resolution test The full manuscript must include MSM validation metrics, comparison to MSMs built from conventional adaptive sampling or unbiased simulations, and a demonstration that lineage-aware seeding improves model quality or statistical efficiency.
