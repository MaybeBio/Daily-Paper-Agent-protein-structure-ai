Identification of a novel equine rotavirus B from Missouri in 2025, which is distantly related to group B rotaviruses.

Soni, Shalini; Hause, Ben; Uprety, Tirth; Yu, Jieshi; Shuisong, Ni; Eertink, Lianne G; Fang, Zheng; Giri, Kripa; Erdal, Erol; Morgan, Jocelynn; Lawson, Steven; Nelson, Eric; Graves, Amy J; Morrow, Jennifer K; Adam, Emma N; Sheng, Zizhang; Kennedy, Michael A; Wang, Dan; Li, Feng

摘要

Despite the wide circulation of group B rotaviruses in animals and humans, rotavirus B was not found in horses until 2021 in Kentucky, USA. Little is known about the genetic diversity and evolution of rotavirus B in this new host. Here, we described a divergent equine rotavirus B (ERVB) from a foal with idiopathic diarrhea in Missouri (designated MO-ERVB-25). Full-genome sequencing and phylogenetic analysis revealed that MO-ERVB-25 is distantly related to all previously known group B rotaviruses, including ERVB strains identified in 2021 in Kentucky (KY-ERVB-21). The mean nucleotide substitution rate of the VP4 segment was estimated as 1.44 x 10(-3) substitutions/site/year, which is a magnitude higher than the VP7 segment. We further demonstrated that MO-ERVB-25, like human RVB, binds chemically neutral LacNAc-containing complex N-linked glycans, while KY-ERVB-21 recognizes negatively charged polysialic acid (polySia) with alpha-2,8-linkage, which is a functional receptor for unrelated enteric human adenovirus 52. Using the AlphaFold-enabled structure prediction, we showed that both MO-ERVB-25 and human RVB VP8* lack a novel C-terminal alpha2 helix that is only observed in the KY-ERVB-21 VP8*, which may help explain the difference in glycan binding specificities. Finally, screening of foal samples by the reverse transcription quantitative PCR assay identified 4.2% of idiopathic foal diarrhea samples (6 out of 140) positive for MO-ERVB-25. These results suggest that MO-ERVB-25 represents a new rotavirus B subgroup that circulates in foals. Its circulation, together with KY-ERVB-25 subgroup, raises the possibility of reassortment and antigenic shift as mechanisms of rotavirus B evolution in equines. IMPORTANCE: Equine RVB (ERVB) has emerged as a significant cause of foal diarrhea since 2021. Our study describes a highly divergent RVB strain identified in a foal with severe diarrhea in 2025. This ERVB variant exhibited low amino acid identity compared with previously reported ERVB strains. The novel ERVB strain escaped routine diagnostic detection. Sequence analysis demonstrated that this new strain grouped with bovine, porcine, caprine, and equine RVB but formed a distinct, independent branch. In addition, glycan array studies revealed the substantial difference in glycan recognition between new and previously described ERVB strains, which is likely determined by the presence or absence of a novel C-terminal alpha2 helix in the VP8* receptor-binding domain. These results suggest that this novel ERVB variant may co-circulate with existing ERVB strains in horses, which may facilitate rapid viral evolution through antigenic diversity, a property previously reported in rotaviruses from other hosts.

摘要翻译

尽管B组轮状病毒在动物和人类中广泛传播，但直到2021年在美国肯塔基州才在马匹中发现B组轮状病毒。关于该病毒在新宿主中的遗传多样性和进化知之甚少。在此，我们描述了一种来自密苏里州一匹患有特发性腹泻的幼驹的差异性马B组轮状病毒（ERVB）（命名为MO-ERVB-25）。全基因组测序和系统发育分析显示，MO-ERVB-25与所有先前已知的B组轮状病毒（包括2021年在肯塔基州发现的ERVB毒株（KY-ERVB-21））关系较远。VP4片段的平均核苷酸替代率估计为1.44 x 10(-3) 替代/位点/年，比VP7片段高一个数量级。我们进一步证明，MO-ERVB-25与人RVB类似，能结合化学中性的含LacNAc复合N-连接聚糖，而KY-ERVB-21识别带有α-2,8-键合的负电荷多糖唾液酸（polySia），后者是不相关的人类肠道腺病毒52的功能性受体。利用AlphaFold支持的结构预测，我们显示MO-ERVB-25和人RVB VP8*均缺乏仅在KY-ERVB-21 VP8*中观察到的新型C端α2螺旋，这可能有助于解释聚糖结合特异性的差异。最后，通过逆转录定量PCR检测幼驹样本，发现4.2%的特发性幼驹腹泻样本（140份中的6份）对MO-ERVB-25呈阳性。这些结果表明，MO-ERVB-25代表了一种在幼驹中循环的新B组轮状病毒亚群。其与KY-ERVB-25亚群共同循环，提出了重配和抗原转移作为马属动物中B组轮状病毒进化机制的可能性。重要性：马RVB（ERVB）自2021年以来已成为幼驹腹泻的重要原因。我们的研究描述了一种在2025年患有严重腹泻的幼驹中鉴定出的高度差异性RVB毒株。该ERVB变体与先前报道的ERVB毒株相比，氨基酸同一性较低。新型ERVB毒株逃过了常规诊断检测。序列分析表明，该新毒株与牛、猪、山羊和马RVB聚为一组，但形成了一个独特且独立的支系。此外，聚糖阵列研究揭示了新ERVB毒株与先前描述的ERVB毒株在聚糖识别上的显著差异，这很可能由VP8*受体结合域中是否存在新型C端α2螺旋决定。这些结果表明，这种新型ERVB变体可能马匹中与现有ERVB毒株共同循环，这可能通过抗原多样性促进病毒快速进化，这一特性先前已在其他宿主的轮状病毒中报道过。
