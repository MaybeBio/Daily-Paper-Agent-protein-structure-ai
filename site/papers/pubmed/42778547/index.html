Protein entanglement misfolding influences whether proteins undergo proteasomal degradation or persist in near-native misfolded states.

Jiang, Yang; Jain, Anushka; Ghaemmaghami, Sina; P O'Brien, Edward
Nature communications · 2026-09-24 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该研究结合蛋白质结构信息、泛素化质谱数据和粗粒化折叠模拟，探究天然缠结蛋白的错折叠与蛋白酶体降解的关系，属于蛋白质结构相关的计算模拟应用，但方法创新性一般。

摘要

A novel class of protein misfolding involving changes in entanglement status occurs across the bacterial cytosolic proteome and likely exists in many other organisms. Here, we test whether this class of misfolding has measurable consequences for protein homeostasis by examining its relationship with ubiquitin-mediated proteasomal degradation immediately after protein synthesis. Integrating protein structural information with ubiquitin mass spectrometry (Ubq-MS) data from human fibroblasts, we find that proteins containing native non-covalent lasso entanglements (NCLEs), which are known to be more prone to misfolding, are 93% (95% Confidence Interval: 44-160%) more likely to be ubiquitinated and targeted for proteasomal degradation than proteins lacking native entanglements. Coarse-grained folding simulations further show that ubiquitinated proteins with native entanglements are four-fold more likely to misfold than non-ubiquitinated proteins without entanglements. These results suggest that entanglement misfolding, primarily through failure to form native entanglements, increases susceptibility to proteasomal degradation. We further estimate that approximately one-third of the globular proteome populates near-native entanglement-misfolded states that evade proteasomal degradation because they remain structurally similar to the native ensemble. Given that entanglement misfolding is inherent to the polymeric nature of proteins, these findings are likely applicable across diverse organisms.

摘要翻译

一类涉及缠结状态改变的蛋白质错误折叠新类别，发生在细菌胞质蛋白质组中，并可能存在于许多其他生物体中。在此，我们通过检验其与蛋白质合成后立即发生的泛素介导的蛋白酶体降解之间的关系，来测试这类错误折叠是否对蛋白质稳态具有可测量的后果。将蛋白质结构信息与来自人成纤维细胞的泛素质谱（Ubq-MS）数据整合，我们发现含有天然非共价套索缠结（NCLEs）的蛋白质——已知这些蛋白质更易发生错误折叠——被泛素化并靶向蛋白酶体降解的可能性比缺乏天然缠结的蛋白质高93%（95%置信区间：44-160%）。粗粒化折叠模拟进一步表明，具有天然缠结的泛素化蛋白质发生错误折叠的可能性是无缠结非泛素化蛋白质的四倍。这些结果表明，缠结错误折叠，主要通过未能形成天然缠结，增加了对蛋白酶体降解的敏感性。我们进一步估计，大约三分之一的球状蛋白质组接近天然缠结错误折叠状态，这些状态因在结构上仍与天然集合相似而逃避了蛋白酶体降解。鉴于缠结错误折叠是蛋白质聚合性质的固有特征，这些发现可能适用于多种生物体。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题: 蛋白质缠结错误折叠（entanglement misfolding）是否影响新生蛋白质被泛素-蛋白酶体系统（UPS）降解的命运？具体而言，含天然 NCLE 的蛋白质是否更易被泛素化降解，以及未被降解的缠结蛋白是否以近天然态错误折叠形式持续存在？
  • 为什么重要: 蛋白质错误折叠与多种疾病相关，但传统研究聚焦于聚集（aggregation）和分子伴侣系统。缠结错误折叠是一类新发现的、涉及蛋白质拓扑变化的错误折叠机制，其与泛素-蛋白酶体降解系统的关系尚不清楚。理解这一关系对蛋白质稳态调控、疾病机制和蛋白质设计均有重要意义。
  • 现有方法为何不足: 此前研究主要基于细菌（大肠杆菌）的模拟和实验，缺乏在人类细胞中的验证；且未将缠结错误折叠与泛素化降解这一关键蛋白质质量控制通路联系起来。
  • 精确的 Can 问题: Can protein entanglement misfolding, specifically the failure to form native non-covalent lasso entanglements (NCLEs), increase the likelihood of nascent proteins being ubiquitinated and targeted for proteasomal degradation in human cells?

06 核心思想

1) 表面方法

本文表面上是将三类数据/工具进行整合分析： - 泛素化蛋白质组学数据：来自 Meadow et al.¹⁹ 的人类成纤维细胞数据，区分年轻泛素化蛋白（young ubiquitinated, YU）与非泛素化蛋白（non-ubiquitinated, NU）； - 结构拓扑分析：利用 AlphaFold 预测结构检测 NCLE； - 粗粒化 MD 模拟：基于 Gō 模型的温度淬灭重折叠模拟和核糖体上共翻译折叠模拟。

2) 核心洞察

本文的核心洞察在于将蛋白质的拓扑特征（缠结状态）与降解命运联系起来，并揭示了一个双向机制：

  • 含 NCLE 的蛋白质更易降解：由于 NCLE 的存在增加了错误折叠概率，错误折叠暴露疏水表面，被 E3 连接酶识别并泛素化，靶向蛋白酶体降解。
  • 近天然态错误折叠可逃逸降解：部分缠结错误折叠构象与天然态结构高度相似（高 Qnorm、低 rSASA），因此不被质量控制系统识别，以可溶但功能受损的状态持续存在。

这一洞察的关键在于区分了两种错误折叠命运：结构偏离大的错误折叠被降解，而近天然态的错误折叠逃逸检测。这解释了为什么缠结错误折叠可以在细胞内持续存在。

3) 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]

  • 拓扑特征可作为降解倾向的预测因子：蛋白质的拓扑复杂性（如 NCLE）可能是一个被忽视的降解信号预测因子，可整合到蛋白质稳定性预测模型中。
  • 近天然态错误折叠是质量控制的盲区：现有质量控制机制主要识别结构严重异常或疏水暴露的蛋白质，对结构相似但功能受损的状态缺乏有效识别，这可能是许多"可溶但无功能"蛋白质疾病的分子基础。
  • 粗粒化模拟 + 组学数据的交叉验证范式：本文展示了如何将大规模组学数据与物理模拟结合，用模拟提供机制解释，用组学数据提供统计效力。

07 方法总览

  • 输入： 1. 人类泛素化蛋白质组数据（Meadow et al.¹⁹，含 YU/NU 分类） 2. AlphaFold 预测结构（人类蛋白质组，pLDTT ≥ 85 过滤） 3. 三个独立泛素化数据集（Wagner²⁶、Kim_btz²⁷、Kim_epox²⁷）用于验证 4. UniProt 蛋白质长度和寡聚状态注释

  • 输出： 1. NCLE 存在与年轻泛素化之间的关联（OR = 1.93） 2. 20 个代表性蛋白质的粗粒化模拟错误折叠概率 3. 对缠结错误折叠命运的机制解释（降解 vs. 近天然态持续存在）

  • 模块： 1. NCLE 检测模块：基于 AlphaFold 结构，使用 Gauss 连接数和最小曲面分析（Topoly⁴⁰）检测非共价套索缠结 2. 统计关联模块：逻辑回归（Eq. 1），控制蛋白质长度作为混杂因素 3. 粗粒化模拟模块：Gō 模型温度淬灭模拟（50 条独立轨迹，2 μs），计算 Q 和 G 序参数 4. 共翻译折叠模拟模块：核糖体上连续合成模拟，使用人类核糖体结构（PDB 8G61） 5. 结构分析模块：Qnorm（Eq. 5）和 rSASA（Eq. 6）评估近天然程度

  • 流程： 1. 从 Meadow 数据中鉴定 YU 和 NU 蛋白 2. 检测这些蛋白的 NCLE 状态 3. 逻辑回归检验 NCLE 与 YU 的关联（控制长度） 4. 在独立数据集中验证 5. 随机选择 10 个 YU-E、10 个 NU-NE、10 个 NU-E 蛋白进行模拟 6. 温度淬灭模拟计算错误折叠概率 7. 比较 YU-E vs NU-NE 的错误折叠概率和近天然程度 8. 对代表性蛋白进行共翻译折叠模拟验证

  • 假设： 1. AlphaFold 预测结构能准确反映天然态拓扑（含 NCLE 状态） 2. Gō 模型能捕捉缠结错误折叠的关键特征 3. 泛素化检测（Kε-GG 肽段）能代表蛋白酶体降解靶向

09 关键公式符号

Eq. 1 - 逻辑回归模型： $$\Pr(Y_i = 1) = \frac{\exp(\eta_i)}{1 + \exp(\eta_i)}, \quad \eta_i = \beta_0 + \beta_1 X_{i,1} + \sum_{j=2}^{M} \beta_j X_{i,j}$$

  • $Y_i$：蛋白质 i 的二元结果（1 = 年轻泛素化 YU，0 = 非泛素化 NU）
  • $X_{i,1}$：主要预测变量（NCLE 存在与否）
  • $X_{i,j}$（j ≥ 2）：混杂因素（如蛋白质长度）
  • $\beta_k$：回归系数（对数优势比）

Eq. 2 - Q（天然接触分数）： $$Q = \frac{\sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in J} \mathbf{1}[(i,j) \in nc]}{\sum_{i \in I} \sum_{j \in J} \mathbf{1}[(i,j) \in nc_{\text{Native}}]}$$

  • $i, j$：残基索引，满足 $j > i + 3$
  • $I, J$：二级结构元件（α-螺旋或 β-折叠）内的残基集合
  • $nc$：当前结构中形成的天然接触集合
  • $nc_{\text{Native}}$：天然结构中的接触集合
  • 接触形成判据：Cα 距离 ≤ 1.2 × 天然距离，且天然距离 ≤ 8 Å

Eq. 3 - G（缠结变化分数）： $$G = \frac{1}{2N} \sum_{(i,j) \in nc} \left( \mathbf{1}[g_N(i,j) \neq g_N^{\text{native}}(i,j)] + \mathbf{1}[g_C(i,j) \neq g_C^{\text{native}}(i,j)] \right)$$

  • $g_N(i,j)$、$g_C(i,j)$：接触 (i,j) 的 N 端和 C 端穿线数（linking number），通过离散 Gauss 积分计算
  • $g^{\text{native}}$：天然结构中的对应值
  • $N$：天然接触总数
  • 积分值按 0.6 阈值取整（如 0.5→0, −1.7→−2）

Eq. 4 - 错误折叠概率： $$P_i^{\text{misfold}} = \frac{1}{n_i} \sum_j \frac{N_{ij}^{\text{misfold}}}{N_{ij}^{\text{total}}}$$

  • $n_i$：蛋白质 i 的模拟轨迹数
  • $N_{ij}^{\text{misfold}}$：轨迹 j 中错误折叠帧数
  • $N_{ij}^{\text{total}}$：轨迹 j 总帧数

Eq. 5 - Qnorm： $$Q_{\text{norm}}(i,j) = \frac{\langle Q_{\text{misfold}} \rangle_{ij}}{\frac{1}{n_i} \sum_j \langle Q_{\text{native}} \rangle_{ij}}$$

  • $\langle Q_{\text{misfold}} \rangle_{ij}$：轨迹 j 中错误折叠态的平均 Q
  • $\langle Q_{\text{native}} \rangle_{ij}$：轨迹 j 中天然态的平均 Q

Eq. 6 - rSASA： $$rSASA(i,j) = \frac{\langle SASA_{\text{misfold}} \rangle_{ij}}{\frac{1}{n_i} \sum_j \langle SASA_{\text{native}} \rangle_{ij}}$$

  • $\langle SASA_{\text{misfold}} \rangle_{ij}$：轨迹 j 中错误折叠态的疏水残基平均 SASA
  • $\langle SASA_{\text{native}} \rangle_{ij}$：轨迹 j 中天然态的平均 SASA
  • 疏水残基：Ile, Val, Leu, Phe, Cys, Met, Ala, Gly, Trp
  • 使用 freesasa 包⁶¹计算，CG 结构先反映射为全原子

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围: 本文仅针对人类成纤维细胞中可被质谱检测的蛋白质，且仅关注共翻译/翻译后早期的泛素化事件（<6 小时）。结论不适用于所有细胞类型、所有时间尺度或所有蛋白质类别。
  • oracle/真值输入: NCLE 检测依赖 AlphaFold 预测结构，而非实验解析结构。AlphaFold 对某些蛋白质类别（如 intrinsically disordered proteins）的预测可靠性有限。泛素化检测依赖 Kε-GG 肽段富集，可能遗漏低丰度或快速周转的泛素化事件。
  • 端到端状态: 本文并非端到端 pipeline，而是将统计关联（观察性）与模拟机制（预测性）相结合。统计关联不能证明因果关系；模拟提供了机制解释但受限于粗粒化模型的精度。
  • 算力成本: 粗粒化 Gō 模型模拟计算成本相对较低（2 μs × 50 轨迹 × 20 蛋白），但未提供具体 GPU/CPU 时数。
  • 历史数据依赖: 泛素化数据来自单一研究（Meadow et al.¹⁹），虽然用三个独立数据集验证，但这些数据集均基于类似的 Kε-GG 富集方法。
  • 模型依赖: 结论高度依赖 Gō 模型的准确性。Gō 模型是结构基模型，天然偏向天然态，可能高估近天然态错误折叠的稳定性。
  • 最难情形: 对含多个结构域、存在 domain swapping 或需要伴侣辅助折叠的蛋白质，本文的简化模型可能不适用。
  • 群体/领域边界: 结论基于人类成纤维细胞，作者推测可能适用于其他生物（因缠结是聚合物固有属性），但未提供跨物种验证。

有边界的复述: 在人类成纤维细胞中，含天然 NCLE 的球状蛋白质在合成后 6 小时内被泛素化并靶向蛋白酶体降解的概率比不含 NCLE 的蛋白质高约 93%（95% CI: 44%-160%）；粗粒化模拟表明，缠结蛋白确实更易错误折叠，但未被泛素化的缠结蛋白倾向于形成近天然态（高 Qnorm、低 rSASA）的错误折叠构象，可能因此逃逸 E3 连接酶识别。

14 学到什么

以下为 Agent 提炼的知识候选，面向「蛋白质结构相关计算研究 × AI/物理模拟」课题方向。

  1. 拓扑特征可作为降解信号预测因子：NCLE 的存在与否可作为蛋白质降解倾向的结构特征，这一思路可迁移到其他结构特征（如 knot、slipknot、domain swap）与降解/稳定性关联的预测模型中。

  2. Gō 模型 + 序参数（Q, G）的缠结错误折叠表征方法：使用 Q（天然接触分数）和 G（缠结变化分数）二维空间来区分错误折叠类型，这一方法可迁移到其他拓扑相关错误折叠研究，也可用于训练 ML 分类器（如预测给定序列是否倾向于缠结错误折叠）。

  3. 粗粒化模拟 + 组学数据交叉验证范式：先用组学数据发现统计关联，再用模拟提供机制解释，最后用独立数据集验证。这种"数据驱动 + 机制驱动"的闭环设计值得借鉴。

  4. 近天然态错误折叠的概念：错误折叠不一定是结构严重异常，近天然态但功能受损的状态可能广泛存在且逃逸质量控制。这对蛋白质设计（避免设计出近天然态陷阱）和疾病机制研究有启示。

  5. 逻辑回归控制混杂因素的简洁性：仅用蛋白质长度作为协变量即可获得稳健关联，说明在组学关联分析中，简洁模型有时比复杂模型更可解释、更稳健。

  6. 共翻译折叠模拟的可行性：在核糖体上模拟连续合成过程是可行的，且能捕捉到翻译过程中的早期错误折叠事件。这为更复杂的共翻译折叠研究（如结合暂停位点、共翻译配体结合）提供了方法学参考。

  7. AlphaFold 结构 + 拓扑分析的组合：利用 AlphaFold 预测结构进行大规模拓扑特征（如 NCLE）检测，是一种低成本、可扩展的方法，适用于蛋白质组范围的拓扑分析。

  • AlphaFold 在蛋白质组学中的应用：与 AlphaFold 蛋白质组结构预测（如 AlphaFold DB）相关，本文展示了如何利用这些结构进行下游功能分析。

16 研究想法

以下为 Agent 生成的研究候选，面向「蛋白质结构相关计算研究 × AI/物理模拟」课题方向。

候选 1：基于深度学习的 NCLE 缠结错误折叠倾向预测器

  • 来源局限/观察：本文通过粗粒化模拟计算 20 个蛋白质的错误折叠概率，但样本量小，且 Gō 模型计算成本限制了全蛋白质组扩展。
  • 核心假设：蛋白质序列和结构特征（如 NCLE 位置、环长度、穿线残基的序列偏好）可用于训练 ML 模型，预测缠结错误折叠倾向。
  • 初步方法：以 AlphaFold 结构为输入，提取 NCLE 相关特征（环长度、穿线深度、接触序等），结合序列特征（进化保守性、共进化信息），训练梯度提升或图神经网络模型，以粗粒化模拟结果为标签。
  • 验证方式：在本文 20 个蛋白质上交叉验证，并在独立蛋白质集上测试；与本文的 OR = 1.93 关联比较。
  • 创新状态：unverified

候选 2：缠结错误折叠与疾病突变的功能关联分析

  • 来源局限/观察：本文发现近天然态缠结错误折叠可逃逸降解，但未探讨与疾病突变的关系。
  • 核心假设：疾病相关突变可能通过改变 NCLE 形成倾向（增加或减少）来影响蛋白质命运，导致功能丧失或毒性获得。
  • 初步方法：收集 ClinVar/UniProt 中疾病突变，对含 NCLE 的蛋白质进行突变体结构预测（AlphaFold/ESMFold），计算突变对 NCLE 稳定性的影响（通过 MD 或能量计算），统计疾病突变是否富集在 NCLE 关键残基。
  • 验证方式：与本文的泛素化数据交叉分析，检验疾病突变是否影响泛素化倾向。
  • 创新状态：unverified

候选 3：共翻译缠结错误折叠的序列编码规则

  • 来源局限/观察：本文仅测试了 2 个蛋白质的共翻译折叠，但发现早期错误折叠事件对最终命运有决定性影响。
  • 核心假设：共翻译缠结错误折叠的发生取决于新生链的延伸速率、核糖体暂停位点与 NCLE 形成关键残基的相对位置。
  • 初步方法：使用粗粒化共翻译折叠模拟（如本文方法），对一组含 NCLE 的蛋白质进行系统模拟，改变延伸速率和暂停模式，建立"序列位置-暂停-错误折叠"的定量关系。
  • 验证方式：与核糖体 profiling 数据（翻译速率图谱）交叉验证，检验预测的暂停位点是否与实验观察一致。
  • 创新状态：unverified

候选 4：基于扩散模型的缠结错误折叠构象生成

  • 来源局限/观察：本文的 Gō 模型只能采样有限的错误折叠构象，且依赖天然结构信息。
  • 核心假设：扩散模型（如 AlphaFold 3 的生成框架）可以学习缠结错误折叠构象的分布，生成多样化的近天然态错误折叠结构。
  • 初步方法：以含 NCLE 的蛋白质为训练集，使用结构扩散模型生成错误折叠构象，用 Q-G 空间和 rSASA 评估生成构象的多样性及与模拟结果的一致性。
  • 验证方式：与本文的粗粒化模拟结果对比，检验生成构象是否覆盖模拟采样的错误折叠态。
  • 创新状态：unverified

候选 5：缠结错误折叠对蛋白质-蛋白质相互作用界面可及性的影响

  • 来源局限/观察：本文聚焦于降解命运，但近天然态错误折叠可能影响蛋白质的相互作用网络。
  • 核心假设：近天然态缠结错误折叠可能遮蔽或暴露蛋白质相互作用界面，导致功能获得或丧失。
  • 初步方法：对本文识别的近天然态错误折叠构象进行界面分析（如 PISA、FoldX），比较错误折叠态与天然态的界面可及性差异；结合蛋白质相互作用数据库（如 BioGRID）分析缠结蛋白的相互作用网络特征。
  • 验证方式：选择代表性蛋白进行实验验证（如交联质谱或共免疫沉淀）。
  • 创新状态：unverified

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The manuscript addresses a potentially interesting question: whether entanglement-related misfolding has measurable consequences for protein homeostasis, specifically proteasomal degradation. The combination of proteome-wide ubiquitination data with coarse-grained simulations is a reasonable approach. However, the current evidence base has several limitations. The statistical association between NCLEs and ubiquitination is based on a binary classification of proteins as ubiquitinated or not, which may be sensitive to thresholds and data quality. The simulation component relies on a coarse-grained model with parameters that are not fully justified, and the sample size of 10 proteins per group is small. The central claim that near-native misfolded states evade degradation is supported only indirectly, through simulation metrics (Qnorm, rSASA) rather than direct experimental evidence. The manuscript would be strengthened by additional validation, sensitivity analyses, and a clearer articulation of the causal model linking misfolding to ubiquitination.

  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers in protein folding, proteostasis, and protein quality control would be the primary audience. The work connects a structural feature (entanglement) to a functional outcome (degradation), which could interest those studying the molecular basis of protein misfolding diseases. Computational biologists working on coarse-grained simulations of protein folding may also find the methodological approach relevant. The claim that near-native misfolded states are widespread and persistent could have implications for understanding age-related loss of protein function.

  • Major strengths 1. The question is novel and timely, connecting a recently described class of misfolding to a well-studied degradation pathway. 2. The use of existing proteome-wide ubiquitination data is efficient and provides a broad context. 3. The combination of statistical analysis with mechanistic simulations is a thoughtful approach. 4. The authors acknowledge limitations of their simulation model and discuss alternative interpretations.

  • Major Concerns

  • Concern ID R1-M1

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Statistical validity
  • Claim pointer "proteins containing native non-covalent lasso entanglements (NCLEs) are 93% more likely to be ubiquitinated and targeted for proteasomal degradation than proteins lacking native entanglements"
  • Evidence pointer Results section "Natively entangled proteins are 93% more likely to be ubiquitinated"; Methods "Logistic regression analysis"; Table S1 (not provided)
  • Concern The logistic regression analysis relies on a binary classification of proteins as ubiquitinated (YU) or non-ubiquitinated (NU) based on detection of Kε-GG peptides. This classification is highly dependent on detection limits, peptide coverage, and the threshold used to define "young" ubiquitination. The authors do not report how many proteins were classified as YU versus NU, what fraction of the proteome each represents, or how sensitive the odds ratio is to the choice of thresholds. The 93% effect size is reported with a confidence interval, but the underlying data quality and potential misclassification bias are not addressed. Additionally, the analysis includes only proteins with high-quality AlphaFold structures (pLDDT ≥ 85), which may introduce selection bias if structural quality correlates with other properties relevant to ubiquitination.
  • Why it matters If the binary classification is noisy or biased, the reported odds ratio may be inflated or spurious. The central claim of the paper depends on this statistical association being robust and meaningful.

  • Resolution test The authors should provide a detailed breakdown of the YU and NU protein sets, including numbers, overlap with the full proteome, and sensitivity analyses varying the thresholds for young age and ubiquitination status. They should also consider continuous measures of ubiquitination rather than binary classification, and test whether the association holds when controlling for additional covariates such as protein abundance, expression level, or structural class.

  • Concern ID R1-M2

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Causal inference
  • Claim pointer "entanglement misfolding, primarily through failure to form native entanglements, increases susceptibility to proteasomal degradation"
  • Evidence pointer Results section "Young ubiquitinated, entangled proteins are more prone to misfolding"; Figure 2G; Methods "Estimation of protein misfolding propensity"
  • Concern The causal direction is asserted but not established. The authors show that entangled proteins are more likely to be ubiquitinated and that entangled proteins have higher misfolding probabilities in simulations. However, they do not demonstrate that misfolding is the cause of ubiquitination for these specific proteins. Ubiquitination could be driven by other features correlated with entanglement, such as protein length, surface hydrophobicity, or expression levels. The simulation results show a correlation between group membership (YU vs NU) and misfolding propensity, but this does not establish that misfolding causes ubiquitination. The authors acknowledge this in the Discussion ("implies but does not establish a causal role"), but the abstract and title present the relationship more strongly.
  • Why it matters The title and abstract make a causal claim that is not supported by the evidence presented. Overstating the causal relationship could mislead readers and overstate the significance of the findings.

  • Resolution test The authors should either soften the causal language throughout the manuscript or provide additional evidence for causality, such as experiments where misfolding is modulated and ubiquitination is measured, or analyses showing that misfolding propensity mediates the relationship between entanglement and ubiquitination.

  • Concern ID R1-M3

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Simulation validity
  • Claim pointer "ubiquitinated proteins with native entanglements are four-fold more likely to misfold than non-ubiquitinated proteins without entanglements"
  • Evidence pointer Results section "Young ubiquitinated, entangled proteins are more prone to misfolding"; Figure 2G; Methods "Temperature quenching simulations"
  • Concern The coarse-grained simulation model is a Gō-based model, which is known to bias folding toward the native state and may not accurately capture misfolding kinetics or thermodynamics. The authors use a temperature quench protocol (800 K to 310 K) that is far from physiological conditions. The choice of 2 μs simulation time is arbitrary, and the claim that misfolded states are "long-lived" is based on extrapolation from these short simulations. The sample size of 10 proteins per group is small, and the selection criteria for these proteins (random selection after excluding transmembrane and elongated proteins) may not be representative. The authors do not report the uncertainty in the misfolding probability estimates beyond bootstrap confidence intervals, and the hierarchical bootstrap method is not fully described.
  • Why it matters The simulation results are used to provide a mechanistic explanation for the statistical association. If the simulations are not reliable or representative, the mechanistic interpretation is weakened.

  • Resolution test The authors should provide more details on the simulation model validation, including comparisons to experimental folding data where available. They should also report the distribution of misfolding probabilities across all proteins in each group, not just the group means, and discuss whether the results are robust to changes in simulation time, temperature protocol, and model parameters.

  • Concern ID R1-M4

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking No
  • Axis Generalizability
  • Claim pointer "approximately one-third of the globular proteome populates near-native entanglement-misfolded states that evade proteasomal degradation"
  • Evidence pointer Discussion section; based on extrapolation from simulation results
  • Concern This estimate is derived from a small sample of 10 proteins per group and relies on the assumption that the simulation results can be extrapolated to the entire proteome. The authors do not describe how this estimate was calculated, what assumptions were made, or what the uncertainty is. The claim that these states "evade proteasomal degradation" is inferred from the absence of ubiquitination, but absence of ubiquitination does not necessarily mean the protein is misfolded or that it evades degradation through other pathways.
  • Why it matters This is a strong quantitative claim that goes beyond the data presented. If the estimate is not well-supported, it could be misleading.

  • Resolution test The authors should provide a clear description of how the one-third estimate was derived, including the statistical model and assumptions. They should also discuss alternative explanations for why some entangled proteins are not ubiquitinated, such as efficient chaperone-mediated refolding or degradation by non-proteasomal pathways.

Risk / unsupported claims

  • The claim that "approximately one-third of the globular proteome populates near-native entanglement-misfolded states that evade proteasomal degradation" is not supported by the data presented. This estimate appears to be derived from a small simulation sample and is not accompanied by a clear statistical derivation.

  • The causal claim that "entanglement misfolding... increases susceptibility to proteasomal degradation" is not established. The data show an association, but causality is not demonstrated.
  • The generalizability of the findings to "diverse organisms" is asserted without direct evidence from non-human systems.
  • The statement that "90% of the non-ubiquitinated, entangled proteins exhibit misfolding" is based on a small sample (n = 10) and the criterion for "exhibiting misfolding" (95% CI not overlapping zero) is not a standard hypothesis test and may be overly liberal.
