Transmembrane Domain Composition Reflects Subcellular Localization of SNARE Proteins.

Baumann, Christian; Pulido-Quetglas, Carlos; Fasshauer, Dirk
Traffic (Copenhagen, Denmark) · 2026-11-09 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文结合统计分析和MD模拟研究SNARE蛋白跨膜结构域组成与亚细胞定位的关系，虽聚焦膜适应机制而非结构预测/生成，但以蛋白质结构（TMD）为对象并采用MD模拟方法，对蛋白结构计算研究有参考价值。

摘要

Transmembrane domains (TMDs) anchor proteins within membranes of distinct composition and biophysical properties, necessitating adaptation to each membrane's unique environment. SNARE proteins, the core mediators of eukaryotic membrane fusion, provide an ideal system to study such adaptations: they are ubiquitous across Eukaryota, with paralogs localized to different compartments that have evolved independently to match their membrane context. Here, we combine statistical analyses of approximately 14 000 SNARE TMDs with MD simulations of 28 human SNARE TMDs to uncover how TMDs have adapted to distinct membrane environments. We identify a clear compositional dichotomy that separates SNAREs of the early secretory pathway (ER and Golgi apparatus) from those of the late secretory and endosomal pathways: early TMDs are enriched in bulky Phe residues, whereas late TMDs are dominated by smaller Ile residues. The larger Phe side chains likely enhance hydrophobic interactions in loosely packed ER and Golgi membranes, while Ile likely prevents disruption of the tight lipid-lipid packing in the plasma and endosomal membranes, potentially contributing to protein localization. TMD lengths derived from MD simulations differ from those inferred by sequence-based approaches and show limited variation across compartments. Together, these results reveal how variations in membrane composition shaped SNARE TMDs across eukaryotic compartments.

摘要翻译

跨膜结构域（TMDs）将蛋白质锚定在具有不同组成和生物物理特性的膜中，要求其适应每种膜独特的环境。SNARE蛋白是真核生物膜融合的核心介导因子，为研究此类适应提供了理想系统：它们普遍存在于真核生物中，定位于不同区室的旁系同源物独立进化以匹配其膜环境。在此，我们结合约14,000个SNARE TMDs的统计分析以及28个人类SNARE TMDs的分子动力学（MD）模拟，揭示TMDs如何适应不同的膜环境。我们识别出一个明确的组成二分法，将早期分泌途径（内质网和高尔基体）的SNAREs与晚期分泌及内体途径的SNAREs区分开来：早期TMDs富含庞大的苯丙氨酸残基，而晚期TMDs则以较小的异亮氨酸残基为主。较大的苯丙氨酸侧链可能增强松散堆积的内质网和高尔基体膜中的疏水相互作用，而异亮氨酸则可能防止质膜和内体膜中紧密脂质-脂质堆积的破坏，从而可能有助于蛋白质定位。从MD模拟中得出的TMD长度与基于序列方法推断的结果不同，且在各区室间显示出有限的变化。综合来看，这些结果揭示了膜组成的变化如何塑造了真核生物各区室中的SNARE TMDs。

通过分析约 14,000 条 SNARE TMD 序列并结合 28 个人类 SNARE TMD 的全原子 MD 模拟，发现早期分泌途径（ER/高尔基体）SNARE TMD 富集 bulky 的 Phe，而晚期分泌/内体途径富集较小的 Ile，且 MD 定义的 TMD 长度在不同亚细胞区室间差异远小于序列预测所暗示的差异。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题: SNARE 蛋白的 TMD 氨基酸组成和膜嵌入长度是否以及如何反映其所在亚细胞区室的膜物理化学性质？
  • 为什么重要: SNARE 是膜融合的核心执行者，其正确定位是囊泡运输特异性的前提。TMD 作为锚定结构域，其与不同膜环境的适配机制尚不清楚。理解这一适配规律有助于揭示膜蛋白分选的一般原理，并对膜蛋白结构预测和设计提供约束。
  • 现有方法不足: 以往研究（如 Sharpe et al. 2010）主要基于序列疏水性预测 TMD 长度，但序列预测的 TMD 边界并不等同于实际的膜嵌入边界；且缺乏对单一蛋白家族（SNARE）在全部区室中的系统性比较。
  • 精确研究问题: Can the amino acid composition and MD-derived membrane-embedded length of SNARE TMDs distinguish SNAREs of the early secretory pathway (ER/Golgi) from those of the late secretory/endosomal pathway (TGN/endosomes/PM)?

06 核心思想

1) 表面方法: - 从 TRACEY 数据库收集 13,941 条 SNARE TMD 序列，按亚细胞定位分为四类（Class I: ER; Class II: 高尔基; Class III: TGN/内体; Class IV: PM/分泌囊泡） - 对 28 个人类 SNARE TMD 进行全原子 MD 模拟（DMPC 和 DOPC 两种膜），用 Cα 相对脂质磷酸基团平面的位置定义膜嵌入边界 - 计算每个 TMD 的氨基酸组成、MD 定义的膜嵌入长度、脂质可及表面积（LASA）

2) 核心洞察: - SNARE TMD 的氨基酸组成呈现清晰的二分法：早期分泌途径（ER/高尔基）富集 Phe，晚期分泌/内体途径富集 Ile。这不是简单的疏水性差异（两者疏水性相近），而是侧链体积/形状的差异——Phe 的 bulky 侧链在松散堆积的 ER/高尔基膜中能增强蛋白-脂质疏水相互作用，而 Ile 的紧凑侧链在紧密堆积的 PM/内体膜中不破坏脂质有序堆积。 - MD 模拟揭示 TMD 长度在不同区室间差异很小，挑战了「TMD 长度随膜厚度递增」的经典假说。序列预测的长度梯度主要反映的是组成差异（Phe/Ile 等）而非真实的膜嵌入长度差异。 - 单个 charged 残基（如 Vti1b 的 Glu）可显著改变 TMD 的膜内行为（诱导弯曲、水通道形成），提示 TMD 并非被动锚定结构，而是主动感知和响应膜环境的模块。

3) 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]: - 序列疏水性预测的「结构域边界」不应被当作物理边界；MD 模拟或实验确定的膜嵌入边界可能显著不同，这对所有膜蛋白的 TMD 预测和建模都有警示意义。 - 氨基酸组成（而非仅长度）是膜蛋白适应不同膜环境的主要进化变量。在设计靶向特定区室的膜蛋白或理解膜蛋白分选信号时，应更多关注组成特征（如 Phe/Ile 比例、bulky 残基分布）而非仅关注疏水核心长度。 - 单个残基的 charged 状态可对 TMD 行为产生非线性影响（水渗透、弯曲），提示在粗粒化或隐式膜模型中需谨慎处理膜内 charged 残基。

07 方法总览

  • 输入:
  • 序列数据：TRACEY 数据库中的 18,810 条 SNARE 序列，过滤后 13,941 条含 TMD 的序列
  • 结构数据：28 个人类 SNARE 的 AlphaFold2（ColabFold）预测结构（C 端 50 残基）
  • 输出:
  • TMD 氨基酸组成谱（按 Class I-IV 分组）
  • MD 定义的 TMD 膜嵌入长度（O-O 和 P-P 两种定义）
  • 每个 TMD 的 LASA
  • 模块: 1. 序列分类与 TMD 预测（GES 疏水性评分，15 残基加权滑动窗口） 2. 统计降维分析（PCA、PLS-DA） 3. 全原子 MD 模拟（CHARMM-GUI Membrane Builder，DMPC/DOPC 双层膜，100 ns × 3 重复） 4. 轨迹分析（TMD 长度、倾斜角、曲率、LASA、水渗透）
  • 工具: CHARMM-GUI, GROMACS 2024.1, R 4.4.1（bio3d, ggplot2, mdatools 等）, PyMOL, ColabFold/AlphaFold2
  • 流程: 序列收集 → GES TMD 预测 → 组成统计（PCA/PLS-DA）→ 选择 28 个人类 SNARE → AF2 结构预测 → MD 模拟（两种膜）→ 轨迹分析 → 交叉验证组成与结构特征
  • 假设:
  • SNARE 的 TMD 是单次跨膜 α-螺旋（Syx17 双螺旋 TMD 除外）
  • DMPC/DOPC 模型膜能代表早期/晚期分泌途径膜的关键物理差异
  • AF2 预测的 TMD 结构可作为 MD 模拟的合理起点

09 关键公式符号

（本文为数据驱动分析，无新推导公式。以下为理解所需的关键定义）

  1. GES 疏水性评分（Goldman-Engelman-Steitz scale）: - 每个氨基酸赋予一个疏水性值（kcal/mol），用于从序列中识别跨膜螺旋 - 滑动窗口：15 残基加权平均，中心残基权重 1.0，向两侧线性递减至 0 - 用途：初步界定 TMD 序列范围

  2. TMD 长度定义（MD）: - O–O 定义：Cα 原子位于两侧脂质酰基氧（acyl-oxygen）平面之间的残基数量 - P–P 定义：Cα 原子位于两侧磷酸（phosphate）平面之间的残基数量 - 直觉：O–O 更严格对应疏水核心，P–P 包含更多界面区域

  3. LASA（Lipid-Accessible Surface Area）: - 使用探针半径 1.88 Å（模拟亚甲基基团大小）计算 TMD 残基可被脂质接触的溶剂可及表面积 - 直觉：LASA 越大，蛋白-脂质接触面越大，疏水相互作用越强

  4. PLS-DA 判别得分: - 偏最小二乘判别分析，将氨基酸组成投影到最大化类别分离的方向 - 得分 = 组成向量 × 载荷向量；载荷反映各氨基酸对分类的贡献权重

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围: 本文仅研究 SNARE 家族 TMD，不涉及其他膜蛋白家族；仅研究 TMD 的组成和长度，不涉及 SNARE 的胞质域或 SNARE 复合物组装。
  • Oracle/真值输入: 亚细胞定位分类基于序列同源性和已知定位的参考 SNARE（TRACEY 数据库注释），非实验验证的定位数据。
  • 端到端状态: 非端到端。序列统计与 MD 模拟是分开的分析，未建立从序列到定位的预测模型。
  • 算力成本: MD 模拟为 28 蛋白 × 2 膜 × 3 重复 × 100 ns = 16.8 μs 总采样，属中等规模；未报告具体 GPU 时数。
  • 历史数据依赖: 依赖 TRACEY 数据库的序列注释质量；TMD 预测依赖 GES 疏水性评分。

14 学到什么

Agent 提炼的知识候选:

  1. TMD 组成而非长度是膜环境适应的主要变量：对膜蛋白结构预测和设计而言，应更多关注 TMD 的氨基酸组成特征（bulky vs 小侧链残基比例），而非仅优化疏水核心长度。可迁移到膜蛋白设计任务中，作为序列设计的约束条件。

  2. MD 模拟可重新定义 TMD 边界：序列预测的 TMD 边界（如 GES）与 MD 定义的膜嵌入边界存在显著差异（PCC 仅 0.29）。在膜蛋白结构预测中，建议用 MD 或至少用考虑界面极性的方法验证 TMD 边界，而非直接采用疏水性预测结果。

  3. Phe/Ile 二分法作为膜区室标志：TMD 中 Phe 富集指示早期分泌途径（ER/高尔基），Ile 富集指示晚期分泌/内体途径。这一特征可用于膜蛋白亚细胞定位的预测特征工程，或作为设计靶向特定区室的膜蛋白的序列模板。

  4. charged 残基的「snorkeling」机制：TMD 内靠近界面的 charged 残基（如 Syx5 的 Lys）可通过 snorkeling 稳定膜内取向，而中心 charged 残基（如 Vti1b 的 Glu）可诱导膜变形。在 MD 模拟中需注意 charged 残基的质子化状态对结果的影响，建议进行 pKa 敏感性分析。

  5. 大规模序列统计 + 靶向 MD 模拟的级联策略：先用大规模序列数据（~14,000）发现模式，再用小规模高精度模拟（28 蛋白 × 2 膜 × 3 重复）验证物理机制，是高效的研究范式。可迁移到其他膜蛋白家族的系统性研究中。

  6. 模型膜选择的敏感性：DMPC 和 DOPC 的厚度差异小，可能低估了 TMD 长度适应的幅度。在设计 MD 模拟研究膜蛋白-膜环境适配时，应选择覆盖生理厚度范围的多种膜组成，或加入胆固醇等关键脂质。

  7. 进化比较作为功能验证的替代：跨物种保守性分析（Phe/Ile 二分法在所有真核谱系中均存在）可作为实验验证的补充证据，尤其适用于难以进行突变实验的系统。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment This manuscript addresses a timely and important question in membrane protein biology: whether TMD composition reflects subcellular localization in the SNARE family. The authors combine a large-scale bioinformatics analysis with MD simulations, which is a powerful and appropriate approach. The central finding of a Phe/Ile dichotomy between early and late secretory pathway SNAREs is potentially significant and extends prior work by Munro. However, the manuscript has several major concerns that must be addressed before the case is fully established. These include the lack of direct statistical comparisons between classes, the limited membrane model diversity in MD simulations, the absence of functional validation, and the unclear relationship between the observed compositional differences and the proposed mechanistic role in protein sorting. The discussion is thoughtful and appropriately cautious in several places, but some claims outpace the evidence presented.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Cell biologists studying membrane trafficking and organelle identity, biophysicists interested in protein-lipid interactions, computational biologists developing tools for membrane protein analysis, and evolutionary biologists studying adaptation of protein families. The findings have implications for understanding how proteins are sorted to specific membranes and how TMD sequence features relate to membrane biophysical properties.
  • Major strengths (1) Large and well-curated dataset of approximately 14,000 SNARE TMDs from the TRACEY database, providing statistical power. (2) Combination of bioinformatics and MD simulations to address the question from complementary angles. (3) The study extends prior work by Munro to a much larger dataset and specifically to the SNARE family. (4) The authors acknowledge limitations of their model systems and discuss alternative interpretations. (5) The observation that MD-derived TMD lengths differ from sequence-based predictions is an important methodological contribution.

  • Concern ID R1-M1
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Statistical rigor
  • Claim pointer The manuscript claims a "clear compositional dichotomy" and "pronounced difference" between Classes I/II and III/IV in Phe and Ile content, and states that these differences are "robust."
  • Evidence pointer Results section "Amino Acid Composition", Figures 2A-C, Supplementary Figures S1-S4

Without quantitative statistical support, the strength and significance of the claimed dichotomy cannot be evaluated.
  • Why it matters The central claim of the paper rests on the statistical robustness of the compositional differences. If the overlap between classes is substantial, the biological significance of the dichotomy is weakened. Moreover, because TMD sequences within a species are not independent (shared evolutionary history), standard tests that assume independence may be inappropriate.
  • Resolution test Provide formal statistical comparisons between class pairs for Phe and Ile content, including effect sizes and confidence intervals. Use methods that account for phylogenetic relatedness (e.g., phylogenetic generalized least squares or permutation tests that resample at the species level). Report the proportion of variance explained by class membership versus other factors.

Risk / unsupported claims

  • The claim that TMD composition "potentially contributing to protein localization" is presented as a functional hypothesis but is not directly tested. This is appropriately hedged in places but the title and abstract imply a stronger causal relationship.

  • Scientific importance The question of how TMDs adapt to different membrane environments is of broad importance in cell biology. The findings have implications for understanding protein sorting, membrane organization, and the evolution of membrane protein families. The potential link between TMD composition and raft partitioning is interesting but not fully developed.
  • Interdisciplinary readership The topic is relevant to cell biologists, biophysicists, computational biologists, and evolutionary biologists. The manuscript is written in a way that is largely accessible to these groups, though some sections (e.g., PLS-DA methodology) may be challenging for non-specialists.
  • Technical soundness The bioinformatics approach is sound, and the MD simulations are appropriate for the questions asked. The limited diversity of membrane models in the MD simulations also limits the generalizability of the length and LASA findings.
  • Readability for nonspecialists The manuscript is generally well-written and the logic is clear. The abstract effectively summarizes the main findings. Recommendation posture

Supportive if technical concerns are resolved. The manuscript addresses an important question with a powerful approach, and the central findings are potentially significant. However, the lack of formal statistical support for the main compositional claims, the limited membrane model diversity in MD simulations, and the absence of functional validation prevent the case from being fully established as presented. The authors should be encouraged to revise and resubmit after addressing these concerns.
