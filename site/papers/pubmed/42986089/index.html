Poised for Allostery: Specific Interactions Underlie Precise Positioning of the C-Terminal Tail in Phosphofructokinase-1.

Khodam Hazrati, Mehrnoosh; Nguyen, Truong-An; Timr, Stepan
The journal of physical chemistry. B · 2026-11-05 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文以人磷酸果糖激酶-1为模型，结合增强采样MD、粗粒化建模和保守性分析，揭示无序C端尾部通过特定氢键和疏水相互作用实现变构定位的机制，属于蛋白质构象采样与变构调控的结构计算研究，与本课题高度相关。

摘要

Disordered terminal segments often regulate enzymes, but how such flexible elements achieve the spatial precision required for allostery remains poorly understood. Using human phosphofructokinase-1 as a model system, we combine enhanced-sampling molecular dynamics simulations, conservation-guided spatial analysis, prior allosteric network analysis, and coarse-grained modeling to show that allosteric positioning of the C-terminal tail, defined by residue-level contacts with the catalytic and regulatory domains, is maintained by a few key hydrogen bonding and hydrophobic interactions. This positioning enables the conserved tail residues L766 and E767 to dynamically couple the tail to the active site through a conserved surface region and to bridge the catalytic and regulatory domains in the T-state, experimentally associated with ATP inhibition. In contrast, coarse-grained simulations lacking explicit directional representation of hydrogen-bonding interactions fail to maintain stable tail docking, whereas including selected OLIVES-derived native hydrogen-bond terms improves the stability of the T-state-like docking pose. These findings support sequence-specific surface docking of a disordered enzyme tail as a physicochemical mechanism for allosteric regulation.

摘要翻译

无序末端区段常调控酶活性，但此类柔性元件如何实现变构所需的空间精度仍知之甚少。以人磷酸果糖激酶-1为模型系统，我们结合增强采样分子动力学模拟、保守性引导的空间分析、先验变构网络分析及粗粒化建模，表明C末端尾部由残基水平接触催化域和调控域所定义的变构定位，由少数关键氢键和疏水相互作用维持。该定位使保守尾部残基L766和E767通过保守表面区域动态耦合尾部与活性位点，并在T态（实验上与ATP抑制相关）中桥接催化域和调控域。相比之下，缺乏氢键相互作用显式方向表征的粗粒化模拟无法维持稳定的尾部对接，而纳入选定的OLIVES衍生天然氢键项则改善了T态样对接姿态的稳定性。这些发现支持无序酶尾部的序列特异性表面对接作为变构调控的物理化学机制。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：无序末端片段（disordered terminal segments）如何在不具备稳定三级结构的情况下，实现变构调控所需的空间精确定位？
  • 为什么重要：约 30% 真核蛋白含长无序区域，其中许多参与调控；理解无序片段如何实现「功能精度」是 IDP/IDR 功能机制的核心未解问题，也对变构药物设计有直接意义。
  • 现有方法为何不足：传统观点认为无序片段通过「fuzzy complex」或熵效应发挥功能，缺乏对「序列特异性 docking」这一机制的定量描述；常规 CG 模拟（如 Martini）因缺乏方向性氢键表示，无法捕捉此类 docking 的稳定性；而全原子 MD 采样不足，难以覆盖尾巴 docking/undocking 的构象空间。
  • 精确研究问题：Can sequence-specific surface docking of a disordered C-terminal tail, mediated by a few key hydrogen-bonding and hydrophobic interactions, maintain the spatial positioning required for allosteric regulation in PFK-1?

06 核心思想

1) 表面方法：对 PFK-1 的 C 端尾巴（约 20-30 残基）进行增强采样 MD（如 replica exchange 或 metadynamics），结合保守性分析（conservation-guided spatial analysis）识别关键残基，再用 CG 模拟（含/不含 OLIVES 氢键项）验证 docking 稳定性。

2) 核心洞察：无序尾巴的「功能精度」不来自整体折叠，而来自少数几个残基（L766, E767）形成的「锚点」——这些残基通过方向性氢键和疏水接触将尾巴「钉」在催化域与调控域之间的表面沟槽中。这种 docking 是序列特异性的（由保守残基编码），而非非特异性的静电吸附。尾巴在 docking 态中保持动态（poised），既能桥接两个结构域，又能响应变构信号。

3) 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：无序区域的功能精度可能普遍通过「少数关键残基的序列特异性 docking」实现，而非整体结构折叠。这意味着：a) 在 IDR 功能研究中，应聚焦于「锚点残基」而非整个无序区；b) CG 力场若要模拟此类机制，必须引入方向性氢键项；c) 变构调控的「空间精度」可能由局部相互作用编码，而非长程折叠。

14 学到什么

Agent 提炼的知识候选：

  1. 「锚点残基」概念：无序尾巴的功能精度可由少数保守残基（如 L766, E767）的序列特异性 docking 实现，而非整体折叠。可迁移到其他 IDR 功能研究：分析 IDR 时，先识别保守残基，再检查其是否形成 docking 锚点。

  2. 增强采样 MD + 保守性分析 + CG 验证的三步策略：先用增强采样 MD 获得构象系综，再用保守性分析聚焦关键残基，最后用 CG 模拟验证力场依赖性。此策略可迁移到其他无序区域-结构域相互作用研究。

  3. CG 力场中方向性氢键的必要性：常规 CG（如 Martini）缺乏方向性氢键表示，可能无法捕捉序列特异性 docking。对从事 CG 模拟的研究者：若目标系统涉及 IDR docking，需考虑引入方向性氢键项（如 OLIVES）。

  4. T 态 docking 与变构网络整合：将 docking 态与已知变构通路连接，可赋予 docking 功能意义。可迁移到其他变构系统：分析变构调控时，关注无序片段是否通过 docking 桥接不同结构域。

  5. 「Poised」动态概念：尾巴在 docking 态中保持动态（poised），而非刚性固定。提示 IDR 功能机制中「动态精度」的可能性——功能精度不排除构象动态。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a mechanistically plausible and potentially interesting hypothesis regarding sequence-specific surface docking of a disordered enzyme tail. However, the evidence base visible in the abstract is insufficient to establish the central claims. The causal link between specific interactions and allosteric function is asserted rather than demonstrated, and the coarse-grained modeling comparison, while suggestive, lacks the quantitative detail needed to assess its validity. The work may be of interest to the biophysical chemistry community, but the case is not currently established from the provided material.

Computational biophysicists working on enhanced-sampling methods and coarse-grained modeling of protein dynamics would also find the methodological comparison relevant. The potential generalizability of sequence-specific surface docking as a regulatory mechanism could attract a broader readership in protein science.

The combination of multiple computational approaches is appropriate for the problem. The explicit comparison between atomistic and coarse-grained models, with attention to hydrogen-bond directionality, is a thoughtful design that could yield mechanistic insight.
