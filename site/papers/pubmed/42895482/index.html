APR-Score: Identification and Scoring of Aggregation-prone Regions in Protein Sequences.

Kaushik, Rahul; Launey, Thomas
Genomics, proteomics & bioinformatics · 2026-10-19 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文开发了基于机器学习的APR-Score方法，利用序列和结构特征预测蛋白质序列中的聚集倾向区域，属于蛋白质序列层面的计算预测，与蛋白质结构计算相关但未涉及结构生成或采样。

摘要

Protein aggregation is a critical phenomenon associated with numerous neurodegenerative and systemic diseases. Understanding the propensity of proteins to aggregate is essential for unraveling the molecular basis of these disorders and for the design and engineering of novel proteins, as well as for modulating the activity/stability of enzymatic proteins. Here, we present APR-Score, a novel machine learning-based computational method designed to identify aggregation-prone regions within protein sequences. APR-Score leverages a combination of sequence-based features to predict regions of proteins that are prone to aggregation. It harnessed the information embedded in compiled sequence and structural features to achieve state-of-the-art accuracy. The method was evaluated through rigorous cross-validation on the training dataset and further validated on an independent test dataset. APR-Score demonstrated robustness and reliability in distinguishing aggregation-prone regions from non-aggregating ones, achieving a Matthews correlation coefficient (MCC) of 0.77, precision of 0.89, and F1-Score of 0.87. This tool offers significant value for researchers investigating protein aggregation-related diseases, as it can expedite the identification of aggregation-prone regions, thereby aiding the development of targeted therapies and diagnostic tools. Moreover, computational protein design and engineering efforts can be facilitated by APR-Score-based identification and screening of aggregation-prone protein sequences. APR-Score is available at https://github.com/TII-BRC/APR-Score.

摘要翻译

蛋白质聚集是与多种神经退行性疾病和系统性疾病相关的关键现象。理解蛋白质聚集的倾向性对于揭示这些疾病的分子基础、设计和工程化新型蛋白质，以及调节酶蛋白的活性/稳定性至关重要。在此，我们提出APR-Score，一种基于机器学习的新型计算方法，旨在识别蛋白质序列中的聚集倾向区域。APR-Score利用序列特征的组合来预测蛋白质中易于聚集的区域。它利用了编译的序列和结构特征中嵌入的信息，达到了最先进的准确性。该方法通过在训练数据集上进行严格的交叉验证评估，并在独立的测试数据集上进一步验证。APR-Score在区分聚集倾向区域与非聚集区域方面表现出稳健性和可靠性，实现了马修斯相关系数（MCC）为0.77，精确度为0.89，F1分数为0.87。该工具为研究蛋白质聚集相关疾病的研究人员提供了重要价值，因为它能加速聚集倾向区域的识别，从而有助于开发靶向治疗和诊断工具。此外，基于APR-Score的识别和筛选聚集倾向蛋白质序列，可以促进计算蛋白质设计和工程化工作。APR-Score可在https://github.com/TII-BRC/APR-Score获取。

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Technical soundness
  • Claim pointer The method achieved state-of-the-art accuracy with MCC 0.77, precision 0.89, and F1-score 0.87.
  • Evidence pointer Abstract, performance metrics section; location not provided
  • Concern The abstract reports performance metrics without providing any details on the dataset size, composition, or the definition of aggregation-prone regions. It is unclear how many protein sequences were used, whether the dataset is balanced, and how the ground truth labels were established.
  • Why it matters Without dataset details, the reported metrics cannot be interpreted. A small or biased dataset could inflate performance, and the absence of label definitions makes the results non-reproducible.

  • Resolution test Provide a detailed description of the training and test datasets, including sequence counts, label sources, and class balance. Report performance metrics with confidence intervals or standard deviations.

  • Concern ID R1-M4

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Validation rigor
  • Claim pointer The method was evaluated through rigorous cross-validation on the training dataset and further validated on an independent test dataset.
  • Evidence pointer Abstract, validation description; location not provided
  • Concern The abstract does not specify the cross-validation scheme (e.g., k-fold, leave-one-out), the number of folds, or the relationship between the training and independent test datasets. It is unclear whether the independent test set was truly independent or derived from the same source.
