A dual-time-scale cooperative memory mechanism for spiking neural networks.

Liu, Junxiu; Zhang, Zhaoxin; Fu, Qiang; Luo, Yuling; Qin, Sheng; Ouyang, Xue
Neural networks : the official journal of the International Neural Network Society · 2026-10-05 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文研究脉冲神经网络的跨时间尺度协同记忆机制，用于多模态分类和关联记忆，不涉及蛋白质结构计算或相关AI/物理模拟方法，与本课题无关。

摘要

Spiking neural networks (SNNs), as core models of brain-inspired computing, simulate the computational mechanisms of biological neural systems. However, existing models lack the division of labor and collaboration mechanisms found in biological fast and slow neural populations, making it difficult for single-timescale neural populations to simultaneously capture transient events and maintain long-term dependencies. Furthermore, these models are deeply coupled with specific data and tasks. Therefore, a spiking neural network with dual-timescale cooperative memory is proposed to simulate the division of labor and collaboration between biological fast and slow neural populations. This architecture employs a data-adaptive feature encoder to map multimodal inputs into a unified space, with its core being a dual-timescale collaborative reservoir. The fast reservoir captures transient events, while the slow reservoir maintains long-term dependencies. The two exchange information through learnable cross-modulation. Additionally, position-aware temporal attention pooling is employed to adaptively focus on key time segments, while feature decoupling diversity regularization forces the two reservoirs to learn complementary representations to prevent memory confusion. Classification and associative memory experiments were conducted on four datasets (i.e., MNIST, CIFAR-10, DVS128 Gesture, and a self-built behavior DVS dataset). In the classification experiments, the model achieved accuracy rates of 99.78%, 96.26%, 98.26% and 99.26%, respectively, outperforming single-pooling baselines with comparable parameter scales. Under various degraded query conditions, the dual-pool model consistently achieved higher Structural Similarity Index Measure scores than the single-pool model. This result demonstrates that a collaborative memory mechanism, formed by neural populations with multiple time constants, effectively enhances both the temporal modeling ability and the cross-task generalization of SNNs. It thus offers a reusable and unified backbone design for brain-inspired memory computing.

摘要翻译

脉冲神经网络（SNNs）作为类脑计算的核心模型，模拟生物神经系统的计算机制。然而，现有模型缺乏生物快慢神经群体的分工与协作机制，使得单一时间尺度的神经群体难以同时捕捉瞬态事件并维持长期依赖。此外，这些模型与特定数据和任务深度耦合。为此，提出一种具有双时间尺度协作记忆的脉冲神经网络，以模拟生物快慢神经群体之间的分工与协作。该架构采用数据自适应特征编码器将多模态输入映射到统一空间，其核心是双时间尺度协作储层。快速储层捕捉瞬态事件，而慢速储层维持长期依赖，两者通过可学习的交叉调制交换信息。此外，采用位置感知的时间注意力池化以自适应聚焦关键时间段，而特征解耦多样性正则化迫使两个储层学习互补表示以防止记忆混淆。在四个数据集（即MNIST、CIFAR-10、DVS128 Gesture和自建行为DVS数据集）上进行了分类和联想记忆实验。在分类实验中，模型分别达到99.78%、96.26%、98.26%和99.26%的准确率，优于参数规模相当的单池基线。在各种退化查询条件下，双池模型始终获得比单池模型更高的结构相似性指数得分。该结果表明，由多个时间常数的神经群体形成的协作记忆机制有效增强了SNNs的时间建模能力和跨任务泛化能力，从而为类脑记忆计算提供了可重用且统一的骨干设计。
