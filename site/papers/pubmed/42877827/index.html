Engineering catalytic promiscuity in serum albumin: synergistic roles of microenvironment, structural dynamics, and nano conjugation.

Patra, Swapan; Dey, Nilanjan
RSC advances · 2026-10-15 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该研究以牛血清白蛋白为对象，实验探究其催化水解活性及结构修饰影响，仅辅以分子对接，缺乏蛋白质结构计算建模或AI/物理模拟方法贡献，与课题关联较弱。

摘要

This study investigates the catalytic hydrolysis of p-nitrophenyl acetate (PNPA) in the presence of bovine serum albumin (BSA) as a biocatalyst, with an emphasis on the role of protein conformation, substrate concentration, and environmental factors. Further experiments with varying BSA and PNPA concentrations demonstrated Michaelis-Menten-type kinetics and a positive correlation between BSA concentration and the rate of hydrolysis. This effect was attributed to increased availability of catalytically active histidine residues and potential conformational changes that expose active sites. Comparisons with alpha-lactalbumin and free histidine under similar conditions indicated the superior catalytic efficiency of BSA, due to both crowding effects and its structural microenvironment. The effects of pH, ionic strength, and substrate alkyl chain length were also examined, revealing optimal catalytic activity at pH 7.4 and a significant rate enhancement for substrates with extended hydrophobic regions, likely due to stronger BSA-substrate interactions. Additionally, BSA structural alterations through denaturation, PEGylation, and gold nanoparticle (Au-NPs) conjugation were evaluated, showing varied catalytic performance due to differences in rigidity and nature of microenvironment etc. The molecular docking studies indicated the involvement of not only histidine but also serine and arginine residues in the catalytic activity.

摘要翻译

本研究探讨了以牛血清白蛋白（BSA）作为生物催化剂时，对硝基苯乙酸酯（PNPA）的催化水解作用，重点研究了蛋白质构象、底物浓度及环境因素的作用。通过改变BSA和PNPA浓度的进一步实验，展示了Michaelis-Menten型动力学特征，并发现BSA浓度与水解速率呈正相关。这一效应归因于催化活性组氨酸残基可用性的增加，以及可能发生的构象变化暴露活性位点。在相似条件下与α-乳白蛋白和游离组氨酸的比较表明，BSA具有更优的催化效率，这既源于拥挤效应，也归因于其结构微环境。研究还考察了pH、离子强度和底物烷基链长度的影响，结果显示在pH 7.4时催化活性最佳，且对于具有扩展疏水区域的底物，速率显著提高，这可能是由于BSA与底物之间更强的相互作用。此外，通过变性、聚乙二醇化（PEGylation）和金纳米颗粒（Au-NPs）偶联对BSA结构进行改变，评估了其催化性能，结果表明由于刚性及微环境性质等差异，催化表现各异。分子对接研究提示，催化活性不仅涉及组氨酸，还涉及丝氨酸和精氨酸残基。
