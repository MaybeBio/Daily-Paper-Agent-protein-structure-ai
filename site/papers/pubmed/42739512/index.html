Saliency-Curated Deep Learning for Predicting Receptor Status in Breast Cancer Brain Metastases.

Christodoulou, Rafail C; Christofi, Giorgos; Theofylaktou, Constantinos; Pitsillos, Rafael; Aristokleous, Iliana; Solomou, Elena E; Vassiliou, Evros; Georgiou, Michalis F
Journal of clinical medicine · 2026-09-15 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文用深度学习从MRI预测乳腺癌脑转移受体状态，属医学影像分类，不涉及蛋白质结构计算，与本课题无关。

摘要

Background: Breast cancer brain metastases (BCBMs) exhibit notable receptor discordance between primary tumors and metastases, yet obtaining intracranial biopsies is rarely practical. We created an interpretable deep learning radiogenomic model to predict estrogen receptor (ER), progesterone receptor (PR), and human epidermal growth factor receptor 2 (HER2) status from MRI scans in BCBM cases. Methods: A total of 241 post-contrast T1-weighted MRIs from 142 patients were analyzed. We developed a mask-free 3D Residual Neural Network (ResNet) ensemble trained directly on cropped, bias-corrected brain images, with comprehensive 3D geometric and intensity augmentations. The model was optimized in a multi-label setting using Asymmetric Focal Loss. Hyperparameters were fine-tuned via Bayesian optimization, and class probabilities were calibrated. Additionally, Integrated Gradients (IG) offered voxel-level saliency maps. Results: Our ResNet ensemble model achieved a micro-averaged AUROC of 0.74 and a macro-averaged AUROC of 0.67. Receptor-specific AUROCs were 0.57 for ER, 0.79 for PR, and 0.64 for HER2. The F1-scores were 0.56, 0.62, and 0.88 for ER, PR, and HER2, respectively. The held-out cohort contained only four HER2-negative patients, so threshold-dependent HER2 metrics are strongly prevalence-driven and AUROC is the more appropriate summary. Qualitatively inspected saliency maps were concentrated on enhancing metastases with limited background attribution. Conclusions: This proof-of-concept study shows that a segmentation-free, interpretable 3D Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) can be trained to capture receptor-associated patterns in post-contrast T1-weighted MRI of BCBMs. Accuracy was modest relative to published radiomics models, and the mask-free, single-sequence design is offered as a methodological contribution rather than a performance gain. These findings are preliminary, do not establish clinical utility, and require validation in larger, multi-center cohorts.

摘要翻译

背景：乳腺癌脑转移瘤（BCBMs）在原发肿瘤与转移灶之间表现出显著的受体不一致性，然而获取颅内活检在实际中很少可行。我们构建了一个可解释的深度学习放射基因组模型，用于从BCBM病例的MRI扫描中预测雌激素受体（ER）、孕激素受体（PR）和人表皮生长因子受体2（HER2）状态。方法：共分析了来自142名患者的241个增强后T1加权MRI图像。我们开发了一种无掩膜的三维残差神经网络（ResNet）集成模型，直接对裁剪、偏置校正后的脑图像进行训练，并采用全面的三维几何和强度增强。该模型在多标签设置下使用非对称焦点损失进行优化。超参数通过贝叶斯优化进行微调，并对类别概率进行了校准。此外，集成梯度（IG）提供了体素级别的显著性图。结果：我们的ResNet集成模型实现了微平均AUROC为0.74，宏平均AUROC为0.67。受体特异性AUROC分别为ER 0.57、PR 0.79和HER2 0.64。F1分数在ER、PR和HER2上分别为0.56、0.62和0.88。保留队列中仅包含四名HER2阴性患者，因此阈值依赖的HER2指标强烈受患病率驱动，AUROC是更合适的总结指标。经定性检查的显著性图集中在增强的转移灶上，背景归因有限。结论：这项概念验证研究表明，可以训练一种无需分割的可解释三维卷积神经网络（CNN），以捕捉BCBMs增强后T1加权MRI中与受体相关的模式。与已发表的放射组学模型相比，准确性适中，无掩膜、单序列设计作为方法学贡献而非性能提升提出。这些发现是初步的，不确立临床实用性，需要在更大规模、多中心队列中进行验证。
