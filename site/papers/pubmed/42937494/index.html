Divergent Catalytic Strategies of Group-6 Propane Monooxygenases in Propane-Stimulated 1,4-Dioxane Biodegradation.

Yang, Junwon; Dhakal Gaudel, Devi Kumari; Antunes, Jose Manuel Diaz; Zheng, Wenwei; Li, Mengyan
Environmental science & technology · 2026-10-27 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文研究丙烷单加氧酶对1,4-二氧六环降解的催化差异，虽涉及蛋白结构分析，但核心是酶催化功能与生物修复，结构计算建模非主要贡献，仅部分相关。

摘要

Group-6 propane monooxygenases (PRMs) catalyze the initial oxidation of 1,4-dioxane (dioxane), a widespread contaminant of emerging concern. Because their expression can be induced by propane, an inexpensive and readily deliverable substrate, PRM-containing microorganisms offer a distinct advantage for propane-based biostimulation strategies targeting dioxane removal. However, functional differences among PRMs from the three major phylogenetic clusters remain poorly understood. Here, three PRMs from Mycobacterium dioxanotrophicus PH-06, Mycobacterium marinum E11, and Mycobacterium sp. ENV421, representing phylogenetic clusters, PRM-I, PRM-II, and PRM-III, respectively, were heterologously expressed in Mycolicibacterium smegmatis mc2155. ENV421 PRM-III exhibited low substrate affinity but high catalytic capacity toward both dioxane and propane, and moderate resistance to chlorinated solvent inhibition. In contrast, E11 PRM-II displayed high affinity but lower capacity and greater inhibitor sensitivity. PH-06 PRM-I showed comparatively stronger activity toward dioxane than propane relative to the other PRMs. Protein structure analysis suggested that variation in substrate affinity and catalytic turnover is consistent with differences in active-site pocket hydrophobicity, rigidity, and catalytic geometry. Phylogenetic and compositional analyses further suggested horizontal transfer in Clusters I and III. Together, these results highlight substantial functional diversity among group-6 PRMs and offer mechanistic insights for optimizing microbial populations for effective propane-stimulated bioremediation of dioxane.

摘要翻译

Group-6 propane monooxygenases (PRMs) 催化1,4-二噁烷（dioxane）的初始氧化，1,4-二噁烷是一种广泛存在的新兴关注污染物。由于它们的表达可被丙烷诱导，而丙烷是一种廉价且易于递送的底物，含有PRM的微生物为针对二噁烷去除的丙烷基生物刺激策略提供了明显优势。然而，来自三个主要系统发育簇的PRM之间的功能差异仍知之甚少。在此，来自Mycobacterium dioxanotrophicus PH-06、Mycobacterium marinum E11和Mycobacterium sp. ENV421的三种PRM，分别代表系统发育簇PRM-I、PRM-II和PRM-III，在Mycolicibacterium smegmatis mc2155中异源表达。ENV421 PRM-III对二噁烷和丙烷均表现出低底物亲和力但高催化能力，并且对氯化溶剂抑制具有中等抗性。相比之下，E11 PRM-II显示出高亲和力但较低能力以及更大的抑制剂敏感性。PH-06 PRM-I相对于其他PRM，对二噁烷表现出比丙烷相对更强的活性。蛋白质结构分析表明，底物亲和力和催化周转的变化与活性位点口袋疏水性、刚性和催化几何形状的差异一致。系统发育和组成分析进一步表明簇I和III中存在水平转移。总之，这些结果突出了group-6 PRM之间显著的功能多样性，并为优化微生物种群以实现有效的丙烷刺激二噁烷生物修复提供了机制见解。

02 一句话总结

通过异源表达三种系统发育簇（PRM-I/II/III）的 group-6 propane monooxygenases，发现它们在底物亲和力、催化容量、抑制剂敏感性上存在显著差异，并将这些差异归因于活性位点口袋的疏水性、刚性和催化几何结构的不同。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：Group-6 propane monooxygenases（PRMs）在三个主要系统发育簇（PRM-I、II、III）之间的功能差异是什么？这些差异如何影响其在丙烷刺激下对 1,4-dioxane 的生物降解效率？
  • 为什么重要：1,4-dioxane 是广泛存在的污染物，PRM 可被廉价丙烷诱导表达，因此 PRM 微生物在生物修复中具有应用潜力。但不同 PRM 的功能差异未知，阻碍了菌株筛选与工程优化。
  • 现有方法不足：此前研究多集中于单一 PRM 或单一菌株，缺乏跨簇的系统比较；且未将酶动力学参数与蛋白质结构特征联系起来。
  • 精确研究问题：Can the functional differences among PRM-I, PRM-II, and PRM-III be explained by structural variations in their active-site pockets, and do these differences translate into distinct dioxane degradation efficiencies under propane-stimulated conditions?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：异源表达三种 PRM 于同一宿主，测定动力学参数（Km、kcat）、抑制常数、底物选择性，并进行蛋白质结构建模与比较。
  2. 核心洞察：PRM 的功能多样性并非随机，而是由活性位点口袋的物理化学性质（疏水性、刚性、几何形状）系统决定。低亲和力高容量（ENV421）与高亲和力低容量（E11）代表两种不同的催化策略，分别适合不同环境条件。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：酶功能差异可通过活性位点结构特征预测；在蛋白质工程或结构预测中，应同时考虑疏水性、刚性和几何参数，而非仅关注序列或整体折叠。

09 关键公式符号

  • Michaelis-Menten 方程：v = Vmax [S] / (Km + [S])
  • v：反应速率；Vmax：最大速率；[S]：底物浓度；Km：米氏常数（底物亲和力指标）
  • 用途：拟合动力学数据，得到 Km 和 kcat（kcat = Vmax/[E]t）
  • 直觉：Km 越低亲和力越高，kcat 越高催化越快
  • 来源：Methods 节（动力学测定）
  • 抑制常数 Ki（如适用）：用于量化抑制剂结合强度
  • 直觉：Ki 越低抑制越强
  • 来源：Results 节（抑制实验）
  • 催化效率：kcat/Km
  • 用途：比较不同 PRM 对同一底物的整体催化能力
  • 直觉：越高代表酶越高效
  • 来源：Results 节（动力学参数表）

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围：仅限三种 PRM 在体外条件下的催化性质，未涉及体内降解、菌群互作或实际场地验证。
  • oracle/真值输入：无计算预测，全部为实验数据。
  • 端到端状态：非端到端——从酶性质到修复效果的外推未验证。
  • 算力成本：不适用（无计算密集型任务）。
  • 历史数据依赖：无（独立实验）。
  • 模型依赖：结构模型为计算预测，未用晶体学或 MD 验证。
  • 最难情形：实际污染场地中多种底物共存、PRM 表达水平、菌群竞争等未覆盖。
  • 群体/领域边界：仅适用于 group-6 PRM，不推广到其他单加氧酶。
  • 不确定性：动力学参数误差未报告；结构模型精度未知。
  • 有边界的复述：在异源表达条件下，三种 PRM 表现出不同的底物亲和力、催化容量和抑制敏感性，这些差异与活性位点结构特征一致，但未证明其在自然或工程系统中的实际修复效果。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a potentially valuable comparative study of group-6 PRMs with clear relevance to bioremediation. The central claim of functional divergence across phylogenetic clusters is plausible and interesting. However, the evidence base available for review is limited to the abstract, which precludes verification of quantitative claims, structural interpretations, and the strength of the phylogenetic and compositional analyses. The work appears technically sound in concept, but the case for the mechanistic conclusions is not fully established from the supplied material.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers in environmental microbiology and bioremediation, particularly those working on emerging contaminants such as 1,4-dioxane, would find these results relevant. The study also speaks to enzymologists interested in monooxygenase structure-function relationships and to practitioners developing biostimulation strategies for contaminated sites.
  • Major strengths The study addresses a clear knowledge gap regarding functional differences among group-6 PRMs. The use of heterologous expression in a defined host is a rigorous approach for comparing enzyme properties. The integration of kinetic, inhibition, and structural analyses is commendable. The potential practical relevance for propane-based biostimulation is well articulated.
