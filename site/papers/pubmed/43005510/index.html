7

In Silico Assessment of SYNGAP1 Missense Variant Pathogenicity.

Ali, Aliaa E; Otranen, Henna-Riikka M; Lehtonen, Jukka V; Courtney, Michael J; Pentikainen, Olli T; Postila, Pekka A
Human mutation · 2026-11-10 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文利用结构预测、稳定性计算及深度学习工具（AlphaMissense、ESM1b等）对SynGAP错义变异致病性进行系统评估，并开发了整合结构可视化的预测服务器，属于蛋白质结构相关计算应用，但方法创新性有限。

摘要

SynGAP, a critical regulator at the postsynaptic density, is associated with Alzheimer's disease, tumorigenesis, and most notably, SYNGAP1-related disorders (SRD), which manifests as autism, epilepsy, and cognitive impairment. SRD is likely primarily caused by SynGAP missense (SGM) variants, whose pathogenicity is difficult to confirm experimentally. To address this issue, a wide range of structure- and/or sequence-based as well as machine learning- and deep learning-based in silico predictors were benchmarked against clinically annotated SRD data. Folding stability calculations (e.g., Foldetta) and deep learning methods ESM1b and AlphaMissense showed the highest prediction accuracy under default settings. Applying optimized nondefault thresholds, especially with AlphaMissense, could further improve predictor performance. The predictions exhibited a strong structural pattern: Pathogenic variants were highly enriched within defined structural domains, particularly the GAP and PH domains, whereas variants located in nondomain and nonmotif regions were predominantly predicted to be benign. The verified phosphosites and predicted phase separation impacting variants occupy matching sections, including the protein's N-terminal region and the vicinity of putative SH3-binding motif in the disordered regions. Thus, these regions collectively could act as phase separation-promoting segments, complementing the protein's trimerization-coupled coiled-coil domain. Finally, the SynGAP Missense (SGM) server was presented to support SynGAP variant assessment. The SGM server integrates a Data Table with top-performing predictors, a 3D Viewer for structure-level assessment and AI-generated lay summaries for all variants arising from single-base substitutions, including those not yet observed in public databases.

摘要翻译

SynGAP是突触后密度中的一个关键调控因子，与阿尔茨海默病、肿瘤发生以及最显著相关的SYNGAP1相关疾病（SRD）有关，后者表现为自闭症、癫痫和认知障碍。SRD可能主要由SynGAP错义（SGM）变异引起，其致病性难以通过实验确认。为解决此问题，一系列基于结构和/或序列以及机器学习和深度学习方法的计算机预测器，针对临床注释的SRD数据进行了基准测试。折叠稳定性计算（如Foldetta）和深度学习方法ESM1b及AlphaMissense在默认设置下显示出最高的预测准确性。应用优化的非默认阈值，尤其是使用AlphaMissense，可进一步提高预测器性能。预测结果表现出强烈的结构模式：致病性变异高度富集于特定结构域内，尤其是GAP和PH结构域，而位于非结构域和非基序区域的变异则主要被预测为良性。已验证的磷酸化位点和预测影响相分离的变异占据匹配区域，包括蛋白质的N端区域以及无序区域中推测的SH3结合基序附近。因此，这些区域共同可能作为促进相分离的片段，补充蛋白质的三聚化偶联卷曲螺旋结构域。最后，介绍了SynGAP错义（SGM）服务器以支持SynGAP变异评估。SGM服务器整合了一个包含顶级预测器的数据表、一个用于结构级评估的3D查看器，以及针对所有由单碱基替换产生的变异的AI生成通俗摘要，包括那些尚未在公共数据库中观察到的变异。

02 一句话总结

本文通过基准测试多种结构/序列及机器学习/深度学习致病性预测器，发现折叠稳定性计算（Foldetta）和深度学习方法（ESM1b、AlphaMissense）在默认设置下对 SYNGAP1 错义变异致病性预测准确率最高，并通过阈值优化进一步提升性能，同时揭示了致病变异在 GAP/PH 结构域富集、相分离影响位点与磷酸化位点共定位的结构规律，最终开发了整合多源预测的 SGM 在线服务器。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题: 如何准确评估 SYNGAP1 错义变异的致病性？哪些计算预测器对 SYNGAP1 最有效？致病变异在 SynGAP 蛋白结构上呈现怎样的分布规律？
  • 为什么重要: SYNGAP1 相关疾病（SRD）是严重的神经发育障碍，约 65% 的 ClinVar 错义变异被归类为意义不明确变异（VUS），实验验证困难且昂贵，亟需可靠的计算预测工具辅助临床诊断。
  • 现有方法不足: ① 预测器性能具有蛋白特异性，缺乏针对 SYNGAP1 的系统评估；② 默认阈值可能不适用于特定基因；③ 现有工具分散在不同平台，缺乏整合资源；④ 对无序区域（IDR）变异和相分离相关变异的评估不足。
  • 精确研究问题: 能否通过系统基准测试和基因特异性阈值优化，建立一套对 SYNGAP1 错义变异致病性具有高准确率和覆盖率的最佳计算预测方案，并揭示其结构基础？

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法: 对 14 种致病性预测器进行系统基准测试，使用 ClinVar 标注的 SYNGAP1 错义变异作为金标准，评估准确率、ROC-AUC、MCC 等指标；对表现最佳的方法进行基因特异性阈值优化；分析致病变异的结构域分布；开发 SGM 在线服务器整合所有预测结果。

  2. 核心洞察: - 折叠稳定性计算（Foldetta）和深度学习方法（ESM1b、AlphaMissense）在 SYNGAP1 上表现最佳，说明结构稳定性变化和蛋白语言模型捕获的进化/序列模式是 SYNGAP1 致病性的两个互补信号。 - AlphaMissense 的 ROC-AUC 最高但默认阈值非最优，通过基因特异性阈值优化可显著提升性能，说明默认阈值不具普适性，基因特异性校准是必要的。 - 致病变异在 GAP 和 PH 结构域高度富集，而 IDR 区域变异多为良性，提示结构域完整性对 SynGAP 功能至关重要。 - 相分离影响位点与磷酸化位点在 IDR 区域共定位，提示IDR 变异可能通过影响相分离和磷酸化调控而非蛋白折叠来致病。

  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]: - 对任何单基因变异致病性研究，多方法基准测试 + 基因特异性阈值优化应成为标准流程，而非直接套用默认阈值。 - 结构方法（折叠自由能）和序列/深度学习方法互补而非替代，最佳策略是整合两者。 - IDR 区域的变异评估需要超越传统折叠稳定性框架，考虑相分离、翻译后修饰等非折叠相关机制。

09 关键公式符号

本文核心公式为评估指标和阈值优化方法，具体如下：

  1. Youden's J 统计量（用于阈值优化）: J = Sensitivity + Specificity − 1 = TPR − FPR - 其中 TPR = TP/(TP+FN)，FPR = FP/(FP+TN) - 用途: 选择使 J 最大化的分类阈值，平衡敏感性和特异性 - 直觉: J 值越大，分类器在区分正负类上的综合能力越强

  2. Matthews 相关系数 (MCC): MCC = (TP×TN − FP×FN) / √[(TP+FP)(TP+FN)(TN+FP)(TN+FN)] - 用途: 评估二分类性能，对类别不平衡不敏感 - 直觉: 取值范围 [−1, 1]，1 为完美预测，0 为随机预测

  3. Phi 系数 (φ)（用于预测器间一致性分析）: φ = (n₁₁×n₀₀ − n₁₀×n₀₁) / √(n₁₊×n₀₊×n₊₁×n₊₀) - 用途: 衡量两个二分类预测器之间的一致性 - 直觉: 与 MCC 数学等价，用于评估预测器间的共识程度

  4. ROC-AUC（用于评估判别能力）: AUC = ∫₀¹ TPR(FPR⁻¹(x)) dx - 用途: 衡量分类器在所有可能阈值下的综合判别能力 - 直觉: 0.5 为随机猜测，1.0 为完美判别

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围: 本文仅针对 SYNGAP1 基因的错义变异，不涵盖截短变异、同义变异或非编码变异；预测器性能评估限于 ClinVar 标注数据。
  • Oracle/真值输入: 使用 ClinVar 的临床注释作为金标准，但 ClinVar 本身存在标注偏差（致病变异多来自已发表病例，良性变异多来自人群测序），且部分标注可能基于计算预测而非实验验证。
  • 端到端状态: 本文是评估研究而非端到端预测系统开发；SGM 服务器是信息整合平台，不提供新的预测算法。
  • 算力成本: 未提供具体算力需求；MD 模拟（211 个变异 × 3 重复 × 150 ns）成本较高，但已预先完成。
  • 历史数据依赖: 预测器性能高度依赖 ClinVar 标注质量；随着新变异不断提交，性能需重新评估。
  • 模型依赖: AlphaMissense 和 ESM1b 为预训练模型，其训练数据包含大量蛋白序列/结构，对 SYNGAP1 的适用性受预训练分布影响。
  • 最难情形: IDR 区域的错义变异预测可靠性最低；位于结构域边界或影响蛋白-蛋白相互作用界面的变异难以通过折叠稳定性方法捕捉。
  • 群体/领域边界: 结论仅适用于 SYNGAP1；对其他基因的推广需重新基准测试。SGM 服务器面向 SRD 诊断和研究群体。
  • 有边界的复述: 在 ClinVar 标注的 SYNGAP1 错义变异上，折叠稳定性计算和深度学习方法（特别是阈值优化后的 AlphaMissense）能提供高准确率的致病性预测；致病变异主要富集于 GAP/PH 结构域；IDR 区域的相分离影响位点与磷酸化位点共定位，提示非折叠相关致病机制；SGM 服务器整合了这些资源供临床和研究使用。

14 个人收获与启示

  1. 多方法基准测试 + 基因特异性校准是变异致病性预测的标准流程，可直接迁移到其他疾病基因的研究中。
  2. 折叠稳定性计算与深度学习方法互补：结构方法提供物理机制解释，深度学习方法提供序列模式覆盖，整合两者优于单一方法。
  3. IDR 区域的变异评估需要新框架：传统折叠稳定性方法不适用于无序区域，相分离、PTM 等非折叠机制可能是 IDR 致病的核心。
  4. 在线服务器是研究成果转化的重要形式：SGM 服务器将复杂预测结果整合为可查询的临床工具，值得借鉴。
  5. AI 生成摘要提高可读性：用 LLM 生成变异解释摘要，降低非专业用户的使用门槛。

15 与已有知识连接

  • 相似方法: 与 Pejaver 等 (2022) 的 ClinGen 校准框架、Bergquist 等 (2024) 的 AlphaMissense 阈值研究一致，均强调基因特异性校准的重要性。
  • 工具对比: AlphaMissense (Cheng et al., 2023, Nature) 和 ESM1b (Rives et al., 2021, PNAS) 是当前 SOTA 的深度学习方法；本文验证了它们在 SYNGAP1 上的有效性。
  • 结构方法: FoldX (Schymkowitz et al., 2005) 和 Rosetta (Kuhlman et al., 2003) 是经典的折叠稳定性计算工具；Foldetta 整合两者是新的尝试。
  • 相分离预测: PSMutPred (Hou et al., 2023) 是新兴的相分离变异预测工具，本文将其应用于 SynGAP 的 IDR 区域。
  • MD 模拟: 本文引用了先前研究 [46] 的 MD 模拟数据，将 MD 结果与致病性预测整合。
  • 与 AlphaFold 的关系: 使用 AlphaFold 模型（AF-Q96PV0-F1）作为结构模板，体现了 AlphaFold 在变异致病性研究中的基础性作用。
  • 可迁移方向: 本文方法框架可直接迁移到其他神经发育障碍相关基因（如 SHANK3、NLGN3 等）的变异致病性研究。

审稿人评审

Review setup

  • Input scope Full manuscript text
  • Assessment boundary Scientific content, methodology, claims, and conclusions as presented in the provided text
  • Shared manuscript claim summary The authors benchmark multiple in silico pathogenicity predictors against clinically curated SYNGAP1 missense variants, identify top performers (folding stability calculations, ESM1b, AlphaMissense), optimize thresholds for improved accuracy, describe structural enrichment patterns of pathogenic variants, propose a link between missense variation and phase separation/phosphoregulation, and present a new web server (SGM) for variant assessment.
  • Visible evidence base Abstract, Introduction, Results (Sections 2.1–2.7), Discussion, Conclusions, Methods (Sections 5.1–5.8), Data Availability Statement
  • Missing materials affecting confidence Figures 1–6, Tables 1 and S1–S12, Supporting Information text and equations, Supplementary Figures S1–S8, benchmark dataset details, server implementation details

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment This manuscript addresses a clinically relevant problem, the interpretation of SYNGAP1 missense variants, using a broad panel of computational predictors. The benchmarking approach is systematic, and the development of a publicly accessible server is a valuable community resource. However, the study's core conclusions are limited by the small and potentially biased benchmark set, the lack of experimental validation, and the absence of key methodological details in the provided text. The claims regarding phase separation and phosphoregulation are largely speculative and require further support. The manuscript is technically sound in its computational execution but the clinical utility of the proposed framework remains to be established.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Clinical geneticists and molecular diagnosticians interpreting SYNGAP1 variants, researchers studying SYNGAP1-related disorders, computational biologists developing and benchmarking variant effect predictors, and the broader neurodevelopmental disease community. The SGM server provides a practical tool for variant assessment.
  • Major strengths 1. Systematic benchmarking of a wide range of predictors, from traditional sequence-based to state-of-the-art deep learning methods. 2. Gene-specific threshold optimization with cross-validation, demonstrating a clear improvement for AlphaMissense. 3. Identification of structural domain enrichment patterns that align with biological expectations. 4. The SGM server is a practical, accessible resource that integrates multiple data types and provides AI-generated summaries. 5. The study highlights important limitations of current predictors for disordered regions.
  • Major Concerns
  • Concern ID R1-M1
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Benchmark dataset adequacy
  • Claim pointer The benchmark set is described as "limited but carefully curated" and is used to draw strong conclusions about predictor performance and to calibrate thresholds for clinical use.
  • Evidence pointer Section 5.2, Figure 1d, Tables 1 and S1–S3
  • Concern The benchmark set contains only 191 clinically labeled variants (75 benign, 53 likely benign, 21 pathogenic, 42 likely pathogenic). This is a very small sample for evaluating and calibrating predictors, especially for a gene with a high burden of pathogenic missense variation. The set is likely imbalanced and may not represent the full spectrum of SYNGAP1 variants. The inclusion of "conflicting" variants reclassified based on submission counts is a subjective criterion that could introduce bias. The small sample size raises concerns about overfitting during threshold optimization, despite the cross-validation approach.
  • Why it matters The clinical utility of the SGM server and the recommended thresholds depend entirely on the quality and representativeness of this benchmark set. If the set is biased or too small, the reported performance metrics and optimized thresholds may not generalize to real-world clinical cases, potentially leading to misclassification.

  • Resolution test Provide a detailed breakdown of the benchmark set, including the distribution of variants across protein domains and disorder regions. Justify the reclassification of conflicting variants with explicit criteria. Perform a power analysis to demonstrate that the sample size is sufficient to detect meaningful performance differences. Ideally, validate the optimized thresholds on an independent, larger cohort of SYNGAP1 variants with functional or clinical data.

  • Claim pointer The manuscript claims that top-performing predictors, especially optimized AlphaMissense, provide accurate predictions for SYNGAP1 variant pathogenicity.
  • Evidence pointer Sections 2.1–2.2, Figure 2, Tables 1 and S3
  • Concern The benchmarking is entirely computational, relying on ClinVar labels as ground truth. There is no experimental validation of any predictions, such as functional assays or protein stability measurements. The authors acknowledge that "rigorous testing with any such sets cannot guarantee prediction accuracy" but still present the optimized thresholds as suitable for clinical use. The only experimental dataset mentioned is from a prior study (Meili et al., N = 57), which is not used for validation here.
  • Why it matters ClinVar labels themselves can be incorrect or biased, especially for variants in genes with limited functional data. Without experimental validation, the true accuracy of the predictors, and particularly the optimized AlphaMissense thresholds, remains unknown. This limits the confidence with which the SGM server can be used for clinical decision-making.

  • Resolution test Validate the predictions against an independent experimental dataset, such as the Meili et al. stability measurements or functional assays for a subset of variants. If experimental data are not available, clearly state this as a major limitation and temper the clinical recommendations accordingly.
