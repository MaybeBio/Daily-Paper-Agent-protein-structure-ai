HelixDTA: Dual-Branch Sequence-Structure Learning with Complete Target Structures for Robust and Interpretable Drug-Target Affinity Prediction.

Lou, Shang; Li, Xuhua; Peng, Yujie; Yuan, Yuan; Peng, Bo; Yi, Yingyu; Zhang, Xuchao; Zhao, Hailong; Wang, Beilei; Li, Kun; Qian, Hong; Ren, Tao; Gu, Hongcang; Zhang, Fan
Journal of chemical information and modeling · 2026-10-22 · 仅摘要 · 原文

提出双分支深度学习框架HelixDTA，融合蛋白序列与完整结构预测药物-靶标亲和力，虽以药物靶点为核心但涉及蛋白结构建模，方法可迁移至蛋白质结构相关计算。

摘要

Accurate prediction of drug-target affinity (DTA) is crucial for accelerating the discovery of drug candidates. Despite recent advances in machine learning methods, existing models often rely solely on either sequence information or protein pocket structures. This limitation prevents these models from fully capturing the critical three-dimensional (3D) physical information that governs molecular recognition, thus failing to meet the high-precision demands of drug discovery. To address this, we introduce HelixDTA, a deep learning framework featuring a parallel dual-branch architecture that independently learns representations from two distinct but complementary modalities: sequence context and complete target structures. By integrating sequence-derived features with structure-derived features at the prediction layer, this architecture preserves modality-specific insights while characterizing drug-target interactions more comprehensively. HelixDTA achieved strong and competitive performance on the Davis and KIBA benchmarks and maintained clear advantages across most similarity-based cold-start settings, demonstrating robust generalization to compounds with unseen scaffolds and novel protein targets. Furthermore, its built-in attention mechanism enhances interpretability by highlighting molecular regions that contribute to affinity prediction. Finally, a WRN case study demonstrates the potential use of HelixDTA for structure-aware allosteric candidate prioritization by integrating complete target-level structural context with docking-based pose inspection. In conclusion, HelixDTA offers a highly accurate, interpretable, and robust solution for DTA prediction. It highlights the value of integrating structural context with sequence-derived information and demonstrates significant potential to empower precision drug design and accelerate the drug discovery pipeline.

摘要翻译

药物-靶点亲和力（drug-target affinity, DTA）的准确预测对于加速候选药物发现至关重要。尽管机器学习方法近期取得了进展，现有模型往往仅依赖序列信息或蛋白质口袋结构。这一局限性阻碍了这些模型充分捕捉控制分子识别的关键三维（3D）物理信息，从而无法满足药物发现的高精度需求。为解决此问题，我们引入了HelixDTA，一种深度学习框架，采用并行双分支架构，独立地从两种不同但互补的模态中学习表征：序列上下文和完整靶点结构。通过在预测层整合序列衍生特征与结构衍生特征，该架构保留了模态特定见解，同时更全面地刻画药物-靶点相互作用。HelixDTA在Davis和KIBA基准上取得了强劲且具有竞争力的性能，并在大多数基于相似性的冷启动设置中保持明显优势，展示了对具有未见骨架化合物和新颖蛋白质靶点的稳健泛化能力。此外，其内置注意力机制通过突出贡献于亲和力预测的分子区域，增强了可解释性。最后，一项WRN案例研究展示了HelixDTA在结构感知变构候选物优先排序中的潜在应用，通过整合完整靶点级结构上下文与基于对接的构象检查。总之，HelixDTA为DTA预测提供了一种高精度、可解释且稳健的解决方案。它强调了将结构上下文与序列衍生信息整合的价值，并展示了赋能精准药物设计和加速药物发现流程的巨大潜力。

02 一句话总结

针对现有 DTA 模型仅用序列或口袋结构而丢失完整 3D 物理信息的问题，HelixDTA 采用并行双分支架构分别学习序列上下文与完整靶标结构表征，在预测层融合两模态特征，在 Davis 和 KIBA 基准上取得强竞争性能，并在多数相似性冷启动设置下保持优势，同时通过注意力机制提供可解释性。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：如何利用完整靶标三维结构（而非仅序列或口袋）与序列信息联合建模，提升药物-靶标亲和力预测的精度、泛化性和可解释性？
  • 为什么重要：DTA 预测是药物发现的关键环节，准确的亲和力预测可加速候选药物筛选；现有方法因忽略完整 3D 物理信息而难以满足高精度需求。
  • 现有方法为何不足：多数模型仅依赖序列（如基于 transformer 的序列编码）或仅使用口袋结构（如 pocket-based 方法），无法捕获靶标整体构象、远端残基相互作用等对分子识别至关重要的 3D 信息。
  • 精确研究问题：Can a dual-branch architecture that integrates complete target structures with sequence context achieve robust and interpretable DTA prediction across standard and cold-start settings?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：构建并行双分支深度学习架构，一个分支编码靶标序列上下文，另一个分支编码完整靶标三维结构，在预测层将两分支特征拼接融合，输出亲和力预测值；内置注意力机制用于可解释性。
  2. 核心洞察：序列与完整结构是互补模态——序列提供进化保守性与一级结构信息，完整结构提供全局 3D 物理上下文（包括口袋外残基、构象状态），二者在预测层融合可保留模态特异性，避免早期融合导致的信息混淆。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：对于蛋白质-配体相互作用预测，完整结构信息（而非仅口袋）可能提供关键的长程相互作用与构象约束；双分支并行 + 晚期融合是一种有效的多模态整合策略，可迁移至其他结构相关任务（如结合位点预测、变构位点识别）。

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a plausible and potentially valuable contribution to the drug-target affinity prediction field, specifically in combining sequence and complete structural information within a dual-branch architecture. However, the abstract lacks quantitative evidence, methodological specificity, and comparative rigor. The claims of robustness, interpretability, and practical utility cannot be evaluated from the supplied material. The work may be of interest to computational drug discovery researchers, but the current evidence base is insufficient to establish the stated advantages.

The inclusion of cold-start settings and a case study on allosteric candidate prioritization suggests attention to practical deployment scenarios beyond standard benchmark performance.
