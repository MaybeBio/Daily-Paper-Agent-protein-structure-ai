Structural basis of active state coupling of metabotropic glutamate receptor 8 to beta-arrestins.

Marx, Dagan C; Huynh, Kevin; Gonzalez-Hernandez, Alberto J; Strauss, Alexa; Rico, Carlos; Gallo, Pamela N; Sharghi Moshtaghin, Sheida; Arefin, Anisul; Broichhagen, Johannes; Eliezer, David; Khelashvili, George; Levitz, Joshua
Nature communications · 2026-09-11 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文通过冷冻电镜、负染EM和分子动力学模拟解析mGluR8与β-arrestin复合物的活性态结构及偶联机制，属于蛋白质结构计算研究，但核心贡献偏向实验结构解析，MD模拟为辅助验证。

摘要

Metabotropic glutamate receptors (mGluRs) are prototypical, dimeric family C G protein-coupled receptors (GPCR) that perform crucial modulatory roles throughout the nervous system. While mGluR activation and signaling through G proteins has been studied extensively, how these receptors interact with and are desensitized by beta-arrestins (beta-arrs) is not well understood. Here, we use an integrative biophysical and structural approach to probe the coupling of mGluR8 and beta-arrs. Using negative stain electron microscopy (EM), we identify tail- and core-bound orientations and stoichiometries of mGluR8/beta-arr complexes. Cryo-EM structures of mGluR8 alone or bound to either G proteins or beta-arr1 reveal mGluR8 active states with transducer-specific differences. The mGluR8/beta-arr structure shows a distinct complex orientation compared to other GPCR/beta-arr structures which supports a steric mechanism of mGluR desensitization involving interactions with both subunits and the lipid bilayer. Coupling of mGluR8 to beta-arr1 in an active-like conformation is verified by live-cell and single molecule FRET analysis. Finally, molecular dynamics simulations further define the positioning and dynamics of mGluR8-bound beta-arr1 and the importance of critical mGluR8 residues for stabilizing beta-arr1 complexes. Together, our data provide a framework for agonist-driven family C GPCR/beta-arr coupling.

摘要翻译

代谢型谷氨酸受体（mGluRs）是典型的二聚体C家族G蛋白偶联受体（GPCR），在整个神经系统中发挥关键的调节作用。尽管mGluR的激活及通过G蛋白的信号传导已被广泛研究，但这些受体如何与β-arrestins（beta-arrs）相互作用并被其脱敏的机制尚不明确。在此，我们采用整合的生物物理和结构方法，探讨mGluR8与β-arrestins的偶联。通过负染色电子显微镜（EM），我们识别了mGluR8/β-arr复合物的尾部结合和核心结合取向及化学计量比。单独mGluR8或与G蛋白或β-arr1结合的冷冻电镜（cryo-EM）结构揭示了mGluR8活性状态，并显示出转导子特异性差异。mGluR8/β-arr结构展示了与其他GPCR/β-arr结构不同的复合物取向，支持一种涉及两个亚基及脂质双层的mGluR脱敏空间位阻机制。mGluR8与β-arr1在活性样构象中的偶联通过活细胞和单分子FRET分析得到验证。最后，分子动力学（molecular dynamics）模拟进一步定义了mGluR8结合的β-arr1的定位和动态，以及关键mGluR8残基在稳定β-arr1复合物中的重要性。综合来看，我们的数据为激动剂驱动的C家族GPCR/β-arr偶联提供了框架。

  • 面向本课题的迁移: 在蛋白质结构预测（如AlphaFold）中，可以尝试预测不同转导蛋白（G蛋白 vs. beta-arr）结合下的受体构象，以验证本文发现的构象差异。在分子对接中，可以模拟beta-arr与二聚体受体的对接，并考虑脂双层的影响。
