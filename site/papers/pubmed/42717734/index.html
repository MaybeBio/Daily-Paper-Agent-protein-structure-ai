Molecular characterization of vitellogenin and its receptor with CRISPR-based sgRNA validation in the legume pod borer, Maruca vitrata (Geyer) (Lepidoptera: Crambidae).

Pradeep, Chalapathi; Sujatha, Parvathy Madhusoodanan; Nagesha, Somakalahalli Narasimhappa; Asokan, Ramasamy; Karuppannasamy, Ashok
Bulletin of entomological research · 2026-09-10 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该研究对豆荚螟卵黄原蛋白及其受体进行分子克隆、同源建模和蛋白-蛋白对接，并验证CRISPR sgRNA切割活性，虽涉及蛋白结构建模和对接，但核心是昆虫生殖基因功能表征与基因编辑，非蛋白质结构计算方法的创新，且对象为昆虫蛋白，与课题相关性中等。

摘要

Maruca vitrata, the legume pod borer, causes yield losses of up to 80% in grain legumes. Increasing insecticide resistance and environmental concerns necessitate sustainable pest management alternatives. In the present study, the complete vitellogenin (Vg) coding sequence (CDS), a key reproductive gene involved in oogenesis and embryonic development, was cloned and molecularly characterised from M. vitrata. The assembled Vg CDS ( approximately 5.3 kb) shared 99.04% sequence identity with the reported M. vitrata Vg sequence (MG799570.1). Phylogenetic analysis demonstrated close evolutionary association with related Lepidopteran species, while protein domain analysis identified three conserved domains, namely LPD_N, DUF1943, and VWD. Among these, the single exon-encoded LPD_N domain was selected as the target region for CRISPR/Cas9-mediated editing. Homology models of Vg and vitellogenin receptor (VgR) (Global Model Quality Estimation (GMQE): 0.58 and 0.51) showed a favourable interaction by protein-protein docking (score: -295.66). Three single-guide RNAs (sgRNAs) were designed, synthesised through in-vitro transcription, and evaluated using in vitro cleavage assays. sgRNA1 targeting the LPD_N domain and sgRNA2 targeting the signal peptide region exhibited efficient site-specific cleavage activity, whereas sgRNA3 failed to induce cleavage because of an unfavourable secondary structure that likely impaired Cas9-sgRNA complex formation. Overall, this study provides the first CRISPR-oriented functional characterisation and sgRNA validation of the M. vitrata Vg gene, together with structural characterisation of VgR and Vg-VgR interaction analysis, providing preliminary molecular resources for future CRISPR/Cas9 studies and supporting future embryo microinjection and heritable genome editing for sustainable management of M. vitrata.

摘要翻译

豆荚螟（Maruca vitrata）是豆类作物的重要害虫，可导致籽粒豆类产量损失高达80%。由于杀虫剂抗性增加及环境问题，亟需可持续的害虫管理替代方案。本研究从豆荚螟中克隆并分子鉴定了卵黄原蛋白（vitellogenin, Vg）的完整编码序列（CDS），该基因是参与卵子发生和胚胎发育的关键生殖基因。组装的Vg CDS（约5.3 kb）与已报道的豆荚螟Vg序列（MG799570.1）具有99.04%的序列一致性。系统发育分析显示其与相关鳞翅目物种具有密切的进化关联，而蛋白质结构域分析鉴定出三个保守结构域，即LPD_N、DUF1943和VWD。其中，由单一外显子编码的LPD_N结构域被选为CRISPR/Cas9介导编辑的靶标区域。Vg与卵黄原蛋白受体（vitellogenin receptor, VgR）的同源模型（全局模型质量评估（GMQE）：0.58和0.51）通过蛋白质-蛋白质对接显示出有利的相互作用（得分：-295.66）。设计并合成了三种单导向RNA（sgRNA），通过体外转录合成，并利用体外切割实验进行评估。靶向LPD_N结构域的sgRNA1和靶向信号肽区域的sgRNA2表现出高效的位点特异性切割活性，而sgRNA3因不利的二级结构（可能损害Cas9-sgRNA复合物形成）未能诱导切割。总体而言，本研究首次提供了豆荚螟Vg基因的CRISPR导向功能表征及sgRNA验证，同时包括VgR的结构表征及Vg-VgR相互作用分析，为未来CRISPR/Cas9研究提供了初步分子资源，并支持未来通过胚胎显微注射和可遗传基因组编辑实现豆荚螟的可持续管理。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题: 如何获得豆荚螟（M. vitrata）卵黄原蛋白（Vg）及其受体（VgR）的分子特征，并验证用于CRISPR/Cas9基因编辑的sgRNA的有效性？
  • 为什么重要: 豆荚螟是豆类作物的重要害虫，可造成高达80%的产量损失。化学杀虫剂抗性增加和环境影响促使需要可持续的害虫管理策略。Vg是卵子发生和胚胎发育的关键生殖基因，通过CRISPR/Cas9敲除Vg可导致雌虫不育，是一种潜在的害虫遗传控制方法。
  • 现有方法为何不足: 此前对M. vitrata Vg的序列信息有限（仅有一个参考序列MG799570.1），缺乏针对该物种Vg基因的CRISPR导向的功能表征和sgRNA验证，无法直接用于后续的胚胎显微注射和可遗传基因组编辑。
  • 精确的「Can ... ?」研究问题: Can we molecularly characterize the Vg and VgR genes in M. vitrata and validate functional sgRNAs for CRISPR/Cas9-mediated editing of the Vg gene?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法: 克隆Vg全长CDS，进行系统发育和蛋白结构域分析；对Vg和VgR进行同源建模和蛋白-蛋白对接；设计三条sgRNA，通过体外转录合成，并用体外切割实验验证其效率。
  2. 核心洞察: 通过结合分子表征（序列、结构域）和结构预测（同源建模、对接），可以更合理地选择CRISPR靶向区域（如LPD_N结构域），并提前通过体外实验筛选出高效的sgRNA，从而降低后续体内实验的试错成本。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]: 在非模式生物中应用CRISPR时，先进行体外sgRNA验证是提高成功率的关键步骤。同时，结构预测（即使模型质量中等）可以为靶点选择提供生物学依据（如靶向保守功能域），而不仅仅是基于序列的随机设计。

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围: 仅限于M. vitrata一个物种的Vg基因的分子表征和sgRNA体外验证。
  • oracle/真值输入: 序列比对基于一个公开参考序列；结构模型基于同源建模，非实验结构。
  • 端到端状态: 未进行体内（胚胎显微注射）实验，因此未实现从sgRNA设计到可遗传编辑的完整流程。
  • 算力成本: 低（同源建模和对接计算量小）。
  • 历史数据依赖: 依赖已发表的M. vitrata Vg序列（MG799570.1）。
  • 模型依赖: 结构预测依赖SWISS-MODEL的模板库。
  • 最难情形: 未测试sgRNA在复杂基因组环境（如染色质状态）下的效率。
  • 群体/领域边界: 结论仅适用于M. vitrata，对其他物种的Vg基因编辑需重新设计sgRNA。
  • 不确定性: 体外切割效率与体内编辑效率的相关性未知；结构模型的准确性未经验证。
  • 有边界的复述: 本研究成功克隆了M. vitrata的Vg基因，并基于其序列和结构预测，通过体外实验验证了两条sgRNA的切割活性，为未来在该物种中进行Vg基因的体内CRISPR编辑提供了初步的分子工具，但尚未证明其在活体胚胎中的编辑效果。

14 学到什么

Agent 提炼的知识候选: 1. 可迁移概念: 结构域引导的CRISPR靶点选择。本文选择靶向Vg的LPD_N结构域（一个单外显子编码的保守功能域），而非随机区域。这为在蛋白质结构计算研究中设计基因编辑实验提供了思路：优先靶向编码关键功能域（如活性位点、结合界面）的外显子，可能更有效地破坏蛋白功能。 2. 可迁移方法: 体外sgRNA预筛选流程。在投入昂贵的体内实验前，通过体外转录和切割实验快速验证sgRNA效率。此方法可迁移到任何需要CRISPR编辑的蛋白质结构研究项目中，用于筛选靶向特定结构域或位点的sgRNA。 3. 可迁移实验设计: 同源建模与对接辅助功能研究。即使模型质量中等，仍可用于初步预测蛋白-蛋白相互作用（如Vg-VgR），为后续实验（如Co-IP、突变分析）提供候选界面。在蛋白质结构计算研究中，可先用快速建模工具（如SWISS-MODEL、AlphaFold）生成模型，再用对接工具（如HDOCK、ClusPro）预测复合物，指导实验验证。

    • 来源局限/观察: 本文同源建模GMQE值较低（0.58, 0.51），模型精度有限。
    • 核心假设: AlphaFold2（或AlphaFold-Multimer）能生成比SWISS-MODEL更准确的Vg-VgR复合物结构，并揭示关键的相互作用残基。
    • 相对本文的增量: 使用更先进的结构预测方法，获得高置信度模型，并预测出具体的结合界面残基。
    • 初步方法: 使用AlphaFold2/AlphaFold-Multimer对M. vitrata Vg和VgR进行多序列比对和结构预测；分析预测的界面残基；与本文的对接结果进行比较。
    • 验证方式: 通过点突变（如将预测的关键残基突变为丙氨酸）和体外结合实验（如SPR或ITC）验证预测的相互作用。
    • 可能的失败模式: AlphaFold2预测的复合物结构可能仍不准确，或预测的界面残基在实验中不关键。
    • 创新状态: unverified

  2. 名称: 基于结构预测的M. vitrata Vg基因高效sgRNA全基因组筛选与脱靶评估

    • 来源局限/观察: 本文仅测试了三条sgRNA，且未进行脱靶分析。
    • 核心假设: 结合Vg蛋白结构域信息和全基因组序列，可以设计出效率更高、脱靶更低的sgRNA。
    • 相对本文的增量: 扩大sgRNA筛选范围，并引入脱靶评估。
    • 初步方法: 使用CRISPOR、CHOPCHOP等工具，基于M. vitrata基因组，设计靶向Vg所有外显子的sgRNA；优先选择靶向编码关键结构域（如LPD_N）且脱靶评分低的sgRNA；通过体外切割实验验证前10个候选sgRNA的效率。
    • 验证方式: 对筛选出的最优sgRNA进行全基因组脱靶预测（如Cas-OFFinder），并选择预测脱靶位点进行体外切割验证。
    • 可能的失败模式: 体外高效的sgRNA在体内仍可能因染色质状态而无效；脱靶预测可能不准确。
    • 创新状态: unverified

Review setup

  • Input scope Abstract only
  • Assessment boundary Claims and evidence presented in the abstract
  • Shared manuscript claim summary The authors report the molecular characterisation of vitellogenin (Vg) and its receptor (VgR) in Maruca vitrata, including cloning, phylogenetic analysis, protein modelling, docking, and CRISPR-based sgRNA design and in vitro validation.
  • Visible evidence base Abstract text only; no figures, tables, methods, or supplementary data provided
  • Missing materials affecting confidence Full manuscript, all figures/tables, detailed methods (cloning, CRISPR design, in vitro cleavage assay protocols, docking parameters), sequence data, and statistical analyses

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a logical progression from gene characterisation to CRISPR tool development for a pest of agricultural importance. However, the claims are supported only by summary statements without any visible data. The core functional validation—sgRNA cleavage activity—is described qualitatively, and the docking result is presented without context or error metrics. The abstract alone does not provide sufficient evidence to evaluate the technical soundness or reproducibility of the work.

  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers working on lepidopteran pest management, insect reproductive biology, and CRISPR-based gene editing in non-model insects. The work provides preliminary molecular resources for future functional studies of Vg in M. vitrata.

  • Major strengths 1. Addresses a relevant pest species with high economic impact. 2. Integrates multiple approaches (cloning, phylogenetics, modelling, CRISPR) in a single study. 3. Reports a negative result (sgRNA3 failure) which is valuable for sgRNA design guidelines.

  • Major Concerns

  • Concern ID R1-M1
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Technical soundness – data availability
  • Claim pointer "sgRNA1 targeting the LPD_N domain and sgRNA2 targeting the signal peptide region exhibited efficient site-specific cleavage activity, whereas sgRNA3 failed to induce cleavage because of an unfavourable secondary structure"
  • Evidence pointer Abstract only; no figure or table cited
  • Concern The abstract states that sgRNA1 and sgRNA2 showed "efficient site-specific cleavage activity" and that sgRNA3 failed due to "unfavourable secondary structure," but no quantitative data (e.g., cleavage efficiency percentages, gel images, replicate numbers) are provided. The claim about secondary structure is speculative without supporting structural predictions or experimental validation.
  • Why it matters The central functional claim of the study—that validated sgRNAs are ready for embryo microinjection—rests entirely on these in vitro cleavage results. Without visible data, the claim cannot be assessed for reproducibility or statistical significance.

  • Resolution test Provide gel images, quantification of cleavage efficiency (e.g., band intensity ratios), replicate data, and secondary structure predictions for all three sgRNAs.

  • Concern ID R1-M2

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Technical soundness – docking analysis
  • Claim pointer "Homology models of Vg and VgR (GMQE: 0.58 and 0.51) showed a favourable interaction by protein-protein docking (score: -295.66)"
  • Evidence pointer Abstract only; no figure or table cited
  • Concern The docking score of -295.66 is presented without units, comparison to a control (e.g., random or known non-interacting proteins), or any measure of confidence (e.g., Z-score, RMSD, or multiple docking runs). GMQE values of 0.58 and 0.51 indicate moderate model quality, which may limit the reliability of the docking prediction.
  • Why it matters The docking result is used to support the biological relevance of the Vg-VgR interaction, but without proper validation, the claim of a "favourable interaction" is unsubstantiated.

  • Resolution test Provide docking statistics, control docking results, model validation metrics (e.g., Ramachandran plots), and a clear statement of what the score represents.

  • Concern ID R1-M3

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking No
  • Axis Scientific importance – novelty
  • Claim pointer "this study provides the first CRISPR-oriented functional characterisation and sgRNA validation of the M. vitrata Vg gene"
  • Evidence pointer Abstract only
  • Concern The abstract does not clarify how this work differs from the previously reported M. vitrata Vg sequence (MG799570.1), with which it shares 99.04% identity. The novelty appears limited to the CRISPR component, but the functional characterisation (cloning, phylogenetics, domain analysis) largely replicates existing data.
  • Why it matters The claim of "first CRISPR-oriented functional characterisation" is potentially valid, but the abstract does not demonstrate that the CRISPR work goes beyond sgRNA design and in vitro cleavage to actual functional disruption (e.g., in embryos or adults).

  • Resolution test Clarify in the abstract or full text what new biological insight the CRISPR validation provides beyond confirming that sgRNAs can cut the target in vitro.
