Comparative molecular dynamics of two cyclic peptide inhibitors bound to the Zika virus NS2B/NS3 protease.

Marinho, Camilla Vitoria Silva; de Oliveira, Maycon Vinicius Damasceno; Lima, Anderson H
Journal of molecular modeling · 2026-09-07 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文通过分子动力学模拟比较两种环肽抑制剂与寨卡病毒NS2B/NS3蛋白酶的结合模式、构象变化和结合自由能，直接涉及蛋白质-配体相互作用的计算模拟，属于蛋白质结构计算课题。

摘要

CONTEXT: The Zika virus NS2B/NS3 protease is an important antiviral target, and macrocyclic peptide inhibitors represent promising scaffolds because they combine multibasic recognition motifs with conformational restriction. Here, we investigated two structurally related cyclic peptide inhibitors, referred to as CP1 and CP2, which share a conserved macrocyclic framework but differ in the chemical nature of their linker substituents. Molecular dynamics simulations showed that both peptides display broad intramolecular distance distributions in aqueous solution, indicating that key crystallographic contacts are not intrinsically maintained in the unbound state. Upon binding to the protease, CP2 adopted a more defined conformational ensemble and preserved a short intramolecular contact compatible with its crystallographic arrangement, whereas CP1 lost its crystallographic d2 contact and sampled a broader bound-state distribution. CP2 also showed a more focused interaction network involving residues from the catalytic and substrate-recognition regions and more favorable binding free-energy estimates. METHODS: Molecular dynamics simulations were performed for each cyclic peptide in aqueous solution and bound to the Zika virus NS2B/NS3 protease using Amber 20. The protein was described with the ff14SB force field, the cyclic peptides with GAFF2, and the systems were solvated with the TIP3P water model. Three independent 500 ns simulations were performed for each system. Trajectories were analyzed using structural clustering, intramolecular distance distributions, residue-level interaction analysis, and MM/PBSA and MM/GBSA binding free-energy calculations.

摘要翻译

寨卡病毒NS2B/NS3蛋白酶是一个重要的抗病毒靶点，大环肽抑制剂因其将多碱性识别基序与构象限制相结合而成为有前景的支架。在此，我们研究了两种结构相关的环肽抑制剂，分别称为CP1和CP2，它们共享一个保守的大环框架，但连接子取代基的化学性质不同。分子动力学 (molecular dynamics) 模拟显示，两种肽在水溶液中均表现出广泛的分子内距离分布，表明关键的晶体学接触在未结合状态下并非固有地维持。与蛋白酶结合后，CP2采用了更明确的构象集合，并保持了与其晶体学排列兼容的短分子内接触，而CP1则失去了其晶体学d2接触，并采样了更广泛的结合态分布。CP2还显示出涉及催化区和底物识别区残基的更集中的相互作用网络，以及更有利的结合自由能估计。方法：使用Amber 20对每个环肽在水溶液中以及与寨卡病毒NS2B/NS3蛋白酶结合的状态下进行分子动力学 (molecular dynamics) 模拟。蛋白质用ff14SB力场描述，环肽用GAFF2力场描述，系统用TIP3P水模型溶剂化。每个系统进行了三次独立的500 ns模拟。轨迹通过结构聚类、分子内距离分布、残基级相互作用分析以及MM/PBSA和MM/GBSA结合自由能计算进行分析。

02 一句话总结

本文通过 3×500 ns 的 Amber MD 模拟，比较两个共享大环骨架但 linker 化学性质不同的环肽抑制剂（CP1 与 CP2）在水溶液及与 ZIKV NS2B/NS3 蛋白酶结合状态下的构象行为，发现 CP2 在结合态保留了晶体学 d1 分子内接触并呈现更聚焦的相互作用网络与更优的结合自由能，而 CP1 丢失 d2 接触并采样更宽的构象分布。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题: 环肽抑制剂中 linker 取代基的化学性质差异如何影响其在水溶液中的构象偏好、与 ZIKV NS2B/NS3 蛋白酶结合后的构象重组织、分子内稳定化接触以及结合自由能？
  • 为什么重要: ZIKV NS2B/NS3 蛋白酶是抗病毒药物设计的关键靶点；环肽抑制剂结合多碱性识别基序与构象限制，但 linker 化学修饰对结合态构象与能量的动态影响尚不清楚。理解 linker 如何调控结合态预组织可为理性设计更优环肽抑制剂提供依据。
  • 现有方法为何不足: 晶体结构仅提供静态快照，无法揭示溶液态与结合态的构象动态差异；先前的实验研究（Huber et al.）虽报告了活性差异（CP2 Ki=2.33 nM vs CP1 340 nM），但缺乏原子水平的动态解释。
  • 精确研究问题: Can atomistic MD simulations distinguish the linker-dependent conformational and energetic differences between two cyclic peptide inhibitors bound to the ZIKV NS2B/NS3 protease, and can these differences explain their experimentally observed potency gap?

04 背景与发展脉络

  • 阶段一: 黄病毒蛋白酶抑制剂开发 — 代表性方法: 线性肽抑制剂、拟肽类抑制剂；优点: 高亲和力；局限: 代谢稳定性差、膜通透性低。
  • 阶段二: 大环化策略 — 代表性方法: 环肽抑制剂（如 Huber et al. 系列）；优点: 构象限制增强稳定性与亲和力；局限: 环化 linker 的化学修饰对构象与活性的影响缺乏系统动态理解。
  • 阶段三: MD 模拟辅助环肽构象分析 — 代表性方法: 增强采样、MM/PBSA(GBSA) 结合自由能计算；优点: 提供动态构象系综与能量分解；局限: 力场精度、采样时间尺度限制。
  • 本文位置: 在阶段三框架下，通过比较两个结构相关但活性差异显著的环肽（CP1 vs CP2），建立 linker 化学性质 → 结合态构象预组织 → 相互作用网络 → 结合自由能的动态解释链条。
  • 脉络标注: 该脉络基于本文引言与讨论的叙述框架，未经外部系统核验。

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法: 对两个环肽抑制剂分别进行水溶液与蛋白结合态的 3×500 ns MD 模拟，结合 RMSD/RMSF、结构聚类、分子内距离分布、MM/PBSA 与 MM/GBSA 结合自由能计算、残基相互作用指纹与计算丙氨酸扫描。
  2. 核心洞察: 环肽的 linker 化学性质通过调控分子内羰基与 P1 胍基的取向（d1/d2 接触），决定结合态构象预组织程度；CP2 的 linker 设计更有利于在蛋白环境中稳定晶体学接触，从而形成聚焦于催化残基的相互作用网络与更优的结合自由能。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]: 环肽抑制剂的活性不仅取决于与靶点的直接接触，还取决于 linker 是否能在结合态维持有利的分子内稳定化接触；MD 模拟中的分子内距离分布可作为评估环肽「构象预组织倾向」的动态描述符，适用于其他环肽-蛋白酶体系。

07 方法总览

  • 输入: PDB 晶体结构 7PFY（CP1 复合物）、7PGC（CP2 复合物）；配体结构经 Gaussian09 HF/6-31G* 优化
  • 输出: 构象系综、RMSD/RMSF 曲线、聚类代表结构、分子内距离分布、MM/PBSA(GBSA) 结合自由能、残基相互作用指纹、丙氨酸扫描 ΔΔG 值
  • 模块: 1. 结构准备: PDB2PQR 质子化（pH 7.4，His51 为 HID 互变异构体）、UCSF Chimera 加氢 2. 参数化: 蛋白 Amber ff14SB；配体 GAFF2 + RESP 电荷（HF/6-31G* 静电势） 3. 系统构建: TIP3P 水盒子，最小溶质-盒边距 12 Å，中和离子 4. 能量最小化: 多阶段（水分子/氢原子/全系统） 5. 加热与平衡: 10→298 K 200 ps 加热，300 ps 密度调整，500 ps NPT 平衡 6. 生产模拟: 3×500 ns NPT，298 K（Langevin 恒温），1 atm（Berendsen 恒压），8/10 Å 非键截断，2 fs 步长，Verlet 积分 7. 分析: RMSD/RMSF、k-means 聚类（5 类）、DSSP 二级结构、分子内距离分布、MM/PBSA(GBSA)（igb=2，α=0.8，盐浓度 0.1 M）、残基相互作用指纹、计算丙氨酸扫描（SIETRAJ）
  • 假设: 晶体结构为初始结合构象；力场参数可准确描述环肽构象；500 ns 时间尺度足以采样结合态构象系综

09 关键公式符号

  • ΔE_MM = ΔE_int + ΔE_ele + ΔE_vdW (Eq. 1): 分子力学能量，含内能、静电、范德华项
  • ΔG_sol = ΔG_pl + ΔG_np (Eq. 2): 溶剂化自由能，含极性与非极性项
  • ΔG_total = ΔE_MM + ΔG_sol (Eq. 3): 总结合自由能（不含熵）
  • ΔG_bind = ΔG_MM/GBSA − TΔS_NMA (Eq. 4): 熵校正结合自由能，NMA 为简正模分析
  • ΔΔG_Ala = ΔG_bind^Ala − ΔG_bind^WT (Eq. 5): 丙氨酸扫描突变效应，正值表示原残基有利
  • 符号说明: ΔE_int 内能变化；ΔE_ele 静电；ΔE_vdW 范德华；ΔG_pl 极性溶剂化；ΔG_np 非极性溶剂化；TΔS_NMA 熵项

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围: 仅比较两个环肽抑制剂（CP1 vs CP2）在 MD 模拟中的构象与能量差异，不涉及新抑制剂设计或实验验证。
  • oracle/真值输入: 使用晶体结构作为初始构象，实验 Ki 值作为外部参考，但 MD 结果未直接预测 Ki。
  • 端到端状态: 非端到端；计算流程为结构准备→MD→分析，各步骤独立，无自动反馈回路。
  • 算力成本: 12 条 500 ns 轨迹，需 GPU 加速（具体硬件未提供）；MM/PBSA(GBSA) 计算需额外后处理。
  • 历史数据依赖: 依赖晶体结构（7PFY/7PGC）与 Huber et al. 的实验数据。
  • 模型依赖: 依赖 ff14SB/GAFF2 力场精度；环肽非标准残基参数化质量影响结果。
  • 最难情形: 环肽在溶液态的高构象灵活性导致宽距离分布，难以用单一构象描述；结合态构象差异较小，需精细分析。
  • 群体/领域边界: 结论仅适用于 ZIKV NS2B/NS3 蛋白酶与这两类 linker 化学；不能外推至其他黄病毒蛋白酶或环肽骨架。
  • 不确定性: 未报告误差传播分析；MM/PBSA(GBSA) 绝对值不可直接解释为实验结合自由能；熵估计依赖 NMA 近似。
  • 有边界的复述: 在 ff14SB/GAFF2 力场与 500 ns 时间尺度下，CP2 相比 CP1 在结合态更有效地保留了分子内 d1 接触，并呈现更聚焦的催化区相互作用网络与更优的计算结合自由能，与实验活性趋势一致；但该结论不构成对活性差异的因果证明。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment This manuscript presents a molecular dynamics study comparing two cyclic peptide inhibitors of the Zika virus NS2B/NS3 protease. The study is well-structured and addresses a relevant question about how linker modifications affect inhibitor conformation and binding. However, several methodological concerns and missing details limit the strength of the conclusions. The work would benefit from additional validation, clearer reporting of simulation parameters, and a more critical discussion of the limitations of the computational approach.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers in antiviral drug discovery, particularly those working on flavivirus proteases and macrocyclic peptide inhibitors, would be interested in the structural insights into how linker chemistry influences bound-state conformation and interaction networks. The study also provides a methodological framework for using MD simulations to compare closely related inhibitors.
  • Major strengths The study addresses a clear and relevant question about structure-activity relationships in cyclic peptide inhibitors. The use of multiple independent replicas (3 x 500 ns) is appropriate for assessing conformational reproducibility. The combination of intramolecular distance analysis, residue-level interaction profiling, and binding free-energy calculations provides a multi-faceted view of the inhibitor behavior. The comparison between solution and bound states is informative.
  • Major Concerns
  • Concern ID R1-M1
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Methodology / Reproducibility
  • Claim pointer The authors claim that the simulations show CP2 preserves a short intramolecular contact (d1) compatible with its crystallographic arrangement, while CP1 loses its crystallographic d2 contact.
  • Evidence pointer Section "Distinct linker geometries control intramolecular stabilization in the bound state", Figure 4
  • Concern The analysis of intramolecular distances d1 and d2 is central to the main conclusion, but the definition of these distances is ambiguous. The text states that d1 corresponds to "the intramolecular hydrogen bond between the P4 carbonyl oxygen and the P1 guanidinium group" and d2 monitors "an additional intramolecular contact involving a linker/backbone carbonyl located within the macrocyclic segment between P4 and P1." However, the specific atoms used to calculate these distances are not explicitly defined. Without atom-level definitions, the analysis cannot be reproduced or independently verified. Furthermore, the distance distributions in Figure 4 are presented without error bars or confidence intervals from the three replicas, making it impossible to assess the statistical significance of the observed differences between CP1 and CP2.
  • Why it matters The central claim of the paper—that CP2 preserves a key crystallographic contact while CP1 does not—rests entirely on this distance analysis. If the distance definitions are ambiguous or the distributions are not statistically robust, the main conclusion is unsupported.
  • Resolution test Provide explicit atom names and residue numbers for each distance (d1 and d2) in both CP1 and CP2. Show the distance distributions for each individual replica (e.g., as separate histograms or overlaid curves) to demonstrate reproducibility. Include statistical measures (e.g., mean ± standard deviation across replicas) for the key distance populations.
  • Concern ID R1-M2
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Methodology / Validation
  • Claim pointer The authors claim that CP2 shows more favorable binding free-energy estimates than CP1, with values of -34.8 vs -27.6 kcal/mol (MM/PBSA) and -83.3 vs -69.8 kcal/mol (MM/GBSA).
  • Evidence pointer Section "Residue-level interactions and binding free energy support stronger recognition of inhibitor 2", Table S2 (referenced)
  • Concern The absolute binding free-energy values from MM/PBSA and MM/GBSA are far more negative than the experimental values (-11.6 and -8.7 kcal/mol for CP2 and CP1, respectively). The authors acknowledge this discrepancy but do not discuss its implications for the reliability of the relative ranking. More critically, the MM/GBSA values (-83.3 and -69.8 kcal/mol) are more than double the MM/PBSA values (-34.8 and -27.6 kcal/mol), indicating a large method-dependent discrepancy. The authors do not explain which method is more appropriate for this system or why the relative difference between CP1 and CP2 is so much larger in MM/GBSA (13.5 kcal/mol) than in MM/PBSA (7.2 kcal/mol). Without a justification for the choice of method and an explanation of the discrepancy, the energetic conclusions are not reliable.
  • Why it matters The binding free-energy calculations are used to support the claim that CP2 is a stronger inhibitor. If the calculations are not validated or the method-dependent discrepancies are not explained, the energetic comparison is unconvincing.
  • Resolution test Provide a detailed comparison of MM/PBSA and MM/GBSA results, including the individual energy components (electrostatic, van der Waals, polar solvation, nonpolar solvation) for each method. Discuss why the methods give different absolute values and different relative differences. If possible, benchmark the calculations against the experimental Ki values using a correlation or error analysis. Report the standard errors of the mean for the binding free energies across the three replicas.
  • Concern ID R1-M3
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking No
  • Axis Methodology / Force field and sampling
  • Claim pointer The authors claim that the simulations provide an "atomistic description" of the conformational states of the cyclic peptides.
  • Evidence pointer Section "Methodology", Section "Results and discussion"
  • Concern The cyclic peptides are parameterized with GAFF2, which is a general force field for small molecules. However, cyclic peptides can have complex conformational energetics that may not be well-captured by GAFF2 without specific validation. The authors do not provide any validation of the force field parameters for these specific macrocyclic compounds (e.g., comparison to quantum mechanical torsional profiles or NMR data). Additionally, the 500 ns simulation time per replica may be insufficient to fully sample the conformational space of cyclic peptides, which are known to have slow conformational transitions. The authors do not discuss convergence of the conformational sampling (e.g., using block analysis or autocorrelation times).
  • Why it matters If the force field is not validated for these compounds or the sampling is insufficient, the observed conformational differences between CP1 and CP2 may be artifacts of the simulation setup rather than genuine physical differences.
  • Resolution test Provide evidence that the GAFF2 parameters are appropriate for these macrocycles (e.g., by comparing dihedral energy profiles from QM calculations). Demonstrate that the conformational ensembles are converged within the 500 ns simulations (e.g., using RMSD convergence plots, cluster size evolution, or autocorrelation analysis). Discuss the limitations of the force field and sampling time.
