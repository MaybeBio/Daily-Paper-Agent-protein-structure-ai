Kinetic and Thermodynamic Control of Macrocyclic Peptide Conformation through Late-Stage Chemical Modification.

Ou, Yang Daniel; Hur, Joonseong; Baloh, Chloe; Huang, Benzhen; Kovljenic, Nenad; Dudding, Travis; Yudin, Andrei K
Journal of the American Chemical Society · 2026-09-06 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文通过化学修饰控制大环肽构象，结合NMR和分子动力学模拟研究构象变化，涉及蛋白质结构计算（MD模拟），但核心是化学方法而非AI/深度学习，对象为肽类，问题为构象控制，方法有MD模拟，综合评7分。

摘要

We describe the use of lactone ring-opening as a chemical tool for producing differentiated conformations of macrocyclic peptides. Under kinetic control, the lactone opening is highly atroposelective across macrocyclic precursors tested. Under thermodynamic control, reversible lactone opening equilibrates the atropisomers. A combined NMR/molecular dynamics study reveals that the atropisomers arising from lactone opening can adopt markedly different conformations, ranging from a 310-helix to various noncanonical conformations. In the RGD-containing macrocycle, the kinetic atropisomer recapitulates the 3D geometry involved in integrin binding, whereas the thermodynamic atropisomer adopts a distinct conformation that was not previously described. Together, these results establish a new strategy to control the conformational states of macrocyclic peptides.

摘要翻译

我们描述了利用内酯开环作为一种化学工具，用于产生大环肽的差异化构象。在动力学控制下，内酯开环在所测试的大环前体中表现出高度的对映选择性。在热力学控制下，可逆的内酯开环使阻转异构体达到平衡。结合核磁共振/分子动力学研究揭示，由内酯开环产生的阻转异构体可以呈现显著不同的构象，范围从310-螺旋到各种非经典构象。在含有RGD的大环中，动力学阻转异构体重现了参与整合素结合的三维几何结构，而热力学阻转异构体则采用了一种先前未被描述的不同构象。综合这些结果，确立了一种控制大环肽构象状态的新策略。

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：通过内酯开环反应，在动力学条件（低温、短时间）下获得单一 atropisomer，在热力学条件（加热、长时间）下实现 atropisomer 平衡，从而产生不同构象的大环肽。
  2. 核心洞察：内酯开环反应的可逆性（热力学控制）和不可逆性（动力学控制）可被利用来分别获得稳定构象和亚稳构象，且这些构象在结构上差异显著（如 310-螺旋 vs 非规范构象）。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：化学修饰（如开环、闭环）可作为构象的“开关”，其动力学与热力学选择性可迁移至其他大环系统（如环肽、环核苷酸），用于设计构象依赖的功能分子。对于本课题，此策略可启发构象生成中的化学控制方法，而非仅依赖物理模拟或 AI 预测。

07 方法总览

  • 输入：含内酯的大环肽前体（如 RGD 大环肽）。
  • 输出：不同构象的 atropisomers（动力学产物和热力学产物）。
  • 模块： 1. 化学合成：制备内酯大环肽前体。 2. 动力学控制开环：在低温（如 -78°C）下用亲核试剂（如胺）快速开环，获得单一 atropisomer。 3. 热力学控制开环：在加热条件下（如 60°C）进行可逆开环，使 atropisomers 达到平衡。 4. 构象表征：NMR（NOESY、ROESY）确定溶液构象；MD 模拟（如 AMBER 力场）生成构象系综并与 NMR 数据对比。
  • 训练：不适用（无机器学习）。
  • 工具：NMR 谱仪、MD 模拟软件（未指定）。
  • 反馈回路：NMR 实验约束用于验证 MD 模拟的构象，MD 模拟帮助解释 NMR 数据。
  • 假设：内酯开环的 atroposelectivity 由过渡态能量差异（动力学）或产物稳定性差异（热力学）决定；MD 模拟能准确反映溶液构象。

  • oracle/真值输入：NMR 数据作为构象验证的“真值”，但 NMR 本身有分辨率限制。
  • 端到端状态：从化学合成到构象表征的完整流程，但未涉及生物活性测试（如 integrin 结合实验）。
  • 算力成本：MD 模拟成本未提及，但通常较低（小分子体系）。
  • 历史数据依赖：不依赖历史数据，基于实验和模拟。
  • 模型依赖：MD 模拟依赖力场（如 AMBER），可能影响构象准确性。
  • 最难情形：大环肽柔性高时，构象平衡可能难以完全控制。
  • 群体/领域边界：仅适用于大环肽化学领域，不直接适用于蛋白质结构预测。
  • 不确定性：atroposelectivity 的机制（过渡态 vs 产物稳定性）未完全阐明。
  • 有边界的复述：本文证明，对于含内酯的大环肽前体，通过控制开环条件（动力学 vs 热力学）可以产生两种截然不同的构象（如 310-螺旋和非规范构象），其中动力学构象与已知生物活性几何匹配，但该方法尚未在更广泛的大环肽系统中验证，也未进行直接生物活性测试。

  • Shared manuscript claim summary The authors report that lactone ring-opening in macrocyclic peptides can be used to generate distinct atropisomeric conformations. Under kinetic control, the process is highly atroposelective; under thermodynamic control, reversible ring-opening equilibrates atropisomers. NMR and molecular dynamics studies show that the resulting atropisomers adopt markedly different conformations, from a 310-helix to noncanonical structures. In an RGD-containing macrocycle, the kinetic atropisomer reproduces the integrin-binding geometry, while the thermodynamic atropisomer adopts a previously undescribed conformation. Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a conceptually interesting approach to conformational control of macrocyclic peptides via lactone ring-opening, with a clear distinction between kinetic and thermodynamic atropisomer outcomes. The claim of achieving atroposelective ring-opening and the observation of divergent conformations, including a 310-helix, are potentially significant. However, the abstract alone provides insufficient evidence to evaluate the robustness of the synthetic methodology, the rigor of the conformational assignments, or the biological relevance of the RGD-containing system. The central claims are plausible but not verifiable from the supplied material.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers in peptide chemistry, macrocycle synthesis, conformational analysis, and drug discovery would be interested. The work addresses a long-standing challenge in controlling the 3D structure of macrocyclic peptides, which is relevant to the design of peptide-based therapeutics targeting protein-protein interactions. The atroposelective ring-opening strategy could offer a practical route to conformationally defined macrocycles, and the RGD-integrin example provides a direct application context.
  • Major strengths The conceptual distinction between kinetic and thermodynamic control of atropisomer formation is clear and potentially generalizable. The use of lactone ring-opening as a late-stage modification is an elegant approach that avoids early-stage conformational bias. The inclusion of an RGD-containing macrocycle with a known biological target (integrin) strengthens the translational relevance. The combination of NMR and MD to characterize conformations is appropriate for the question posed.
