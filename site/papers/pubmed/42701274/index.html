Kinetic and Thermodynamic Control of Macrocyclic Peptide Conformation through Late-Stage Chemical Modification.

Ou, Yang Daniel; Hur, Joonseong; Baloh, Chloe; Huang, Benzhen; Kovljenic, Nenad; Dudding, Travis; Yudin, Andrei K
Journal of the American Chemical Society · 2026-09-06 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文通过化学修饰控制大环肽构象，结合NMR和分子动力学模拟研究构象差异，涉及蛋白质结构计算中的构象采样和MD方法，但核心是化学工具而非AI/深度学习，对象为肽类，问题和方法与蛋白质结构计算相关但方法创新有限。

摘要

We describe the use of lactone ring-opening as a chemical tool for producing differentiated conformations of macrocyclic peptides. Under kinetic control, the lactone opening is highly atroposelective across macrocyclic precursors tested. Under thermodynamic control, reversible lactone opening equilibrates the atropisomers. A combined NMR/molecular dynamics study reveals that the atropisomers arising from lactone opening can adopt markedly different conformations, ranging from a 310-helix to various noncanonical conformations. In the RGD-containing macrocycle, the kinetic atropisomer recapitulates the 3D geometry involved in integrin binding, whereas the thermodynamic atropisomer adopts a distinct conformation that was not previously described. Together, these results establish a new strategy to control the conformational states of macrocyclic peptides.

摘要翻译

我们描述了利用内酯开环作为化学工具，以产生大环肽的不同构象。在动力学控制下，内酯开环在所测试的大环前体中具有高度阻转选择性。在热力学控制下，可逆的内酯开环使阻转异构体达到平衡。结合核磁共振/分子动力学 (NMR/molecular dynamics) 研究表明，由内酯开环产生的阻转异构体可以呈现显著不同的构象，范围从310-螺旋到各种非经典构象。在含RGD的大环中，动力学阻转异构体重现了参与整合素结合的3D几何结构，而热力学阻转异构体则采用了一种先前未描述过的独特构象。这些结果共同确立了一种控制大环肽构象状态的新策略。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：如何通过后期化学修饰（而非从头设计）精确控制大环肽的构象？
  • 为什么重要：大环肽的构象决定其生物活性（如与靶标蛋白的结合），但现有方法（如环化策略、侧链修饰）难以在保持序列不变的前提下产生多样且可控的构象。
  • 现有方法为何不足：传统方法通常依赖环化前的前体设计或刚性骨架引入，灵活性有限；而后期修饰（如内酯开环）尚未被系统用于构象控制。
  • 精确研究问题：Can lactone ring-opening be used as a late-stage chemical tool to kinetically and thermodynamically control the conformation of macrocyclic peptides, producing distinct atropisomers with different 3D structures?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：通过内酯开环反应，在动力学控制下高选择性生成一种 atropisomer，在热力学控制下可逆开环使 atropisomers 平衡，从而获得不同构象的大环肽。
  2. 核心洞察：内酯开环产生的轴手性（atropisomerism）是构象差异的根源；动力学和热力学控制分别对应不同的能量路径，允许在相同序列下产生截然不同的 3D 结构（如 310-螺旋 vs. 非规范构象）。
  3. [Analysis] 可能的普适教训：化学修饰的立体化学（如轴手性）可被用作构象控制的“开关”，这一概念可迁移至其他环状分子（如环状核酸、环状糖类）的构象设计。对于蛋白质结构计算，这意味着实验上可获得的构象异构体可作为 MD 模拟的初始构象或对接配体库。

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a conceptually interesting approach to conformational control in macrocyclic peptides, a topic of significant current interest in medicinal chemistry and chemical biology. The central idea, using lactone ring-opening under kinetic versus thermodynamic control to access distinct atropisomeric conformations, is potentially valuable. The use of kinetic versus thermodynamic control to access different atropisomeric states of a macrocyclic peptide is an elegant idea that could have broad applicability. The inclusion of an RGD-containing macrocycle with relevance to integrin binding provides a potentially impactful demonstration. The combination of NMR and molecular dynamics to characterize conformations suggests a rigorous structural approach.
