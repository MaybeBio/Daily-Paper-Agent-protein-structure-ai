Discovery of Novel Endolytic Hyaluronate Lyases Belonging to a New Subfamily of GH56 and Enhancement of Catalytic Activity through Catalytic Cleft Engineering.

Yu, Xiangfan; Jing, Yiwen; Liu, Lei; Ma, Zhenyu; Jiang, Xukai; Xu, Li; Xiao, Min
Journal of agricultural and food chemistry · 2026-11-11 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文发现GH56新亚家族内切型透明质酸裂解酶并通过催化裂隙工程提升活性，虽以酶为对象，但利用分子动力学模拟分析结构-功能关系，对蛋白质结构计算研究有参考价值。

摘要

Hyaluronate lyases have been widely used to degrade hyaluronic acid (HA) and prepare HA oligosaccharides. To date, no hyaluronate lyase has been reported in glycoside hydrolase family 56 (GH56). Herein, SpskHL from Sporothrix schenckii 1099-18 was first identified as a GH56 endolytic hyaluronate lyase, and then its four homologues were discovered. These five microbial GH56 enzymes cleaved HA, yielding unsaturated HA oligosaccharides with DeltaHA2 as the end product, forming a new GH56 subfamily of endolytic hyaluronate lyase. Through alanine-scanning mutagenesis of 15 residues in the catalytic cleft of SpskHL and combining three positive mutations, triple-site mutant SpskHLM3 (Y120A/N125A/H189A) increased activity by 126%. Grafting of the triple-site mutation onto four other hyaluronate lyases increased their activities by 74%-155%. Molecular dynamics simulations suggested that all five triple-site mutants had an expanded catalytic cleft, a larger cavity near the catalytic residue E, and a shorter catalytic OE2-O4 distance, which might contribute to their enhanced activities.

摘要翻译

透明质酸裂解酶已被广泛用于降解透明质酸（HA）并制备HA寡糖。迄今为止，在糖苷水解酶家族56（GH56）中尚未报道过透明质酸裂解酶。本研究首次将来自申克孢子丝菌1099-18的SpskHL鉴定为GH56内切型透明质酸裂解酶，随后发现了其四个同源物。这五种微生物GH56酶能够切割HA，生成以DeltaHA2为终产物的不饱和HA寡糖，从而形成了一个新的GH56亚家族，即内切型透明质酸裂解酶。通过对SpskHL催化裂隙中15个残基进行丙氨酸扫描诱变，并结合三个正向突变，三突变体SpskHLM3（Y120A/N125A/H189A）的活性提高了126%。将三突变位点移植到其他四种透明质酸裂解酶上，其活性提高了74%-155%。分子动力学模拟表明，所有五个三突变体均具有扩大的催化裂隙、靠近催化残基E的更大空腔，以及更短的催化OE2-O4距离，这可能有助于其增强的活性。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：GH56 家族中是否存在内切型透明质酸裂解酶？如何通过理性工程改造提升其催化活性？
  • 为什么重要：透明质酸裂解酶用于制备 HA 寡糖（生物医药、化妆品、食品领域），发现新酶源与活性提升策略具有应用价值；GH56 家族此前无透明质酸裂解酶报道，填补家族功能空白。
  • 现有方法不足：已知透明质酸裂解酶主要属于多糖裂解酶家族（PL），GH56 家族功能未被探索；传统活性改造依赖随机突变或定向进化，缺乏基于结构的理性设计。
  • 精确研究问题：Can GH56 family members function as endolytic hyaluronate lyases, and can catalytic cleft engineering enhance their activity through structural mechanisms detectable by MD simulation?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：从 Sporothrix schenckii 中鉴定 GH56 内切型透明质酸裂解酶 SpskHL，发现 4 个同源酶；对催化裂隙 15 个残基进行丙氨酸扫描，组合正突变获得三突变体；将三突变移植到同源酶；用 MD 模拟分析结构变化。
  2. 核心洞察：催化裂隙的空间几何（宽度、空腔体积、催化残基 E 与底物 OE2 的距离）是活性决定因素，而非单一残基的化学性质。通过扩大裂隙和缩短催化距离，可系统性提升内切型裂解酶活性。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：对于依赖底物进入催化位点的酶（如裂解酶、糖苷酶），工程改造应优先考虑「空间可及性」与「催化距离」两个几何参数，而非仅关注保守残基突变；MD 模拟可作为突变设计的预筛选工具。

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围：仅限 GH56 家族中 5 个微生物酶的内切型透明质酸裂解活性；不涵盖所有 GH56 成员或真核来源。
  • oracle/真值输入：活性测定为实验真值；MD 模拟为解释性工具，非预测性验证。
  • 端到端状态：从序列鉴定到活性验证到工程改造到机制解释的完整链条，但 MD 模拟未与实验突变体逐一对应（仅 WT vs M3）。
  • 算力成本：未提供 MD 模拟规模（体系大小、时长），无法评估计算成本。
  • 历史数据依赖：依赖已知 GH56 序列信息与同源建模（未详述）。
  • 模型依赖：MD 结果依赖力场与初始结构质量，未提供验证（如 RMSD 收敛性）。
  • 最难情形：未测试极端 pH、高温、高浓度底物等工业条件；未测试长链 HA 与短链 HA 的活性差异。
  • 群体/领域边界：仅微生物来源；未涉及哺乳动物透明质酸酶（功能不同）。
  • 不确定性：活性提升的百分比基于特定测定条件，未提供误差范围或重复次数。

有边界的复述：在 GH56 家族中，SpskHL 及其 4 个同源酶被证实为内切型透明质酸裂解酶；通过丙氨酸扫描与组合突变，M3 突变体在实验条件下活性提升 126%，移植到同源酶后提升 74%–155%；MD 模拟显示这些突变体具有扩张的催化裂隙、更大的 E 残基附近空腔和更短的 OE2–O4 距离，但这些结构变化与活性提升的因果关系尚未通过逐点突变验证。

  • Shared manuscript claim summary The authors report the first identification of hyaluronate lyases in glycoside hydrolase family 56 (GH56), specifically SpskHL from Sporothrix schenckii 1099-18 and four homologues. Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a potentially novel finding in enzyme discovery, namely the first GH56 hyaluronate lyases, with a clear application-oriented goal of enhancing catalytic activity. However, the evidence base is limited to the abstract, and several claims require verification from the full manuscript. The novelty claim is plausible but not fully established without phylogenetic and sequence-level evidence. The activity enhancement data are promising but lack statistical context. The molecular dynamics interpretation is speculative without simulation details. Overall, the work is of interest to the glycoside hydrolase and carbohydrate-active enzyme community, but the case is not fully established from the provided material.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers in glycoside hydrolase enzymology, particularly those studying GH families and lyase mechanisms, would be interested in the expansion of GH56 substrate specificity. Applied microbiologists and biotechnologists working on HA oligosaccharide production for pharmaceutical, cosmetic, or nutraceutical applications would also find the activity enhancement results relevant. The study may also attract computational enzymologists interested in structure-function relationships in catalytic cleft engineering.
  • Major strengths The identification of a new GH56 subfamily with endolytic hyaluronate lyase activity represents a potentially significant expansion of known GH56 function. The combination of alanine-scanning mutagenesis with grafting of beneficial mutations across homologues is a systematic and practical approach. The use of molecular dynamics simulations to propose a mechanistic rationale for enhanced activity adds a structural dimension to the work.

  • Concern ID R1-M1
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Novelty and classification
  • Claim pointer The abstract claims that SpskHL is "first identified as a GH56 endolytic hyaluronate lyase" and that the five enzymes "form a new GH56 subfamily of endolytic hyaluronate lyase."
  • Evidence pointer Abstract; no phylogenetic tree, sequence alignment, or subfamily classification data are visible
  • Concern The claim of a new GH56 subfamily requires robust phylogenetic and sequence-based evidence, such as a phylogenetic tree with bootstrap support, pairwise sequence identity comparisons, and conserved motif analysis. The abstract does not provide any of this. Additionally, the assertion that no hyaluronate lyase has been reported in GH56 must be verified against current CAZy database annotations, which are not cited or discussed.

  • Resolution test Provide a phylogenetic tree including known GH56 members and related lyases, with statistical support values, and a clear definition of the subfamily criteria. Confirm the absence of prior GH56 hyaluronate lyase reports with a literature and database search.
