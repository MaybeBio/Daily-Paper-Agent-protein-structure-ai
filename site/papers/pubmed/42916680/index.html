7

XL-MS and De Novo Protein Design Identified a Common Motif for TREM2 Binding.

Perera, Doniesha; Ajiboye, Emmanuel; Hewavidanage, Kalana Pitakatuwana; Wier, Sophia; Duong, Vincent; Wu, Haifan
ACS chemical neuroscience · 2026-10-23 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文结合XL-MS交联质谱与蛋白-蛋白对接及从头设计小蛋白，解析ApoE3/TREM2复合物结合模式，属于蛋白质结构计算建模与设计研究，但方法以实验约束引导的对接为主，AI/物理模拟方法贡献较弱。

摘要

Apolipoprotein E (APOE) and triggering receptor expressed on myeloid cells 2 (TREM2) are the two strongest genetic risk factors of late-onset Alzheimer's disease. ApoE binds to the low-density lipoprotein receptor (LDLR) to facilitate the uptake of ApoE-lipoprotein particles. TREM2 is a cell surface receptor expressed on microglia in the brain. The activation of TREM2 is essential for microglia to carry out protective functions against AD pathology. Several studies have shown that TREM2 signaling is activated through direct interaction between TREM2 and ApoE. In addition to its important role in AD pathogenesis, the ApoE/TREM2 interaction has been shown to induce immunosuppression of neutrophils within the tumor microenvironment. Despite its clinical importance, a high-resolution molecular understanding of the complex remains elusive. Here, we carried out chemical cross-linking mass spectrometry (XL-MS) analysis of the ApoE3/TREM2ECD complex to identify intra- and interprotein cross-links, which were used as restraints to guide integrative protein-protein docking. Our data support a binding model in which a helix-loop-helix motif within the ApoE3 hinge and C-terminal region forms a transient binding pocket that wraps around the hydrophobic tip of the TREM2 ectodomain. This model is further supported by de novo-designed miniprotein binders, which show the same binding mode as identified by our XL-MS experiment. These results establish a robust framework for developing miniprotein-based TREM2 agonists.

摘要翻译

载脂蛋白E（APOE）和髓样细胞触发受体2（TREM2）是晚发性阿尔茨海默病最强的两个遗传风险因素。ApoE与低密度脂蛋白受体（LDLR）结合，促进ApoE脂蛋白颗粒的摄取。TREM2是脑内小胶质细胞表面表达的一种细胞表面受体。TREM2的激活对于小胶质细胞执行对抗AD病理的保护功能至关重要。多项研究表明，TREM2信号通过TREM2与ApoE之间的直接相互作用被激活。除了在AD发病机制中的重要作用外，ApoE/TREM2相互作用已被证明能在肿瘤微环境中诱导中性粒细胞的免疫抑制。尽管其具有临床重要性，但对该复合物的高分辨率分子理解仍难以捉摸。在此，我们对ApoE3/TREM2ECD复合物进行了化学交联质谱（XL-MS）分析，以识别蛋白质内和蛋白质间的交联，并将这些交联作为约束条件指导整合性蛋白质-蛋白质对接。我们的数据支持一种结合模型，其中ApoE3铰链区和C末端区域内的螺旋-环-螺旋基序形成一个瞬时结合口袋，包裹住TREM2胞外域的疏水尖端。该模型进一步得到从头设计的微型蛋白结合物的支持，这些结合物显示出与我们的XL-MS实验所识别的相同结合模式。这些结果为开发基于微型蛋白的TREM2激动剂建立了稳健的框架。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：ApoE 与 TREM2 直接相互作用的分子机制（高分辨率结合模式）尚不明确。
  • 为什么重要：ApoE 和 TREM2 是晚发性阿尔茨海默病（LOAD）最强的两个遗传风险因子；ApoE/TREM2 相互作用对 TREM2 信号激活（小胶质细胞保护功能）及肿瘤微环境中中性粒细胞免疫抑制均至关重要。
  • 现有方法为何不足：缺乏 ApoE/TREM2 复合物的高分辨率结构（如晶体或 cryo-EM 结构）；传统 docking 缺乏实验约束，易产生假阳性模型。
  • 精确研究问题：Can XL-MS-derived distance restraints, combined with integrative docking and de novo-designed miniprotein binders, define a reliable binding model for the ApoE3/TREM2 ectodomain complex?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：对 ApoE3/TREM2 胞外域复合物进行 XL-MS 分析，鉴定分子内和分子间交联，作为距离约束引导整合蛋白-蛋白 docking；随后用 de novo 设计的 miniprotein binder 验证结合模式。
  2. 核心洞察：ApoE3 的铰链区（hinge）和 C 端区域形成一个 helix-loop-helix 基序，构成一个瞬时结合口袋，包裹 TREM2 胞外域的疏水尖端（hydrophobic tip）。该结合模式被 XL-MS 约束和 de novo 设计 binder 共同支持，提示该基序是 TREM2 结合的「共同基序」。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：实验约束（如 XL-MS）与计算建模（docking）结合，再用独立设计方法（de novo binder）验证，可形成「约束-建模-验证」的闭环，显著提高复合物模型的可靠性。该策略可迁移至其他难以结晶的膜蛋白复合物研究。

  1. 候选名称：XL-MS 约束的 AlphaFold-Multimer 复合物模型筛选 - 来源局限/观察：本文用 XL-MS 约束引导传统 docking，但未使用 AlphaFold；AlphaFold-Multimer 预测复合物时无实验约束，可能产生错误界面。 - 核心假设：XL-MS 交联距离可作为打分项，筛选或重排 AlphaFold-Multimer 生成的多个候选模型，提高复合物预测准确率。 - 相对本文的增量：将本文的「约束 + docking」升级为「约束 + 深度学习预测」，利用 AlphaFold 的构象采样能力。 - 初步方法：对 ApoE3/TREM2 复合物运行 AlphaFold-Multimer 生成 top 模型；计算每个模型与 XL-MS 交联距离的满足度；用满足度重排模型；与本文 docking 模型对比。 - 验证方式：与本文模型及未来高分辨率结构对比；用已知结合突变体验证界面残基。 - 可能的失败模式：AlphaFold 预测的构象空间可能不包含满足交联约束的模型；交联数据稀疏时筛选能力有限。 - 创新状态：unverified

  2. 候选名称：TREM2 疏水尖端结合基序的序列偏好分析 - 来源局限/观察：本文识别了 helix-loop-helix 基序包裹疏水尖端的结合模式，但未分析该基序的序列偏好。 - 核心假设：TREM2 结合基序存在特定序列模式（疏水残基排列、loop 长度），可用于预测其他 TREM2 配体。 - 相对本文的增量：从单一复合物模型推广到序列基序预测。 - 初步方法：收集 ApoE 家族（ApoE2/3/4）及已知 TREM2 配体序列；多序列比对；分析 helix-loop-helix 区域的疏水残基保守性；用 Rosetta 设计突变体验证。 - 验证方式：设计突变体结合实验（SPR/ITC）；与本文 binder 序列比对。 - 可能的失败模式：结合基序可能不保守；疏水相互作用对突变不敏感。 - 创新状态：unverified

  3. 候选名称：ApoE 脂质化状态对 TREM2 结合模式的影响 - 来源局限/观察：本文使用 ApoE3 蛋白可能为脂质-free 状态，但体内 ApoE 以脂蛋白颗粒形式存在。 - 核心假设：脂质结合改变 ApoE3 构象，影响 helix-loop-helix 基序对 TREM2 的暴露与结合亲和力。 - 相对本文的增量：将体外模型推广到生理相关状态。 - 初步方法：制备脂质化 ApoE3（如 POPC 重组）；重复 XL-MS 与 docking；比较结合模式差异；MD 模拟脂质化 ApoE3 与 TREM2 复合物。 - 验证方式：脂质化 vs 非脂质化 ApoE3 的 TREM2 结合亲和力对比；交联谱差异。 - 可能的失败模式：脂质化 ApoE3 难以形成稳定复合物；MD 模拟体系复杂。 - 创新状态：unverified

  4. 候选名称：基于共同基序的 TREM2 激动剂 miniprotein 设计优化 - 来源局限/观察：本文提出建立 miniprotein-based TREM2 激动剂框架，但未报告 binder 的亲和力或功能活性。 - 核心假设：以 helix-loop-helix 基序为模板，可设计高亲和力 TREM2 激动剂 miniprotein。 - 相对本文的增量：从结合模式验证推进到功能激动剂开发。 - 初步方法：以本文 binder 为起点，Rosetta 序列设计优化；酵母展示或噬菌体展示筛选高亲和力变体；细胞实验验证 TREM2 信号激活。 - 验证方式：SPR 亲和力测定；TREM2 下游信号（如 SYK 磷酸化）检测。 - 可能的失败模式：高亲和力 binder 不一定激活 TREM2 信号；体内稳定性差。 - 创新状态：unverified

审稿人评审

Review setup

  • Input scope Abstract only
  • Assessment boundary Claims and evidence presented in the abstract; no full text, figures, tables, or supplementary materials were provided
  • Shared manuscript claim summary The authors report that chemical cross-linking mass spectrometry (XL-MS) of the ApoE3/TREM2 ectodomain (TREM2ECD) complex, combined with integrative protein-protein docking, supports a binding model in which a helix-loop-helix motif in the ApoE3 hinge and C-terminal region forms a transient pocket around the hydrophobic tip of the TREM2 ectodomain. They further claim that de novo-designed miniprotein binders reproduce this binding mode, establishing a framework for miniprotein-based TREM2 agonists.
  • Visible evidence base Abstract text only; no experimental details, cross-link tables, docking scores, design statistics, or validation data are visible
  • Missing materials affecting confidence Full manuscript, all figures and tables, XL-MS raw data and analysis parameters, docking methodology and scoring, miniprotein design and experimental validation details, and any statistical analyses

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a potentially interesting structural hypothesis for the ApoE/TREM2 interaction, supported by two complementary approaches. However, the level of evidence described is insufficient to evaluate the robustness of the proposed binding model or the claim that miniprotein binders recapitulate the native interaction. The abstract lacks quantitative details, validation metrics, and experimental confirmation of the functional consequences of the proposed binding mode. The work may be of interest to the Alzheimer's disease and neuroimmunology communities, but the current evidence base does not establish the central claims.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers studying Alzheimer's disease genetics and microglial biology, particularly those focused on TREM2 signaling and APOE-related mechanisms. Structural biologists interested in protein-protein interactions involving lipoprotein receptors and immune receptors. Drug discovery groups developing TREM2-targeted therapies or miniprotein-based biologics for neurodegenerative and neuroinflammatory conditions.
  • Major strengths The combination of XL-MS with integrative docking is a technically appropriate approach for studying transient or challenging protein complexes. The use of de novo-designed miniprotein binders as an orthogonal validation strategy is conceptually strong. The potential translational relevance for TREM2 agonist development is clear and clinically motivated.

  • Concern ID R1-M1

  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Evidence sufficiency
  • Claim pointer "Our data support a binding model in which a helix-loop-helix motif within the ApoE3 hinge and C-terminal region forms a transient binding pocket that wraps around the hydrophobic tip of the TREM2 ectodomain."
  • Evidence pointer Abstract; location not provided
  • Concern The abstract states that XL-MS data "support" the proposed binding model, but no information is provided on the number, type, or quality of cross-links identified, the confidence of the docking solutions, or how the model was selected among alternatives. The term "transient binding pocket" implies conformational dynamics that are not demonstrated by the described static cross-linking and docking approach.
  • Why it matters Without quantitative cross-link data and docking metrics, the reader cannot assess whether the proposed model is uniquely supported or merely one of many possible solutions. The claim of a "transient" pocket requires dynamic or ensemble evidence that is not described.
  • Resolution test Provide the number of unique interprotein cross-links, their sequence coverage, the docking score distribution, and a comparison of the proposed model against alternative docking solutions. If "transient" is claimed, provide supporting evidence such as molecular dynamics simulations or conformational ensemble analysis.
