Messenger RNA vaccine platforms from molecular optimization to infectious disease and cancer immunotherapy: a narrative review.

Wang, Qian; Sun, Zhangzhang; Liu, Heyong
Translational cancer research · 2026-11-08 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文是mRNA疫苗平台的叙述性综述，聚焦RNA分子优化、递送和传染病/癌症免疫治疗，不涉及蛋白质结构计算建模或AI/物理模拟方法，与本课题关系微弱。

摘要

BACKGROUND AND OBJECTIVE: Messenger RNA (mRNA) vaccines now encompass licensed respiratory vaccines, a self-amplifying ribonucleic acid (RNA) product, and individualized cancer immunotherapies in late-stage development. The expansion of the field has made platform-level comparisons increasingly important because efficacy, tolerability, manufacturability, and tissue targeting are influenced by the RNA architecture as well as by the delivery system. This narrative review examines molecular engineering, delivery, emerging RNA architectures, infectious-disease translation, and cancer immunotherapy, with emphasis on developments reported through 2026. METHODS: Targeted literature searches were conducted in PubMed through 31 August 2026, complemented by reference chaining and targeted searches of official regulatory sources. English-language mechanistic studies, clinical trials, regulatory documents, and focused reviews were prioritized. Particular attention was given to evidence published from 2023 to 2026 on self-amplifying RNA (saRNA), circular RNA (circRNA), molecular optimization, and individualized neoantigen vaccines. KEY CONTENT AND FINDINGS: Nucleoside-modified non-replicating mRNA formulated in lipid nanoparticles remains the most clinically validated architecture. Molecular optimization now extends beyond nucleoside substitution to cap chemistry, untranslated-region selection, coding-sequence and RNA-structure design, poly(A) control, and removal of double-stranded RNA (dsRNA) impurities. saRNA has reached regulatory authorization and offers dose-sparing potential, whereas circRNA remains predominantly preclinical and still faces unresolved manufacturing and quality-control questions. Infectious-disease programs have established reusable capabilities in antigen design, lipid nanoparticle formulation, manufacturing, and regulatory comparability. In oncology, intismeran autogene plus pembrolizumab has produced durable recurrence-free survival (RFS) benefit in resected melanoma, while pancreatic-cancer studies of autogene cevumeran show that vaccine-induced T cell responses vary substantially between patients. Tumor clonality, neoantigen quality, antigen presentation, the immune microenvironment, treatment setting, and manufacturing time are therefore as important as the number of encoded mutations. Artificial intelligence (AI) can improve neoantigen ranking but does not remove the need for biological validation. CONCLUSIONS: No RNA architecture is uniformly superior across indications. Further clinical progress will depend on matching transcript design and delivery to the intended immune response, improving predictive antigen selection and patient stratification, and transferring validated manufacturing and regulatory capabilities from infectious-disease vaccines to individualized oncology without assuming that platform maturity alone is sufficient for antitumor efficacy.

摘要翻译

背景与目的：信使RNA（mRNA）疫苗现已涵盖已获许可的呼吸道疫苗、一种自扩增核糖核酸（RNA）产品，以及处于后期开发阶段的个体化癌症免疫疗法。该领域的扩展使得平台级比较日益重要，因为疗效、耐受性、可制造性和组织靶向性均受RNA结构及递送系统的影响。本叙述性综述考察了分子工程、递送、新兴RNA结构、传染病转化和癌症免疫疗法，重点关注截至2026年报告的发展。方法：通过PubMed进行了截至2026年8月31日的针对性文献检索，并辅以参考文献追踪和对官方监管来源的定向检索。优先考虑了英文的机制研究、临床试验、监管文件和重点综述。特别关注了2023年至2026年间发表的关于自扩增RNA（saRNA）、环状RNA（circRNA）、分子优化和个体化新抗原疫苗的证据。关键内容与发现：脂质纳米颗粒配制的核苷修饰非复制型mRNA仍是临床验证最充分的结构。分子优化现已超越核苷替代，扩展到帽化学、非翻译区选择、编码序列和RNA结构设计、poly(A)控制以及双链RNA（dsRNA）杂质的去除。saRNA已达到监管授权，并提供剂量节省潜力，而circRNA仍主要处于临床前阶段，且面临未解决的制造和质量控制问题。传染病项目已在抗原设计、脂质纳米颗粒配制、制造和监管可比性方面建立了可重复使用的能力。在肿瘤学中，intismeran autogene联合帕博利珠单抗在切除的黑色素瘤中产生了持久的无复发生存期（RFS）获益，而autogene cevumeran的胰腺癌研究显示，疫苗诱导的T细胞反应在患者间差异显著。因此，肿瘤克隆性、新抗原质量、抗原呈递、免疫微环境、治疗背景和制造时间与编码突变数量同等重要。人工智能（AI）可改善新抗原排序，但并不能消除生物学验证的需求。结论：没有任何一种RNA结构在所有适应症中均具有普遍优越性。进一步的临床进展将依赖于将转录本设计和递送与预期免疫反应相匹配，改进预测性抗原选择和患者分层，并将经过验证的制造和监管能力从传染病疫苗转移到个体化肿瘤学，同时不能假设平台成熟度本身足以保证抗肿瘤疗效。
