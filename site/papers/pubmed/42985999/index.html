Coiled Coils as a Programmable Platform for Computational Biosensor Design.

Kluge, Paul; Shabani, Faezeh; Gemming, Sibylle; Kunze, Georg
Chembiochem : a European journal of chemical biology · 2026-11-05 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该综述聚焦卷曲螺旋蛋白基序的计算设计平台，涵盖AI/机器学习辅助的序列-结构关系建模与可编程生物传感器设计，虽以应用为导向且为综述性质，但核心对象为蛋白质结构计算设计，方法涉及计算与AI策略，与课题相关。

摘要

Coiled coils are a versatile class of alpha-helical protein motifs characterized by recurring sequence patterns that direct their assembly into defined oligomeric bundles and underpin a wide range of biological functions. The well-established sequence-structure relationships governing coiled-coil formation enable predictive control over stability, specificity, and oligomerization state, making them attractive and chemically tractable building blocks for biosensor design in applications ranging from single-molecule detection to synthetic biochemical circuits and functional nanomaterials. After revisiting the theoretical foundations of coiled-coil architecture, this review outlines strategies to engineer programmable binding interfaces that can be rationally tuned to generate orthogonal, high-affinity interaction pairs, enabling flexible and modular biosensor platforms. Particular emphasis is placed on recent advances in computational and data-driven design approaches, including machine-learning and AI-based strategies, alongside established rational and empirical methods for coiled-coil construction. These complementary design principles underpin the development of coiled-coil-based biosensors and are discussed together with selected application examples to illustrate how engineered coiled-coil interactions support sensitive and specific molecular recognition as well as switchable control in synthetic biology and chemical biology. Overall, this review highlights coiled coils as programmable, stable, and highly designable scaffolds at the interface of molecular engineering, computation, and chemical biology.

摘要翻译

卷曲螺旋是一类多功能的α-螺旋蛋白基序，其特征是重复的序列模式，这些模式引导其组装成明确的多聚体束，并支撑广泛的生物学功能。调控卷曲螺旋形成的成熟序列-结构关系使得对其稳定性、特异性和寡聚状态的预测性控制成为可能，使其成为生物传感器设计中具有吸引力且化学上易处理的构建模块，应用范围涵盖单分子检测、合成生化电路和功能性纳米材料。在重新审视卷曲螺旋架构的理论基础后，本综述概述了工程化可编程结合界面的策略，这些界面可通过理性调节生成正交、高亲和力的相互作用对，从而实现灵活且模块化的生物传感器平台。特别强调了计算和数据驱动设计方法的最新进展，包括机器学习和基于人工智能的策略，以及用于卷曲螺旋构建的既有理性和经验方法。这些互补的设计原则支撑了基于卷曲螺旋的生物传感器的发展，并与选定的应用实例一起讨论，以说明工程化卷曲螺旋相互作用如何支持灵敏和特异的分子识别，以及合成生物学和化学生物学中的可切换控制。总体而言，本综述强调卷曲螺旋作为分子工程、计算和化学生物学交叉领域的可编程、稳定且高度可设计的支架。

  • 具体问题：如何利用 coiled-coil 基序的可预测序列-结构关系，通过计算和数据驱动方法设计可编程、正交、高亲和力的结合界面，从而构建模块化的生物传感器平台？

  • 现有方法为何不足：传统理性设计依赖经验规则，通量低；纯实验筛选成本高、周期长；而通用蛋白设计方法未必针对 coiled-coil 的特定规则优化。机器学习/AI 方法虽已进入蛋白设计领域，但在 coiled-coil 传感器场景中的系统整合与评估仍缺乏综述性梳理。
  • 精确研究问题：Can coiled-coil motifs serve as a programmable computational platform for designing orthogonal, high-affinity, switchable biosensor components?
