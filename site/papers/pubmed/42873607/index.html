RNADaemon: Reinforcement Learning-Guided Molecular Dynamics for RNA Tertiary Structure Prediction.

Xu, Wentao; Wu, You; Yu, Xiang
Journal of chemical information and modeling · 2026-10-14 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文将RNA三级结构预测建模为马尔可夫决策过程，用强化学习引导全原子分子动力学模拟，虽对象为RNA而非蛋白质，但其RL+MD框架可迁移至蛋白质构象采样与结构预测，具有方法借鉴价值。

摘要

Accurate prediction of RNA tertiary structure remains a central challenge in structural biology, yet existing computational approaches face fundamental limitations arising from the ruggedness of the RNA free-energy landscape and the scarcity of experimentally resolved structures. Here we present RNADaemon, a framework that formulates RNA tertiary structure prediction as a Markov Decision Process and trains a Soft Actor-Critic agent to apply adaptive external force-field biases that steer all-atom molecular dynamics toward native-like conformations to minimize the eRMSD metric. Trained on 479 monomeric RNA structures and evaluated on 14 test cases from CASP16, RNA-Puzzles, and the PDB, the agent trains stably, approaches SimRNA and FARFAR2 in median RMSD, and recovers the global fold of most reference structures. However, performance remains markedly inferior to that of AlphaFold 3 and trRosettaRNA. We identify the scalar Bellman error as a fundamental information bottleneck that currently limits the performance of the agent. To our knowledge, this is the first application of continuous-action deep reinforcement learning (RL) with all-atom force fields to RNA tertiary structure prediction. This study establishes a benchmark for future comparisons and provides diagnostic insights for RL-guided RNA structure modeling.

摘要翻译

RNA三级结构的准确预测仍是结构生物学中的核心挑战，然而现有计算方法因RNA自由能景观的崎岖性以及实验解析结构的稀缺性而面临根本性限制。在此，我们提出RNADaemon，一个将RNA三级结构预测表述为马尔可夫决策过程（Markov Decision Process）的框架，并训练一个Soft Actor-Critic智能体，以施加自适应外部力场偏置，引导全原子分子动力学 (molecular dynamics) 趋向天然样构象，从而最小化eRMSD指标。该智能体在479个单体RNA结构上训练，并在来自CASP16、RNA-Puzzles和PDB的14个测试案例上评估，训练稳定，在中位RMSD上接近SimRNA和FARFAR2，并恢复大多数参考结构的整体折叠。然而，其性能仍明显逊于AlphaFold 3和trRosettaRNA。我们识别出标量贝尔曼误差（Bellman error）作为当前限制智能体性能的基本信息瓶颈。据我们所知，这是连续动作深度强化学习 (deep reinforcement learning, RL) 与全原子力场在RNA三级结构预测中的首次应用。本研究为未来比较建立了基准，并为RL引导的RNA结构建模提供了诊断性见解。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：如何利用强化学习引导全原子分子动力学采样，使 RNA 三级结构预测从初始构象收敛到近天然构象？
  • 为什么重要：RNA 三级结构预测是结构生物学核心挑战；RNA 自由能景观崎岖，传统 MD 易陷入局部极小，而现有采样方法（如 SimRNA、FARFAR2）计算昂贵且精度有限。
  • 现有方法为何不足：端到端深度学习方法（AlphaFold 3、trRosettaRNA）缺乏物理一致性且对构象系综采样能力有限；物理采样方法（MD、蒙特卡洛）受困于能量景观粗糙度和采样效率；此前 RL 与 MD 结合的工作多限于小分子或蛋白侧链，未在 RNA 全原子尺度验证。
  • 精确研究问题：Can a continuous-action deep RL agent (SAC) learn to apply adaptive external force-field biases that steer all-atom MD toward native-like RNA conformations, as measured by eRMSD, with performance competitive to established RNA structure prediction methods?

06 核心思想

1) 表面方法：将 RNA 三级结构预测建模为 MDP，状态为当前 MD 构象，动作为施加于原子上的外力场偏置，奖励为 eRMSD 的改善；用 Soft Actor-Critic 训练策略网络，在每个 MD 步决策施加何种偏置以引导采样。

2) 核心洞察：与其让 MD 盲目探索能量景观，不如让 RL 智能体学习"何时、何地、施加多大外力"的策略，将采样引导向近天然区域；eRMSD 作为奖励信号比 RMSD 对 RNA 局部构象差异更敏感，更适合作为引导目标。

3) 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：标量奖励（scalar reward）是 RL 引导物理模拟的通用瓶颈——将高维构象信息压缩为单一标量会丢失关键梯度信息；未来可探索基于分布的奖励、对比学习奖励或分层奖励设计。此外，RL 与 MD 的耦合方式（动作频率、偏置形式）可能比网络架构更影响性能。

07 方法总览

  • 输入：RNA 初始构象（序列 + 起始结构，可能来自粗粒化建模或随机构象）
  • 输出：预测的 RNA 三级结构（全原子坐标）
  • 模块： 1. 环境：全原子 MD 模拟器（力场未明确，推测为 AMBER 类） 2. 状态编码器：将当前构象编码为状态向量 3. SAC 策略网络：输出外力偏置动作（连续动作空间） 4. 奖励计算器：基于 eRMSD 改善计算奖励 5. 训练循环：SAC 离线/在线更新
  • 训练：479 个单体 RNA 结构；SAC 智能体学习施加外力偏置的策略
  • 工具：未提供具体 MD 引擎和 RL 框架
  • 假设：eRMSD 是比 RMSD 更合适的引导指标；外力偏置可有效引导 MD 采样
  • 文字流程：初始构象 → MD 模拟 → 每 N 步将当前状态输入 SAC 策略 → 策略输出外力偏置 → 施加于 MD → 继续模拟 → 计算 eRMSD 改善作为奖励 → 更新策略 → 重复直至收敛 → 输出最终构象

14 学到什么

Agent 提炼的知识候选

  1. RL 引导 MD 的范式可迁移：将结构预测建模为 MDP，用 RL 控制 MD 外力偏置，这一框架可迁移至蛋白构象采样、蛋白-配体结合路径采样、蛋白折叠等课题。关键设计选择：动作空间（外力形式）、动作频率（每 N 步决策）、奖励设计（结构相似性指标）。

  2. 标量奖励瓶颈的教训：单一标量奖励（如 eRMSD）可能不足以引导高维构象空间中的精细决策。对蛋白结构预测课题的启示：可探索基于分布的奖励（如预测误差分布）、对比学习奖励（如正负构象对）或分层奖励（全局 + 局部）。

  3. eRMSD 作为 RNA 特异性指标：eRMSD 对 RNA 局部构象（碱基堆积、配对模式）比 RMSD 更敏感，适合作为 RNA 结构优化的目标函数。对蛋白课题的启示：选择或设计结构相似性指标时，应考虑目标分子的特异性（如蛋白可用 TM-score、GDT-TS，RNA 可用 eRMSD、INF）。

  4. oracle 奖励的局限与应对：训练时依赖天然结构计算奖励，实际应用中不可行。可迁移思路：用自监督代理（如能量函数、深度学习打分函数）替代 oracle 奖励，或采用两阶段策略（先 oracle 训练，再无 oracle 微调）。

  5. RL 与 MD 耦合的工程细节：训练稳定性是 RL+MD 的关键挑战，作者将"trains stably"作为贡献，暗示此前工作不稳定。对课题的启示：需关注奖励归一化、动作限幅、MD 步长与 RL 决策频率的匹配。

  6. 基准对比的定位策略：作者将方法定位为"中等精度但新范式"，而非直接挑战 SOTA。对课题的启示：新方法可先建立可行性基准，再逐步优化性能。

15 与已有知识连接

  • RL 引导 MD 的早期工作：此前 RL+MD 多用于小分子构象搜索或蛋白侧链优化（如 RL 控制 MD 中的温度或偏置势），本文首次扩展至 RNA 全原子三级结构预测，属于该方向的规模与对象扩展。
  • SAC 算法：Soft Actor-Critic（Haarnoja et al., 2018）是连续动作 RL 的经典算法，本文将其应用于 MD 外力控制，属于算法在新环境中的迁移。
  • RNA 结构预测方法谱系：与 SimRNA（粗粒化蒙特卡洛）、FARFAR2（Rosetta 片段组装）、AlphaFold 3（端到端深度学习）、trRosettaRNA（深度学习 + 距离图）形成对照，本文属于"RL 引导物理采样"类别。
  • eRMSD 指标：eRMSD 由 Bottaro et al. 提出，用于 RNA 构象比较，本文将其作为奖励函数，属于该指标在新场景（RL 奖励）中的应用。
  • [Analysis] 候选连接：与蛋白结构预测中的 RL 引导 MD 工作（如 RL 控制 replica exchange 或 metadynamics 的 collective variables）可能存在方法学相似性，但本文未引用，需进一步核验。

Agent 生成的研究候选

候选 1：基于分布的奖励替代标量奖励 - 名称：Distributional Reward for RL-Guided MD - 来源局限/观察：作者识别标量 Bellman 误差为瓶颈，但未探索替代方案 - 核心假设：用分布型奖励（如 eRMSD 的分布、或构象系综的多样性奖励）可提供更丰富的学习信号 - 相对本文的增量：直接回应作者指出的瓶颈，可能显著提升性能 - 初步方法：将奖励从标量 eRMSD 改为 eRMSD 分布（如分位数回归）或增加多样性奖励项（如与已采样构象的差异） - 验证方式：在相同 14 测试集上对比标量 vs 分布奖励的 RMSD 和 eRMSD - 可能的失败模式：分布奖励增加训练复杂度，可能不稳定 - 创新状态：unverified

候选 2：无 oracle 奖励的自监督 RL 引导 MD - 名称：Oracle-Free RL-Guided RNA Folding - 来源局限/观察：训练依赖天然结构计算 eRMSD，实际预测中不可用 - 核心假设：用物理能量函数或深度学习打分函数替代 eRMSD 作为奖励，可实现无 oracle 的 RL 引导 - 相对本文的增量：提升实际可用性 - 初步方法：奖励 = −ΔG（来自力场能量）或 −Rosetta 能量，或 −AlphaFold 3 的 pLDDT 置信度 - 验证方式：在无天然结构信息的条件下测试，对比 oracle 版本 - 可能的失败模式：能量函数与 eRMSD 相关性低，引导方向错误 - 创新状态：unverified

候选 3：分层奖励设计（全局 + 局部） - 名称：Hierarchical Reward for RNA Structure Prediction - 来源局限/观察：标量 eRMSD 可能无法区分全局折叠错误与局部构象误差 - 核心假设：分层奖励（全局拓扑奖励 + 局部碱基配对奖励）可提升采样效率 - 相对本文的增量：更精细的引导信号 - 初步方法：全局奖励用 RMSD 或 GDT-RNA，局部奖励用碱基配对正确率或局部 eRMSD - 验证方式：对比单层 vs 分层奖励的收敛速度和最终精度 - 可能的失败模式：两层奖励权重难调，可能冲突 - 创新状态：unverified

候选 4：RL 引导 MD 迁移至蛋白构象采样 - 名称：RL-Guided MD for Protein Conformational Sampling - 来源局限/观察：本文框架针对 RNA，但 MDP + RL + MD 范式可迁移至蛋白 - 核心假设：SAC 引导外力偏置可提升蛋白构象采样效率（如 loop 重构、domain 运动） - 相对本文的增量：跨分子类型的方法迁移 - 初步方法：将状态编码改为蛋白残基图，奖励用 TM-score 或 Rosetta 能量 - 验证方式：在蛋白构象采样基准（如 CASP 目标）上测试 - 可能的失败模式：蛋白自由度更高，动作空间更大，RL 训练更难 - 创新状态：unverified

候选 5：多智能体 RL 引导多片段协同折叠 - 名称：Multi-Agent RL for Multi-Domain RNA Folding - 来源局限/观察：本文仅限单体 RNA，未涉及多结构域协同折叠 - 核心假设：多个 RL 智能体分别控制不同结构域的偏置，可协同折叠多结构域 RNA - 相对本文的增量：扩展至更复杂的 RNA 体系 - 初步方法：每个结构域一个 SAC 智能体，共享全局奖励 - 验证方式：在多结构域 RNA 测试集上对比单智能体 - 可能的失败模式：多智能体 credit assignment 困难 - 创新状态：unverified

审稿人评审

Review setup

  • Input scope Abstract only
  • Assessment boundary Claims and evidence presented in the abstract; no access to methods, figures, tables, or supplementary material
  • Shared manuscript claim summary The authors present RNADaemon, a reinforcement learning framework that applies adaptive external force-field biases to steer all-atom molecular dynamics simulations toward native-like RNA tertiary structures, trained on 479 monomeric RNA structures and evaluated on 14 test cases. The agent reportedly approaches SimRNA and FARFAR2 in median RMSD, recovers global folds of most reference structures, but underperforms AlphaFold 3 and trRosettaRNA. The authors attribute a performance ceiling to the scalar Bellman error and claim this is the first continuous-action deep RL application with all-atom force fields for RNA tertiary structure prediction.
  • Visible evidence base Abstract text only; no quantitative results, figures, tables, or methodological details provided
  • Missing materials affecting confidence Full manuscript, all figures and tables, training and evaluation protocols, hyperparameters, baseline comparison details, statistical significance tests, and code availability

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a technically ambitious framework that combines deep reinforcement learning with all-atom molecular dynamics for RNA structure prediction. The core idea is novel and potentially of interest to the structural biology and machine learning communities. However, the abstract provides insufficient quantitative detail to evaluate the strength of the reported results. The performance claims are stated qualitatively, and the central diagnostic claim regarding the scalar Bellman error is presented without supporting evidence. The framing is honest about current limitations relative to state-of-the-art methods, which is commendable, but the evidence base is too thin to establish the case for the framework's utility or the diagnostic insight.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Computational structural biologists working on RNA folding and prediction, researchers developing machine learning approaches for biomolecular simulation, and method developers in the reinforcement learning community interested in scientific applications. The benchmark comparison and diagnostic analysis would be of interest to those seeking to understand the limitations of RL-guided simulation approaches.
  • Major strengths The formulation of RNA tertiary structure prediction as a Markov Decision Process with continuous-action Soft Actor-Critic is conceptually novel and represents a meaningful departure from existing prediction paradigms. The honest reporting of performance relative to AlphaFold 3 and trRosettaRNA is scientifically appropriate. The identification of a specific information bottleneck, the scalar Bellman error, provides a concrete hypothesis for future investigation.
  • Major Concerns The abstract lacks quantitative results, making it impossible to assess the magnitude of the reported performance. The claim of approaching SimRNA and FARFAR2 in median RMSD is vague without actual numbers or statistical comparisons. The diagnostic claim about the scalar Bellman error is presented as a finding but no evidence is shown. The evaluation on only 14 test cases raises questions about statistical power and generalizability.
  • Minor Comments The abstract would benefit from reporting specific RMSD values and error bars. The claim of being the first application of this specific RL approach should be verified against a broader literature search. The relationship between the training set of 479 structures and the diversity of RNA folds represented is not addressed.
  • Technical failings that need to be addressed before the case is established R1-M1, R1-M2, R1-M3
  • Assessment against Nature-style criteria Originality is high, as the combination of continuous-action RL with all-atom force fields for RNA prediction appears novel. Scientific importance is moderate, given the central challenge of RNA structure prediction, but the demonstrated performance does not yet establish a competitive advantage. Interdisciplinary readership is plausible, spanning structural biology, computational chemistry, and machine learning. Technical soundness cannot be assessed from the abstract alone, as no methodological details or quantitative results are provided. Readability for nonspecialists is adequate, though the abstract assumes familiarity with RL terminology and RNA structure metrics.

  • Concern ID R1-M1
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Evidence sufficiency
  • Claim pointer The agent "approaches SimRNA and FARFAR2 in median RMSD" and "recovers the global fold of most reference structures"
  • Evidence pointer Abstract; location not provided
  • Concern The abstract reports no quantitative RMSD values, no comparison statistics, and no definition of what constitutes "approaching" or "recovering the global fold." Without specific numbers, confidence intervals, or statistical tests, the performance claims cannot be evaluated or reproduced.
  • Why it matters The central contribution of the work is the performance of the RL-guided framework. If the performance relative to baselines is not quantified, the reader cannot determine whether the approach offers any practical utility or whether the reported limitations are meaningful.

  • Resolution test Provide median and per-case RMSD values for RNADaemon, SimRNA, FARFAR2, AlphaFold 3, and trRosettaRNA, with appropriate statistical comparisons and a clear definition of "global fold recovery."

  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Claim support

  • Evidence pointer Abstract; location not provided
