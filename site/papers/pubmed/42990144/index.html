Generative design of functional disordered regions in a developmental transcription factor.

Denes, Lance; Emenecker, Ryan J; Wang, Nongyun; Chirumamilla, Devi; Mohotti, Rashmi; Holehouse, Alex S; Holt, Liam; Lionnet, Timothee
bioRxiv : the preprint server for biology · 2026-11-06 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文针对转录因子RUNX2的无序区（IDR）进行基于生物物理原理的生成式设计，通过构象动力学预测功能并生成功能性IDR，将蛋白质结构设计扩展到无序蛋白区域，属于蛋白质结构计算设计的前沿方向。

摘要

Rapid advances in protein structure design have not translated to intrinsically disordered regions (IDRs). This is largely due to our incomplete understanding of how IDR sequence encodes function. Here, we use stem cell differentiation as a model to map functional sequence features in the C-terminal IDR of the transcription factor RUNX2, which is required to bind chromatin and regulate gene expression during osteogenesis. Surprisingly, linear IDR motifs are dispensable; instead, the conformational dynamics of the IDR predict function. We develop an algorithm for rational IDR design based on biophysical principles, and use it to generate functional IDRs de novo . These findings extend generative protein design beyond the folded proteome.

摘要翻译

蛋白质结构设计的快速进展尚未转化为对固有无序区域（IDRs）的相应突破。这主要归因于我们对IDR序列如何编码功能的理解尚不完整。在此，我们以干细胞分化为模型，绘制转录因子RUNX2的C端IDR中功能序列特征图谱，该区域对于成骨过程中结合染色质和调控基因表达至关重要。出乎意料的是，线性IDR基序并非必需；相反，IDR的构象动力学预测了其功能。我们基于生物物理原理开发了一种用于理性IDR设计的算法，并利用该算法从头生成功能性IDRs。这些发现将生成性蛋白质设计扩展至折叠蛋白质组之外。

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a potentially significant advance in the design of intrinsically disordered regions, a field that has lagged behind folded protein design. The claim that conformational dynamics, rather than linear motifs, encode function in the RUNX2 IDR is conceptually interesting and would be of broad interest if rigorously supported. However, the abstract alone provides insufficient evidence to assess the validity of the central claims. The key assertions regarding dispensability of motifs, the predictive power of conformational dynamics, and the success of de novo designed functional IDRs are stated without quantitative support, experimental detail, or statistical context. The work may be important, but the case is not established from the supplied material.
