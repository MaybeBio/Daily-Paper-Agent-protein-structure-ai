Pressure-temperature phase diagram from global structure prediction and self-consistent phonon calculations based on polynomial machine learning potentials: Application to elemental silicon.

Wakai, Hayato; Seko, Atsuto; Tanaka, Isao
The Journal of chemical physics · 2026-10-09 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文基于多项式机器学习势进行全局结构预测和自洽声子计算，构建硅的压力-温度相图，虽涉及结构预测和机器学习方法，但对象为元素硅晶体而非蛋白质，且算法框架对蛋白质结构计算的迁移性有限。

摘要

Polynomial machine learning potentials (MLPs) based on polynomial rotational invariants have been systematically developed for various systems and applied to efficiently predict crystal structures. In this study, we propose a methodology founded on polynomial MLPs to enumerate crystal structures under high-pressure conditions and evaluate their phase stability at finite temperatures. The proposed approach involves constructing polynomial MLPs with high predictive accuracy across a broad range of pressures, conducting reliable global structure searches for structures containing up to the specified number of atoms, and performing exhaustive self-consistent phonon calculations with reduced errors. We demonstrate the effectiveness of this approach by examining elemental silicon at pressures up to 100 GPa and temperatures up to 1000 K, revealing stable phases across these conditions. The framework established in this study offers a powerful strategy for predicting crystal structures and phase stability under high-pressure and finite-temperature conditions.

摘要翻译

基于多项式旋转不变量的多项式机器学习势（MLPs）已系统性地开发用于多种系统，并有效应用于晶体结构的预测。在本研究中，我们提出了一种基于多项式MLPs的方法论，用于枚举高压条件下的晶体结构，并评估其在有限温度下的相稳定性。所提出的方法包括构建在广泛压力范围内具有高预测精度的多项式MLPs，对包含至指定原子数的结构进行可靠的全局结构搜索，以及执行误差减小的详尽自洽声子计算。我们通过考察元素硅在高达100 GPa压力和高达1000 K温度下的行为，展示了该方法的有效性，揭示了这些条件下的稳定相。本研究建立的框架为在高压和有限温度条件下预测晶体结构和相稳定性提供了一种强有力的策略。
