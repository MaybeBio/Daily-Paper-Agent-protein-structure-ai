Phyfold: environment aware mathematical modeling for protein folding dynamics integrated with physics informed neural network.

Ul Rahman, Jamshaid; Noureen, Iqra; Mannan, Abdul; Uwitije, Rongin
Journal of cheminformatics · 2026-11-06 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文提出PhyFold，将反应扩散方程与物理信息神经网络及ProtT5序列嵌入结合，实现环境依赖的蛋白质折叠动力学建模，是对静态结构预测向动态物理一致模拟的扩展，与蛋白质结构计算课题高度相关。

摘要

Protein folding remains a fundamental problem in computational biology, where understanding folding trajectories and dynamic behavior under physiological conditions is critical for linking structure to function. Recent advances such as AlphaFold have revolutionized sequence to structure prediction, establishing a powerful and robust foundation for computational protein modeling. Building upon this success, we introduce a novel physics informed computational framework that expands static structure prediction toward dynamic and physically consistent modeling of protein folding processes. The proposed framework unifies a mathematically grounded reaction diffusion model of folding kinetics with deep sequence embeddings through a physics informed neural network (PINN), ensuring consistency with governing physical laws while remaining biologically grounded. We refer to this unified framework as PhyFold. Our results illustrate that the proposed approach effectively captures environment dependent folding dynamics and shows strong agreement with both numerical reference solutions and experimental fluorescence measurements under physiologically relevant conditions. By explicitly integrating environmental factors such as temperature, pressure, and pH, PhyFold enables realistic, physics aware simulation of protein folding dynamics. This approach offers a natural extension of modern structure prediction methods and opens new directions for protein design, structure guided drug discovery, and personalized medicine. The code to reproduce the work is available at https://github.com/jamshaidwarraich/PhyFold.git .Scientific contributionPhyFold introduces a hybrid Bio-Physics Informed Neural Network framework that models protein folding kinetics as a reaction diffusion partial differential equation with environmentally modulated kinetic coefficients, integrated with ProtT5 sequence embeddings for sequence specific spatiotemporal prediction an approach not previously demonstrated in the literature. Unlike purely data driven or static structure prediction methods, PhyFold explicitly encodes temperature, pH, and pressure dependence into governing kinetic coefficients, enabling physically interpretable simulation of cooperative folding transitions across diverse physiological conditions. Dual validation against finite difference reference solutions and experimental GFP fluorescence measurements confirms mathematical consistency and biological relevance, establishing PhyFold as a computationally efficient surrogate for environment aware protein folding dynamics.

摘要翻译

蛋白质折叠仍然是计算生物学中的一个基本问题，理解生理条件下的折叠轨迹和动态行为对于将结构连接至功能至关重要。AlphaFold等近期进展彻底改变了从序列到结构的预测，为计算蛋白质建模建立了强大而稳健的基础。在此成功之上，我们引入了一种新颖的物理信息计算框架，将静态结构预测扩展至蛋白质折叠过程的动态和物理一致性建模。所提出的框架通过物理信息神经网络（PINN）将折叠动力学的数学基础反应扩散模型与深度序列嵌入统一起来，确保与主导物理定律的一致性，同时保持生物学基础。我们将此统一框架称为PhyFold。我们的结果表明，所提出的方法有效捕捉了环境依赖的折叠动力学，并在生理相关条件下与数值参考解和实验荧光测量均表现出强烈一致性。通过明确整合温度、压力和pH等环境因素，PhyFold实现了蛋白质折叠动力学的现实、物理感知模拟。该方法为现代结构预测方法提供了自然扩展，并为蛋白质设计、结构引导的药物发现和个性化医学开辟了新方向。复现本工作的代码可在 https://github.com/jamshaidwarraich/PhyFold.git 获取。科学贡献PhyFold引入了一种混合生物物理信息神经网络框架，将蛋白质折叠动力学建模为具有环境调制动力学系数的反应扩散偏微分方程，并与ProtT5序列嵌入集成，用于序列特异性时空预测，这种方法在文献中尚未有先例。与纯数据驱动或静态结构预测方法不同，PhyFold明确将温度、pH和压力依赖性编码到控制动力学系数中，使得在不同生理条件下对协同折叠转变进行物理可解释的模拟成为可能。通过有限差分参考解和实验GFP荧光测量的双重验证，确认了数学一致性和生物学相关性，确立了PhyFold作为环境感知蛋白质折叠动力学的计算高效替代模型。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题: 如何在保持物理一致性的前提下，实现蛋白质折叠过程的时空动态预测，并显式纳入环境因素（温度、pH、压力）的影响？
  • 为什么重要: 蛋白质折叠是连接序列与功能的中心问题。现有深度学习方法（如 AlphaFold）主要预测静态平衡结构，无法捕捉折叠动力学过程；而传统 MD 模拟计算成本极高，难以覆盖生理相关时间尺度。理解折叠动力学对于蛋白质设计、错误折叠疾病研究和药物发现至关重要。
  • 现有方法不足:
  • AlphaFold 等深度学习方法预测静态结构，不提供动力学信息
  • MD 模拟计算成本高，时间尺度受限
  • 传统 PINN 应用多采用固定系数 PDE，缺乏序列特异性
  • 缺乏将环境因素（温度、pH、压力）显式纳入折叠动力学模型的方法
  • 精确的 Can 问题: Can a physics-informed neural network, conditioned on deep sequence embeddings and governed by an environment-modulated reaction-diffusion equation, accurately predict protein folding dynamics that are both mathematically consistent and biologically validated against experimental fluorescence measurements?

06 核心思想

1) 表面方法: PhyFold 将蛋白质折叠建模为反应扩散 PDE（式 1），其中折叠分数 u(x,t) 由扩散项 D∂²u/∂x² 和反应项 f(u;Θ) 共同控制。反应项采用环境调制的 logistic 形式（式 2），折叠速率 α(Θ) 和展开速率 β(Θ) 分别依赖温度、pH、压力。PINN 以空间坐标 x、时间 t 和 ProtT5 序列嵌入 e 为输入，通过复合损失函数（PDE 残差 + 初始条件 + 边界条件 + 数据拟合）训练，实现对折叠动力学的预测。

2) 核心洞察: - 降维建模: 将高维原子坐标空间压缩为一维反应坐标 x ∈ [0,1]，基于能量景观理论，认为折叠可沿主导反应坐标描述 - 环境调制: 将环境因素（温度、pH、压力）通过高斯函数和线性项直接嵌入反应系数，使模型具有环境感知能力 - 序列条件化: 通过 ProtT5 嵌入将序列特异性信息注入 PINN，使同一物理模型可适应不同蛋白 - 双重验证: 数学验证（与 FDM 对比）+ 生物学验证（与实验荧光对比）

3) 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]: - 物理约束（PDE 残差）与数据拟合的平衡是 PINN 成功的关键，本文通过消融实验证明移除 PDE 项导致 r 从 0.822 降至 0.699 - 预训练语言模型嵌入可作为连接序列空间与物理模型的通用桥梁 - 一维反应坐标模型虽简化，但对双态折叠蛋白（如 GFP）足够有效，提示「适度简化」在复杂生物系统中的价值

07 方法总览

  • 输入: 空间坐标 x ∈ [0,1]，时间 t ∈ [0,1]，ProtT5 序列嵌入 e ∈ R^1024，环境参数 Θ={T, pH, P}
  • 输出: 折叠分数 u(x,t) ∈ [0,1] 的时空分布
  • 网络架构: 全连接前馈网络，输入层 1026 神经元（x, t, e），两个隐藏层各 64 神经元，tanh 激活，输出层 sigmoid 激活，Xavier 初始化
  • 物理模型: 反应扩散方程 ∂u/∂t = D∂²u/∂x² + f(u;Θ)，其中 f(u;Θ) = α(Θ)u(1-u) - β(Θ)u
  • 环境调制: α(Θ) = α₀·exp(-κ_T(T-T_opt)²)·exp(-κ_pH(pH-pH_opt)²)，β(Θ) = β₀(1+γ_P(P-P₀))
  • 损失函数: L = λ_PDE·L_PDE + λ_data·L_data + λ_IC·L_IC + λ_BC·L_BC，其中 λ_data=190, λ_PDE=10, λ_IC=100, λ_BC=50
  • 训练: Adam 优化器，初始学习率 5×10⁻³，ReduceLROnPlateau 调度器（patience=5000），早停（patience=5000）
  • 验证: ① 与 FDM 数值解对比（数学一致性）；② 与 GFP 实验荧光数据对比（生物学验证）
  • 数据: GFP 荧光数据集（Sarkisyan 2016），21,446 条序列，80/20 随机划分（seed=1234），训练 17,157 条，测试 4,289 条

文字流程: 1. 对每条蛋白序列，通过 ProtT5 生成 1024 维嵌入向量 e 2. 将 (x, t, e) 拼接输入 PINN，网络输出预测折叠分数 u(x,t) 3. 在训练中同时最小化 PDE 残差（物理约束）、初始/边界条件误差和实验数据拟合误差 4. 训练完成后，对给定蛋白序列和环境条件，预测其折叠动力学的时空演化 5. 将预测的时空平均折叠分数与实验荧光值对比，评估生物学一致性

09 关键公式符号

式(1) 反应扩散方程: ∂u/∂t = D·∂²u/∂x² + f(u;Θ)

  • u(x,t): 折叠分数，u=0 完全展开，u=1 完全折叠
  • x ∈ [0,1]: 归一化反应坐标（非物理空间维度）
  • t ≥ 0: 时间
  • D: 扩散系数，控制构象空间中的扩散速率

式(2) 反应项: f(u;Θ) = α(Θ)·u·(1-u) - β(Θ)·u

  • α(Θ)u(1-u): 协同折叠项（logistic 增长），描述成核-传播机制
  • β(Θ)u: 展开项（线性衰减），描述热涨落和环境应力导致的去稳定化

式(3) 折叠速率环境调制: α(Θ) = α₀·exp(-κ_T·(T-T_opt)²)·exp(-κ_pH·(pH-pH_opt)²)

  • α₀ = 2.0: 最优条件下的基础折叠速率
  • κ_T ∈ [0.001, 0.01]: 温度敏感系数
  • κ_pH ∈ [0.5, 5.0]: pH 敏感系数
  • T_opt = 37°C, pH_opt = 7.4: 生理最优条件

式(4) 展开速率环境调制: β(Θ) = β₀·(1 + γ_P·(P - P₀))

  • β₀ = 0.2: 参考条件下的基础展开速率
  • γ_P ∈ [0.001, 0.1]: 压力敏感系数
  • P₀ = 1.0 atm: 参考大气压

式(5) 初始条件: u(x,0) = A·exp(-(x-x₀)²/(2σ²))，其中 A=0.3, x₀=0.5, σ=0.1，并裁剪至 [0,1]

式(6) 边界条件: ∂u/∂x = 0 在 x=0 和 x=1（Neumann 条件）

式(7) 复合损失函数: L = λ_PDE·L_PDE + λ_data·L_data + λ_IC·L_IC + λ_BC·L_BC

  • λ_data = 190, λ_PDE = 10, λ_IC = 100, λ_BC = 50

式(8) FDM 离散格式: (U_i^{n+1} - U_i^n)/Δt = D·(U_{i+1}^{n+1} - 2U_i^{n+1} + U_{i-1}^{n+1})/Δx² + α(Θ)U_i^{n+1}(1-U_i^{n+1}) - β(Θ)U_i^{n+1}

式(9)-(12) 评估指标: - RMSE = √(1/N Σ(y_i - ŷ_i)²) - MAE = (1/N) Σ|y_i - ŷ_i| - R² = 1 - Σ(y_i - ŷ_i)² / Σ(y_i - ȳ)² - r = Pearson 相关系数

11 结论正确解读

任务范围: 本文聚焦于双态折叠蛋白（以 GFP 为代表）的 mesoscopic 尺度折叠动力学建模，不涉及多结构域蛋白、 intrinsically disordered proteins（IDPs）或共翻译折叠等复杂场景。

输入/真值依赖: - 生物学验证依赖 GFP 荧光数据作为折叠状态的代理指标，荧光强度与折叠分数并非严格等价 - 序列嵌入来自预训练 ProtT5 模型，其表示质量影响下游预测 - 环境参数（温度、pH、压力）通过反应系数间接影响，非直接物理模拟

端到端状态: 模型并非端到端的「序列→折叠动态」预测器。它需要先通过 ProtT5 获取嵌入，再通过 PINN 求解 PDE，且 PDE 参数（D, α₀, β₀, κ_T, κ_pH, γ_P）为全局固定值，不随序列变化。

算力成本: 相比 MD 模拟显著降低，但训练 PINN 需要大量 PDE 残差计算；推理时需对每个 (x,t) 点进行网络前向传播。

历史数据依赖: 模型训练依赖 GFP 荧光数据集，该数据集仅包含 GFP 变体，跨蛋白家族的泛化能力未验证。

最难情形: 碱性应激（pH ≥ 8.0）条件下预测精度显著下降（R² = -0.264），表明模型对极端环境条件的建模能力有限。

群体/领域边界: 结论仅适用于与 GFP 类似的 cooperatively folding 的双态蛋白；对多态折叠、knotted proteins、膜蛋白等未验证。

有边界的复述: PhyFold 在 GFP 变体数据集上，通过将反应扩散方程嵌入 PINN 并结合 ProtT5 序列嵌入，实现了与实验荧光高度相关的折叠动力学预测（r=0.822），并在温度、pH、压力变化时表现出物理一致的响应；但该方法的可靠性边界为近生理条件，对极端碱性环境预测能力有限，且跨蛋白家族的泛化性尚未验证。

14 学到什么

Agent 提炼的知识候选：

  1. PDE 约束 + 预训练嵌入的混合架构：将领域物理定律（反应扩散方程）作为硬约束嵌入神经网络损失，同时用预训练语言模型（ProtT5）提供序列条件信息。这种「物理约束 + 序列条件」的范式可迁移到其他蛋白质计算任务，如结合 AlphaFold 的结构预测与分子动力学模拟。

  2. 环境参数显式建模：将温度、pH、压力等环境因素通过解析函数（高斯、线性）直接嵌入反应系数，而非作为黑箱输入。这种「可解释环境调制」策略可迁移到蛋白质稳定性预测、酶活性预测等需要考虑环境条件的任务。

  3. 双重验证框架：数学验证（与数值解对比）+ 生物学验证（与实验数据对比）的验证策略，为计算模型提供了从理论正确性到实际相关性的完整证据链。可迁移到其他蛋白质计算模型的评估设计。

  4. 降维建模的边界意识：作者明确承认一维反应坐标的局限，并指出需要更高维表示。这种「明确模型边界」的做法值得借鉴——任何简化模型都应清楚标注其适用边界。

  5. 消融实验设计：通过系统移除 PDE 损失和改变权重配置来验证各组件贡献，为混合模型的组件贡献分析提供了范例。

  6. GFP 荧光数据集的复用：将实验荧光数据作为折叠状态的代理指标，为计算模型提供了可扩展的验证资源。类似策略可用于其他具有实验可测指标的蛋白质性质预测。

15 与已有知识连接

  • AlphaFold2 (Jumper et al., 2021): 本文明确以 AlphaFold 为对比对象，指出其静态预测局限。PhyFold 可视为对 AlphaFold 的补充——在结构预测基础上增加动力学维度。两者可组合使用：AlphaFold 预测终态结构，PhyFold 预测折叠路径。

  • RoseTTAFold (Baek et al., 2021): 与 AlphaFold 类似，同为静态结构预测方法，本文将其归入「equilibrium conformations」预测类别。

  • ProtT5 (Elnaggar et al., 2021): 本文直接使用 ProtT5 作为序列嵌入提取器，属于蛋白质语言模型在下游任务中的应用。ProtT5 的表示能力是 PhyFold 序列特异性的基础。

  • PINN (Raissi et al., 2019): 本文的 PINN 方法直接继承自该框架，将 PDE 残差作为损失项。本文的创新在于将 PINN 应用于蛋白质折叠这一生物物理问题，并引入序列嵌入条件。

  • 反应扩散方程 (Turing, 1952): 本文的 PDE 模型基于经典反应扩散理论，但将其应用于蛋白质折叠这一新场景，并引入环境调制系数。

  • 能量景观理论 (Bryngelson & Wolynes, 1987; Onuchic et al., 1997): 本文的一维反应坐标建模基于能量景观理论，将折叠视为沿主导反应坐标的扩散过程。

  • Kramers 扩散理论 (Kramers, 1940): 本文在结论中提及 Kramers 扩散形式主义作为一维反应坐标的理论基础。

  • DeepBenchmark (Xia et al., 2023): 本文通过该基准获取 GFP 荧光数据集，属于蛋白质功能预测基准的复用。

  • Sarkisyan et al. (2016): GFP 荧光数据集的原始来源，包含 21,446 个 GFP 变体的荧光测量。

  • 候选连接方向: 与 MD 模拟的粗粒化模型（如 Martini）、增强采样方法（如 metadynamics）存在潜在互补关系；与蛋白质设计工具（如 ProteinMPNN）可组合用于设计具有特定折叠动力学的蛋白。

16 研究想法

Agent 生成的研究候选：

  1. 候选名称: PhyFold-MD：物理约束神经网络与分子动力学模拟的协同折叠动力学预测 - 来源局限/观察: PhyFold 的一维反应坐标无法解析三维结构细节，而 MD 模拟计算成本高但提供原子级分辨率 - 核心假设: 将 PhyFold 的 mesoscopic 预测作为 MD 模拟的初始条件或偏置势，可加速 MD 采样并保持物理精度 - 初步方法: 使用 PhyFold 预测的折叠路径作为 metadynamics 的 collective variable 定义；或使用 PhyFold 预测的折叠分数作为 MD 模拟的增强采样偏置 - 验证方式: 对已知折叠路径的蛋白（如 villin headpiece、BPTI）比较 PhyFold-MD 与纯 MD 的折叠时间预测和路径一致性 - 创新状态: unverified

  4. 候选名称: 多维 collective variable 空间的 PhyFold 扩展 - 来源局限/观察: 作者明确承认一维反应坐标无法解析三维结构复杂性和非局部接触 - 核心假设: 将一维反应坐标扩展为多维 collective variable 空间（如结合折叠分数、二级结构含量、接触序等），可提升对多态折叠蛋白的建模能力 - 初步方法: 使用多个反应坐标定义多维 PDE 系统，或使用图神经网络编码非局部接触 - 验证方式: 对已知存在中间态的蛋白（如 α-lysozyme、RNase H）测试，比较一维 vs 多维模型的预测精度 - 创新状态: unverified

  6. 候选名称: 跨蛋白家族的 PhyFold 泛化性系统评估 - 来源局限/观察: PhyFold 仅在 GFP 数据集上验证，跨家族泛化性未知 - 核心假设: PhyFold 的物理约束使其在非 GFP 蛋白上仍具一定预测能力，但精度取决于蛋白的折叠机制与双态假设的匹配度 - 初步方法: 在多个具有实验折叠数据的蛋白家族（如 IgG 结合域、spectrin 重复域）上测试 PhyFold - 验证方式: 比较不同家族的 r、RMSE、R²，分析误差与蛋白结构特征的相关性 - 创新状态: unverified

  • Assessment boundary Scientific validity, methodological soundness, novelty, and clarity of the proposed PhyFold framework, based solely on the supplied text. No external data, code, or supplementary materials were accessed.

The model is validated against finite difference method (FDM) solutions and experimental GFP fluorescence data, reporting a Pearson correlation of 0.822 on a held-out test set.
  • Visible evidence base Abstract, introduction, related work, model formulation (equations 1-6), PINN architecture description, FDM benchmark description, experimental setup (GFP dataset, train/test split), results (Figures 3-21, Tables 1-4), ablation study, parameter sensitivity analysis, and conclusion.

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The manuscript presents a conceptually interesting hybrid approach that combines a mesoscopic reaction-diffusion model with deep sequence embeddings via a PINN. The idea of using PINNs for protein folding dynamics with environmental modulation is timely and could be of interest to the computational biology community. However, the current manuscript has significant methodological gaps and validation weaknesses that prevent the claims from being fully established. The mathematical well-posedness analysis is superficial, the biological validation is indirect and lacks rigor, and the comparison with existing methods is insufficient. The work is at an early stage and requires substantial revisions and additional experiments before it can be considered for publication.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Computational biologists and bioinformaticians working on protein folding prediction, particularly those interested in moving beyond static structure prediction toward dynamic modeling. Researchers in the PINN community interested in new applications of physics-informed machine learning to biological systems. Scientists studying protein folding under stress conditions (e.g., pH, temperature) may find the environmental modulation aspect relevant.
  • Major strengths 1. The conceptual integration of a reaction-diffusion PDE with sequence embeddings via a PINN is a novel combination not commonly seen in the literature. 2. The explicit inclusion of environmental parameters (T, pH, P) into the governing equation is a thoughtful step toward physiologically relevant modeling. 3. The dual validation approach (numerical vs. biological) is commendable in principle, even if execution is lacking. 4. The authors acknowledge limitations (e.g., 1D reaction coordinate, alkaline stress) and suggest future directions, showing scientific honesty.
  • Major Concerns
  • R1-M1 (Blocking: Yes)
    • Axis Scientific importance / Technical soundness
    • Claim pointer The authors claim that PhyFold "effectively captures environment dependent folding dynamics and shows strong agreement with both numerical reference solutions and experimental fluorescence measurements."
    • Evidence pointer Results section, Figures 3-21, Tables 2-4
    • Concern The biological validation is fundamentally flawed. The model predicts a "folding fraction" u(x,t) from a 1D reaction-diffusion equation, but this is compared to fluorescence intensity, which is a bulk, ensemble-averaged property. The mapping between the two is not rigorously defined. The authors state that "the spatially and temporally averaged folding fraction was computed as a scalar summary per protein sequence and compared against normalized experimental fluorescence labels," but the exact averaging procedure, the time point at which the average is taken, and the normalization of the fluorescence data are not specified. Moreover, the GFP dataset from Sarkisyan et al. measures fluorescence as a proxy for folding stability, but the relationship between the model's u and fluorescence is not a direct one. The reported Pearson correlation of 0.822 could be an artifact of the averaging procedure or the specific normalization chosen. Without a clear, mechanistic link between the model output and the experimental observable, the biological validation is not convincing.
    • Why it matters The central claim of the paper is that PhyFold produces biologically meaningful predictions. If the validation metric is not properly aligned with the model output, the entire premise of the work is undermined. The reader cannot assess whether the model is genuinely capturing folding dynamics or merely fitting a curve to fluorescence data through an arbitrary post-processing step.
    • Resolution test The authors must provide a clear, mathematical definition of how u(x,t) is converted to a predicted fluorescence value. This should include: (1) the exact spatial and temporal averaging scheme, (2) the time point(s) at which predictions are compared to experimental data, (3) the normalization procedure for both predicted and experimental values, and (4) a justification for why this mapping is physically meaningful. Additionally, the authors should show a scatter plot of predicted vs. experimental values with error bars and discuss the distribution of residuals, not just the correlation coefficient.
  • R1-M2 (Blocking: Yes)
    • Axis Technical soundness
    • Claim pointer The authors state that "the proposed reaction diffusion model is mathematically well posed and admits a unique, bounded, and physically meaningful solution."
    • Evidence pointer "Existence and uniqueness of solution" subsection
    • Concern The well-posedness analysis is superficial and incomplete. The authors show that the reaction term is Lipschitz continuous, but they do not address several critical aspects: (1) the regularity of the solution given the Gaussian initial condition and Neumann boundary conditions, (2) the long-time behavior of the solution (does it converge to a steady state?), (3) the dependence of the solution on the environmental parameters Θ, and (4) whether the solution remains in the physically meaningful range [0,1] for all times. The claim that the "parabolic maximum principle" ensures boundedness is not sufficient, as the reaction term f(u;Θ) can drive u outside [0,1] if α(Θ) > β(Θ) and u is near 1. The authors need to provide a more rigorous analysis or at least numerical evidence that the solution stays within the admissible range.
    • Why it matters The mathematical foundation of the model is a key selling point of the paper. If the well-posedness is not properly established, the reliability of all subsequent numerical results is questionable. Moreover, if the solution can leave the physically meaningful range, the biological interpretation of the results is compromised.
    • Resolution test The authors should provide a complete well-posedness proof, including: (1) existence and uniqueness in the appropriate Sobolev or Hölder spaces, (2) a proof or numerical demonstration that u(x,t) ∈ [0,1] for all t > 0 given the initial and boundary conditions, and (3) a sensitivity analysis of the solution with respect to the environmental parameters. Alternatively, they could add a projection or clipping step in the PINN architecture to enforce the physical bounds and discuss the implications of this choice.
  • R1-M3 (Blocking: Yes)
    • Axis Scientific importance / Technical soundness
    • Claim pointer The authors claim that "PhyFold introduces a hybrid Bio-Physics Informed Neural Network framework... an approach not previously demonstrated in the literature."
    • Evidence pointer Introduction, Related works
    • Concern The novelty claim is overstated. While the specific combination of a reaction-diffusion PDE with ProtT5 embeddings in a PINN may be new, the general idea of using PINNs for protein folding or biophysical processes is not. The authors do not adequately compare their approach with existing PINN-based methods for protein modeling or with other hybrid physics-ML approaches. For example, there are works on using PINNs for molecular dynamics, for solving Fokker-Planck equations related to folding, and for integrating sequence information into physical models. The authors should provide a more thorough literature review and clearly delineate what is genuinely novel about their approach.
    • Why it matters The scientific contribution of the paper depends on its novelty. If the approach is largely an incremental combination of existing ideas, the impact is significantly reduced. The authors need to convince the reader that their framework offers a distinct advantage over existing methods.
    • Resolution test The authors should add a dedicated section comparing their method with at least 3-5 existing approaches (e.g., other PINN-based protein models, traditional kinetic models, deep learning-based folding predictors). The comparison should include both methodological differences and, ideally, quantitative performance comparisons on the same dataset.
  • R1-M4 (Blocking: No)
    • Axis Technical soundness
    • Claim pointer The authors state that "the shared reaction diffusion parameters D, α₀, and β₀ represent universal physical mechanisms governing folding kinetics, while protein specific variability is captured through the high dimensional embedding conditioning."
    • Evidence pointer "Formulation of PINN" subsection
    • Concern The mechanism by which the ProtT5 embedding conditions the PINN is not described in sufficient detail. The authors mention that the input layer receives [x, t, e] where e ∈ R^1024, but it is unclear how the embedding interacts with the PDE parameters. Does the embedding modify the reaction term f(u;Θ) directly? Does it affect the diffusion coefficient D? Is the embedding used as a global conditioning vector that modulates the entire solution, or is it concatenated at every spatial point? The architecture description is too vague to be reproducible.
    • Why it matters Reproducibility is a core requirement for scientific publication. Without a clear description of how the sequence embedding influences the PDE solution, other researchers cannot implement or build upon the method.
    • Resolution test The authors should provide a detailed architectural diagram and a precise mathematical description of how the embedding is incorporated into the PINN. This should include the exact dimensions of all layers, the activation functions, and the forward pass equations.
  • R1-M5 (Blocking: No)
    • Axis Interdisciplinary readership / Readability
    • Claim pointer The authors claim that the model is "computationally efficient surrogate for environment aware protein folding dynamics."
    • Evidence pointer Abstract, Conclusion
    • Concern No computational cost analysis is provided. The authors do not report training time, inference time, or hardware requirements. The claim of computational efficiency is unsubstantiated. Moreover, the comparison with FDM is only for a 1D problem; the computational advantage of PINNs over traditional numerical methods is well-known for high-dimensional problems, but for a 1D problem, FDM is typically very fast. The authors need to demonstrate a concrete computational advantage.
    • Why it matters The practical utility of the method depends on its computational efficiency. If the PINN is slower than a simple FDM solver, the advantage of the approach is unclear.
