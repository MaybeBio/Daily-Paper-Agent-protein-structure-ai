Structural basis of DnaB ring opening by the ancestral helicase loader DciA.

Araujo-Bazan, Lidia; Arias-Palomo, Ernesto
Nucleic acids research · 2026-10-25 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该研究通过冷冻电镜解析DciA介导DnaB解旋酶开环的结构机制，属于纯实验结构表征，不涉及蛋白质结构计算建模或AI/物理模拟方法，与本课题方向无关。

摘要

Ring-shaped helicases must be opened and loaded onto DNA to initiate genome replication. In bacteria, most species lack canonical AAA+ helicase loaders and instead rely on DciA, an evolutionarily conserved, ATP-independent factor whose mechanism has remained unclear. Here we define how DciA remodels the bacterial replicative helicase DnaB. A 3.5 A cryo-EM structure reveals a DnaB6.DciA3 complex in which DciA binds the periphery of the helicase and stabilizes a right-handed open-helical conformation with a single cracked interface. Three DciA molecules dock onto alternate linker-helix/docking-helix modules, while one N-terminal domain (NTD) engages the cracked interface, occupying the pocket vacated by the displaced linker helix and acting as a wedge to promote ring opening. Functional analyses reveal how DciA couples ATPase suppression with efficient helicase loading through coordinated actions of its NTDs and C-terminal domains. Together, these findings define a minimal, ATP-independent mechanism for helicase loading and show how an ancestral loader remodels DnaB to overcome a fundamental topological barrier.

摘要翻译

环状解旋酶必须被打开并装载到DNA上以启动基因组复制。在细菌中，大多数物种缺乏典型的AAA+解旋酶装载因子，而是依赖DciA——一种进化上保守、不依赖ATP的因子，其机制此前尚不明确。在此，我们定义了DciA如何重塑细菌复制解旋酶DnaB。一个3.5 Å的冷冻电镜结构揭示了DnaB6.DciA3复合物，其中DciA结合解旋酶的外围，并稳定一种具有单一裂开界面的右手开放螺旋构象。三个DciA分子对接在交替的连接螺旋/对接螺旋模块上，而一个N端结构域（NTD）则与裂开界面结合，占据被置换的连接螺旋所空出的口袋，并作为楔子促进环的打开。功能分析揭示了DciA如何通过其NTD和C端结构域的协同作用，将ATPase抑制与高效解旋酶装载耦合起来。综合这些发现，定义了一种最小化的、不依赖ATP的解旋酶装载机制，并展示了祖先装载因子如何重塑DnaB以克服基本的拓扑障碍。
