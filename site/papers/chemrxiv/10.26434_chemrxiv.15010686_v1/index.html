DeImmu-MPNN: Reducing Immunogenicity in De Novo Protein Design

Niklas Halbwedl; Sebastian Springer; Martin Zacharias
ChemRxiv 预印本 · 2026-10-19 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文通过直接偏好优化微调ProteinMPNN，在保持结构的前提下降低从头设计蛋白的免疫原性，属于蛋白质序列设计结合AI方法的计算研究，与课题高度相关。

摘要

De novo-designed proteins lack an evolutionary history of immune tolerance, making antidrug antibody (ADA) responses a barrier to their clinical translation. ProteinMPNN is widely used to design sequences for a given protein backbone, but it does not account for immune tolerance, which is important for therapeutic applications. The ADA response to injected proteins is driven principally by MHC class II presentation of protein-derived peptides to CD4+ T-helper cells. We fine-tuned ProteinMPNN using direct preference optimization to reduce ADA-relevant class II epitopes across a six-allele HLA-DRB1 panel, while also reducing class I epitopes. The resulting model, DeImmu-MPNN, reduces NetMHCIIpan-predicted MHC class II presentation by 41.4% relative to the baseline model, bringing predicted presentation to the level of length-matched human proteins, and NetMHCpan-predicted MHC class I presentation by 33.0% on the same designs, for 2.1 points of sequence recovery and no detectable structural cost. The reduction transfers without retraining to unseen DRB1 alleles, the DRB3/4/5 paralogs, and HLA-DQ. It generalizes to 11 published de novo binder complexes, with mean reductions of 50.4% for class II and 30.2% for class I and no systematic predicted cost to interface confidence. DeImmu-MPNN provides a framework for incorporating immune presentation into protein sequence design and may dramatically facilitate the design of proteins with reduced immunogenicity.

摘要翻译

从头设计（de novo-designed）的蛋白质缺乏免疫耐受的进化历史，使得抗药抗体（antidrug antibody, ADA）反应成为其临床转化的障碍。ProteinMPNN 被广泛用于为给定蛋白质骨架设计序列，但它未考虑免疫耐受，而这对于治疗应用至关重要。对注射蛋白质的 ADA 反应主要由 MHC II 类分子将蛋白质衍生肽呈递给 CD4+ 辅助性 T 细胞驱动。我们使用直接偏好优化（direct preference optimization）对 ProteinMPNN 进行微调，以在六等位基因 HLA-DRB1 面板中减少 ADA 相关的 II 类表位，同时减少 I 类表位。所得模型 DeImmu-MPNN 将 NetMHCIIpan 预测的 MHC II 类呈递相对于基线模型降低了 41.4%，使预测呈递水平达到与长度匹配的人类蛋白质相当，并在相同设计中使 NetMHCpan 预测的 MHC I 类呈递降低了 33.0%，序列恢复仅损失 2.1 分，且无可检测的结构代价。该减少无需重新训练即可转移至未见过的 DRB1 等位基因、DRB3/4/5 旁系同源物及 HLA-DQ。它泛化至 11 个已发表的从头设计结合物复合物，II 类平均减少 50.4%，I 类平均减少 30.2%，且界面置信度无系统性预测代价。DeImmu-MPNN 为将免疫呈递纳入蛋白质序列设计提供了框架，并可能显著促进具有降低免疫原性蛋白质的设计。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：De novo 设计蛋白缺乏免疫耐受进化历史，导致抗药抗体（ADA）反应成为其临床转化的主要障碍；现有序列设计工具（如 ProteinMPNN）不考虑免疫原性。
  • 为什么重要：治疗性蛋白的 ADA 反应会中和药物活性、改变药代动力学并可能引发过敏反应；降低免疫原性是治疗性蛋白设计的核心需求之一。
  • 现有方法不足：ProteinMPNN 等序列设计方法仅优化结构恢复率与序列自然度，不包含免疫呈递信息；已有的免疫原性降低策略（如去免疫化）多为事后修补，而非在设计过程中内建。
  • 精确研究问题：Can a fine-tuned ProteinMPNN variant reduce predicted MHC class II presentation in de novo designed proteins without compromising structural integrity or sequence recovery?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：用直接偏好优化（DPO）微调 ProteinMPNN，以 NetMHCIIpan 预测的 MHC II 呈递为偏好信号，同时纳入 MHC I 呈递约束。
  2. 核心洞察：免疫原性（MHC II 呈递）可以作为序列设计的可微偏好信号，而非事后过滤；DPO 允许在保持序列生成能力的同时引导输出分布远离免疫原性表位。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：任何可预测的生物学不利属性（如毒性、聚集倾向、蛋白酶敏感性）都可以通过类似 DPO 框架嵌入序列设计模型，形成「多属性偏好优化」范式。

07 方法总览

  • 输入：蛋白质骨架结构（backbone coordinates）
  • 输出：免疫原性降低的氨基酸序列
  • 模块： 1. 基线模型：ProteinMPNN（预训练） 2. 偏好信号：NetMHCIIpan（MHC II 呈递预测）+ NetMHCpan（MHC I 呈递预测） 3. 优化算法：Direct Preference Optimization（DPO） 4. 评估模块：序列恢复率、结构置信度（未指定具体指标）、NetMHCIIpan/NetMHCpan 预测分数
  • 训练：在 ProteinMPNN 基础上用 DPO 微调，偏好对由 NetMHCIIpan 预测分数构建（低呈递序列为 preferred，高呈递为 dispreferred）
  • 工具：ProteinMPNN, NetMHCIIpan, NetMHCpan, DPO
  • 反馈回路：DPO 损失 → 更新 ProteinMPNN 参数 → 生成新序列 → 重新预测呈递分数
  • 假设：NetMHCIIpan 预测分数可作为真实免疫原性的可靠代理；降低预测呈递可转化为实际 ADA 反应降低
  • 流程：骨架输入 → ProteinMPNN 生成候选序列 → NetMHCIIpan/NetMHCpan 预测呈递 → 构建偏好对 → DPO 微调 → 输出免疫原性降低序列

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围：仅针对 de novo 设计蛋白的序列设计阶段，不涉及抗体/融合蛋白等其他生物制品。
  • oracle/真值输入：使用 NetMHCIIpan/NetMHCpan 预测分数作为免疫原性代理，非实验免疫数据。
  • 端到端状态：计算端到端（骨架→序列→免疫预测），未包含体外/体内实验验证。
  • 算力成本：未提供。
  • 历史数据依赖：依赖 NetMHCIIpan/NetMHCpan 训练数据的等位基因覆盖与预测准确性。
  • 模型依赖：结果依赖 ProteinMPNN 骨架生成能力与 NetMHCIIpan 预测可靠性。
  • 最难情形：未测试高同源人类蛋白、多结构域蛋白、翻译后修饰位点等复杂情形。
  • 群体/领域边界：仅覆盖 HLA-DRB1 六等位基因面板及有限扩展；未覆盖 HLA-DP、HLA-A/B/C 等位基因多样性。
  • 不确定性：预测呈递降低不等于实际免疫原性降低；结构成本「不可检测」受限于所用结构指标。
  • 有边界复述：在计算预测层面，DPO 微调 ProteinMPNN 可显著降低 de novo 设计蛋白的 MHC II/I 呈递预测分数，且结构恢复率损失有限；该效果可迁移到未训练等位基因与已发表 binder 复合物，但未经实验免疫验证。

14 学到什么

Agent 提炼的知识候选： 1. DPO 用于序列设计属性优化：直接偏好优化可作为一种通用框架，将任何可预测的生物学属性（免疫原性、毒性、聚集倾向）嵌入序列生成模型，无需修改模型架构。 2. 预测器作为可微偏好信号：NetMHCIIpan 等预测器可作为「oracle」提供偏好信号，构建「生成器-预测器」联合优化管线。 3. 多等位基因面板训练策略：使用六等位基因 HLA-DRB1 面板训练，可迁移到未训练等位基因，提示等位基因覆盖策略对泛化的重要性。 4. 双 MHC 类同时优化：同时优化 MHC I 和 MHC II 呈递，避免单一优化导致另一类风险上升。 5. 结构-免疫权衡量化：以序列恢复率损失（2.1 点）作为结构保持的代理指标，为「免疫原性降低 vs 结构保持」权衡提供量化框架。 6. 跨复合物验证范式：在已发表 binder 复合物上验证泛化，为方法迁移性提供证据。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a potentially useful contribution to the field of de novo protein design by addressing immunogenicity, an underexplored constraint in sequence design. The reported effect sizes are substantial and the transfer claims are ambitious. However, the abstract alone does not provide sufficient detail to assess the validity of the training procedure, the robustness of the evaluation, or the biological relevance of the predicted reductions. Several claims, particularly regarding structural cost and generalization, require verification against the full manuscript.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers in computational protein design, therapeutic protein engineering, and immunogenicity prediction would be interested. The work offers a practical method to reduce predicted T-cell epitopes during sequence design, which is directly relevant to the development of protein therapeutics and vaccines. The transferability claims may also interest those working on HLA allele diversity and population coverage.
  • Major strengths The problem addressed is clinically relevant and timely. The use of direct preference optimization on a widely used model is a sensible methodological choice. The reported reductions are large and the transfer to unseen alleles and paralogs, if robust, would strengthen the practical utility. The inclusion of class I epitopes alongside class II is a useful extension beyond the primary ADA mechanism.
  • Major Concerns
  • Concern ID R1-M1
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Technical soundness
  • Claim pointer The model reduces predicted MHC class II presentation by 41.4% and class I by 33.0% relative to baseline, with no detectable structural cost.
  • Evidence pointer Abstract only; location not provided
  • Concern The abstract reports percentage reductions and a sequence recovery change but provides no information on the variance, number of designs evaluated, statistical significance, or the distribution of outcomes across designs. The claim of "no detectable structural cost" is unsupported without structural metrics, such as Rosetta energy, RMSD to the design model, or experimental validation.
  • Why it matters Without measures of spread and significance, the reported reductions could be driven by outliers or a subset of designs. The structural cost claim is central to the practical utility of the method, and its absence weakens the case that the trade-off is acceptable.
  • Resolution test Provide per-design distributions, confidence intervals, and statistical tests for the immunogenicity reductions. Include structural metrics comparing DeImmu-MPNN and baseline designs, such as predicted energy, structural deviation, or experimental folding data.
  • Concern ID R1-M2
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Reproducibility and data provenance
  • Claim pointer The model was fine-tuned using direct preference optimization on a six-allele HLA-DRB1 panel.
  • Evidence pointer Abstract only; location not provided
  • Concern The abstract does not describe the training data, the construction of preference pairs, the choice of alleles, or the fine-tuning protocol. Without these details, the method cannot be reproduced or assessed for potential biases, such as overfitting to the training alleles or to the NetMHCIIpan predictor.
  • Why it matters Reproducibility is a core requirement for computational methods. The choice of alleles and the source of training sequences directly affect the generalizability claims. If the preference optimization was driven by a narrow sequence set, the reported reductions may not hold on diverse therapeutic proteins.
  • Resolution test Provide a detailed methods section covering data sources, allele selection rationale, preference pair construction, hyperparameters, and training/validation splits. Include an analysis of performance across sequence diversity.

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis External validity
  • Claim pointer The reduction transfers without retraining to unseen DRB1 alleles, DRB3/4/5 paralogs, and HLA-DQ, and generalizes to 11 published de novo binder complexes.
  • Evidence pointer Abstract only; location not provided
  • Concern The abstract states transfer and generalization but provides no details on the number of alleles tested, the selection criteria for the 11 complexes, the baseline used for comparison, or the metrics for interface confidence.
