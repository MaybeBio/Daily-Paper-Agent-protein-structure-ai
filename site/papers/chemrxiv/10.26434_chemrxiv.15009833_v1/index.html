Machine Learning-Accelerated Inverse Design of Sequence-Controlled Terpolymers

Jeffrey C. Foster
ChemRxiv 预印本 · 2026-10-04 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文用机器学习加速序列控制三元共聚物的逆设计，研究对象是合成高分子而非蛋白质结构，虽涉及序列设计但不可直接迁移至蛋白质结构计算，故相关性低。

摘要

Sequence-controlled polymers hold immense potential for advanced materials design, yet discovering their experimental recipes is bottlenecked by the high-dimension kinetic parameter space of multicomponent copolymerization. Traditional ordinary differential equation (ODE) solvers provide rapid macrokinetic predictions but are fundamentally blind to explicit backbone microstructures. To bridge this gap, we use a stochastic simulation algorithm (SSA) engine to generate high-throughput terpolymer sequence data from a physically constrained parametric sweep across a 9-dimensional kinetic space, charting how feed stoichiometry and kinetic propensities dictate macromolecular structure. Information-theoretic analysis and t-SNE dimensionality reduction of the resulting 27-component triad frequency matrices for each simulation reveal profound microstructural degeneracies, demonstrating that traditional ensemble descriptors, such as the Harwood run number, are fundamentally incapable of describing sequence specifics. To accelerate optimization, we establish a digital twin strategy utilizing an XGBoost regressor to instantaneously predict localized chain-end propagation probabilities based on target sequence motifs. Finally, we demonstrate a closed-loop inverse design strategy that passes the 2 machine learning predictions to a deterministic root-finding optimizer, isolating exact initial monomer feeds and reactivity ratios needed to create them. By training an ML surrogate using explicit chain sequences from SSA, this framework provides a powerful alternative to classical macrokinetic equations, establishing an immediate, predictive tool to accelerate the discovery and reliable synthesis of sequence-controlled functional macromolecules.

摘要翻译

序列控制聚合物在先进材料设计中具有巨大潜力，然而发现其实验配方受限于多组分共聚反应的高维动力学参数空间。传统的常微分方程（ODE）求解器能提供快速的宏观动力学预测，但对明确的骨架微观结构基本无感知。为弥合这一差距，我们采用随机模拟算法（SSA）引擎，在9维动力学空间中进行物理约束的参数扫描，生成高通量三元共聚物序列数据，描绘进料化学计量和动力学倾向如何决定大分子结构。对每次模拟所得的27组分三元组频率矩阵进行信息论分析和t-SNE降维，揭示了深刻的微观结构简并性，证明传统系综描述符（如Harwood运行数）从根本上无法描述序列细节。为加速优化，我们建立了一种数字孪生策略，利用XGBoost回归器基于目标序列基序即时预测局部链端传播概率。最后，我们展示了一种闭环逆向设计策略，将2个机器学习预测传递给确定性求根优化器，精确分离出创建这些序列所需的初始单体进料和反应活性比。通过使用SSA的显式链序列训练ML替代模型，该框架为经典宏观动力学方程提供了强大替代方案，建立了一种即时预测工具，以加速序列控制功能大分子的发现和可靠合成。
