Borrowing the fold, building the site: non-catalytic protein scaffolds in the age of computational metalloenzyme design

Andrea Rodil; Jan Deska
ChemRxiv 预印本 · 2026-10-28 · 仅摘要 · 原文

这篇Perspective讨论如何利用非催化蛋白支架和计算设计（含结构预测）构建人工金属酶活性位点，涉及蛋白结构计算但以观点综述为主，方法创新有限。

摘要

The design of novel metalloenzymes has gained its motivation and momentum less from what enzymes already do well rather than from what they do not do at all, that is, the organic chemistry outside any biosynthetic pathways. In search for starting points to create proteins that are active in new-to-Nature transformations, computational design in particular offers powerful solutions by supplying folds made to order en route to inventing tailor-made de novo biocatalysts. Nature, however, rarely invents; it decorates. A large share of biological catalysis sits on backbones whose original role was structural or recognition-based, and in which the metal center occupies no more than a handful of side chains within an otherwise unchanged fold. In this Perspective we make the case that especially in systems addressing abiological catalysis, promising and successful approaches oftentimes adapt non-catalytic scaffolds as their foundation, taking binding modules, small disulfide-locked domains and synthetic miniproteins as hosts. We organize the discussion around the minimal functional site: the smallest ligand set that, placed on a rigid and pre-organized backbone, holds a metal in a defined geometry. Whether a given scaffold is able to offer such a site is a structural question before it is a chemical one. We examine how far current structure prediction can guide the placement of such sites, where its confidence scores mislead, and which spectroscopic and computational evidence remains indispensable. Scaffold repurposing, we conclude, offers organic synthesis a route to catalysts that enzymology alone would not deliver.

摘要翻译

新型金属酶 (metalloenzyme) 的设计，其动力与势头的来源，与其说在于酶已经做得很好的事情，不如说在于它们完全不做的事情，即任何生物合成途径之外的有机化学。在寻找创建能够催化自然界全新转化 (new-to-Nature transformation) 的蛋白质的起点时，计算设计尤其提供了强大的解决方案，它通过提供按需定制的折叠结构，为发明量身定做的从头生物催化剂 (de novo biocatalyst) 铺平道路。然而，自然界很少发明；它只是装饰。生物催化的很大一部分建立在骨架之上，这些骨架最初的作用是结构性的或基于识别的，其中金属中心仅占据少数几个侧链，而其余折叠结构保持不变。在本展望 (Perspective) 中，我们提出这样的论点：特别是在处理非生物催化 (abiological catalysis) 的系统中，有前景且成功的方法往往以非催化支架 (non-catalytic scaffold) 为基础进行改造，以结合模块、小型二硫键锁定结构域和合成迷你蛋白 (miniprotein) 作为宿主。我们围绕最小功能位点 (minimal functional site) 组织讨论：即最小的配体集合，将其置于刚性且预先组织好的骨架上时，能够以确定的几何构型固定金属。一个给定的支架是否能够提供这样的位点，首先是一个结构问题，其次才是一个化学问题。我们考察当前的结构预测在多大程度上能够指导此类位点的放置，其置信度评分在何处产生误导，以及哪些光谱学和计算证据仍然不可或缺。我们得出结论：支架重定位 (scaffold repurposing) 为有机合成提供了一条通往催化剂的途径，而仅靠酶学本身无法实现这一点。

06 核心思想

1) 表面方法：不从头发明折叠，而是「借用折叠、构建位点」——选取原本非催化的支架（binding module、二硫键锁定小结构域、合成 miniprotein），在其刚性、预组织骨架上放置最小功能位点（能定义金属几何的最小配体集合）。 2) 核心洞察：判断一个支架能否承载金属位点，首先是结构问题（骨架是否刚性、预组织、能否以确定几何固定金属），其次才是化学问题；因此结构预测与结构判据应前置，而结构预测的置信度分数不能直接等同于「位点可放置性」。 3) 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：在「结构预测/生成 × 功能位点设计」类任务中，应把「骨架可承载性」与「局部化学互补性」解耦评估；对预测置信度需做任务特定的校准，而非直接采信全局分数。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a coherent and intellectually engaging thesis that scaffold repurposing, rather than de novo fold invention, is a pragmatic and often successful strategy in computational metalloenzyme design. The framing around the "minimal functional site" is conceptually useful and could resonate with a broad readership. However, the abstract is largely programmatic and lacks specific evidence, case examples, or quantitative assessments. The strength of the argument cannot be evaluated from the supplied material, and several claims are asserted without visible support. The manuscript may have merit, but the current evidence base is insufficient to establish the case.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers in computational protein design, metalloenzyme engineering, biocatalysis, and synthetic organic chemistry would find this Perspective relevant. The emphasis on repurposing non-catalytic scaffolds and the critical assessment of structure prediction tools speaks directly to practitioners seeking practical guidance in enzyme design. The conceptual framework of the "minimal functional site" could also interest structural biologists and those working on metal coordination in proteins.
  • Major strengths The abstract articulates a clear and timely thesis that challenges the default emphasis on de novo fold design. The concept of the "minimal functional site" as a structural prerequisite is elegant and provides a useful organizing principle. The authors explicitly acknowledge limitations of current structure prediction, which signals a balanced and critical perspective. The writing is accessible and avoids unnecessary jargon, making the argument understandable to nonspecialists.

Major Concerns

  • Concern ID R1-M1
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Evidence sufficiency
  • Claim pointer The authors claim that "promising and successful approaches oftentimes adapt non-catalytic scaffolds as their foundation" and that scaffold repurposing "offers organic synthesis a route to catalysts that enzymology alone would not deliver."
  • Evidence pointer Abstract; location not provided
  • Concern The abstract asserts that non-catalytic scaffolds are frequently used and successful in metalloenzyme design, but no specific examples, case studies, or comparative data are provided. The claim of success is central to the Perspective's thesis, yet it is presented without any visible support.
  • Why it matters Without concrete examples or a survey of the literature, the reader cannot evaluate whether the claim is an overgeneralization or a well-supported observation. The credibility of the entire Perspective rests on this assertion.

  • Resolution test The full manuscript must include specific case studies, a literature survey, or quantitative comparisons showing that scaffold repurposing is indeed a common and successful strategy. Ideally, the authors would provide examples of designed metalloenzymes with non-catalytic scaffolds and their performance metrics.

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Claim support
  • Claim pointer The authors state that "whether a given scaffold is able to offer such a site is a structural question before it is a chemical one" and that "current structure prediction can guide the placement of such sites, where its confidence scores mislead."
  • Evidence pointer Abstract; location not provided
  • Concern The abstract makes a strong claim about the role of structure prediction and its limitations, but no evidence is presented to support the assertion that confidence scores mislead in specific ways. The claim is plausible but unsubstantiated.

  • Resolution test The full manuscript should include examples or analyses showing where structure prediction succeeded and where it failed, ideally with quantitative assessments of confidence score reliability in the context of metal-binding site placement.

  • Evidence pointer Abstract; location not provided
