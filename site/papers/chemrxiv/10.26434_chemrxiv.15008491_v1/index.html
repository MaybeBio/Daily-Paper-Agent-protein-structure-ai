PARAM-DOCK: An Integrated Framework for Parallel Multi-Protein–Multi-Ligand Docking and Scoring for Scalable Structure-Based Drug Discovery

Samarth Kittad; Mallikarjunachari Uppuladinne; Aneesh Kotipalli; Shruti Koulgi; Vinod Jani; Uddhavesh Sonavane
ChemRxiv 预印本 · 2026-09-08 · 仅摘要 · 原文

提出一个集成多蛋白-多配体并行对接与评分的开源框架PARAM-DOCK，结合经验打分函数和迭代局部搜索，在蛋白质-配体对接问题上表现竞争性，但方法创新性有限，属于工程化应用。

摘要

Molecular docking is a central component of structure-based drug discovery, but practical docking studies often require several independent programs for receptor and ligand preparation, binding-site definition, docking, visualisation, interaction analysis, and downstream compound assessment. This fragmentation becomes particularly limiting when multiple protein targets and multiple ligands must be evaluated in the same study. Here, we present PARAM-DOCK, an open-source molecular docking engine and integrated web workflow implemented in Python with Numba-compiled numerical kernels. PARAM-DOCK supports parallel multi-protein-multi-ligand docking and automates major pre-processing and post-processing steps within a single platform. The docking engine combines a seven-term empirical scoring function with an iterated local search strategy in which Monte Carlo perturbations are followed by bounded quasi-Newton minimisation using analytical gradients. Final pose selection can additionally incorporate binding-basin population and pocket-complementarity descriptors when they are informative. The web platform integrates molecular preparation, docking setup, pose ranking, 2D and 3D protein-ligand interaction analysis, comparative score and interaction heatmaps, molecular visualisation, and ADMET prediction. PARAM-DOCK was benchmarked against Vina using matched receptor and ligand PDBQT files, identical search boxes, symmetry-corrected RMSD evaluation, and identical hardware. On the 285-complex CASF-2016 core set, PARAM-DOCK achieved a Pearson correlation of 0.631 for scoring power and a mean within-target Spearman correlation of 0.579, compared with 0.604 and 0.528 for Vina. In de novo redocking of 255 complexes, PARAM-DOCK placed the top-ranked pose within 2.0 Å in 68.6% of cases and recovered a near-native pose among nine outputs in 84.3%, compared with 74.1% and 93.7% for Vina. Conditional on generating a near-native pose, PARAM-DOCK ranked it first in 81.4% of complexes versus 79.1% for Vina. These results indicate competitive scoring and ranking performance, while conformational sampling remains the main area for further improvement.

摘要翻译

分子对接是基于结构的药物发现的核心组成部分，但实际对接研究通常需要多个独立程序用于受体和配体准备、结合位点定义、对接、可视化、相互作用分析以及下游化合物评估。当同一研究中必须评估多个蛋白质靶点和多个配体时，这种碎片化变得尤为受限。在此，我们提出PARAM-DOCK，一个开源分子对接引擎和集成网络工作流程，采用Python实现并带有Numba编译数值内核。PARAM-DOCK支持并行多蛋白-多配体对接，并在单一平台内自动化主要预处理和后处理步骤。该对接引擎将七项经验评分函数与迭代局部搜索策略相结合，其中蒙特卡洛扰动后接使用解析梯度的有界拟牛顿最小化。最终构象选择在信息丰富时还可额外纳入结合盆地群体和口袋互补性描述符。该网络平台整合了分子准备、对接设置、构象排序、2D和3D蛋白-配体相互作用分析、比较评分和相互作用热图、分子可视化以及ADMET预测。PARAM-DOCK使用匹配的受体和配体PDBQT文件、相同的搜索框、对称校正RMSD评估以及相同硬件，与Vina进行了基准测试。在包含285个复合物的CASF-2016核心集上，PARAM-DOCK的评分能力皮尔逊相关系数为0.631，靶内平均斯皮尔曼相关系数为0.579，而Vina分别为0.604和0.528。在对255个复合物进行从头重新对接时，PARAM-DOCK在68.6%的案例中将排名第一的构象置于2.0 Å以内，并在84.3%的案例中从九个输出中恢复出近天然构象，而Vina分别为74.1%和93.7%。在生成近天然构象的条件下，PARAM-DOCK在81.4%的复合物中将其排在第一，而Vina为79.1%。这些结果表明其具有竞争力的评分和排序性能，而构象采样仍是进一步改进的主要领域。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题: 当前分子对接工作流碎片化，需要多个独立程序处理受体/配体准备、对接、可视化和分析，尤其在多蛋白-多配体场景下效率低下。
  • 为什么重要: 结构药物发现中，高通量筛选和虚拟筛选需要高效、可扩展且集成的工具，以减少人工干预和错误，加速先导化合物发现。
  • 现有方法为何不足: 现有工具如AutoDock Vina虽然强大，但通常需要外部工具进行准备和分析，缺乏统一的并行化支持，且工作流不连贯。
  • 精确的「Can ... ?」研究问题: Can an integrated, parallelized molecular docking framework with automated pre- and post-processing achieve competitive scoring and ranking performance compared to established tools like AutoDock Vina?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法: 开发一个集成的、并行化的分子对接框架，包含一个基于经验评分函数和迭代局部搜索的对接引擎，以及一个自动化的Web工作流。
  2. 核心洞察: 通过将并行计算（多蛋白-多配体）和工作流自动化（准备、对接、分析、ADMET）整合到一个开源平台中，可以显著提升虚拟筛选的效率和可重复性，而无需牺牲与Vina等成熟工具相当的评分和排名性能。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]: 在计算生物学中，集成化和自动化是提升工具实用性和可扩展性的关键，即使核心算法（如评分函数）并非革命性创新。一个设计良好的工作流可以降低使用门槛，并促进大规模、系统性的研究。

07 方法总览

  • 输入: 受体结构（PDB格式）、配体结构（SDF或MOL2格式）、对接盒（中心坐标和尺寸）。
  • 输出: 对接构象（PDBQT格式）、评分、2D/3D相互作用图、热图、ADMET预测结果。
  • 模块:
    1. 准备模块: 自动化受体和配体准备（加氢、分配电荷、生成PDBQT）。
    2. 对接引擎: 核心算法，包含评分函数和搜索算法。
    3. 后处理模块: 构象聚类、排名、相互作用分析、ADMET预测。
    4. Web界面: 提供用户友好的交互式工作流。
  • 训练: 评分函数的参数通过拟合实验结合亲和力数据（如PDBbind）进行优化（未提供具体细节）。
  • 工具: Python, Numba (用于数值计算加速), Web框架（未指定）。
  • 反馈回路: 无显式反馈回路。对接过程是单向的：准备 -> 搜索 -> 评分 -> 分析。
  • 假设: 经验评分函数能够有效近似结合自由能；迭代局部搜索能够找到全局最优构象；自动化准备步骤不会引入显著错误。
  • 文字流程: 用户通过Web界面上传受体和配体文件 -> 准备模块自动生成PDBQT文件 -> 用户定义对接盒 -> 对接引擎并行执行多蛋白-多配体对接（每个任务独立进行） -> 后处理模块对每个对接任务的结果进行聚类、排名和相互作用分析 -> 生成综合报告（包括热图、ADMET预测）。

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围: 结论仅限于分子对接的评分、排名和采样性能，不涉及结合自由能预测的绝对精度或药物发现中的下游成功率。
  • Oracle/真值输入: 所有测试均使用已知的晶体结构作为参考，这代表了理想情况。在真实虚拟筛选场景中，受体结构可能未知或存在构象变化。
  • 端到端状态: 结论基于端到端的对接流程（准备+对接+分析），但准备模块的自动化效果未单独验证。
  • 算力成本: 未提供与Vina的计算时间对比，因此无法评估其效率优势。
  • 历史数据依赖: 评分函数参数可能依赖于PDBbind等训练集，其泛化能力受限于训练数据。
  • 模型依赖: 结论依赖于特定的评分函数和搜索算法，不适用于其他方法。
  • 最难情形: 采样能力是主要瓶颈，尤其在构象空间大的柔性配体或蛋白上。
  • 群体/领域边界: 结论基于CASF-2016和255个复合物，可能不适用于所有蛋白-配体系统（如蛋白-蛋白、核酸-配体）。
  • 不确定性: 评分和排名性能的差异（如Pearson r 0.631 vs 0.604）可能不具有统计显著性，摘要未提供置信区间或p值。
  • 有边界的复述: 在CASF-2016基准测试和255个复合物的de novo redocking测试中，PARAM-DOCK的评分和排名性能与AutoDock Vina相当，但其采样能力（特别是top-1构象的准确性）低于Vina。

14 学到什么

Agent 提炼的知识候选

  1. 可迁移概念: 集成化工作流设计。PARAM-DOCK将准备、对接、分析、ADMET预测整合到一个平台。对于蛋白质结构计算研究，可以借鉴此思路，将AlphaFold结构预测、MD模拟、自由能计算、突变分析等步骤整合到一个自动化工作流中，减少人工干预和错误。
  2. 可迁移方法: 并行多任务处理。PARAM-DOCK支持多蛋白-多配体并行对接。在蛋白质设计或虚拟筛选场景中，可以设计类似的并行框架，同时处理多个蛋白质突变体与多个配体或多个蛋白质序列与多个构象的对接或评分任务。
  3. 可迁移实验设计: 条件性性能评估。作者在de novo redocking中引入了“conditional rank-1 rate”，即仅在采样成功时评估排名能力。这种条件性评估可以更精细地解耦不同模块（采样 vs. 评分）的贡献，适用于评估其他两步式方法（如构象生成 + 评分）。
  4. 可迁移思路: 开源与可扩展性。PARAM-DOCK是开源的，允许社区贡献和改进。对于本课题，开发开源、模块化的蛋白质结构计算工具（如可插拔的评分函数、搜索算法）可以促进方法创新和复现。

15 与已有知识连接

  • 相似方法: 与AutoDock Vina (Trott & Olson, 2010) 直接对比，PARAM-DOCK在评分和排名上表现相当，但采样能力较弱。其经验评分函数和迭代局部搜索策略与Vina的算法家族相似。
  • 组合方法: PARAM-DOCK的集成化思路与Schrödinger Suite (商业) 和 MOE (商业) 类似，但它是开源的。其Web界面设计可参考SwissDock (Grosdidier et al., 2011) 等在线对接平台。
  • 冲突方法: 与基于深度学习的对接和评分方法（如EquiDock (Ganea et al., 2022), DiffDock (Corso et al., 2023)）形成对比。PARAM-DOCK代表经典的物理/经验方法，而AI方法在采样和评分上可能提供不同的性能特征。
  • 可迁移领域: 该框架的并行化和工作流自动化设计理念可直接迁移到蛋白质-蛋白质对接、蛋白质-核酸对接或虚拟筛选的其他领域。

16 研究想法

Agent 生成的研究候选

  1. 名称: 基于集成化工作流的蛋白质-配体结合自由能预测平台

    • 来源局限/观察: PARAM-DOCK的评分函数是经验性的，精度有限。其集成化工作流可以扩展以包含更精确的物理方法。
    • 核心假设: 将PARAM-DOCK的集成化工作流与MM/GBSA或FEP等更精确的自由能计算方法结合，可以在保持自动化优势的同时，提升结合亲和力预测的准确性。
    • 相对本文的增量: 在PARAM-DOCK的后处理模块中集成MM/GBSA或FEP计算，并自动化输入准备和结果分析。
    • 初步方法: 1) 使用PARAM-DOCK进行初始对接和构象采样。2) 对每个配体的top-N构象，自动运行MM/GBSA计算（如使用AmberTools）。3) 整合MM/GBSA评分与原始对接评分，生成最终排名。
    • 验证方式: 在CASF-2016或更大的数据集上，对比PARAM-DOCK原始评分、MM/GBSA评分和组合评分的Pearson r和Spearman ρ。
    • 可能的失败模式: MM/GBSA计算耗时，可能抵消并行化带来的效率优势；MM/GBSA的精度提升有限，且对力场和溶剂模型敏感。
    • 创新状态: unverified

  2. 名称: 基于深度学习的构象采样增强模块

    • 来源局限/观察: PARAM-DOCK的采样能力是其主要瓶颈，低于Vina。
    • 核心假设: 使用一个预训练的生成式AI模型（如扩散模型或流模型）来生成初始配体构象，可以显著提升PARAM-DOCK的采样效率，使其达到或超过Vina的水平。
    • 相对本文的增量: 用AI生成的构象作为PARAM-DOCK迭代局部搜索的初始点，或直接替换其搜索算法。
    • 初步方法: 1) 使用DiffDock或Torsional Diffusion等模型，为每个配体生成一组初始构象。2) 将这些构象作为PARAM-DOCK局部优化（quasi-Newton minimisation）的起点。3) 使用PARAM-DOCK的评分函数进行最终排名。
    • 验证方式: 在de novo redocking测试中，对比增强版PARAM-DOCK与原始PARAM-DOCK和Vina的top-1和top-9近原生构象恢复率。
    • 可能的失败模式: AI生成的构象可能偏离真实结合模式，导致局部优化陷入错误区域；AI模型的计算开销可能抵消采样效率的提升。
    • 创新状态: unverified

  3. 名称: 多蛋白-多配体对接结果的交互式可视化与比较分析工具

    • 来源局限/观察: PARAM-DOCK提供了热图和2D/3D图，但缺乏对多蛋白-多配体结果的交互式、可比较的深度分析。
    • 核心假设: 开发一个交互式可视化工具，允许用户同时比较不同蛋白-配体对的结合模式、相互作用指纹和评分，可以加速先导化合物的识别和优化。
    • 相对本文的增量: 在PARAM-DOCK的后处理模块中增加一个交互式Web组件，支持多结果对比。
    • 初步方法: 1) 使用Plotly或D3.js开发交互式热图、散点图和3D叠加视图。2) 允许用户选择多个蛋白-配体对，并排显示其相互作用指纹（如PLIF）。3) 集成聚类分析，自动识别具有相似结合模式的配体。
    • 验证方式: 通过用户研究，评估该工具在辅助药物化学家进行先导化合物选择时的效率和准确性。
    • 可能的失败模式: 交互式可视化可能增加计算和渲染负担；用户可能更习惯使用PyMOL或ChimeraX等专业工具。
    • 创新状态: unverified

审稿人评审

Review setup

  • Input scope Full manuscript (ChemRxiv preprint)
  • Assessment boundary Methods, results, and claims as presented in the provided text
  • Shared manuscript claim summary The authors present PARAM-DOCK, an open-source integrated framework for parallel multi-protein–multi-ligand docking and scoring, and claim it achieves competitive performance against AutoDock Vina on standard benchmarks.
  • Visible evidence base Benchmarking results on CASF-2016 core set (285 complexes) and de novo redocking (255 complexes), including Pearson correlation, Spearman correlation, and RMSD-based success rates.
  • Missing materials affecting confidence No source code, no web platform URL, no detailed scoring function parameters, no statistical uncertainty (e.g., confidence intervals, standard errors) for benchmark metrics, no ablation studies, no runtime benchmarks, no comparison with other modern docking tools beyond Vina.

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The manuscript describes a potentially useful integrated docking platform, but the evidence provided is insufficient to establish the claimed advantages over existing tools. The benchmarking is limited in scope, lacks statistical rigor, and does not demonstrate the key claimed features (parallel multi-protein–multi-ligand docking, web integration) with concrete data. The technical novelty of the scoring function and search algorithm is unclear from the description.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Computational chemists and structural biologists working on virtual screening and drug discovery may be interested if the platform is demonstrated to be practically useful, scalable, and accessible. The integrated workflow could reduce tool-switching overhead.
  • Major strengths 1. The concept of an integrated, open-source platform that automates multiple steps of the docking workflow is practically motivated. 2. The benchmarking against Vina using matched inputs and identical hardware is a fair comparison approach. 3. The use of Numba for numerical acceleration is a sensible technical choice for Python-based performance.
  • Major Concerns
  • Concern ID R1-M1
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Evidence sufficiency
  • Claim pointer "PARAM-DOCK supports parallel multi-protein–multi-ligand docking and automates major pre-processing and post-processing steps within a single platform."
  • Evidence pointer Section: Results (benchmarking)
  • Concern The manuscript provides no evidence that the parallel multi-protein–multi-ligand docking feature actually works. All benchmarking is on single-protein–single-ligand redocking/scoring tasks. There are no results showing simultaneous docking of multiple ligands against multiple targets, no scalability data (e.g., wall-clock time vs. number of targets/ligands), and no demonstration of the web platform's functionality.
  • Why it matters The parallel multi-protein–multi-ligand capability is a central claimed innovation and the namesake of the tool. Without any evidence, this claim is unsupported, and the tool's practical value over existing single-target docking tools is unsubstantiated.
  • Resolution test Provide results from a multi-protein–multi-ligand docking experiment (e.g., a small virtual screening campaign against 5–10 targets with 100+ ligands), including runtime, success rates, and comparison with sequential single-target docking. Provide a live or documented demonstration of the web platform.
  • Concern ID R1-M2
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Statistical rigor
  • Claim pointer "PARAM-DOCK achieved a Pearson correlation of 0.631 for scoring power and a mean within-target Spearman correlation of 0.579, compared with 0.604 and 0.528 for Vina."
  • Evidence pointer Section: Results (benchmarking)
  • Concern The reported correlations are point estimates without any measure of uncertainty (e.g., confidence intervals, standard errors, or bootstrapped ranges). It is impossible to assess whether the observed differences (0.027 for Pearson, 0.051 for Spearman) are statistically significant or within the noise of the benchmark. The sample size (285 complexes) is moderate, but without error bars, the claim of "competitive performance" is not quantitatively supported.
  • Why it matters The core claim of the paper is that PARAM-DOCK's scoring and ranking performance is competitive with Vina. Without statistical uncertainty, the reader cannot evaluate whether the small differences are meaningful or due to random variation.
  • Resolution test Provide 95% confidence intervals for all correlation metrics (e.g., via bootstrapping) and perform a statistical test (e.g., paired comparison) to assess significance of differences between PARAM-DOCK and Vina.
  • Concern ID R1-M3
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Completeness of evidence
  • Claim pointer "In de novo redocking of 255 complexes, PARAM-DOCK placed the top-ranked pose within 2.0 Å in 68.6% of cases and recovered a near-native pose among nine outputs in 84.3%, compared with 74.1% and 93.7% for Vina."
  • Evidence pointer Section: Results (benchmarking)
  • Concern The redocking success rates show Vina outperforming PARAM-DOCK by 5.5% (top-1) and 9.4% (top-9). The authors acknowledge "conformational sampling remains the main area for further improvement," but this is a substantial deficit. The claim of "competitive scoring and ranking performance" is undermined by the fact that Vina finds a near-native pose more often. The conditional ranking metric (81.4% vs. 79.1%) is only meaningful when a near-native pose exists, which is less frequent for PARAM-DOCK.
  • Why it matters For a docking tool, sampling performance (finding a near-native pose) is as important as scoring (ranking it correctly). The observed deficit in sampling is a significant practical limitation that is downplayed.
  • Resolution test Provide a more detailed analysis of sampling failures (e.g., by target class, ligand flexibility, binding site characteristics). Discuss whether the sampling deficit is inherent to the search algorithm or could be mitigated by parameter tuning. Consider reporting additional metrics such as median RMSD or enrichment factors.
  • Concern ID R1-M4
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking No
  • Axis Novelty and technical depth
  • Claim pointer "The docking engine combines a seven-term empirical scoring function with an iterated local search strategy in which Monte Carlo perturbations are followed by bounded quasi-Newton minimisation using analytical gradients."
  • Evidence pointer Section: Methods (scoring function and search)
  • Concern The description of the scoring function and search algorithm is too brief to assess novelty or technical soundness. The seven terms are not listed, their functional forms are not given, and the parameterization method is not described. The iterated local search with Monte Carlo + quasi-Newton is a common approach (e.g., used in AutoDock Vina itself). Without details, it is impossible to determine whether the engine offers any algorithmic innovation over existing tools.
  • Why it matters The docking engine is the core technical contribution. Without sufficient detail, the work cannot be reproduced, and the novelty cannot be evaluated.
  • Resolution test Provide the full scoring function (terms, weights, functional forms), the parameterization procedure (e.g., training set, optimization method), and a detailed description of the search algorithm (e.g., number of Monte Carlo steps, convergence criteria, gradient computation). Consider including a pseudocode or algorithm box.
