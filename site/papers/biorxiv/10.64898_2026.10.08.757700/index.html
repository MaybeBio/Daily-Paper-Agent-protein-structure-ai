Predicted structure of the 89-kDa invasion-tip protein of Holospora obtusa, an endonuclear symbiont of Paramecium caudatum, suggests a pH-dependent conformational change

Yasuhiro Takenaka; Toshiyuki Murai; Toru Nakatsu; Yasushi Shigeri; Masahiro Fujishima
bioRxiv 预印本 · 2026-10-10 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文用AlphaFold2预测和分子动力学模拟研究霍洛孢菌89-kDa入侵尖蛋白的pH依赖性构象变化，属于蛋白质结构预测与构象采样计算研究，与课题高度相关。

摘要

The endonuclear symbiont Holospora obtusa invades the macronucleus of Paramecium caudatum using an infectious-form invasion-tip containing a major 89-kDa periplasmic protein implicated in escape from the host digestive vacuole and macronuclear invasion. Sequence-based analyses placed this protein within a Holospora-specific family and predicted an N-terminal membrane-anchoring module and a long alpha-helical C-terminal region. AlphaFold2 modeling followed by molecular dynamics indicated that the C-terminal region forms an antiparallel, spectrin-repeat-like helical solenoid containing a localized three-helix coiled-coil. The protein exhibits a large pH-dependent charge difference, becoming 55 units more positive at pH 4 than at pH 7. Independent simulations at these pH values differed mainly in the relative orientation of the N-terminal core and C-terminal rod, described by DynDom as an approximately 35 degrees rigid-body rotation about a hinge centered on Gly291. However, none of the nearby glutamates (Glu273, Glu275, and Glu302) formed a pH-dependent salt bridge, and no specific protonation-dependent interaction responsible for the conformational difference was identified. We nonetheless propose, as a testable hypothesis, that acidification of the digestive vacuole may promote this reorientation, thereby repositioning the N-terminal membrane-anchoring module at the bacterial surface as an early step toward escape from the vacuole.

摘要翻译

核内共生菌 *Holospora obtusa* 通过一种感染型入侵尖端侵入草履虫 (*Paramecium caudatum*) 的大核，该尖端含有一个主要的89-kDa周质蛋白，该蛋白与逃逸宿主消化泡及大核入侵有关。基于序列的分析将该蛋白归入一个 *Holospora* 特异性家族，并预测其具有N端膜锚定模块和长α螺旋C端区域。AlphaFold2建模结合分子动力学 (molecular dynamics) 表明，C端区域形成反向平行的、类血影蛋白重复的螺旋螺线管，其中包含一个局部的三螺旋卷曲螺旋。该蛋白表现出显著的pH依赖性电荷差异，在pH 4时比pH 7时正电荷多55个单位。在这些pH值下的独立模拟主要差异在于N端核心与C端杆状区域的相对取向，DynDom将其描述为围绕Gly291铰链的约35度刚体旋转。然而，附近的谷氨酸残基（Glu273、Glu275和Glu302）均未形成pH依赖性盐桥，也未鉴定出导致构象差异的特异性质子化依赖相互作用。尽管如此，我们提出一个可检验的假说：消化泡的酸化可能促进这种重取向，从而将N端膜锚定模块重新定位至细菌表面，作为逃逸泡体的早期步骤。

02 一句话总结

本文用 AlphaFold2 预测 Holospora obtusa 89-kDa invasion-tip 蛋白结构，结合不同 pH 下的 MD 模拟，发现其 C 端形成 spectrin-repeat 样螺旋 solenoid，且 pH 4 与 pH 7 下 N 端核心与 C 端杆状域相对取向存在约 35° 刚体旋转（铰链位于 Gly291），但未鉴定到具体 pH 敏感残基对，仅提出酸化促进构象重排作为可检验假说。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：Holospora obtusa 的 89-kDa invasion-tip 蛋白（Hob89）如何从宿主消化泡逃逸并介导大核入侵？其结构是否具有 pH 依赖性构象变化？
  • 为什么重要：该蛋白是共生菌入侵机制的关键因子，理解其 pH 响应构象变化有助于阐明细菌如何感知并响应宿主细胞内环境（消化泡酸化），对宿主-病原/共生互作机制研究有普遍意义。
  • 现有方法为何不足：该蛋白无实验结构，序列同源性低，传统同源建模不可行；且其功能依赖环境 pH，静态结构预测不足以揭示构象变化机制。
  • 精确研究问题：Can AlphaFold2 预测的 H. obtusa 89-kDa invasion-tip 蛋白结构，结合不同 pH 下的 MD 模拟，揭示一个可重复的、由 pH 驱动的构象重排，并定位其结构基础？

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：用 AlphaFold2 预测全长结构，然后在两种 pH 条件（pH 4 与 pH 7）下分别进行 MD 模拟，比较构象差异，并用 DynDom 量化刚体运动。
  2. 核心洞察：该蛋白的 N 端核心与 C 端杆状域之间存在一个以 Gly291 为中心的铰链区，pH 变化导致两域相对取向改变约 35°，提示该蛋白可能作为 pH 传感器，通过构象重排将 N 端膜锚定模块重新定位到细菌表面。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：AF2 静态结构 + 环境条件 MD 模拟的组合，可以系统性地探索「环境依赖构象变化」，即使无法定位到具体残基对，也能提出可检验的功能假说。这对蛋白质构象采样研究具有方法学启示：结构预测的下一步不应止于静态模型，而应引入环境变量（pH、配体、温度等）进行动态采样。

  • 训练：AF2 为预训练模型，未进行微调；MD 为无训练物理模拟。

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围：仅覆盖计算预测与模拟，无 wet-lab 验证。
  • oracle/真值输入：AF2 预测结构作为唯一结构来源，无实验结构交叉验证。
  • 端到端状态：非端到端——从序列到功能假说链条中，结构预测与 MD 模拟为中间步骤，功能结论仅为推测。
  • 算力成本：未提供。
  • 历史数据依赖：依赖 AF2 训练数据中的同源 fold 信息；Holospora-specific 家族可能缺乏直接同源结构。
  • 模型依赖：结果高度依赖 AF2 预测准确性及 MD 力场/采样充分性。
  • 最难情形：pH 敏感残基未鉴定，构象变化的分子驱动机制不明。
  • 群体/领域边界：结论仅针对 H. obtusa 该单一蛋白，不可推广至其他 invasion-tip 蛋白或共生菌。
  • 不确定性：作者未提供 MD 模拟的收敛性分析、重复次数或统计误差估计。
  • 有边界的复述：在 AF2 预测结构与有限 MD 模拟条件下，该蛋白在 pH 4 与 pH 7 下表现出约 35° 的域间刚体旋转，但具体 pH 敏感残基未鉴定；酸化驱动构象重排仅为待检验假说，未经实验证实。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a plausible computational hypothesis linking pH change to conformational reorientation in a symbiont invasion protein. However, the evidence base is limited to sequence analysis and two simulation conditions, and the authors explicitly state that no specific pH-dependent interaction was identified. The proposed mechanism therefore remains speculative. The work may interest researchers in symbiosis, microbial invasion, and computational structural biology, but the current abstract does not establish the functional relevance of the observed conformational difference.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers studying intracellular symbiosis, particularly the molecular mechanisms of host invasion by bacterial endosymbionts, will find the structural characterization of a novel protein family relevant. Computational biologists interested in pH-dependent conformational dynamics and AlphaFold2-based structure prediction may also find the methodological approach of interest.
  • Major strengths The study applies state-of-the-art computational methods (AlphaFold2, molecular dynamics, DynDom) to a biologically important but understudied system. The authors are appropriately cautious in framing the pH-dependent conformational change as a testable hypothesis rather than a demonstrated mechanism. The explicit reporting of the absence of a specific protonation-dependent interaction is scientifically honest.

  • Blocking Yes

The simulations show a pH-dependent charge difference and a rigid-body rotation, but the authors state that no specific protonation-dependent interaction was identified. Without a mechanistic link or experimental validation, the connection between the computational observation and the proposed biological role is unsupported.
