8

Predicted and experimental protein-ligand coordinates with distance labels and evaluation splits

Klamt T
bioRxiv 预印本 · 2026-09-09 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文构建了PLI-Parallax数据集，提供预测与实验的蛋白质-配体坐标及校准的置信度标签，并设计了评估拆分，直接服务于蛋白质-配体结构预测的可靠性与泛化性评估，与本课题的蛋白质结构计算建模高度相关。

摘要

Structure prediction tools now provide protein–ligand geometry for pairs no laboratory experiment has resolved thus far. This data is typically provided without calibrated, practically usable per-system measures of whether a given complex is correct and how much to trust it. Agreement between independently constructed methods is an established signal for this type of problem. What has been missing is a way to derive what a given level of agreement is worth. We present PLI-Parallax, which deposits predicted geometry together with experimental data needed to calibrate it. Chai-1, two Boltz-2 configurations, and the docking engine smina were run over shared inputs across an experimentally resolved crystal tier of 19,350 complexes and a corpus tier of 31,746 cross-docked pairs without experimental ground truth on predicted receptors, yielding 307,314,646 residue-to-ligand-atom distance records, which the stored coordinates allow a consumer to recompute at a cutoff of their own choosing. Their mutual agreement is fitted against observed accuracy where experimental data permits it and carried to where it does not. This way 30,567 systems carry predicted label accuracy together with a split-conformal interval. On the crystal tier that interval covers observed accuracy at the stated rate. On the corpus tier it ranks systems by expected label quality, since both distributions differ. The deposit is accompanied by 646 evaluation configurations across seven data split families, and 631 of them report how far their training and test entities separate under a two-sample test. The 906 protein accessions were partitioned to control sequence leakage, so a protein-cold split here tests generalisation across sequence space. The two tiers support evaluation against experimental data and, where this is absent, supervision weighted by how far the configurations agree.

摘要翻译

结构预测工具现已能提供实验室实验尚未解析的蛋白质-配体几何结构。这些数据通常缺乏经过校准、实际可用的系统级度量，以判断给定复合物是否正确及其可信程度。独立构建的方法之间的一致性是该类问题的一个既定信号。缺失的是一种推导特定一致性水平价值的方法。我们提出PLI-Parallax，它将预测几何结构与校准所需的实验数据一同存储。Chai-1、两种Boltz-2配置以及对接引擎smina在共享输入上运行，覆盖了包含19,350个复合物的实验解析晶体层（crystal tier）和包含31,746个交叉对接对（cross-docked pairs）的语料层（corpus tier），后者在预测受体上缺乏实验真值，共产生307,314,646条残基-配体原子距离记录，存储的坐标允许用户自行选择截断值重新计算。它们之间的一致性在实验数据允许的情况下与观测准确率进行拟合，并推广至无实验数据的区域。通过这种方式，30,567个系统携带预测标签准确率及分裂共形区间（split-conformal interval）。在晶体层上，该区间以所述比率覆盖观测准确率。在语料层上，它根据预期标签质量对系统进行排序，因为两者的分布不同。该存储伴随有跨越七个数据分裂族（data split families）的646个评估配置，其中631个报告了其训练集与测试集在双样本检验（two-sample test）下的分离程度。906个蛋白质登录号（protein accessions）被分区以控制序列泄漏（sequence leakage），因此此处蛋白质冷分裂（protein-cold split）测试的是跨序列空间的泛化能力。这两个层支持基于实验数据的评估，以及在缺乏实验数据时，根据配置间一致性程度加权的监督。

02 一句话总结

为解决预测蛋白质-配体结构缺乏可用可信度度量的问题，本文构建了 PLI-Parallax 数据集（含 19,350 个晶体复合物和 31,746 个 cross-docked 对），用 Chai-1、两种 Boltz-2 配置和 smina 生成 3.07 亿条残基-配体原子距离记录，通过多方法一致性拟合实验精度，并用 split-conformal 区间为 30,567 个系统提供校准后的标签精度估计，在晶体层达到标称覆盖率。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：当结构预测工具给出蛋白质-配体几何结构时，消费者如何知道该结构是否正确、可信度有多高？现有输出缺乏逐系统的、校准过的、可实际使用的不确定性度量。
  • 为什么重要：随着 AlphaFold 类工具扩展到蛋白质-配体复合物，大量无实验验证的预测结构被用于药物筛选、机制研究等下游任务。若无可信度度量，下游决策无法区分高置信与低置信预测。
  • 现有方法为何不足：单一模型的自置信度（如 pLDDT）往往校准不良；实验验证成本高且无法覆盖所有预测；缺乏将多方法一致性转化为绝对精度度量的框架。
  • 精确研究问题：Can we derive a calibrated, per-system measure of predicted protein-ligand complex accuracy from the agreement between independently constructed prediction methods, and validate it against experimental data where available?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：构建大规模数据集，用多个独立预测工具（Chai-1、两种 Boltz-2、smina）对共享输入生成结构，计算残基-配体原子距离标签；在实验数据可得的晶体层拟合多方法一致性与观测精度之间的关系；用 split-conformal 方法生成校准区间；将校准关系外推到无实验数据的 corpus 层；提供 646 个评估配置以控制序列泄漏。

  2. 核心洞察：多方法一致性本身不是精度，但一致性-精度关系是可标定的——在实验数据存在的子集上拟合这一关系，然后将其作为函数外推到无实验数据的系统。这本质上是将「共识」从定性信号转化为定量、校准的精度估计。Split-conformal 保证了在标定分布上覆盖率的统计保证。

  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：对于任何「预测无真值」的结构生物学任务（如构象采样、对接打分），多方法一致性 + 实验子集标定 + 外推是一个可迁移的框架。它不要求新模型，而是为已有模型输出附加可信度层。此外，序列泄漏控制（按蛋白质 accession 划分）应成为所有结构预测基准的默认设计。

07 方法总览

  • 输入：蛋白质序列/结构、配体分子（来自 PDB 晶体层 19,350 复合物 + corpus 层 31,746 cross-docked 对）
  • 输出：307,314,646 条残基-配体原子距离记录；30,567 个系统的预测标签精度 + split-conformal 区间；646 个评估配置
  • 模块： 1. 数据收集与分层（晶体层 + corpus 层） 2. 多工具预测（Chai-1、Boltz-2×2、smina） 3. 距离标签计算（残基-配体原子距离） 4. 一致性-精度拟合（实验数据可得处） 5. Split-conformal 区间生成 6. 评估划分生成（7 个划分族，646 配置）
  • 训练：无传统训练；一致性-精度关系拟合为监督回归

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围：仅覆盖蛋白质-配体复合物的距离标签精度评估，不涉及结合亲和力、构象采样质量或动力学性质。
  • Oracle/真值输入：晶体层使用实验解析结构作为真值；corpus 层无真值，依赖一致性代理。
  • 端到端状态：非端到端预测系统，而是评估层——为已有预测工具的输出附加可信度。
  • 算力成本：未提供（4 个工具 × 5 万+ 复合物的计算成本未在摘要中说明）。
  • 历史数据依赖：依赖 PDB 中已有实验结构；corpus 层依赖预测受体结构。
  • 模型依赖：结论依赖 Chai-1、Boltz-2、smina 的具体实现；更换工具可能改变一致性-精度映射。
  • 最难情形：corpus 层（无真值）的校准外推是最大不确定性来源；cross-docked 对的受体为预测结构，误差可能累积。
  • 群体/领域边界：仅覆盖晶体解析的复合物类型；膜蛋白、无序蛋白、共价配体等可能不适用。
  • 不确定性：Split-conformal 区间仅保证覆盖率，不保证逐点精度；corpus 层区间含义与晶体层不同。
  • 有边界的复述：在晶体复合物分布上，多方法一致性经标定后可提供覆盖率达标的精度区间；在无真值分布上，一致性可排序系统质量，但绝对精度不可验证。

14 学到什么

Agent 提炼的知识候选：

  1. 一致性-标定-外推框架：对于蛋白质结构预测、构象采样等无真值任务，可构建「多方法一致性 → 实验子集标定 → 外推」的三步框架，为每个预测附加校准后的可信度。可迁移到 MD 模拟的构象系综评估（如多力场一致性标定实验构象）。

  2. Split-conformal 用于结构标签：将 conformal prediction 从分类/回归扩展到结构距离标签，为每个残基-配体原子距离提供覆盖率保证的区间。可迁移到 AlphaFold 输出中 pLDDT 的校准替代。

  3. 双层数据设计（有真值+无真值）：同时提供实验验证层和预测层，使标定与外推在同一数据生态内完成。可迁移到分子对接基准设计（如 DUD-E 有真值 + 大规模虚拟筛选无真值）。

  4. 序列泄漏控制的评估划分：按蛋白质 accession 而非复合物划分训练/测试，是评估泛化能力的必要设计。可迁移到所有蛋白质-配体预测基准。

  5. 可重计算标签存储：存储坐标而非预计算标签，允许消费者按自定义截断重算，增加数据复用灵活性。可迁移到构象数据集设计。

  6. 双样本检验用于划分质量：用两样本检验报告训练/测试实体分离度，为划分质量提供量化指标。可迁移到任何数据划分的验证。

Agent 生成的研究候选：

  1. 名称：多力场一致性标定 MD 构象可信度 - 来源局限/观察：本文用多工具一致性标定静态结构精度；MD 模拟的构象系综缺乏类似可信度度量。 - 核心假设：多个独立力场（如 AMBER、CHARMM、GROMOS）对同一体系的构象采样一致性，经实验构象标定后，可预测无实验体系的结构可信度。 - 初步方法：对已知晶体结构的蛋白-配体体系运行多力场 MD，计算构象系综间距离分布的一致性；拟合一致性-实验偏差关系；外推到无晶体结构体系。 - 验证方式：在留出集上比较一致性标定与单力场置信度的预测误差。 - 创新状态：unverified

  2. 名称：AlphaFold 输出与对接引擎的交叉一致性作为亲和力代理 - 来源局限/观察：本文用 Chai-1/Boltz-2 与 smina 的一致性标定结构精度；未涉及亲和力预测。 - 核心假设：AlphaFold 预测的配体结合姿态与 smina 对接姿态的一致性，经实验亲和力标定后，可预测无实验体系的相对亲和力排序。 - 初步方法：对 PDBbind 体系计算 AlphaFold 姿态与 smina 姿态的 RMSD 一致性；拟合一致性-实验 Ki/Kd 关系；外推到虚拟筛选库。 - 验证方式：在 DUD-E 或 LIT-PCBA 上比较一致性排序与 docking score 排序的富集因子。 - 创新状态：unverified

  3. 名称：序列-配体双泄漏控制的评估划分生成器 - 来源局限/观察：本文仅控制蛋白质序列泄漏（906 accession），未提及配体相似性控制。 - 核心假设：同时控制蛋白质序列和配体骨架相似性的划分，能更准确评估泛化能力。 - 初步方法：基于 MMseqs2 序列聚类 + 配体 ECFP 相似性聚类，生成联合划分；用双样本检验量化分离度。 - 验证方式：在多个预测工具上比较联合划分与单维划分的泛化差距。 - 创新状态：unverified

  4. 名称：Split-conformal 区间在构象采样中的覆盖率验证 - 来源局限/观察：本文在晶体层验证了 conformal 覆盖率；未涉及构象系综的覆盖率。 - 核心假设：对 MD 或生成模型（如扩散模型）采样的构象系综，split-conformal 区间能覆盖实验构象的指定比例。 - 初步方法：对已知晶体结构体系运行构象采样，计算系综到实验构象的距离分布；用 split-conformal 生成区间并验证覆盖率。 - 验证方式：在不同蛋白家族和采样方法上比较覆盖率稳定性。 - 创新状态：unverified

  5. 名称：预测结构可信度标签用于虚拟筛选的加权打分 - 来源局限/观察：本文提供 30,567 个系统的可信度标签，但未展示下游应用。 - 核心假设：用可信度标签加权 docking score，可提高虚拟筛选的富集率（低可信度预测降权）。 - 初步方法：在 corpus 层对 smina 打分乘以可信度权重；在已知活性体系上比较加权与未加权的 AUC。 - 验证方式：在 DUD-E 子集上评估富集因子。 - 创新状态：unverified

Review setup

  • Input scope Abstract
  • Assessment boundary Claims and evidence presented in the abstract only
  • Shared manuscript claim summary The authors present PLI-Parallax, a deposited dataset of predicted and experimental protein-ligand coordinates with distance labels, designed to provide calibrated per-system confidence measures for predicted complexes. The dataset includes 307 million distance records across two tiers (crystal and corpus), uses inter-method agreement to predict label accuracy, and provides split-conformal intervals. The deposit also includes 646 evaluation configurations across seven data split families, with 631 reporting two-sample test separation, and 906 protein accessions partitioned to control sequence leakage.
  • Visible evidence base Abstract text only; no figures, tables, or supplementary materials provided
  • Missing materials affecting confidence Full manuscript, figures, tables, supplementary data, code repository, dataset access details, and any experimental validation results

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract describes a potentially valuable resource for the structural biology and computational drug discovery communities. The core idea—using inter-method agreement to calibrate confidence in predicted protein-ligand geometries—is conceptually sound and addresses a recognized gap. However, the abstract is dense and lacks sufficient detail to evaluate the technical validity, statistical rigor, and practical utility of the proposed approach. Key claims about calibration, coverage, and generalisation are stated without supporting evidence or methodological justification. The assessment is therefore provisional, pending full manuscript review.

  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Computational structural biologists, drug discovery researchers, and developers of protein-ligand prediction methods. The dataset could serve as a benchmark for evaluating prediction tools and as a resource for training confidence models. The split-conformal intervals and evaluation splits are of direct interest to practitioners needing reliable uncertainty estimates for predicted complexes.

  • Major strengths 1. Addresses a clear and important gap: the lack of calibrated, per-system confidence measures for predicted protein-ligand geometries. 2. Large-scale dataset (307 million distance records) with both experimental and predicted data, enabling calibration and evaluation. 3. Use of inter-method agreement as a signal for accuracy is a well-motivated approach. 4. Inclusion of multiple evaluation splits (including protein-cold) and two-sample tests for leakage control is methodologically rigorous.

  • Major Concerns

    • Concern ID R1-M1
    • Severity Major
    • Blocking Yes
    • Axis Statistical validity / calibration
    • Claim pointer "On the crystal tier that interval covers observed accuracy at the stated rate."
    • Evidence pointer Abstract only; no figure or table provided
    • Concern The abstract claims that the split-conformal interval achieves nominal coverage on the crystal tier, but provides no quantitative results (e.g., coverage rate, interval width, calibration plot). Without these, the claim is unverifiable.
    • Why it matters Calibration is the central claim of the work. If the intervals do not achieve the stated coverage, the entire premise of providing "practically usable per-system measures" is undermined.

    • Resolution test Provide a calibration plot (e.g., empirical coverage vs. nominal level) for the crystal tier, along with interval width statistics. Report the exact coverage rate achieved at the stated nominal level.

    • Concern ID R1-M2

    • Severity Major
    • Blocking Yes
    • Axis Generalisation / transferability
    • Claim pointer "On the corpus tier it ranks systems by expected label quality, since both distributions differ."
    • Evidence pointer Abstract only
    • Concern The abstract states that the interval ranks systems on the corpus tier, but does not explain how this ranking is validated or what metric is used to assess ranking quality. The claim that "both distributions differ" is vague and does not justify the transfer of calibration from the crystal to the corpus tier.
    • Why it matters The corpus tier is where the method is most needed (no ground truth). Without a validation strategy for ranking quality, the practical utility of the intervals on this tier is unclear.

    • Resolution test Describe the validation approach for ranking on the corpus tier (e.g., using held-out experimental data, synthetic benchmarks, or cross-validation). Report a ranking metric (e.g., Spearman correlation, AUC for identifying correct vs. incorrect complexes).

    • Concern ID R1-M3

    • Severity Major
    • Blocking No
    • Axis Reproducibility / data access
    • Claim pointer "The deposit is accompanied by 646 evaluation configurations across seven data split families, and 631 of them report how far their training and test entities separate under a two-sample test."
    • Evidence pointer Abstract only
    • Concern The abstract does not specify what the 15 missing configurations are, why they are missing, or whether this affects the completeness of the evaluation. The two-sample test used is not named, and the threshold for "separation" is not defined.
    • Why it matters Reproducibility and completeness of the evaluation are critical for a resource paper. Missing configurations and undefined metrics reduce confidence in the reported results.
    • Resolution test List the 15 missing configurations and explain their absence. Name the two-sample test (e.g., Kolmogorov-Smirnov, Wasserstein distance) and state the threshold used to define separation.
