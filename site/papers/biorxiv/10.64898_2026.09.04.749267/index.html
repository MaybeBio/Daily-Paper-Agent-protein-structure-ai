De novo Rubisco design with protein language models

Alexander J. Kehl; Simon K. S. Chu; Jose Henrique Pereira; Jennifer Lee; Renee Z. Wang; Michael Gigl; Paul D. Adams; Patrick M. Shih; Justin B. Siegel
bioRxiv 预印本 · 2026-09-09 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文利用蛋白质语言模型（ProGen-2和ESM-2）从头设计Rubisco酶，生成大量新序列并验证了活性与结构，核心贡献在蛋白质结构相关的序列设计与结构预测，方法为AI深度学习，与本课题高度相关。

摘要

Ribulose-1,5-bisphosphate carboxylase/oxygenase (‘Rubisco’) fixes the majority of carbon dioxide globally but is challenged with low specificity for CO 2 versus O 2 and low catalytic efficiencies. Traditional engineering efforts have remained difficult because folding, assembly, specificity, and catalysis are tightly coupled, hampering efforts to explore sequence space. Therefore, we leveraged recent advances in protein large language models (PLMs) to generate sequences beyond those observed in nature, using both ProGen-2 that was fine-tuned on a limited dataset of non-Form I Rubiscos and an ESM-2 discriminator. With this approach, we generated 5.6 million novel Rubisco-like sequences and identified 21 highly diverse candidates predicted to be active that occupy regions of Rubisco phylogenetic space not previously observed in nature. Six designs were soluble in Escherichia coli , and five were shown to produce quantifiable 3PGA. One design produced an apparent CO 2 /O 2 specificity estimate beyond the range of the natural representative Rubiscos assayed. We also solved the crystal structure of one de novo design that reproduced the predicted dimer and active-site geometry with sub-angstrom Cα agreement. Sequence-only generation followed by independent structural filtering therefore recovered soluble, active Rubiscos from regions of sequence space that are not represented in genomic databases. Together, these results establish a scalable strategy for accessing previously unexplored Rubisco sequence space, providing a broadly accessible path toward generating de novo Rubiscos that have activity and specificity parameters needed to address longstanding limitations in biological carbon fixation.

摘要翻译

核酮糖-1,5-二磷酸羧化酶/加氧酶（'Rubisco'）固定了全球大部分的二氧化碳，但面临对CO₂与O₂的低特异性以及低催化效率的挑战。传统的工程改造尝试一直困难重重，因为折叠、组装、特异性和催化过程紧密耦合，阻碍了对序列空间的探索。因此，我们利用蛋白质大语言模型（PLMs）的最新进展，生成了超越自然界中观察到的序列，使用了在非Form I型Rubisco的有限数据集上微调的ProGen-2以及一个ESM-2判别器。通过这种方法，我们生成了560万个新型Rubisco样序列，并识别出21个预测具有活性的高度多样化候选序列，这些序列占据了Rubisco系统发育空间中此前在自然界中未观察到的区域。其中6个设计在大肠杆菌（Escherichia coli）中可溶，5个被证明能产生可量化的3PGA。一个设计产生的表观CO₂/O₂特异性估计值超出了所测天然代表性Rubisco的范围。我们还解析了一个从头设计的晶体结构，该结构以亚埃级Cα一致性再现了预测的二聚体和活性位点几何构型。因此，仅通过序列生成再经独立结构过滤，便从基因组数据库中未代表的序列空间区域恢复了可溶、有活性的Rubisco。这些结果共同建立了一种可扩展的策略，用于探索此前未涉足的Rubisco序列空间，为生成具有解决生物碳固定中长期限制所需活性和特异性参数的从头Rubisco提供了一条广泛可行的路径。

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法: 使用微调的 ProGen-2 生成 Rubisco 序列，再用 ESM-2 判别器进行结构过滤，筛选出候选序列进行实验验证。
  2. 核心洞察: 序列生成（PLM）与独立的结构过滤（ESM-2）结合，可以绕过传统工程中折叠-组装-活性的耦合，直接从序列空间探索非天然区域，并恢复活性。
  3. [Analysis] 可能的普适教训: 对于复杂功能酶，序列生成模型（如 PLM）可以生成大量候选，但需结合结构或功能判别器（如 ESM-2 或 AlphaFold）进行预筛选，以降低实验成本。此范式可迁移到其他酶设计任务。

07 方法总览

  • 输入: 非 Form I Rubisco 序列数据集（用于微调 ProGen-2）

  • 训练: ProGen-2 微调于非 Form I Rubisco 数据集（未提供具体规模）；ESM-2 判别器未微调（预训练模型）。

  • 反馈回路: 无（序列生成和过滤是单向的，实验验证后未迭代）
  • 假设: 序列生成模型能捕获 Rubisco 的进化约束；ESM-2 能区分可折叠序列。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a potentially transformative approach to Rubisco engineering using protein language models. The concept of generating de novo Rubisco sequences that are soluble, active, and structurally validated is highly novel and could address a long-standing challenge in synthetic biology. However, the abstract alone provides insufficient detail to evaluate the robustness of the computational pipeline, the rigor of the experimental validation, and the validity of the specificity claim. Critical methodological and quantitative details are absent, preventing a full assessment of the work's technical soundness and reproducibility.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers in synthetic biology, protein engineering, carbon fixation, and computational protein design would be interested. The work offers a scalable strategy for exploring sequence space beyond natural evolution, which could have implications for improving Rubisco and other recalcitrant enzymes. The use of protein language models for de novo design of a complex, multi-subunit enzyme is a significant methodological advance.
  • Major strengths 1. The use of protein language models to generate sequences beyond natural diversity for a notoriously difficult enzyme is a conceptually novel and ambitious approach. 2. The combination of computational generation with experimental validation (solubility, activity, and crystal structure) provides a multi-pronged evidence base. 3. The claim of achieving a specificity estimate beyond natural Rubiscos, if validated, would represent a major breakthrough.
