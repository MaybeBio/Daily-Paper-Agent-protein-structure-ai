Beyond Structure: Protein and Solution Dynamics Shape Ligand-Binding Thermodynamics

Kacie A. Evans; Morgan Powers; Robert L. Rider; Carter Lantz; Arthur Laganowsky; Hays S. Rye; David H. Russell
bioRxiv 预印本 · 2026-09-30 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文用变温电喷雾质谱研究GroEL配体结合热力学，虽涉及蛋白构象动态，但核心是实验质谱方法，未采用AI或物理模拟计算建模，与蛋白质结构计算课题关联较弱。

摘要

Variable-temperature electrospray ionization native mass spectrometry (vT-ESI nMS) provides a means to determine how temperature-dependent changes in protein and solution dynamics influence individual ligand-binding reactions within multiligand protein complexes. Here, the GroEL single-ring mutant (SR1), which binds up to seven nucleotides, serves as a model system due to its sensitivity to solution conditions. Native MS resolves individual ligand-bound populations, while vT-ESI extends these measurements across temperature (3-43 °C), enabling determination of binding affinities and the associated Gibbs free energy, enthalpy, entropy, heat capacity changes (ΔC p ), and enthalpy-entropy compensation (EEC) for each binding reaction. Temperature-dependent changes in van’t Hoff curvature produce distinct ΔC p profiles, indicating changes in the dynamics governing ligand binding that may reflect contributions from protein conformational and protonation microstates. Temperature-dependent shifts in average charge state, consistent with changes in solvent-accessible surface area, indicate corresponding changes in protein conformation. Binding is predominantly enthalpy-driven below ∼23 °C, with increasing entropic contributions at higher temperatures, while the seventh ADP binding reaction exhibits a distinct EEC profile. Comparison of the seventh ADP binding reaction in H 2 O and D 2 O reveals pronounced differences in van’t Hoff curvature, ΔC p , and EEC, demonstrating that changes in the hydration environment alter the thermodynamic response. Collectively, these measurements show that temperature-dependent changes in protein and solution dynamics alter the distributions and thermodynamics of individual ligand-bound states and demonstrate the utility of vT-ESI nMS for resolving these effects in complex multiligand systems.

摘要翻译

变温电喷雾电离原生质谱（vT-ESI nMS）提供了一种手段，用于确定蛋白质和溶液动力学中温度依赖性变化如何影响多配体蛋白质复合物内的单个配体结合反应。在此，GroEL单环突变体（SR1）能结合多达七个核苷酸，因其对溶液条件的敏感性而作为模型系统。原生质谱解析单个配体结合群体，而vT-ESI将这些测量扩展到温度范围（3-43°C），从而能够确定每个结合反应的结合亲和力及相关的吉布斯自由能、焓、熵、热容变化（ΔC p ）和焓-熵补偿（EEC）。范特霍夫曲率中的温度依赖性变化产生不同的ΔC p 谱，表明调控配体结合的动力学变化，这可能反映蛋白质构象和质子化微态的贡献。平均电荷态的温度依赖性偏移与溶剂可及表面积的变化一致，指示蛋白质构象的相应变化。在约23°C以下，结合主要由焓驱动，在较高温度下熵贡献增加，而第七个ADP结合反应表现出独特的EEC谱。在H 2 O和D 2 O中比较第七个ADP结合反应，揭示了范特霍夫曲率、ΔC p 和EEC的显著差异，表明水合环境的变化改变了热力学响应。总体而言，这些测量表明，蛋白质和溶液动力学中的温度依赖性变化改变了单个配体结合状态的分布和热力学，并展示了vT-ESI nMS在解析复杂多配体系统中这些效应的实用性。
