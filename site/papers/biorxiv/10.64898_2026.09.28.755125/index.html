GalaxyMemMD: an end-to-end Galaxy toolset for reproducible membrane embedding, molecular dynamics simulation, and analysis of membrane proteins

joshi j; Raubenolt B; Katzenmeyer N; Cumbo F; Blankenberg D
bioRxiv 预印本 · 2026-10-04 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文开发了GalaxyMemMD，一个端到端可复现的膜蛋白嵌入、分子动力学模拟与分析工作流，整合AlphaFold结构输入和对接流程，虽为工程化工具但直接服务于蛋白质结构计算模拟。

摘要

Membrane proteins play crucial roles in cellular signaling, transport, and pharmacological regulation, yet their structural and functional characterization remains challenging due to experimental limitations. Molecular dynamics (MD) simulations have become indispensable for probing membrane protein behavior at atomic resolution; however, executing these simulations at scale remains a significant bottleneck. Existing tools excel at system preparation for individual cases, but none offer a complete, high-throughput pipeline spanning embedding, simulation, and analysis across multiple membrane compositions, and rarely integrate modern structure prediction and molecular docking frameworks. Here, we present GalaxyMemMD, a fully automated, open-source Galaxy framework that provides a direct, scalable alternative to existing preparation-only tools. GalaxyMemMD integrates every stage membrane embedding, system preparation, MD execution, and downstream analysis into a single reproducible pipeline. Leveraging Galaxy's HPC-ready job scheduling, GalaxyMemMD natively supports high-throughput parallel simulations across multiple membrane compositions and protein variants, including AlphaFold-derived structural inputs and downstream molecular docking workflows, without repeated manual setup. We validate GalaxyMemMD across six structurally diverse membrane proteins four GPCRs, one ion channel, and one beta-barrel demonstrating consistent system preparation, while the benchmark analysis shows stable embeddings, and reproducible trajectory-level production workflow. Across these benchmarks, GalaxyMemMD matches the setup quality of established tools, therefore serves as both a practical alternative and a capability extension for the membrane protein simulation community.

摘要翻译

膜蛋白在细胞信号传导、运输和药理学调控中发挥关键作用，然而由于实验限制，其结构和功能表征仍具挑战性。分子动力学 (molecular dynamics, MD) 模拟已成为在原子分辨率下探究膜蛋白行为的不可或缺工具；然而，大规模执行这些模拟仍是一个显著瓶颈。现有工具在个别案例的系统准备方面表现出色，但没有任何工具提供涵盖嵌入、模拟和分析的完整高通量流程，适用于多种膜组成，且很少整合现代结构预测和分子对接框架。在此，我们提出GalaxyMemMD，一个全自动、开源的Galaxy框架，为现有仅准备工具提供直接、可扩展的替代方案。GalaxyMemMD将膜嵌入、系统准备、MD执行和下游分析等每个阶段整合到单一可复现流程中。利用Galaxy的HPC就绪作业调度，GalaxyMemMD原生支持跨多种膜组成和蛋白变体的高通量并行模拟，包括AlphaFold衍生的结构输入和下游分子对接工作流，无需重复手动设置。我们通过六个结构多样的膜蛋白（四个GPCR、一个离子通道和一个β-桶）验证了GalaxyMemMD，展示了一致的系统准备，而基准分析显示稳定的嵌入和可复现的轨迹级生产流程。在这些基准测试中，GalaxyMemMD匹配了既有工具的设置质量，因此可作为膜蛋白模拟社区的实际替代方案和能力扩展。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：膜蛋白 MD 模拟的完整流程（嵌入 → 模拟 → 分析）缺乏端到端、可扩展、可重复的自动化工具；现有工具多为「仅系统制备」或针对单一膜组成，且很少整合 AlphaFold 结构输入与下游分子对接。
  • 为什么重要：膜蛋白是药物靶点与信号传导关键分子，MD 模拟是研究其动态行为的主要计算手段；但流程碎片化导致低通量、低可重复性，阻碍规模化应用。
  • 现有方法为何不足：现有工具（如 CHARMM-GUI、PACKMOL-Memgen）擅长单案例系统构建，但不覆盖模拟执行与下游分析；不支持多膜组成并行；未原生整合 AlphaFold 预测结构与对接工作流。
  • 精确研究问题：Can a single Galaxy-based pipeline provide reproducible, high-throughput membrane embedding, MD simulation, and analysis across multiple membrane compositions and protein variants, with setup quality comparable to established tools?

06 核心思想

  1. 表面方法：构建一个 Galaxy 工具集，将膜嵌入、系统制备、MD 执行、轨迹分析封装为模块化步骤，通过 Galaxy 的作业调度实现并行与可重复。
  2. 核心洞察：膜蛋白 MD 的瓶颈不在单步算法精度，而在流程工程——将已有成熟工具（嵌入、MD 引擎）以标准化方式串联，并利用平台级调度（Galaxy HPC）实现规模化，即可在不牺牲质量的前提下大幅提升吞吐与可重复性。
  3. 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：在结构计算领域，当单步方法已成熟时，工作流集成本身即是创新——尤其对「结构预测 → 模拟 → 对接」这类多步串联任务，平台化封装可显著降低使用门槛并提升可重复性，这一思路可迁移到其他蛋白体系（如可溶性蛋白的 MD-对接串联）。

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围：仅覆盖膜蛋白的 MD 模拟流程（嵌入→模拟→分析），不涉及新力场、新采样算法或新嵌入方法。
  • oracle/真值输入：依赖用户提供的蛋白结构（实验或 AlphaFold），工具本身不验证结构正确性。
  • 端到端状态：端到端指流程自动化，而非端到端预测精度——MD 引擎与嵌入工具的物理精度继承自底层软件。
  • 算力成本：未提供；实际成本取决于 Galaxy 集群资源与模拟规模。
  • 历史数据依赖：无（非 ML 方法）。
  • 模型依赖：依赖底层 MD 引擎与嵌入工具的准确性；若底层工具对某脂质类型或蛋白拓扑失效，GalaxyMemMD 无法补偿。
  • 最难情形：β-桶蛋白（仅 1 个测试）与非常规脂质组成的嵌入可能更具挑战，但证据不足。
  • 群体/领域边界：结论仅适用于测试的 6 个蛋白与所选膜组成；不能外推至所有膜蛋白或复杂膜环境。
  • 不确定性：所有「一致」「稳定」「可重复」均未提供量化定义或统计检验，属于定性声明。
  • 有边界的复述：GalaxyMemMD 在 6 个测试蛋白上实现了与既有工具相当的制备质量，并提供了可重复的端到端流程；其优势在于工程集成与高通量，而非物理精度提升。

  1. 候选名称：AlphaFold 结构质量感知的膜嵌入参数自动选择 - 来源局限/观察：GalaxyMemMD 接受 AlphaFold 输入但未利用 pLDDT 信息；低置信度区域可能在嵌入中产生人为张力。 - 核心假设：根据 pLDDT 分数调整嵌入位置或约束，可减少模拟初始构象的伪影。 - 初步方法：在 GalaxyMemMD 嵌入模块中增加 pLDDT 加权的位置约束；对比固定嵌入与加权嵌入的 RMSD 与构象分布。 - 验证方式：在 6 个基准蛋白上比较模拟平衡时间与构象采样效率。 - 创新状态：unverified。

  2. 候选名称：多膜组成并行模拟的构象集合对接筛选 - 来源局限/观察：GalaxyMemMD 支持多膜并行，但未说明如何利用多膜结果进行下游对接。 - 核心假设：不同膜组成下采样的构象集合，比单一膜条件更能代表蛋白的功能构象空间，提升对接富集率。 - 初步方法：对同一蛋白在 3 种膜组成下模拟，提取构象集合，分别对接已知配体，比较富集因子。 - 验证方式：使用 DUD-E 或社区基准集，对比单膜 vs 多膜构象集合的对接性能。 - 创新状态：unverified。

  3. 候选名称：Galaxy 工作流中的 MD-对接闭环迭代 - 来源局限/观察：GalaxyMemMD 将对接作为下游终点，未实现「对接结果 → 重新模拟」的闭环。 - 核心假设：对接命中物在 MD 中的稳定性可反馈筛选，形成「模拟-对接-再模拟」迭代，提高命中率。 - 初步方法：在 Galaxy 中构建循环工作流：MD 采样 → 对接 → 对 top 命中物再模拟 → 按结合自由能排序。 - 验证方式：在已知体系上比较单轮 vs 迭代流程的命中率。 - 创新状态：unverified。

  4. 候选名称：膜蛋白 β-桶嵌入的专用参数优化 - 来源局限/观察：基准测试仅含 1 个 β-桶，可能掩盖该拓扑的嵌入难点（如桶轴取向、环区位置）。 - 核心假设：β-桶嵌入需要针对桶轴与膜法线夹角进行特殊处理，通用嵌入参数可能产生非物理构象。 - 初步方法：系统扫描 β-桶嵌入角度与脂质组成，对比桶倾斜角分布与实验值。 - 验证方式：使用已知 β-桶结构（如 OmpF）进行嵌入，比较模拟与晶体结构的桶倾斜角。 - 创新状态：unverified。

审稿人评审

Review setup

  • Input scope Abstract only
  • Assessment boundary Claims and evidence presented in the abstract; no full text, figures, tables, or supplementary materials were provided
  • Shared manuscript claim summary The authors present GalaxyMemMD, an open-source Galaxy framework that integrates membrane embedding, system preparation, MD execution, and downstream analysis into a single reproducible pipeline. They claim high-throughput parallel simulation support across multiple membrane compositions and protein variants, compatibility with AlphaFold-derived structures and molecular docking workflows, and validation across six membrane proteins demonstrating consistent system preparation, stable embeddings, and reproducible trajectory-level production.
  • Visible evidence base Abstract text only; no quantitative results, benchmark details, or methodological descriptions are available
  • Missing materials affecting confidence Full manuscript, all figures and tables, benchmark protocols, software documentation, code repository, and any statistical analyses

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract describes a potentially valuable software contribution that addresses a real gap in membrane protein MD simulation workflows. The integration of embedding, simulation, and analysis within a Galaxy framework is a sensible and potentially impactful approach. However, the abstract provides insufficient technical detail and quantitative evidence to evaluate the core claims of reproducibility, scalability, and equivalence to established tools. The validation across six proteins is mentioned but not substantiated with any metrics or comparisons. The manuscript cannot be assessed for technical soundness from the supplied material.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Computational biophysicists and structural biologists who use MD simulations to study membrane proteins would be the primary audience. Researchers seeking accessible, reproducible, and scalable simulation pipelines, particularly those without extensive HPC expertise, would benefit. The Galaxy community and developers of bioinformatics workflows may also find the integration approach of interest. The potential to incorporate AlphaFold structures and docking workflows broadens appeal to the structural biology community.
  • Major strengths The proposed tool addresses a clear and important bottleneck in membrane protein simulation, namely the lack of a complete end-to-end pipeline. The use of Galaxy provides a well-established framework for reproducibility and accessibility. The claimed support for high-throughput parallel simulations across multiple membrane compositions and protein variants is a meaningful capability extension beyond existing preparation-only tools. The integration of modern structure prediction and docking frameworks is forward-looking.
  • Major Concerns
  • Concern ID R1-M1
  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Evidence sufficiency
  • Claim pointer The authors claim that GalaxyMemMD "matches the setup quality of established tools" and demonstrates "consistent system preparation, stable embeddings, and reproducible trajectory-level production workflow" across six membrane proteins.
  • Evidence pointer Abstract only; location not provided
  • Concern The abstract states that validation was performed across six proteins and that benchmark analysis shows certain outcomes, but no quantitative data, comparison metrics, or statistical analyses are presented. The claim of matching established tools is made without any comparative results.
  • Why it matters The central value proposition of this work is that GalaxyMemMD is a practical alternative to existing tools. Without quantitative evidence of equivalence or superiority, this claim is unsupported. Similarly, the reproducibility and stability claims require specific metrics such as RMSD, RMSF, lipid bilayer properties, or system preparation validation data.
  • Resolution test Provide benchmark results comparing GalaxyMemMD output to established tools for the same systems, including quantitative metrics for system quality, simulation stability, and reproducibility across replicate runs.

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes

  • Evidence pointer Abstract only; location not provided
  • Concern No methodological details are provided regarding how the pipeline handles different membrane compositions, how parallelization is implemented, how reproducibility is ensured, or how the integration with AlphaFold and docking tools is achieved. Without details on architecture, dependencies, supported force fields, membrane builders, or MD engines, the claims cannot be evaluated.
