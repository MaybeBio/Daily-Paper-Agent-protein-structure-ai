Coupling of oceanographic state to the dark proteome: a foundation for genome-informed marine productivity modeling

Nelson DR; Plouviez M; Daakour S; Jaiswal A; Fu W; Amin SA; Salehi-Ashtiani K
bioRxiv 预印本 · 2026-09-28 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文用蛋白语言模型和AlphaFold 3分析海洋浮游植物蛋白组与环境耦合，虽涉及蛋白质结构预测，但核心是生态学建模而非蛋白质结构计算问题。

摘要

<h4>ABSTRACT</h4> Phytoplankton drive ∼ 46% of global primary production, yet whether their protein repertoires encode ocean conditions—and whether reference databases capture the environmentally responsive fraction—is unknown. We used a protein language model to classify 447.7 million proteins from 2,357 samples and analyzed domain profiles for 231.7 million algal proteins across 2,044 samples. Under spatial block cross-validation, environment predicted individual domain abundances at R 2 up to 0.59, while domain profiles predicted sea surface temperature at R 2 = 0.38. The strongest coupling lay beyond annotated sequence space: 33,950 families clustered from 201 million Pfam-dark proteins coupled to environment 2.29-fold more strongly than Pfam domains. Cross-taxonomic selection, protein language modeling, and AlphaFold 3 predictions of 138 well-folded, InterPro-unannotated representatives support the Pfam-dark proteome as a biologically structured evolutionary compartment. Thus, the proteins most tightly coupled to ocean state are those least represented in reference databases. <h4>GRAPHICAL ABSTRACT</h4> Language modeling with AI for algal amino acid Sequence Representation (LA 4 SR) classified 447.7 million proteins from a 2,357-sample input collection; the 2,044-sample domain-analysis set contained 231.7 million proteins classified as algal. Protein domain composition and ocean state predict each other, and 33,950 Pfam-dark protein families show 2.29-fold stronger environmental coupling than Pfam domains.

摘要翻译

<h4>摘要</h4> 浮游植物驱动约46%的全球初级生产力，但其蛋白质组库是否编码海洋状况——以及参考数据库是否捕获环境响应部分——尚属未知。我们使用蛋白质语言模型对来自2,357个样本的4.477亿个蛋白质进行分类，并分析了来自2,044个样本的2.317亿个藻类蛋白质的结构域谱。在空间块交叉验证下，环境预测单个结构域丰度的R²最高达0.59，而结构域谱预测海表温度的R²为0.38。最强的耦合存在于注释序列空间之外：从2.01亿个Pfam-dark蛋白质中聚类出的33,950个家族，其与环境耦合强度比Pfam结构域高2.29倍。跨分类选择、蛋白质语言建模以及对138个折叠良好、InterPro未注释代表的AlphaFold 3预测，支持Pfam-dark蛋白质组作为生物学结构化的进化区室。因此，与海洋状态耦合最紧密的蛋白质，恰恰是参考数据库中代表性最差的那些。<h4>图形摘要</h4> 使用AI进行藻类氨基酸序列表示的语言建模（LA⁴SR）对来自2,357个样本输入集合的4.477亿个蛋白质进行了分类；2,044个样本的结构域分析集包含2.317亿个被分类为藻类的蛋白质。蛋白质结构域组成与海洋状态相互预测，且33,950个Pfam-dark蛋白质家族显示出比Pfam结构域强2.29倍的环境耦合。
