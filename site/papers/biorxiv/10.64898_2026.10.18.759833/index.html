BiHC: Protein domain segmentation by bidirectional hierarchical clustering using Voronoi tessellation and physical self-containment

Le QK
bioRxiv 预印本 · 2026-10-20 · 全文(europepmc) · 原文

该文提出基于Voronoi接触面积和双向层次聚类的蛋白质结构域分割方法BiHC，通过物理自包含性评分实现结构域边界识别，与深度学习方法性能相当，属于蛋白质结构计算建模问题，方法创新且可迁移。

摘要

Protein domains are commonly regarded as structurally separated, functionally relevant, and evolutionarily separable units. Although classification systems may assign different domain boundaries to the same structure, structural separation remains a shared criterion, and many domain parsers assess it through inter-residue distances, contact numbers, or contact densities. However, the Voronoi contact area, which is widely used to study protein–protein interactions and predict contact sites, has seen limited use in domain parsing. There are two potential challenges to this problem. First, unlike in protein-protein interaction, partitions are not known in advance and must be identified before their interface can be evaluated. Second, the contact area depends on fragment size and internal packing, making it difficult to compare candidate partitions. Here, we introduce BiHC (Bidirectional Hierarchical Clustering), a domain parser based on all-heavy-atom Voronoi contact areas. By normalizing the contact area for fragment size and internal packing, and incorporating residue hydropathy, we define a pairwise self-containment score F. BiHC applies F in two independent hierarchical searches: top-down recursive splitting until no admissible division remains, and bottom-up merging of secondary-structure elements until the entire structure becomes a single fragment. Intermediate partitions from both searches are ranked by a global partition quality score G, derived on the same principle. On two CATH–ECOD consensus benchmarks, under balanced evaluation, BiHC achieved the strongest performance among all tested criterion-based parsers and was comparable to Chainsaw, the best-performing deep learning method tested. These results show that the Voronoi-based criterion can yield competitive domain parsing. <h4>Software availability</h4> https://github.com/LeKhang97/BiHC <h4>Statement of importance</h4> Protein domains are functional units that are structurally separated. Therefore, one natural solution is to identify domains by measuring the Voronoi contact areas among potential fragments. However, potential fragments are not known in advance, and distinguishing candidate partitions is also challenging. This study shows that BiHC addresses both problems using Voronoi-based criteria, providing competitive domain-parsing performance and demonstrating that such criteria can support both boundary discovery and partition assessment.

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：如何仅凭显式的物理/几何准则（而非学习到的表征）自动识别蛋白质结构域边界？
  • 为什么重要：蛋白质结构域是结构、进化、功能的基本单元，域分割是结构分类、进化分析、基于结构的设计的必要前置步骤。随着实验和预测结构数量激增，自动化、可解释的域分割工具需求日益增长。
  • 现有方法为何不足：
  • 深度学习方法（Chainsaw、Merizo、DPAM-AI）性能强，但边界决策依赖学习到的表征，难以追溯每个决策的结构依据；
  • 传统准则型方法（PUU、DomainParser、PDP、SWORD、DDOMAIN、UniDoc）多使用距离截断或距离函数表示接触，未直接度量界面的几何范围；且候选生成与候选评估常使用不同准则，需要额外校准，增加过拟合风险。
  • 精确的「Can ... ?」研究问题：Can a single Voronoi-based physical criterion, normalized for fragment size and internal packing and supplemented by hydropathy, be used consistently for both candidate domain-boundary discovery and partition quality assessment, achieving performance competitive with deep learning methods?

06 核心思想

1) 表面方法

BiHC 是一个纯准则型（criterion-based）域分割器，包含三个核心组件： - 结构表示：以 SSE（二级结构元件）为基本单元，用全重原子 Voronoi 镶嵌（Voronota v1.29，1.4 Å 探针）计算残基间接触面积； - 成对自包含分数 F：将残基间 Voronoi 接触面积按残基亲水性（Kyte-Doolittle 标度归一化到 [0,1]）加权，再除以片段尺寸相关的归一化项（约化尺寸项 µ_AB 的 β 次方，β = 0.86）； - 全局分割质量分数 G：由 F 导出的全局分数，同时要求域间接触弱（低 F）且域内最弱可允许切割也弱（高 F），以调和平均组合。

搜索策略为双向层次聚类：top-down 从完整结构开始递归施加最弱可允许切割（单切或双切），bottom-up 从 SSE 开始递归合并 F 最高的接触对，两条轨迹的所有中间分割均作为候选，最后用 G 排序选择。

2) 核心洞察

  • Voronoi 接触面积直接度量界面几何范围，比距离截断更物理；
  • 亲水性加权：域间界面平均而言比域内接触更亲水（极性），因为疏水残基更倾向于埋在域内部；
  • 归一化是关键：原始接触面积随片段尺寸增长，必须用约化尺寸项归一化才能跨候选比较；
  • F 同时用于生成与评估：同一准则贯穿候选发现与候选排序，避免生成-评估准则分离带来的校准问题；
  • 双向搜索互补：top-down 和 bottom-up 各自覆盖不同的分割空间，保留所有中间分割可避免早期停止的局部最优问题。

3) 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]

  • 物理准则的「可解释性红利」：当深度学习模型在基准上占优时，显式物理准则仍可提供可追溯的决策依据，且可作为集成、蒸馏或异常检测的补充信号；
  • 「生成-评估同源」原则：若生成候选与评估候选使用同一准则，可减少超参数校准负担，降低过拟合风险——这一原则可迁移到其他结构分割/聚类任务；
  • 双向搜索 + 全轨迹保留：不依赖单一方向的贪心停止，而是保留所有中间状态再全局排序，是一种通用的搜索策略，适用于构象采样、聚类等存在层次结构的问题；
  • 归一化设计需匹配任务几何：尺寸归一化的指数（β）需与接触面积的标度行为匹配（本文通过 PDP 的 α 推导 β = 2α = 0.86），这种「从已有方法推导标度指数」的思路可迁移。

07 方法总览

  • 输入：蛋白质链的 3D 结构（PDB 格式），需 ≥60 残基
  • 输出：域分割（每个残基的域分配），以及每个候选分割的全局质量分数 G
  • 模块： 1. 结构表示模块：SSE 分配（STRIDE）→ 全重原子 Voronoi 镶嵌（Voronota v1.29）→ 残基-残基接触面积矩阵 2. 打分模块：计算成对自包含分数 F（亲水性加权 + 尺寸归一化） 3. 搜索模块：top-down 递归分裂 + bottom-up 递归合并，保留所有中间分割 4. 排序模块：用全局分数 G 对所有候选分割排序，选择最优 5. 后处理模块：合并短片段（<40 残基或 <2 个规则 SSE）到相邻片段
  • 训练：无监督，无需训练数据；唯一需选择的超参数为阈值 t（在 tuning set 上选取）
  • 工具：STRIDE（SSE 分配）、Voronota v1.29（Voronoi 镶嵌）、自研 Python 实现
  • 文字流程： 1. 输入结构 → STRIDE 分配 SSE → Voronota 计算全重原子 Voronoi 镶嵌 → 汇总为残基-残基接触面积 2. 对任意两个片段 A、B，计算亲水性加权的 Voronoi 界面面积 W_AB 和约化尺寸项 µ_AB，得 F_AB = W_AB / (µ_AB)^β 3. Top-down：从完整结构开始，找到 F 最低的可允许切割（单切或双切，每侧 ≥40 残基），递归分裂直到无合法切割；记录所有中间分割 4. Bottom-up：从 SSE 开始，反复合并 F 最高的接触对，直到整个结构合并为一个片段；记录所有中间分割 5. 对每个候选分割 P，计算全局分数 G_P,t（域间 F 与域内最弱切割 F 的调和平均，相对于阈值 t） 6. 选择 G 最高的分割作为最终预测；后处理合并短片段

09 关键公式符号

注：原文公式以文字描述为主，具体符号形式未完整给出。以下基于 Methods 描述重构，标注 [Analysis] 表示推断。

1. 亲水性加权界面面积

W_AB = Σ_(r∈A, s∈B) a_rs · h_rs

  • W_AB：片段 A、B 之间的亲水性加权 Voronoi 接触面积
  • a_rs：残基 r 和 s 之间的 Voronoi 接触面积（由 Voronota 计算）
  • h_rs：残基 r、s 的归一化亲水性值的均值（Kyte-Doolittle 标度归一化到 [0,1]）
  • 用途：衡量域间界面的物理接触强度，亲水残基贡献更大
  • 直觉：域间界面应相对亲水（疏水残基埋在域内），因此高 W 表示强接触

2. 约化尺寸项

µ_AB = (N_A · N_B) / (N_A + N_B) [Analysis：基于「harmonic mean」描述推断]

  • N_A、N_B：片段 A、B 的残基数
  • 用途：归一化界面面积，消除片段尺寸影响
  • 直觉：与两片段尺寸的调和平均成正比，大片段自然有更大接触面积

3. 成对自包含分数

F_AB = W_AB / (µ_AB)^β

  • β = 0.86（= 2α，α = 0.43 来自 PDP）
  • 用途：衡量 A、B 是否应合并为同一域
  • 直觉：F 低 → 界面弱（相对尺寸而言）→ 应分割；F 高 → 界面强 → 应合并
  • 标度推导：当 N_A ≈ N_B ≈ N 时，µ_AB ≈ N/2，F 的尺寸标度为 N^(1-β) = N^0.14，与 PDP 的 N^(2α) = N^0.86 标度一致（在 comparable-size limit）

4. 几何 Voronoi 接触密度（用于定义 F 的分母）

ρ_A = (内部接触面积之和) / (N_A 相关归一化项) [Analysis：原文描述为 "geometric Voronoi contact density"，具体公式未给出]

  • 用途：衡量片段 A 的内部堆积紧密程度
  • 直觉：高密度 → 结构紧凑 → 更可能是一个独立域

5. 全局分割质量分数

G_P,t = H( 域间分数, 域内分数 ) [Analysis：原文描述为 "harmonic mean of the inter-domain and intra-domain scores"，具体形式未给出]

  • 域间分数：所有接触域对的 F 相对于阈值 t 的满足程度（要求 F < t）
  • 域内分数：每个域的最弱可允许内部切割的 F 相对于阈值 t 的满足程度（要求 F > t）
  • 用途：对候选分割 P 的整体质量打分
  • 直觉：好的分割应使域间界面弱（F < t）且域内无弱切割（所有内部切割 F > t）

11 结论正确解读

  • 任务范围：本文仅评估了域分割（domain segmentation），即给定一条链，预测域的数量和边界。未涉及域分类、域功能注释或域间相互作用预测。
  • 基准范围：CONSENSUS-43/44 基于 CATH 与 ECOD 的共识注释，排除了两者不一致的结构。这意味着基准偏向于「注释一致」的较清晰案例，真实世界中的模糊案例可能更多。
  • 真值/oracle 输入：BiHC 的输入仅为 3D 结构（PDB），无任何域注释或同源信息。对比的深度学习方法（Chainsaw、Merizo）使用训练集学习域组织模式，其训练数据可能包含与基准重叠的结构。
  • 端到端状态：BiHC 是完全无监督的，无需训练；但阈值 t 在 tuning set 上选择，tuning set 与 CONSENSUS-43 同源于 CATH v4.3，存在一定程度的「调参泄漏」——虽然 tuning set 排除了 CONSENSUS-43 本身，但两者来自同一分布。
  • 算力成本：Voronoi 镶嵌（Voronota）为多项式时间算法，层次搜索为 O(N²) 量级（基于 SSE 数），远低于深度学习方法的前向推理成本（需 GPU）。但本文未报告具体运行时间。
  • 历史数据依赖：无。BiHC 不依赖任何训练数据或历史注释。
  • 模型依赖：无。BiHC 不依赖预训练模型。
  • 最难情形：多域结构（≥3 域）、紧密堆积的域间界面、无规则 SSE 的卷曲区域。BiHC 在多域结构上准确率显著低于深度学习方法。
  • 有边界的复述：在 CATH-ECOD 共识基准上，BiHC 作为无监督物理准则方法，其域数预测准确率与深度学习方法 Chainsaw 相当，且显著优于其他准则型方法；但其多域分割能力仍弱于深度学习方法，且其性能边界受限于物理去耦假设（即注释域边界不一定对应物理弱界面）。

14 学到什么

标题：Agent 提炼的知识候选

  1. 物理准则的「生成-评估同源」设计：BiHC 的核心设计原则是让同一个物理分数 F 同时驱动候选生成（top-down 切割和 bottom-up 合并）和候选评估（全局分数 G）。这种「同源准则」避免了 STRUDL 中生成与接受准则分离导致的额外校准问题。可迁移到：构象采样中的打分函数设计、聚类算法中的距离度量选择。

  2. 双向层次搜索 + 全轨迹保留：BiHC 不依赖单一方向的贪心决策，而是同时运行 top-down 和 bottom-up 两条轨迹，保留所有中间分割，再用全局分数排序。这避免了早期停止的局部最优问题。可迁移到：蛋白质构象空间的层次划分、MD 模拟中的状态聚类、序列设计中的层次搜索。

  3. Voronoi 接触面积作为「几何界面」度量：相比距离截断，Voronoi 面片面积直接度量原子间的几何接触范围，且可通过镶嵌一次计算、反复查询。可迁移到：蛋白-蛋白相互作用界面预测、蛋白-配体结合位点识别、MD 轨迹中的接触分析。

  4. 亲水性加权的物理直觉：域间界面平均更亲水（疏水残基埋在域内），这一物理先验可以增强边界信号。可迁移到：蛋白-蛋白 docking 的界面打分、膜蛋白的跨膜区域预测、蛋白设计中的表面残基选择。

  5. 尺寸归一化的标度推导：从已有方法（PDP 的 α = 0.43）推导自己的归一化指数（β = 2α = 0.86），而不是自由拟合。这种「从先验方法推导标度」的思路可避免过拟合。可迁移到：任何需要尺寸归一化的几何/物理打分函数设计。

  6. 基准构建的严谨性：CATH-ECOD 共识过滤（域数一致 + IoU ≥ 0.90）、序列和结构双重去冗余、对训练集泄漏的控制（排除 Chainsaw/Merizo 训练集相关簇）——这些基准构建原则对评估任何结构预测/分割方法都适用。

  7. 物理准则与深度学习的互补定位：BiHC 在单域结构上最优、在多域结构上弱于深度学习方法，说明物理准则擅长「识别独立单元」而弱于「解析复杂组织」。可迁移到：将物理准则作为深度学习模型的先验/正则化，或用于生成训练标签。

15 与已有知识连接

  • Wodak & Janin (1981)：最早提出用界面面积极小值识别域边界。BiHC 继承「弱界面 = 域边界」的物理直觉，但用 Voronoi 面片面积替代了溶剂可及表面积（SASA）计算。
  • STRUDL (Wernisch, Hunting, and Wodak 1999)：首次将 Voronoi 接触面积用于域分割，使用 radical plane 加权 Voronoi 和 Kernighan-Lin 图分割。BiHC 的 F 分数可视为 STRUDL 思想的推广，但统一了生成与评估准则，并增加了亲水性加权。
  • PDP (Alexandrov & Shindyalov 2003)：使用距离相关的接触数和尺寸归一化（α = 0.43）。BiHC 的 β = 2α = 0.86 直接源自 PDP 的标度行为。
  • PUU (Holm & Sander 1994)：基于接触数的 top-down 递归分割。BiHC 的 top-down 搜索与之类似，但使用 Voronoi 面积而非接触数。
  • SWORD / SWORD2 (Postic et al. 2017; Cretin et al. 2022)：bottom-up 合并策略的先例。BiHC 的 bottom-up 搜索与之类似，但使用 F 而非接触密度。
  • UniDoc (Zhu et al. 2023)：结合 top-down 和 bottom-up 的准则方法。BiHC 与之最接近，但 UniDoc 使用距离/接触准则，BiHC 使用 Voronoi 面积 + 亲水性。
  • Chainsaw (Wells et al. 2024)：深度学习方法，预测残基对同域概率。BiHC 的对比基准，也是 CATH v4.4 注释流程的组成部分。
  • Merizo (Lau, Kandathil, & Jones 2023)：深度学习方法，预测域掩码。BiHC 的对比基准。
  • Voronota (Olechnovič & Venclauskas 2014)：Voronoi 镶嵌计算工具，BiHC 的几何计算基础。
  • STRIDE (Frishman & Argus 1995)：二级结构分配工具，BiHC 的 SSE 定义来源。
  • Kyte-Doolittle 疏水性标度 (1982)：亲水性加权的物理基础。
  • CATH (Sillitoe et al. 2020) / ECOD (Schaeffer et al. 2026)：域注释数据库，BiHC 的基准来源。
  • MMseqs2 (Steinegger & Söding 2017)：序列聚类工具，用于去冗余。
  • Foldseek (van Kempen et al. 2024)：结构相似性搜索工具，用于结构去冗余。

16 研究想法

标题：Agent 生成的研究候选

候选 1：物理-学习混合域分割器

  • 来源局限/观察：BiHC 在单域结构上最优但在多域结构上弱于深度学习方法；深度学习方法性能强但决策不可解释。
  • 核心假设：将 BiHC 的 F/G 物理分数作为特征或正则化项注入深度学习模型（如 Chainsaw 的架构），可以兼得物理可解释性和学习表征的表达力。
  • 增量：在 Chainsaw/Merizo 的架构中加入 F 分数作为辅助输入或辅助损失；或在 BiHC 的候选排序阶段用学习到的排序器替代固定阈值 t。
  • 初步方法：在 CONSENSUS-43 上训练一个轻量排序器，输入为 BiHC 各候选分割的 F/G 特征 + 结构几何特征，输出为候选排序；与纯 BiHC 和纯 Chainsaw 对比。
  • 验证方式：CONSENSUS-43/44 基准，报告 exact-K、K-score、IoU、NDO、CSD、DBD；重点分析多域子集。
  • 失败模式：物理特征与学习表征冗余，混合后无增益；或排序器过拟合 tuning set。
  • 创新状态：unverified

候选 2：Voronoi 接触面积作为 MD 轨迹的域动态描述符

  • 来源局限/观察：BiHC 的 F 分数是静态结构上的快照；但域边界在 MD 模拟中可能动态变化。
  • 核心假设：沿 MD 轨迹计算 F/G 分数的时间序列可以揭示域的动态稳定性、域间界面的开合事件，以及域边界随构象变化的移动。
  • 增量：将静态域分割扩展到动态域分割（dynamic domain segmentation），为构象采样和 MD 分析提供新工具。
  • 初步方法：选取若干已知域结构的蛋白，运行 μs 级 MD；每帧计算 F/G；聚类 F/G 时间序列，识别域边界移动或域合并/分裂事件。
  • 验证方式：与实验已知的域运动（如 domain swapping、open-closed 构象）对比；检查 F/G 变化是否与构象转变同步。
  • 失败模式：F/G 对热波动过于敏感，时间序列噪声大；或域边界在 MD 中过于稳定，无动态事件可捕捉。
  • 创新状态：unverified

候选 3：F 分数作为 AlphaFold 预测结构的域质量评估器

  • 来源局限/观察：AlphaFold 预测结构的域边界可能与实验结构不一致；pLDDT 是残基级置信度，缺乏域级质量度量。
  • 核心假设：BiHC 的 F/G 分数可以评估预测结构的域内自包含性和域间去耦性，从而作为域级置信度指标，辅助识别预测结构中不可靠的域边界。
  • 增量：为 AlphaFold 预测结果提供域级质量评估，补充 pLDDT 的残基级信息。
  • 初步方法：在 CONSENSUS-43 上，对每个结构的 AlphaFold 预测版本计算 F/G；比较预测结构与实验结构的 F/G 差异；训练一个分类器，用 F/G 特征预测域边界是否正确。
  • 验证方式：在独立测试集上评估域边界预测准确率；与 pLDDT 基线对比。
  • 失败模式：F/G 对结构误差不敏感，无法区分正确与错误的域边界；或 pLDDT 已包含足够信息，F/G 无增量。
  • 创新状态：unverified

候选 4：基于 F 分数的蛋白设计域边界约束

  • 来源局限/观察：蛋白设计（如 de novo design）中，域边界的合理放置影响折叠和功能；现有设计工具缺乏显式的域边界约束。
  • 核心假设：将 F/G 分数作为设计目标的正则化项，可以引导设计出域间界面弱、域内自包含性强的多域蛋白。
  • 增量：为蛋白设计提供物理可解释的域级目标函数。
  • 初步方法：在现有设计流程（如 RFdiffusion + ProteinMPNN）中，加入 F/G 分数作为可微损失项；设计若干多域蛋白，验证其是否折叠为预期域结构。
  • 验证方式：实验表达和结构验证（或 AlphaFold 预测验证）；与无 F/G 约束的设计对比。
  • 失败模式：F/G 不可微或梯度噪声大，难以优化；或设计出的域边界与天然蛋白差异大，折叠失败。
  • 创新状态：unverified

候选 5：双向层次搜索策略迁移到构象聚类

  • 来源局限/观察：BiHC 的双向搜索（top-down 分裂 + bottom-up 合并）避免了单一方向的局部最优；构象聚类（如 MD 轨迹聚类）同样面临层次结构未知的问题。
  • 核心假设：将 BiHC 的双向搜索策略迁移到构象空间聚类，可以比单一方向的层次聚类（如 agglomerative 或 divisive）更鲁棒地识别构象状态层次。
  • 增量：为构象聚类提供新的搜索策略，可能改善对稀有构象和中间态的识别。
  • 初步方法：将 F 分数替换为构象间的距离/相似度度量（如 RMSD、接触图相似度），实现双向层次聚类；在标准 MD 轨迹集上评估。
  • 验证方式：与常见聚类方法（如 GROMOS、DBSCAN、层次聚类）对比，使用 VRC、silhouette 等内部指标和已知构象状态的恢复率。
  • 失败模式：双向搜索的计算成本过高；或构象空间缺乏清晰的层次结构，双向搜索无优势。
  • 创新状态：unverified

审稿人评审

Review setup

  • Input scope Full manuscript text (abstract, introduction, results, discussion, methods) for a single-author preprint submitted to bioRxiv.
  • Assessment boundary Scientific soundness, methodological clarity, validity of claims relative to presented evidence, and suitability for a general structural biology audience. No assessment of code functionality or reproducibility beyond what is described in the text.
  • Shared manuscript claim summary The authors introduce BiHC, a criterion-based protein domain parser that uses Voronoi tessellation-derived contact areas, normalized by fragment size and internal packing, and weighted by residue hydropathy, to define a pairwise self-containment score F and a global partition quality score G. BiHC performs independent top-down and bottom-up hierarchical searches, and is claimed to achieve state-of-the-art performance among criterion-based parsers and comparable performance to deep learning methods on CATH-ECOD consensus benchmarks.
  • Visible evidence base Abstract, full introduction, results overview (including dataset construction, tuning, benchmark performance, and case studies), discussion, and methods sections. Figures are referenced but not provided. Supplementary files are referenced but not provided.
  • Missing materials affecting confidence Figures 1-7, all supplementary tables (S1-S7), Supplementary File 1 (including ablation study), Supplementary File 2 (per-chain results), and the actual benchmark datasets. Without these, quantitative claims cannot be independently verified.

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment This manuscript presents a conceptually interesting approach to protein domain segmentation, leveraging Voronoi tessellation in a way that has been underexplored in modern domain parsers. The methodological framework is clearly described and the motivation is well-articulated. However, the manuscript as presented has several critical gaps. The results section is largely descriptive without presenting actual numerical data, making it impossible to assess the strength of the claimed performance. The reliance on a single-author submission with no code or data available at the time of review further limits verification. The core claims, while plausible, are not substantiated by the evidence visible in this manuscript. The work has potential, but in its current form, the case is not fully established.

  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Structural biologists, bioinformaticians, and researchers working on protein structure annotation and classification would be the primary audience. The work is relevant to those interested in criterion-based (non-machine-learning) approaches to domain parsing, as it offers a transparent alternative to deep learning methods. The use of Voronoi tessellation and physical self-containment criteria may also appeal to researchers studying protein geometry and packing. The comparative benchmarking against Chainsaw and Merizo would be of interest to the broader protein structure prediction community.

  • Major Strengths 1. The conceptual framing is strong. The authors correctly identify a gap in the field: most modern domain parsers rely on learned representations, and explicit geometric criteria are underrepresented. The use of Voronoi contact areas, normalized for size and packing, is a thoughtful and physically motivated approach. 2. The methodological design is coherent. The bidirectional search strategy (independent top-down and bottom-up) is a sensible way to explore the partition space, and the use of a single scoring function F throughout both candidate generation and evaluation is a principled improvement over methods like STRUDL that use different criteria at different stages. 3. The authors are appropriately cautious in their claims. They acknowledge the multi-domain performance gap and the potential for curated annotations to reflect non-geometric information. This balanced perspective is commendable. 4. The inclusion of a reconstructed STRUDL (STRUDL-R) as a baseline is a useful contribution, as it allows for direct comparison with a historical Voronoi-based method.

  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Evidence sufficiency
  • Claim pointer The manuscript claims that "On the primary CONSENSUS-43 benchmark, BiHC showed a clear class-dependent performance profile" and that "BiHC achieved the strongest performance among all tested criterion-based parsers and was comparable to Chainsaw" under balanced evaluation.
  • Evidence pointer Results section, "BiHC performance on benchmark datasets"; Figures 4 and 5 are referenced but not provided; Tables S2-S7 are referenced but not provided.
  • Concern The central quantitative claims of the manuscript are entirely unsupported by the visible text. No numerical results are presented in the body of the manuscript. The reader is told that BiHC performed well, but is not shown any actual performance values, confidence intervals, or statistical comparisons. The figures and supplementary tables that would contain this information are not available for review.   • Why it matters Without access to the actual performance numbers, it is impossible to evaluate whether the claims of "strongest among criterion-based parsers" and "comparable to Chainsaw" are justified. The reader cannot assess the magnitude of differences, the variance across protein classes, or the practical significance of the results. This is a blocking issue for any assessment of the manuscript's validity.

  • Resolution test The authors must provide the actual numerical results in the main text or in accessible supplementary materials. This should include at minimum: exact-K accuracy, K-score, IoU, NDO, CSD, and DBD for all methods on both benchmarks, with appropriate measures of uncertainty. The figures must be made available for review.

  • Concern ID R1-M2

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking Yes
  • Axis Methodological completeness
  • Claim pointer The manuscript states that "BiHC applies F in two independent hierarchical searches" and that "Intermediate partitions from both searches are ranked by a global partition quality score G, derived on the same principle."
  • Evidence pointer Results section, "Overview of the BiHC framework"; Materials and Methods, "Pairwise self-containment score" and "Global partition quality score".
  • Concern The mathematical definitions of the key quantities F and G are not fully specified in the visible text. The methods section describes the components of F (hydropathy-weighted interface, reduced-size term, geometric Voronoi contact density) but the actual equations are not shown. The text refers to equations that are not present in the manuscript. Similarly, the definition of G is described verbally but the exact formula is not given. The threshold t is mentioned but its precise role in the scoring is not fully formalized.
  • Why it matters The manuscript's central contribution is a novel scoring function. Without the explicit mathematical formulation, the method cannot be reproduced, evaluated for correctness, or compared to alternative approaches. The reader cannot assess whether the normalization is sound, whether the hydropathy weighting is appropriate, or whether the thresholding scheme is principled. This is a fundamental gap in a methods-focused paper.

  • Resolution test The authors must provide the complete mathematical definitions of F, G, and the thresholding procedure. This should include all equations, definitions of all variables, and a clear statement of how the threshold t is applied. The equations should be numbered and referenced in the text.

  • Concern ID R1-M3

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking No
  • Axis Benchmark validity
  • Claim pointer The manuscript claims that CONSENSUS-43 and CONSENSUS-44 are appropriate benchmarks, with CONSENSUS-43 being "the primary benchmark" and CONSENSUS-44 used "to assess the stability of tool performance."
  • Evidence pointer Results section, "Overview of the dataset construction"; Materials and Methods, "Data preprocessing" and "CATH-ECOD consensus filtering".
  • Concern The benchmark construction is described in detail, but several aspects raise concerns. First, the CONSENSUS-44 set is derived from CATH v4.4, which the authors acknowledge "is not fully independent of that method" (i.e., Chainsaw) because CATH v4.4 used Chainsaw in its annotation pipeline. This means that the benchmark itself may be biased toward Chainsaw's predictions, making any comparison on this set difficult to interpret. Second, the filtering criteria (CATH-ECOD agreement on domain count and partition IoU ≥ 0.90) may select for "easy" cases where the two databases agree, potentially reducing the difficulty of the benchmark and limiting its ability to discriminate between methods. Third, the number of multi-domain structures in CONSENSUS-43 (200 out of 3231) is small, which may limit the statistical power of the multi-domain comparisons.
  • Why it matters The validity of the benchmark directly affects the interpretability of the performance claims. If the benchmark is biased or too easy, the reported performance may not generalize to real-world domain parsing tasks. The small number of multi-domain structures is particularly concerning, as this is where the authors acknowledge BiHC struggles.

  • Resolution test The authors should provide a more detailed analysis of the benchmark characteristics, including the distribution of domain counts, protein lengths, and structural classes. They should also discuss the potential impact of the CATH v4.4 annotation bias on CONSENSUS-44 results and consider whether additional benchmarks (e.g., from ECOD alone or from a manually curated set) would strengthen the evaluation.

  • Concern ID R1-M4

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking No
  • Axis Comparison fairness
  • Claim pointer The manuscript states that "BiHC achieved the strongest performance among all tested criterion-based parsers and was comparable to Chainsaw, the best-performing deep learning method tested."
  • Evidence pointer Results section, "BiHC performance on benchmark datasets"; Materials and Methods, "Evaluated domain parsers".
  • Concern The comparison with other methods is described, but the details of how these methods were run are not fully specified. For example, were the deep learning methods (Chainsaw, Merizo) run with their default settings, or were they fine-tuned on the benchmark? Were the criterion-based methods (UniDoc, SWORD2) run with their default parameters, or were they optimized for the benchmark? The manuscript mentions that "All tools were obtained from their official repositories" but does not state the specific versions or configuration used. Additionally, the reconstruction of STRUDL (STRUDL-R) is described as having a "logistic classifier... fitted on the tuning set," which raises the question of whether STRUDL-R was given an advantage by being trained on data from the same distribution as the benchmark.
  • Why it matters Fair comparison is essential for the validity of the performance claims. If other methods were not given the same opportunity to perform well (e.g., through hyperparameter tuning), the comparison may be biased in favor of BiHC. Conversely, if STRUDL-R was trained on the tuning set, it may have an unfair advantage over methods that were not similarly tuned.

  • Resolution test The authors should provide a detailed description of how each comparison method was configured and run, including specific versions, parameters, and any tuning procedures. They should also clarify whether the tuning set was used to optimize any aspect of the comparison methods, and if so, how this was done.

  • Concern ID R1-M5

  • Severity Major
  • Blocking No
  • Axis Ablation study rigor
  • Claim pointer The manuscript states that "we conducted an ablation study on the tuning set to assess the contributions of its individual components" and that "most components of F, including hydropathy and size-dependent terms, contributed to the performance."
  • Evidence pointer Discussion section; Supplementary File 1, "Ablation study" section (not provided).
  • Concern The ablation study is only mentioned in passing, with no results presented in the main text. The reader is told that removing components led to "a slight decrease in balanced domain-count performance, although the differences were not significant." This is a critical piece of evidence for the design of F, but the details are entirely relegated to a supplementary file that is not available for review. The lack of significance is also concerning, as it suggests that the individual components may not be essential, which would weaken the justification for the specific form of F.
  • Why it matters The ablation study is the primary evidence that the specific formulation of F is well-justified. If the components do not significantly contribute, the method could potentially be simplified, or the choice of components may be arbitrary. The reader cannot evaluate this without seeing the actual results.

  • Resolution test The authors should present the ablation study results in the main text or provide the supplementary file for review. They should also discuss the implications of the lack of statistical significance for the design choices.
