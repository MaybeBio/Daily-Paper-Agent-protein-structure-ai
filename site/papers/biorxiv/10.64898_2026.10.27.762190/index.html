9

De novo Protein Generating Model via Structure-Aware Graph Neural Networks

Chance M Gammill; Lakshman Tamil
bioRxiv 预印本 · 2026-10-28 · 全文(europepmc) · 原文

该文提出一种基于结构感知图神经网络和可微结构预测（ESMFold）的蛋白质从头生成模型，以结构精度而非序列恢复为优化目标，直接针对蛋白质结构计算建模问题，方法创新且与课题高度相关。

摘要

The focus of this paper is to create a protein conformation prediction model that is optimized for structural accuracy rather than sequence recovery. To achieve this goal, we created a graph neural network (GNN) [19] for the design of computational proteins that integrates differentiable structure prediction through ESMFold [13] via a structural loss function, allowing direct optimization of the 3D conformational accuracy. Our model takes protein backbone coordinates as input and predicts an amino acid sequence using a composite loss that heavily weights structural similarity. On a matched evaluation sample, our model achieves structural precision (RMSD, TM-score, and GDT-TS) that is statistically indistinguishable from ProteinMPNN, a leading sequence recovery-focused method, despite training with no direct sequence recovery supervision and substantially lower native sequence recovery. Our results demonstrate that sequence recovery is not necessary to achieve competitive structural accuracy and that proteins can possess multiple non-unique solutions, suggesting that protein sequence prediction models may perform comparably well under purely structural conformation training. This distinction in training data and model structure allows for the training of a protein-generating model on non-natural proteins. Creating a model designed for de novo protein generation.

摘要翻译

本文的重点是构建一个以结构精度而非序列恢复为优化目标的蛋白质构象预测模型。为实现这一目标，我们创建了一个用于计算蛋白质设计的图神经网络（GNN）[19]，通过ESMFold [13]集成可微分的结构预测，并采用结构损失函数，从而能够直接优化三维构象的准确性。我们的模型以蛋白质骨架坐标为输入，利用一个高度加权结构相似性的复合损失来预测氨基酸序列。在匹配的评估样本上，尽管训练过程中没有直接的序列恢复监督，且天然序列恢复率显著较低，我们的模型在结构精度（RMSD、TM-score和GDT-TS）上与以序列恢复为重点的领先方法ProteinMPNN在统计上无显著差异。我们的结果表明，序列恢复并非实现竞争性结构精度的必要条件，且蛋白质可能存在多个非唯一解，这提示蛋白质序列预测模型在纯结构构象训练下可能表现同样出色。训练数据和模型结构的这一区别使得我们能够在非天然蛋白质上训练蛋白质生成模型，从而创建一个专为从头蛋白质生成设计的模型。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：能否在不以 sequence recovery 为训练目标的情况下，仅通过结构导向的损失（含可微结构预测）训练序列生成模型，从而获得与序列恢复导向方法相当的结构精度？
  • 为什么重要：若结构精度不依赖序列恢复监督，则模型可训练于非天然蛋白数据，面向 de novo 蛋白生成；同时提示蛋白序列解的非唯一性。
  • 现有方法为何不足：以 sequence recovery 为核心的方法（如 ProteinMPNN）优化的是与天然序列的一致性，而非直接优化 3D 构象精度；作者主张 sequence recovery 并非达到竞争性结构精度的必要条件。
  • 精确研究问题（Can ... ?）：Can a sequence-generating model trained without direct sequence recovery supervision, using a structure-aware loss with differentiable structure prediction, achieve structural accuracy statistically indistinguishable from a sequence recovery-focused method on a matched evaluation sample?

06 核心思想

1) 表面方法：GNN 以 backbone 坐标为输入预测序列；用复合损失，其中结构相似度权重很高；结构信号来自 ESMFold 的可微结构预测。 2) 核心洞察：结构精度可由结构损失直接优化，而不必通过 sequence recovery 间接获得；因此可在无直接序列恢复监督下达到竞争性结构精度。 3) 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：在结构相关生成/设计任务中，将可微结构预测器作为「结构 oracle」嵌入训练回路，可把优化目标从序列空间迁移到结构空间；对构象采样/生成与对接等任务，类似「可微物理/结构评估器 + 生成器」的回路可能比代理指标更贴近最终目标。

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents an interesting hypothesis, namely that structural accuracy can serve as a sufficient training signal for protein sequence design without explicit sequence recovery objectives. However, the evidence provided in the abstract is insufficient to evaluate the validity of this claim. Key quantitative results are mentioned but not reported, the statistical comparison to ProteinMPNN is described but not substantiated, and the relationship between the reported findings and the stated goal of de novo protein generation is not clearly established. The work may be of interest to the protein design community, but the current abstract does not provide enough information to assess technical soundness or scientific significance.

Researchers focused on de novo protein design and generative models for proteins would also find the proposed approach relevant.
  • Major strengths The central hypothesis is clearly stated and potentially impactful. The use of a differentiable structure prediction module within a GNN framework is a reasonable methodological direction. The authors explicitly acknowledge the trade-off between sequence recovery and structural accuracy, which frames the contribution in a useful way.
