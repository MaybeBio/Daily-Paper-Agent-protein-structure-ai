RNAbridge: a database of extended and non-canonical helices

Zakrzewski D; Antczak M; Zok T
bioRxiv 预印本 · 2026-09-17 · 全文(europepmc) · 原文

该文构建RNA非经典螺旋结构数据库，虽涉及结构计算分析，但对象为RNA而非蛋白质，且主要贡献是数据库资源而非可迁移的算法框架，与蛋白质结构计算课题关联较弱。

摘要

RNA function is dictated by 3D architecture. Although 2D structural models based on canonical Watson-Crick base pairs are widely used, they often fail to capture the non-canonical interactions, tertiary contacts, and coaxial stacking important for biological activity. We have developed RNAbridge, a comprehensive database and web application that systematically identifies, quantifies, and visualizes extended non-canonical helices and multi-way junctions. Using a geometry- and stacking-based pipeline, we analyzed the Protein Data Bank and compiled a catalog of 135,541 structural motifs. RNAbridge includes a user-friendly interface with interactive filters, synchronized 2D and 3D visualizations, and detailed structural data, including helical bend angles and stacking paths. By connecting simplified 2D topologies with complex 3D structures, RNAbridge serves as a valuable resource for structural biologists and lays the foundation for future machine learning applications in RNA structure prediction. The database, available at https://rnabridge.cs.put.poznan.pl/, is automatically updated once a week.

摘要翻译

RNA功能由三维结构决定。尽管基于经典Watson-Crick碱基配对的二维结构模型被广泛使用，但它们往往无法捕捉对生物活性至关重要的非经典相互作用、三级接触和共轴堆叠。我们开发了RNAbridge，一个综合性的数据库和网络应用程序，能够系统地识别、量化和可视化扩展的非经典螺旋和多路连接。利用基于几何和堆叠的分析流程，我们分析了蛋白质数据银行（Protein Data Bank），并编制了包含135,541个结构基序的目录。RNAbridge提供用户友好的界面，包括交互式过滤器、同步的二维和三维可视化，以及详细的结构数据，如螺旋弯曲角度和堆叠路径。通过将简化的二维拓扑结构与复杂的三维结构相连接，RNAbridge为结构生物学家提供了宝贵的资源，并为未来RNA结构预测中的机器学习应用奠定了基础。该数据库可在https://rnabridge.cs.put.poznan.pl/访问，每周自动更新一次。
