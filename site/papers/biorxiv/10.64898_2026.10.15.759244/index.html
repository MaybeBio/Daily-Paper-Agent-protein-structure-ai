8

Two Pleckstrin homology domains scaffold SNAP-47-containing SNARE complexes

Alickovic I; Wise M; Tela R; Fausnaught C; Lee L; Beasley C; Carr C; Carr C; Schwindt L; Peters JJ
bioRxiv 预印本 · 2026-10-16 · 全文(europepmc) · 原文

该研究结合AlphaFold3预测、BioEmu和分子动力学模拟，解析SNAP-47 SNARE复合物中两个PH结构域的结构与功能，属于蛋白质结构计算建模在神经突触融合机制中的应用。

摘要

Long-term potentiation requires the regulated insertion of AMPA receptors into the postsynaptic membrane of neurons, a process mediated by a SNARE complex containing SNAP-47, syntaxin-3, and synaptobrevin-2. Compared to other SNAP proteins, SNAP-47 exhibits slower fusion kinetics and contains uncharacterized regions whose functions remain unclear. Here, we integrated biochemical approaches with artificial intelligence structure prediction tools and molecular dynamics to investigate the architecture of SNAP-47-containing SNARE complexes. AlphaFold3 predictions of the ternary SNAP-47-containing SNARE complex revealed one known Pleckstrin homology domain (PH1) and one previously unidentified PH domain (PH2), which we validated experimentally. BioEmu and molecular dynamics simulations indicated that PH1 and PH2 form a stable and energetically favorable interface. In this bound state, PH1 stably interacted with the SNARE coiled coil, and in silico acetylation at a lysine whose acetylation is known to impair SNAP-47 function disrupted this PH1-SNARE interface. AMPAR insertion via SNAP-47-containing SNARE complexes is triggered by the calcium sensor synaptotagmin, and we observed that PH1, PH2, and synaptotagmin-1 can simultaneously engage with the plasma membrane, consistent with a pro-fusion conformation. Together, we provide new mechanistic insight into how PH domains regulate SNAP-47-mediated membrane fusion during synaptic plasticity.

摘要翻译

长时程增强需要AMPA受体受调控地插入神经元突触后膜，这一过程由包含SNAP-47、syntaxin-3和synaptobrevin-2的SNARE复合体介导。与其他SNAP蛋白相比，SNAP-47表现出较慢的融合动力学，并含有功能尚不明确的未表征区域。在此，我们整合了生物化学方法、人工智能结构预测工具和分子动力学 (molecular dynamics) 来研究含SNAP-47的SNARE复合体的结构。AlphaFold3对含SNAP-47的三元SNARE复合体的预测揭示了一个已知的Pleckstrin同源结构域 (PH1) 和一个先前未识别的PH结构域 (PH2)，我们通过实验验证了这一点。BioEmu和分子动力学模拟表明，PH1和PH2形成了一个稳定且能量上有利的界面。在这种结合状态下，PH1与SNARE卷曲螺旋稳定相互作用，并且对已知会损害SNAP-47功能的赖氨酸进行计算机模拟乙酰化，破坏了这一PH1-SNARE界面。通过含SNAP-47的SNARE复合体进行的AMPAR插入由钙传感器突触结合蛋白触发，我们观察到PH1、PH2和突触结合蛋白-1可以同时与质膜结合，这与促融合构象一致。总之，我们提供了关于PH结构域如何在突触可塑性过程中调控SNAP-47介导的膜融合的新机制见解。

06 核心思想

1) 表面方法： - 使用 AlphaFold3 预测 SNAP-47-containing SNARE 三元复合物结构 - 通过 BioEmu 和分子动力学模拟验证结构稳定性与能量特征 - 结合生化实验验证 PH 结构域的存在与功能 - 通过 in silico 乙酰化模拟研究翻译后修饰对结构的影响

2) 核心洞察： - SNAP-47 含有两个 PH 结构域（PH1 和 PH2），其中 PH2 是此前未识别的 - PH1 和 PH2 形成稳定且能量有利的界面，在该结合状态下 PH1 与 SNARE 卷曲螺旋稳定互作 - 已知损害 SNAP-47 功能的赖氨酸乙酰化会破坏 PH1-SNARE 界面，提供翻译后修饰调控的结构机制 - PH1、PH2 和 synaptotagmin-1 可同时与质膜结合，支持促融合构象

3) 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]： - AI 结构预测（如 AlphaFold3）可以发现实验方法难以识别的结构域，但必须结合实验验证 - 将已知功能相关突变/修饰映射到预测结构上，可以快速生成可检验的机制假说 - 多工具整合（AlphaFold3 + BioEmu + MD + 生化）是结构生物学研究的有效范式

  • Overall assessment The abstract presents a potentially interesting mechanistic hypothesis regarding the role of PH domains in SNAP-47-containing SNARE complexes. However, the evidence base available for review is limited to the abstract, which precludes any substantive evaluation of the experimental rigor, data quality, or validity of the conclusions.
