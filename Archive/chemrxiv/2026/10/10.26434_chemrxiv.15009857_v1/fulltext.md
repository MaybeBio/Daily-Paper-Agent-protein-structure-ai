pH plays a key role in many biological processes. When the solution pH equals the isoelectric point (pI) of a protein, the protein carries zero net charge, which minimizes its solubility and thus promotes aggregation. Here we present ESMpI, a sequence-based protein pI predictor that combines optimized pKa values of seven ionizable residue types and two terminals with embeddings from a pre-trained protein language model. Benchmarked against experimental protein pI values, ESMpI outperforms other state-of-the-art methods, including structure-based approaches that derive the pI of a protein from residue-specific pKa calculations. Finally, ESMpI requires only sequence information, making it well suited for high-throughput proteomescale analyses and protein engineering.
