- **Shared manuscript claim summary** The authors propose that biomolecular condensates alter the folding landscape of protein alpha-helices through a balance of multivalent interactions (unfolding) and crowding (folding), and that this process is kinetically frustrated due to coupling with co-condensate protein contact rearrangement. They use atomistic simulations and Bayesian optimization to support this framework.

The proposed framework—balancing multivalent interactions and crowding, with kinetic frustration—is conceptually appealing. However, the provided text lacks the quantitative evidence, methodological details, and data necessary to support the central claims. Without access to figures, tables, or supplementary materials, the manuscript cannot be evaluated for technical soundness or reproducibility. The claims are interesting but currently unsubstantiated from the supplied material.
