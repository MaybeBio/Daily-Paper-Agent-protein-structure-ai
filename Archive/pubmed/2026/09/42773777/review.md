The integration of paratope embeddings and AI-based structure prediction into a TCR specificity database is conceptually appealing and aligns with current trends in computational immunology. However, the abstract lacks quantitative evidence, validation details, and a clear description of how the new features improve upon existing tools. The claims of enhanced noise filtering and reliability support are plausible but not substantiated from the provided material. The resource is likely to be of interest to the TCR and immunoinformatics communities, but the case for its novelty and impact is not fully established from the abstract alone.

The database update could serve as a reference resource for benchmarking and training predictive models.
