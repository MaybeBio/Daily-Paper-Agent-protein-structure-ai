The reported success rate, structural validation, and atomic accuracy are notable. However, the abstract alone provides insufficient detail to fully assess the methodological novelty, the robustness of the design pipeline, and the significance of the improvements relative to prior fusion approaches. The claims are plausible but require full manuscript scrutiny.

The experimental validation is strong, with multiple sequence variants confirmed by cryo-EM at atomic resolution and low C-alpha RMSD values. The reported success rate of 2 out of 15 designs is reasonable for computational protein design and suggests methodological maturity.
