# Correction to: Master of Metals2: a graph neural network based architecture for the prediction of zinc binding sites in protein structures


## Main Text
  This is a correction to: Vincenzo Laveglia, Cosimo Ciofalo, Enrico Morelli, Claudia Andreini, Antonio Rosato, Master of Metals2: a graph neural network based architecture for the prediction of zinc binding sites in protein structures, Briefings in Bioinformatics, Volume 27, Issue 2, March 2026, bbag078, https://doi.org/10.1093/bib/bbag078

  In the originally published version of this article, Claudia Andreini was inadvertently not designated as a co-corresponding author. Claudia Andreini (Department of Chemistry, University of Florence, Sesto Fiorentino, Italy; Magnetic Resonance Center (CERM), University of Florence, Sesto Fiorentino, Italy; Consorzio Interuniversitario di Risonanze Magnetiche di Metallo Proteine, Sesto Fiorentino, Italy; Email: claudia.andreini@unifi.it) has now been added as a co-corresponding author.

  The original article has been corrected online.
