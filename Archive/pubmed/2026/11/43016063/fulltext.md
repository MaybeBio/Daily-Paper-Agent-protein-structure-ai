CATH (https://www.cathdb.info) is a widely used bioinformatics resource that classifies protein structural domains from the wwPDB and integrates evolutionary, structural, and functional annotation alongside interactive visualization and analysis tools. CATH is recognized as a Core Data Resource by ELIXIR and a Global Core Biodata Resource by the Global Biodata Coalition. CATH continues to expand its coverage of protein structure space by integrating experimentally determined structures from the Protein Data Bank with the rapidly growing collections of predicted structures from the AlphaFold Protein Structure Database as part of The Encyclopedia of Domains, the new sister resource of CATH. We provide an update on advances in new homology detection protocols and automated workflows providing end-to-end domain annotation and classification for processing large-scale protein structural data.
